Welcome to the blog! This is where we post updates, tips, and deeper dives that don't fit neatly into the documentation.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Release notes** — what changed and why
- **Tutorials** — longer, narrative walkthroughs
- **Tips & tricks** — short posts on getting the most out of the tool

## How the Site Is Organized

The main documentation lives under [Docs](/docs/). It covers installation, concepts, guides, and the full CLI reference. Think of it as the reference you reach for when you know what you're looking for.

This blog is for everything else — context, motivation, and the occasional deep dive.

## Getting Started

If you're new here, the best place to start is [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started/). It walks you through installation and your first project in about five minutes.
