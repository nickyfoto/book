MacWorks turns a large collection of daily Markdown briefings into a focused editorial edition. The current desk highlights the newest reporting while every past edition remains available at a permanent address.

The publishing pipeline stays intentionally simple: Markdown remains the source of truth, Hugo renders the reading pages, and GitHub Pages serves the result.
