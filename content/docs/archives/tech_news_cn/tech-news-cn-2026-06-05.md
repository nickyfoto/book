## Engineering & Dev
In the realm of AI agents and enterprise security, Alibaba's AAIG AI Red Team is set to present its "REAL" (Root Cause, Effect, Layer) risk matrix at the upcoming AICon in Shanghai, as outlined in **[阿里AAIG实验室AI红队负责人宋奇钊(胖錿)确认出席AICon上海站](https://www.infoq.cn/article/VrBbVKeqPHjOi2rdH4Hc)**. This three-dimensional framework helps engineering teams systematically categorize and defend against multi-step Agent vulnerabilities, mapping out how cross-layer prompt injections can hijack an Agent's execution layer. Meanwhile, the openJiuwen community has open-sourced "Auto Harness," detailed in **[openJiuwen 社区又上新： JiuwenSwarm 给 Harness 装上「后训练」](https://www.infoq.cn/article/lk4PwQKXhgPSlM7a8ACU)**, which is an automated framework that allows AI Agents to self-optimize their own operational code and domain expertise without manual human tuning, effectively bringing post-training principles to the Agent's harness. 

On the architecture and database front, DuckDB has launched a new protocol covered in **[DuckDB Quack：基于 HTTP 的客户端/服务器协议，面向多用户分析](https://www.infoq.cn/article/au8ICoBCuxOaOuyr0wWI)**. The HTTP-based Quack protocol enables concurrent multi-user analytics directly against DuckDB's native formats without switching to heavyweight databases, boasting transfer speeds roughly 3.5 times faster than Arrow Flight. In backend ecosystem updates, Node.js announced a major shift in its release cycle in **[Node.js 从 27 版本开始改为每年发布一个大版本](https://www.infoq.cn/article/9yfZrDDTaZYOSP2idLXh)**. Scrapping its decade-old odd/even LTS system, the runtime will now release only one major version annually starting with version 27, significantly reducing the chronic maintenance burden on its volunteer teams. Finally, addressing the rapid adoption of AI coding tools, InfoQ recommends exploring "Change Cases" in **[架构变更案例：面向演进式架构的实用方法](https://www.infoq.cn/article/arLDulGp7TW4dAUaCMHB)**. The article argues that over-reliance on specific AI coding agents requires architects to systematically evaluate the cost of future architectural tear-downs and vendor migrations.
