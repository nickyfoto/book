# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-08-17

## Top Story
The launch of **"Token Loan" (Token贷)** by the Bank of China in Guangzhou's Haizhu district represents a pioneer fintech mechanism for the AI era. This credit facility uses an AI firm’s real-time Token consumption as a key proxy for business activity and creditworthiness, moving bank risk assessment from static "balance sheets" to dynamic, operational "data traffic". ## Engineering & Dev
- **LLM Frontiers & Post-Training Scale**: 
  The developer community is buzzing with two major LLM updates. Zhipu AI officially launched **[GLM-5.3](https://www.infoq.cn/article/xWyWwu4ZNptlhpb15tia)**, bringing a massive 50% improvement in coding capabilities over GLM-5.2 and passing an offline python coding benchmark designed by GPT-5.6 Sol with a perfect score. Trained using the open-source asynchronous reinforcement learning framework "slime," the model focuses heavily on long-context task execution, enabling agents to handle multi-step engineering tasks from end to end. Simultaneously, Alibaba's Qwen team has made waves by open-sourcing **[Qwen3.8-27B](https://www.infoq.cn/article/MljtE2Xk6hVkd061LY7k)**, a native multimodal dense model that outperforms Claude Opus 4.6 Max on competitive coding and agentic tasks while remaining small enough to run offline on local consumer-grade GPUs. Global developers have immediately mobilized, releasing optimized Jinja templates and tools like `qwen38-mtp` to leverage its Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) head, boosting inference speeds on local RTX graphics cards by up to 150%.
- **HarmonyOS & Collaborative Agent Architectures**: 
  Operating system design is undergoing a paradigm shift towards agentic execution. In **[当操作系统开始“理解意图”：鸿蒙 AI 如何改变开发者的工作方式](https://www.infoq.cn/article/FEdxCeBCNO8DENqbGNZ0)**, architect Liu Guangzhi breaks down how HarmonyOS 7's entire core architecture is being reorganized around the HarmonyOS Mobile Agent Framework (HMAF 2.0) and system-level assistant Xiaoyi, matching user natural language intents to collaborative, cross-app sub-agents. For developers, this shift is mirrored in the DevEco Code and CLI tools, which support dual-track AI coding with plan-and-build collaborative agents. Furthermore, openJiuwen (jointly built by Huawei 2012 Lab, Huawei Cloud, etc.) has launched **[WorkSwarm](https://www.infoq.cn/article/77NthDLZNgqTgBuMKsQh)**, a multi-agent cooperative platform that enables groups of AI agents—such as lyricists, composers, and performers—to dynamically assign, hand off, and execute workflows in a shared workspace alongside human creators. To further bridge the multi-platform gap, Huawei has released the first public beta of Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP) for HarmonyOS, detailed in **[渲染内存降95%、GC卡顿率降90%：KMP 是怎么在鸿蒙上跑起来的](https://www.infoq.cn/article/M7RAkplwuMQrs72dSYfj)**; by implementing a custom Concurrent Mark-Sweep-Compact (CMC) garbage collection algorithm and "half-self-drawing" rendering pipe, KMP lets developers share Kotlin business logic natively across Android, iOS, and HarmonyOS while slashing memory overhead by 95%.
- **Software Engineering & Governance**: 
  As AI-generated code proliferates, the industry is rethinking quality control and auditability. In **[调用成功不等于判断正确：KDC 的行动治理主张](https://www.infoq.cn/article/ViFwzzK2B2ifEIgXaSTC)**, Xiao Bo from vivo proposes "Knowledge-driven Computing" (KDC), arguing that tool execution success does not guarantee correct authorized actions. KDC introduces the "Reasoning Object" as an external, auditable structure that documents decision-making contexts and enforces safety constraints before high-impact operations like refunds. Meanwhile, the developer paradigm of small pull requests is also being challenged, as discussed in **[AI 智能体改变代码评审逻辑，Rootly 废止小 PR 规则](https://www.infoq.cn/article/FzWV9Jzv2CPK8yjLPxdA)**; because AI agents write cohesive end-to-end features rather than small incremental lines, Rootly has abandoned counting lines of code in favor of assessing "blast radius" via feature flags and progressive, automated risk-based review.

## News & Commentary
- **Global AI Arms Race and Defense Clashes**: 
  Geopolitical competition in AI continues to strain corporate and state boundaries. In **[人工智能军备竞赛：中国快速进步，美国政策摇摆](https://cn.nytimes.com/usa/20260817/military-ai-china-anthropic/)**, the New York Times analyzes the intense conflict between the Pentagon and Anthropic over military usage restrictions on the "Mythos" model. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth banned Claude AI from defense systems like Project Maven after Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei insisted on a 25-page limitation policy—banning Claude from domestic surveillance or fully autonomous lethal weapons. However, the ban was immediately compromised when Anthropic released its formidable cyber exploitation model "Mythos," prompting the NSA to secure an exception for "experimental" offensive cyber operations, exposing deep administrative contradictions.
- **Data Sovereignty and Cultural Framing**: 
  Simultaneously, the battle over training data has shifted to national security. In **[具有中国特色的AI数据：中国争夺人工智能时代话语权](https://cn.nytimes.com/china/20260817/china-ai-data-chatbots/)**, the Times details how China's State Data Bureau plans to establish the country as a "data powerhouse" by 2028. Concerned that Western LLMs, trained on English-heavy datasets, propagate Western political bias (and make factual blunders like calling basketball star Yao Ming a woman), the Shanghai AI Lab has open-sourced the massive "Wanjuan" (万卷) dataset. Curated to align with "mainstream Chinese values," this multi-lingual data is being offered globally as part of Beijing's strategy to export its cultural framing and establish technological lock-in in developing nations.
- **Weaponized Visa Policies**: 
  This technology friction is increasingly spilling into physical borders. In **[干预政策、压制批评，特朗普政府将美国签证“武器化”](https://cn.nytimes.com/usa/20260817/trump-americas-visas/)**, the Times exposes how the US State Department has expanded visa restrictions to target foreign officials associated with Chinese technology firms. Most notably, Chilean telecom officials who approved a 19,000 km undersea fiber-optic cable project involving Hong Kong have had their US visas summarily revoked, while similar visa-cancellation threats have been issued against Argentine and Costa Rican lawmakers who opposed local bans on Huawei.
