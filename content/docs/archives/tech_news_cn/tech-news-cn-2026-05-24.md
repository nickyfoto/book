# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-05-24

## Top Story
The most striking report today details how London's rampant smartphone thefts are fueling a sophisticated extortion ring tied to Chinese electronics markets. Stolen iPhones are smuggled to hubs like Shenzhen, where criminal networks use psychological warfare and violent threats—including fake Apple security alerts and videos of firearms—to coerce victims into unlocking their Apple IDs, allowing the devices to be resold on the black market at a premium rather than scrapped for parts.

## Products & Digital
For macOS users seeking a streamlined workflow, the sspai community highlighted a new free and open-source screenshot utility. Introduced in [capcap：一键搞定截图、标注与图床，免费、开源的 macOS 截图工具](https://sspai.com/post/109741), `capcap` is triggered with a double-tap of the Command key, allowing users to capture, annotate, and instantly upload images to custom cloud storage solutions like Tencent Cloud COS or Alibaba Cloud OSS. Built purely with AppKit and featuring zero third-party dependencies, the lightweight tool focuses on object-oriented annotations that remain editable long after they are initially drawn.

## News & Commentary
In national news, a devastating gas explosion at the Liushenyu coal mine in Shanxi Province has claimed at least 82 lives, making it one of China's deadliest mining accidents in recent years. As reported by the New York Times Chinese edition in [山西煤矿爆炸事故已致82人死亡，仍有人下落不明](https://cn.nytimes.com/china/20260525/xi-coal-mine-explosion-rescue/), President Xi Jinping issued rapid instructions for rescue operations and accountability, a high-level intervention that often signals an anticipated worsening of the crisis before official death tolls are fully finalized. The mine, operated by Shanxi Tongzhou Coal Group, had previously been flagged in 2024 by national regulators for dangerous gas concentration levels.
