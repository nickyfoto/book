# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-04-30

## Top Story
China's National Development and Reform Commission has officially [blocked Meta's $2 billion acquisition of Chinese AI startup Manus](https://www.infoq.cn/article/OYx1rWyzd6A0ZMzkghZT), ordering the parties to immediately unwind the transaction. This unprecedented intervention marks a significant turning point in cross-border tech deals, illustrating that Beijing now views top AI talent and products as strategic national assets rather than free-market commodities,. The collapse of this deal sends a chilling signal to Chinese startups seeking Silicon Valley capital and underscores the deepening fragmentation of the global tech ecosystem,.

## Engineering & Dev
Google has officially open-sourced its [Agent Skills repository](https://www.infoq.cn/article/ihRlIjt3N5seuQN27OQe), addressing the critical challenge of context inflation in large language models by providing lightweight, on-demand markdown files that teach agents how to use Google Cloud services efficiently without overloading their context windows,. To further streamline this, Google Cloud also launched the [Agents CLI](https://www.infoq.cn/article/O9hWJ1JZYBPF9PZKkY3C), a tool offering seamless integration with agent frameworks like Gemini CLI and Claude Code to handle infrastructure provisioning and deployment locally,. On the architecture front, Slack engineers detailed their [context management solution for long-running multi-agent systems](https://www.infoq.cn/article/v51PWObFB4xuqM2lkMpg), moving away from simply accumulating chat history in favor of using structured memories, expert reviews, and timeline distillation to prevent hallucinations and context exhaustion,. 

In a fascinating display of AI capabilities, [Claude Code discovered a 23-year-old remotely exploitable vulnerability](https://www.infoq.cn/article/5RYdZtLgisqRJ65zJBpo) in the Linux kernel's NFS driver that had been hiding in plain sight since 2003,. Meanwhile, a major cultural shift is occurring in the open-source community as Mitchell Hashimoto announced that his highly popular project [Ghostty is fleeing GitHub](https://www.infoq.cn/article/mOXNlB1fzANrPZIrNfsK) after 18 years of loyalty,. Hashimoto cited chronic reliability failures with GitHub Actions and expressed frustration over the platform's shifting focus toward AI-driven code generation at the expense of human-centric developer experiences,. 

## Products & Digital
Valve has finally released the [new Steam Controller](https://www.ifanr.com/1664641) over a decade after the original, featuring upgraded TMR joysticks and refined trackpads that provide the best mouse-simulation experience on the market, though it notably drops the dual-stage triggers of its predecessor,,. In an interesting counter-trend to hyper-connectivity, the [Tin Can retro phone](https://www.ifanr.com/1664605) has become a massive hit among North American parents seeking a screen-free, strictly whitelisted communication tool for their kids, capitalizing on the growing anxiety over childhood smartphone addiction,,. 

On the mobile front, OnePlus launched the [OnePlus Ace 6 Extreme Edition](https://www.ifanr.com/1664739), a gaming-focused smartphone featuring the MediaTek Dimensity 9500 and a dedicated "God of Guns" magnetic controller attachment that essentially turns the phone into a seamless handheld console,. Additionally, Xiaomi's unreleased [CyberOne V2 humanoid robot](https://www.ifanr.com/1664575) was previewed, showing off an incredibly lifelike 1:1 robotic hand with 22-27 degrees of freedom. Most surprisingly, the robot features a micro-liquid cooling system that mimics human "sweat" to dissipate heat from its internal motors during heavy workloads.

## News & Commentary
An eye-opening research report from Anthropic challenges the narrative that AI primarily threatens low-skilled labor, revealing instead that [highly educated and higher-income professionals are the most exposed to AI replacement](https://www.infoq.cn/article/IICj9OxXvZuDj8M6qRN5). The data shows that programmers, customer service agents, and data entry clerks top the list, with youth hiring in these highly-exposed sectors already dropping by 14% since the ChatGPT boom,. Complementing this anxiety, an IFANR op-ed explores the cultural juxtaposition of the ["SBTI" meme versus the fear of being distilled into an AI "Skill"](https://www.ifanr.com/1664362),. The piece captures the modern worker's dread (FOBO — Fear Of Becoming Obsolete) of having their professional expertise and intuition compressed into a replaceable AI agent module,.
