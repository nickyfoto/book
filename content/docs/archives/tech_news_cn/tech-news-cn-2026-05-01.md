# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-05-01

However, the release of [DeepSeek V4](https://www.infoq.cn/article/K7hpIOogPsLlPQz4lwXu), running natively on Huawei's Ascend chips, offers a localized, compliant, and highly capable solution that matches closed-source flagship models.
