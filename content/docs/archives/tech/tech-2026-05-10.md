# Engineering @ Scale — 2026-05-10

## Signal of the Day
The most instructive signal today is Oracle's strategic decision to migrate enterprise-grade features into the open-source Community Edition for the MySQL 9.7 LTS release, an ecosystem move designed to retain developer trust and address community concerns over the project's long-term viability.

## Deep Dives

**MySQL 9.7 LTS: Shifting Enterprise Features to Community** · Oracle · [Source](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/05/mysql-97-lts/)
Oracle has announced the general availability of MySQL 9.7.0, which marks the beginning of a new Long-Term Support (LTS) release series and is the first major LTS update since version 8.4. The most notable aspect of this release is the transition of Enterprise features into the Community Edition. This release strategy arrives as a response to mounting community concerns regarding declining development activity and questions about Oracle's long-term commitment to the MySQL project. For engineering teams, this highlights the ongoing tension maintainers of mature data systems face: balancing the monetization of enterprise features against the risk of community attrition. 

**Framing AI as the Next Industrial Infrastructure** · NVIDIA · [Source](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-ceo-carnegie-mellon-commencement-address/)
During an address at Carnegie Mellon University, NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang framed the current AI landscape not merely as an application-layer evolution, but as the largest technology infrastructure buildout in human history. He drew direct architectural parallels between this era and previous foundational platform shifts—such as PCs, the internet, mobile, and cloud—noting that intelligence is now becoming a foundational layer for every industry. From a systems design perspective, Huang emphasized that AI is built to automate specific tasks (like reading scans) while elevating the broader purpose of the worker (patient care). Scaling this infrastructure safely requires engineers to advance capabilities and safety guardrails concurrently, ensuring the technology remains broadly accessible as it drives a new industrial era.

## Patterns Across Companies
A shared theme across today's updates is the strategic push for broader technological accessibility to stabilize or grow platforms. Oracle is pushing enterprise database capabilities down to the open-source community to reinforce its ecosystem, while NVIDIA is focused on scaling AI compute infrastructure broadly enough to close the technology divide and drive reindustrialization.
