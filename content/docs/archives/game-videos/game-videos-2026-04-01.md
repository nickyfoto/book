# Gaming Videos — 2026-04-01

## Watch First
If you only watch one thing today, check out [THE HERDCRAFT UPDATE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qpkGGA_TJ3Q). It is a brilliantly executed April Fools' style concept that completely overhauls inventory management by forcing you to literally "herd" your blocks and command your items around the world in real-time . 

## Highlights by Theme

### Trailers & Announcements
The official Minecraft channel committed hard to a hilarious seasonal joke by releasing two videos for their new mock-patch: a quick 22-second [Herdcraft Update Short](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A9WqcysntVg) and a full [1:33 announcement trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qpkGGA_TJ3Q). The update's premise entirely ditches dull traditional inventory menus in favor of a "mindful" approach where you use herd blocks to manage your materials . It is a fantastic, absurd spoof on survival crafting mechanics that is definitely worth a watch .

### Everything Else
For Chinese-speaking viewers and lore hunters, there is an excellent 18-minute deep-dive compilation titled [99.99% 的人不知道！盘点 8 大最疯狂的游戏彩蛋！](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qBR66CZqepw).
