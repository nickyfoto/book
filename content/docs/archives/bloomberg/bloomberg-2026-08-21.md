# Bloomberg — 2026-08-21

## Lead Story
US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's unexpected decision to at least double long-dated bond buybacks has sent shockwaves through global markets, driving the dollar to a three-month low and fueling a massive debasement trade that pushed Bitcoin past \$75,000 and gold toward a third weekly gain. The aggressive fiscal intervention is already drawing comparisons to Japan's yield-containment policies and raising concerns that it undermines new Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh, though the White House clarified that Bessent acted on his own authority.
