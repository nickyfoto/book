After consulting with dozens of VP-level leaders, Claire Vo unpacked the harsh realities and operational drag of organizational AI adoption. She noted that while many leaders are eager to spend tokens on new tools, they are deeply hesitant to kill outdated processes or redesign jobs, leading to costly friction. A critical takeaway from her synthesis is that a leader's lack of hard AI skills actively drags down the organization, and companies must realize that endless "alignment" meetings are actually far more expensive than the token costs of AI tools.
