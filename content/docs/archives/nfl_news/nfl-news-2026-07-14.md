# NFL News — 2026-07-14

## Read First
The NFL community is mourning the loss of Houston Texans Co-Founder and Senior Chair Janice S. McNair, who passed away at 89, prompting an outpouring of condolences from Commissioner Roger Goodell and teams across the league.
