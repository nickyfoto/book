# NFL News — 2026-06-10

## Read First
The blockbuster ripple effects of the offseason are in full swing as minicamps conclude, but no story is bigger than the seismic shift in the pass-rushing landscape following the Cleveland Browns' trade of reigning Defensive Player of the Year Myles Garrett to the Los Angeles Rams. The move drastically alters the NFC West arms race and leaves a massive void in Cleveland's defense as teams transition toward training camp.

## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
The 2026 draft class is already making waves in minicamp. In Chicago, third-round receiver Zavion Thomas is blazing past defenders with his 4.28 speed, drawing heavy praise from OC Ben Johnson. Down in Dallas, first-round defensive back Caleb Downs is taking cross-training reps at both nickel and safety, impressing defensive coordinator Christian Parker with his high football IQ. Over in Cleveland, the Browns have officially signed all 10 of their draft picks, including first-round versatile weapon **[KC Concepcion](https://www.clevelandbrowns.com/news/browns-sign-wr-kc-concepcion)**. Meanwhile, the Raiders are taking a deliberate approach with No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza, letting him learn the ropes behind veteran Kirk Cousins.

### Roster Moves & Free Agency
Significant extensions and depth signings headlined the transaction wire today. The Green Bay Packers locked down their receiving corps of the future, signing **[Christian Watson](https://www.packers.com/news/contract-extension-only-further-fuels-christian-watson-s-fire-june-10-2026)** to a long-term extension just weeks after paying Jayden Reed. The Eagles added veteran depth to the trenches, signing **[A.J. Epenesa and Michael Jordan](https://www.philadelphiaeagles.com/news/eagles-sign-aj-epenesa-michael-jordan)** while waiving Chandler Martin and Isiah King. Out west, the Raiders added **[Brandon Johnson](https://www.raiders.com/news/raiders-sign-wr-brandon-johnson-wr-corey-rucker-reserve-injured-2026-transactions)** and placed Corey Rucker on injured reserve, while the 49ers bolstered their backfield by signing **[Sincere McCormick](https://www.49ers.com/news/49ers-sign-rb-sincere-mccormick-to-one-year-deal-waive-rb-jordan-mims)** and waiving Jordan Mims.

### Team Beats
Quarterback battles and major coaching shifts dominated practice fields across the league. In New England, HC Mike Vrabel is watching rookie Drake Maye build rapid chemistry with newly-acquired star A.J. Brown. Cleveland is hosting an open QB competition between Deshaun Watson and Shedeur Sanders under new HC Todd Monken. The 49ers are integrating Mike Evans into their offense, while George Kittle praises the rookie class's maturity. In Seattle, Jaxon Smith-Njigba is reflecting on his Super Bowl LX ring, AP Offensive Player of the Year honors, and his record-breaking contract as the team navigates Brian Fleury's new offensive install. And in Indianapolis, Daniel Jones is remarkably on track for training camp just six months after a devastating Achilles tear.

### League News & Culture
Historic franchises are honoring their legends, as the 49ers announced four-time Super Bowl champion LB **[Keena Turner](https://www.49ers.com/news/keena-turner-to-be-inducted-into-the-edward-j-debartolo-sr-san-francisco-49ers-hall-of-fame)** will be the 34th inductee into the Edward J. DeBartolo Sr. Hall of Fame. In Cleveland, the end of an era is official with the retirement of **[Joel Bitonio](https://www.clevelandbrowns.com/news/joel-bitonio-personified-standard-of-consistency-and-set-precedent-for-new-group-of-browns-offensive-linemen-the-huddle)**, who hangs up his cleats after 12 seasons and an incredible 6,481 consecutive snaps.

## Storylines to Watch
The dramatic reshuffling of head coaching seats and elite talent will define the 2026 season. From Joe Brady taking over in Buffalo and John Harbaugh shifting to the Giants, to massive veteran acquisitions like A.J. Brown in New England and Mike Evans in San Francisco, the league's hierarchy is being rewritten. Keep a close eye on the quarterback battles in Cleveland between Shedeur Sanders and Deshaun Watson, and in Arizona between Gardner Minshew, Jacoby Brissett, and Carson Beck, as training camps approach.
