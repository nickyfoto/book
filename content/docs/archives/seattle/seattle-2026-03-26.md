**[33 Face Charges Over $1M UW Protest Damage]** · [KOMO News](http://twitter.macworks.dev/komonews/status/2037245291011662153#m)

Governor Bob Ferguson has signed a new law regulating artificial intelligence chatbots, making Washington the first state to enact such safety measures. The legislation requires built-in safeguards for minors, including mandatory flags for signs of self-harm, addressing growing warnings from families and officials regarding AI risks.
