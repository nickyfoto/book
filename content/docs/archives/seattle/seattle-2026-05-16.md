# Seattle Local — 2026-05-16

## Top Story
19-year-old University of Washington student Juniper Blessing was fatally stabbed at an off-campus student housing complex on Sunday. ## Local News

**[New Public Bathrooms Unveiled Ahead of World Cup]** · [KOMO News](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Izuu7NcVewg)
Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson and community leaders officially opened four new free public bathrooms in the Pioneer Square and SoDo neighborhoods on Friday. The installations are part of a pilot program with private company Throne Labs aimed at addressing downtown sanitation issues and making the stadium district more welcoming for the influx of visitors expected for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

**[Monroe Prison Escapee Captured at Transit Center]** · [KING 5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bph2Ku0kOEk)
Authorities have apprehended 28-year-old Samuel Evans Kimble after he escaped from the minimum security unit at the Monroe Correctional Complex late Thursday night. Officials reported that Kimble's absence went unnoticed for roughly 12 hours before he was taken into custody without incident at the Lynnwood Transit Center.

**[Auburn Police Officer Arrested in Child Exploitation Sting]** · [KIRO 7](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kzee-62NPEw)
An Auburn Police Department officer was arrested Friday morning at the department's headquarters on allegations of felony immoral communication with a minor. Detectives from the Pierce County Internet Crimes Against Children task force took the officer into custody after he allegedly sent inappropriate messages to undercover investigators he believed were underage.

**[Health Officials Monitor Hantavirus Exposures]** · [KOMO News](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=18TpOhmvLBw)
Public Health officials are tracking a fourth King County resident for possible exposure to the Andes hantavirus after they shared a flight from Johannesburg to Amsterdam with an ill passenger connected to the MV Hondius cruise ship outbreak. Separately, health authorities confirmed an isolated case of the rare Sin Nombre hantavirus in Chelan County, which has no connection to the cruise ship and is instead linked to home mouse exposure.

**[Aging Infrastructure Causes Frequent Queen Anne Power Outages]** · [KOMO News](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sr4j_6gPCVY)
Seattle City Light attributes a recent surge in power outages in the Queen Anne neighborhood to aging underground electrical infrastructure. Frustrated southwest Queen Anne residents report experiencing multiple outages in a single week—some lasting up to 12 hours—prompting District 7 Councilman Bob Kettle to closely monitor the ongoing issue.
