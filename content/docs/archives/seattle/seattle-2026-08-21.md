**Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson’s Favorability Plummets to 37% Amid Public Safety Concerns** · [FOX 13](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/seattle-mayor-favorability-drops-new-poll)
Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson’s favorability rating has dropped 20 percentage points since April, falling to 37% in a new August survey commissioned by the Seattle Metro Chamber. **Seattle Seahawks \$9.6 Billion Sale Clears Key NFL Committee Vote** · [FOX 13](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/seahawks-sale-clears-committee-vote)
The proposed \$9.612 billion sale of the Seattle Seahawks to the Khosla family has cleared a major hurdle after receiving unanimous approval from key NFL committees. The league's finance and ownership policy committees have recommended that all 32 team owners approve the purchase at an upcoming special meeting in Atlanta next Wednesday. To finalize the transfer of ownership from the Paul Allen estate, a minimum of 24 owners must vote "yes" to approve the deal.

**Starbucks Details Layoffs of Over 200 Corporate Employees at SoDo Headquarters** · [FOX 13](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/starbucks-announces-details-layoffs-200-plus-seattle-headquarters-employees)

The cuts primarily impact store designers and IT personnel who declined to relocate to the company's Nashville office ahead of its opening next year. This round of layoffs highlights a tightening local job market as King County's unemployment rate sits at approximately 4.9%.
