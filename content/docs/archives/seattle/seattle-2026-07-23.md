# Seattle Local — 2026-07-23

## Top Story
Seattle voters will head to the polls this November to decide on a proposed 0.3% sales tax increase to fund local transit expansion. If passed, the measure would raise the city's combined sales tax rate to 10.85%—amounting to 30 cents per $100 spent—to maintain and expand bus and transit services that residents rely on daily.

**[Semi-Truck Crash Spills Corn Across I-5 Ramp](https://www.kiro7.com/news/local/i-5-traffic-jblm-diverted-after-semi-truck-crashes-spilling-corn-across-road/6ORA522QEVA2NFGQU4ONMKTU2M/#Echobox=1784810343-2)** · KIRO 7
A southbound I-5 exit ramp near Joint Base Lewis-McChord was blocked Thursday morning after a semi-truck crashed into a ditch, spilling a massive load of corn on the cob across the roadway. Washington State Patrol troopers cited the operator for second-degree negligent driving, confirming that drowsy driving was the cause of the collision. Fortunately, no major injuries were reported, though morning commuters faced significant detours during the cleanup.

**[Seattle Fire Department Proposes Drone Program](https://komonews.com/news/local/seattle-residents-weigh-in-on-proposed-drone-program-for-fire-department-sfd-drones-technology-emergency-response-aerial-tech-city-council-approval-boston)** · KOMO News
Seattle Fire Chief Harold Scoggins is seeking public feedback on a proposal to add three drones to the department's emergency response toolkit. This aerial technology would assist crews by providing live video feeds during structure fires, water rescues, and natural disasters, with potential deployment as early as 2027 if approved. Officials are currently hosting community meetings to address privacy concerns and fulfill requirements under the city's Surveillance Technology Ordinance.

**[Universal Free School Meals Plan Delayed](https://www.seattletimes.com/education-lab/free-meals-at-all-seattle-schools-likely-wont-start-next-school-year/#Echobox=1784770079-2)** · The Seattle Times
Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson’s ambitious plan to provide free school meals to all public school students has been delayed by at least a full school year following a City Council committee vote. Due to ongoing funding uncertainties, city leaders instead advanced a scaled-back meal voucher program. The delay disrupts hopes of rolling out universal meal access for families this coming fall.

**[Wildfires and Industrial Blazes Challenge Fire Crews](https://komonews.com/news/local/fire-breaks-out-at-manufacturing-plant-in-arlington-residents-asked-to-not-use-water-stella-jones-plant-rust-sediment-in-pipes)** · KOMO News
Firefighters remain heavily tasked across the region, with 50 personnel successfully containing a two-alarm fire at the Stella-Jones wood manufacturing plant in Arlington early Wednesday. Meanwhile, Eastern Washington continues to battle severe wildfires, including the 87,000-acre Kaiser Canyon Fire and the Modrite Fire, which recently swept through a Ferry County animal sanctuary. In response to the extreme heat and dry conditions, Jefferson County has elevated its fire danger level to high and prohibited all wood burning and open flames.
