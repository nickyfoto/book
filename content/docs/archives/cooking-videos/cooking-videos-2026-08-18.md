### Food Science & Tips
Chef Chen Zongming shares a brilliant kitchen tip to fix highly acidic, store-bought cooking wine by pre-soaking it with ginger, green onions, and Sichuan peppercorns to neutralize its sourness and better eliminate meat odors. He also explains the food science behind meat selection, emphasizing that chicken thigh is far superior to chicken breast, which easily becomes dry and woody when stir-fried. Additionally, in the instant noodle ranking, host James shares a clever hack of placing the solid fat pack near the stove or on a steaming lid to melt it completely before mixing.
