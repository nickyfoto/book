### Recipes & Tutorials
The channel 老饭骨 delivers a phenomenal masterclass in their [Cumin Lamb](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gs-3EIYsG0w) video, inviting fans into the kitchen to learn the crucial wok technique of "dry-browning" (乾香) the meat to render the fat and achieve an intensely fragrant texture, rather than just focusing on simple tenderness. The secret lies in gently squeezing the shredded radish to remove exactly 80% of the salt and moisture before mixing it with a savory egg batter, Sichuan peppercorn powder, and pan-frying it to a crispy golden yellow. 

### Food Science & Tips
