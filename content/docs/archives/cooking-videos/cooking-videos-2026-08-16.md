### Food Science & Tips
In the realm of food science, **尚食厨房 ShangshiKitchen** highlights that unrefined traditional sesame oil has a very low smoke point and easily develops a bitter taste, making a 50/50 blend with cooking oil and low-heat frying essential for aromatic results. Over on **鲁菜老厨陈宗明**, Chef Chen reveals that eggplants must be fried twice; the first fry forms a protective starch shell, and the second high-heat fry at 200°C evaporates surface water to lock in a dry, crispy crust. Additionally, soaking the eggplant pieces in salted water before cooking prevents rapid oxidation and helps the starch coating adhere tightly.
