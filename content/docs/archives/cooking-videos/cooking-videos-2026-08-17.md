### Food Science & Tips
*   **The Fried Rice Formula**: **老饭骨** reveals that you should always use **freshly steamed rice of the day** rather than cold leftover rice to ensure the ultimate texture, and steaming the rice with a few drops of oil keeps the grains glossy and quick to separate. Coating the raw rice grains directly with raw egg yolks prior to frying ensures they absorb the egg completely, sealing in moisture and creating a uniform golden coat.
*   **Aromatics & Textures**: Chef Meng points out that carrots must be blanched beforehand so they are tender rather than hard and raw in the finished rice, and dried shiitakes must be boiled or steamed to awaken their naturally fermented umami. He also utilizes a **double-scallion technique**—frying scallions first to infuse the oil with deep, caramelized flavor, and adding a second handful at the very end for a fresh, aromatic lift. 
*   **Shrimp Shell Magic**: **老东北美食** explains that trimming the shrimp's eyes and spear and removing the sandbag releases a beautiful ring of rich, red shrimp oil into the pan, which then fully flavors and tenderizes the cabbage.
*   **Dough & Fermentation**: When prepping the dough for steamed buns, adding a pinch of white sugar alongside yeast stimulates the fermentation process, ensuring a light, fluffy rise.
