# Apple's 50th Anniversary Finale, iOS 27 Siri Multitasking, and AirPods Max 2 Reviews — 2026-03-31

## Highlights
Today's news is heavily anchored by Apple's monumental 50th-anniversary celebration, capped off by an exclusive Paul McCartney concert at Apple Park and commemorative gifts for employees. In the software realm, leaks point to massive Apple Intelligence upgrades in iOS 27, including a significantly smarter Siri capable of handling multi-step queries and a completely revamped Shortcuts experience. Meanwhile, the hardware front sees the launch and mixed reviews of the AirPods Max 2, which bring much-needed H2 chip features but leave the polarizing original design untouched.

## Top Stories
- **[Watch Tim Cook ring the Nasdaq Opening Bell as part of Apple’s 50th anniversary celebrations](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/31/watch-tim-cook-ring-the-nasdaq-opening-bell-as-part-of-apples-50th-anniversary-celebrations/)**: Apple marks its half-century milestone with CEO Tim Cook remotely ringing the Nasdaq Opening Bell from Apple Park, celebrating "50 Years of Thinking Different". Employees are receiving limited-edition vintage-inspired schwag, and the festivities culminate in an exclusive Paul McCartney concert at the Apple Park campus tonight.
- **[Apple is working to make the next-gen Siri handle multiple commands at once](https://www.macworld.com/article/3103586/apple-is-working-to-make-the-next-gen-siri-handle-multiple-commands-at-once.html)**: Bloomberg reports that iOS 27 will overhaul Siri using Google's Gemini technology, finally allowing the assistant to process multiple requests within a single prompt. Additionally, backend code reveals that the Shortcuts app will use Apple Intelligence to let users generate unique, custom actions strictly through natural language voice commands. 
- **[AirPods Max 2 review: High-end adds modern features at last](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/31/airpods-max-2-review/)**: The new $549 AirPods Max 2 feature the H2 chip, delivering 1.5x better Active Noise Cancellation, Adaptive Audio, and USB-C connectivity. While audio quality and ANC have noticeably improved, reviewers lament that the heavy 385-gram design and much-maligned Smart Case remain completely unchanged from the 2020 original. 
- **[Apple Sets Privacy Rules for Third-Party Access to Live Activities and Notifications](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/31/apple-sets-privacy-rules-live-activities-alerts/)**: To comply with the EU's Digital Markets Act, Apple is opening Live Activities and notification forwarding to third-party accessories in iOS 26.5. However, the updated Developer Program License Agreement strictly prohibits third parties from using this forwarded data for advertising, profiling, or cloud storage, ensuring decryption happens only on the target device. 
- **[Ninth Circuit unanimously denies Apple’s rehearing requests in Epic Games case](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/30/ninth-circuit-unanimously-denies-apples-rehearing-requests-in-epic-games-case/)**: The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit has rejected Apple's petitions for both a three-judge panel and an *en banc* rehearing regarding the Epic Games antitrust ruling. This setback leaves Apple with limited legal options regarding the mandate that forces the company to allow developers to link out to alternative payment methods. 
- **[App Store expands support to 11 new languages](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=97t4mt64)**: Apple has expanded App Store Connect to support localized app metadata in 11 new languages, prominently focusing on the Indian market with additions like Bangla, Gujarati, Tamil, and Telugu. This brings the total number of supported localizations to 50, allowing developers to better tailor their app names, descriptions, and screenshots to diverse global audiences.
