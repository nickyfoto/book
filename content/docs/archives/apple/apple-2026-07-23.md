**[macOS 27 Beta Hides Trippy Siri 'Voice Pad’ Interface With 13 Voices](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/23/macos-27-beta-hidden-siri-voice-ui/)**
Apple is experimenting with a radical, immersive new interface for selecting Siri AI voices in the latest macOS 27 Golden Gate beta. The hidden "Voice Pad" replaces standard system settings with a dark canvas featuring 13 voice slots arranged in a constellation-like grid. Moving the cursor utilizes spatial audio to dynamically adjust volume, reverb, and filtering as you preview various vocal characteristics. If fully implemented, it points toward a highly interactive and localized onboarding experience for Apple's upcoming LLM-powered assistant.

**[ChatGPT’s Apple Health Integration Now Rolling Out to U.S. Users](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/23/chatgpt-apple-health-integration/)**
OpenAI is expanding the rollout of its ChatGPT Health feature to U.S. users, allowing the AI to securely connect with Apple Health and supported medical records. When granted permission, the chatbot can reference your medications, lab results, and workout routines to provide highly contextualized responses—such as modifying restaurant suggestions based on your specific dietary restrictions. OpenAI notes that user health data will remain private and will not be used to train AI models. However, users should remain highly cautious about medical AI, as highlighted by an ongoing lawsuit involving ChatGPT hallucinating medical advice and advising a patient to ignore a deadly pulmonary embolism.

**[MacBook Neo 2: Here’s everything we know so far](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/22/macbook-neo-2-features-release-date/)**
Following the immense success of the first-generation MacBook Neo, Apple is reportedly developing a successor tentatively targeted for a 2027 release. The next iteration is rumored to utilize a binned A19 Pro chip featuring a 5-core GPU, which boasts built-in Neural Accelerators for faster compute tasks. Crucially, the base memory configuration is expected to bump up from 8GB to 12GB of RAM, enabling support for Apple’s advanced on-device AI model for enhanced dictation and Siri personalization.
