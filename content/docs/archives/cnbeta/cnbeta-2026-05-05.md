# CNBeta — 2026-05-05

## Top Story
The ongoing legal battle between Elon Musk and OpenAI has dropped several bombshells, exposing deep conflicts within the AI giant's transition to a for-profit model. According to a **[cnbeta report](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561388.htm)**, OpenAI President Greg Brockman admitted in court that he holds a $30 billion equity stake in the for-profit entity despite investing zero dollars. Furthermore, testimonies revealed massive conflicts of interest, including OpenAI's $20 billion chip order with Cerebras, a startup in which Brockman and CEO Sam Altman hold personal stakes. This trial is pivotal as it could force OpenAI to open-source its technology or dramatically restructure its massive valuation.

## Tech & AI
The era of completely free AI tools in China is ending. A **[ByteDance Doubao subscription report](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561330.htm)** notes that the domestic AI leader has launched a tiered paid service reaching up to 5,088 RMB annually for complex tasks like data analysis and PPT generation, maintaining basic functions for free. Globally, OpenAI continues its rapid rollout by launching **[GPT-5.5 Instant](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561428.htm)** as the new default ChatGPT model, boasting massive gains in math and multimodal reasoning alongside improved memory management. In the hardware space, OpenAI is reportedly accelerating its timeline for an **[AI phone developed with MediaTek](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561404.htm)**, targeting a 2027 release to boost its IPO narrative. The AI hardware boom is also shaking up executive suites, as **[Qualcomm's second-in-command Alex Katouzian](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561390.htm)** jumps to Intel to lead its Client Computing and Physical AI group. Meanwhile, **[AMD's Q1 earnings](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561452.htm)** impressed the market with a 57% surge in data center revenue, cementing its role as a key alternative to Nvidia for hyperscalers.

## Consumer & Devices
Apple's iPhone 17 standard edition was the **[best-selling smartphone globally in Q1 2026](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561350.htm)**, capturing 6% of the market. Because the standard iPhone 18 is reportedly delayed until Spring 2027, the **[iPhone 17 production cycle](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561376.htm)** is being extended, making it one of Apple's longest-living base models. However, the premium tier is facing build quality complaints, with users reporting severe **[paint peeling on the iPhone 17 Pro's aluminum frame](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561384.htm)**, a material choice Apple is still expected to keep for the iPhone 18 Pro. On the Mac side, the global AI-driven memory chip shortage has forced Apple to **[cut RAM upgrade options for the Mac Studio and Mac mini](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561438.htm)**, restricting supply options to streamline production. In a more open software move, the upcoming **[iOS 27](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561448.htm)** will reportedly allow users to set third-party AI models from Google and Anthropic as their default Siri backend, moving away from strict ChatGPT exclusivity.

## Gaming
*Forza Horizon 6* has **[officially gone gold](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1561342.htm)**, with developers revealing a massive soundtrack across 9 radio stations and new accessibility features like ASL/BSL picture-in-picture cutscenes. In a long-awaited generational shift, the *Call of Duty* franchise is **[abandoning PS4 and Xbox One](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1561346.htm)** after a 13-year run, making the upcoming *Modern Warfare 4* a current-gen exclusive. Meanwhile, Microsoft's new Xbox leadership is pivoting away from AI gimmicks, officially **[canceling the Xbox Copilot AI assistant project](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1561450.htm)** for both console and mobile interfaces.

## Science & Space
NASA's Curiosity rover has discovered **[diverse complex organic molecules in Mars' Gale Crater](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1561354.htm)**, including nitrogen-bearing compounds resembling DNA building blocks, proving that the Martian crust can preserve organics for up to 3.5 billion years. Closer to home, astronomers have accurately mapped the **[Milky Way's "hidden edge"](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1561358.htm)** for the first time by tracking the U-shaped age distribution of stars, revealing exactly where the galaxy's star-forming disk abruptly ends.

埃隆·马斯克与 OpenAI 之间正在进行的法律战爆出多项猛料，揭露了这家 AI 巨头向营利性模式转型过程中的深层利益冲突。根据**[cnbeta的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561388.htm)**，OpenAI 总裁 Greg Brockman 在法庭上承认，他“零成本”持有了该营利性实体估值达 300 亿美元的股份。此外，证词还揭示了巨大的利益冲突：OpenAI 曾向芯片初创公司 Cerebras 下达高达 200 亿美元的订单，而 Brockman 和 CEO Sam Altman 均持有该公司的个人股份。这场世纪审判至关重要，其结果可能迫使 OpenAI 开源其技术或重组其庞大的估值体系。

## 科技与人工智能
