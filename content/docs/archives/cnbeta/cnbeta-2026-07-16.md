# CNBeta — 2026-07-16

## Top Story
A [recent report](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569192.htm) highlights escalating US-China tech tensions as the US Congress moves to ban Apple from purchasing memory chips from Chinese suppliers like CXMT and YMTC due to national security concerns. This legislative push aims to prevent American companies from relying on firms allegedly linked to the Chinese military, potentially forcing Apple to abandon cost-effective memory alternatives amid rising global DRAM prices.

## Tech & AI
[Google has delayed the release of Gemini 3.5 Pro](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569170.htm) to improve its coding capabilities after facing fierce competition from OpenAI and Anthropic. In the meantime, the company is integrating its AI deeper into its ecosystem, officially [renaming NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569186.htm) and adding AI avatars to [Google Vids via the Gemini Omni model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569204.htm). Behind the scenes, an [accidental email misfire by an Apple lawyer](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569178.htm) reportedly derailed out-of-court settlement talks between Apple and OpenAI over hardware talent poaching, leading to a public lawsuit. Meanwhile, former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati's new startup has [launched Inkling](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569142.htm), a new AI model that interestingly draws architectural inspiration from China's DeepSeek-V3. 

Over in the foundry business, [TSMC's CEO C.C. Wei remarked](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569222.htm) that choosing a chip manufacturer is "not like buying milk at a 7-Eleven," emphasizing the long-term commitments required as [demand for TSMC's 2nm process surges](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569206.htm) with tape-outs quadrupling compared to the 3nm node. Japan is also making hardware moves, planning to [buy 27,500 Nvidia Rubin chips](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569166.htm) to develop a domestic robotics AI model and reduce reliance on foreign systems.

## Consumer & Devices
OPPO is refining its global portfolio, [focusing its realme brand on overseas markets while positioning OnePlus as its primary domestic driver](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569122.htm) within China. For Apple, the [base model iPhone 18 is reportedly facing delays](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569218.htm) as the company prioritizes strong demand for the Pro models, which may feature new [vapor chamber cooling technology](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569154.htm) currently entering mass production. 

In the auto sector, EV penetration is growing but [cost and affordability remain major hurdles](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569172.htm) for global consumers. The pressure is showing in the retail space, with [multiple IM Motors dealerships abruptly collapsing](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569124.htm) across China, leaving customers who paid in full stranded without their vehicles. On the hardware front, a new [open-source, DRM-free "Open Printer" project](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569208.htm) powered by a Raspberry Pi is making waves as a direct challenge to the predatory cartridge practices of major printer brands.

## Gaming
*GTA 6* hype is reaching fever pitch, with [pre-orders reportedly breaking historical records](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1569182.htm) and analysts predicting an astonishing 51 million copies sold in its first week. Meanwhile, concerns are growing that if the [PlayStation 6 launches without a disc drive](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1569148.htm), gamers in over 120 countries without official PSN access could become isolated "digital islands". Roblox has also expanded creator tools by introducing ["Build", a new generative AI feature](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1569174.htm) that allows mobile users to generate playable game prototypes simply by typing text prompts.
