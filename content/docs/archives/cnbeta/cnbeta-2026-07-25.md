# CNBeta — 2026-07-25

## Top Story
According to a [cnbeta report](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570266.htm), China's State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) has slapped a massive 5.179 billion yuan ($720 million) fine on online travel giant Ctrip for monopolistic practices. An [Economic Daily analysis](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570268.htm) highlighted that Ctrip used "soft" traffic throttling and algorithmic price-adjusting tools to enforce exclusivity and "lowest price" mandates, making this a landmark ruling against algorithm-driven platform monopolies in the Chinese tech sector. Ctrip has since announced 19 rectification measures, including dropping its controversial lowest-price mandate entirely.

## Tech & AI
Facing US export controls on High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) equipment, [Chinese memory makers like CXMT are pivoting to custom 3D DRAM](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570238.htm) to bypass sanctions and maintain competitiveness in the AI hardware market. In a related geopolitical battle, [Apple CEO Tim Cook is reportedly lobbying the Trump administration](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570298.htm) to allow the use of memory chips from China's CXMT and YMTC to lower consumer costs, though he faces fierce opposition from Micron. On the software side, the [APEC 21 members signed the "Chengdu Declaration"](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570242.htm), elevating open-source AI cooperation to the ministerial level and creating policy breathing room for Chinese models like DeepSeek and GLM 5.2. Amid calls from US politicians to restrict access to Chinese AI, [Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang published his first-ever post on X](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570290.htm) advocating for the global necessity of open-source models to enhance cybersecurity and innovation. Meanwhile, [US and UK AI Safety Institutes evaluated Moonshot's Kimi K3](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570244.htm), finding that while it can autonomously attack small enterprise systems, it still lacks top-tier arbitrary code execution capabilities.

## Consumer & Devices
Rising memory chip costs are hitting smartphone price tags hard; [Qualcomm's upcoming Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570240.htm) is set for a double-digit percentage price increase, and [Google's Pixel 11 series will see a $100 price hike](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570304.htm) globally. In the EV sector, a [controversial anti-smuggling measure by Chinese automakers like Zeekr](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570264.htm) is geoblocking infotainment features and locking gloveboxes when cars are driven outside China, severely frustrating legitimate cross-border travelers. Elsewhere, tech brand [Nothing is scaling back global operations](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570218.htm) by exiting over 12 markets and laying off roughly 40% of its workforce, including a 50% cut to its R&D team in China. Finally, [Apple and Samsung have reportedly collaborated to eliminate the screen crease](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570288.htm) for the highly anticipated iPhone Ultra foldable launching this September.

## Gaming
Despite industry pushes towards a digital-only future, physical media remains incredibly popular: the [PS5 Digital Edition accounts for only 27% of US console sales](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1570296.htm) since launch. In community drama, a [World of Warcraft Game Master was fired](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1570286.htm) by Blizzard after players caught them using developer-exclusive "Touch of Death" spells to help friends cheat in high-level dungeons. Lastly, rumors suggest the upcoming [Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake for the Switch 2](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1570300.htm) will receive a themed Pro Controller featuring a green-and-gold Triforce design rather than a full limited-edition console.

## Science & Space
SpaceX continues its aggressive testing cadence with [Starship Flight 13 successfully deploying 20 Starlink V3 satellites](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1570232.htm) and completing a soft splashdown of the upper stage, though the Super Heavy booster exploded during its landing attempt. On the sustainability front, a [new alkaline thermal treatment process developed by UCLA and Ewha Womans University](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1570228.htm) can convert unsorted mixed plastics directly into high-purity hydrogen fuel. Deep space exploration also marked a milestone as [astronomers detected a helium atmosphere on the habitable-zone exoplanet LHS 1140 b](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1570314.htm) using ground-based telescopes.
