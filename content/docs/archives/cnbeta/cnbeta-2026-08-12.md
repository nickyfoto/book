# CNBeta — August 12, 2026

## Top Story
According to a **[cnbeta report on Manus's split from Meta](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572840.htm)**, the generic AI Agent developer Butterfly Effect (Manus) has officially announced its return to independence after China's National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC) banned Meta's acquisition on security grounds. This unprecedented regulatory intervention forces the Singapore-based startup—whose annual recurring revenue (ARR) skyrocketed to \$400–\$500 million in July—to delete Meta-era user data, while local tech giants like Tencent and Sequoia China are reportedly negotiating a buyback at a \$2 billion valuation. The collapse of this multi-billion dollar deal marks a major turning point in cross-border AI mergers and acquisitions, reflecting rising geopolitical and antitrust scrutiny.

## Tech & AI
To comply with local regulations, a **[cnbeta report on iOS 27 code](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572822.htm)** reveals that Apple is integrating Alibaba's Tongyi Qianwen model as the core underlying LLM for Apple Intelligence in China, providing text comprehension, image recognition, and multimodal capabilities on compatible devices. Navigating these complex geopolitical waters, Apple is also adjusting its leadership; as detailed in a **[cnbeta report on Gatten's appointment](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572942.htm)**, the company hired former American Airlines lobbyist Nate Gatten as its new VP of Government Affairs, a crucial move as John Ternus prepares to take the helm as CEO.

Meanwhile, Chinese AI firms are triggering global price wars; according to a **[cnbeta report on DeepSeek-V4-Pro](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572966.htm)**, DeepSeek launched its 1.6-trillion-parameter V4-Pro-0813 model with a cost of just \$0.87 per million output tokens, outperforming Anthropic’s Claude 3 Opus 4.8 at 1/57th of the price. This intense competition has rattled US rivals, with an **[insider report on Google DeepMind's restructuring](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572972.htm)** indicating that co-founder Sergey Brin has stepped in to push for rapid progress, leading to Demis Hassabis stepping down as CEO and Koray Kavukcuoglu taking complete control to prioritize commercialization. Concurrently, conversational AI is moving into monetization; a **[cnbeta report on Doubao's commissions](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572836.htm)** discloses that ByteDance's AI assistant (with over 380 million monthly active users) has started charging a 12% commission on hotel bookings completed through its interface, shifting from a free tool into an OTA-style transaction channel.

In infrastructure news, a **[cnbeta report on SpaceX's AI satellite constellation](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572824.htm)** discusses plans to launch a 1-million-satellite "Starmind" network for off-grid space-based AI computing, though scientists warn that re-entering satellites will cause massive "artificial metal rain" and ozone damage. During a **[SpaceX all-hands speech](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572872.htm)**, Elon Musk predicted that space-based AI inference will eventually account for 99% of the company's valuation, and announced that all future Tesla models, including the Cybercab, will integrate physical Starlink hardware to guarantee network reliability. Despite geopolitical frictions, a **[cnbeta report on the US MATCH Act](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572876.htm)** reveals that US lawmakers quietly removed the export ban on advanced cryogenic etching equipment. This comes as Changxin Storage (CXMT) secures a 7.6% global DRAM market share, and a **[cnbeta report on YMTC](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572900.htm)** highlights Yangtze Memory's rise to the world’s third-largest NAND flash supplier with a 14% global capacity market share.

## Consumer & Devices
The mobile phone market is witnessing bold design transformations. According to a **[cnbeta report on Honor's Robot Phone](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572936.htm)**, Honor has launched a 9,999 RMB (\$1,385) device that integrates a pop-up 4D titanium mechanical gimbal. Developed in partnership with cinema giant ARRI and powered by a custom Yuguang H1 AISP chip, it provides cinematic-grade stabilization and intelligent target tracking.

In the automotive sector, tactile buttons are returning; a **[cnbeta report on Audi's physical buttons](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572852.htm)** highlights how carmakers like Audi, BYD, and Geely are rolling back "all-touchscreen" layouts due to driver safety concerns and high accident risks from touch misclicks. BYD is also conquering the Japanese market; a **[cnbeta report on BYD's K-Car](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/movie/1572854.htm)** reveals that its new Japan-exclusive electric K-Car, the RACCO, priced at 2.14 million JPY (\$14,800), has won over local critics with its solid cornering and premium features, securing over 1,000 orders in its first week.

For computer builders, a severe memory crisis is causing market distortion; a **[cnbeta report on Windows 11 price hikes](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572906.htm)** explains that high AI server demand for HBM is starving consumer RAM supplies, raising PC manufacturing costs alongside Microsoft's 7% to 10% hike in OEM license fees. This budget crunch has caused a "reverse evolution" where, as outlined in a **[cnbeta report on CPU sales](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572916.htm)**, four-year-old DDR4-compatible budget chips like the Ryzen 5 5500 are outselling modern DDR5 flagships. On China's streets, a ruthless business war is taking place; a **[cnbeta report on "copycat yellow" power banks](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572820.htm)** details how generic, cheaper power bank brands are painting their machines in Meituan’s signature yellow to fool consumers into returning chargers to the wrong docking stations, trapping users in high "buyout" fees while hijacking premium restaurant foot traffic.

In AI gaming projects, miHoYo is scaling back its companion experiments; a **[cnbeta report on miHoYo's Olivia Lin shutdown](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1572816.htm)** reveals its digital-human AI desktop companion was shut down after just 28 days of early access on Steam, with resources reallocated to other projects. In biotech and ecology, a **[cnbeta report on pork-infused lettuce](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1572832.htm)** explains how scientists have genetically engineered lettuce to produce pig myoglobin to help address nutritional iron deficiencies. Conversely, a **[cnbeta article on a trapped crab](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1572812.htm)** warns of a silent ecological threat where ocean plastic waste acts as a "pseudo-habitat"; researchers found a crab that slipped into a HDPE barrel as a tiny larva and grew too large to escape its 24mm opening, surviving trapped for two months by eating algae and passing juvenile fish.
