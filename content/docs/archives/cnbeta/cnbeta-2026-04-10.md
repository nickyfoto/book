# CNBeta — 2026-04-10

## Top Story
**[DeepSeek V4 to Launch in Late April with Native Chinese AI Chip Support](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557310.htm)**
DeepSeek's upcoming V4 model is expected to arrive in late April, boasting trillion-parameter scale and million-level context windows. Crucially, V4 marks a major milestone in China's "de-CUDA-ization" by achieving deep adaptation with domestic hardware like Huawei's Ascend chips. Chinese tech giants including Alibaba, ByteDance, and Tencent have reportedly pre-ordered hundreds of thousands of new AI chips in anticipation, driving up local AI chip prices by 20%.

## Tech & AI
In a major shift for the open-source software ecosystem, a **[CNBeta report confirms Red Hat is laying off its entire China R&D team](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557416.htm)** of roughly 419 employees. The move relocates engineering tasks to other APAC hubs and underscores a broader trend of US enterprise tech giants retreating from China, creating an opening for local players like Huawei's openEuler, which now holds over 57% of new installations in China.

The AI compute arms race is heating up, as an **[OpenAI investor memo bashes Anthropic's compute constraints](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557318.htm)**, claiming OpenAI will reach 30 gigawatts of compute by 2030 compared to Anthropic's projected 7-8 gigawatts by 2027. This infrastructure expansion is driving massive shifts in the industry, evidenced by the departure of key OpenAI infrastructure executives who originally helped design the "Stargate" data center initiative before the company pivoted to a partnership model. Meanwhile, **[xAI is suing Colorado](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557328.htm)** over the state's landmark AI anti-discrimination law, arguing it violates the First Amendment by forcing developers to adopt state-mandated viewpoints. Further highlighting the societal impact of AI, a new study reveals that **[AI-generated content has officially surpassed human-original content online](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557398.htm)** since the launch of ChatGPT. Also of note, a **[20-year-old man was arrested](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557448.htm)** in San Francisco for throwing a Molotov cocktail at Sam Altman's residence.

## Consumer & Devices
Apple's long-rumored foldable is moving forward, with reports indicating the **[iPhone Fold is in smooth trial production and on track for a September release](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557364.htm)**. The device features a 7.76-inch inner display with an under-screen camera and uses a liquid metal hinge to minimize creases. In the semiconductor space, TSMC's advanced nodes are set to push next-gen mobile SoCs like the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen6 Pro to **[break the 5GHz clock speed barrier for the first time](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557356.htm)**. In stark contrast, Huawei's latest Kirin 9030 remains capped below 3GHz due to US sanctions restricting its access to advanced EUV lithography. Also in hardware, **[Intel's market cap hit a 25-year high](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557358.htm)** thanks to its partnership with Elon Musk's TERAFAB project, which aims to build the world's largest 2nm chip factory in Texas. Finally, Chinese EV maker Seres has successfully patented an **[in-car toilet that hides under passenger seats](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557400.htm)**, offering a quirky solution for emergency roadside needs.

## Gaming
The cost of next-generation consoles may be astronomical, with leaks suggesting the **[Sony PS6 could be priced as high as $1,000](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1557438.htm)**. Soaring RAM costs and looming global tariffs are heavily inflating the projected $743 bill of materials for the AMD-powered console. In a bizarre physical release trend, the **[Elden Ring Switch 2 physical edition is listed at $80](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1557340.htm)**, yet the box contains nothing but a digital download code.

## Science & Space
The Artemis II mission is bracing for a perilous return, as **[NASA acknowledges known defects in the Orion capsule's heat shield](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1557334.htm)**. Despite internal dissent from experts who fear the Avcoat material might critically fail during its high-speed reentry, NASA officials remain confident the crew will splash down safely in the Pacific. Meanwhile, a joint Sino-UK research team analyzing Chang'e-5 and Chang'e-6 lunar soil samples has **[made the first discovery of nitrogen-bearing organics](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1557394.htm)** on the moon's surface, indicating that asteroid impacts delivered life-essential elements to the lunar environment.
