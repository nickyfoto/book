Disgruntled receivers and disastrous quarterback contracts are dominating the transaction wire. In Dallas, the decision to hit their star receiver with a $27.3 million tag drew heavy fire in FOX's **[WR George Pickens unhappy with franchise tag, time for Cowboys to trade him? | The Herd](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kvIGCUT2oR8)**, framing expensive wideouts as volatile luxury sports cars. ## League Pulse
The primary storyline right now is the ruthless intersection of team-building and contract leverage. Franchises are loudly refusing to bend to player demands, whether by coldly offering the franchise tag to stars like George Pickens or actively seeking trades for recent top picks like Anthony Richardson. The draft is over, but the financial chess match has only just begun.
