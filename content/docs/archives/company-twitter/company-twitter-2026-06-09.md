# Company@X — 2026-06-09

## Signal of the Day
Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5, a new "Mythos-class" frontier model deemed safe for general availability. The release fundamentally resets the industry baseline, with capabilities exceeding all of Anthropic's previously available models and immediately forcing downstream tools and policymakers to adapt to this new intelligence threshold.

## Key Announcements

**Anthropic & Cursor** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/cursor_ai/status/2064394824313376787#m)
Cursor integrated Claude Fable 5 into its development environment immediately upon release. The model quickly established a new state-of-the-art benchmark on CursorBench at 72.9%, outperforming the previous leading model by a massive 8-point margin. This rapid integration underscores how aggressively AI-native dev tools are adopting top-tier models to maintain a competitive edge.

**Google DeepMind** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleDeepMind/status/2064366504745828689#m)
Google unveiled Gemini 3.5 Live Translate, an audio model capable of streaming, continuous speech-to-speech translation across over 70 languages without pauses. By processing speech as it is streamed while preserving the speaker's tone, pace, and pitch, the model represents a significant infrastructural leap for simultaneous interpretation. It is launching in private preview for enterprise Google Meet users, rolling out globally on the Google Translate mobile apps, and opening to developers via the Gemini Live API.

**Waymo** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Waymo/status/2064149354123047221#m)
Waymo acquired a 5,500-acre proving ground in Wittmann, Arizona. This massive physical infrastructure investment secures dedicated freeway and urban tracks for the autonomous vehicle company. The acquisition signals Waymo's intent to rapidly scale testing and validate software in highly controlled environments before deploying updates to public roads.

**AWS** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/awscloud/status/2064378538535391544#m)
Amazon Web Services launched the AWS FinOps Agent into preview. The new tool allows cloud engineering teams to investigate cost anomalies before they compound and generates optimization reports tailored to specific stakeholders. This natively embeds cloud-cost optimization directly into the daily workflows of engineers, addressing a critical pain point as enterprise AI and compute workloads scale.

**Hugging Face & Arcee AI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ClementDelangue/status/2064323874049679643#m)
Hugging Face announced a multi-million dollar partnership with American AI lab Arcee AI. In a notable infrastructure shift, Arcee AI is completely replacing AWS S3 with Hugging Face for the hosting of all its public and private datasets and models. This marks the first time a major AI lab has fully migrated its core model storage off legacy AWS architecture to Hugging Face.

**xAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/xai/status/2064426048146800780#m)
xAI detailed a strategic integration with delivery service Gopuff to build a highly personalized shopping assistant. The partnership will leverage xAI's chat, voice, and image models to power the platform's consumer experience.
