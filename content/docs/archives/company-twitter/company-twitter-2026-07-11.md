# Company@X — 2026-07-11

## Signal of the Day
OpenAI's GPT-Live reaching global availability alongside the impressive clinical benchmarks of GPT-5.6 signals major leaps in both mass-market multimodal accessibility and domain-specific reasoning. 

## Key Announcements

**OpenAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/athyuttamre/status/2075768216866205786#m)
GPT-Live has officially rolled out globally to all ChatGPT users. To drive initial user engagement and testing, the company temporarily doubled the voice usage limits for the weekend. This move cements real-time voice interaction as a core, widely available feature of their platform rather than a limited beta.

**OpenAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/sama/status/2075985056846451123#m)
A new medical evaluation reveals that specialty-matched physicians found fewer flaws in clinical responses generated by GPT-5.6 than in those written by human doctors. The benchmark also highlights massive cost-efficiency gains at the frontier; the smaller GPT-5.6 Luna variant outperforms the highest reasoning efforts of the previous GPT-5.5 model while costing 25 times less, whereas the larger GPT-5.6 Sol model pushes the absolute performance ceiling.

**xAI & Box** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/grok/status/2076022407048139041#m)
Box demonstrated Grok 4.5's enterprise reasoning capabilities by using the model to analyze a complex 30-page commercial office lease. Grok 4.5 successfully connected disjointed clauses across 33 sections and 10 attachments to uncover a true early termination cost of approximately $830K, bypassing the misleadingly simple $267K figure stated in the primary termination clause. This showcases the viability of frontier reasoning models for executing deep, accurate analyses of unstructured enterprise documents.

**Tesla** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Tesla/status/2075766625593389515#m)
Tesla announced that internal employee rides in the Cybercab will be starting soon at the Giga Texas facility. This marks a critical operational milestone, transitioning the autonomous robotaxi platform into real-world internal testing.
