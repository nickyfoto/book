# Company@X — 2026-05-22

## Signal of the Day
The decisive shift toward autonomous, long-running agentic workflows was on full display today. Both Google and OpenAI announced solutions that decouple AI from synchronous chat: Google introduced Gemini Spark, a 24/7 personal agent for persistent digital workflows, while OpenAI launched a "Goal mode" allowing Codex to autonomously pursue complex objectives over several days. 

## Key Announcements

**Google** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2057881884219035752#m)
At Google I/O, the company launched Gemini Omni, a multimodal model that generates and edits video by natively blending text, image, and video inputs with an underlying understanding of real-world physics. Simultaneously, Google unveiled Gemini Spark, a 24/7 persistent AI agent designed to navigate users' digital lives, learn new skills, and execute recurring workflows automatically.

**OpenAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/OpenAI/status/2057617860986593680#m)
OpenAI significantly upgraded Codex, highlighted by a new "Goal mode" in the app, CLI, and IDE extension that allows the agent to work hands-off on complex tasks for hours or even days. The update also introduced deep OS-level integration, enabling Codex to securely operate Mac applications from a user's phone, even when the Mac screen is locked.

**Google DeepMind** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleDeepMind/status/2057842131142590512#m)
Project Genie is expanding its capabilities by ingesting Google Maps Street View data to transform real U.S. locations into interactive, playable worlds. Rolling out to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers, this signals a massive step toward procedural generation of immersive 3D environments tied directly to geospatial reality.

**Cursor** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/cursor_ai/status/2057913121558413770#m)
Cursor released its SDK featuring Composer 2.5, now available in Python and TypeScript. This effectively commoditizes the creation of custom AI coding agents, allowing developers to build and deploy their own specialized agentic workflows directly on top of the Cursor editor. 

**Anthropic** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AnthropicAI/status/2057909102542549503#m)
Anthropic revealed that its collaborative AI cybersecurity initiative, Project Glasswing, has successfully identified over 10,000 high- or critical-severity software vulnerabilities in the past month. The company warned that the industry must aggressively adapt its patching infrastructure to handle the sheer volume of zero-days that upcoming models like "Claude Mythos Preview" will uncover.

**Y Combinator & OpenAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/snowmaker/status/2057563683421855746#m)
OpenAI is offering a massive $2M compute token grant to every startup in Y Combinator's Spring and Summer 2026 batches. This unprecedented subsidy illustrates the aggressive, high-stakes battle among hyperscalers to lock the next generation of AI-native applications into their specific model ecosystems.

**Hugging Face & Common Crawl** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/vanstriendaniel/status/2057830058241261807#m)
Common Crawl's massive 2.19 billion web page dataset is now hosted on Hugging Face Storage Buckets, optimizing data infrastructure for AI training. This architecture allows developers to run zero-download SQL queries directly over the massive dataset using DuckDB.
