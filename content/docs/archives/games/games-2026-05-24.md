## News & Reviews

**[Rocket League to Receive First Game Engine Tune Up in 11 Years as Psyonix Teases Unreal Engine 6 Update](https://www.ign.com/articles/rocket-league-to-receive-first-game-engine-tune-up-in-11-years-as-psyonix-teases-unreal-engine-6-update)** · IGN
Psyonix is finally moving *Rocket League* away from Unreal Engine 3, officially dropping a teaser for the game's massive transition to Unreal Engine 6. Revealed at the Paris Major alongside a brand new Unreal Engine 6 logo, the update promises a massive visual facelift for arenas and potentially paves the way for extensive custom maps and user-generated content. 

**[Star Citizen Officially Crosses the $1 Billion Funding Raised Barrier as $5,000 Spaceship You Can’t Fly In-Game Goes on Sale](https://www.ign.com/articles/star-citizen-officially-crosses-the-1-billion-funding-raised-barrier-as-5000-spaceship-you-cant-fly-in-game-goes-on-sale)** · IGN
Fourteen years into its polarizing development, *Star Citizen* has surpassed a staggering $1 billion in crowdfunding. Cloud Imperium Games celebrated this monumental milestone by selling the Anvil Odin—a 752-meter battlecruiser that costs $5,000 in real cash and currently cannot even be flown in-game.

**[A Petition Calling on Sony to Greenlight Destiny 3 Has Over 120,000 Signatures Already — and It’s Rising Fast](https://www.ign.com/articles/a-petition-calling-on-sony-to-greenlight-destiny-3-has-over-120000-signatures-already-and-its-rising-fast)** · IGN
Following Bungie's announcement that *Destiny 2* support will officially end in June, a desperate fan petition begging Sony to greenlight *Destiny 3* has rocketed past 120,000 signatures. Sadly, a sequel is highly unlikely given the ballooning costs of AAA development and reports of impending "significant" layoffs hitting Bungie.

**[Hands Over, a Horror Party Game, Announced for PC](https://www.ign.com/articles/hands-over-announced-for-pc)** · IGN
Developer Artdock is turning innocent childhood mini-games into absolute nightmare fuel with the announcement of *Hands Over* for PC. This multiplayer horror party game relies on simple mechanics and influence cards to build extreme tension, tasking players with surviving deadly twists on classic games while trying to sabotage their friends.

**[Karmine Corp Crowned Champions of Rocket League Paris Major](https://www.ign.com/articles/karmine-corp-wins-rlcs-paris-major-2026)** · IGN
The French esports titans Karmine Corp defended their home turf to win the 2026 RLCS Paris Major, defeating Saudi Arabian squad Twisted Minds 4-2. The victory secured them the biggest share of a $350,000 prize pool in front of a roaring hometown crowd of up to 25,000 fans at the La Défense arena.
