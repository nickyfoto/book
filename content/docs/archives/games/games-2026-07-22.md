# Gaming News — 2026-07-22

## Top Story
The biggest shake-up in the industry right now is Sony's controversial decision to end the production of physical PlayStation discs for new games starting in January 2028, a move analysts warn could completely destroy the $7.2 billion second-hand video game market. While digital sales dominate modern gaming, the massive online backlash from players and preservationists highlights the growing tension over consumer choice and true game ownership in an all-digital future.

**[Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/avatar-legends-the-fighting-game-review)** · IGN
*Avatar Legends* delivers arguably the most fun, fast-paced fighting mechanics and wild character mobility we've seen in years, perfectly nailing the spirit of the beloved show. Unfortunately, its stellar 1v1 gameplay does all the heavy lifting, as the overall package is severely held back by a lackluster story mode, missing training features, and bare-bones 2-player-only online lobbies. 

**[Stalker 2 Turned a Profit Before It Came Out Thanks to Money Microsoft Paid for Xbox Exclusivity](https://www.ign.com/articles/stalker-2-turned-a-profit-before-it-came-out-thanks-to-money-microsoft-paid-for-xbox-exclusivity)** · IGN

**[Wreckreation 2 Announced Despite Studio Closure, Just Nine Months After the Original Arrived](https://www.ign.com/articles/wreckreation-2-announced-despite-studio-closure-just-nine-months-after-the-original-arrived)** · IGN
