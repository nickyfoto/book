# Gaming News — 2026-04-27

## Top Story
Valve officially confirmed the highly anticipated Steam Controller will launch on May 4, 2026, for a highly competitive $99, while the standalone Steam Machine has been delayed due to the ongoing AI-fueled RAM shortage. At the same time, Valve's Pierre-Loup Griffais confirmed the company is "hard at work" on the Steam Deck 2, though it currently has no firm release window as they evaluate next-generation silicon advancements.

## News & Reviews

**[Steam Controller Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/steam-controller-review-2026)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/steam-controller-review-2026)
After more than ten years, Valve's new Steam Controller is a massive success, praised for its innovative haptic touchpads and symmetrical, PlayStation-style layout. Priced at a reasonable $99, it boasts TMR (Tunnel Magnetoresistance) sensors in the analog sticks that use magnets to prevent physical wear, making them virtually immune to stick drift. It stands out as one of the best and most fully-featured PC controllers on the market, serving as the perfect bridge for the upcoming Steam Machine hardware ecosystem.

**[Helldivers 2 Dev Promises Action Following AMA](https://www.ign.com/articles/helldivers-2-dev-promises-action-and-communication-after-players-deliver-fair-and-painful-criticism-during-reddit-ama)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/helldivers-2-dev-promises-action-and-communication-after-players-deliver-fair-and-painful-criticism-during-reddit-ama)
Following immense community backlash on the game's subreddit, Arrowhead Game Studios CCO Johan Pilestedt hosted an AMA to address "hate, misinformation, and disappointment". Acknowledging that players' demands for more meaningful changes to the core experience are "fair" but "painful to read," Pilestedt promised bi-weekly communication updates to rebuild trust. The studio hopes that this new level of transparency will lead to better-maintained systems and accurate patch notes for the dedicated player base.

**[Crimson Desert Sales Soar, Netting Huge Dev Bonuses](https://www.ign.com/articles/crimson-desert-has-sold-so-well-developer-pearl-abyss-has-reportedly-given-every-employee-a-3400-bonus)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/crimson-desert-has-sold-so-well-developer-pearl-abyss-has-reportedly-given-every-employee-a-3400-bonus)
Developer Pearl Abyss has handed out $3,400 bonuses to its employees to celebrate their action-adventure game *Crimson Desert* surpassing an incredible 5 million copies sold. The massive success of the South Korean title highlights a massive industry shift towards single-player console experiences originating from the region, earning praise from the country's Prime Minister.

**[Capcom Raises Profit Forecasts Thanks to Resident Evil Requiem](https://www.ign.com/articles/resident-evil-requiem-sales-prompt-capcom-to-raise-profit-forecast)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/resident-evil-requiem-sales-prompt-capcom-to-raise-profit-forecast)

The staggering success of the latest survival horror title, combined with strong back-catalog sales, has prompted Capcom to confidently raise its earnings forecast to roughly $1.2 billion in net sales for the fiscal year ending March 2026.

**[Pokémon Champions Producer Admits Visual Limitations](https://www.ign.com/articles/pokemon-champions-producer-acknowledges-visual-limitations-amid-ongoing-fan-scrutiny-of-series-graphics)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/pokemon-champions-producer-acknowledges-visual-limitations-amid-ongoing-fan-scrutiny-of-series-graphics)
Following intense fan criticism regarding *Pokémon Champions'* basic visuals and a 30fps lock on the Nintendo Switch 2, technical director Masaaki Hoshino acknowledged the community's frustration. Hoshino defended the game's individual Pokémon shadows and bespoke battle effects, but admitted that the hardware and competitive nature of the game presented more limitations compared to his past work on *Pokkén Tournament*.

**[Diablo 4: Lord of Hatred Drops Tomorrow](https://www.ign.com/articles/diablo-4-lord-of-hatred-global-release-times-and-details-confirmed)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/diablo-4-lord-of-hatred-global-release-times-and-details-confirmed)
