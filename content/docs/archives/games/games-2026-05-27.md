# Gaming News — 2026-05-27

## Top Story
CD Projekt Red dropped an absolute bombshell today: *The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt* is getting a massive third expansion titled *Songs of the Past*, slated for release in 2027. Twelve years after the legendary RPG originally launched, players will return as Geralt of Rivia for a brand new adventure co-developed alongside the veterans at Fool's Theory.

## News & Reviews

**[Valve Hikes Steam Deck OLED Prices](https://www.ign.com/articles/steam-deck-price-increase-announced-by-valve)**
In a painful blow to portable PC gamers, Valve has drastically increased the price of new Steam Deck OLED models due to rising component costs, with the premium 1TB model jumping from $649 up to a staggering $949. This massive hike makes the beloved handheld a much harder sell, though budget-conscious players can still grab the refurbished LCD versions at their original low prices.

**[Dragon Quest 12 Restarted and Renamed](https://www.ign.com/articles/dragon-quest-12-beyond-dreams-update)**
Square Enix revealed that *Dragon Quest 12* has been completely restarted from scratch and rebranded from *The Flames of Fate* to *Beyond Dreams*. Promising a "bright and exciting future" that ditches its originally planned darker tone, this pivot aims to evolve the classic RPG formula into something unprecedented while still featuring the late Akira Toriyama's character designs.

**[Mina the Hollower Review: A Zelda/Castlevania Masterpiece](https://www.ign.com/articles/mina-the-hollower-review)**
Yacht Club Games’ *Mina the Hollower* is a retro revelation that brilliantly blends *The Legend of Zelda*'s open-world structure with *Castlevania*'s gothic horror and *Bloodborne*-esque progression. Earning incredible praise for its rich combat systems and unmatched puzzle-solving, this demanding top-down action-RPG is a delightfully weird adventure you shouldn't miss.

**[Destiny 2 Fans Plot Server Crash Protest](https://www.ign.com/articles/destiny-2-fans-plan-mass-protest-to-crash-the-servers-and-beat-marathons-all-time-player-record)**
Furious over Bungie's decision to end content updates for the long-running shooter in favor of *Marathon*, *Destiny 2* players are organizing a mass login protest for June 9 to intentionally crash the servers. It’s a desperate final hurrah meant to prove to Sony that the *Destiny* franchise still has a massive player base worth investing in.

**[Take-Two Shuts Down GTA 5's RAGE:MP Mod Platform](https://www.ign.com/articles/take-two-forces-gta-5-multiplayer-platform-ragemp-to-shut-down-and-migrate-to-fivem)**
Take-Two Interactive is cracking down, forcing the popular *GTA 5* multiplayer modding platform RAGE:MP to cease operations by August 31, 2026. Rockstar is mandating that all server owners migrate to FiveM, the mod platform they officially acquired last year, tightening their absolute grip on the game's modding ecosystem.

**[No Man's Sky Unleashes The Swarm](https://www.ign.com/articles/no-mans-sky-launches-the-swarm-update-adds-mysterious-death-star-like-construct-to-the-game)**
Hello Games continues to deliver the goods with "The Swarm" update, plunging players into a massive 8-week community war against a "Death Star like construct" called the Hive of Glass. Get ready for huge space battles against robotic drone swarms in what Sean Murray calls their most epic encounters yet.
