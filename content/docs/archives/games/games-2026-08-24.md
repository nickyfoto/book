**[Amazon and Twitch Face Class-Action Lawsuit After Introducing Opt-Out Setting That Uses Streamers’ Content to Train AI](https://www.ign.com/articles/amazon-and-twitch-face-class-action-lawsuit-after-introducing-opt-out-setting-that-uses-streamers-content-to-train-ai)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/amazon-and-twitch-face-class-action-lawsuit-after-introducing-opt-out-setting-that-uses-streamers-content-to-train-ai)
Amazon and Twitch are being sued in a class-action lawsuit filed in California after Twitch introduced a privacy setting that automatically opts streamers into training Amazon's generative AI on their channel content by default. Streamers are outraged that their intellectual property is being used without direct consent or compensation, but Twitch's Chief Product Officer Mike Minton bluntly defended the move, stating that "if it was opt-in, nobody would opt in." The lawsuit seeks contract damages for this alleged abuse and exploitation of creators' hard work.

**[Nautus Exclusive Hands-On Preview: It Feels Like Mash-Up of Steamworld Dig and Subnautica](https://www.ign.com/articles/nautus-exclusive-hands-on-preview-it-feels-like-mash-up-of-steamworld-dig-and-subnautica)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/nautus-exclusive-hands-on-preview-it-feels-like-mash-up-of-steamworld-dig-and-subnautica)
Developed by Magic Design Studios, *Nautus* is a delightfully weird and colorful aquatic survival-crafting adventure that perfectly balances cozy island-building with tense deep-sea diving. Gameplay on the tropical surface focuses on building up a community of quirky, Cartoon Network-style characters, but once you plunge in your submersible, it shifts into an eerie, Metroidvania-inspired survival-action game where you must manage oxygen and fend off hostile marine life. A closed beta is scheduled to kick off on Steam on August 31, with sign-ups open now.

**[France and Saudi Arabia to Build \$7 Billion Dragon Ball Z Theme Park Outside Paris](https://www.ign.com/articles/france-and-saudi-arabia-to-build-7-billion-dragon-ball-z-theme-park-outside-paris)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/france-and-saudi-arabia-to-build-7-billion-dragon-ball-z-theme-park-outside-paris)
The Elysee Palace has announced a massive €6 billion (\$7 billion USD) partnership between France and Saudi Arabia to construct a sprawling Dragon Ball Z theme park near Paris. Funded by Saudi Arabia's Qiddiya project, the park will span over 30 attractions and rides, including a 70-meter-tall Shenron statue and recreations of iconic spots like Kame House and Capsule Corporation. This represents another massive investment by Saudi Arabia into the gaming and anime space, following their recent \$55 billion acquisition of EA.

**[Xbox and PlayStation Appear to Be Publicly Backing Down from Promoting Multiplatform Releases](https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-and-playstation-appear-to-be-publicly-backing-down-from-promoting-multiplatform-releases)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-and-playstation-appear-to-be-publicly-backing-down-from-promoting-multiplatform-releases)

Xbox recently purged PS5 and Nintendo Switch 2 logos from its latest *Minecraft Dungeons 2* trailer, while PlayStation is abandoning PC releases for its major first-party single-player titles like *Ghost of Yotei* and reintroducing "only on PlayStation" branding in new ads. This strategic shift indicates that both companies are eager to shore up their own console ecosystems and rebuild brand strength.

**[Buddy Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/buddy-movie-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/buddy-movie-review)
Directed by Casper Kelly, the creator of the viral Adult Swim short *Too Many Cooks*, *Buddy* is a brilliant feature-length mascot horror film that presents as a lost 90s children's television show but quickly devolves into an edgy, surrealist descent into madness. Keegan-Michael Key delivers an outstanding voice performance as Buddy the orange unicorn, expertly balancing cheery, saccharine positivity with chilling, violent undertones as the kids begin to question their clubhouse existence. While the film suffers from some mid-movie pacing issues, its incredible production design, custom puppets, and dark psychological themes make it a standout entry in the genre.

**[Black Myth: Zhong Kui Doesn’t Look As Epic as Wukong – and That’s Great](https://www.ign.com/articles/black-myth-zhong-kui-doesnt-look-as-epic-as-wukong-and-thats-great)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/black-myth-zhong-kui-doesnt-look-as-epic-as-wukong-and-thats-great)
