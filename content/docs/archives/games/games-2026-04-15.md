# Gaming News — 2026-04-15

## Top Story
Pearl Abyss's open-world adventure *Crimson Desert* is an absolute smash hit, crossing 5 million copies sold less than a month after its March 19 launch. The massive milestone is well-deserved, with the game even earning high praise from *The Witcher 3* director Konrad Tomaszkiewicz, who commended it for delivering a "quite fresh" departure from standard triple-A RPGs.

## News & Reviews

**Marathon Overhauls Gameplay to Encourage Friendly Encounters** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/marathon-patch-106-update-pushes-players-to-be-nice-and-brings-big-gameplay-changes-everything-you-need-to-know)
Bungie dropped patch 1.0.6 for its extraction shooter *Marathon*, introducing a new C.A.R.R.I. system that rewards players with "CyberAcme Commendations" for extracting alongside strangers. The community reaction is surprisingly wholesome, with players actively reviving enemies and sharing loot rather than immediately shooting each other. This update also brought sweeping changes to weapons, equipment, and Shells, making the notoriously brutal game slightly more approachable for newcomers.

**Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream Review** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/tomodachi-life-living-the-dream-review)
The Switch sequel to *Tomodachi Life* is an incredibly funny and deeply personal life simulator, but it unfortunately drops the ball on its social features. While the revamped Mii Maker and sharp HD graphics offer brilliant character customization, the baffling decision to restrict Mii sharing to local wireless heavily isolates the experience. Still, it provides a delightful, unpredictable playground for your characters if you can look past the sharing constraints.

**Next God of War Rumored to Feature Multiple Mythologies** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/next-god-of-war-features-multiple-mythologies-report)
According to recent reports, the next *God of War* from Sony Santa Monica won't stick to just one mythological pantheon. Leaks suggest the unannounced project may incorporate both Chinese and Japanese mythological elements as it moves beyond the Greek and Norse sagas. If accurate, this expands the franchise significantly while setting up fresh, action-focused conflicts for Kratos's universe.

**PlayStation Plus May 2026 Lineup Leaked** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/playstation-plus-games-lineup-leaks)
The ever-reliable leaker billbil-kun has revealed the PS Plus games arriving in May 2026. Subscribers can expect to claim *Horizon Zero Dawn Remastered*, *The Crew Motorfest*, and *Football Manager 26 Console*. It's a stacked month for open-world exploration, racing fans, and soccer tacticians alike.

**Jacksepticeye to Produce Bloodborne Movie** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/jacksepticeye-comments-on-bloodborne-movie)
Sony Pictures is officially moving forward with an R-rated *Bloodborne* animated movie, co-produced by PlayStation Productions and YouTuber Jacksepticeye. The massive FromSoftware fan insists he is taking his role incredibly seriously, promising to pour all of his passion, energy, and soul into making the adaptation a masterpiece.

**Starfield PS5 Sales Deemed "Lukewarm"** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/starfield-ps5-sales-estimate)
Despite its highly anticipated transition to PlayStation 5, Bethesda's *Starfield* reportedly only sold around 140,000 copies in its first week on the console. Analysts suggest these numbers are not fantastic for a port of this magnitude, raising questions about Microsoft's current strategy for delayed multiplatform releases.
