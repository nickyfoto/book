# Gaming News — 2026-08-27

## Top Story
Rockstar Games and Netflix have finally pulled back the curtain on **[Grand Theft Auto 6](https://www.ign.com/articles/grand-theft-auto-6-everything-revealed-on-netflix-ign-and-more)** with a massive **27-minute gameplay showcase** captured entirely on PlayStation 5, revealing unprecedented NPC diversity, RPG-style physical attributes, and a highly immersive Vice City ahead of its **November 19, 2026, launch**.

## News & Reviews

**[Rockstar North Unleashes First Official GTA 6 Gameplay Reveal](https://www.ign.com/articles/grand-theft-auto-6-everything-revealed-on-netflix-ign-and-more)** · IGN
Fans and developers are celebrating after Rockstar debuted **GTA 6 gameplay** in a stunning 27-minute presentation on Netflix. Running on a base PlayStation 5, the Extended Look highlighted a deeply immersive world, optional Bonnie and Clyde-style romance mechanics, and an expansive **80-hour average story playthrough**. Rockstar is clearly raising the bar for the open-world genre with highly advanced AI behaviors, interactive safehouses, and complex carjacking systems that require specialized tools like electronic key cloners.

**[Elden Ring Gets Its Biggest Balance Patch in Years Ahead of Switch 2 Release](https://www.ign.com/articles/elden-ring-update-patch-notes-reveal-biggest-changes-in-years)** · IGN
FromSoftware has dropped a surprise balance patch for Elden Ring, representing the game's **most significant update since October 2024**. The update introduces massive buffs to various weapons—including perfume bottles, throwing weapons, and greatshields—alongside **improved parry performance** across the board. These gameplay adjustments are designed to prepare the game for tomorrow's launch of **Elden Ring: Tarnished Edition on the Nintendo Switch 2** and its accompanying Tarnished Pack DLC on other platforms.

**[Fable Combat Hands-On Reveals Hilarious Spells and An Avoided October Release](https://www.ign.com/articles/fable-combat-preview)** · IGN
A new hands-on preview from Gamescom 2026 reveals that Fable's action-RPG combat features a fluid, satisfying synergy between traditional melee and a highly creative suite of spells. Most notable is the hilarious **"Befowl" spell**, which lets you turn enemies into zombie chickens that can be fireballed into freshly roasted snacks. While the gameplay is incredibly polished, game director Ralph Fulton revealed the game was originally aiming for an October 2026 release but was **pushed to February 23, 2027**, specifically to dodge the chaotic shadow of Rockstar's GTA 6.

**[Star Citizen Spinoff Squadron 42 Delayed to 2027 to Avoid GTA 6 'Attention Buzz Saw'](https://www.ign.com/articles/star-citizen-spinoff-squadron-42-delayed-to-2027-dev-insists-its-because-of-gta-6)** · IGN
Cloud Imperium Games has officially delayed its star-studded single-player space epic, Squadron 42, into the **second quarter of 2027**. CEO Chris Roberts candidly explained that the developer did not want to launch its decade-long project into the massive **"attention buzz saw" of GTA 6**. Roberts noted that even though GTA 6 is skipping PC at launch, its cultural gravity will completely dominate media outlets and content creators, leaving very little room for other game coverage.

**[Forza Horizon 6 PS5 Port Reportedly Delayed Beyond 2026](https://www.ign.com/articles/forza-horizon-6-ps5-launch-reportedly-scrubbed-for-2026)** · IGN
The highly anticipated PlayStation 5 release window for Forza Horizon 6 has reportedly been scrubbed, meaning the perfect 10/10 open-world racer **will no longer arrive in 2026**. While Xbox has not officially commented, reports confirm the shift, which perfectly matches Microsoft's recent strategy to scale back multiplatform promotion in favor of promoting its own hardware and October's Gears of War: E-Day. There is no indication that the port is completely canceled, but PS5 fans will have to wait longer.

**[The Witcher 3 Remastered Lands in September with Help from Top Modder](https://www.ign.com/articles/cd-projekt-red-enlisted-modder-to-help-make-the-witcher-3-look-better-than-ever-in-new-remaster)** · IGN
CD Projekt Red has announced that its official remaster of The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt will launch on **September 29, 2026**, for PC, Nintendo Switch 2, and current-gen consoles. The studio collaborated with well-known community modder HalkHogan to extensively rework characters, monsters, and environmental textures. While the upgrade is **completely free to existing game owners**, those looking to buy the game on Steam will now face a quiet base price increase from \$39.99 to \$49.99.

**[Peter Cullen, Iconic Voice of Optimus Prime, Dies at 85](https://www.ign.com/articles/peter-cullen-optimus-prime-in-transformers-dies-at-85)** · IGN
The voice acting world is mourning the loss of legendary actor Peter Cullen, who passed away peacefully at his home on Wednesday, August 26, 2026, at the age of 85. While famously revered as the heroic voice of Autobot leader **Optimus Prime** for over four decades, Cullen’s deeply resonant and compassionate delivery also defined characters like Eeyore in Winnie the Pooh and the original Predator. His family has asked fans to honor his massive legacy by leading with integrity and remembering his famous motto: **"be strong enough to be gentle"**.
