## News & Reviews

**[Sony Predicts Influx of Players as Helldivers 2 Drops on PlayStation Plus](https://www.ign.com/articles/sony-predicts-influx-of-new-players-as-helldivers-2-drops-on-the-playstation-plus-game-catalog-alongside-new-update-and-warhammer-40000-warbond)** · IGN
Cooperative shooter sensation *Helldivers 2* has surprise-dropped on the PlayStation Plus Game Catalog for Extra and Premium subscribers. The drop lands alongside the massive update 7.0.0 "Devoid of Liberty," which introduces the long-awaited, horrifying Illuminate faction to the galactic war. This is an incredible win for both existing players and newcomers looking to spread some managed democracy with the new SEAF NPC reinforcements.

**[The Elder Scrolls 6 Demo to Xbox Leadership Met with Inflatable Rat Protest](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-elder-scrolls-6-demo-to-xbox-leadership-included-a-giant-inflatable-rat-placed-outside-bethesda-offices-in-protest-at-microsofts-mass-layoffs)** · IGN
As Xbox boss Asha Sharma visited Bethesda to watch a live playthrough of *The Elder Scrolls 6*, union members inflated a giant rat named Scabby outside the office doors. The CWA union protest, backed by hundreds of red flags symbolizing laid-off employees, condemned Microsoft's recent devastating downsizing of Xbox. While Sharma teased the game's secret eight-character subtitle, developers warned that the loss of key talent will have a cascading effect on the highly anticipated RPG.

**[Saber Interactive Denies Replacing Rideshare Writer with ChatGPT](https://www.ign.com/articles/space-marine-2-maker-saber-interactive-denies-writers-claim-they-were-replaced-by-chatgpt-but-admits-that-rideshare-stimulator-will-feature-ai-by-necessity)** · IGN
*Space Marine 2* publisher Saber Interactive has strongly denied allegations from former lead writer Stella Sacco that she was replaced by ChatGPT midway through development on taxi game *Rideshare Stimulator*. Saber maintains that the game's main story was penned entirely by humans, though they admitted the game's experimental, infinite "free-ride" mode incorporates AI passengers by necessity. The controversy has fueled mounting backlash on Steam from players demanding an explicit AI disclosure on the storefront.

**[Coyote vs. Acme Debuts to Perfect Rotten Tomatoes Score After Tax-Write-Off Near Death](https://www.ign.com/articles/coyote-vs-acme-debuts-with-perfect-100-score-on-rotten-tomatoes)** · IGN
The heavily embattled Looney Tunes movie *Coyote vs. Acme* has debuted to critical acclaim, initially sporting a perfect 100% score on Rotten Tomatoes before settling at an outstanding 94%. Reviewers, including IGN's Clint Gage who awarded the film a 9/10, praised the clever corporate satire and dry absurdist comedy of Wile E. Coyote suing his supplier. The film's successful theatrical rescue by Ketchup Entertainment is a heartwarming victory over Warner Bros.' original plan to permanently shelf it for a tax write-off.

**[Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick Added as Playable Character in WWE 2K26](https://www.ign.com/articles/take-two-boss-strauss-zelnick-says-he-asked-to-be-a-playable-character-in-wwe-2k26-because-i-thought-it-would-be-funny-for-insiders-but-dont-expect-him-to-pop-up-in-gta-6)** · IGN
In one of the most bizarre DLC drops in sports gaming history, Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick has joined the *WWE 2K26* roster as a playable wrestler with a rating of 77. The 69-year-old executive revealed that he personally requested his inclusion, did his own motion capture, and even licensed Frank Sinatra's "My Way" as his exclusive entrance theme. Zelnick confirmed the DLC is performing well, but quickly put to bed any rumors of a cameo in the upcoming *Grand Theft Auto 6*.
