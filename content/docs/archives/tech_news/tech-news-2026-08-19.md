## Top Stories

**[Stripe Acquires AI Model Marketplace OpenRouter for \$7.5 Billion](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/19/business/stripe-openrouter-ai.html)** · The New York Times
Stripe is acquiring AI model router and marketplace OpenRouter in a massive deal valued at \$7.5 billion, representing a staggering jump from the startup’s \$1.3 billion valuation just three months ago. OpenRouter has built a massive developer following by letting them dynamically route prompts to cost-efficient, open-weight AI models, particularly from Chinese labs like DeepSeek and Z.ai. Stripe's push beyond payments into AI infrastructure signals its desire to control the underlying transaction and token-routing layer as companies aggressively ramp up their AI expenditures.

**[Moderna and Merck Personalized Cancer Vaccine Succeeds in Phase 3 Trial](https://science.slashdot.org/story/26/08/19/1725205/moderna-merck-say-mrna-vaccine-prevents-melanoma-from-returning)** · Slashdot
Moderna and Merck announced that their custom-tailored mRNA cancer vaccine, when paired with the immunotherapy drug Keytruda, successfully reduced cancer recurrence and spread in a massive Phase 3 clinical trial of high-risk melanoma patients. The made-to-order vaccine is formulated individually based on an analysis of mutations in the patient's own tumor, representing the first positive late-stage trial for an individualized mRNA cancer treatment. Moderna shares more than doubled on the news, adding \$30 billion to its market value and raising broad industry expectations for mRNA's post-pandemic future.

**[Chinese Humanoid Robot Giant Unitree Soars in Shanghai Market Debut](https://hardware.slashdot.org/story/26/08/19/0559230/chinese-robotics-giant-unitree-soars-in-stock-market-debut)** · Slashdot
Humanoid robotics pioneer Unitree (Yushu Technology Co Ltd) surged over 600% on its debut on the Shanghai stock market, raising 6.1 billion yuan in the first mainland Chinese listing of its kind. The Hangzhou-based company shipped over 5,500 humanoid robots last year and stands out as a rare profitable player in the sector, bringing in 278 million yuan in net profit for 2025. The company is already actively shipping commercial humanoid models like its \$13,500 child-sized G1, putting intense pressure on US competitors like Tesla who have yet to begin commercial deliveries.

**[Amazon Prime Air Plans Sixfold US Expansion to 500 Cities](https://www.theverge.com/tech/981908/amazon-prime-air-drone-delivery-us-expansion)** · The Verge
Amazon aims to expand its Prime Air drone delivery service to nearly 500 U.S. cities and towns by the end of 2026, marking a massive sixfold increase in its geographical footprint. The service, which will soon launch in major metropolitan areas surrounding Chicago, Atlanta, Cleveland, Syracuse, and Boise, delivers packages weighing up to five pounds in under an hour. While Amazon is highlighting its automated safety features, the rapid rollout is expected to test public tolerance for drone noise and face local community pushback following previous drone crashes.

**[T-Mobile Severed Cables to Expel Chinese State Hackers](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/19/t-mobile-chopped-a-cable-to-expel-chinese-hackers-from-its-network/)** · TechCrunch
T-Mobile's cybersecurity team resorted to physically cutting a network cable to immediately stop and expel Chinese state-backed hackers from its network. The intrusion was discovered early after internal investigations were triggered by recent revelations surrounding the "Salt Typhoon" espionage group, allowing the telecom provider to prevent a massive-scale data breach. The incident highlights the high-stakes physical and digital maneuvers companies are adopting to protect critical infrastructure from highly sophisticated nation-state actors.

**[SK Hynix Stabilizes Stock with Historic \$29 Billion Share Buyback](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/sk-hynix-announces-28-6-billion-share-buy-back-on-ai-boom)** · Bloomberg
SK Hynix has unveiled a 40 trillion won (\$29 billion) share buyback and cancellation program to calm investor anxiety over the durability of the AI spending boom. The announcement triggered a surge in the memory maker’s stock, which had collapsed more than 50% in the preceding two months amid a wider global semiconductor selloff. By aggressively returning profits to shareholders, the dominant high-bandwidth memory (HBM) supplier hopes to counter market fears of a capital-expenditure bubble.

**[New Album Releases Linked to a 15% Spike in Fatal Car Crashes](https://entertainment.slashdot.org/story/26/08/18/2225215/top-album-releases-linked-to-rise-in-fatal-crashes)** · Slashdot
A new study published in *JAMA Network Open* has revealed a 15.1% relative increase in US traffic fatalities on the release days of major, highly streamed music albums. Researchers from Harvard Medical School suggest that streaming new releases on smartphones acts as a major driver distraction, requiring multi-step navigation such as unlocking screens and reading tracklists. The risk was found to be disproportionately higher among younger drivers, male drivers, solo commuters, and those driving cars equipped with built-in infotainment screens.
