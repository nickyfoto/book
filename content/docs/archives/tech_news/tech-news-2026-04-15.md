# Tech News — 2026-04-15

## Story of the Day
Struggling sustainable shoe brand Allbirds has announced a bizarre pivot to AI compute infrastructure, rebranding as NewBird AI and sending its stock soaring over 700%. This drastic transformation underscores the current frenzy in the tech market, where simply attaching an AI label to a failing $21 million apparel business can instantly attract millions in new funding.

## Top Stories

**Live Nation and Ticketmaster Ruled an Illegal Monopoly** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/policy/912689/live-nation-ticketmaster-antitrust-monopoly-trial-verdict)
A federal jury in Manhattan found that Live Nation and Ticketmaster operate an illegal monopoly in the live event ticketing and amphitheater markets. The verdict leaves the entertainment giant open to a potential breakup and massive damages, marking a major victory for the DOJ and state attorneys general who argued the company systematically overcharged fans.

**Amazon Acquires Globalstar in Satellite Internet Push** · [Slashdot](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/26/04/15/0642220/amazon-buys-globalstar-for-108-billion-moving-to-expand-its-satellite-internet-service)
Amazon is acquiring satellite communications company Globalstar for $10.8 billion to expand its low-Earth orbit internet network and directly challenge SpaceX's Starlink. The massive acquisition includes a key partnership with Apple to support satellite connectivity for iPhones, signaling a major consolidation in the race for orbital internet dominance.

**Anthropic’s 'Mythos' Ignites Geopolitical AI Debate** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-15/bessent-calls-anthropic-s-mythos-a-breakthrough-in-china-ai-race)
Anthropic’s newly teased AI model, Mythos, has triggered widespread alarm and excitement, driving its valuation offers past $800 billion while US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent hailed it as a breakthrough against China. The model is seemingly so powerful that Anthropic is treating it as a critical security asset, prompting Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang to call for urgent US-China dialogue to establish safety guardrails.

**Snap Slashes 16% of Workforce, Citing AI** · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/big-tech/snap-is-laying-off-16-percent-of-its-workforce-blames-ai-162456069.html?src=rss)
Snap is laying off roughly 1,000 employees and closing 300 open roles in a cost-cutting move aimed at achieving net-income profitability. CEO Evan Spiegel explicitly cited rapid advancements in AI as a driver for the cuts, pointing to small, AI-leveraged squads that are replacing repetitive human work to boost platform efficiency.

**Claude Agent Unearths 23-Year-Old Linux Vulnerability** · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/04/claude-code-linux-vulnerability/)
An Anthropic research scientist used the Claude Code AI model to discover multiple zero-day vulnerabilities in the Linux kernel, including a heap buffer overflow in the NFS driver hidden since 2003. The AI agent's ability to find deeply buried, complex vulnerabilities that human auditors missed for decades suggests a fast-approaching paradigm shift where AI-assisted zero-day discovery becomes routine for both defenders and attackers.

**Google Brings Native Gemini App to macOS** · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/ai/google-unleashes-a-native-gemini-app-for-the-mac-170500185.html?src=rss)
Google has launched a dedicated, native Gemini AI app for macOS, allowing users to summon the chatbot via a keyboard shortcut to analyze their screen and local files. This aggressive desktop integration attempts to front-run Apple’s own upcoming generative AI revamp for Siri, pushing Google’s ecosystem deeper into Apple's hardware.
