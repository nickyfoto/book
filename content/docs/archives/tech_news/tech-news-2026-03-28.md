# Tech News — 2026-03-28

## Top Stories

**Uterus kept alive outside the body for the first time** · [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/03/28/1134766/womans-uterus-kept-alive-outside-the-body-first/)
Researchers at the Carlos Simon Foundation in Spain successfully maintained a freshly donated human uterus outside the body for 24 hours using a custom perfusion machine nicknamed "Mother". The breakthrough could eventually extend the notoriously short viability window for organ transplants and allow scientists to study human embryo implantation in a living organ. 

**Anthropic and the Pentagon clash over autonomous weapons** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/audio/2026-03-28/odd-lots-ai-and-the-future-of-autonomous-weapons-podcast)
Anthropic is fiercely objecting to the use of its AI models in fully autonomous weapons and domestic surveillance, sparking a major rift with the Pentagon just as hostilities commence in Iran. The conflict underscores the growing tension between AI safety-focused startups and the US military's aggressive push to integrate generative tech into modern warfare.

**Global regulators crack down on kids' social media access** · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/austria-is-pursuing-a-social-media-ban-for-kids-under-14-190755295.html?src=rss)
Indonesia just became the first Southeast Asian country to enforce a nationwide social media ban for users under 16, while Austria is preparing legislation to block kids under 14. These legislative moves arrive as platforms like Meta and YouTube face hundreds of millions of dollars in liability following devastating US jury verdicts over child safety harms.

**NASA scraps lunar space station for a $20 billion moon base** · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/science/space/nasa-pauses-its-lunar-gateway-plan-a-comet-reverses-its-spin-and-more-science-news-160000163.html?src=rss)
NASA is officially pausing its international Lunar Gateway orbital station, redirecting its budget toward establishing a permanent $20 billion moon base. The major pivot, aimed at cementing an enduring human presence on the moon before the end of the Trump administration, will rely on commercial contractors to build semi-habitable infrastructure ahead of a crewed landing planned for 2028.

**Samsung brings banner ads to its smart fridges** · [Slashdot](https://slashdot.org/story/26/03/28/0537232/ads-are-popping-up-on-the-fridge-and-it-isnt-going-over-well)
Owners of Samsung's $1,400 Family Hub smart refrigerators are furious after a software update began serving ads for water filters and Apple TV shows directly on their kitchen displays. The banner ad widget is part of a broader "Screens Everywhere" initiative, proving that even luxury home appliances are no longer safe from post-purchase monetization.

**Sony hikes PS5 prices as AI eats the memory supply** · [Slashdot](https://games.slashdot.org/story/26/03/28/1925241/sony-is-raising-playstation-5-prices-again-between-100-and-150)
Sony is raising the US price of all PlayStation 5 models by $100 to $150, pushing the base disc model to $650 and the PS5 Pro to an eye-watering $900. The culprit is a severe shortage of RAM and flash memory chips, as manufacturers shift production lines away from consumer tech to feed the insatiable demand for AI data center accelerators like Nvidia's H200.
