# Seahawks Press — 2026-08-10

## Top Takeaway
Head coach **Mike Macdonald** confirmed that the Seahawks hosted former Alabama cornerback **Terion Arnold** for a visit and tryout, but stressed that the team is conducting thorough due diligence regarding his off-the-field situation to ensure any potential signing protects the locker room culture they have built.

## Press Conferences & Interviews

**Mike Macdonald / Training Camp Press Conference - August 10, 2026** · [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aE0hPCErb8s)
Macdonald provided a massive piece of roster news by revealing the tryout of former Alabama cornerback **Terion Arnold**, noting they enjoyed a great visit but are carefully evaluating off-the-field circumstances to protect their culture. He also highlighted the intense competition in the secondary, praising **DeAnthony Bell** for getting his hands on footballs while noting his final roster status will depend heavily on special teams and personnel packages. Macdonald hinted that the team might take a 'different approach' to quarterback playtime in Saturday's preseason game, leaving snap splits between **Drew Lock**, **Sam**, and **Jaylen Milro** open for evaluation. His tone was candid and highly detailed, showing a defensive mastermind who is deeply focused on building competitive depth while protecting the building's standard.

**Mike Macdonald: "Our Offensive Line Has Had A Really, Really Good Camp" | Seattle Seahawks** · [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=itET3F5GAMQ)
In this targeted session, Macdonald offered rare and glowing praise for his starting offensive line, declaring that returning all five starters has built exceptional chemistry and 'banked reps' that can elevate the entire offense. While the starting five are firmly locked in, Macdonald emphasized that the real battle is raging further down the depth chart, with intense competition and jockeying for position at the backup tackle and interior backup guard spots. His tone was bullish and excited about the unit's progress, showing a coach who believes his trenches are ahead of schedule.

**Rylie Mills: "I Feel Better Than I Did In College" | Seattle Seahawks** · [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqdpZHT0yC4)
Young defensive tackle **Rylie Mills** delivered a high-energy, confident interview, declaring himself 100% healthy and moving 'honestly better than I did in college' after playing through a knee injury with a brace during last season's Super Bowl. Mills credited his rapid technical growth to morning film sessions with coach **AD** and pre-practice field work with coach **Hines**. He expressed immense appreciation for veterans **Leonard Williams** and **Jarran Reed**, noting that Williams routinely pulls him aside to share tips on how to maximize his length and size. Mills was loose and fired up, calling his journey from a regular-season injury to a Super Bowl sack a storybook ending, and looking ahead to dominate camp with his signature power moves.

**Football fest was a full circle moment for AJ Finley** · [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VKl7o92Va5Q)
Safety **AJ Finley** was visibly emotional yet proud as he described today's practice as a 'full circle moment' after grabbing a highlight interception on the very field where he suffered a season-ending injury during this exact practice last year. Finley was incredibly candid, sharing that staying close to the team during his grueling recovery last year helped keep his mind right and stick to his day-to-day process. He praised his wife, family, and teammates for keeping him grounded throughout the 'hell of a ride' back to full health.
