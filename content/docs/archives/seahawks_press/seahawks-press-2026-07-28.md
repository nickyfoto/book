# Seahawks Press — 2026-07-28

## Top Takeaway
Leonard Williams provided the most significant scheme revelation of camp so far, explaining that the Seahawks' new offense is utilizing so much fullback and tight end pre-snap motion that it is actively preventing the defensive line from anticipating run directions. 

## Press Conferences & Interviews

**Leonard Williams / Defensive Lineman** · [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XrS0klcNtJ0)
Entering his 12th year, Williams gave a highly candid assessment of the daily battles against the new Seahawks offense, noting that the pre-snap communication is "on another level" and heavily features 49ers-esque fullback and tight end motions. He admitted this constant movement is neutralizing his ability to cheat or pre-anticipate plays as a 3-technique, forcing the defensive line to play strictly what is in front of them. The pending free agent brushed off contract concerns, stating he performs worse when focusing on money, and confirmed the team will face the heat in upcoming joint practices in Tennessee.

**Cooper Kupp / Wide Receiver** · [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jl_fSP3WHhU)
Kupp dropped several major schematic hints regarding the new offense, enthusiastically noting the integration of "wing T stuff" and putting receivers in three-point stances to create new wrinkles. He also provided a glowing update on second-year quarterback Jaylen Milroe, stating the young passer has settled down significantly and is finally letting the ball rip with anticipation. Kupp used a significant portion of his time at the podium to advocate for natural grass at Lumen Field, pushing back on data-driven injury metrics by arguing that players' bodies simply feel the negative impact of turf. 

**Ernest Jones IV / Linebacker** · [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oSUIf3Bjar4)
A brief clip reinforced Ernest Jones IV's status as the unquestioned vocal leader of the linebacker room. He was heavily praised as the "alpha in the middle of defense" who successfully manages the unit's temperature and sets the tonal attitude for the entire operation. 

## Reading Between the Lines
There is a coordinated, locker-room-wide push to replace the turf at Lumen Field. Both Cooper Kupp and Leonard Williams went out of their way to advocate for natural grass today, indicating this is a frequent topic of conversation among players behind closed doors. On the field, the new offensive scheme is clearly designed to break traditional defensive keys; with Kupp highlighting unusual receiver alignments and Williams admitting the defense can no longer successfully guess play direction, the offense is actively weaponizing pre-snap confusion.
