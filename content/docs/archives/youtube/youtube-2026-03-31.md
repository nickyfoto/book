# YouTube — 2026-03-31

## Watch First
[How to break Magic the Gathering.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x3dE-NJ1UDQ) from *Stand-up Maths* brilliantly explains how a specific three-card loop generates a finite but uncomputable number so massive it requires a 30-tier power tower to express. It is a delightful, mind-bending watch that explores the collision of complex game mechanics and outrageous mathematics, highly recommended even if you have never touched a playing card.
