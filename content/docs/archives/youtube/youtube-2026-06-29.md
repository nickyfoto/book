# YouTube — 2026-06-29

## Watch First
**[I Was Kicked Out of China](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=38klsvCK1ik)** from The New York Times is a stark, essential watch on the reality of reporting under intense surveillance. The reporter recounts how Chinese officials and tipped-off locals physically blocked investigations into sensitive topics like domestic violence and human trafficking before ultimately canceling her visa.
