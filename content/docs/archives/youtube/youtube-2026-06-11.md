# YouTube — 2026-06-11

## Watch First
The Financial Times offers a brilliant, unexpected deep dive into the global population collapse in **[Why birth rates are falling everywhere all at once | FT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lFXmDk-tps)**. They argue convincingly that the synchronized drop in coupling and fertility across drastically different cultures points to a unified culprit: the rise of smartphones and the social media "cultural leapfrogging" that has fractured face-to-face socializing.
