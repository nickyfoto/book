# YouTube — 2026-06-09

## Watch First
The absolute standout today is **[The Love of My Life (and Why I Need to Share It with You)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lBx86QE4k3s)** from TED. Novelist Ann Patchett shares a wonderfully human story about a chance 1986 airport encounter with a Hare Krishna to frame a broader, urgent defense of reading, independent bookstores, and maintaining a "long format brain" in a hyper-connected world.
