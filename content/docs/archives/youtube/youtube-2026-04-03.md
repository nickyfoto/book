# YouTube — 2026-04-03

## Watch First
If you only watch one thing today, make it Ayşe Coskun’s [The Story You’re Not Hearing About AI Data Centers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i3CA9osjYPo), which masterfully argues that instead of just being energy hogs, AI's massive data centers could actually be scheduled to act as flexible "virtual batteries" that stabilize the renewable energy grid.

### News & Business
Global trade and geopolitical tensions dominate the feeds today, with [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ogGGWeLu8Yo) and [CNBC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EqdHVIrkpJc) both analyzing the economic fallout of President Trump's "Liberation Day" global tariffs one year later, noting rising domestic costs and a plateau in manufacturing jobs. ### Learning & Ideas
For a fascinating slice of history, [LIFEANO CLUB](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W-94JZ0zS0U) tells the intense, almost fanatical story of Eliezer Ben-Yehuda, who essentially locked his son in isolation to successfully resurrect Hebrew from a dead liturgical script into the modern spoken language of Israel. Over at the [Hoover Institution](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R0MehhNPW7c), economist David Neumark delivers a compelling, data-driven lecture arguing that the Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC) is a vastly superior tool for fighting poverty than raising the minimum wage, which he shows often misses the poorest families. In a lighter cultural critique, [Susie Woo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1spK9L6vswo) unpacks the quirks behind why British people seem to lack a modern "hustle culture," exploring how historical class structures and a strong preference for work-life balance lead to staggering amounts of personal debt.

### Tech & AI
In tech strategy, [美投侃新闻](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3ork1X_Kew) offers an excellent breakdown of how Microsoft CFO Amy Hood's cautious capital expenditure decisions—acting as the ultimate referee for scarce AI computing power—might be sacrificing short-term cloud growth but successfully protecting the company's long-term software moat. The channel also highlights a massive spike in memory chip prices, driven entirely by new AI agent workloads that demand exponentially more tokens to run high-concurrency, multi-step tasks.
