# CNBC — 2026-08-25

## Lead Story
The Trump administration's aggressive economic offensive dominated today's coverage as Canada retaliated with "dollar-for-dollar" tariffs on \$20 billion of U.S. goods, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's upscaled bond-buyback plans fueled a major resurgence of the "debasement trade" in gold and bitcoin. This aggressive policy shift sparked sharp warnings from billionaire investor Stanley Druckenmiller, who criticized the bond interventions as a dangerous and futile form of artificial yield suppression.

## Markets & Economics

Government spending worries and upscaled bond buybacks powered the "debasement trade," sending gold to a three-month high of **\$4,677.19** and bitcoin jumping **3.1% to \$79,739**; chartist Katie Stockton noted in an **[on-air technical analysis](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/25/bitcoin-is-not-overbought-but-isnt-oversold-anymore-says-fairlead-strategies-katie-stockton.html)** that the flagship cryptocurrency is currently breaking out of a long-term basing phase. In trade developments, Canadian trade minister Dominic LeBlanc outlined Ottawa's "dollar-for-dollar" retaliatory duties on \$20 billion in U.S. goods during a **[Squawk Box appearance](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/25/canadian-minister-leblanc-on-retaliating-against-trump-tariffs-were-not-waiting-by-the-phone.html)**.
