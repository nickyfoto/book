# Apple Releases 26.4 RCs, Cracks Down on 'Vibe Coding', and Primes iPhone 18 Pro Cameras — 2026-03-18

## Highlights

Today's ecosystem news is dominated by the release candidate (RC) builds for Apple's 26.4 operating system updates, bringing critical bug fixes and welcome quality-of-life improvements to the Mac and iPhone. Meanwhile, developer relations take center stage as Apple secures a decisive App Store legal victory and simultaneously begins cracking down on a new wave of AI-powered "vibe coding" applications.

## Top Stories

- **iOS and macOS 26.4 Release Candidates Arrive**: Apple has seeded the RC builds for iOS, iPadOS, macOS Tahoe, watchOS, tvOS, and visionOS 26.4 ahead of a planned public launch next week. Notable features include offline Shazam integration in Control Center, a keyboard accuracy fix that addresses a bug affecting fast typists, native charge limit toggles for Mac, and an overhaul to Family Sharing that finally allows adult members to use their own payment methods. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/18/apple-seeds-ios-26-4-release-candidate/))
- **Apple Blocks AI 'Vibe Coding' App Updates**: Apple is reportedly blocking App Store updates for AI "vibe coding" tools like Replit and Vibecode, which allow users to generate functional apps using natural language. Apple cites App Store Guideline 2.5.2, taking issue with these apps generating and executing software within an embedded web view, which essentially alters their core functionality and circumvents the traditional App Store review process. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/18/apple-pushing-back-on-vibe-coding-iphone-apps-developers-say/))
- **Decisive Victory in Musi App Store Lawsuit**: A federal judge dismissed a lawsuit brought by the music streaming app Musi, confirming Apple's explicit right to remove apps from the App Store at any time "with or without cause". The judge also heavily sanctioned Musi's legal team for making up facts and relying on baseless claims during the litigation over the YouTube-based music player's removal. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/18/apple-wins-victory-musi-app-store-lawsuit/))
- **iPhone 18 Pro Rumored for Major Camera Overhauls**: Six months out from launch, rumors point to substantial camera upgrades exclusive to the iPhone 18 Pro lineup. The main 48-megapixel camera is expected to gain a variable aperture for superior depth-of-field control, while the Telephoto lens will see an upgraded, larger aperture to vastly improve low-light photography. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/18/iphone-18-pro-rumors-features-list/))
- **Perplexity Launches Agentic 'Comet' Browser on iOS**: AI startup Perplexity has brought its Comet browser to the iPhone, featuring a built-in Comet Assistant. To bypass iOS sandboxing limitations, Comet handles autonomous "browse for me" tasks by temporarily transferring a user's cookies to a virtualized cloud browser that navigates the web on their behalf. ([Source](https://www.macstories.net/news/comet-is-the-first-agentic-browser-for-ios-worth-trying/))
