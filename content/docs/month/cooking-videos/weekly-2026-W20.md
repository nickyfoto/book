## Highlights by Theme

### Recipes & Tutorials
Over on 詹姆士官方專屬頻道, Chef James delivers an incredibly accessible guide to foolproof, tender chicken in his [White Cut Chicken (白斬雞) tutorial](#) by using boneless thighs and a stress-reducing steeping method. For those looking to master traditional wok frying, the masters at 老饭骨 offer a brilliant deep dive in [【國宴大師•焦溜肉片】](#), breaking down a 1970s state banquet crispy sliced pork dish with precise starch ratios and bias-cutting techniques. If you need a lightning-fast weeknight lifesaver, Rosalina's Kitchen (蘿潔塔的廚房) shares a visually stunning, five-minute, music-driven tutorial for juicy, sweet and sour [五分鐘上菜！豆芽蔥段豬肉捲](#) (bean sprout and scallion pork rolls). Finally, MASA's channel brings a clever, appetizing fusion twist in [泰式屋台風雞肉炒河粉](#), swapping out traditional rice noodles for chewy, premium Japanese wheat noodles in a sweet and sour Chicken Pad Thai.

### Food Science & Tips
Chef James dropped essential culinary science this week, explaining that adding salt at the very end of a high-heat stir-fry prevents [Hand-Torn Cabbage (手撕高麗菜)](#) from turning into a soggy, watery mess. He also breaks down the chemistry of aromatics, noting that you must dissolve salt and sugar in natural juices or water before pouring hot oil over a scallion-ginger mix, doing so in stages to coax out complex, layered flavors. Meanwhile, Chef Chen shares a brilliant mechanical trick for notoriously spongy vegetables: coating scored eggplant in egg wash to physically fill its empty internal cavities, preventing it from soaking up excess oil during the pan-frying process.
