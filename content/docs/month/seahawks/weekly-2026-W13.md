# Seahawks — Week of 2026-03-24 to 2026-03-26

## Top Story

The biggest news out of the VMAC this week is Jaxon Smith-Njigba officially signing a massive, multi-year $168.6 million extension, making him the highest-paid wide receiver in NFL history. Behind-the-scenes footage highlighted the momentous occasion alongside head coach Mike Macdonald and GM John Schneider, securing a vital piece of Seattle's championship core for the foreseeable future. The front office and the 12s alike celebrated the milestone, praising JSN's elite on-field production and his stellar character off the field, including his regular visits to the local burn unit.

## News & Buzz

**[Defending Champs to Host 2026 Kickoff]** · [Reddit](https://reddit.com/r/seahawks)
It is officially confirmed that the defending Super Bowl LX champion Seattle Seahawks will open the 2026 NFL season at home in prime time on Wednesday, September 9. While the specific Week 1 opponent remains a mystery until later this spring, 12s are already gearing up to watch the championship banner get raised.

**[Bobo and Depth Pieces Re-Sign]** · [Twitter/X](https://twitter.com/seahawks)
Fan-favorite wide receiver Jake Bobo has officially returned to Seattle, declaring he would choose the organization "10 out of 10 times" and sparking "2026 MVP" proclamations from the faithful. The front office also shored up the roster by re-signing linebacker Chazz Surratt and securing defensive secondary depth with cornerback Shemar Jean-Charles and safety D'Anthony Bell.

**[Massive VMAC Expansion Planned]** · [Reddit](https://reddit.com/r/seahawks)
The Seahawks are moving forward with an 80,000-square-foot expansion to the Virginia Mason Athletic Center, featuring a new three-story structure set to be completed by the fall of 2028. Fans on r/Seahawks are taking this major infrastructural investment as a highly optimistic sign of long-term ownership stability, signaling that any prospective buyer intends to keep the franchise rooted in the Pacific Northwest.

**[Draft Rumors & Running Back Visits]** · [Reddit](https://reddit.com/r/seahawks)
With the draft looming, John Schneider is doing his due diligence by hosting veteran free agent RB Najee Harris, Toledo prospect Chip Trayanum, and LSU safety AJ Haulcy for visits. Meanwhile, the fan base is actively debating the backfield's future, with many 12s pushing back against rumors of drafting UW power back Jonah Coleman, preferring that the team targets a breakaway runner to replace the dynamic element lost with K9.

**[Jalen Sundell's Incredible Journey]** · [r/Seahawks](https://reddit.com/r/seahawks)
An amazing story circulated this week about starting center Jalen Sundell's path to hoisting the Lombardi Trophy. After being rejected by the Browns, Sundell was driving for Uber before finding his home in Seattle and eventually anchoring the offensive line for our Super Bowl run.

## 12s Pulse

The 12s are absolutely thriving, still riding the immense high of our Super Bowl LX victory and the front office's massive financial commitment to JSN. Between actively debating draft targets, creating bead-art homages to the "Shaheed Stampede" playoff run, and tracking season ticket relocation logistics, the fanbase's mood is defined by supreme confidence and stability.
