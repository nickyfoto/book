# NFL News — Week of 2026-08-15 to 2026-08-21

## Read First
**The single most important story of the week is the NFL's historic crackdown on training camp brawls, slapping both the Dallas Cowboys and New Orleans Saints with unprecedented \$500,000 fines (\$1 million combined).** This aggressive financial warning shot, combined with a new rule allowing the league office in New York to directly eject players for throwing punches, signals a zero-tolerance policy that will force coaches to heavily prioritize team discipline alongside physical preparation.

## Week in Review
**The dominant written-news narratives this week centered on powerhouse contenders locking down cornerstone pieces through high-stakes extensions while actively scouring the veteran market for championship-caliber depth.** Simultaneously, training camps entered a grueling middle phase where **viciously competitive joint practice sessions resulted in a mounting toll of devastating, season-altering injuries** to key defensive anchors and starting interior linemen. This physical toll has turned the final weeks of the preseason into an intense race against time to rebuild roster depth before the regular season begins.
