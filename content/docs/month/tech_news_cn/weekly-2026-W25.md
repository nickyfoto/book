# Chinese Tech — Week of 2026-06-13 to 2026-06-19

## Week in Review
This week, the Chinese tech ecosystem was heavily dominated by the meteoric rise of native AI agents transforming fundamental OS and application architectures, shifting the industry paradigm from simple conversational interfaces into autonomous, cross-app execution. Amidst this rapid software evolution, geopolitical tensions continued to fragment the global AI landscape, highlighted by DeepSeek's massive $7.4 billion funding round that cements domestic AI independence, contrasting sharply with the swift, government-mandated takedown of Anthropic's flagship model in the US.

## Engineering & Dev
The AI coding space experienced a seismic shift as SpaceX acquired Cursor in a $60 billion all-stock deal, immediately introducing a 1.5T parameter model and an agent-native "Origin" Git platform designed to challenge Microsoft's GitHub. Simultaneously, Zhipu AI launched GLM-5.2, proving its formidable capability in complex million-token context coding tasks and matching the performance of models like Claude Opus 4.8. On the orchestration and infrastructure front, Google open-sourced Agent Substrate and Agent eXecutor to provide Kubernetes-like, "zero-idle" scheduling for AI agents. Meanwhile, traditional infrastructure teams also saw vital updates, with Broadcom releasing Spring Boot 4.1 featuring delayed physical database connection fetching, and Slack securely migrating over 700 EMR jobs from SSH access to a REST-based scheduling architecture. Finally, security professionals were put on high alert following the discovery of critical directory traversal and SQL injection vulnerabilities within the BigAnt enterprise IM system.

## Products & Digital
The integration of AI into consumer software reached a new milestone as Huawei's HarmonyOS transitioned to an intent-driven, Agent-based architecture, granting the Xiaoyi assistant deep OS-level privileges to dynamically coordinate background workflows across applications. Similarly, Alipay began beta-testing an "Agent OS" interface, allowing users to command its onboard assistant, "Abao," to autonomously navigate mini-programs like McDonald's ordering or Ant Forest energy collection. In the hardware space, Apple’s iOS 27 and macOS 27 developer betas introduced "Liquid Glass" UI tweaks and strongly hinted at upcoming foldable devices via new API code strings. DJI escalated an ongoing patent war with Insta360 in US courts just as it launched the new dual-camera Osmo Pocket 4P. Furthermore, sspai published a comprehensive review of the Shokz OpenDots 2 open-ear earbuds, praising their superior sound-leakage control and spatial audio capabilities over the budget-friendly Air model.

## News & Commentary
DeepSeek secured an unprecedented 50 billion RMB funding round while maintaining absolute control for CEO Liang Wenfeng through a highly unusual LP structure and a five-year lockup period, highlighting the immense negotiating leverage of top-tier Chinese AI firms. However, global market fragmentation was starkly evident as the US government forced Anthropic to pull its newly launched Claude Fable 5 model offline over "jailbreak" vulnerabilities, while the New York Times Chinese edition analyzed China's distinct absence from the massive hardware windfall currently enriching chipmakers in Taiwan and South Korea. In major corporate leadership shifts, Noam Shazeer, the co-lead of Google's Gemini, departed for OpenAI in a move that emphasizes ongoing developer frustration with Google's complex bureaucracy and productization bottlenecks.

## Also Worth Knowing
* SpaceX went public on the Nasdaq in a record-shattering IPO, reaching a $2.1 trillion valuation and officially minting CEO Elon Musk as the world's first trillionaire.
* WeChat Pay is actively testing an "AI Exclusive Card" that allows users to set strict authorization limits and spending caps for autonomous AI-driven purchases.
* OpenAI's Codex introduced a "Record & Replay" feature that learns software operation visually by observing graphical user interfaces, bypassing the need for traditional API integrations.
* Smart cleaning brand Dreame initiated a major organizational restructuring, shifting its heavy-asset divisions into a research institute and reportedly cutting 12% of its workforce.
* Google filed a major lawsuit alongside the FBI against a Chinese cybercriminal network utilizing Gemini to orchestrate massive AI-generated phishing campaigns impersonating US government services.
