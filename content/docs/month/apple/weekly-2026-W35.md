# Apple — Week of 2026-08-22 to 2026-08-28

## Week in Review
In a historic week for Apple, the company announced that Tim Cook is stepping down as CEO after 15 years to become Executive Chairman, handing leadership to hardware chief John Ternus on September 1 ahead of the newly announced September 9 "Surprise and Shine" keynote. Apple also blindsided the industry with an unannounced desktop silicon drop, unveiling the industry's first 2nm processor in the M6 alongside the quad-die M5 Ultra powerhouse inside refreshed Mac mini and Mac Studio models. At the same time, unprecedented software branch leaks and tester feedback brought Apple's upcoming hardware roadmap into sharp focus, detailing the foldable iPhone Ultra and camera-equipped AirPods ahead of a major fall product cycle.

## Top Stories
**Tim Cook Transitions to Executive Chairman as John Ternus Takes Reins as CEO** · [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/24/tim-cook-replaced-steve-jobs-15-years-ago-today/)
Marking exactly 15 years since succeeding Steve Jobs, Tim Cook is stepping down as Apple CEO on September 1 to become Executive Chairman, focusing on regulatory policy and global government relations. Senior Vice President of Hardware Engineering John Ternus will assume the chief executive position just ahead of Apple's autumn hardware launches, capping an era in which Cook grew Apple's market valuation to roughly \$4.5 trillion and oversaw the transition to Apple silicon. The company's September 9 fall keynote will stand as the first official product presentation under the Ternus administration.

**Apple Confirms September 9 "Surprise and Shine" Keynote and Splits iPhone Release Cadence** · [TidBITS](https://tidbits.com/2026/08/28/apples-surprise-and-shine-event-set-for-9-september-2026/)
Apple officially announced its fall hardware showcase for Wednesday, September 9, 2026, streaming live from Apple Park to unveil the iPhone 18 Pro lineup, Apple Watch Series 12, Apple Watch Ultra 4, and its first foldable phone. In a strategic realignment, Apple is splitting its product release schedule to concentrate September entirely on premium flagships while deferring the standard iPhone 18 and iPhone Air 2 until spring 2027. Customer pre-orders for the flagship devices will kick off on Saturday, September 12—intentionally shifting past the September 11 anniversary.

**Next-Gen Apple Silicon Debuts: 2nm M6 and Quad-Die M5 Ultra Power New Desktops** · [MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/news/apple-announces-new-m6-and-m5-pro-mac-minis-along-with-m5-max-and-m5-ultra-mac-studios/)
In an unexpected late-August announcement, Apple debuted its next-generation silicon, introducing the M6 as the industry's first 2-nanometer chip alongside the quad-die M5 Ultra with up to an 80-core GPU and 1.2TB/s of memory bandwidth. The M6 migrates a three-tier CPU layout down to entry-level silicon with a Dual 16-core Neural Engine, though Apple is skipping M6 Pro and Max variants to prioritize the M7 family for 2027. Available in newly upgraded Mac mini and Mac Studio configurations shipping in September, Apple even automatically upgraded unfulfilled M4 Mac mini customer orders to the new M6 and M5 Pro models at no extra cost.

**Foldable "iPhone Ultra" Roadmap Surfaces with 7.8-Inch Canvas and Key Compromises** · [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/23/apple-foldable-iphone-early-tester-thoughts/)
Early tester reports praised Apple's upcoming book-style foldable for its hinge durability, pocketability, and tablet-like multitasking across an unfolded 7.8-inch inner screen. To achieve an ultra-thin 4.5mm profile, however, Apple has implemented notable hardware compromises, including substituting Face ID with a power-button Touch ID, omitting a telephoto lens, leaving out MagSafe, and removing the physical Action button. Despite an expected price tag exceeding \$2,000 and consumer survey skepticism over high costs, market analysts project Apple will ship over 10 million units in its first year.

**Internal Software Merge Error Leaks Camera AirPods and Smart Home Pipeline** · [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/24/how-apple-leaked-itself/)
A software branch merge error in the macOS Tahoe 26.7 Release Candidate inadvertently exposed references to more than 20 unreleased Apple devices to developers. The leak confirmed an instructional video for camera-equipped AirPods featuring Visual Intelligence slated for late 2027, as well as an A18-powered 7-inch "HomePad" smart home hub. The disclosure aligns with Apple retail stores actively redesigning their perimeter display areas to prepare for an aggressive smart home push this fall.

## Also Worth Knowing
*   **Apple Services Rates and Maps Ads**: Apple increased monthly subscription pricing for standalone Apple TV from \$12.99 to \$14.99 (\$119 annually) and Apple One Individual to \$21.95, while officially rolling out sponsored search ads across Apple Maps in the U.S. and Canada.
*   **Siri AI Class Action Settlement Timeline**: Court filings established that eligible purchasers of iPhone 15 Pro and iPhone 16 devices can submit claims between September 21 and December 21, 2026, for payouts between \$25 and \$95 under Apple's \$250 million settlement regarding delayed Apple Intelligence features.
*   **iCloud+ Hide My Email Policy Reversal**: Following significant user pushback over concerns that disposable email aliases would be easily blocked by websites, Apple reversed plans to migrate iCloud+ Hide My Email addresses to a shared private.icloud.com domain, keeping them on icloud.com.
*   **Digital Driver's Licenses Expand to Virginia**: Virginia became the 15th U.S. state to launch digital driver's licenses and state IDs in Apple Wallet, allowing residents to present authenticated identity credentials at participating businesses, venues, and TSA airport checkpoints.
