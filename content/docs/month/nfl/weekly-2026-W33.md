### Game Analysis & Strategy
The schematic trend of the week is a dramatic league-wide return to under-center alignments, designed to build lethal play-action attacks and keep hyper-aggressive defenses off-balance. Washington is leaning into this look to help rookie **Jayden Daniels** (**[Dan Quinn on Jayden Daniels’ Next Step & the Commanders’ New Offense](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rw7JJN6XnVs)**), while **Joe Burrow** completed over half his snaps under center in a massive shift from Cincinnati’s shotgun-heavy past (**[Concerns for Joe Burrow, Bengals in preseason? + ALL EYES on Tua Tagovailoa this season 👀 | NFL Live](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4_YeTSNkdlk)**). At the same time, defensive coordinators are struggling with an explosion of trick plays while desperately engineering new ways to collapse the Eagles' infamous short-yardage "tush push" (**[Can defenses continue to 'close the gap’ on the Eagles’ tush push? | Pro Football Talk | NFL on NBC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tNoqK-oAHIU)**).
