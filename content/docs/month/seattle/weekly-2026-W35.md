# Seattle Local — Week of 2026-08-22 to 2026-08-28

## Top Story
Over 90% of Seattle Public Schools educators voted to authorize a strike after contract negotiations stalled over an \$87 million district budget deficit and special education workload concerns. The walkout authorization threatens to delay the scheduled September 2 start of classes for roughly 50,000 students while leaving families across the city scrambling for child care. The Seattle Education Association and district leaders remain locked at the bargaining table with the current labor contract set to expire on August 31.

## Local News
**[Mayor Katie Wilson Recall Petition Withdrawn as Public Safety Pressures Mount]** · [The Seattle Times](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/politics/mayor-katie-wilson-recall-effort-dismissed-before-it-got-off-the-ground/?utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=owned_echobox_tw_m&utm_source=Twitter#Echobox=1787869002-2)
A legal petition to recall Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson was dismissed in King County Superior Court after petitioners withdrew their filing to consult legal counsel, though organizers maintain the challenge is not over. The effort unfolded alongside a 20% decline in Wilson's favorability ratings amid heightened public alarm over gun violence and City Council demands for a comprehensive public safety strategy. Councilmembers also continue pressing the executive administration for formal explanations regarding the recent firing of former Police Chief Shon Barnes.

**[NFL Unanimously Approves Record \$9.6 Billion Seahawks Sale to Khosla Family]** · [KOMO News](https://komonews.com/sports/nikos-sports-zone/seahawks-sale-khosla-family-allen-estate-approved-players-reactions-coach-mike-macdonald-seattle-nfl-football-super-bowl-champs-12s)
NFL team owners voted unanimously at a special league meeting to approve the \$9.6 billion purchase of the Seattle Seahawks from the Paul G. Allen Trust by the billionaire Khosla family. The transaction marks the largest franchise purchase in NFL history and concludes nearly three decades of Allen estate ownership. In tandem with the ownership transition, the Seahawks locked down three-time Pro Bowl defensive tackle Leonard Williams with a three-year, \$90 million contract extension.

**[SPD Freezes Hiring Bonuses Amid Budget Constraints While Search for New Chief Opens]** · [KOMO News](https://komonews.com/news/local/seattle-police-department-spd-law-enforcement-budget-concerns-hiring-incentives-officers-guild-council-sara-nelson-lateral-bonuses-renton-police-chief-september-1)
Mayor Katie Wilson launched a nationwide search for the Seattle Police Department's next permanent chief, requiring candidates to commit to visible, accountable leadership across local neighborhoods. Concurrently, SPD announced an indefinite freeze on all recruitment bonuses and lateral incentives beginning September 1 until future budget forecasts become clearer. The suspension drew fierce criticism from the Seattle Police Officers Guild, which labeled the decision reckless while the department remains operating hundreds of officers below staffing goals.

**[Boeing Union Overwhelmingly Rejects Contract, Authorizing Potential October Strike]** · [KOMO News](https://komonews.com/news/local/boeing-strike-engineers-technical-workers-speea-union-reject-contracts-approve-strike-authorization-october-contarct-expires-negotiations)
Engineers and technical personnel represented by the SPEEA union overwhelmingly voted down Boeing's proposed collective bargaining agreements covering two major bargaining units. Members simultaneously authorized a strike that could begin as early as October if further negotiations fail to reach an agreement before the current pacts expire. The vote significantly escalates labor friction for the planemaker as it works to maintain demanding commercial production targets.

**[Sound Transit Advances \$87 Million Fare Gate Pilot as Regional Transit Shifts]** · [The Seattle Times](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/transportation/seattle-light-rail-inches-closer-to-installing-fare-gates/?utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=owned_echobox_tw_m&utm_source=Twitter#Echobox=1787873269-1)
Sound Transit's governing board approved an \$87 million pilot program to install physical fare gates at up to 14 high-traffic light rail stations by 2029–2030, departing from its long-standing proof-of-payment system to improve revenue recovery. The policy change arrives alongside broader network adjustments, including the retirement of six express bus routes—such as Route 550—to transition commuters over to expanded Link light rail service. Meanwhile, the City of Seattle activated a dedicated eastbound bus lane on Denny Way to reduce chronic delays on the heavily traveled Route 8.
