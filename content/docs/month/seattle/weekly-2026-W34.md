# Seattle Local — Week of 2026-08-15 to 2026-08-21

## Top Story
**Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson** is facing an intense political crisis as a formal recall petition over her public safety response is scheduled for a **King County Superior Court** hearing on **August 31**. The legal challenge, which Wilson has opted to skip by sending legal counsel instead, comes as a newly released **Seattle Metropolitan Chamber of Commerce** poll reveals her favorability rating has plummeted 20 percentage points to a low of **37%**. Voters cited deep frustration with the city’s handling of public safety, crime, and homelessness, mounting significant pressure on her administration.

## Local News
**[Boeing SPEEA Union Overwhelmingly Rejects Contract, Authorizes Strike]** · [The Seattle Times](https://www.seattletimes.com/business/boeing-aerospace/boeings-contract-offer-just-not-enough-some-speea-workers-say/#Echobox=1787059348-2)
Members of Boeing’s white-collar union, **SPEEA**, initially voiced strong opposition to the company's latest contract proposal, stating that it lacked an adequate guaranteed wage increase and fell short of regional engineering commitments. In a decisive vote, engineers and technicians overwhelmingly rejected the offer and authorized a strike if a new agreement is not reached by **October 6**. This escalating labor dispute represents a critical threat to the Puget Sound’s aerospace economy, affecting thousands of regional workers.

**[West Seattle Light Rail Extension Delayed to 2035]** · [KIRO 7](https://www.kiro7.com/news/local/west-seattle-light-rail-extension-delayed/HRMOJLXRWJEZRGT7SC4PB4G4AE/)
**Sound Transit** has officially postponed the projected opening of the **West Seattle** light rail extension to **2035**, representing a multi-year delay of three to five years. The delay is driven by a staggering **\$3.5 billion agency budget gap**. This timeline shift has sparked significant frustration among West Seattle residents who face rising taxes with no immediate transit relief.

**[Pioneer Square Shootout Ignites Debate Over City Surveillance Cameras]** · [KOMO News](https://www.youtube.com/watch/v/7uJUQw9iw1o)
A chaotic gunfight in **Pioneer Square** that left one person injured has fueled an intense municipal debate regarding public surveillance. The controversy erupted after it was revealed that three critical city security cameras near the scene had been offline since the end of the **FIFA World Cup**. While anti-surveillance activists rallied at City Hall to demand the removal of existing safety cameras, a newly released poll showed that **75% of Seattle voters** support utilizing public safety cameras in active investigations.

**[Seattle Pending Home Sales Plummet Amid Tech Sector Shifts]** · [KOMO News](https://komonews.com/news/local/pending-home-sales-fall-drastically-in-seattle-area-amid-changing-tech-industry-technology-houston-national-average-real-estate-brokerage-artificial-intelligence-emerald-city)
Pending home sales in the Seattle metro area dropped **15.6% last month**, marking the sharpest decline of any metropolitan region in the nation. Real estate analysts point to changing dynamics within the local technology industry, particularly shifts related to **artificial intelligence**, as a primary driver of this market cooling. However, affordability remains a severe barrier, with Seattle's median home-sale price holding at a steep **\$809,479**, which is roughly double the national average.

**[Edmonds Approves 24/7 School Zone Speed Camera Enforcement]** · [KOMO News](https://komonews.com/news/local/school-zone-speed-cameras-edmonds-city-council-vote-tickets-citations-fines-student-pedestrian-safety-24-hours-grace-period-warnings-speeding)
The **Edmonds City Council** has officially approved keeping its school zone speed cameras operational 24 hours a day, seven days a week, to bolster pedestrian and student safety. Since these cameras were installed two and a half years ago, data shows speeding has dropped by **50%** during school hours. Under the new year-round policy, drivers caught speeding in school zones at any hour could face automated fines of up to **\$290**.

**[Starbucks and TikTok Announce Corporate Layoffs in Seattle and Bellevue]** · [FOX 13](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/starbucks-announces-details-layoffs-200-plus-seattle-headquarters-employees) / [The Seattle Times](https://www.seattletimes.com/business/technology/tiktok-lays-off-75-bellevue-workers-mostly-in-e-commerce-division/)
**Starbucks** has filed a **WARN notice** with Washington state announcing the layoff of **224 corporate employees** at its SoDo headquarters, primarily affecting store designers and IT personnel who declined to relocate to Nashville. On the Eastside, **TikTok** is permanently laying off **75 employees** from its Bellevue office, targeting its cuts within the **TikTok Shop** e-commerce division. These corporate downsizings come as King County's regional unemployment rate sits at approximately **4.9%**.
