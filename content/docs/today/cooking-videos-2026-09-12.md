# Cooking Videos — 2026-09-12

## Watch First
The single video most worth watching today is **[Mastering Scallion and Sichuan Pepper Oils](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=foRFNqlEN94)** by veteran chef channel **鲁菜老厨陈宗明**, where master chef Chen Zongming breaks down foundational Lu cuisine oil-infusion techniques. He demonstrates how combining animal lard and vegetable peanut oil while maintaining gentle thermal extraction around 130°C–140°C pulls deep aromatic flavor from scallions and Sichuan peppercorns without scorching or bittering the oil, giving home cooks an indispensable secret for elevates stir-fries and braises.
