## News & Reviews

**[Fable’s Main Quest Will Take About 20 Hours to Beat](https://www.ign.com/articles/fables-main-quest-will-take-about-20-hours-to-beat)** · IGN
Playground Games associate game directors Will Kennedy and Craig Littler confirmed that **Fable** will feature roughly 15 to 20 hours of mainline quest gameplay, accompanied by an additional 15 to 20 hours of side quests and open-world exploration. While considerably leaner than 50-plus hour open-world behemoths like *The Witcher 3* or *Elden Ring*, it stands as the longest campaign in franchise history and is backed by limitless life simulation systems that players can pursue indefinitely. It is shaping up to deliver focused storytelling without unnecessary filler when it launches on **February 23, 2027** for PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S.

**[GTA 5’s Michael Actor Defends GTA 6 After Doubters Say Rockstar’s Games Look Too Similar](https://www.ign.com/articles/gta-5s-michael-actor-defends-gta-6-after-doubters-say-rockstars-games-look-too-similar)** · IGN
Ned Luke, the actor behind *Grand Theft Auto 5* protagonist Michael De Santa, fired back at vocal online critics who claim **Grand Theft Auto 6** looks too similar to its 2013 predecessor, calling naysayers "out of their minds". Luke pointed out that the 13-year technical evolution is immediately visible in genuine human locomotion, detailed character models, vehicle destruction, and visual polish that surpasses even *Red Dead Redemption 2*. His spirited defense adds veteran weight to Rockstar's showcase ahead of the game's anticipated launch on **November 19, 2026** for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.

**[4 Penny Coffins Lets You Play 19th-Century Detective – Or a 10-Foot Demon Ripping Friends in Half](https://www.ign.com/articles/4-penny-coffins-preview-jack-the-ripper)** · IGN
Other Ocean Interactive debuted a gameplay look at **4 Penny Coffins**, an eight-player social deduction title described as Jack the Ripper meets *Among Us* with a dose of Tim Schafer-style irreverent humor. Seven investigators utilize distinct forensic abilities—such as blood spatter and fingerprint analysis—to expose the murderer before another killing occurs, while one undercover player can transform into a 10-foot demonic beast to butcher teammates. Featuring a striking monochromatic graphic novel art style inspired by *From Hell* and an offline solo "Magistrate" deduction mode, it is shaping up to be an inventive party game for mystery and horror fans.

**[The Pokémon Company on PokémonXP’s Lottery System, Long Lines, and Limited Access to Workshops](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-pokmon-company-on-pokmonxps-lottery-system-long-lines-and-limited-access-to-workshops)** · IGN
The Pokémon Company International responded to attendee frustration regarding badge shortages, long lines, and exclusive workshop lotteries at the inaugural **PokémonXP** fan convention in San Francisco. Global events producer Chris Brown explained that an interest list lottery was the only stable solution after more than 130,000 fans registered for just 50,000 available passes. Brown emphasized that attendee feedback will dictate future operational adjustments, while confirming plans to expand future World Championships internationally and double competitive player qualification over the next five years.

**[GTA 6 Fans Think They Found Jason and Lucia’s Vice City Apartment in Real Life](https://www.ign.com/articles/gta-6-fans-think-they-found-jason-and-lucias-vice-city-apartment-in-real-life)** · IGN
Dedicated Grand Theft Auto sleuths used historical Google Maps Street View data dating back to 2013 to identify the real-life architectural inspiration for Jason and Lucia’s Vice City apartment as Miami's former Belmar Condominium. Public property records indicate the 1973 complex was sold for \$12 million in 2022 and subsequently demolished, meaning players cannot visit the physical location today. Archived real estate listings revealed that Rockstar meticulously reproduced both the external facades and internal apartment layouts seen during recent gameplay footage.

**[Every Cosmere Tabletop Game Based on Brandon Sanderson’s Epic Book Universe](https://www.ign.com/articles/all-cosmere-tabletop-and-board-games)** · IGN
Brotherwise Games is actively expanding author Brandon Sanderson’s fantasy universe across the tabletop scene with titles spanning *Call to Adventure: The Stormlight Archive*, the *Cosmere RPG*, and *Mistborn: The Deckbuilding Game*. The releases translate novel lore into strategic card drafting, Allomantic metal burning, and narrative roleplaying systems. Enthusiasts can also look forward to upcoming expansions, including *The Well of Ascension* for the deckbuilder, dedicated *Mistborn* TTRPG rulebooks, and the area-control board game *War for Roshar* arriving on Kickstarter.
