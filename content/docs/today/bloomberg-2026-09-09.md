# Bloomberg — 2026-09-09

## Lead Story
Global energy markets were rocked as Brent crude surged past \$100 a barrel to top \$101 for the first time since July following a dramatic military escalation between the US and Iran in the Strait of Hormuz. US Central Command destroyed five Iranian crude oil tankers near Kharg Island after Tehran launched ballistic missiles at an American warship, prompting Iranian officials to warn of a prolonged, intensified conflict. The sudden disruption to Middle Eastern oil flows has triggered a broad selloff in global equities and reignited fears of a worldwide inflationary spike.

## Markets & Economics
* **[Yen Surge Triggers Wall Street Split as Bessent Jawbones Traders](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-09/hedge-funds-bet-yen-will-strengthen-beyond-150-by-year-end)**: US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent issued a direct warning to currency markets by asserting "I am the house" regarding yen intervention and predicting Bank of Japan policy shifts. * **[China Inflation Accelerates on Rising Energy Costs and AI Capex](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-09/china-consumer-producer-price-pressures-revived-by-energy-spike)**: China’s consumer price inflation picked up for the first time since April while producer prices increased faster than expected, driven by crude oil cost spikes and industrial demand tied to artificial intelligence hardware.
