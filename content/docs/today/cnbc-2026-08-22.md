# CNBC — 2026-08-22

## Lead Story
Trade talks between the U.S. and Canada **collapsed late Friday**, triggering **50% tariffs** on approximately **\$20 billion** worth of Canadian imports starting Saturday morning, with Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney suspending negotiations and vowing a **dollar-for-dollar retaliation**.

## Markets & Economics

In an effort to preserve the stock market rally, the U.S. Treasury Department announced it would **more than double the size of its buybacks** of longer-dated government debt, though yields rebounded as Brent crude oil climbed over **5% on the week** to close at **\$94.39 per barrel**. Meanwhile, geopolitical premiums remained elevated as U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent warned of the **"greatest coordinated economic isolation in history"** against Iran, ahead of a highly anticipated economic measures briefing on Monday.
