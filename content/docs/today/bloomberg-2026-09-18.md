# Bloomberg — 2026-09-18

## Lead Story
The Bank of Japan raised its benchmark interest rate by 25 basis points to 1.25%, executing its fastest pace of monetary tightening in 36 years as it grapples with persistent inflation and explicit policy calls from Washington. However, two dissenting votes on the policy board and cautious commentary from Governor Kazuo Ueda sparked a sharp decline in the yen, as markets interpreted the split decision as a dovish signal. The rate hike underscores mounting pressure on global central banks navigating persistent price pressures and volatile bond markets.
