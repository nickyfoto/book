# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-04-02

## Top Story
Anthropic accidentally leaked the source code for its [Claude Code](https://www.infoq.cn/article/oyztKc9IQUguMOOx6imT) agent, revealing not only its architectural "harness" but also controversial privacy practices and an "undercover mode" for open-source contributions. The leak sparked a massive open-source frenzy, with a Korean developer using AI to rewrite the entire codebase in Python within hours, setting a rapid GitHub stars record. This incident underscores the extreme speed of AI-assisted code propagation and raises serious questions about copyright and corporate transparency in the AI era.

## Engineering & Dev
Cloudflare engineers successfully "slop forked" Next.js over a single weekend using AI, creating a production-ready Vite-based alternative called [Vinext](https://www.infoq.cn/article/XNfsebiwgEd1hbcissWd) for just $1,100 in API tokens. This rapid AI-driven refactoring contrasts with the industry's ongoing debate over [Harness Engineering](https://www.infoq.cn/article/vblM3MlOEs86dmVdH8d1), where Anthropic is building thick, complex control frameworks to guide AI agents, while OpenAI's Codex team advocates for thinner harnesses that grant models more autonomy within secure sandboxes. In cloud infrastructure, [SenseTime](https://www.infoq.cn/article/vh9j9d6ceChVul80VLG4) detailed its AI-native compute pools and virtual clusters to enable seamless resource flow and second-level scaling for complex training and inference workloads. Concurrently, Tencent Cloud's [TDSQL-C](https://www.infoq.cn/article/meRvbxZQtilVUHLijL1Y) is leveraging AI to reinvent database query optimization, utilizing large language models to tackle complex HTAP workloads and execution plan management. Despite the rapid integration of AI tools, UML co-founder Grady Booch argued in a recent [podcast](https://www.infoq.cn/article/lOu1d22q6Qom0qp5ifxP) that LLMs are an architectural "dead end" lacking true creativity, urging developers to take responsibility for AI-generated code rather than blaming the tools. Meanwhile, Fudan University launched the [4th World AI for Science Competition](https://www.infoq.cn/article/UMPFZoYAiPsvqbIoETR1), featuring a unique track where autonomous scientific agents attempt to independently match or exceed research from top-tier CNS journals.

## Products & Digital
Xpeng aggressively updated its [MONA M03 2026 model](https://www.ifanr.com/1660774), adding the Turing AI chip for advanced autonomous driving and an upgraded cabin, while keeping the starting price at a disruptive 119,800 RMB. On the consumer electronics front, Apple quietly released the [AirPods Max 2](https://www.ifanr.com/1660740) for 3,999 RMB, featuring the H2 chip, USB-C, and significantly improved adaptive noise cancellation. Vivo's new [X300 Ultra](https://www.ifanr.com/1660620) was also highlighted for pushing mobile imaging boundaries, utilizing the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5's 20-bit AI ISP to capture full-focal-length 4K 120fps Log video. In software productivity, [Tencent Meeting](https://www.ifanr.com/1660770) rolled out deep AI integrations, offering specialized summary templates for different meeting types and allowing users to directly query the "Yuanbao" AI about the meeting's context. For developers looking for a bit of fun, the Claude Code leak revealed an unreleased April Fools' feature called [Claude Buddy](https://sspai.com/post/108197), an ASCII Tamagotchi-style virtual pet that hatches and interacts with users in the terminal.

## News & Commentary
The capital markets are experiencing a distinct shift in [AI valuations](https://www.infoq.cn/article/jTBOKdUUCdR86YYD1cBF), with Anthropic becoming the hot commodity on secondary markets while OpenAI shares face cooling demand. Locally, [Zhipu AI](https://www.infoq.cn/article/0Bu3qODSVfDfWgxFsNVQ) released a blockbuster earnings report showing a 131.9% revenue surge to 724 million RMB; despite raising API prices by 83%, API usage spiked 400%, signaling explosive enterprise demand. ByteDance's Volcengine mirrored this trend, announcing that its [Doubao LLM](https://www.ifanr.com/1660703) now processes over 120 trillion tokens daily, a 1000-fold increase over the past year driven heavily by enterprise agent deployments and the new Seedance 2.0 video generation API. In regional tech news, Baidu's [Apollo Go](https://cn.nytimes.com/business/20260402/baidu-robotaxi-wuhan-china/) robotaxi fleet suffered a major system failure in Wuhan, causing multiple vehicles to freeze in traffic and trapping passengers. Furthermore, China successfully [extradited Li Xiong](https://cn.nytimes.com/asia-pacific/20260402/china-cambodia-huione-extradition/), the former chairman of Huione Group from Cambodia, dismantling a key node in a global money-laundering network heavily utilized by cyber scam operations.
