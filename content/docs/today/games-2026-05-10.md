# Gaming News — 2026-05-10

## Top Story
*Darkest Dungeon* developer Red Hook Studios has firmly declared they will never use artificial intelligence to recreate the voice of the late narrator Wayne June. Despite June generously giving the studio permission before his passing in January 2025, co-founder Chris Bourassa declined the offer, stating the actor's delivery was fundamentally human and the team refuses to erode his legacy with hollow machine imitations. 

## News & Reviews

**[Lego Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight Quietly Adds Denuvo DRM](https://www.ign.com/articles/lego-batman-legacy-of-the-dark-knight-quietly-adds-denuvo-drm-weeks-before-launch)** 
Warner Bros. and TT Games have stealthily baked the controversial Denuvo DRM into *Lego Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight* just weeks ahead of its May 22 launch. This is a massive red flag for PC gamers, as the Unreal Engine 5 title already demands an absurd 16 GB of RAM and requires frame generation just to hit 30 fps, sparking legitimate fears of an *Arkham Knight*-level performance disaster on PC.

**[Mortal Kombat II Box Office & Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-devil-wears-prada-2-blocks-box-office-challenge-from-mortal-kombat-ii-crosses-400-million)** 
The *Mortal Kombat II* movie punched its way to a solid $63 million global opening weekend, scoring an impressive 8/10 review from IGN for clearing the video game adaptation bar. While it couldn't topple the box office juggernaut *The Devil Wears Prada 2*, it's on track to outperform its 2021 predecessor, and Warner Bros. already has a third MK film in development.

**[Every LEGO Game You Can Play on PS5](https://www.ign.com/articles/lego-games-on-ps5-in-order)** 
If you're craving some brick-building action, there are over 30 LEGO games currently available on the PS5 across three console generations.
