# Company@X — 2026-08-21

## Signal of the Day
**Microsoft** has officially taken delivery of its first production Nvidia **Vera Rubin** GPUs for Azure data centers, marking the first physical deployment of Nvidia’s highly anticipated next-generation AI architecture in the public cloud.

## Key Announcements

**Starcloud** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/PhilipJohnston/status/2090816398973178041#m)
Starcloud has raised **\$250 million** at a **\$2.3 billion valuation** to build orbital AI compute capacity, backed by Manhattan West, Nvidia, Cisco, Benchmark, and others. The space startup is moving into a new 100,000-square-foot facility next week to ramp up satellite production to **100 units per week**. This aggressive scale-up aims to bypass terrestrial data center power and real estate constraints by deploying high-density compute infrastructure directly into space.

**OpenAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/OpenAI/status/2090885187634905500#m)
OpenAI is dropping API and credit pricing of its flagship **GPT-5.6 Sol** model by over **20% for the next three months**. The discount is available on the API immediately and is rolling out across eligible plans for ChatGPT Work and Codex credits, while standard consumer subscription pricing remains unchanged. This aggressive temporary cut targets enterprise developers, amplifying the price wars among top-tier foundation model providers.

**Waymo** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Waymo/status/2090891693147013179#m)
Waymo has launched its newest autonomous vehicle, the **"Ojai,"** which is now active and available to all riders in San Francisco, Los Angeles, and Phoenix. The vehicle features automated sliding doors, extensive legroom, and built-in **Gemini in Waymo** integration on the cabin's screen. Riders can use voice commands hands-free to adjust climate controls, adjust seating positions, and ask for localized recommendations.

**AWS** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/awscloud/status/2090907611172385096#m)
Amazon Web Services has launched **AWS Glue 6.0**, introducing a **30% price reduction** alongside support for Apache Iceberg v3 with VARIANT shredding. The serverless integration engine now runs Spark 4.1 and Python 3.13 runtimes, supporting Spark Declarative Pipelines and Real-Time Mode. The release is structurally designed to streamline ETL operations, allowing developers to write significantly less boilerplate orchestration code.

**Google** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2090897214285508832#m)
Google is offering college students free one-year plans for Google AI Pro (US) and Google AI Plus (internationally), complete with a dedicated learning hub and diagnostic study tools. Most notably, Google is introducing **Deep Research into Gemini Live**, enabling students to trigger comprehensive, multi-step research reports using voice commands. Once initiated, the research agent works in the background, allowing the user to lock their screen or switch tasks while it generates the final report.

**xAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/grok/status/2090879561982451931#m)
xAI has expanded access to its autonomous **"Grok Bot"** agents to all SuperGrok Plus, Cursor Pro+, and Cursor Teams subscribers, alongside rolling out a limited free trial. Grok Bots are designed to function as always-on AI colleagues equipped with their own virtual computers to execute software workflows without logging off. The deployment signals xAI’s shift from search-centric chatbots toward agentic productivity tools capable of independent computer use.

**LightwheelAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/LightwheelAI/status/2090800612745117725#m)
In partnership with Hugging Face, LightwheelAI has open-sourced **EgoSuite-Open100K**, a massive 100,000-hour egocentric human dataset for physical AI training. The commercially licensed dataset features over 15,000 real-world tasks and scenes—spanning factory floors, kitchens, and retail spaces—fully annotated with hand/body pose data and wrist camera coverage. The first 10,000 hours are live immediately, establishing an open public data layer to scale embodied robotics.
