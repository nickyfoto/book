## Key Announcements

**Y Combinator** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ycombinator/status/2096970626036855197#m)
Y Combinator has released a deep dive into the state of "agent harnesses," showing that the same model weights scoring only 30% on ARC-AGI can achieve 95% accuracy when paired with an optimized harness. Leaders and founders explored transitioning harnesses from simple prompt scaffolding to a full "agentic OS" that enables persistent computation, memory caches, and agent-to-agent communication. This represents a crucial paradigm shift from standard prompting to building complex multi-agent frameworks, illustrating how specialized scaffolding exposes hidden reasoning capabilities of existing frontier models.

**Google Cloud** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleCloudTech/status/2096991886586057015#m)
Google Cloud has rolled out new administrative settings within the Gemini Enterprise console specifically designed to manage and govern the use of `@antigravity` inside organizations. The new controls consolidate spend, security, observability, and usage metrics into a single dashboard, allowing enterprises to maintain full administrative oversight. This update signals a major push by Google to provide robust, IT-governed guardrails for advanced agentic toolkits in enterprise environments.

**gentrajectory** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/gentrajectory/status/2097015968287617258#m)
In a major breakthrough for hardware automation, gentrajectory demonstrated reinforcement learning (RL) being used to teach a Kimi base model to design physical medium-power transformers. The resulting model successfully met 93% of unseen engineering specifications, compressing multi-week physical hardware engineering workflows into a few minutes of inference. This represents a powerful strategic signal that AI is moving beyond software generation to actively optimize and design the complex physical power infrastructure it runs on.

**adamdotnew** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/adamdotnew/status/2096995659803136134#m)
A new SOTA in agentic CAD has been established using GPT-6 Astra, enabling the AI agent "Adam" to design a complete, tool-free flat-six engine from a written description. The agent successfully generated all six pistons, connecting rods, split crankcase, and flywheel as an editable parametric CAD file, adjusting bores and rod sizes automatically when the piston diameter was changed partway through. The developer is now partnering with physical hardware engineering teams to run entire engineering processes—from first specification to BOMs and assembly—through the agent.

**Google DeepMind** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/dharshsky/status/2096891498813927525#m)
A newly published paper in *Nature Machine Intelligence* by researchers at Google DeepMind and Princeton provides causal evidence that LLMs actively use confidence to guide their behavior rather than just passively reporting it. The study proved that boosting or suppressing a model's confidence directly alters its decision to either provide an answer or abstain entirely. This represents a massive milestone in LLM self-calibration, offering a path to build more reliable systems that know when to halt or ask for help instead of hallucinating.

**OpenRouter & Stripe** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/thdxr/status/2096714610027954340#m)
OpenRouter and fast-growing YC startups are currently facing a massive, coordinated fraud wave targeting LLM API access, prompting direct intervention from Stripe to help defend their platforms. Developers warn that if this threat is not quickly resolved, accessing LLMs will become significantly more annoying and restricted for legitimate builders due to added security hurdles. This highlights the critical security and payment infrastructure bottlenecks that AI distribution networks face as they scale at unprecedented speed.
