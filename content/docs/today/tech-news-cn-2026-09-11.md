# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-09-11

## Top Story
Apple's inaugural foldable smartphone, the iPhone Duo, has sparked an intense response across Chinese tech circles, prompting scalpers to list early reservation slots for up to 99,000 RMB while real secondary market premiums hover around 5,000 RMB. Samsung US mocked the hardware release on X as "heating up leftovers," while Chinese tech commentators highlighted iOS 27's deeper UI adaptation—relocating the Dock to the right side, introducing vertical Dynamic Island alignments, and deploying fluid Liquid Glass layout adjustments to bypass physical hinge creases. Despite its \$2,000 entry price (15,999 RMB in China), industry analysts project the eSIM-only flagship could ship 5 million units in 2026, capturing nearly a quarter of the global foldable market.
