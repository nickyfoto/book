## Top Stories

**[SpaceX Unveils $55 Billion Plan to Build AI Chips in Texas]** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/926356/spacex-terafab-plant-cost-ai-chips)
Elon Musk is aggressively pushing into the AI semiconductor space with "Terafab," a massive Austin-based chip plant that could eventually see up to $119 billion in total investment. The ambitious facility aims to produce enough chips to support 200 gigawatts of computing power annually, while SpaceX has simultaneously inked a deal allowing Anthropic to utilize its entire Memphis data center. This marks a major escalation in the race to control the physical infrastructure underpinning the AI boom.

**[Google Kills the Fitbit Screen to Take on Whoop]** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/925458/google-health-fitbit-air-ai-coaching-wearables-fitness-trackers)
Google is pivoting its wearable strategy with the $99 Fitbit Air, a screenless sensor puck designed for continuous, low-friction health tracking. Launching alongside a complete rebrand of the Fitbit app into "Google Health," the device leans heavily on a new $10/month Gemini-powered AI Health Coach to interpret your metrics rather than just displaying raw data. It's a clear shot at the subscription-heavy Whoop ecosystem, signaling that the future of fitness wearables is about ambient data collection and AI interpretation, not just step counting.

**[Apple's Camera-Equipped AirPods Near Mass Production]** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-07/apple-s-camera-equipped-airpods-reach-advanced-testing-stage-in-ai-device-push)
Apple's first major hardware play for the AI era appears to be a new version of AirPods equipped with low-resolution cameras. Currently in the design validation test stage, the earbuds won't take standard photos; instead, they will constantly capture visual information so users can query Siri about their surroundings, such as asking for recipe ideas based on ingredients sitting in front of them. This pushes Apple further into the ambient computing space, turning wireless earbuds into multimodal AI assistants.

**[Cloudflare Slashes 20% of Workforce in 'AI-First' Pivot]** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-07/cloudflare-to-cut-one-fifth-of-workers-in-move-to-ai-first-model)
Cloudflare is laying off more than 1,100 employees globally, missing revenue estimates as it restructures the company around an agentic, AI-first operating model. The massive cut highlights the brutal reality of the current tech landscape: even critical infrastructure providers are aggressively shedding human staff to fund and accelerate their transition toward autonomous AI systems.

**[OpenAI Slashes Agent Latency with New WebSocket API]** · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/05/openai-websocket-responses-api/)
OpenAI has completely overhauled how AI agents communicate with its models, introducing a WebSocket-based execution mode that drops the traditional HTTP request-response cycle. By maintaining a persistent, bidirectional connection, the update slashes latency by up to 40% and solves the "cold start" problem for multi-step reasoning workflows, a massive win for platforms building complex AI tool chains.

**[Hackers Hold Canvas LMS Hostage, Prompting University Outages]** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/926458/canvas-shinyhunters-breach)
Universities across the US went dark as the Canvas learning management platform was brought down by the cybercrime group ShinyHunters. The hackers defaced school login pages with extortion messages after breaching student names, ID numbers, and private messages, highlighting the severe vulnerability of consolidated educational infrastructure.
