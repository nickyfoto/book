# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-07-25

## Top Story
The artificial intelligence model landscape saw significant shifts today with Anthropic's release of Claude Opus 5, which reportedly matches the performance of Fable 5 at only half the cost. The new closed-source model maintains the API pricing of Opus 4.8 while introducing an adjustable "effort" gear that significantly enhances its autonomous verification capabilities in complex tasks like coding and long-document reasoning.

## Engineering & Dev
On the front-end architecture side, GitHub recently modernized its navigation for Issues, shifting to a local-first approach to drastically reduce developer-perceived latency. As detailed in **[GitHub Issues 大改造：用缓存和预取，让页面打开快了数倍](https://www.infoq.cn/article/yDgq3fh4YxZM93u21Kr5)**, the engineering team utilized IndexedDB for persistent storage, memory caching, and predictive prefetching via Service Workers to cut median latency from 1,200ms down to 700ms,. 

Meanwhile, as AI Agents enter core enterprise workflows, traditional rule-based security is failing against semantic attacks. At the upcoming AICon Shenzhen, a Tencent security expert will present on **[以 AI 对抗 AI：构建越用越强的 Agent Runtime 安全防线](https://www.infoq.cn/article/fovd670ZuVMSMI7Z0zO4)**, advocating for an "AI against AI" defense system that handles prompt injection, intent deviation, and data loss prevention through continuous self-iteration based on production data,. 

In model training developments, the WeChat AI team collaborated with Tsinghua University to introduce the DynamicRubric post-training framework. This system allows an evaluation standard generator and a policy model to evolve together dynamically, a method which has already been deployed to fully upgrade WeChat's search AI base model,. Additionally, Tencent's Hunyuan officially merged its large language model and multimodal teams into a unified foundational model department to improve cross-modal efficiency.

## Products & Digital
For digital lifestyle and home management, *sspai* published a highly practical guide on **[看不见、摸得着的家居要素：聊聊湿度管理的方法与实践](https://sspai.com/post/112093)** just in time for the damp plum rain season in the Jiangnan region. The article breaks down the science of absolute versus relative humidity and dew points, detailing exact mathematical formulas to calculate dehumidification requirements for various scenarios like wardrobes or camera dry boxes, while comparing the real-world efficiency of air conditioners versus dedicated dehumidifiers,,. 

On the gadget front, the new **[OPPO K15](https://www.ifanr.com/1673124)** officially went on sale for 2,299 RMB, featuring a Dimensity 7360 SUPER chip, a massive 8000mAh battery, and an active cooling fan built directly into the chassis alongside a vapor chamber. 

In a more eccentric hardware experiment highlighted in today's digest, a developer taped an Apple AirPod to a frisbee to create a makeshift telemetry device. Using a custom app called Kinapod Disc, the setup leverages the earbud's motion sensors to track spin rate, release speed, and hang time, though its long-term durability against crashes remains highly questionable,.

## News & Commentary
The debate over AI openness took center stage as Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang posted his first-ever tweet to co-sign an open letter with over 20 institutions advocating for the protection of open-weight models,. This united front follows the recent open-sourcing of Moonshot AI's Kimi K3, which sparked intellectual property disputes and regulatory concerns from closed-source competitors, pushing the open-vs-closed route debate back into the spotlight.

In a related governance push, Elon Musk suggested in an interview that leading AI labs should establish a mutual peer-review system for frontier models prior to their public release. He argued that competing labs are much closer to the R&D frontline and better positioned than governments to identify technical risks like safety bypasses, even offering to temporarily set aside his differences with OpenAI CEO Sam Altman for the sake of public safety,.

In the semiconductor space, domestic memory chip maker ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT) has signed a five-year, $7 billion supply agreement with ByteDance as AI data center expansion drives up memory demand. After turning around a decade of losses with a massive 719% year-over-year revenue increase in Q1, CXMT is now preparing for an $8.6 billion IPO in Shanghai.
