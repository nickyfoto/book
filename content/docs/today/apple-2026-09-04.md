# Transition, Foldables, and Future Roadmaps: The New Apple Era Takes Shape — 2026-09-04

## Highlights
**The Apple ecosystem is on the cusp of its most historic leadership and product transition in fifteen years.** Following John Ternus officially stepping in as CEO on September 1, the tech giant is gearing up for its highly anticipated "Surprise and Shine" keynote next week, headlined by the rumored "iPhone Ultra" foldable and the 2nm-based iPhone 18 Pro. However, supply chain bottlenecks for the foldable form factor and surprising feature deprecations in upcoming OS updates reveal a complex roadmap as Apple navigates the next generation of its hardware and software platforms.

## Top Stories
- **[John Ternus Takes the Helm as Apple's New CEO](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/04/new-profile-of-john-ternus-offers-insight-into-life-and-reputation-of-apple-ceo/)**: John Ternus has officially assumed the role of CEO of Apple as of September 1, succeeding Tim Cook, who transitions to Executive Chairman after fifteen years leading the company. A fresh profile from the *Financial Times* describes Ternus as "highly competent and unshowy," noted for his passion for amateur car racing, which partners describe as reflecting a calm, dedicated drive for excellence. Ternus's first major public appearance in his new capacity will be hosting next week's highly anticipated hardware keynote. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/04/new-profile-of-john-ternus-offers-insight-into-life-and-reputation-of-apple-ceo/))

- **[Foldable 'iPhone Ultra' Limited to a Few Hundred Units Daily in Production](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/04/iphone-ultra-production-extremely-limited/)**: Due to Apple's exceptionally high quality control standards, initial production of the upcoming book-style foldable iPhone is currently limited to just "a few hundred units a day". Suppliers at Foxconn are reportedly working around the clock to improve yields for hinge performance and display flatness, which has delayed commercial production by weeks. This bottleneck could lead to severe supply shortages at launch, with rumors suggesting a staggered market rollout initially restricted to the United States. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/04/iphone-ultra-production-extremely-limited/))

- **[iPhone 18 Pro Pre-orders Rumored for Unusual Saturday Midnight Time Slot](https://www.macworld.com/article/3222711/iphone-18-pro-pre-orders-saturday-midnight-pacific-time.html)**: In a break from traditional Friday morning pre-sales, Apple is rumored to open pre-orders for the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone Ultra, and Apple Watch Series 12 on Saturday, September 12 at midnight Pacific Time. The shift from the typical Friday slot is out of respect for September 11, which falls on the Friday following the event and is a national day of remembrance and mourning in the U.S.. Apple previously utilized Saturday pre-orders eleven years ago in 2015 for the iPhone 6s and 6s Plus for the exact same reason. ([Source](https://www.macworld.com/article/3222711/iphone-18-pro-pre-orders-saturday-midnight-pacific-time.html))

- **[Apple TV and HomePod Overhauls Planned Alongside AI Home Security Camera for 2027](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/04/apple-plans-to-launch-home-security-and-monitoring-service-report/)**: Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman reports that Apple is developing its first privacy-forward home security camera and monitoring service slated for a 2027 launch. The camera will uniquely employ local artificial intelligence to monitor the surrounding environment rather than recording raw video footage, preserving maximum user privacy. This hardware will anchor an overhaul of Apple's smart home pipeline, which also includes upcoming refreshes to the Apple TV 4K, HomePod mini, and a dedicated smart home hub display. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/04/apple-plans-to-launch-home-security-and-monitoring-service-report/))

- **[Claude Joins CarPlay as Anthropic Adds Voice-Based In-Car Chatbot Support](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/04/anthropics-claude-is-coming-to-carplay/)**: Anthropic has updated its Claude iOS app to introduce official Apple CarPlay support, enabling conversational voice interactions inside vehicles. Claude becomes the fifth major AI chatbot to integrate with CarPlay since Apple introduced support in iOS 26.4, joining ChatGPT, Grok, Perplexity, and Meta AI. Drivers can initiate new voice chats or review recent threads via the hands-free CarPlay template, though Claude cannot execute system-level vehicle or phone controls. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/04/anthropics-claude-is-coming-to-carplay/))

- **[watchOS 27 to Deprecate Several Features, Including Walkie-Talkie and Multitasking Gesture](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/04/watchos-27-removes-three-features-that-apple-watch-users-might-miss-after-updating/)**: The upcoming public release of watchOS 27 is set to retire three notable features that users may miss after updating. Most prominently, the peer-to-peer Walkie-Talkie app is being removed entirely, and the useful Digital Crown double-click multitasking shortcut will be disabled. Additionally, pressing the Digital Crown will now default to a new "dynamic app grid" showing Siri and five suggested apps rather than launching the traditional full app list. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/04/watchos-27-removes-three-features-that-apple-watch-users-might-miss-after-updating/))
