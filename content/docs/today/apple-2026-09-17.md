# Apple Ecosystem Daily: iPhone 18 Pro & Watch Series 12 Reviews Arrive, macOS Golden Gate Deep Dive, and UK Encryption Backdoor Legal Challenge — 2026-09-17

## Highlights
On the eve of official store availability for the **iPhone 18 Pro lineup**, **Apple Watch Series 12**, **Ultra 4**, and **AirPods 5**, initial hands-on reviews and unboxings have landed, spotlighting major hardware advances such as the 2nm A20 Pro chip, a physical variable aperture camera, and continuous heart rate tracking. Simultaneously, Apple is making waves across software and legal fronts with the unexpected seeding of the **iOS 27.2** and **macOS 27.2** developer betas, a massive 50-feature breakdown of **macOS 27 Golden Gate**, and a high-stakes court challenge against secret UK government encryption backdoor mandates.

## Top Stories
- **iPhone 18 Pro and Apple Watch Series 12 Reviews Land Ahead of Launch**: Initial reviews and unboxing reports praise the iPhone 18 Pro's **2nm A20 Pro processor**, physical variable aperture camera system, 3x larger vapor chamber cooling, and significantly accelerated 55-minute fast charging. Meanwhile, coverage of the Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 highlights **continuous 5-second heart rate monitoring** and dynamic daily **Readiness scores**, though watchOS 27 reassigns the double-click crown shortcut away from app switching. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/review/apple-watch-series-12-ultra-4/))

- **Apple Challenges UK Government Secrecy Over Secret Encryption Backdoor Order**: In a hearing before the Investigatory Powers Tribunal in London, Apple argued that the UK government's "Neither Confirm Nor Deny" stance regarding secret demands for an **iCloud encryption backdoor** is farcical and directly undermines open justice. Apple's legal team is seeking to lift the strict gag order so the public can openly discuss government mandates attempting to compromise user privacy. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/17/apple-pushes-uk-court-to-lift-secrecy-around-encryption-backdoor-order/))

- **Looming iPhone Price Hikes as Apple Accepts 30%–40% Higher Memory Costs for 2027**: Driven by massive AI datacenter demand for DRAM and NAND memory chips, Samsung has reportedly quoted Apple component prices that are **30% to 40% higher** for Q1 2027. Having already raised hardware prices across Macs, iPads, and iPhones in 2026 due to component shortages, analysts warn these escalating supplier costs could force further retail price increases in 2027. ([Source](https://www.macworld.com/article/3237776/more-iphone-price-hikes-loom-as-apple-reportedly-accepts-brutal-memory-deal.html))

- **Apple Seeds Beta 1 for iOS 27.2, iPadOS 27.2, and macOS 27.2 Golden Gate**: Just days after releasing its major annual platform upgrades to the public, Apple has seeded the first developer betas for **iOS 27.2**, **iPadOS 27.2**, and **macOS 27.2 Golden Gate**. The 27.2 update completely skips the 27.1 beta cycle and offers early indications of expanding **Siri AI** language support to French and Spanish. ([Source](https://osxdaily.com/2026/09/16/first-beta-of-ios-27-2-ipados-27-2-macos-27-2-available-for-testing/))

- **Renowned Photographers Benchmark iPhone 18 Pro Camera Upgrades**: Professional photographers Tyler Stalman and Austin Mann released detailed camera reviews focusing on the iPhone 18 Pro's physical **variable aperture mechanism** and manual Pro controls. Both reviews demonstrate that while auto-aperture excels in challenging lighting, new capabilities like **Apple Reference Image** and Photographic Styles 3 with Film grain give creators unprecedented control over authenticity and depth. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/17/photographer-austin-mann-reviews-the-iphone-18-pro-camera/))

- **watchOS 27 Deprecates Digital Crown Double-Click Shortcut Across Watch Models**: Apple's watchOS 27 update reassigns the Digital Crown double-click gesture to trigger the upcoming **Live Rewind** audio transcript feature on Series 12 and Ultra 4 models. Consequently, users updating older Apple Watch models lose the ability to toggle between recent apps via double-click, even though older hardware cannot run Live Rewind. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/17/watchos-27-removes-useful-apple-watch-feature/))
