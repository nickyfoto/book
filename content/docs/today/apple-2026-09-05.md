# A Historic Hand-Off: Ternus Steps Up as Foldable iPhone Ultra and 2nm Silicon Beckon — 2026-09-05

## Highlights
**The Apple ecosystem is on the verge of a historic transformation as John Ternus officially steps up as CEO to lead Apple's upcoming "Surprise and Shine" event featuring the folding iPhone Ultra and next-gen 2nm silicon.** At the same time, accessory makers at IFA 2026 are showcasing cutting-edge thermal and solid-state charging designs to support these hardware leaps. Additionally, Apple's platform sees major momentum this holiday weekend with highly anticipated indie app launches and massive Labor Day sales on popular hardware and lifetime software deals.

## Top Stories
- **[John Ternus Succeeded Tim Cook Ahead of Apple's Special Event]**: John Ternus has officially taken over as Apple's CEO, with Tim Cook transitioning to Executive Chair after 15 years leading the company. In his first employee memo, Ternus expressed gratitude to Cook and teased a "huge launch" scheduled for next week. His first public showcase as headliner will occur on September 9 at Apple's "Surprise and Shine" event, where he will introduce the company's latest hardware lineup. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/05/top-stories-suprise-and-shine-preview/))
- **[M6 Chip Architecture Previews TSMC 2nm Power of the Upcoming A20 Pro]**: Apple's recent unveiling of the M6 chip has provided key architectural insights into the A20 Pro processor slated for the iPhone 18 Pro. **The chip is expected to move to TSMC's 2nm (N2) node, offering up to an 18% performance increase and 30% higher power efficiency.** Bandwidth improvements are expected to be modest due to the continued use of Samsung-sourced LPDDR5X memory, though performance will be enhanced by advanced Wafer-Level Multi-Chip Module (WMCM) packaging. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/05/what-m6-tells-us-about-a20/))
- **[Foldable iPhone Ultra Form Factor Detailed Ahead of Debut]**: Apple's highly anticipated book-style folding iPhone, expected to be named the iPhone Ultra, will sport a 5.5-inch outer display and a 7.6-inch inner display. Real-world comparisons with the identically sized Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 reveal that the iPhone Ultra will offer an iPad mini-like workspace that comfortably folds down for portability. **The ultra-thin premium device is expected to retail starting at \$2,000, feature dual rear cameras, and retain MagSafe charging capabilities.** ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/05/iphone-ultra-form-factor-comparison/))
- **[Apple Introduces Submission Cap on Security Bug Bounty Portal]**: To manage a flood of AI-generated vulnerability reports, Apple has implemented a submission cap and a 30-day cooldown period on its bug reporting portal. While designed to prevent triage teams from being overwhelmed, security advocates warn the restrictions lock out legitimate researchers reporting high-severity exploit chains. **Because malicious actors do not face submission throttles, throttling defensive researchers could lead to an asymmetric security vulnerability.** ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/05/capping-bug-bounty-submissions-is-the-wrong-response-in-the-ai-era-of-security-threats/))
- **[Enterprise AI Demand Spikes Out-of-Season Mac Mini and Studio Refreshes]**: Apple pulled forward the announcements of its latest Mac mini and Mac Studio desktop systems due to unexpectedly massive demand from enterprise AI buyers. **The global memory shortage combined with this enterprise surge had previously left many Mac desktop configurations out of stock for months.** In response, Apple held dedicated corporate-focused events with executives from major AI firms like Anthropic to highlight the hardware's business capabilities. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/05/top-stories-suprise-and-shine-preview/))
- **[Indie App Spotlight: KeyAlarm Pioneers iOS 26's AlarmKit Framework]**: KeyAlarm has launched on the App Store, allowing users to automatically convert critical calendar events into persistent alarms based on custom keywords. Utilizing the newly introduced AlarmKit framework in iOS 26, the app supports multi-alarm setup for single events and calendar-specific triggers to prevent users from missing important appointments. **The application is available for free with a 30-day trial, followed by a one-time \$0.99 purchase.** ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/05/indie-app-spotlight-keyalarm-calendar-events-into-alarms/))

**[Apple @ Work: Capping bug bounty submissions is the wrong response in the AI era of security threats]** ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/05/capping-bug-bounty-submissions-is-the-wrong-response-in-the-ai-era-of-security-threats/))
This editorial critiques Apple’s decision to impose a submission cap and a 30-day cooldown on its vulnerability reporting portal. **While Apple designed this cap to mitigate a massive surge in AI-generated "slop" reports, it has inadvertently blocked legitimate security startups like Bynario from submitting high-severity exploits.** The author highlights the recent \$116 million Coldcard cryptocurrency hack as evidence that AI tools are now highly effective at uncovering years-old logic errors in code. It is a critical read because it exposes the asymmetry of defense throttling in a landscape where attackers face no submission caps or cool-off periods.
