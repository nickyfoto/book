# CNBC — 2026-06-01

## Lead Story
**[Nvidia's aggressive entry into the PC processor market](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/06/01/d-a-davidsons-gil-luria-says-nvidias-push-into-pc-chips-is-a-very-big-deal.html)** with its new Arm-based chip and RTX Spark superchip is redefining the semiconductor landscape. As D.A. Davidson's Gil Luria highlighted today, this pivot directly challenges incumbents Intel and AMD while setting the stage for a new era of agentic AI.

## Markets & Economics
The U.S.-Iran ceasefire talks are at an impasse, rattling markets and pushing Brent crude up 2.45% to $93.35 and WTI up 2.8% to $89.78. **[Former Defense Secretary Mark Esper noted on air](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/06/01/former-defense-secretary-mark-esper-says-u-s-iran-deal-is-broken-down-for-the-foreseeable-future.html)** that the deal is broken down "for the foreseeable future". President Trump shrugged off the collapse in an interview with CNBC's Eamon Javers, claiming oil prices will soon drop "like a rock" despite the geopolitical volatility. Meanwhile, the S&P 500 closed at a record high, though analysts warn that market breadth is narrowing in a manner eerily similar to the 2000 dotcom bubble.

## Business & Earnings
**[Berkshire Hathaway made a $6.8 billion bet on the housing market](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/06/01/berkshire-hathaway-buys-taylor-morrison-for-6-point-8-billion.html)** by acquiring Taylor Morrison Home at a 24% premium. **[Taylor Morrison CEO Sheryl Palmer called the alignment "very rare"](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/06/01/taylor-morrison-ceo-says-berkshire-hathaway-deal-marks-aa-very-exciting-timea-for-the-company.html)** and a signal that the housing cycle is poised for a turnaround. In the tech space, **[Hewlett Packard Enterprise shares skyrocketed over 30%](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/06/01/hewlett-packard-enterprises-stock-jumps-more-than-20percent-on-blockbuster-quarter.html)** after posting a blockbuster fiscal second quarter with server revenue blowing past estimates. Additionally, **[Barry Diller's People Inc. launched a $48.30-per-share cash bid](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/06/01/barry-diller-to-make-18-billion-bid-for-mgm.html)** for casino giant MGM Resorts.

## Investing & Commentary
SoftBank CEO Masayoshi Son told CNBC from Paris that the current AI boom is "50x bigger" than the dotcom revolution, advising investors to treat any market correction as a prime buying opportunity. **[Fundstrat's Tom Lee echoed this bullishness](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/06/01/we-could-see-some-of-the-stock-market-gains-in-our-lifetime-after-2026-says-fundstrats-tom-lee.html)**, projecting massive stock market gains extending well beyond 2026. **[Allianz's Mohamed El-Erian is also leaning into this momentum](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/06/01/allianzs-mohamed-el-erian-i-continue-to-raise-my-targets-in-asia-and-tech-not-but-europe.html)**, noting he continues to raise his targets for Asia and tech while remaining cautious on Europe.
