## News & Reviews

**[Rockstar Defends Firing 31 GTA 6 Developers at Tribunal as Miami Approves \$3M Marketing Deal](https://www.ign.com/articles/gta-6-developer-rockstar-says-it-was-forced-to-put-privacy-films-on-its-buildings-after-drones-were-used-to-snap-photos-through-office-windows)** · IGN
An employment tribunal in Glasgow has kicked off where Rockstar Games is defending its dismissal of 31 UK developers over alleged Discord leaks, revealing extreme security measures like installing privacy films on office windows to block spying drones. Meanwhile, Miami Beach city commissioners voted 4-3 to approve a \$3 million "discreet" *GTA 6* marketing campaign that will directly fund local football field renovations. This marks a major moment for gamers tracking the road to *GTA 6*'s November 19 launch, demonstrating both Rockstar's hypervigilant secrecy and Vice City's real-world anticipation.

**[Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Legacy Review: A Retro Masterpiece That Holds Up](https://www.ign.com/articles/grand-theft-auto-vice-city-the-legacy-review)** · IGN
IGN published a retrospective review of 2002’s *Grand Theft Auto: Vice City*, celebrating how Ray Liotta’s voiced Tommy Vercetti, expanded vehicle variety, and iconic 1980s soundtrack redefined open-world gaming just 12 months after *GTA III*. Despite quirky auto-targeting and a lack of mid-mission checkpoints, its sun-kissed neon atmosphere and radio lineup remain unmatched in gaming history. It serves as an essential nostalgic look back as fans prepare to return to Vice City in *GTA 6*.

**[Space Marine 3 to Benefit From Enhanced Swarm Tech Built for Toxic Commando](https://www.ign.com/articles/how-warhammer-40000-space-marine-3-will-benefit-from-tech-improvements-made-for-john-carpenters-toxic-commando)** · IGN
Saber Interactive's Tim Willits confirmed that the proprietary swarm and AI technology refined during development of *John Carpenter's Toxic Commando* will directly power the upcoming *Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 3*. The studio aims to build on *Space Marine 2*'s massive success by expanding play environments, improving enemy AI, and evolving its engine technology without using generative AI. For 40K fans, this signals even larger and more intense battles against galactic threats on the horizon.

**[Tim Schafer Reflects on Double Fine Going Independent After Xbox Split](https://www.ign.com/articles/i-really-want-game-pass-to-work-after-navigating-the-xbox-reset-double-fine-boss-tim-schafer-is-indie-once-again)** · IGN
Following Double Fine's split from Microsoft, studio head Tim Schafer discussed returning to independent status and launching an Amnesia Fortnight Kickstarter alongside the new Double Fine Action Labs label for experimental micro-games. Schafer expressed ongoing support for subscription models like Game Pass while reflecting on the industry's severe four-year economic downturn. He also noted that he would happily make *Brutal Legend 2* if a backer provided \$100 million and Jack Black agreed to return.

**[Emberville Preview: A Charming Mashup of Stardew Valley and Diablo](https://www.ign.com/articles/emberville-is-a-charming-blend-of-stardew-valley-and-diablo-ign-preview)** · IGN
Cygnus Cross’ debut title *Emberville* delivers an intriguing blend of ARPG combo-based combat, rich pixel-art aesthetics, and cozy gathering and village-building systems ahead of its October early access release. The preview highlighted impressive voice work from talent like Matt Mercer and satisfying multi-hit martial mechanics, though questions remain on how seamlessly crafting will mesh with dungeon crawling. It stands out as an exciting crossover title capable of appealing to both action-RPG veterans and cozy sim fans.

**[Nintendo Wins Legal Victory to Ban Switch Piracy Devices Across Europe](https://www.ign.com/articles/nintendo-secures-legal-victory-against-sale-of-switch-game-piracy-devices)** · IGN
The District Court of The Hague ruled in favor of Nintendo, making it unlawful to sell the MIG Switch and similar flash-cart piracy devices across Europe. The court ordered specific online retailers to immediately stop offering these circumvention tools that bypass console anti-piracy measures. Nintendo emphasized that this legal action protects both its own intellectual property and independent game developers who rely on legitimate sales.

**[A Whisper of Fall: Jinyiwei Brings Martial-Arts Wuxia Spectacle to Soulslikes](https://www.ign.com/articles/a-whisper-of-fall-jinyiwei-brings-serious-martial-arts-spectacle-to-soulslikes-ign-preview)** · IGN
Set during the Ming Dynasty, this upcoming action-RPG combines fast wuxia swordplay, wall-running parkour, and tense martial-arts duels reminiscent of *Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon*. Early impressions praise its atmospheric boss encounters against equally matched human martial artists, fluid dual-weapon combo switching, and vertical exploration. It looks like a stylish contender for action fans seeking grounded, skill-focused combat.
