## Highlights by Theme

### News & Business
**[Make IPOs Great Again: SEC Chairman Paul Atkins on Ending Regulation by Enforcement](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYQVjdos0g4)** 
A meaty interview with new SEC Chairman Paul Atkins outlining his deregulatory agenda. He sharply pivots away from Gary Gensler's legacy, aiming to foster capital formation by rolling back heavy-handed ESG disclosures and replacing "regulation by enforcement" in the crypto space with clear, proactive guidelines.

**[袁Sir聊战雄关：为了“民族融合”，钓鱼城就要投降？](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUB9-F2iLGU)**

**[Cleaning up farm emissions? | FT Rethink](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CpsDkfSj_qI)** 
The *Financial Times* examines the contentious nitrogen emission limits hitting Dutch farmers. It is a nuanced look at the tension between top-down EU environmental mandates and the ground-level economic realities of agriculture, highlighting moves to convert excess manure into biogas.

**[#高晓松|世界杯|巴西02|殖民史|葡萄牙分封制...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Lj8x2sH_-E)**

He explores Portugal's historical ineptitude, the fascinating cultural melting pot of Brazil, and why South America's economic trajectory diverged so sharply from North America's.

**[What happens to Schrödinger’s cat?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_VB-oc_pmk)** 
A quick, accessible explainer of quantum superposition and entanglement from *Veritasium*. It serves as a great reminder that Schrödinger's original thought experiment was actually meant to highlight the absurdity of magnifying quantum states to the macroscopic level.
