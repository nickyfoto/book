### Food Science & Tips
The culinary masters at **[老飯骨](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=luQ9ykesDDs)** share brilliant scientific hacks for flawless steamed layers. First, avoid adding ground white pepper to the egg whites as it will discolor them; keep them pure white while adding a splash of water for a delicate, soft texture. Second, never add water to the steamed yolks so they remain firm enough to slice cleanly. Finally, the ultimate secret to preventing the distinct egg layers from separating due to steam moisture is dusting them with potato starch (生粉), which acts as a crucial culinary binder.

### Restaurant & Travel
The chefs at **[老飯骨](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=luQ9ykesDDs)** delve deep into Chinese culinary heritage and state banquet culture. They reflect on their careers at the Great Hall of the People (人民大會堂), explaining how they represented the "second generation" of national chefs recruited from various provinces in 1973 to inherit and unite China's regional cooking traditions. They lament that the three-color steamed egg is a vanishing classic because hotels find it too tedious, making this tutorial a rare preservation of a historic recipe.
