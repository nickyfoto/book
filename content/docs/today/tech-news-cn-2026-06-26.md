# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-06-26

## Top Story
The fierce battle over AI intellectual property and model distillation has taken center stage as Anthropic formally accused Alibaba's Qwen lab of an industrial-scale operation to extract Claude's capabilities using nearly 25,000 fake accounts. This geopolitical friction comes at a highly sensitive moment, as Chinese AI models like Zhipu's GLM-5.2 are rapidly closing the performance gap with US frontrunners while dramatically undercutting them on API costs. The clash highlights the growing anxiety in Silicon Valley over low-cost Chinese alternatives and the increasingly porous boundaries of IP in the generative AI era.

## Engineering & Dev
API7.ai founder Wen Ming shared his profound shift in development workflows in InfoQ's **[API7.ai Founder: Burning Billions of Tokens to Rewrite a Production Gateway](https://www.infoq.cn/article/5YIK6SvTf5h07YckXZd3)**. He argues that AI coding capabilities have already surpassed those of senior engineers in execution, shifting the human bottleneck from coding to high-level architecture design and decision-making. He notes that he now makes 40 to 50 high-quality technical decisions a day while Claude Code acts as his execution engine for rewriting the gateway in Rust.

As autonomous agents become primary cloud consumers, InfoQ covers how **[Alibaba Cloud Redefines the Cloud Paradigm for Agentic AI](https://www.infoq.cn/article/s6TAS5JMIW1miPSqIsk0)**. The platform is transitioning from traditional API interactions to Spec-Driven Development (SDD) and integrating the Model Context Protocol (MCP). This ensures that AI agents operate within secure, auditable, and intent-driven boundaries, preventing hallucinated prompts from causing catastrophic infrastructure changes.

To combat the rising threat of supply chain attacks, InfoQ reports that **[VS Code 1.123 Adds a 2-Hour Extension Update Delay](https://www.infoq.cn/article/iZskGZVEV04jivWZtR00)**. This brief window is designed to give automated scanners and the community time to flag malicious packages before they infect developers' machines, though "trusted publishers" are controversially exempt from this delay.

Tencent Cloud showcased how AI is overhauling traditional system engineering in **[Migrating 1,200 Microservices for Indonesian Telco XLSMART in 4.5 Months](https://www.infoq.cn/article/TA7VPXQqYgTsu4Yl8qLa)**. Instead of relying on manual audits, AI tools like CodeBuddy were deployed to automatically scan cloud resources, generate architecture documentation, and convert Terraform scripts, drastically reducing the friction of multi-vendor coordination.

Over on his popular blog, Ruan Yifeng highlighted **[Technology Enthusiast Weekly Issue 401: How to Make a Billion Dollars](http://www.ruanyifeng.com/blog/2026/06/weekly-issue-401.html)**. He recaps Paul Graham's recent Oxford speech, doing the math on how maintaining a 15% monthly growth rate over five years can multiply a startup's revenue by 4,384 times.

## Products & Digital
Apple has implemented a rare, synchronized price hike across its hardware lines in China, as reported by ifanr in **[Apple Officially Raises Prices, MacBook Pro Starts at 15,999 RMB](https://www.ifanr.com/1670010)**. The company cited a surge in memory and storage component costs driven by the rapid expansion of AI data centers. The starting prices for Macs and iPads saw significant bumps, with the MacBook Air now starting at 9,999 RMB.

Meitu is pivoting its AI strategy from pure generation to direct outcome delivery with **[8 New AI Products](https://www.ifanr.com/1670054)**. Standouts include Picchi, which learns a user's specific photo-editing style to bypass tedious prompt engineering, and MVLAND, an AI agent that automatically synchronizes video generation with musical beats and emotional structures.

Alipay's health application "Ant Afu" launched a nationwide **["Lose 100 Million Jin" Campaign](https://www.ifanr.com/1670032)**. By subsidizing smart scales to a mere 0.01 RMB plus shipping, the app pairs comprehensive body-metric hardware with a real-time AI coach. This initiative democratizes personalized health management for millions of users, moving AI from passive chatbots into actionable, daily lifestyle routines.

For productivity hackers, a new sspai tutorial demonstrates how to **[Explore the Limits of Shortcuts: Creating Rich Interfaces with Webviews](https://sspai.com/prime/story/create-webview-in-shortcuts)**. By passing JSON data through the iOS clipboard, developers can create rich, app-like HTML/JS interfaces for shortcuts, bypassing the platform's traditionally clunky native menu limitations.

## News & Commentary
The New York Times analyzed the geopolitical tightrope of AI in **[Chinese AI Models Close the Gap with Anthropic and OpenAI](https://cn.nytimes.com/technology/20260626/zai-china-artificial-intelligence-models/)**. Zhipu's GLM-5.2 is making waves globally for matching top-tier performance at a fraction of the cost. Simultaneously, InfoQ covers how **[Anthropic Sues Qwen Over Distillation](https://www.infoq.cn/article/MVDrYfoCovqj6zBNxr9e)**, accusing Alibaba of utilizing fake accounts to clone Claude's reasoning capabilities, escalating US-China AI tensions while Anthropic's CEO clashes with the White House over export controls.

Google is suffering a highly visible talent drain, according to InfoQ's **[Google is Experiencing a Long Bleed](https://www.infoq.cn/article/AKtLb52kbIfp68SkOHuU)**. In a single week, the company lost Transformer co-author Noam Shazeer to OpenAI and AlphaFold co-creator John Jumper to Anthropic. This signals a major shift in how top researchers evaluate the organizational efficiency, equity upside, and compute allocation of frontier AI labs.

A rare display of civic action erupted in southwestern China, as reported by NYT in **[Dog Abuse in Chongqing Sparks Protests and Calls for Animal Protection Laws](https://cn.nytimes.com/china/20260626/dog-animal-abuse-china-protest/)**. A local man's abuse of his dog drew hundreds of citizens to his apartment, leading to multi-day sit-ins and clashes with police. The incident highlights a growing, grassroots push among young Chinese for comprehensive animal welfare legislation.
