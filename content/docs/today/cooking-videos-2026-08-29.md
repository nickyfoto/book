# Cooking Videos — 2026-08-29

## Watch First
The undisputed standout is **[Classic Lu Cuisine: Sweet and Sour Carp](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZQVzz6toxP4)** from **鲁菜老厨陈宗明**, an extraordinary culinary masterclass demonstrating the rare **"two curls and four openings" (两翘四开)** technique to fry a whole live carp into a dramatic, upright crescent moon silhouette. Master Chen delivers an exacting breakdown of knife skills—including 45-degree wheat-ear scoring sliced directly to the backbone—paired with a beer-leavened starch batter and a calibrated sugar-to-vinegar sauce that every serious home cook should study.

Rounding out the savory lineup, **[Boiled Pork Slices](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h1ofVXWrgyQ)** cooks sliced pork from a cold-water start with scallions and ginger to produce a clean, slightly tangy broth made for dipping in chili oil and soy sauce alongside warm steamed flower rolls.

### Food Science & Tips
In **[Poached Meatballs with Shredded Radish](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jeNOzMyTUHY)** by **老东北美食**, the cook reveals an essential kitchen tip: stirring the meat mixture with **50°C to 60°C warm water** to break down and emulsify the pork fat, ensuring the poached meatballs remain remarkably juicy and tender without feeling heavy. Meanwhile, in **[Classic Lu Cuisine: Sweet and Sour Carp](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZQVzz6toxP4)**, Master Chen debunks the myth of pulling the lateral sensory line to eliminate gaminess, explaining that thoroughly scraping away the inner dark mucosal membrane is the true key to neutralizing fishy odors. He also explains the food science behind his batter, where mixing **beer into the starch** introduces yeast and bubbles that aerate the coating for long-lasting crispness, while adding a measured handful of wheat flour provides the necessary adhesion to keep the batter clinging to the fish during frying.

### Restaurant & Travel
In **[Japanese Potato and Meat Stew (Nikujaga)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xsKiEpYWHmw)**, **詹姆士官方專屬頻道** spotlights the culinary folklore and domestic culture surrounding Japan's quintessential home-cooked beef and potato braise. The video explores the long-standing cultural trope of whether mastering this comforting staple was historically considered the definitive domestic benchmark for Japanese women preparing for marriage. It offers an entertaining look into how a simple family-style stew evolved into a cultural symbol of warmth and hearth in Japanese culinary lore.

### Everything Else
For pantry preparation, **老东北美食** shares a versatile batch recipe for **[Chili Oil](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JqnXkgxsBOE)**, demonstrating the smart technique of quickly washing whole spices—star anise, cassia bark, bay leaf, Sichuan pepper, and fennel—in clean water to remove dust before scalding them in hot oil for an aromatic seasoning base. Additionally, the channel honors the seasonal arrival of winter (Lidong) with **[Pickled Cabbage Dumplings](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mavUm7blfk)**, calibrating a dough ratio of 500 grams of flour to 280 grams of warm water and stuffing the wrappers with crispy rendered pork cracklings and fermented sour cabbage.
