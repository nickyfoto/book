# CNBC — 2026-09-14

## Lead Story
Artificial intelligence leaders led by Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, and xAI CEO Elon Musk issued an unprecedented call for an industry-wide slowdown in AI model development over existential safety concerns. The coordinated warning sparked a sharp tech sell-off, dragging semiconductor and data center stocks down while triggering a massive rally in cybersecurity providers.

## Markets & Economics
Major U.S. stock indices closed lower as the Dow fell 152 points (-0.3%), the S&P 500 lost 0.5%, and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 0.6%. Benchmark borrowing costs spiked as the **[10-year Treasury yield briefly hit 5%](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/14/bespokeas-paul-hickey-reacts-to-the-u-s-10-year-treasury-yield-hitting-5-percent.html)** for the first time since October 2023, pushing the **[30-year fixed mortgage rate to 7.17%](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/14/30-year-fixed-mortgage-rate-hits-highest-level-since-january-2025.html)**. Crude oil prices surged past \$100 per barrel—with West Texas Intermediate settling above \$101 and Brent crude topping \$105—after drone strikes forced Saudi Arabia to shut its strategic East-West pipeline. Ahead of Wednesday's decision, market watchers like JPMorgan's Jack Caffrey expressed high confidence in a **[Fed rate hike this week](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/14/jpmorganas-jack-caffrey-highly-confident-weall-see-a-fed-rate-hike-this-week.html)**, with futures pricing in a 92% probability of a quarter-point increase to 3.75%–4.00% to combat 3.4% CPI inflation.

## Business & Earnings
In executive interviews, **[Broadcom CEO Hock Tan](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/14/broadcom-ceo-on-anthropics-slowdown-push-ai-revenue-targets-havent-changed.html)** stood firm on his \$115 billion FY27 AI semiconductor target, while **[CrowdStrike CEO George Kurtz](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/14/crowdstrike-ceo-anthropics-ai-safety-warning.html)** noted that existing AI models make robust protection mandatory as CrowdStrike shares surged 14% to record highs. Meanwhile, **[Novo CEO Mike Doustdar](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/14/novo-nordisk-rebrands-to-novo-ceo-mike-doustdar.html)** detailed the Danish drugmaker's rebrand from Novo Nordisk and strategic pivot to regain obesity market share from Eli Lilly. In dealmaking news, Johnson & Johnson entered talks to sell its DePuy Synthes orthopedics business to Apollo Global Management for roughly \$20 billion, while Rum Group jumped 18% following a reported \$13.7 billion compute contract with Anthropic. Elsewhere on Wall Street, Bank of America CEO Brian Moynihan warned that third-quarter investment banking fees are projected to drop over 10% year-over-year.

## Investing & Commentary
CNBC's Jim Cramer advised investors to 'sit on their hands' during the tech pullback, highlighting Intel and Micron as potential buy candidates if shares fall 10%. Wall Street analysts issued several actionable upgrades, with Wolfe Research raising Affirm to Outperform with a \$90 price target (26% upside) and Morgan Stanley initiating EchoStar at Overweight with a \$134 target as a discounted entry into SpaceX. Concurrently, tech strategists like DA Davidson's Gil Luria dismissed the AI slowdown rhetoric as incumbent moat-building, while Bank of America's Savita Subramanian warned that the S&P 500 is overdue for a pullback after triggering 50% of bear market signposts.
