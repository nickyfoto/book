# CNBeta — 2026-09-13

## Top Story
According to [a cnbeta report on China's automotive supply chain](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577694.htm), major component suppliers are actively turning down massive contracts—including a 500 million RMB order rejected by cable maker Kaibeiyi—as fierce terminal price wars drive auto industry profit margins down to a razor-thin 2.4%. Faced with severe margin pressure, Chinese auto parts manufacturers are pivoting toward AI data center hardware, commercial aerospace, energy storage, and overseas facilities in Southeast Asia. This strategic shift highlights a broader restructuring of China's industrial base, as suppliers exit low-margin auto contracts to supply global tech and computing infrastructure.
