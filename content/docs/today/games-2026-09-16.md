# Gaming News — 2026-09-16

## Top Story
Nintendo and Intelligent Systems' **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** launches tomorrow for Nintendo Switch 2, locking in a rare **10/10 masterpiece review score from IGN**. This ambitious tactical RPG combines four interwoven hero campaigns, grueling colosseum battles, and a clever single-save time travel system that makes every strategic choice count.

## News & Reviews

**[Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-review)
**Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** has earned a glowing **10/10 rating from IGN**, cementing itself as a phenomenal tactical RPG that refines turn-based combat and continental exploration. Players guide four distinct hero paths through the capital city of Dagsion before their stories converge into an epic finale, expertly managed via a single-save system with chapter-replay snapshots. With its risk-reward Blaze Arts and deep map management, this Switch 2 exclusive is an absolute must-play for strategy fans.

**[Wardogs Steam Success & CEO Crunch Defense](https://www.ign.com/articles/wardogs-outpacing-arc-raiders-and-helldivers-2-on-steam-and-has-already-made-more-money-than-marathon-across-all-platforms-to-date-analysis-shows)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/wardogs-outpacing-arc-raiders-and-helldivers-2-on-steam-and-has-already-made-more-money-than-marathon-across-all-platforms-to-date-analysis-shows)
Bulkhead's tactical shooter **Wardogs** has become a massive breakout hit, selling **2.2 million copies** and generating over **\$70 million in revenue** on Steam to outpace launch sales of Arc Raiders and Helldivers 2. However, studio CEO Joe Brammer ignited intense industry debate after publicly defending crunch periods and admitting the company avoids hiring developers who speak out against crunch. Despite the workplace controversy, the \$40 early-access shooter continues to sit near the top of Steam's most-played charts.

**[Xbox Game Pass Reportedly Considering Dropping Day-One Releases](https://www.ign.com/articles/will-microsoft-kill-day-1-launches-via-game-pass-after-word-leaks-of-potential-changes-xbox-says-it-has-nothing-to-announce-today)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/will-microsoft-kill-day-1-launches-via-game-pass-after-word-leaks-of-potential-changes-xbox-says-it-has-nothing-to-announce-today)
A high-profile leak suggests Microsoft is planning a major Xbox Game Pass overhaul for next spring that could **eliminate day-one game launches** from standard tiers. The reported restructuring includes a \$12.99 ad-supported tier and a \$19.99 ad-free plan, with cloud streaming and family sharing offered as separate add-on costs. Microsoft responded by stating it has "no changes to announce today," notably stopping short of denying the leaked plans.

**[Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-review)
Falcom's **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** remake delivers an incredible modernization of the classic JRPG, earning high praise for its emotional narrative and visual overhaul. Picking up right after Joshua's sudden disappearance, the sequel introduces full voice acting and a refined hybrid combat system featuring real-time Quick Battles and turn-based Command Battles. While minor late-game backtracking slows the pace, its streamlined focus and engaging timeline mechanics make it a top-tier recommendation for JRPG lovers.

**[Zach Cregger's Resident Evil Movie Delivers High-Octane Horror](https://www.ign.com/articles/resident-evil-movie-2026-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/resident-evil-movie-2026-review)

Separately, Cregger revealed his dream casting choice for a future live-action Lady Dimitrescu would be Mad Men star **Christina Hendricks**.
