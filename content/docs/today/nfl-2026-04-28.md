### Free Agency & Trades
The off-season veteran market is serving up heavy drama, starting with a bizarre quarterback limbo in Pittsburgh. As covered in ESPN's [What the Steelers placing a UFA tender on Aaron Rodgers means 👀](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Prvtbjjei9k), the Steelers slapped a rare UFA tender on Aaron Rodgers for roughly $15 million, creating a weird insurance policy that traps him into either signing with a new team before training camp or returning to Pittsburgh. Meanwhile, in Dallas, contract negotiations have turned hostile; NBC's [Report: George Pickens has yet to sign franchise tender](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ccuBbjevHNk) explores how wideout George Pickens is refusing to sign his $27.29 million tag to leverage a long-term deal or force a trade before the July 15 deadline. Up in Buffalo, Brandon Beane is trying to spin a broken situation on NBC's [Brandon Beane: Bills have ‘hit the reset button’ with Keon Coleman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nK8GdLnx97k), insisting the Bills haven't found a trade partner for their disgruntled receiver and are hoping to salvage the relationship moving forward.
