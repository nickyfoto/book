# Frontier Pacing, Corporate Cartels, and the Open Source Moat — 2026-09-13

## Highlights
Today's AI Twitter discourse is dominated by Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's essay "We Must Pace the Frontier," which advocates for third-party safety audits and a managed slowdown of frontier model releases. The proposal ignited a fierce debate across Silicon Valley, pitting safety advocates against critics who view the move as pre-IPO market conditioning and regulatory capture. Meanwhile, technical commentators and defense contractors highlighted open-source models outperforming frontier APIs at a fraction of the cost, challenging the commercial moats of top AI labs.
