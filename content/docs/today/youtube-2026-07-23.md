# YouTube — 2026-07-23

## Watch First
If you only watch one thing today, make it **[The Invisible Threat Taking Away Our Brightest Minds | Drew McCartor | TED](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=57afWpv-9u4)**, which reveals the staggering global toll of lead poisoning on children's developing brains—robbing humanity of an estimated two million IQ points daily—and explains how this entirely solvable crisis is being tackled globally. The talk brilliantly shifts from horrifying statistics, such as lead taking more lives than all active wars and natural disasters combined, to an actionable, optimistic model for regulating and removing toxic exposure worldwide.
