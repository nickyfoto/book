### Food Science & Tips
ShangshiKitchen shares a genius, unconventional tip: using a new, thoroughly washed pair of gardening shears to snip through tough pork ribs, which saves your chef's knife and cutting board from potential damage. The video also explains the science of fruit marinades, noting that the natural proteases and fruit acids in the pineapple break down the meat for an incredibly tender, melt-in-your-mouth texture. Meanwhile, Chef Chen offers a vital prep tip for offal, demonstrating how to properly butterfly pork kidneys and use specific cutting angles so they curl beautifully into "flowers" when hitting the hot oil. 

### Restaurant & Travel
Offering a fantastic glimpse into Chinese culinary heritage, the 老饭骨 (Lao Fan Gu) video details how the "Mei Wan" meatball dish evolved from a regional Jiangxi specialty to a coveted staple of State Banquets in 1983. The chefs highlight how these historic banquet foods meticulously integrated the techniques of China's Eight Great Cuisines and aristocratic cooking, lamenting that such delicate, labor-intensive creations are rarely prepared in modern dining.
