## Top Stories

**[NASA Launches Roman Space Telescope to Map the Dark Universe](https://www.theverge.com/science/986544/nancy-grace-roman-space-telescope-launch)** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/986544/nancy-grace-roman-space-telescope-launch)
NASA successfully launched its \$4.3 billion Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope aboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy rocket, kicking off a one-million-mile journey to the Sun-Earth L2 Lagrange point. Equipped with a 300-megapixel near-infrared camera providing a field of view 100 times larger than Hubble's, the observatory can survey the cosmos 1,000 times faster to probe dark energy and dark matter. The spacecraft also carries an advanced coronagraph designed to directly image exoplanets, prompting President Donald Trump to dial directly into NASA's post-launch briefing to congratulate the mission team.

**[Massive 12TB Steam Leak Exposes a Decade of Unreleased Valve and PC Game History](https://arstechnica.com/gaming/2026/08/a-12tb-steam-teraleak-spills-more-than-a-decade-of-lost-pc-gaming-history/)** · [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/gaming/2026/08/a-12tb-steam-teraleak-spills-more-than-a-decade-of-lost-pc-gaming-history/)
A massive BitTorrent data dump has surfaced online containing over 12 terabytes of historical files from Valve's legacy Steam2 server architecture spanning 2003 through 2013. Community archivists analyzing thousands of internal depots have already uncovered unreleased assets from the canceled *Half-Life 2: Episode 3*, cut *Portal 2* mechanics including the camera-based prototype *F-Stop*, and early builds of major third-party franchises. The leak abruptly cuts off at 2013 because Valve migrated distribution to its HTTP-based SteamPipe system, making this release an unprecedented time capsule of early digital PC gaming history.

**[Meta Deploys Robots Inside Data Centers to Cut AI Infrastructure Labor Costs](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/inside-metas-push-to-put-robots-to-work-in-data-centers/)** · [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/inside-metas-push-to-put-robots-to-work-in-data-centers/)
Meta is quietly piloting robotic systems from vendors including Kinova, Watney Robotics, and ABB to automate routine physical maintenance across its data centers. The trial evaluates robotic arms like the Kinova Gen3 to swap networking cables and power-cycle servers as Meta seeks to curb operating expenses amid soaring capital expenditures on AI infrastructure. Internal data center personnel estimate the machines could automate up to 80 percent of specific routine physical tasks, sparking fresh workplace anxieties among facility technicians.

**[AWS Open-Sources Kiro Crew to Power Asynchronous Multi-Agent Coding Workspaces](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/kiro-crew-coding-agents/)** · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/kiro-crew-coding-agents/)
Amazon open-sourced Kiro Crew, an agent orchestration framework developed internally as MeshClaw and already adopted by more than 39,000 AWS engineers. Operating on the Agent Client Protocol with sandbox defenses, the system allows developers to delegate long-running tasks like incident triage, PR monitoring, and dependency migrations to background subagents that retain persistent memory across sessions. While software engineers welcomed the multi-session autonomy, early community feedback warned that running concurrent background agents consumes API tokens significantly faster than standard CLI tools.

**[Linux Foundation Warns AI Crawlers Are Overwhelming Kernel Infrastructure](https://linux.slashdot.org/story/26/08/30/2235224/ais-creepy-crawlers-criticized-by-linux-foundations-it-infrastructure-director)** · [Slashdot](https://linux.slashdot.org/story/26/08/30/2235224/ais-creepy-crawlers-criticized-by-linux-foundations-it-infrastructure-director)
The Linux Foundation's IT infrastructure director disclosed that automated AI scrapers seeking clean, human-written training data now account for roughly 98 percent of all traffic hitting git.kernel.org. The aggressive crawlers cycle through millions of residential and mobile IP proxies to bypass security challenges, consuming 14 dedicated CPU cores around the clock purely to render git commits and diffs as HTML. In response, administrators are stripping away resource-intensive web features and establishing stricter verification gates for anonymous visitors.

**[Texas Governor Freezes Funding for Flock AI Surveillance Cameras Following Backlash](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/986541/texas-governor-abbott-flock-cameras)** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/986541/texas-governor-abbott-flock-cameras)
Texas Governor Greg Abbott halted state expenditures on Flock Safety's automated license plate recognition cameras ahead of an investigation revealing over \$30 million was spent on the system via auto insurance fee surcharges. The surveillance network has faced mounting bipartisan scrutiny regarding mass location tracking, unauthorized warrantless access, and civil liberties concerns. The spending freeze comes after multiple criminal charges were filed against law enforcement officers who abused Flock cameras for personal stalking.
