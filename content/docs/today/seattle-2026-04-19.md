**[Small Plane Makes Emergency Landing in Auburn](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/small-plane-emergency-landing-auburn)** · FOX 13
A pilot was injured Saturday afternoon after her small plane lost both engines and crash-landed into a field at an Auburn construction site. The California flight was originally attempting to divert to Renton before the engine failures forced the emergency landing, though a passenger onboard escaped without injuries.

**[Bold Apartment Fire Rescue in Mountlake Terrace](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/firefighters-rescue-apartment-blaze)** · FOX 13
Dozens of Mountlake Terrace residents experienced a terrifying morning Sunday when a three-story apartment building went up in flames. Fire crews executed a dramatic ladder rescue, successfully pulling two people and a dog from the third floor of the burning structure.

**[Washington Ballot Initiatives Certified for November](https://komonews.com/news/politics/campaigns-raise-money-for-and-against-girls-sports-parents-rights-issues-on-nov-ballot-transgender-school-privacy-washington-gender-identity-biological-t)** · KOMO
Voters will decide on at least two statewide issues this November backed by the Let's Go Washington campaign. The measures include the "Protecting Fairness in Girls’ Sports" act, which would require physicals proving biological sex for gendered sports teams, and the "Parents' Bill of Rights," aiming to increase transparency in schools.

**[Targeted Burglaries Hit Local Film Crews](https://ebx.sh/2lF1Bv)** · The Seattle Times
Local film production crews in the Seattle area are falling victim to a recent spate of burglaries. According to reports, these targeted thefts have resulted in the loss of hundreds of thousands of dollars worth of vital production equipment.
