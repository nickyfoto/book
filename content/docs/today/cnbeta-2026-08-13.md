# CNBeta — August 13, 2026

## Top Story
According to **[a cnbeta report on market capitalization](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573054.htm)**, domestic DRAM memory pioneer Changxin Technology has officially surpassed internet giant Tencent to become China's most valuable listed company, reaching a market capitalization of 3.5 trillion RMB (\$485B). This seismic realignment represents the crowning of China's state-backed semiconductor self-reliance drive over traditional consumer internet platforms, reflecting national strategic priorities that are fundamentally reshaping the global technology supply chain.

## Tech & AI
China's generative AI landscape took a massive leap forward as **[Alibaba open-sourced its flagship Qwen3.8-Max model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572978.htm)**, a 2.4-trillion-parameter system whose weights are now public, matching global closed-source systems in long-term autonomy and agent capabilities. On the same night, **[DeepSeek launched the formal version of its V4 Pro model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572996.htm)**, scoring just 0.1 points behind Anthropic's Fable 5 on the Terminal Bench agent evaluation, though the startup simultaneously announced a sharp 11x API price hike during peak hours to manage skyrocketing compute demands. These rapid open-weights advances contrast sharply with **[Google Gemini's delayed release schedules](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573044.htm)**, with sources attributing Gemini's multi-month delays to severe chip shortages, internal divisions, and the high-profile exit of AI pioneers Jeff Dean and Oriol Vinyals to launch a rival startup. Meanwhile, **[Elon Musk is heavily turning to natural gas](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573094.htm)** to power his massive xAI and SpaceX "Colossus" supercomputers in the US, utilizing off-grid generators to bypass years-long utility connection delays as AI electricity demands reach unprecedented heights. To bolster its voice assistant, Apple is offering a unique pay-per-use licensing model, discussing a multi-million dollar budget to **[pay publishers for real-time news in Siri AI](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1572982.htm)**.

## Consumer & Devices
The explosive global demand for AI chips is squeezing consumer devices, as a **[report on the death of sub-\$100 smartphones](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573088.htm)** reveals a 64% drop in ultra-low-end US phone sales as RAM and NAND flash components now consume over 64% of their manufacturing costs. Legacy hardware is also buckling under heavy AI workloads; **[Tesla HW3 motherboards are overheating to 96°C](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573058.htm)** and bricking after FSD OTA updates, a hardware crisis that highlights the protection offered by China's strict "approve-before-push" regulatory policy that bans heavy updates on under-powered hardware. In contrast, premium niche features are thriving, as **[Sony's Xperia 1 VIII reported strong sales](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573074.htm)** due to its stubborn retention of the 3.5mm headphone jack and microSD slot, which premium users highly value. Meanwhile, folding screens continue to struggle with durability, with **[iFixit's teardown of the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573108.htm)** showing severe hinge vulnerabilities to fine dust and awarding the device a dismal 4/10 repairability score.

## Gaming
The Chinese gaming industry saw the dark resolution of its most notorious tragedy as **[legal details of the Yoozoo Games poisoning case](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573056.htm)** were officially published, detailing how former executive Xu Yao meticulously manufactured multiple lethal toxins to poison founder Lin Qi over corporate disputes before being executed on May 21. In the corporate sphere, **[Netflix is scaling back its gaming ambitions](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573132.htm)** by shutting down internal studios like Night School (creator of *Oxenfree*) to focus on lower-risk external IPs. On a more positive note, **[Warner Bros. officially confirmed Hogwarts Legacy 2](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573068.htm)**, marking a heavy reinvestment in its billion-dollar franchise following the original's record-shattering 40 million sales.

## Science & Space
For future space exploration, **[China's 2035 lunar base construction plans](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1572992.htm)** propose deploying quadruped robotic dogs to handle water-ice mining and habitat assembly, shielding human astronauts from extreme lunar hazards. In astrophysics, an international team studying a magnetar has captured **[the strongest evidence of vacuum birefringence](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1573118.htm)**, confirming Werner Heisenberg's 1936 quantum prediction that "vacuum is not empty" by measuring highly polarized X-ray emissions through intense magnetic fields. Additionally, **[astronomers captured a supernova shock breakout](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1573116.htm)** of the Type Ic supernova SN 2026gzf, a rare event that reveals the violent, final-decade mass-loss histories of massive Wolf-Rayet stars.
