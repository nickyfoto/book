# Seattle Local — 2026-09-18

## Top Story
Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson signed Executive Order 2026-07 on Thursday to overhaul the city's emergency management framework and AlertSeattle emergency notification system. The mandate establishes clearer chains of command, designated department leaders, and updated employee training following delayed public notifications during the recent Bite of Seattle shooting.

**[Downtown Seattle visitor foot traffic hits seven-year peak in August](https://bit.ly/479fv0o)** · KOMO News

However, DSA officials noted that daily worker return rates still lag behind historical benchmarks.
