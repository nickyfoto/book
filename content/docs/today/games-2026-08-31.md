**[PlayStation Claims Digital Games Are Not Really Owned by Players in Response to Class-Action Lawsuit](https://www.ign.com/articles/playstation-claims-digital-games-are-not-really-owned-by-players-in-response-to-class-action-lawsuit)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/playstation-claims-digital-games-are-not-really-owned-by-players-in-response-to-class-action-lawsuit)
In a formal court response to a consumer class-action lawsuit, Sony explicitly argued that reasonable consumers in the digital era should know they are purchasing a revocable software license rather than permanent ownership of a game. The legal defense ignited immediate outrage across social media, where players blasted storefronts for using "Buy" buttons for temporary access. The controversy adds fuel to an ongoing player backlash as PlayStation prepares to phase out physical disc production in January 2028.

**[PlayStation Is Set to Pay Out \$7.85 Billion to Users as Part of Class-Action Lawsuit](https://www.ign.com/articles/playstation-is-set-to-pay-out-785-billion-to-users-as-part-of-class-action-lawsuit)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/playstation-is-set-to-pay-out-785-billion-to-users-as-part-of-class-action-lawsuit)
A federal court granted preliminary approval for Sony Interactive Entertainment to settle a landmark **\$7.85 billion class-action antitrust lawsuit** over allegations that it monopolized digital purchases by barring third-party retailers from selling PlayStation game download codes. U.S. citizens who bought eligible digital PlayStation games between April 1, 2019, and December 31, 2023, could see automated deposits directly into their PSN accounts pending an October 15 final approval hearing. This represents one of the largest legal settlements in console gaming history, giving digital buyers some much-deserved compensation.

**[Housemarque Co-Founder Ilari Kuittinen Announces Exit from Saros Developer After 31 Years](https://www.ign.com/articles/housemarque-co-founder-ilari-kuittinen-announces-exit-from-saros-developer-after-31-years)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/housemarque-co-founder-ilari-kuittinen-announces-exit-from-saros-developer-after-31-years)
Ilari Kuittinen, co-founder of Housemarque, announced his retirement after 31 years steering the Finnish development powerhouse behind arcade classics like *Resogun*, *Returnal*, and *Saros*. Kuittinen guided the studio from an independent shoot-’em-up outfit to an elite AAA developer under the PlayStation Studios banner before handing the reins to managing director Sami Hakala. His departure marks the end of an era for one of gaming’s most distinctive arcade-action creators.
