# CNBC — 2026-09-11

## Lead Story
Hotter-than-expected August core CPI of 0.3% month-over-month (3.4% headline YoY) cemented expectations for a Federal Reserve interest rate hike next week, pushing CME FedWatch odds above 85%. The impending central bank tightening comes as persistent Middle East conflict keeps Brent crude above \$100 per barrel and sent U.S. diesel fuel to a record high of \$6.05 per gallon.

## Markets & Economics
U.S. equities broke a four-day losing streak as the Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 509 points (0.98%), the S&P 500 gained 0.86%, and the Nasdaq Composite advanced 0.96%. Macro sentiment was driven by the Bureau of Labor Statistics' August CPI report—detailed in CNBC's breakdown on **[consumer prices rising in August](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/11/consumer-prices-rose-0-point-4-percent-in-august-as-expected-core-inflation-was-higher-than-estimated.html)**—which showed headline inflation at 3.4% year-over-year and core CPI accelerating 0.3% month-over-month. Fixed income markets adjusted rapidly, with the 10-year Treasury yield hovering near multi-year highs at 4.94% and the 2-year yield climbing to 4.59%, while German 10-year yields crossed 3.5% for the first time since 2011 following the ECB's rate hike to 2.5%. Adding to economic headwinds, University of Michigan consumer sentiment plunged 7.5% to 47.8—the second-lowest level on record—as 1-year inflation expectations surged to 4.6% amid record \$6.05 per gallon national diesel prices.

## Business & Earnings
Enterprise tech led market action following Oracle's fiscal Q1 report—featured in **[Oracle shares spike on earnings beat](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/10/oracle-shares-spike-on-earnings-and-revenue-beat.html)**—where revenue grew 30% to \$19.35 billion and cloud infrastructure sales jumped 121%. Dell Technologies rallied 10% after RBC Capital Markets initiated coverage with an Outperform rating and \$640 price target, citing a \$95 billion server backlog and \$16.4 billion in Q2 AI server sales. In healthcare, Johnson & Johnson gained FDA approval for its Ottava surgical robotic platform, positioning the company to compete in the \$18.75 billion domestic surgical robotics market currently dominated by Intuitive Surgical. Internationally, Chinese Nvidia rival Enflame surged 206% in its Shanghai trading debut after its retail IPO order book was oversubscribed by 6,000 times.

## Investing & Commentary
Wall Street analysts issued major tactical calls on Friday, with Bernstein initiating Shopify at Outperform with a \$160 price target, maintaining that AI will expand merchant entry rather than disrupt e-commerce software. Morgan Stanley noted that \$6+ per gallon diesel prices strengthen the thesis for Tesla's Semi, estimating autonomous electric transport can cut operating costs by 20% per mile and generate \$12,000 to \$18,000 per month in high-margin software fees per truck. Meanwhile, investor Sarat Sethi identified Verisk Analytics as a compelling value opportunity, pointing to its 50%+ EBITDA margins and 80%+ recurring revenue model while trading at a discounted 15x forward EV/EBITDA.
