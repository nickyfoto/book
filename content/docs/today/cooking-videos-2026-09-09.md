### Food Science & Tips
Chef **鲁菜老厨陈宗明** shares essential culinary science in **[跟师父学鲁菜 | 排骨怎么做才能汤鲜味美? 一道营养丰富的【莲藕炖排骨】#师父和我做鲁菜 #美食教程 #传统鲁菜 #家常菜](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zaWKVkZvsK4)**, explaining that salting soup too early causes meat proteins to dehydrate and turn tough, whereas a medium-low heat simmer renders out animal fat into skimmable foam.
