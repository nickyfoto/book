# Apple Daily Digest — 2026-07-29

## Highlights
Today's news revolves around Apple's deeper push into the smart home and wearable markets, with an impending smart home hub and a privacy-focused smart glasses reveal targeted for next year. Meanwhile, regulatory battles continue to brew as Apple pushes back against UK App Store regulations and fights for discovery in a US antitrust lawsuit. Finally, iOS 27 betas reveal thoughtful UI tweaks, and the new Apple Upgrade program promises a more convenient way to lease devices.

## Articles Worth Reading

**[Sideloading changed everything and nothing about my iPhone](https://www.macworld.com/article/3199905/sideloading-changed-everything-and-nothing-about-my-iphone.html)** ([Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/3199905/sideloading-changed-everything-and-nothing-about-my-iphone.html))
This editorial explores the real-world impact of the EU's Digital Markets Act forcing Apple to allow third-party app marketplaces on iOS. Despite initial excitement, the author notes that the cumbersome installation process and the dominance of the official App Store have kept alternative marketplaces like AltStore PAL relegated to niche audiences. Ultimately, the ability to sideload apps represents a major platform shift, but the average user experience has remained largely unchanged.

**[iOS 27 Lets You Hide the Messages Button Everyone Hits by Mistake](https://www.macrumors.com/how-to/ios-hide-dictation-messages-button/)** ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/how-to/ios-hide-dictation-messages-button/))
Apple has finally addressed a widespread usability annoyance in the upcoming iOS 27 update by allowing users to hide the audio/dictation button in the Messages app. Because the icon sits precariously close to the send arrow, accidental taps frequently pause audio playback or trigger unintended recordings. Users can now navigate to Settings and adjust the text field to show the audio icon, dictation icon, or nothing at all, solving a design flaw that even celebrities have complained about.
