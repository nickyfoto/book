# CNBeta — September 16, 2026

## Top Story
According to [a cnbeta report on Huawei's internal executive speech](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578222.htm), former rotating chairman Guo Ping outlined Huawei’s ICT ambition to become the "Nvidia" of the AI era, focusing strictly on connections and compute without further business expansion. To navigate advanced semiconductor process constraints, Huawei is relying on hardware-software architecture innovations—such as its Ascend 950 cluster supernodes and the "Tao Law" strategy of substituting geometric chip scaling with time scaling—to close the performance gap with global leaders. This strategic blueprint underscores how Chinese tech giants are re-engineering systemic co-design to build resilient AI infrastructure under international trade headwinds.

## Tech & AI
China's open-source AI ecosystem is rapidly closing the performance gap with Silicon Valley. A [cnbeta report on Mozilla's open-source AI report](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578278.htm) highlights that Chinese open-weight models, such as Moonshot AI's Kimi K3, have narrowed the technical capability lag behind top U.S. closed-source models to just 4.4 months while offering roughly one-fifth the API deployment costs.

Concurrently, the global debate over frontier AI governance continues to intensify. A [cnbeta summary of White House AI plans](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578204.htm) indicates President Trump intends to convene tech CEOs next week after dismissing catastrophic AI risk warnings as a "hoax," aligning with Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's stance that existing regulations are sufficient. Taking a different angle, [a cnbeta report on Elon Musk's governance proposal](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578220.htm) suggests leading AI firms—including those in China—should cross-test each other's models for bioweapon and nuclear safety risks prior to public launch.

In enterprise AI, [Anthropic has unified its Claude workspace suite](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578298.htm), merging its chat interface with long-form Cowork, Docs, Slides, and Design tools into a single digital assistant. Meanwhile, [OpenAI is in early fundraising talks](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578218.htm) as its annual run-rate revenue crossed \$40 billion following the launches of GPT-5.6 and Astra.

On the silicon side, memory bottlenecks are worsening across AI and consumer hardware. [Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan warned that memory shortages are escalating](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578258.htm) as HBM demand pushes prices up 5 to 7 times. To expand supply and diversify manufacturing, [SK Hynix is discussing a partnership with Intel](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578246.htm) to produce memory chips at Intel’s Ohio fab complex or establish a joint venture with major cloud providers.

## Consumer & Devices
In Chinese automotive tech, a heated online debate erupted over [a cnbeta report detailing Huawei and Seres' partnership terms](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578268.htm), clarifying that Huawei previously took a 10% fee (2% tech licensing plus 8% channel marketing) per vehicle, which is now being restructured to focus solely on tech licensing as Seres reclaims direct sales control. The shift caused immediate ripples in China's EV market, driving sharp price adjustments in the secondhand Aito M7 and M9 market. Separately, debt-burdened [Zotye Auto returned to profitability](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578224.htm) by exporting its new Wink Y01 compact EV to overseas markets like Indonesia, India, and Algeria through local contract manufacturing deals.

In mobile OS updates, [a detailed review of iOS 27](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578230.htm) notes performance speedups and practical additions like holiday alarm support, though Apple Intelligence remains unavailable in mainland China, putting iPhones at a feature disadvantage against agent-heavy Chinese Android skins. Meanwhile, [a cnbeta report on an iOS 27 UI bug](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578214.htm) reveals that pulling down the notification shade and control center simultaneously can freeze the screen completely.

In wearables, [Meta is developing camera-free Ray-Ban smart glasses code-named "Luna"](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578262.htm), equipped with six microphones and open-ear speakers for voice-first AI interaction to avoid public privacy pushback. Looking ahead to future hardware, [Apple detailed a photo verification mechanism for the iPhone 18 Pro](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578302.htm) that uses sensor-level hardware private keys to sign raw image pixels at the point of capture to combat AI deepfakes.
