# CNBC — 2026-09-10

## Lead Story
**Resurgent inflation fears** gripped Wall Street as **West Texas Intermediate crude** surged past \$100 per barrel amid escalating U.S.-Iran hostilities in the Strait of Hormuz. Paired with an August **Producer Price Index (PPI)** print showing wholesale prices up 5.4% year-over-year, traders pushed the probability of a **Federal Reserve interest rate hike** next week to 70% while benchmark 10-year Treasury yields touched 4.86%.

## Markets & Economics
U.S. equities suffered a fourth consecutive losing session, with the **Dow Jones Industrial Average** falling 300 points (0.6%), the **S&P 500** losing 0.6%, and the **Nasdaq Composite** dropping 0.7%. Wholesale inflation came in at a 0.4% monthly rise (5.4% YoY), driving 30-year fixed mortgage rates past 7.07% for the first time since May 2025 **[Average 30-year fixed mortgage rate tops 7%](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/10/average-30-year-fixed-rate-tops-7-percent.html)**. Overseas, the **European Central Bank** enacted a 25-basis-point rate hike as Eurozone energy inflation surged 14.3%. Housing metrics signaled further contraction, as August existing home sales dropped 2% to a multi-year low despite inventory rising to a 4.9-month supply **[Existing home sales fall 2% in August](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/10/existing-home-sales-fall-2percent-in-august-hitting-lowest-level-in-over-a-year.html)**. Bond market pressure deepened as the 30-year Treasury yield touched 5.307%, completely absorbing the Treasury Department's expanded \$6 billion debt buyback operation.

## Business & Earnings
**Oracle** headlined enterprise earnings after hours, surging 7% on a fiscal Q1 beat (\$1.92 adjusted EPS vs. \$1.74 expected; \$19.35 billion revenue) powered by cloud infrastructure revenue doubling to \$7.4 billion. In retail, **Starbucks CEO Brian Niccol** announced cafe renovations and service upgrades on CNBC following 7.9% Q3 same-store sales growth **[Starbucks CEO Brian Niccol says next stage of turnaround focuses on cafe makeovers](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/10/starbucks-ceo-brian-niccol-cafe-renovations-customer-service.html)**, while **Macy's** raised full-year guidance despite near-term Q3 loss forecasts. Enterprise AI advancements expanded as **OpenAI** launched "ChatGPT for Financial Services" with Morgan Stanley and Evercore, deploying GPT-6 Astra to automate pitchbooks and valuation models for Wall Street analysts **[OpenAI targets work of Wall Street junior bankers](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/10/openai-chatgpt-for-financial-services-targets-work-of-junior-bankers.html)**. Across tech, **Apple CEO John Ternus** debuted the \$1,999 foldable iPhone Duo, **TSMC** reported record August revenue up 53% year-over-year, and **Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky** urged executives at Communacopia to embrace AI adoption **[Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky: AI is an existential risk](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/10/watch-cnbcs-full-interview-airbnb-ceo-brian-chesky.html)**.

## Investing & Commentary
At the CNBC Investing Club Monthly Meeting, **Jim Cramer** revealed six favorite stocks to buy now: **Meta Platforms**, **Micron**, **Intel**, **Bank of New York**, **Kimberly-Clark**, and **FedEx**. Wall Street issued aggressive tech calls as **JPMorgan** upgraded Meta to Overweight with an \$820 price target on agentic AI growth, while **Piper Sandler** initiated AMD (\$600 PT) and Nvidia (\$300 PT) as Overweight due to persistent GPU shortages. On asset allocation, **BlackRock** alternatives head Fabio Osta recommended replacing the traditional 60/40 portfolio with a 50/30/20 mix incorporating private markets to play the AI infrastructure buildout. Meanwhile, Jim Cramer warned that the 30-year Treasury yield climbing to 5.3% is becoming the primary driver of equity valuations, making risk-free government yields an increasingly tough competitor for stocks.
