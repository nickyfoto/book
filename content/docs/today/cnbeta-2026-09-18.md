# CNBeta — 2026-09-18

## Top Story
According to [a CNBeta report on semiconductor expansion](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578622.htm), **TSMC has secured construction permits for its \$49 billion 1.4-nanometer fab** in Taichung, with trial production scheduled for 2027 to power next-generation sub-nanometer chips. This milestone comes as [iPhone 18 Pro demand surges across China](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578634.htm), forcing supply chain factories to offer **triple overtime pay** to churn out 2nm A20 Pro processors and fulfill a 95-million-unit annual target. Together, these developments underscore how Taiwan and mainland China's manufacturing ecosystem continues to dictate the pace of global hardware innovation.
