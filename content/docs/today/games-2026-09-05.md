**[Zach Cregger Says He’s Done With Franchise Films After Resident Evil](https://www.ign.com/articles/zach-cregger-says-hes-done-with-franchise-films-after-resident-evil)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/zach-cregger-says-hes-done-with-franchise-films-after-resident-evil)

**[Insomniac Clears the Air After Marvel’s Wolverine Fans Confuse Lady Deathstrike for Psylocke](https://www.ign.com/articles/insomniac-clears-the-air-after-marvels-wolverine-fans-confuse-lady-deathstrike-for-psylocke)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/insomniac-clears-the-air-after-marvels-wolverine-fans-confuse-lady-deathstrike-for-psylocke)
Insomniac Games had to shut down a viral wave of internet confusion following the latest launch trailer for its PS5-exclusive *Marvel's Wolverine*. Fans mistook a sneering villain wielding a glowing purple sword for Psylocke, forcing the developer to clarify that the character is actually Lady Deathstrike. Players will get to face off against Lady Deathstrike's deadly finger claws when the highly anticipated action game launches on September 15, 2026.

**[Left 4 Dead 2 Dev Admits Valve Intentionally Leaked Its Own Trailer After the ESRB Blocked It from Release](https://www.ign.com/articles/left-4-dead-2-dev-admits-valve-intentionally-leaked-its-own-trailer-after-the-esrb-blocked-it-from-release)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/left-4-dead-2-dev-admits-valve-intentionally-leaked-its-own-trailer-after-the-esrb-blocked-it-from-release)
Former Valve writer Chet Faliszek spilled the beans on a hilarious piece of gaming history, admitting Valve secretly leaked its own *Left 4 Dead 2* "Zombie Survival Guide" trailer back in 2009. The unconventional leak was a calculated move to bypass ESRB restrictions, which had blocked the trailer's official release due to intense mature content. The clever stunt successfully generated massive player hype ahead of the game's critically acclaimed launch.
