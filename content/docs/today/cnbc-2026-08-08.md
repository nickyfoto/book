# CNBC — 2026-08-08

## Lead Story
**Berkshire Hathaway’s Q2 results revealed that CEO Greg Abel is putting Warren Buffett’s cash pile to work, deploying nearly \$20 billion in net equity purchases and reversing a 14-quarter selling streak. This massive capital redeployment, combined with a record-shattering week in options trading, drove the S&P 500 to historic highs above the 7,700 mark.**

## Markets & Economics
**The S&P 500 surged 3.6% this week to record highs above 7,700, propelled by a record 4 million index call contracts on Tuesday and a VIX sliding near its lowest level since January. This equity rally was further amplified by a weaker-than-expected July jobs report in which nonfarm payrolls unexpectedly fell by 23,000, lifting the implied probability of a September Fed rate pause to 55%. Geopolitical risk in energy markets remained acute as Brent crude closed at \$83.55 a barrel after an Abu Dhabi National Oil Company (ADNOC) tanker was targeted by a missile in the Strait of Hormuz. On the policy front, the Senate passed a short-term funding bill through December 11 to avert a government shutdown weeks before the midterm elections. Finally, Senate Republicans moved to advance the **[Clarity Act](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/06/clarity-act-needs-to-happen-now-says-neoclassic-capitals-michael-bucella.html)**, a landmark regulatory framework that would establish a comprehensive federal rulebook defining when digital tokens are securities or commodities.**

## Business & Earnings
**Berkshire Hathaway's operating earnings climbed 16% year-over-year to \$12.98 billion, as retail and energy strength offset underwriting declines, while Abel deployed \$4.5 billion toward stock buybacks. SpaceX rebounded to finish up 23% for the week despite reporting a massive \$18.4 billion in Q2 capital expenditures and announcing it will build its AI infrastructure exclusively on Nvidia chips. Elevator giant Otis, struggling with customer retention in its critical high-margin service business, slashed its full-year profit outlook and is deploying a \$50 million reinvestment plan to curb outages. Formula One parent Liberty Media saw shares rise nearly 4% as positive commercial outlooks for sponsorships and licensing overshadowed a nearly 40% revenue decline caused by canceled Middle East races. In advanced technology, the Trump administration announced a \$3 billion investment in strategic minerals, while Abu Dhabi-based Orbitworks is preparing its first satellite launch under its **[nascent space manufacturing program](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/08/uae-orbitworks-satellite-launch.html)**.**

## Investing & Commentary
**Advisors are urging retirees to reject overly conservative models and instead maintain a 40% to 80% allocation to equities to mitigate inflation and longevity risk, highlighting dividend ETFs like CGDV and FDVV for passive income. As interest rate uncertainty persists, VettaFi’s Todd Rosenbluth highlights growing investor interest in **[collateralized loan obligation (CLO) ETFs](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/08/rate-uncertainty-sparking-demand-for-clo-exposure-among-etfs-vettafi.html)**, noting that senior-secured, AAA-rated tranches offer attractive yields without long-term maturity risk. For aggressive growth, chip stocks represent a key recovery play; the iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) surged 7.6% and the Corgi Lithography & Semiconductor Photonics ETF (EUV) jumped 13% as investors bought the dip following the collapse of a major leveraged AI hedge fund.**
