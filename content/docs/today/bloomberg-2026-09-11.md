# Bloomberg — 2026-09-11

## Lead Story
The US inflation outlook darkened as August core CPI rose 0.3% month-over-month—beating forecasts on surges in cellular phone plans and energy costs—to reach 2.4% annually. The hotter reading cemented market expectations for a Federal Reserve interest-rate hike next week under Chair Kevin Warsh, driving benchmark 10-year Treasury yields toward the 5% threshold. Compounding inflationary pressures, crude oil spiked above \$107 a barrel and US diesel prices breached \$6 a gallon for the first time on record after Saudi Arabia closed its critical East-West pipeline following attacks.
