# Gaming News — 2026-06-15

## Top Story
Microsoft’s gaming division is undergoing a massive shakeup with the sudden departure of Xbox Game Studios boss Craig Duncan and chief of staff Louise O'Connor. This leadership exodus comes amidst dire internal warnings about slim profit margins and reports that beloved studios like Ninja Theory, Double Fine, and Compulsion Games are actively negotiating to avoid being shut down completely.

## News & Reviews

**[Capcom Pulls Dragon’s Dogma 2 Microtransactions Ahead of Dark Arisen Expansion Release](https://www.ign.com/articles/capcom-pulls-dragons-dogma-2-microtransactions-ahead-of-dark-arisen-expansion-release)** · IGN
Capcom is finally doing away with the controversial microtransactions in *Dragon's Dogma 2*, pulling items like the character editor and Portcrystals from sale on June 24. This move, alongside a permanent discount on the base game, clears the air just in time for the highly anticipated *Dark Arisen* expansion arriving on October 9, 2026. It's a massive win for players who heavily criticized the day-one monetization of standard fast-travel and revival items.

**[Ubisoft Bids Farewell to Assassin’s Creed Shadows With Final Story Update](https://www.ign.com/articles/ubisoft-bids-farewell-to-assassins-creed-shadows-with-final-story-update)** · IGN
The Sengoku era is coming to a close as *Assassin's Creed Shadows* receives its final content update, adding a concluding chapter to Naoe and Yasuke's journey. The farewell patch also introduces a brutal new endgame challenge mode called Domains, alongside crossover outfits for the upcoming *Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced*. It's a bittersweet send-off for a game that struggled with delays and canceled post-launch content, but it offers a solid final bow for the samurai and shinobi.

**[EA Sports FC, Madden NFL Publisher Announces EA Advertising](https://www.ign.com/articles/ea-sports-fc-madden-nfl-publisher-announces-ea-advertising)** · IGN
In a move that's sure to frustrate fans already paying full price for annual sports titles, EA has announced "EA Advertising," a platform designed to inject real-time, dynamic brand placements directly into games like *Madden NFL* and *EA Sports FC*. While EA claims this will "enhance, not disrupt" the experience with branded cosmetics and stadium signage, it reflects a broader industry push to aggressively monetize existing player bases. We'll have to wait and see if this eventually bleeds into other EA franchises like *The Sims*.

**[Final Fantasy Resonance Interview: The First HD-2D FF Was Conceptualized 6-7 Years Ago](https://www.ign.com/articles/final-fantasy-resonance-interview-the-first-hd-2d-ff-was-conceptualized-6-7-years-ago-and-inspired-by-final-fantasy-v)** · IGN
*Final Fantasy Resonance* is bringing the legendary series back to its turn-based roots using a gorgeous HD-2D art style, adapting the story from the mobile game *Brave Exvius*. The developers drew heavy inspiration from the customizable Job system of *Final Fantasy V* and the unique character traits of *Final Fantasy VI* to build an intricate, modernized combat system featuring a break/stagger mechanic. Releasing this October, it looks like an absolute must-play for fans hungry for a classic, world-saving JRPG adventure.

**[Hot Wheels: Infinite Rush Is All About Exploring an Open-World Toy Box | IGN Preview](https://www.ign.com/articles/hot-wheels-infinite-rush-is-all-about-exploring-an-open-world-toy-box-ign-preview)** · IGN
Developer Milestone is taking the iconic orange tracks into an open-world setting with *Hot Wheels: Infinite Rush*, and our hands-on preview reveals a game heavily inspired by *Burnout Paradise*. Players can freely explore massive diorama-style zones, swap between 150 different toy cars, and seamlessly jump into chaotic races and destruction derbies. It successfully captures the childhood magic of an overgrown toy box and adds a brilliant new layer of freedom to the arcade racing franchise.
