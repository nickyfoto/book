# CNBeta — August 18, 2026

## Top Story
According to **[a cnbeta report on a massive new AI facility](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573800.htm)**, OpenAI has signed a 20-year lease to occupy a historic Cold War-era nuclear enrichment site in Ohio, which is being converted into a massive 8-gigawatt AI super-factory. The ambitious project is being built by SoftBank's SB Energy with a \$1.5 billion investment and up to \$105 billion in credit guarantees from Nvidia to help secure financing. This colossal project, expected to eventually host up to 1.5 million GPUs, signals a strategic pivot in the global AI race where the primary bottlenecks have shifted from chip fabrication capacity to land acquisition and electrical grid infrastructure.

## Tech & AI
Chinese AI champion Zhipu has isolated several high-risk network security tasks from its public portal following the launch of its GLM-5.3 model. According to **[a cnbeta report on Zhipu's new GLM-5.3 model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573728.htm)**, the model demonstrates advanced capabilities in reproducing and exploiting vulnerabilities. Although GLM-5.3 scores highly on isolated testing benchmarks like CyberGym and ExploitBench, it still lags behind leading US closed-source systems in long-chain stability and executing multi-step attacks. 

Meanwhile, thermal management has emerged as the latest physical wall limiting AI chip performance. As highlighted in **[a cnbeta feature on synthetic diamonds](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573704.htm)**, Nvidia's next-generation Vera Rubin GPUs will adopt a "diamond-copper composite cooling" scheme to dissipate extreme localized heat fluxes. This technological shift is driving massive interest in China’s Henan province, the global epicenter of synthetic diamonds, where local manufacturers are rapidly upgrading HPHT and CVD diamond technologies to target the lucrative semiconductor market.

In a landmark legal truce, the Motion Picture Association (MPA) and ByteDance have signed a memorandum of understanding, marking the first major AI copyright agreement between Hollywood and a tech giant. Detailed in **[a cnbeta report on the Hollywood-ByteDance agreement](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/movie/1573708.htm)**, this framework establishes intellectual property protections around ByteDance’s video and image generation models, such as Seedance and Seedream, following intense friction earlier this year over unauthorized data use.

Furthermore, **[a cnbeta analysis of DeepSeek API pricing](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573754.htm)** explores how the startup's recent price hikes for V4-Pro and V4-Flash have drastically reshaped developer behavior. While DeepSeek remains highly competitive for enterprise calls, the dramatic increase in cache hit pricing has caused price-sensitive individual developers on platforms like OpenCode to slash their API calls by more than 50%. 

Geopolitical restructuring is also accelerating, as described in **[a cnbeta report on Windows 10 in China](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573782.htm)**. China has reportedly ordered government related departments to immediately uninstall customized "Shenzhou Wangxin" Windows 10 Government Edition operating systems, pushing up de-Americanization timelines by several months to migrate critical operations exclusively to domestic alternatives like Kylin and Unity.

## Consumer & Devices
Representing a brand-new hardware category, **[cnbeta's coverage of Honor's Robot Phone launch](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573696.htm)** details the world's first "robot smartphone". Priced from 9,999 yuan, the device features a titanium alloy 4DoF physical gimbal, enabling the camera to actively track subjects and rotate up to 360 degrees per second. The smartphone is also the first product resulting from Honor's mobile imaging collaboration with German cinematic equipment giant ARRI.

On the wearable side, **[a cnbeta leak about camera-equipped AirPods](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573730.htm)** reveals that demonstration videos of the device were discovered hidden inside the macOS Tahoe 26.7 Release Candidate. Code-named B790, these future AirPods will utilize cameras to feed real-time ambient information into Apple's "Visual Intelligence" system, allowing Siri to answer questions about the user's surroundings.

Meanwhile, **[a cnbeta report on Google's Pixel manufacturing](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573742.htm)** reveals that Google has notified suppliers of its plans to completely move the production of all Pixel smartphones, smartwatches, and wireless earbuds out of China by 2027. Vietnam and India are expected to absorb the majority of the manufacturing capacity as Google seeks to insulate its hardware business from geopolitical frictions.

Ford is executing a similar supply chain shift, as described in **[a cnbeta report on Ford's Lincoln Nautilus](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573746.htm)**. Due to a punishing 52.5% composite tariff on Chinese-assembled vehicles entering the US, as well as strict connected vehicle regulations, Ford has decided to repatriate Nautilus assembly from Hangzhou, China, back to the US by 2030.

Developed by S-Game with action design by martial arts superstar Donnie Yen, the dark wuxia title is set to launch on October 29, 2026, across PS5, Steam, Epic, and WeGame platforms.

On the behavioral science front, **[a cnbeta summary of a recent Steam study](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573734.htm)** explores a paradoxical shift in player sentiment. While initial negative reviews are dominated by short-term playtimes under 2 hours, researchers found that players who cross the 200-hour mark see their negative review rates spike to 28.2%, highlighting how deep, committed attachment often manifests as highly critical feedback.

Furthermore, **[a cnbeta report on game industry layoffs](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573780.htm)** highlights a massive ongoing industry "reset," with over 10,618 jobs cut and 29 studios closed globally in 2026. The report cites a severe post-pandemic spending cooling, rising hardware component costs, and the major market-siphoning effect of anticipated blockbusters like GTA 6 as primary drivers for the downturn.

## 技术与人工智能

在版权保护领域，好莱坞与字节跳动达成历史性“休战”。根据**[cnbeta关于好莱坞与字节跳动达成版权协议的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/movie/1573708.htm)**，美国电影协会（MPA）与字节跳动签署谅解备忘录，为防范其Seedance及Seedream等视频与图像生成模型未经授权使用版权内容建立起了合作保护框架。
