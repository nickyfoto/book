# Gaming News — 2026-06-08

## Top Story
Microsoft has finally admitted that Xbox Game Pass lost "millions of subscribers" over the span of a few months following the massive 50% price hike to the Ultimate tier in October 2025. Fortunately, Xbox executives claim the service has since stabilized and returned to growth after rolling back the monthly cost to $22.99, but the initial damage highlights a critical misstep in their subscription strategy.

## News & Reviews

**Xbox Clarifies "Case-by-Case" Exclusivity Future** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-game-exclusivity-will-be-decided-on-a-case-by-case-basis-microsoft-says)
Following the reveal that *Gears of War: E-Day* will skip PlayStation, Xbox exec Matt Booty clarified that future exclusivity decisions will be made on a "case-by-case basis". However, he confirmed that large live-service games and titles already promised to PlayStation—like the upcoming *Halo: Campaign Evolved*—will remain multiplatform. This mixed messaging shows Microsoft is still carefully trying to balance pleasing loyal Xbox fans with securing lucrative PlayStation sales.

**Nintendo Direct Announced Ahead of Switch 2** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/nintendo-direct-announced-for-this-week-as-fans-expect-huge-switch-2-reveals)
A massive 50-minute Nintendo Direct is officially set for June 9, backed by sky-high anticipation as fans eagerly await reveals for the Switch 2. Rumors point to a *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* remake and potential glimpses of heavy-hitting third-party titles like *Spyro: A Realm Beyond* and *Monster Hunter Wilds* coming to the next-generation console.

**Spider-Man: Brand New Day Trailer Leaks, Confirms Massive Runtime** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/spider-man-brand-new-days-second-trailer-leaks-online-with-sony-now-issuing-copyright-takedowns)
Sony is frantically issuing copyright takedowns after an extremely low-quality version of the second trailer for *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* leaked online, confirming an appearance by Mark Ruffalo's Hulk. As if the leak wasn't enough news, AMC Theatres revealed the highly anticipated film will boast a staggering 150-minute runtime, making Tom Holland's fourth solo outing the longest Spidey film ever made.

**Ninja Theory Cancels Project Mara to Focus on Senua** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/hellblade-developer-ninja-theory-has-cancelled-project-mara)
Ninja Theory has officially pulled the plug on *Project Mara*, its highly experimental psychological horror title that was set entirely in a photorealistic apartment. Studio head Dom Matthews made the tough call to shift all 85 developers over to *Senua*, allowing the entire team to fully realize the potential of their newly announced third entry in the *Hellblade* series.

**Gears of War: E-Day Open Beta Set for August** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/gears-of-war-e-day-open-beta-how-to-play)
The upcoming Xbox-exclusive origin story *Gears of War: E-Day* has locked in an open multiplayer beta starting on August 6, 2026. Players who preorder any edition of the game or have an active Game Pass subscription will get early access to the bloody action during the first weekend.
