# CNBC — 2026-09-17

## Lead Story
The Federal Reserve unanimously raised its benchmark interest rate by 25 basis points to a target range of 3.75%–4.00% in its first hike in three years, triggering a brief market sell-off before equities rebounded. Chairman Kevin Warsh delivered a hawkish tone warning that persistent inflation—driven in part by crude oil crossing \$100 per barrel—could warrant additional rate increases later this year.

## Markets & Economics
Following Wednesday's 630-point decline in the Dow, major U.S. stock indices mounted a recovery on Thursday as the **[S&P 500](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/stocks-are-rallying-after-fed-sell-off-why-now-may-not-be-the-time-to-buy-the-dip.html)** gained 1.1% and the Nasdaq Composite advanced 1.7%. Benchmark **[10-year Treasury yields](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/treasury-yields-move-lower-after-fed-kicks-off-hiking-cycle.html)** pulled back to 4.95% after topping 5.00%, while two-year yields hovered near 4.70%. Energy market pressures remained acute as **[U.S. diesel fuel](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/diesel-gas-prices-oil-us-economy.html)** hit a record \$6.31 per gallon, even as WTI crude settled near \$102 per barrel following Saudi Arabia's ship-to-ship crude transfers near Oman. Internationally, the **[Bank of England](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/bank-of-england-interest-rate-decision-fed-rate-hike-uk-inflation.html)** signaled it would hold rates at 3.75% despite U.K. August CPI reaching 3.1%, while markets anticipate a Bank of Japan rate hike on Friday. In economic releases, U.S. **[pending home sales](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/17/august-pending-home-sales-rises-0-point-3-percent.html)** managed a modest 0.3% rise in August despite persistent mortgage rate headwinds.

## Business & Earnings
Power infrastructure firm **[Generac](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/generac-shares-surge-on-amazon-deal-wall-street-thinks-it-has-more-to-go-.html)** surged 19% after announcing a \$2.4 billion agreement to supply backup power generators for Amazon data centers, alongside \$340 million in stock warrants for Amazon. Semiconductor sentiment got a boost as **[Nvidia](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/nvidia-huang-ai-chip-guidance.html)** CEO Jensen Huang projected the company will double its chip sales next year, while **[Arm Holdings](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/jim-cramer-arm-ceo-ai-revenue-goal.html)** CEO Rene Haas voiced increased confidence in achieving \$2.0 billion in customer demand for its new custom AGI CPU. In automotive, **[General Motors](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/chevy-silverado-gmc-sierra-v-8-engines.html)** unveiled new gas-powered V-8 and diesel engines for its 2027 Silverado and Sierra pickup trucks to protect its full-size truck leadership amid slowing EV adoption. Meanwhile, **[Boeing](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/stocks-stage-a-comeback-after-fed-induced-declines-plus-whats-ailing-boeing.html)** shares dipped after CEO Kelly Ortberg admitted that stabilizing 737 Max production at 47 aircraft per month is taking longer than expected due to wing manufacturing delays.

## Investing & Commentary
Market analyst Steve Grasso highlighted **[Cloudflare](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/investor-steve-grasso-is-buying-cloudflare.html)** as a top momentum buy, citing its accelerating 36% revenue growth and key role acting as a toll collector for AI web traffic. Wealth managers at Ritholtz pointed to **[Illumina](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/theres-a-biotech-stock-on-josh-browns-list-thats-ignoring-the-market-noise-and-pushing-higher.html)** as an independent technical breakout, supported by 9.5% revenue growth and its upcoming promotion into the S&P 500 index. Strategists at Barclays touted unloved **[U.K. equities](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/market-volatility-global-equities.html)** as an undervalued hedge against global volatility, while RBC Capital initiated **[Kraft Heinz](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/khc-has-lagged-in-the-past-year-rbc-thinks-thats-about-to-change.html)** with an Outperform rating and \$32 price target on expected 2027 organic growth.
