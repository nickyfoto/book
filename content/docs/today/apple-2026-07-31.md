# Apple's Record Q3 Meets Supply Chain Realities — 2026-07-31

## Highlights
Apple posted a record-breaking $109.4 billion quarter for Q3 2026, though celebrations are tempered by severe supply constraints and skyrocketing memory costs that will heavily impact the fall product rollout. Rumors indicate this component crunch could drive the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro models up by as much as $300, even as the iOS 27 public beta continues to reveal impressive new system capabilities ahead of September.

## Top Stories
- **Apple’s Q3 2026 Earnings—Record Revenue and Marketing to the Street**: Outgoing CEO Tim Cook announced a record $109.4 billion quarter with $29.8 billion in net profit, but warned of significant supply constraints driven by skyrocketing memory costs. This supply crunch, alongside slowing Services growth due to regulatory changes, caused Apple stock to open down roughly 8.6%, wiping $430 billion from its market value. ([Source](https://tidbits.com/2026/07/31/apples-q3-2026-earnings-record-revenue-and-marketing-to-the-street/))
- **iPhone 18 Pro Models Could Be Up to $300 More Expensive, Says Analyst**: Analyst Jeff Pu claims the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max could see massive price hikes of $250 to $300 over the iPhone 17 Pro lineup. The steep increase is reportedly due to soaring DRAM/NAND costs and the expensive shift to advanced 2nm silicon during the AI infrastructure buildout. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/31/iphone-18-pro-models-300-more-expensive/))
- **MacBook Ultra’s New Design Won’t Stay Exclusive for Long**: The highly anticipated OLED touchscreen MacBook Ultra is expected to debut a thinner and lighter chassis that removes the display notch in favor of a hole-punch camera and Dynamic Island. This radical redesign is expected to eventually trickle down to the entry-level M7 MacBook Pro by 2027. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/30/macbook-ultra-design-wont-stay-exclusive/))
- **iPhone Air 2 Expected to Launch Early Next Year With Five New Features**: Apple is preparing an early 2027 launch for the second-generation iPhone Air, bringing a secondary 48-megapixel Ultra Wide camera and a smaller Dynamic Island. It will feature the new A20 Pro 2nm chip, alongside the C2 5G modem and N2 networking chip for maximum power efficiency. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/31/iphone-air-2-five-new-features-rumored/))
- **Set a Custom EQ for Your AirPods in iOS 27**: Apple is finally allowing users to adjust the audio frequency spectrum of their AirPods natively through a new three-band equalizer in iOS 27. Exclusive to models with the H2 chip, this custom EQ profile will also carry over to connected iPads and Macs. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/how-to/set-custom-eq-airpods-ios/))
