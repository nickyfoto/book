## News & Reviews

**[Nintendo Warns of Impending Switch 2 Price Hike](https://www.ign.com/articles/nintendo-now-warning-in-store-customers-of-switch-2-price-hike-with-just-over-2-weeks-to-go-until-cost-increases)** · IGN

**[Wild Blue Skies Review: A Disappointing Star Fox 64 Clone](https://www.ign.com/articles/wild-blue-skies-review)** · IGN
IGN's review of *Wild Blue Skies* calls out the game for being an uninspired rail-shooter clone that copies *Star Fox 64* a bit too literally. While it mimics the classic's branching paths, scoring systems, and level designs, it stumbles significantly on **sluggish ship handling, weak starting blasters, and a punishing health system**. Ultimately, the game is only worth playing when it has the courage to innovate, but those moments are far too rare.

**[Pokémon Go Celebrates 10th Anniversary with Massive Mewtwo and Master Ball Giveaway](https://www.ign.com/articles/theres-never-been-a-better-time-to-log-into-pokemon-go-as-the-game-gives-away-a-perfect-stat-mewtwo-and-master-ball)** · IGN
In a surprise move, developers have added a **free, guaranteed 100% perfect-stat Mewtwo and a rare Master Ball** for all players who log in before September 6. While officially framed as a 10th-anniversary celebration, players see this as a major apology for a recent, highly controversial invite-only event in Times Square. It is an absurdly generous reward, considering that Master Balls were previously locked behind a **\$7.99 premium ticket**.

**[Co-Creator Marcus Lehto Criticizes Halo Remake's "Offensive" Menu Ring](https://www.ign.com/articles/halo-studios-went-too-far-in-the-wrong-direction-co-creator-of-halo-hits-out-at-the-main-menu-ring-in-xboxs-new-remake)** · IGN
Bungie co-creator Marcus Lehto has publicly criticized the new *Halo: Campaign Evolved* remake, labeling its main menu Forerunner ring as **"the most offensive thing we see"**. Lehto argued that the ring looks like a "shiny new toy" that lacks the imposing scale, micro-detail, and mysterious, ancient vibe of the original 2001 game. He also slammed the developer's decision to omit split-screen PvP from the remake, calling it a **major planning misstep**.

**[Did Bethesda Hide the True Name of The Elder Scrolls 6 in Starfield?](https://www.ign.com/articles/it-looks-like-bethesda-hid-the-true-name-of-the-elder-scrolls-6-in-starfield-years-ago)** · IGN
A highly compelling fan theory suggests that Bethesda hid the official subtitle for *The Elder Scrolls 6*—which appears to be **Sentinel**—inside *Starfield*'s character creator three years ago. When divided into pairs, a character ID number on the screen translates alphabetically to **spell out the word "Sentinel" perfectly**. Sentinel is a major capital city in Hammerfell, the region where the highly anticipated sequel is widely believed to take place.

**[Netflix Abruptly Shutters Night School Studio Following Unhinged Launch](https://www.ign.com/articles/night-school-staff-speak-out-after-netflix-shuts-down-developer-following-top-tier-launch-of-unhinged)** · IGN
In a shocking development, Netflix has closed down *Oxenfree* developer Night School Studio just six weeks after the launch of their narrative horror game *Unhinged*. Although Netflix co-CEO Greg Peters praised *Unhinged* as a top-performing cloud game launch, the company has made organizational cuts to streamline its gaming efforts. Displaced staff took to social media to share their pride in the product, even as they find themselves back on the job market.
