# Seattle Local — 2026-08-31

## Top Story
Classes for nearly 50,000 Seattle Public Schools students remain in limbo as an imminent teacher strike looms following the breakdown of contract talks between the district and the Seattle Education Association ahead of Wednesday's scheduled school start. With the union's previous collective bargaining agreement expiring at midnight on August 31, district leadership and union negotiators are attempting last-minute mediation after the school district walked away from the bargaining table over the weekend.

**[Thousands of Washington State Workers Walk Out Over Stalled Pay Talks](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/politics/wa-state-workers-stage-walkout-to-protest-pay-proposals-from-ferguson/)** · The Seattle Times
Thousands of Washington state public employees represented by the Washington Federation of State Employees walked off the job Monday at more than 125 worksites across the state, including regional agency offices, community colleges, and healthcare facilities. Union members are protesting wage proposals offered by Gov. Bob Ferguson's administration in ongoing contract negotiations, which workers argue fail to keep pace with escalating living costs. The coordinated labor action could disrupt government services throughout King County and Western Washington as union representatives push the state for higher general wage increases.

**[Washington Joins FTC Lawsuit Accusing Amazon of Misleading Ad Prices](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/wa-ftc-lawsuit-amazon-misleading-advertising-prices)** · FOX 13 Seattle
Washington state has joined the Federal Trade Commission and 21 other states in a major lawsuit accusing Seattle-based retail giant Amazon of artificially inflating advertising prices and misleading merchant partners. Regulators allege that Amazon's deceptive fee structures harmed small- and medium-sized businesses that rely heavily on the platform to reach customers. The legal action marks a significant escalation in regulatory scrutiny against the tech giant's marketplace dominance right in its hometown.

The layoffs reflect ongoing tech sector volatility and structural workforce adjustments impacting Puget Sound tech workers and regional real estate.

**[Record-Low Oxygen Levels in Hood Canal Spark Massive Fish Die-Off](https://www.king5.com/article/news/local/dead-fish-wash-ashore-as-hood-canal-oxygen-levels-hit-record-lows/281-5b64c5f2-3703-47ea-97fc-34f175819b64)** · KING 5
University of Washington oceanographers reported near-zero dissolved oxygen levels in the waters of southern Hood Canal near Potlatch and Twanoh, triggering a severe fish die-off along local shorelines. Marine scientists noted the readings are among the lowest ever recorded in the waterway, suffocating bottom-dwelling sea life unable to escape the hypoxic zone. Local and state environmental agencies are monitoring the waterway as late-summer conditions continue to strain Puget Sound marine habitats.
