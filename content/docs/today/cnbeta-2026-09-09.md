# CNBeta — 2026-09-09

## Top Story
According to a [CNBeta report on DeepSeek's capital strategy](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577124.htm), the Hangzhou-based generative AI pioneer has hired CITIC Securities to guide an initial public offering on Shanghai's STAR Market, targeting a valuation of up to RMB 500 billion (\$75 billion) to fund massive compute infrastructure and counter aggressive talent poaching from ByteDance and Xiaomi. The IPO push comes as [DeepSeek released its V4.1 Flash model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577126.htm) with inference speeds exceeding 500 tokens/s and [slashed Flash API pricing by up to 60%](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577090.htm), underscoring China's rapid push to commercialize high-speed, cost-effective LLM infrastructure on a global scale.

## Tech & AI

Meanwhile, Counterpoint's latest [smartphone SoC market share analysis](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577174.htm) shows MediaTek maintaining its global lead at 31%, while Huawei HiSilicon's share rose to 5% driven by the flagship Pura 90 series.

Geopolitical tensions over artificial intelligence escalated as the US government [accused major Chinese AI firms of model distillation](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577070.htm), alleging that DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, and Alibaba extracted outputs from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google to accelerate domestic model development. Concurrently, safety concerns inside top labs mounted as [Anthropic's alignment head validated extinction risks](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577132.htm), agreeing with a resigned researcher that the probability of AI causing human extinction within this decade exceeds 10%.

In global AI infrastructure, [Google committed €13 billion in Finland](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577148.htm) to expand AI data centers supported by a 22-year nuclear power agreement with Fortum. Enterprise security budgets are also shifting rapidly, with [Anthropic's Claude Mythos driving an AI security overhaul](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577170.htm) as corporations reallocate funds toward autonomous AI vulnerability scanners and defense agents.

## Consumer & Devices
Apple held its fall hardware event, unveiling its first [foldable iPhone Duo alongside the iPhone 18 Pro](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577222.htm), featuring a 7.6-inch inner display, Apple Pencil support, and a \$1,999 base price, while new CEO John Ternus hosted his inaugural keynote. Powered by the new [2nm A20 Pro chip featuring 4.9GHz peak clock speeds](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577202.htm), the Pro series starts at \$1,099 with 256GB storage; however, severe global DRAM shortages forced [Apple to raise prices on existing iPhone 17 models](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577228.htm) and delay the standard iPhone 18 release to 2027.

In autonomous mobility, a [breakdown of Tesla Cybercab's first week in Austin](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577108.htm) uncovered a hidden software joystick used for manual operator intervention, lower base fares undercut by peak surge pricing, and an active NHTSA regulatory investigation into its lack of physical controls. Meanwhile, Xiaomi addressed a [Pengcheng EV test drive crash incident](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577072.htm), clarifying that a driver accidentally pressed the accelerator into a greenbelt with no injuries sustained.

Apple also expanded its personal hardware ecosystem by launching the [AirPods 5 with real-time speech translation](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577216.htm) and 5-hour ANC battery life. Additionally, the company introduced the [Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577220.htm) priced at \$399 and \$799, debuting a "Readiness" recovery tracking app and 128GB of internal storage.
