# CNBC — 2026-08-13

## Lead Story
U.S. wholesale prices came in flat for July, undershooting the 0.2% expected increase and reinforcing that inflation pressures are easing on the heels of yesterday's consumer price index release. This cooling economic backdrop, combined with robust earnings, helped propel the S&P 500 to a record closing high above 7,800.

## Markets & Economics
U.S. stock futures remained stable after the **[S&P 500](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/.SPX/)** hit an intraday record of 7,816.70 on Thursday, buoyed by the tech and communication services sectors. This rally was reinforced as the July producer price index (PPI) came in flat, undershooting economists' expectations of a 0.2% increase and further tempering wagers on a September Federal Reserve rate hike. In fixed income, the 10-year U.S. Treasury note yield dipped to 4.674%, while the 2-year yield, which closely tracks Fed policy, fell to 4.176%. On the commodities front, oil prices climbed due to persistent tensions in the Middle East and a worsening oil spill near Oman, raising concerns over energy flows through the Strait of Hormuz. For a deeper look at the domestic energy landscape, watch our coverage on **[why America could get its first major refinery in 50 years](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/12/why-america-could-get-its-first-major-refinery-in-50-years.html)**.

## Business & Earnings
In corporate moves, **[Workday](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/WDAY/)** shares skyrocketed over 19% before trading was halted on reports that private equity firm Silver Lake is in talks to acquire the company. ## Investing & Commentary
In sector commentary, Bill Ackman's Pershing Square disclosed a new position in **[Netflix](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/NFLX/)**, arguing the company has "effectively won the streaming wars" and trades at an attractive discount of 21 times forward earnings after a 50% pullback. However, "Big Short" investor Steve Eisman warned of an AI "Achilles heel," noting that massive tech hyperscalers are overly dependent on OpenAI and Anthropic for 25% to 35% of their cloud revenue. For investors seeking income, Mizuho tapped Sunbelt office REIT Cousins Properties and grocery-anchored Phillips Edison as top real estate picks for August, offering attractive yields of 4.34% and 3.24% respectively. Additionally, Jefferies noted that with 10-year real TIPS yields sitting at 2.36%, investors should focus on high-quality, low-P/E yield plays like JPMorgan Chase and ConocoPhillips.
