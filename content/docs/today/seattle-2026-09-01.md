# Seattle Local — 2026-09-01

## Top Story
**Seattle Public Schools (SPS)** and the **Seattle Education Association (SEA)** are engaged in last-minute mediated negotiations to avert a strike that could delay the start of the school year for **50,000 students** on Wednesday. **Superintendent Ben Shuldiner** expressed optimism that a tentative contract agreement can be reached before the midnight deadline, though the union's **6,000 members** remain prepared to strike over issues including class sizes, special education support, and pay raises.

## Local News

**[Former Police Chief Shon Barnes Breaks Silence on Resignation and Mass Shooting](https://komonews.com/news/local/former-seattle-police-chief-shon-barnes-talks-forced-resignation-unsolved-bite-of-seattle-mass-shooting-response-mayor-katie-wilson-exclusive-interview-delay-public-update-public-safety-crime)** · KOMO News
Former Seattle Police Chief Shon Barnes broke his silence in an exclusive interview, stating that **Mayor Katie Wilson** has never explained why she asked him to resign following last month’s unsolved mass shooting at the Bite of Seattle. Barnes defended his department's response to the incident, asserting that he followed long-standing safety protocols and did not order a delay in updating the public. The interview has intensified questions regarding the city's communication during the crisis and the circumstances surrounding his forced departure.

**[Ballard Battery Manufacturer Under Investigation After Lead Sidewalk Contamination](https://www.king5.com/article/tech/science/environment/seattle-battery-manufacturer-says-its-mistake-prompted-lead-investigation-closures/281-1139803b-4255-4f4f-bc42-9a87b41ae4cf)** · KING 5
An Interbay battery manufacturer acknowledged that a flaw in its battery-making process allowed **toxic lead dust** to escape and coat a nearby public sidewalk. **Public Health — Seattle & King County** has launched an investigation and is urging residents to avoid a portion of **23rd Avenue West** in Ballard to limit exposure. The manufacturer confirmed the error and is cooperating with health officials as cleanup and monitoring efforts begin.

**[Secretary Steve Hobbs Rejects Federal Voter Fraud Probe in Washington](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/secretary-hobbs-dhs-wa-voter-fraud?taid=6a963529ef34f600018759b8&utm_campaign=trueAnthem%3A+Trending+Content&utm_medium=trueAnthem&utm_source=twitter)** · FOX 13
The **U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS)** has reportedly ordered a surge to investigate alleged voter fraud in Washington and eight other states ahead of the midterm elections. Washington **Secretary of State Steve Hobbs** strongly criticized the federal investigation, describing it as a "troubling" waste of taxpayer dollars. State officials expressed surprise at the sudden federal scrutiny of Washington's mail-in voting, which is currently subject to a temporary court-ordered block on voting rules changes.

**[Thousands of Washington State Employees Stage Walkouts Amid Contract Negotiations](https://www.king5.com/article/news/politics/keep-washington-running-thousands-state-workers-walk-out-amid-contract-talks/281-a8776688-9009-4fd1-9213-b7a859a50e2e)** · KING 5
Thousands of Washington state employees staged walkouts at **more than 100 locations** on Monday, calling for higher pay, safer staffing, and progress in ongoing contract negotiations. Demonstrators participating in the **"Keep Washington Running"** campaign emphasized that current staffing levels and salaries are insufficient to maintain essential services. The massive labor action highlights escalating tensions as union members seek cost-of-living adjustments from state negotiators.

**[Amazon to Lay Off 121 Corporate Employees Across Western Washington Locations](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/amazon-layoffs-wa?taid=6a96f306ef34f60001875fac&utm_campaign=trueAnthem%3A+Trending+Content&utm_medium=trueAnthem&utm_source=twitter)** · FOX 13
Amazon has issued a **Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification (WARN)** stating that the company will lay off **121 employees** from its western Washington operations. The job cuts are scheduled to take effect in October and will impact workers at facilities in **Seattle, Bellevue, and Sumner**. State employment security agencies are preparing to assist affected personnel with career transition and job search services.

**[Kirkland Homeowner Discovers Bobcat Perched on Staircase After Suspected Burglary Call](https://www.kiro7.com/news/local/bobcat-sneaks-into-kirkland-home-mistaken-burglar/WRVQVIA5MZB5JAV5BCZ7ERRNLE/#Echobox=1788276632)** · KIRO 7
A Kirkland resident who reported a possible burglary in progress after hearing loud banging inside their house was startled when responding officers found a wild **bobcat**. Kirkland Police Department officers discovered the unexpected intruder perched calmly at the **top of a staircase**. Working alongside state wildlife officials, police safely captured and released the uninjured bobcat near **Forbes Creek**.

Local weather will remain mild with highs in the **70s** on Tuesday under mixed clouds, followed by a strong, fall-like weather system on Wednesday that is forecast to bring a much-needed soaking rain of up to an **inch** across the Puget Sound region. Residents seeking unique local experiences can visit the **"Inconvenience Store"** in downtown Seattle, where an old 7-Eleven has been transformed into an immersive art show with reimagined convenience items for sale, or catch a movie at the historic **Edmonds Theater**, which was recently purchased and saved from closure by travel writer **Rick Steves**.
