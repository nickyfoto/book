# CNBC — 2026-09-18

## Lead Story
**[Warren Buffett](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/buffett-stepping-down-as-berkshire-chairman.html)** is stepping down as chairman of Berkshire Hathaway after six decades, transitioning to chairman emeritus while appointing his son Howard Buffett as chairman and confirming Greg Abel remains in operational control as CEO. The 96-year-old legendary investor noted "Father Time always wins," concluding a historic tenure that delivered a 19.7% compounded annual return since 1965.

## Markets & Economics
Wall Street digested the Federal Reserve's quarter-point rate hike to 3.75%–4.00%, as markets analyzed **[Chairman Kevin Warsh's commentary](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/three-words-from-kevin-warsh-have-wall-street-wondering-how-far-the-fed-will-go-with-rate-hikes.html)** framing the move as removing "a dose of accommodation" rather than restrictive tightening. For the week, the Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 1.7% and the S&P 500 slipped 0.1%, while the Nasdaq Composite gained 0.7% as tech shares stabilized. Overseas, the **[Bank of Japan raised its policy rate](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/japan-raises-rates-30-year-high-yen-jgb.html)** by 25 basis points to a 31-year high of 1.25% in a split 7–2 vote, though the yen unexpectedly weakened past 157 against the dollar and the Nikkei 225 rose 1.5% due to the less hawkish tone. In fixed income and commodities, the benchmark 10-year Treasury yield held near 4.95%, while **[Brent crude futures fell 0.9%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/oil-prices-today-brent-wti-saudi-arabia-houthi.html)** to \$103.83 per barrel as alternative Saudi export routes helped ease immediate supply fears despite ongoing Houthi strikes.

## Business & Earnings
In corporate moves, **[Disney appointed Karandeep Anand](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/disney-cto-tech.html)**, former CEO of Character.AI, as its first Chief Technology Officer to lead its AI, streaming, and enterprise infrastructure push under CEO Josh D'Amaro. AI infrastructure provider **[Nscale filed for an IPO](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/nscale-ai-cloud-provider-ipo-nscl.html)** under ticker NSCL, reporting a 1,252% revenue jump to \$140.6 million for the first half of 2026 alongside a \$1.02 billion net loss and \$8 billion in debt. In aerospace, **[SpaceX expanded its NASA contract](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/spacex-scores-950-million-from-nasa-for-3-more-crewed-flights-to-iss-.html)** by \$950 million for three additional crewed Dragon flights to the ISS through 2030, bringing its total mission earnings under the program to \$5.92 billion. Meanwhile, one of Wendy's largest U.S. franchisees, **[Meritage Hospitality Group, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/wendys-franchisee-files-for-chapter-11-bankruptcy-protection.html)** across 314 locations following six consecutive quarters of same-store sales declines for the burger chain. Lastly, **[Nike parted ways with soccer star Kylian Mbappé](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/kylian-mbappe-signs-with-on-after-nike-parts-ways.html)**, allowing Swiss sportswear brand On to sign the Real Madrid forward in its first major football endorsement.

## Investing & Commentary
On Wall Street, **[Wells Fargo downgraded Netflix](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/netflix-is-in-the-doldrums-a-comeback-is-unlikely-wells-fargo-says.html)** to underweight and cut its price target to \$57 from \$80, citing an 8% drop in subscriber engagement and a weak second-half content pipeline. Conversely, **[JPMorgan upgraded Tyson Foods](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/stocks-making-the-biggest-moves-premarket-nflx-nue-xene.html)** to overweight with a \$63 price target, arguing feed cost headwinds in its chicken segment are already priced into the stock. In crypto, **[VanEck forecasted Bitcoin could reach \$100,000](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/bitcoin-cryptocurrency-forecast-vaneck.html)** by next year, pointing to unsustainable sovereign debt levels and institutional buying as key tailwinds.
