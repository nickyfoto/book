# Company@X — 2026-07-29

## Signal of the Day
Microsoft reported its FY26 Q4 earnings, revealing that Azure revenue surpassed $100 billion for the first time with 41% growth, while Microsoft 365 Copilot reached over 30 million paid seats. This massive structural revenue validates sustained, high-volume enterprise willingness to pay for AI capabilities and infrastructure at scale.

## Key Announcements

**[OpenAI]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/OpenAI/status/2082577277246972300#m)
OpenAI revealed that applying GPT-5.6 Sol to optimize its own production resulted in 20% lower serving costs and over 15% better token-generation efficiency. The company also demonstrated that enabling "retained reasoning" and "context compaction" in its Responses API tripled the model's score on the ARC-AGI-3 benchmark while using 6x fewer output tokens. This highlights how API settings and harness design drastically alter frontier model performance.

**[Federal Communications Commission]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/BrendanCarrFCC/status/2082193391149048145#m)
The FCC added foreign-produced advanced robotic devices, including humanoids and quadrupeds, as well as foreign-made power inverters to its Covered List. This effectively bans new versions of these devices from import or sale in the United States. The decision follows determinations from national security agencies that these devices pose unacceptable risks, though case-by-case exemptions may apply.

**[Waymo]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Waymo/status/2082512474822086810#m)
Waymo introduced its new "Ojai" vehicle platform, which features a revamped in-car user interface and integrates Google's Gemini for cabin controls and knowledge queries. Concurrently, the company is resuming freeway routing for faster rides, beginning in Phoenix and expanding to other cities soon.

**[Google Cloud]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleCloudTech/status/2082537759097397347#m)
Google Cloud announced the general availability of its Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. The centralized, end-to-end platform is designed to help enterprises securely manage and scale their AI agents and workflows.

**[CoreAutoAI]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/sonyatweetybird/status/2082549709223436658#m)
Former OpenAI reasoning lead Jerry Tworek and former Gemini pre-training lead Rohan Anil launched CoreAutoAI. The startup argues that models are hindered by their inability to learn after deployment and aims to build an automated lab where AI systematically uncovers new research advances instead of relying on human researchers.

**[OpenAI]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/OpenAI/status/2082516370949062989#m)
OpenAI launched "ChatGPT for Academic Researchers," offering free access to its GPT-5.6 frontier models. The program starts with 10,000 scientists, mathematicians, and engineers, and plans to expand to 100,000 participants by 2027 to accelerate scientific discovery.
