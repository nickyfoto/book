### Food Science & Tips
A crucial preparation technique highlighted in this cucumber recipe relies on the power of salt to draw out excess moisture for the perfect texture. By tossing the sliced cucumbers with 10 grams of salt and letting them sit for just three minutes, the vegetable softens slightly but retains a snappy, irresistible crunch. Gently squeezing the drawn liquid out of the cucumbers before dressing ensures they perfectly absorb the zesty lemon-mustard sauce instead of watering it down.
