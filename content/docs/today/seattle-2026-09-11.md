# Seattle Local — 2026-09-11

## Top Story
A coalition of 38 major Seattle corporate leaders—including executives from Microsoft, Starbucks, Costco, Nordstrom, Alaska Airlines, and the Seattle Mariners—sent a joint letter to Mayor Katie Wilson and the City Council demanding an immediate escalation of city public safety measures. The business group called for a 100-day action plan that includes increased police patrols, restoring surveillance cameras in high-crime areas, clearing encampments, and setting clear benchmarks for police response times.
