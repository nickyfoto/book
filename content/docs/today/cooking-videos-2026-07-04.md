### Food Science & Tips
Tucked away at the end of his latest vlog, Chef James drops a vital piece of kitchen science regarding pressure cookers. He explains that the most critical difference between a pressure cooker and a standard pot is moisture control; because liquid doesn't evaporate during the pressurized cooking process, you have to carefully reduce the amount of water you add to your recipes to avoid a soggy dish.
