## League Pulse
The NFL world is completely obsessed with two polarizing figures right now: Ty Simpson and Tom Brady. Simpson is splitting the draft community down the middle, with evaluators warring over whether his elite footwork makes up for his 15 collegiate starts and smaller stature. Meanwhile, the media is heavily scrutinizing a restless 48-year-old Tom Brady, who seems entirely unable to commit to his ownership or broadcast roles while openly flirting with a return to the gridiron.
