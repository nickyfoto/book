**[Shuhei Yoshida Warned Expedition 33 Devs to Avoid "Turn-Based" Stigma](https://www.ign.com/articles/its-incredibly-well-made-but-former-playstation-boss-warned-clair-obscur-expedition-33-devs-it-would-be-overlooked-if-they-called-it-a-turn-based-rpg)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/its-incredibly-well-made-but-former-playstation-boss-warned-clair-obscur-expedition-33-devs-it-would-be-overlooked-if-they-called-it-a-turn-based-rpg)
Former PlayStation indies boss Shuhei Yoshida revealed he advised Sandfall Interactive not to market *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33* as a "turn-based RPG" due to **negative stigmas surrounding the genre being outdated**. It's a fascinating look at how branding can make or break a masterpiece in today's market.
