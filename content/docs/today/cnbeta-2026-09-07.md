# CNBeta — September 7, 2026

## Top Story
A major breakthrough has emerged in the Chinese semiconductor ecosystem with the launch of Huawei's tri-fold Mate XT 2, powered by the new Kirin 9050 Pro chip. According to [a cnbeta report on the Kirin 9050 Pro launch](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1576770.htm), this SoC is the first to implement "logic folding" 3D stacking packaging technology, enabling a 52% boost in multi-core performance through vertical signal routing. This architectural shift, validated by [Huawei's newly published scientific paper responding to thermal concerns](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1576746.htm), proves that Chinese chip designers can achieve generational scaling in transistor density and drastic power reductions without relying on advanced lithography machinery.
