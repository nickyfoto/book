# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-07-23

## Top Story
Anthropic's latest feature for Claude Cowork, "Record a skill," is being heralded as a profound shift in knowledge management. As analyzed in **[AI Takes Master's Judgement in 23 Seconds](https://www.infoq.cn/article/TWXI9ulbiPjRYmPFTGST)**, the tool bypasses the traditional bottleneck of writing standard operating procedures by allowing users to simply record their screen and dictate their reasoning, effectively transforming tacit human knowledge into reusable AI agents instantly. 

## Engineering & Dev
In the developer tooling space, Tencent Cloud has unveiled CodeBuddy NPC, effectively turning AI into a native participant within the software development lifecycle. By utilizing an "AI Native Git" paradigm, this agent operates directly within CI/CD pipelines, lowering token costs by 90% and handling everything from requirement breakdown to code verification, as detailed in **[Tencent Cloud Releases First Process-Native R&D Agent](https://www.infoq.cn/article/w6DHDb3QUYPLC1vaU8z1)**. On the infrastructure front, Google, Microsoft, and others have launched the Agentic Resource Discovery (ARD) specification. Discussed in **[Google and Partners Release ARD Spec](https://www.infoq.cn/article/9sEaQuLww0wAu016qH5R)**, this standard allows AI agents to discover and verify external tools and APIs across organizational boundaries with built-in governance. Meanwhile, open-source networking sees a significant update with Linkerd 2.20. As noted in **[Linkerd 2.20 Released](https://www.infoq.cn/article/TJL8TIiuD6Cev0Ig0AAS)**, the new version introduces rate-limit-aware load balancing and slashes control plane memory usage by up to 85%. 

For those following operating system evolutions, HarmonyOS is leaning heavily into AI natively. According to insights from **[From HarmonyOS to AI Native](https://www.infoq.cn/article/Ti4HDYkZAlbNmEFFYp24)**, the OS is transitioning from an app runtime environment to a systemic agent foundation, offering ecosystem tools like OpenDesk and SmartServe to reduce the friction of deploying edge AI. In the realm of physical AI, Daxiao Robotics released its Kairos 3.1 world model, which unifies perception, generation, and prediction to drastically reduce the trial-and-error costs of robots operating in complex real-world environments, covered in **[World's First Edge-Driven Embodied World Model](https://www.infoq.cn/article/u0hD10fj7HWAfb10Dbr3)**. Lastly, Meta has open-sourced Brain2Qwerty v2, a non-invasive brain-computer interface system that translates MEG/EEG signals into text with 61% accuracy, as shared in **[Meta Open-Sources Brain2Qwerty v2](https://www.infoq.cn/article/39UvyRHmCOs2Emx9T8Hl)**.

## Products & Digital
In consumer electronics, Samsung has officially launched its newest generation of foldables, the Galaxy Z Fold8 and Flip8, alongside the Galaxy Watch9 and Watch Ultra2. Highlighted in **[Samsung Releases New Foldables and Watches](https://sspai.com/post/112647)**, the Fold8 introduces a wider aspect ratio featuring a 10:16 cover screen while keeping the device at just 9.7mm thick. For creators, the newly reviewed DJI Pocket 4P is making waves. As argued in **[DJI Pocket 4P Review](https://sspai.com/post/112428)**, its 1-inch sensor, 10-bit D-Log 2 support, and physical gimbal offer stabilization and cinematic depth that computational photography on high-end smartphones still struggles to match. 

Hardware form factors are also adapting to the AI era in unexpected ways. A fascinating piece titled **[Modding an Old Mouse into an AI Hack](https://www.ifanr.com/1672824)** explores how users can remap standard mouse buttons using Logi Options+ macros to instantly trigger AI summarizations, translations, and voice inputs without needing dedicated AI keyboards. Apple's upcoming Mac lineup refresh is similarly being viewed through an AI lens. According to **[Apple Macs to Get Full Lineup Update](https://www.ifanr.com/1672867)**, the distinction is increasingly between "Macs for humans" offering OLED touchscreens and headless "Macs for AI" like the Mac mini and Studio, where unified RAM capacity dictates the size of local LLMs and agents users can run continuously.
