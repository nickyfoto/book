# Gaming News — 2026-09-18

## Top Story
Remedy Entertainment has officially launched **Control Resonant**, delivering a mind-bending, gravity-defying sequel that scored an **8/10 from IGN** alongside praise as one of the best-optimized PC releases of the year. Starring Dylan Faden as he fights through a distorted, folded New York City, the title blends aggressive melee combat with path-traced visual brilliance.

## News & Reviews

**[Control Resonant Review & PC Impressions](https://www.ign.com/articles/control-resonant-review)** · IGN
Remedy's action sequel shifts perspective to Dylan Faden, featuring aggressive melee combat with the morphing Aberrant weapon and mind-bending gravity mechanics in a distorted Manhattan. IGN awarded the game an **8/10 score** for its inventive level design and deep worldbuilding, while hardware testing confirms it is a picture-perfect PC port running smoothly with path tracing and DLSS. It stands out as a must-play release for Remedy fans looking for a fresh spin on paranatural action.

**[Resident Evil (2026) Movie Review & Record-Breaking Release](https://www.ign.com/articles/resident-evil-movie-2026-review)** · IGN
Director Zach Cregger’s fresh take on *Resident Evil* has officially surpassed *Sonic the Hedgehog 3* to become the **highest-rated video game movie of all time**, earning a 91% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes and an 8/10 from IGN. Bypassing traditional game protagonists to follow an ordinary medical courier named Bryan during a chaotic Raccoon City outbreak, the film blends terrifying bioweapon survival horror with pitch-black comedy. It proves that capturing the thrilling, unpredictable spirit of playing a survival horror game beats doing a rigid line-by-line adaptation.

**[Level-5 Scrambles to Reupload Trailers Following Generative AI Backlash](https://www.ign.com/articles/professor-layton-developer-level-5-scrambles-to-reupload-trailers-as-ai-backlash-intensifies)** · IGN
Developer Level-5 issued formal apologies and announced clean-up edits for its Tokyo Game Show trailers after fans identified widespread generative AI artifacts in background artwork during its recent showcase. Studio CEO Akihiro Hino admitted to experimenting with AI tools to speed up workflow, but promised that released in-game content relies on human artistry. Gamers remain vocal about the incident, pushing back against the inclusion of AI slop in beloved franchises like *Professor Layton* and *Yokai Watch*.

**[Former PlayStation Execs Satirize Sony’s Digital Shift at TGS](https://www.ign.com/articles/former-playstation-execs-recreate-iconic-disc-sharing-xbox-diss-without-the-physical-games-this-time)** · IGN
PlayStation veterans Shuhei Yoshida and Adam Boyes recreated their iconic 2013 PS4 disc-sharing video at Tokyo Game Show, but posed with empty hands to mock Sony's upcoming phase-out of physical media. With Sony slated to end physical disc manufacturing for new titles by January 2028, fans highlighted the irony of PlayStation abandoning the very pro-consumer physical disc stance it once championed. The viral moment underscores growing community concern over game preservation as platforms push toward all-digital ecosystems.

**[GTA 5 Source Code Leak Details Scrapped Liberty City & Single-Player DLC](https://www.ign.com/articles/new-gta-5-leak-suggests-rockstar-had-ambitious-city-sized-dlc-plans-that-were-ultimately-scrapped)** · IGN
A massive 200GB leak of *Grand Theft Auto V* source code has exposed scrapped single-player DLC plans, including a full Liberty City expansion and a secret-agent story starring Trevor. Rockstar co-founder Dan Houser previously confirmed the Trevor DLC was roughly half-finished before being shelved to focus on *GTA Online* and *Red Dead Redemption 2*. The leak provides a fascinating look at what could have been before Rockstar shifted entirely toward live-service multiplayer updates.

**[Final Fantasy 7 Revelation Director Warns of 200GB Install Footprint](https://www.ign.com/articles/final-fantasy-7-revelation-director-warns-of-200gb-install-size-for-remake-trilogy-final-chapter)** · IGN

Hamaguchi noted that the massive footprint reflects over a decade of work aiming to deliver an uncompromising, expansive finale.

**[Activision Exposes \$8.5 Billion Annual Video Game Cheat Industry](https://www.ign.com/articles/call-of-duty-maker-activision-says-the-video-game-cheat-market-generates-85-billion-a-year)** · IGN
