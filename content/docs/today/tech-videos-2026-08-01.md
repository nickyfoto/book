# Tech Videos — 2026-08-01

## Watch First
**[Teaching AI to Find Real Vulnerabilities — Prof. David Brumley, Bugcrowd](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZFxh7sqbUZo)** is the standout presentation today for its rigorous, technically grounded breakdown of how to properly use Reinforcement Learning to train AI to find and exploit zero-day vulnerabilities in hardened targets like Chrome's V8 engine. It cuts through AI marketing noise to demonstrate actual capabilities and the mechanics of evaluating multi-step exploit generation.

## Highlights by Theme

### Developer Tools & Platforms
GitHub released a brief demo, **[How to use voice prompting in the GitHub Copilot app](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BOSujC3TrjU)**, showing how developers can use voice input to brain-dump messy thoughts directly into prompts. While just a short marketing clip, it highlights the industry's push toward lower-friction, multi-modal input for unblocking architecture planning rather than staring at a blank text box.

### AI & Machine Learning
In **[Teaching AI to Find Real Vulnerabilities — Prof. David Brumley, Bugcrowd](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZFxh7sqbUZo)**, Prof. David Brumley reveals that models like Mythos can achieve arbitrary code execution in Chrome's V8 engine 73% of the time, arguing that single-bug synthetic benchmarks actually train LLMs to "reward hack" rather than deeply explore targets. Similarly focused on evaluation flaws, Rayan Garg notes in **[Rethinking Environments for Long-Horizon Work — Rayan Garg, Theta Software](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2aS7aKoXn64)** that existing software benchmarks lack state-change complexity, advocating for LLM "judge models" with environment access to properly verify multi-tool trajectories. On a more theoretical note, Grant Sanderson briefly argues in **[The Problem With How LLMs Generate Text - Grant Sanderson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2j2KbctXMz8)** that the auto-regressive, next-token prediction paradigm inherently fails to incentivize the unlikely, creative connections that drive high-quality human reasoning.

### Hardware & Infrastructure
Marques Brownlee reviews the new foldable flagship in **[Galaxy Z Fold 8 Review: Honeymoon's Over](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z6z_feacXW8)**, praising its Snapdragon 8 Elite chip, 4800 mAh silicon-carbon battery, and thin titanium design. However, he notes the $1,900 device is still plagued by unplayable app aspect ratios and mediocre cameras that lack a telephoto lens. On the financial side of infrastructure, David Sacks argues in **[David Sacks: The Chip Stock Crash is Based on Momentum, NOT Fundamentals](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ucwxs7KLUI4)** that the recent 30-40% crash in AI memory chip stocks is simply a leveraged momentum trade unwinding, rather than a failure of AI capital expenditure to deliver long-term ROI.
