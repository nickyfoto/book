# CNBeta — 2026-05-12

## Tech & AI
The ongoing legal battle surrounding OpenAI has exposed massive internal friction, with **[OpenAI changing its corporate bylaws](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562182.htm)** to require a supermajority to fire CEO Sam Altman. This surfaces alongside **[Ilya Sutskever's testimony](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562184.htm)** that he had wanted to oust Altman for at least a year due to "dishonest" behavior. The trial also revealed **[Greg Brockman's private 2017 diaries](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562172.htm)**, which documented early struggles over profit motives and a desire to distance the company from Elon Musk. Furthermore, Microsoft's CEO Satya Nadella testified that his company targeted a staggering $92 billion return on its early OpenAI investment, according to **[this trial report](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562188.htm)**.

In the hardware space, **[Samsung is accelerating its dual-track packaging strategy](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562208.htm)** by constructing a new advanced HBM plant in Onyang to meet surging global AI demand. Simultaneously, **[TSMC and Applied Materials are investing $5 billion](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562238.htm)** to build an EPIC center in Silicon Valley, aiming to overcome 3D transistor bottlenecks for next-gen chips.

Elon Musk's own AI endeavor seems to be stalling, as **[Grok downloads plummeted](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562164.htm)** from 20 million in January to just 8.3 million in April. In a surprising pivot, SpaceX has reportedly begun renting out its data center computing power to rival Anthropic, a move analysts believe could generate billions in revenue.

In the cross-border e-commerce arena, the high-stakes **[Shein vs. Temu copyright lawsuit](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562248.htm)** has officially kicked off in London. Shein alleges industrial-scale copyright infringement, while Temu has countersued, claiming Shein uses anti-competitive exclusivity agreements to stifle the market.

## Consumer & Devices
Google announced a massive overhaul to its ecosystem, headlined by the launch of **[Googlebooks](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562254.htm)**, a new AI-native laptop line replacing Chromebooks that integrates Gemini directly into a "Magic Pointer" cursor. The tech giant is also rolling out **[Gemini Intelligence for Android](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562256.htm)**, introducing capabilities like multi-step task automation across apps. Part of this rollout includes the **[Create My Widget feature](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562252.htm)**, which generates personalized home screen panels using natural language prompts. To combat smartphone addiction, Android 17 is adding a **["Pause Point" tool](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562250.htm)** that forces a 10-second delay before opening distracting apps.

On the Apple front, the **[iPhone 18 Pro series will reportedly miss out on tandem OLED displays](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562244.htm)**, meaning significant peak brightness improvements will be delayed until at least 2028. However, Apple is expected to maintain an **[aggressive pricing strategy for the iPhone 18 Pro](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562246.htm)** to stay competitive despite rising memory costs. For the company's **[first foldable iPhone](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562186.htm)**, leaks suggest a low-key premium strategy offering only two color options to prioritize manufacturing yield. Looking further into the future, **[iOS 27 will bring deep customization](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562274.htm)** to the iPhone camera app, turning various controls into widgets for a professional-grade interface.

Over in hardware, Sony is reportedly considering **[scrapping plans for 32GB of RAM in the PlayStation 6](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1562232.htm)** to keep the console's launch price under control amid soaring component costs.

Nintendo president Shuntaro Furukawa reassured investors that **[more Switch 2 games are in the pipeline](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1562194.htm)**, urging patience following concerns over a lack of heavyweight first-party announcements. Meanwhile, hype continues to build for Rockstar's next epic as **[GTA 6 received a "Mature 17+" rating](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1562202.htm)** from the ESRB, alongside a **[UK retailer leak](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1562212.htm)** claiming pre-orders will open May 12 with a £69.99 price tag.
