# Gaming News — 2026-08-08

## Top Story
Following devastating Xbox layoffs that gutted **Doom** developer id Software, lead programmer Chris Hays openly blasted Microsoft, claiming the tech giant **[fundamentally doesn't understand art](https://www.ign.com/articles/they-fundamentally-dont-understand-art-doom-dev-blasts-xbox-after-microsoft-gutted-id-software-with-layoffs)** or games. Hays warned that cutting half the studio makes delivering another massive AAA title "impossible" and criticized how Xbox's Game Pass model hides real performance.

## News & Reviews

**[The Future of PlayStation Exclusives: Sony Double-Downs on Single-Player Hits](https://www.ign.com/articles/upcoming-ps5-games)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/upcoming-ps5-games)
Sony is officially returning to strict single-player console exclusivity, revealing a packed PS5 roadmap that includes **Marvel’s Wolverine** (September 15, 2026) and **God of War Laufey** (February 16, 2027). While timed exclusives like S-Game's **Phantom Blade Zero** will still hit PC initially, future major single-player titles will remain locked to the console. This shift marks the end of simultaneous PC ports for Sony's first-party narrative adventures, forcing PC players to wait for their favorite titles.

**[Asus ROG XReal R1 XR Glasses Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/asus-rog-xreal-r1-xr-glasses-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/asus-rog-xreal-r1-xr-glasses-review)
The new Asus ROG XReal R1 XR Glasses offer an impressive 1080p, 120Hz display with an awesome instant 3D mode when plugged directly into a handheld like the Asus ROG Xbox Ally X. However, at \$849, this pricey package is severely dragged down by its mandatory, glitchy desktop dock which suffers from broken visual profiles and a headache-inducing 240Hz mode. Unless you absolutely need to plug your PS5 or PC into your face, you are far better off saving your cash and buying the standard \$449 XReal 1S.

**[Freddy Fazbear's Pizza is Coming to Life in 2027](https://www.ign.com/articles/freddy-fazbears-pizza-opening-permanent-location-featuring-animatronic-stage-shows-in-2027)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/freddy-fazbears-pizza-opening-permanent-location-featuring-animatronic-stage-shows-in-2027)
Five Nights at Freddy's creator Scott Cawthon has partnered with the American Dream entertainment center in New Jersey to open a permanent, real-life Freddy Fazbear's Pizza restaurant in 2027. This immersive venue will feature arcade games, a real-life functional restaurant, animatronic stage shows with the Fazbear Band, and multiplayer survival-horror experiences. Fans of the iconic horror franchise can finally eat a slice of real-life Freddy's pizza, but they should probably watch their backs for roaming animatronics.

**[Mortal Kombat 3 Remains in Limbo as Director Doubts Return](https://www.ign.com/articles/mortal-kombat-director-not-sure-about-return-for-third-movie-as-mortal-kombat-3-remains-in-limbo)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/mortal-kombat-director-not-sure-about-return-for-third-movie-as-mortal-kombat-3-remains-in-limbo)
The future of a third *Mortal Kombat* movie is highly uncertain after the second film underperformed at the box office, grossing just \$129 million globally against an \$80 million budget. Director Simon McQuoid has cast doubt on his own return, stating he is unsure about the film's status and is shifting his focus to other character-focused projects. While a script was reportedly in development back in 2025, the franchise is currently frozen on the battlefield.
