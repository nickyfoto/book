## Watch First
[What Satellite Images Reveal About Iran’s Attacks on U.S. Bases](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JRnoi3repjo) from *The New York Times* is easily the most compelling piece today, utilizing European satellite data to bypass US restrictions and verify the true extent of Iranian drone strikes on American military outposts. It's a sobering, strictly analytical look at a rapidly evolving vulnerability that challenges official defensive narratives.
