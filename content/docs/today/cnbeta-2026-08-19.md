# CNBeta — August 19, 2026

## Top Story
Hangzhou-based robotic pioneer Unitree Robotics (宇树科技) made history today by debuting on the Shanghai Stock Exchange's Star Market (科创板) as the "first A-share humanoid robot stock." According to **[a cnbeta report on Unitree's blockbuster listing](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573884.htm)**, the startup opened at 1,100 RMB per share—representing an astronomical 629.44% surge from its initial public offering price of 150.80 RMB—pushing its valuation near \$50 billion. This highly anticipated IPO, backed by a premier strategic lineup including Sequoia China's Neil Shen, Tencent, and Alibaba, marks a massive commercial milestone for the global embodied AI sector as Unitree stands out as one of the very few universally profitable robotics companies in the world.

## Tech & AI
AI model efficiency and hardware supply chains are undergoing major shifts. China's leading AI developer, Zhipu AI, officially launched its **[next-generation base model GLM-5.3 API](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573890.htm)**, scoring 60 points on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index to rival global closed-source leaders like Claude Fable 5 and matching Kimi K3 as the top open-source model. Meanwhile, China's premier 3D NAND flash player, Yangtze Memory Technologies Corp (YMTC), completed its **[IPO tutoring phase](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573886.htm)**, signaling its imminent public market debut as the company commands a historic 14% global market share. On the global front, Stripe has agreed to a **[blockbuster acquisition of AI aggregator OpenRouter](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574030.htm)** for a rumored \$7 billion. Simultaneously, rising demands in AI memory and hardware are causing massive supply chain price adjustments: Morgan Stanley warns that mature DRAM shortages could trigger a **[50% surge in DDR4 prices](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573860.htm)**, while Samsung has raised its **[advanced foundry quotes by up to 15%](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573964.htm)**. This price hike heavily impacts Chinese clients who face equipment restrictions under US export curbs.

## Consumer & Devices
Hardware innovation continues to balance high-end specs with physical simplicity and repairability. The **[Minimal Phone 2 launched on Kickstarter](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573834.htm)**, offering a sleek, metal-bodied, BlackBerry-like physical QWERTY keyboard combined with a 4-inch 90Hz AMOLED screen and a distraction-free Minimal OS designed to combat digital addiction. Adhering to the right-to-repair movement, modular smartphone **[Fairphone 6+ officially landed in the US](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573844.htm)** with a perfect 10/10 repairability score, a Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chip, and software support running until 2033. In the premium EV sector, Lucid took center stage at Monterey Car Week by introducing its **[1,070-horsepower Gravity GT-S SUV](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573900.htm)**, aiming directly at the luxury electric seven-seater market with active three-chamber air suspension and an impressive 373-mile range. However, some future device timelines have shifted: Bloomberg reports that despite leaked demo videos in macOS Tahoe, **[Apple's camera-equipped AirPods Pro won't debut until 2027](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574028.htm)**, as development teams resolve supply chain and software bottlenecks. On the corporate side, Nokia is reportedly **[cutting staff and shrinking its mobile network business in China](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573906.htm)** to pivot toward cloud and AI data center opportunities. This retreat comes as domestic players advance rapidly, though even local giants face growing pains—joint venture luxury brand Yijing (东风-华为) had its **[X9 booking app crash on its pre-order night](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573912.htm)** due to server overload.

## Gaming
The gaming community is caught between pre-release leaks and debates over digital ownership. Anti-digital-distribution hacker group "Cyberleek" retaliated against Take-Two's digital-only pricing policies by leaking **[confidential gameplay and map footage of GTA 6](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573846.htm)**. Cyberleek's **[provocative hacker manifesto](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573952.htm)** demands Rockstar apologize and allow players to cancel pre-orders, arguing that digital pre-orders exploit consumers. Despite this controversy, the blockbuster title has surpassed 4.3 million pre-orders. Former PlayStation executive Shuhei Yoshida defended the trend, noting that **[he has long abandoned physical discs](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573896.htm)** and pointing out that even physical buyers must download massive patches. Meanwhile, nostalgic players have something to watch as Nintendo **[registered a new trademark for Dr. Mario World](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573950.htm)**, sparking hopes of a future Switch port.

## Science & Space
China's space and physics sectors hit multiple monumental milestones. LandSpace's **[Zhuque-3 reusable rocket successfully completed its land recovery](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1573864.htm)**, making it the nation's first stainless steel, liquid oxygen-methane orbital launcher to achieve a successful land landing using advanced 7-meter landing legs. Backed by data from the failed Yao-1 test, this successful land recovery marks a giant leap for China's commercial satellite launches. On the lunar exploration front, the **[Chang'e-7 spacecraft completed its vertical transfer](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1573910.htm)** to the launch pad at Wenchang, preparing for an ambitious mission to probe permanently shadowed craters at the lunar South Pole with a specialized flying hopper. Groundbreaking physics research was also unveiled, as a Chinese research team from USTC **[extended quantum memory entanglement to 420 kilometers](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1573968.htm)**, a record-breaking distance that breaks theoretical communication limits and lays a firm foundation for the future quantum internet.

# CNBeta 每日精选 — 2026年8月19日

## 焦点新闻
被称为“A股人形机器人第一股”的杭州宇树科技（Unitree Robotics）正式在科创板挂牌上市，在业内引发强烈关注。根据 **[CNBeta 关于宇树科技震撼上市的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573884.htm)**，该公司首日开盘价飙升至每股 1,100 元人民币，较 150.80 元的发行价暴涨 629.44%，市值接近 500 亿美元。此次备受瞩目的 IPO 吸引了包括红杉中国沈南鹏、腾讯和阿里巴巴等明星投资阵容，由于宇树科技是全球极少数实现规模收入与实质盈利的通用机器人企业，其上市标志着中国具身智能商业化进程迈入了资本市场的新纪元。

## 技术与人工智能
AI 模型的能效比和存储硬件供应链正迎来结构性大调整。中国头部 AI 独角兽智谱 AI 正式发布了其 **[新一代基座模型 GLM-5.3 API](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573890.htm)**，该模型在 Artificial Analysis 智能指数中取得 60 分，成功跻身全球前沿模型能力区间，并与 Kimi K3 并列开源模型第一。与此同时，国内 3D NAND 闪存龙头长江存储已完成了其 **[IPO 辅导验收阶段](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573886.htm)**，为其登陆公开市场做好了准备，目前其在 NAND 闪存领域已占有 14% 的全球份额，位列全球第三。在国际市场上，支付巨头 Stripe 同意以传闻超过 70 亿美元的估值 **[重金收购 AI 模型聚合平台 OpenRouter](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574030.htm)**。然而，AI 的爆发也对成熟半导体供应造成了挤压：摩根士丹利预警 HBM 产能转移可能导致 Q3 传统 **[DDR4 内存价格暴涨 50%](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573860.htm)**，而三星也因 AI 先进制程产能吃紧，将部分 **[晶圆代工报价上调高达 15%](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573964.htm)**，这进一步加重了面临设备出口限制的中国客户的代工成本。

## 消费电子与设备
硬件创新领域在极致性能与极简防沉迷、可修复性之间寻找新的平衡点。配备实体键盘的墨水屏手机进化版 **[Minimal Phone 2 登陆 Kickstarter 开启众筹](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573834.htm)**，金属机身和全尺寸背光 QWERTY 物理键盘搭配 4 英寸 90Hz AMOLED 屏，凭借专门屏蔽数字干扰的 Minimal OS，为戒断网瘾的用户提供高级硬件体验。坚持环保与可修复理念的模块化手机 **[Fairphone 6+ 正式进入美国市场](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573844.htm)**，该机荣获 iFixit 10/10 满分评测，搭载骁龙 7s Gen 4 并承诺提供长达 2033 年的软件更新。高端纯电 SUV 领域，Lucid 在蒙特雷汽车周发布了其 **[1,070 马力的旗舰 Gravity GT-S](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573900.htm)**，标配三腔自适应空气悬架与后轮转向，自动降低车身并具备 373 英里的高续航，剑指高收入多人口家庭市场。不过，备受关注的苹果首款带摄像头 AirPods 遭遇跳票，彭博社记者古尔曼透露，由于供应链与 AI 软件挑战，**[带摄像头的 AirPods Pro 将推迟至 2027 年发布](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574028.htm)**。此外，跨国巨国诺基亚正逐步 **[裁员并退出中国移动网络基础设施业务](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573906.htm)**，全力转型云计算与 AI 数据中心。而本土造车新势力也在狂飙中面临挑战，东风与华为合作的中高端品牌奕境首款车型 **[奕境 X9 预售当晚 App 遭遇崩溃无法下单](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573912.htm)**，引发总经理曾清林自嘲“草台班子”。

## 游戏娱乐
游戏界正处于预购热潮与反预售抗议的冰火交融中。黑客组织“Cyberleek”因抗议 Take-Two 转向纯数字版发行和高昂预购政策，通过网络泄露了 **[《GTA 6》的两段实机画面与包含五个县的 Leonida 地图](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573846.htm)**。该组织还发表了 **[言辞激烈的黑客宣言](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573952.htm)**，要求 R 星公开道歉，认为消费者不应在游戏发售前为数字商品付款。尽管泄密闹得沸沸扬扬，《GTA 6》预购量依然突破了 438 万份。前 PlayStation 高管吉田修平也在采访中力挺数字化，坦言 **[自己早已抛弃实体盘](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573896.htm)**，并指出即使买实体也需要下载大量补丁。此外，经典消除游戏的粉丝迎来好消息，任天堂为已关服的经典手游 **[《马里奥医生：世界》注册了新商标](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573950.htm)**，暗示该作有望移植至 Switch 平台。

## 科学与太空
中国在空天探索和量子物理前沿均取得了历史性飞跃。民营航天龙头蓝箭航天研制的 **[朱雀三号遥二火箭成功入轨并实现陆地回收](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1573864.htm)**，成为我国首款不锈钢、液氧甲烷推进的可重复使用入轨级火箭，采用 7 米多高的着陆主腿扛住了百吨冲击并支持复用 20 次。研发团队通过朱雀三号遥一失利数据进行了改进，标志着中国航天陆地回收技术实现关键跨越。在深空探测领域，**[嫦娥七号探测器与长征五号火箭已垂直转运至发射区](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1573910.htm)**，即将对月球南极进行综合勘察，其搭载的飞跃器将深入月面永久阴影坑寻找水冰证据。在量子通信前沿，中国科学技术大学团队成功将 **[量子存储器间的纠缠距离延长至 420 公里](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1573968.htm)**，打破了远距离直接纠缠分发的物理极限（PLOB 界限），让城际尺度量子网络建设成为可能。
