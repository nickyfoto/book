## Articles Worth Reading

**iOS 27 adds new Phone app feature that’s an instant favorite** ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/24/ios-27-adds-new-phone-app-feature-thats-an-instant-favorite/))
iOS 27 introduces "Call Context," an Apple Intelligence feature that automatically surfaces relevant information when you call a business. For example, calling an airline will automatically display your flight confirmation number from Mail directly on the call screen, and Siri can even read it out loud for you. This quality-of-life improvement eliminates the need to frantically search for details while on the phone, though it requires a compatible Apple Intelligence device.

**watchOS 27 adds new feature to improve your Apple Watch battery life** ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/24/watchos-27-adds-new-feature-to-improve-your-apple-watch-battery-life/))
The upcoming watchOS 27 update introduces "proactive battery extension notifications" to help Apple Watch wearers effortlessly maximize their device's battery life. The operating system will monitor features that run in the background, like Raise to Speak or Start Workout reminders, and detect if you never actually use them. If so, watchOS will automatically disable these power-draining settings, notifying you of the change while offering a simple prompt to re-enable them if desired.

**Anthropic’s New Claude Opus 5 Nearly Matches Flagship Fable 5 at Half the Cost** ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/24/anthropic-opus-5/))
Anthropic has upgraded its popular AI chatbot with the release of Claude Opus 5, which offers near-flagship intelligence for the same price as the outgoing Opus 4.8 model. The new model excels in agentic coding, multidisciplinary reasoning, and processing visual outputs. Developers can also now change the tools Claude has access to mid-conversation without invalidating the prompt cache, making the AI assistant an even more capable tool for complex daily workflows.
