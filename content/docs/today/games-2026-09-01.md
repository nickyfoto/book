# Gaming News — 2026-09-01

## Top Story
**Grand Theft Auto 6** will feature a dynamic **"criminal profile" morality meter** that can actually **shatter** and brand your character as **permanently irredeemable** for an entire playthrough if you unleash too much unchecked chaos. This groundbreaking feature from Rockstar Games offers **separate tracking** for both Jason and Lucia, promising unprecedented gameplay depth when the crime epic launches on **November 19, 2026**.

## News & Reviews

**[Bit Reactor Quietly Furloughed Staff Ahead of Hit Star Wars Release](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-studio-behind-hit-new-star-wars-game-zero-company-quietly-furloughed-most-staff-just-before-launch)** · IGN
Bit Reactor, the studio behind the hit tactical game *Star Wars Zero Company*, **furloughed approximately 80% of its staff** weeks before its highly successful launch on Steam. While the game received a **stellar 9/10 score** from IGN, the studio's CEO was reportedly at Gamescom discussing their next title while employees were left without pay due to funding issues. This disappointing development highlights the **ongoing instability of game development** even amidst massive critical and commercial successes.

**[Sony Faces Backlash and Deploys Harassment Policy Over Disc Cuts](https://www.ign.com/articles/sony-rolls-out-new-customer-harassment-policy-after-physical-disc-controversy-sparks-backlash)** · IGN
Sony Japan has published a strict new customer harassment policy to **protect its workforce from abusive behavior and extreme demands** from disgruntled players. The toxic backlash stems from Sony's plans to **end physical PlayStation disc production by January 2028**, forcing an all-digital future that has already triggered antitrust and monopoly lawsuits in several countries. Gamers are fiercely protesting, as physical ownership remains a core value for collectors and bargain hunters alike.

**[No Rest for the Wicked Version 1.0 Delayed to 2027](https://www.ign.com/articles/no-rest-for-the-wicked-10-launch-delayed-to-march-2027)** · IGN
Moon Studios has announced a delay for the long-awaited 1.0 launch of its action-RPG *No Rest for the Wicked*, now targeting **March 2027**. Game director Thomas Mahler apologized for the delay, explaining that the studio needs extra time to iron out performance issues, improve the class system, and squash bugs. PC early access started back in 2024, but console players will have to wait even longer for official release dates.

**[Orbitals Review: A Gorgeous but Predictable Co-op Journey](https://www.ign.com/articles/orbitals-review)** · IGN
IGN's review of the co-op puzzle-adventure *Orbitals* highlights its **striking, nostalgic 90s-anime visual style** and fantastic eclectic soundtrack. However, the actual puzzling mechanics rely on predictable tropes that fail to evolve, causing the game to **stumble across the finish line** during its final hours. It is still an endearing **10-hour romp** for two players, but it doesn't quite fulfill its philosophical narrative potential.

**[Hideki Kamiya Claims Capcom Developers Are 'Numb' to Horror](https://www.ign.com/articles/resident-evil-2-director-hideki-kamiya-says-capcom-has-grown-numb-to-how-scary-new-re-games-are)** · IGN
Original *Resident Evil 2* director Hideki Kamiya revealed he was entirely serious when begging Capcom to introduce a **"non-scary" mode** for *Resident Evil Requiem*. Kamiya claims the developers have become **numb to how terrifying their photorealistic games are**, preventing horror-averse fans from enjoying the puzzles and action. He proposed a mode replacing scary zombie faces with paper bags and blood splatters with cherry blossoms, but Capcom seems unlikely to budge.

**[Xbox Game Pass September Wave 1 Features Black Ops Cold War](https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-game-pass-september-2026-wave-1-lineup-announced)** · IGN
Microsoft's Wave 1 lineup for September 2026 has been revealed, bringing games like *Call of Duty: Black Ops Cold War* and *RuneScape: Dragonwilds* straight to the subscription service. Indie titles like *Shelldiver* and the competitive *Speedrunners 2* are also joining the lineup to provide plenty of variety. Unfortunately, it's not all good news, as heavy hitters such as **Cyberpunk 2077 and Frostpunk 2 are scheduled to depart on September 15**.

**[Nvidia Launches DLSS 5 Neural Rendering in NBA 2K27](https://www.ign.com/articles/nvidias-dlss-5-neural-rendering-tech-officially-launches-on-september-3-hits-performance-by-around-5060)** · IGN
Nvidia's controversial DLSS 5 neural rendering technology is officially launching on September 3, making its debut alongside *NBA 2K27*. The tech generates high-fidelity AI drawings on top of rendered frames to enhance photorealism, but it **demands an RTX 5000 series GPU** and triggers a whopping **50-60% performance hit**. While early tests show improved visual quality, gamers will need to rely heavily on frame generation and super-resolution to keep their frame rates stable.
