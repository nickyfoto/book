# CNBC — 2026-08-31

## Lead Story
Renewed military hostilities in the Persian Gulf after U.S. forces struck Iranian rocket launchers on Larak Island sent crude prices surging and pushed benchmark borrowing costs higher, dampening broader market sentiment on the final trading day of August. The geopolitical shock catalyzed a late-day selloff that dragged the **Dow Jones Industrial Average down more than 370 points**, compounding investor anxiety over persistent inflation and hawkish Federal Reserve commentary.

## Markets & Economics
Wall Street wrapped up the month on a defensive footing as the **Dow shed over 370 points**, though major averages still logged solid monthly advances with the **Dow climbing 1.3%**, the **S&P 500 rising 2.6%**, and the **Nasdaq Composite advancing 3.9%**. Energy markets reacted swiftly to the Middle East escalation, with West Texas Intermediate crude topping \$85 per barrel and driving fixed-income yields upward as Diana Olick highlighted that the **[average 30-year fixed mortgage rate surged to 6.87%](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/31/30-year-mortgage-rates-surge-to-nearly-7-percent.html)**, marking its highest reading since June 2025. At the G20 finance summit in Asheville, **[Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent defended U.S. debt management](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/31/treasury-secretary-bessent-u-s-bond-market-is-outperforming-other-global-bond-markets.html)**, brushing off billionaire Stanley Druckenmiller’s critique of the administration’s \$4 billion-plus long-term bond buybacks. Concurrently, fed funds futures repriced to a **66.1% probability of a September rate hike** following Kevin Warsh’s Jackson Hole speech, sparking immediate debate between **[SMBC's Joe LaVorgna](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/31/smbcs-joe-lavorgna-i-believe-the-fed-will-and-needs-to-raise-rates-in-september.html)**, who called a rate hike necessary to curb inflation, and **[Fundstrat's Tom Lee](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/31/the-markets-could-rally-very-strongly-if-the-fed-holds-rates-in-september-fundstrats-tom-lee.html)**, who projected stocks could rally sharply if the central bank holds policy steady.

## Business & Earnings
In mega-cap dealmaking, **[Aon CEO Greg Case detailed a \$17 billion agreement to acquire rival USI Insurance Services](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/31/aon-ceo-greg-case-17b-usi-deal-establishes-the-premier-u-s-middle-market-platform.html)** from KKR, financing the acquisition with new debt to capture 200,000 middle-market U.S. business clients. Big Tech marked the closing of a defining chapter as Tim Cook finished his final day after 15 years as Apple CEO, with **[CNBC reviewing Cook's run of more than 2,000% stock appreciation](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/31/end-of-an-era-at-apple-tim-cooks-last-day-as-ceo.html)** ahead of hardware chief John Ternus stepping in on Tuesday. In enterprise tech monetization, **[OpenAI announced its advertising division reached a \$1 billion annualized revenue run rate](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/31/openais-ad-business-hits-1-billion-run-rate.html)** in just 200 days, highlighting revenue diversification ahead of a planned IPO at an \$852 billion valuation. Healthcare M&A remained aggressive as Eli Lilly deployed its GLP-1 cash reserves to purchase privately held **Merida Biosciences for up to \$2.88 billion in cash**, expanding its immunology pipeline into antibody-targeting autoimmune treatments.

## Investing & Commentary
Citadel Securities derivatives strategist Scott Rubner warned that equity risk-reward is deteriorating as the market enters historically treacherous September seasonality, citing cheap single-stock and index volatility alongside corporate buyback blackouts that accelerate around September 12. CNBC’s Mike Santoli highlighted that key indicators like the 10-year Treasury yield above 4.7% and the VIX below 15 are coiling at consequential levels, noting that while Nvidia trades at an undemanding multiple below 20 times forward earnings, broader indices must navigate decelerating profit growth into 2027. Outside tech, Baird issued a high-conviction upgrade on **Deere to outperform with an \$800 price target** (27% upside), arguing that rallies in corn and soybean futures will lift farmer margins above breakeven and trigger an inflection in agricultural equipment demand.
