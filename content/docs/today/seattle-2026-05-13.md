## Local News

**[King County Monitors Potential Hantavirus Exposures](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/king-county-andes-hantavirus)** · FOX 13
Three King County residents are being monitored for symptoms after potential exposure to the Andes hantavirus. The exposures are linked to the recent MV Hondius cruise ship outbreak; two of the residents were exposed on a flight from South Africa while seated near an infected passenger. Health officials emphasize that there are currently no cases in King County and the risk to the general public remains low.
