## News & Reviews

**[Zach Cregger's Resident Evil Reboot Ditches the 'Badass' Tropes]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/resident-evil-2026-teaser-trailer-zombie-movie-zach-cregger)
Director Zach Cregger's upcoming *Resident Evil* movie adaptation will focus on a "hapless everyman" navigating the dreadful streets of Raccoon City, running concurrently with the events of the second video game. While some fans are worried about the lack of traditional lore in the newly released trailer, Cregger insists the film focuses heavily on survival-horror resource management and unique "weird creature stuff" rather than relying purely on classic zombies.

**[Assassin’s Creed Black Flag Resynced Won't Be An RPG]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/assassins-creed-black-flag-resynced-will-see-return-of-whaling-legendary-ships)
Ubisoft is aggressively reassuring fans that the upcoming *Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced* remains a true action-adventure game with no levels or gear scores. The remake brings back classic fan-favorite elements like whaling, Legendary Ships, and Kenway's Fleet, while introducing modern quality-of-life updates like a customizable HUD and an autopilot travel mode for the Jackdaw.

**[Final Fantasy XIV Hits Switch 2, Prepares to Ditch PS4]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/ffxiv-final-fantasy-xiv-director-confirms-switch-2-performance-and-mouse-keyboard-controls)
*Final Fantasy XIV* is officially coming to the Nintendo Switch 2, with Director Naoki Yoshida confirming the handheld version will target a stable 30fps and offer full mouse and keyboard support. To make room for the MMO's massive growing footprint, Square Enix will eventually sunset the aging PS4 version, urging players to upgrade before support officially phases out around Patch 8.3.

**[Hollywood Goes to War Over Battlefield]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/battlefield-movie-bidding-war-netflix-sony-amazon-mgm-universal)
A massive bidding war has erupted between major studios like Netflix, Sony, Warner Bros., and Amazon MGM over the rights to an upcoming *Battlefield* movie adaptation. With *Mission: Impossible* director Christopher McQuarrie attached to helm and Michael B. Jordan producing, the fight for EA's blockbuster shooter could easily birth the next great action film franchise.

**[Invincible VS Review]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/invincible-vs-review)
Quarter Up’s new 3v3 tag fighter *Invincible VS* delivers a fast-paced, *Killer Instinct*-inspired combat system that perfectly captures the raw, brutal power of the comic's characters. Unfortunately, the game is heavily dragged down by obnoxious, uncapped combo-breaking mechanics and an engaging but frustratingly brief story mode that ends abruptly after just an hour.
