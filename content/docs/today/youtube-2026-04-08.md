## Watch First
[The Economic Price of War](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fOASsXuhnAE) is an indispensable listen right now: Hoover Institution economists have mapped 150 years of data to show exactly how armed conflict destroys wealth, dropping GDP by up to 40% in extreme cases and spiking inflation by an average of 20%.

### Tech & AI
[Why AI Chips Made In The U.S. Are Being Sent To Taiwan — Creating A Major Bottleneck](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a0uQ1ZxfxfQ) is a fantastic deep dive from CNBC exposing the hidden choke point of the AI hardware boom: "advanced packaging".
