## News & Reviews

**[James Bond Game 007 First Light Delayed Until 2027 on Nintendo Switch 2](https://www.ign.com/articles/james-bond-game-007-first-light-delayed-until-2027-on-nintendo-switch-2)** · IGN
IO Interactive has announced a major delay for the Nintendo Switch 2 edition of **007 First Light**, pushing its release back to March 2027 to polish hardware performance. The setback comes despite the stealth action title shifting four million copies across other platforms in three months. To keep players engaged elsewhere, IO Interactive launched a PC update featuring path tracing and DLSS 4.5 alongside details on upcoming story DLC starring Lenny Kravitz.

**[Wardogs Sells 2 Million Copies in 5 Days on Steam](https://www.ign.com/articles/wardogs-sells-2-million-copies-in-5-days-as-early-access-shooter-blows-up-on-steam)** · IGN
Bulkhead's tactical early-access shooter **Wardogs** has exploded onto the PC gaming scene, surpassing two million units sold and peaking at 428,666 concurrent players within five days of launch. Despite minor day-one server hiccups, user sentiment has surged to "Very Positive" as squads embrace its intense firefights. The developer expects to spend 1–2 years in Early Access, outlining plans for fighter jets, expanded progression metas, and eventual console releases.

**[Masahiro Sakurai Forced to Abandon Game Designers Award After 16 Years](https://www.ign.com/articles/masahiro-sakurai-forced-to-abandon-game-designers-award-after-16-years-as-he-cant-find-a-successor)** · IGN
*Super Smash Bros.* creator Masahiro Sakurai confirmed that the Japan Game Awards' prestigious **Game Designers Award** will end after 16 years because he failed to find a suitable director to take over as lead judge. The final award went to Team9's localized text puzzle game **Word Game**. Delivering his closing statement, Sakurai warned developers and players that the broader industry risks dying if it stops championing creative experimentation over safe commercialism.

**[Warlock: Dungeons & Dragons Ditches Quest Waypoints](https://www.ign.com/articles/warlock-dungeons-dragons-doesnt-have-waypoints-because-the-devs-want-players-to-figure-things-out-for-themselves)** · IGN
Developer Invoke Studios confirmed that its single-player open-world RPG **Warlock: Dungeons & Dragons** will strictly omit traditional quest markers and navigation waypoints. General Manager Dominic Guay explained that removing hand-holding guidance forces players to engage organically with the world, uncovering questlines through an interconnected "Mystery" system driven by dialogue and investigation. The design philosophy aims to restore genuine player agency rather than leading players on a leash.

**[The Creep Tapes Season 3 Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-creep-tapes-season-3-review)** · IGN
Patrick Brice and Mark Duplass' horror series **The Creep Tapes** returns for a stellar third season on Shudder and AMC+, proving the found-footage subgenre still has plenty of terrifying spark left. IGN praised the six-episode run for its inventive storytelling and commitment to pushing serial killer Peachfuzz's lore into fresh, unpredictable places. Standout guest performances from Kate Siegel and Rekha Shankar anchor an exceptionally crafted season that horror fans shouldn't miss.

**[Golden Axe Animated Series Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/golden-axe-animated-series-review)** · IGN
Paramount+'s new 10-episode **Golden Axe** animated adaptation transforms SEGA's 1989 arcade fantasy into a wacky, self-aware comedy packed with rapid-fire humor and visceral gore. Featuring a standout voice performance from Matthew Rhys as the stubborn battle dwarf Gilius Thunderhead, the show lovingly deconstructs retro gaming tropes while preserving affection for its source material. Despite occasional early pacing bumps, the series settles into a hilarious groove that rewards both long-time arcade veterans and newcomers.
