# Bloomberg — 2026-08-24

## Lead Story
The collapse of high-stakes US-Canada trade negotiations, triggered by last-minute pressure from US Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, has ignited a full-scale trade war, whipsawing equities and sending the Canadian dollar to its worst decline in over two months. President Donald Trump implemented a 50% tariff on hundreds of Canadian imports and threatened to double duties on automobiles starting next year, forcing Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney to recall trade negotiators, commit over C\$11 billion to icebreaker shipbuilding, and prepare \$20 billion in retaliatory measures while warning that Washington is actively trying to "destroy Canadian firms". This sudden breakdown shatters hopes of a near-term resolution, leaving businesses and investors bracing for a protracted standoff that is expected to drag past the 2026 US midterm elections.
