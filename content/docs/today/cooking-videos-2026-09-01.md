### Food Science & Tips
Today's videos are packed with brilliant food science insights that will instantly upgrade your cooking. In Master Chen’s meatball tutorial, he explains that **manually cutting pork belly into soybean-sized cubes—rather than grinding it—is critical** to retaining a moist, bouncy mouthfeel. He also reveals a classic bakers' secret applied to meat: **mixing in a small amount of crumbled steamed bun (馒头) creates tiny air pockets** that keep the meatballs soft and tender, though adding too much will ruin the structural bite. Perhaps most importantly, he teaches the concept of **"secondary seasoning" (二次调口)**: high-protein meats require a heavier salt concentration during the initial mixing phase to release their natural meaty aromas, followed by a final addition of light soy sauce and chicken powder right as the braising liquid reduces to make the flavors explode. On the condiment front, chef James from **詹姆士官方專屬頻道** shares an essential kitchen tip: **always check a commercial chili sauce’s salinity before using it in a recipe**. If the sauce is highly salted, you must reduce the salt in your main dish (like fried rice) to avoid over-seasoning.
