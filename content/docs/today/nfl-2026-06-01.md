### Free Agency & Trades
June 1st just blew the doors off the NFL offseason with two franchise-altering blockbusters. In LA, Les Snead's infamous "F them picks" strategy is back with a vengeance, as the Rams acquired Myles Garrett from the Browns for Jared Verse and a 2027 first-round pick. As Mike Florio outlined on NBC's [Myles Garrett reportedly traded to LA Rams in blockbuster deal | Pro Football Talk | NFL on NBC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1JwY1QiabUc), Cleveland tweaked Garrett's contract back in March to delay a $29.2 million bonus, laying the breadcrumbs for this exact post-June 1 salary cap maneuver. Meanwhile, the Eagles have shipped star wideout A.J. Brown to the Patriots, completely revamping New England's receiver room to properly support young QB Drake Maye. NBC's [Why AJ Brown could be 'game-changing' for New England Patriots | Pro Football Talk | NFL on NBC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mnfRY4kCHV4) argues that Brown's arrival immediately shifts the coverage dictates for offensive coordinator Josh McDaniels, finally giving New England a legitimate WR1 capable of beating single-man coverage and opening up the rest of the field.
