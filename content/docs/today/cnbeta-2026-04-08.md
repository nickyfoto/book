# CNBeta — 2026-04-09

## Top Story
Intel has officially joined Elon Musk's ambitious TERAFAB project, a 2nm semiconductor fabrication initiative aiming to produce 100 to 200 billion chips annually, with 80% of its massive 1-terawatt AI compute capacity destined for space orbit. According to **[a cnbeta report on the partnership](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556986.htm)**, Intel will provide its advanced design, manufacturing, and packaging expertise to support SpaceX, Tesla, and xAI, marking a paradigm shift in global silicon manufacturing to bypass terrestrial power grid limitations.

## Tech & AI
AI token economics are forcing major shifts across the industry, as **[DeepSeek quietly introduced "Fast" and "Expert" modes](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556942.htm)** to optimize its computing resources by routing complex tasks to a stronger model while keeping everyday chats low-latency. This cost-control pivot reflects a broader industry trend where scaling constraints are paramount, further underscored by **[Anthropic restricting API tools like OpenClaw due to massive compute drains](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557044.htm)**. On the capabilities front, **[Meta unveiled "Muse Spark"](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557086.htm)**, a fast, lightweight foundation model with a "Contemplating mode" designed to replace Llama across WhatsApp and Instagram. Meanwhile, **[Anthropic's newly revealed "Claude Mythos Preview"](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556960.htm)** is demonstrating terrifying cybersecurity prowess, uncovering 27-year-old zero-day vulnerabilities in OpenBSD, prompting the company to restrict its use to trusted partners like AWS and CrowdStrike for defensive patching. Also in the AI space, **[Google is pushing Gemini integration in Gmail](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557002.htm)** with promises of isolated data processing, even as a separate evaluation claims **[Google's AI Overviews generate millions of inaccurate answers daily](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557004.htm)**, with hallucination rates worsening in the Gemini 3 upgrade. In ongoing legal drama, **[Elon Musk escalated his lawsuit against OpenAI](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556948.htm)**, seeking to oust CEO Sam Altman and claim $150 billion in damages, while OpenAI accused Musk of colluding with Mark Zuckerberg to acquire OpenAI's IP.

## Consumer & Devices
In hardware news, unexpected demand for the aggressively priced **[Apple MacBook Neo is causing severe supply shortages for the A18 Pro chip](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556978.htm)**, potentially forcing Apple to pay TSMC higher costs to restart production lines. Apple is also reportedly locking down its foldable iPhone supply chain, **[agreeing to exclusively use Samsung's foldable OLED panels](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557054.htm)** equipped with Color-filter-on-Encapsulation (CoE) technology to minimize creasing. For future releases, a new leak points to **[Apple launching the iPhone Air 2 and iPhone 18e in Spring 2027](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557094.htm)**. In the PC market, a new wave of **[Snapdragon X2 Elite laptops from Asus and HP have hit the market](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556980.htm)**, boasting 3nm Oryon architecture and 80 TOPS of NPU performance, albeit at premium price points starting around $1,200. Finally, the end of an era is approaching for early e-readers, as **[Amazon announced it will cut off Kindle Store access for 2012 and older Kindle models](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557104.htm)** by May 20, offering users a 20% discount on upgrades.

## Gaming
Valve appears to be integrating AI into its platform, with **[dataminers uncovering "SteamGPT" in recent client updates](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1557048.htm)**, a tool designed to handle customer service tickets and potentially link with Counter-Strike 2's anti-cheat system to evaluate player trust scores. Meanwhile, Nvidia's upcoming upscaling tech is facing severe backlash; **[71% of surveyed gamers say they will boycott DLSS 5](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1556974.htm)** out of moral objection or fear of homogeneous "AI faces," despite Nvidia's promises of preserving artistic intent. In esports history, the South Korean government announced it will **[issue an official commemorative stamp featuring League of Legends legend Faker](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1556966.htm)** starting in October 2026, marking the first time an esports player has received this national honor. Racing fans also got a major treat with a **[six-minute prologue gameplay reveal of Forza Horizon 6](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1557084.htm)**, showcasing Japan's mountain roads and high-speed train races ahead of its May 19 release.

## Science & Space
Space exploration hit an awkward snag as **[the $23 million toilet system on NASA's Artemis 2 Orion spacecraft broke down](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1556990.htm)** just after setting a new human distance record of 406,000 kilometers from Earth, forcing the four astronauts to use emergency capillary-action collection bags. Despite the plumbing issues, the crew captured stunning imagery, and **[NASA released rare photos of a lunar eclipse of the sun taken from the far side of the moon](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1557040.htm)** as the spacecraft prepares for its April 10 splashdown. Back on Earth, China's pharmaceutical industry has achieved a breakthrough in personalized medicine, **[initiating mass production of 3D-printed pills using Melt Extrusion Deposition (MED) technology](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1557042.htm)** to create complex, multi-chamber medications with programmed release rates. In the realm of military tech, **[the CIA reportedly deployed an AI system called "Ghost Whisper"](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557110.htm)** to locate a downed US pilot in the Iranian desert by isolating the sound of a human heartbeat from miles away.

## Top Story

## Tech & AI
