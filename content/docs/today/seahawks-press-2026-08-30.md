# Seahawks Press — 2026-08-30

## Top Takeaway
General Manager **John Schneider** made it clear that Seattle views its roster as an evolving "70-man" operation rather than a fixed 53, executing a strategy to stash veteran cornerback **Trevon Diggs** on the practice squad while trading for defensive back **Avery Smith** to fortify the secondary. Schneider also addressed the long-term signing of cornerback **Terrion Arnold**, confirming that while Arnold remains on the commissioner's exempt list during his legal process, he is permitted inside the facility to train and attend team meetings.

## Press Conferences & Interviews

**[John Schneider / Initial 53-Man Roster Press Conference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zy0v4JamvJ8)** · [Seahawks Press Pass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zy0v4JamvJ8)
**John Schneider** kicked off cutdown day by detailing how using veteran practice squad spots allows the front office to protect young developmental players from the waiver wire while maintaining the league's highest transactional volume. Sifting through the roster maneuvers, Schneider confirmed the plan to bring back **Trevon Diggs** via the practice squad as he ramps up conditioning, while praising newly acquired **Avery Smith** as an instinctive outside/nickel fit with immediate special teams value. On the health front, rookie wideout **Tory Horton** will not head to injured reserve, running back **Zach Charbonnet** is opening on the PUP list as expected, and third-round corner **Julian Neal** made the cut despite missing preseason snaps with a quad setback. Schneider was candid, humorous, and reflective throughout the presser, acknowledging the emotional difficulty of letting Super Bowl contributors walk while expressing genuine excitement about **Sam Howell's** command of the offense and incoming ownership's technological resources.

## Injury & Roster Updates
Running back **Zach Charbonnet** will open the regular season on the reserve/PUP list, whereas rookie wide receiver **Tory Horton** avoided an injured reserve designation and remains on the active 53. Safety **Nick Emmanwori's** availability for Week 1 is still to be determined as he rehabs, while core special-teamer **Herb Charles** has been designated to return after missing camp. The cornerback room underwent significant remodeling: third-round pick **Julian Neal** secured a roster spot despite his quad injury, **Avery Smith** was added to provide nickel and boundary depth, and **Terrion Arnold** is on the commissioner's exempt list where he can work out and attend meetings but cannot practice. Meanwhile, wideout **Montorie Foster Jr.** officially earned a 53-man spot after an explosive summer following his jump from substitute teaching to an elite gunner and rapid learner at receiver.

## Reading Between the Lines
Schneider’s aggressive late maneuvering in the secondary—invoking Ted Thompson's mantra that you must "always have a guy in the chamber"—reveals that the front office was far more uncomfortable with its corner depth than public coach-speak suggested. Montorie Foster Jr. playing his way onto the 53 over more established names shows that Mike Macdonald’s staff demands relentless special teams buy-in and film-room obsession before awarding back-of-the-roster snaps. Finally, while Schneider conceded the emotional sting of parting with championship veterans, his decisive contract work with Leonard Williams alongside investments in younger DBs proves Seattle is resisting post-Super Bowl complacency.
