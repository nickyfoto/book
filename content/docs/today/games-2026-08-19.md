# Gaming News — 2026-08-19

## Top Story
A massive new security breach has hit Rockstar Games, with gameplay videos and a full map of the highly anticipated **Grand Theft Auto 6** leaked online by a group called 'CyberLeek' to promote a cryptocurrency memecoin. The illegal stunt has forced the grassroots **Stop Killing Games** consumer campaign to issue a statement strongly distancing itself from the hackers' destructive methods and expressing sympathy for the developers.

## News & Reviews

**[PlayStation Reportedly Reboots Horizon Multiplayer Spinoff Following Negative Feedback](https://www.ign.com/articles/playstation-reportedly-reboots-horizon-multiplayer-spinoff-following-negative-feedback)** · IGN
Sony’s aggressive chase for live-service gold has hit yet another roadblock, with reports indicating that the upcoming cartoony co-op spinoff **Horizon Hunters Gathering** has been ordered back to the drawing board. Following negative feedback from closed playtests, developer **Guerrilla Games** is reportedly shifting the project into a traditional, smaller-scope cooperative game with a story mode, stripping out the live-service fluff. This retreat highlights the ongoing retreat of Sony's multiplayer ambitions after high-profile cancellations and disastrous launches like Concord.

**[Dishonored, Deus Ex Designer Stars New Studio to Continue Focusing on Immersive Sims](https://www.ign.com/articles/dishonored-deus-ex-designer-stars-new-studio-black-pony-immersive-immersive-sims)** · IGN
Legendary game designer Harvey Smith has risen from the ashes of Arkane Austin's shutdown to found **Black Pony Immersive**, a new studio dedicated to first-person, narrative-driven immersive sims. Smith also revealed that after Microsoft closed Arkane, he successfully negotiated to let his fired team back into the building to finish and ship the final, bug-squashing **Redfall 1.4 update**. For fans of deep player choice and meticulous world-building, seeing Smith carry the torch for a genre he helped pioneer is a spectacular silver lining to a rough year for the industry.

**[‘You Don’t Get XP, But Zones Do’ — How Control Resonant Is Rewriting the Action RPG Rule Book](https://www.ign.com/articles/you-dont-get-xp-but-zones-do-how-control-resonant-is-rewriting-the-action-rpg-rule-book)** · IGN
Remedy Entertainment is throwing out traditional leveling conventions in its upcoming action-RPG sequel, **Control Resonant**, by leveling up the environments of a corrupted Manhattan rather than the protagonist Dylan. As you complete activities and defeat towering bosses, the city zones grow more dangerous, spawning terrifying new Hiss and Mold threats while simultaneously unlocking passive buffs and hidden arcade challenges. It's a bold, characteristically weird Remedy mechanic that makes the world itself feel like a living, evolving adversary.

**[Director of Marvel’s Wolverine Responds After Extended Gameplay Footage Sparks Backlash](https://www.ign.com/articles/director-of-marvels-wolverine-responds-after-extended-gameplay-footage-sparks-backlash)** · IGN
Following an extended gameplay showcase of **Marvel's Wolverine** that drew online criticism for a 'red scent trail' tracking mechanic and a standard Sentinel boss fight, game director Mike Daly responded to the internet backlash. Daly stated that Insomniac is accustomed to intense scrutiny when handling beloved superhero IPs and remains focused on delivering the best possible game for Logan. While internet reactions have been mixed, IGN's hands-on preview praised the combat for beautifully nailing Wolverine's brutal, fundamentally sound spirit.

**[Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 Beta Will Fix Issues That Have Plagued the Series for the Better Part of a Decade](https://www.ign.com/articles/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-beta-will-fix-issues-that-have-plagued-the-series-for-the-better-part-of-a-decade)** · IGN
The upcoming **Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4** beta, launching August 21, will introduce major quality-of-life improvements, including a standalone client that bypasses the bloated Call of Duty HQ app and a massive reduction in those annoying "update requires restart" prompts. However, the beta will also test a divisive new team-balancing matchmaking system alongside standard open matchmaking to gather data and optimize the experience. If you want a smoother CoD experience without the launcher headaches, this weekend is your first chance to jump in.

**[Subnautica 2 Early Access 1.2 Update Out Now Alongside Patch Notes, Dev Donates \$100,000 to Ocean Cleanup](https://www.ign.com/articles/subnautica-2-early-access-12-update-out-now-alongside-patch-notes-dev-donates-100000-to-ocean-cleanup)** · IGN
Unknown Worlds has released the massive 1.2 "Buddy System" update for **Subnautica 2** in Early Access, bringing critical co-op features to the alien ocean survival game. Players can now enjoy proximity voice chat, direct inventory trading, emotes, and revives that allow friends to share oxygen to keep each other alive. To celebrate the launch, the developer announced a generous \$100,000 donation to the non-profit group **The Ocean Cleanup**.

**[Google Pixel 11 Pro Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/google-pixel-11-pro-review)** · IGN
Google's newly launched Pixel 11 Pro offers a solid annual refresh with a beautiful 6.3-inch display and an exceptional camera system, but it arrives with a steep \$1,099 starting price and a memory reduction to 12GB. While the phone handles typical daily tasks with ease, its Tensor G6 processor and PowerVR GPU fall well behind competitors for high-end mobile gaming, experiencing frequent hitches during demanding titles. It is a fantastic option for photography enthusiasts, but serious mobile gamers should look elsewhere.
