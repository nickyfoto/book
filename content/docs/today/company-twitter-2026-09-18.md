# Company@X — 2026-09-18

## Signal of the Day

The deal marks a major institutional shift toward embedded third-party governance and continuous verification as enterprise clients demand audited AI safety standards.

## Key Announcements

**Anthropic** · [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/accenture-embedded-evaluation)
Anthropic and Accenture announced a partnership to embed independent Accenture evaluators directly within Anthropic to test frontier AI models, backed by a joint investment commitment of at least \$1 billion. The initiative operationalizes Anthropic's commitment to continuous third-party safety and performance audits prior to commercial deployment. This move signals a structural shift toward institutionalized, embedded governance as enterprise clients demand verifiable evaluation standards.

**Waymo** · [Source](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/09/waymo-in-singapore/)
Waymo announced its expansion into Singapore, marking its first major international deployment for autonomous ride-hailing outside the United States. Test vehicles will arrive in Singapore later this year to begin road mapping in early 2027, targeting a commercial driverless launch in 2028. The move demonstrates Waymo's confidence in scaling its autonomous driver to dense Asian urban traffic and foreign regulatory frameworks.

**Google** · [Source](https://goo.gle/4hfExQu)
Google released real-time conversational assistance in Search Live, powered by its latest Gemini 3.8 Live audio model. The update enables low-latency spoken interactions within Search, allowing users to ask complex questions and receive fluid voice responses. This deployment advances Google's strategy to establish multimodal voice interaction as a primary interface for web search.

**Meta** · [Source](https://bit.ly/4rDArq9)
Meta released Segment Anything Model (SAM) 3.1 on the Meta Model API, offering inference tuned specifically for detection, pixel-precise segmentation, and tracking. Developers can now perform object detection and identity-preserving video tracking using a single text phrase prompt in a unified API call. This release reduces latency and integration overhead for building vision applications in production.

**Exa AI Labs** · [Source](https://twitter.macworks.dev/ExaAILabs/status/2100634095395225898#m)
Exa launched Exa Snapshot, a search index containing 400 billion historical webpage snapshots that allows AI agents to query the web as it existed at specific past dates. The system prevents present-day data leakage during backtesting of financial prediction models and isolates human-written pre-2022 web data for AI training. This infrastructure addresses critical data integrity challenges for AI labs training models with reinforcement learning.

**OpenClaw** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/openclaw/status/2101071501785276914#m)
OpenClaw launched "Multiplayer Mode" to solve human "meat proxy" bottlenecks when operating autonomous AI agents across teams. The update introduces shared multi-user agent sessions, `@mentions`, permissions, and live dashboards for team visibility and handoffs. The release shifts open-source agent infrastructure toward multi-user coordination over isolated execution.

**Angle Health** · [Source](https://www.wsj.com/pro/private-equity/ai-startup-angle-health-lands-600-million-for-2-7-billion-valuation-df77de76)
Angle Health secured \$200 million in Series C funding at a \$2.7 billion valuation to expand its AI-native health benefits platform for small businesses. Founded by former Palantir engineers, the company is net-income profitable and manages nearly \$1 billion in annual premium equivalents for over 5,000 businesses. The massive valuation reflects strong investor demand for vertical AI platforms executing in complex regulated sectors.
