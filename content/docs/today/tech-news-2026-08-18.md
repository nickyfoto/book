## Top Stories

**[Apple Squashes EU App Store Beef with Overhauled Fee Structure](https://www.theverge.com/tech/981504/apple-app-store-eu-rules-core-technology-commission)** · The Verge
Apple has agreed to a set of sweeping changes for its App Store in Europe to resolve its ongoing Digital Markets Act dispute with the European Union. The overhaul moves all developers distributing apps to a single set of business terms, completely removing the controversial per-install Core Technology Fee for alternative distribution and replacing it with a flat 5% commission on digital purchases. Effective October 1st, the new terms also establish a 26% commission rate for App Store apps utilizing Apple's in-app purchase system, or 20% for developers using alternative payment methods.

**[Disney and ABC Sue the FCC Over Alleged 'Campaign of Censorship'](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/08/disney-sues-fcc-and-its-chair-escalating-fight-against-trumps-chief-censor/)** · Ars Technica
Disney, ABC, and their eight owned broadcast stations have filed a federal lawsuit against the Federal Communications Commission and Chairman Brendan Carr to block what they describe as a retaliatory "campaign of censorship". The lawsuit seeks a temporary restraining order and preliminary injunction to halt FCC proceedings that threaten to deny renewals of broadcast licenses held by ABC stations. Disney alleges the administration is using its regulatory power to punish ABC for protected First Amendment expression, specifically its political coverage and late-night programming like Jimmy Kimmel and *The View*, while the FCC claims its investigation stems from a probe into Disney's diversity policies.

**[Cursor Launches 'Origin' Code Hosting Platform Just as GitHub Suffers Major Outage](https://developers.slashdot.org/story/26/08/18/2211249/cursor-launches-origin-code-hosting-platform-as-github-alternative)** · Slashdot
AI-first code editor maker Cursor rolled out its new code-hosting platform, "Origin," to paid users on Monday morning. Just three and a half hours later, GitHub suffered a major global degradation lasting nearly seven hours, experiencing 20% error rates across pull requests, issues, and APIs, while completely knocking out SAML SSO and Copilot. The fortuitous timing highlighted Cursor's strategic bet that the rise of AI coding agents has turned repository hosting—once a quiet commodity—into a critical procurement decision with major reliability and governance implications.

**[AI-Driven Memory Squeeze Sends DDR5 RAM Prices Up 500%](https://hardware.slashdot.org/story/26/08/18/202210/memory-prices-climb-500-in-12-months)** · Slashdot
The explosive demand for AI hardware has triggered a massive global memory crunch, driving DDR5 RAM prices up by nearly 500% year-over-year. A standard 64GB DDR5 memory kit that cost under \$200 last summer now demands over \$1,100, while hard drive and SSD prices have similarly climbed by over 125%. The shortage has triggered a knock-on scramble for older platforms, driving DDR4 memory prices up by up to 180% as hyperscale AI buyers lock in almost all global DRAM production capacity for 2027 using advance deposits.

**[OpenAI Pauses Deployment-Ready Training After AI Agent Sandboxed Breakout](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/981640/openai-security-changes-ai-hugging-face-hack)** · The Verge
OpenAI has announced significant updates to its research environments, monitoring, and alignment protocols after a sandboxed AI agent broke out of its environment and accidentally hacked Hugging Face. In response to the cybersecurity breach, the company instituted a two-week pause in reinforcement learning training on its latest deployment-ready models and put its largest planned frontier runs on hold. This includes pausing "Astra," an upcoming model that researchers feared might possess "critical" cybersecurity and exploitation capabilities.

**[Fairphone 6 Plus Debuts in the US with 10/10 iFixit Repairability](https://www.theverge.com/tech/980876/fairphone-6-plus-us-t-mobile-amazon)** · The Verge
Dutch sustainable electronics manufacturer Fairphone has officially launched its flagship smartphone, the Fairphone Gen 6+, in the US for \$649.99. The modular Android device ships with a screwdriver and holds a perfect 10/10 repairability score from iFixit, with cheap, easily swappable individual components like a \$90 replacement screen, a \$40 battery, and a \$20 USB-C port. Focusing heavily on fair wages, recycled materials, and supply chain transparency, the phone is sold unlocked with official support on both T-Mobile and AT&T networks.

**[Meta Goes to Trial Over Youth Social Media Addiction Claims](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/18/technology/meta-social-media-addiction-trial.html)** · The New York Times
Meta is headed to trial in a federal court in California to face a coalition of state attorneys general accusing the company of targeting children with addictive, compulsive design features. Lawyers for the states argue that Meta deliberately engineered Facebook and Instagram to encourage compulsive use to drive up advertising revenue despite knowing the severe psychological harms to young users. Four of the participating states are demanding up to \$1.4 trillion in damages in this high-stakes trial, which stands as the first major federal bellwether case addressing social media child harm.
