**[SpaceX Targets First Starship Orbital Launch for September 22](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/15/spacex-will-try-to-put-starship-in-orbit-for-the-first-time-on-september-22/)** · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/15/spacex-will-try-to-put-starship-in-orbit-for-the-first-time-on-september-22/)

The mission aims to deploy 26 next-generation V3 Starlink satellites before completing six Earth orbits over a 10-hour flight duration. The launch represents a crucial milestone for SpaceX as it tests orbital deployment and long-duration flight capabilities for its heavy-lift architecture.
