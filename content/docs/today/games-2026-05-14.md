## News & Reviews

**[Forza Horizon 6 Maps a Gorgeous Japanese Playground]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/forza-horizon-6-review)
Playground Games finally takes its flagship racing series to Japan, and the results are stunning. *Forza Horizon 6* offers a deeply credible map featuring dense Tokyo cityscapes, rural touge circuits, and parking lots built organically for car meets. With global release unlocking on Steam on May 18 (May 14 for Premium early access), this is shaping up to be a masterful evolution of the formula with incredible performance scaling on PC.

**[Subnautica 2 Makes Massive Early Access Splash]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/subnautica-2-lures-in-nearly-half-a-million-concurrent-players-on-steam-within-first-hour-of-release)
Unknown Worlds' highly anticipated sequel launched into Early Access, drawing in an unbelievable 467,000 concurrent Steam players and selling 1 million copies in its first hour. While early reviews award it a solid 7/10 for its gripping sci-fi narrative and seamless co-op, the current map limits mean you might burn through the mystery a bit fast. It's a fantastic foundation, but patience will be key as the developers expand the murky depths.

**[Bungie Pivots Marathon with New PVE Modes]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/marathon-reveals-new-pve-mode-and-big-plans-for-the-future)
In a bid to broaden its audience after a reportedly rough reception internally, Bungie is introducing a dedicated PVE mode to *Marathon* in Season 2. The extraction shooter will also see massive progression resets, guaranteed map events to make runs more lucrative, and quality-of-life adjustments to ease in new players. It’s a smart move to attract gamers who love Bungie’s gunplay but hate high-stakes PVP sweat fests.

**[Fortnite Axes Lethal Fall Damage, Welcomes Overwatch]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/fortnite-nerfs-lethal-fall-damage-in-big-gameplay-shakeup-as-overwatch-update-arrives)
Epic Games has drastically shaken up *Fortnite's* core battle royale by replacing lethal fall damage with a 1HP "splat" mechanic. Alongside a massive *Overwatch* crossover bringing Tracer's time-bending pistols and maps like Hanamura to the island, Zero Build players also gain unlimited-use "Pocket Items" like Medmist and Shield Bubbles. 

**[Assassin’s Creed Hexe Leaks Point to Ezio's Return]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/assassins-creed-hexe-image-allegedly-leaks-alongside-word-of-a-major-returning-character)
A new leak suggests 2027’s witchcraft-centric *Assassin's Creed Hexe* will feature a female protagonist named Anika navigating creepy European forests alongside her mentor, Wolfgang. Surprisingly, iconic series hero Ezio Auditore is also rumored to return, reportedly manifesting through an ancient Isu memory recording to guide the narrative.

**[Party Animals Review-Bombed Over AI Contest]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/rest-in-peace-party-animals-review-bombed-over-ai-video-contest)
Co-op hit *Party Animals* has been slammed with over 800 negative Steam reviews after developers announced a $15,000 video contest requiring the use of generative AI. Fans are heavily criticizing the studio's choice to mandate AI tools for creative submissions, dragging the game's recent review rating down to "mostly negative".
