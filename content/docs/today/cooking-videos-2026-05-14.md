### Recipes & Tutorials
In [炒高麗菜總是出水又不脆？關鍵在加鹽時機！｜手撕高麗菜 [詹姆士]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_1LKAjuBfAc) on the 詹姆士官方專屬頻道 channel, Chef James demonstrates a fantastic recipe for Hand-Torn Cabbage (手撕高麗菜) that boasts a tantalizing hint of numbing spice. He explains that tearing the cabbage by hand, rather than cleanly slicing it with a knife, creates irregular edges that allow the leaves to absorb the rich seasonings far more effectively. The dish builds a robust flavor profile by stir-frying the thicker cabbage stems first before adding the tender leaves, and finishes with soy sauce, white pepper, Sichuan peppercorn oil, and a splash of black vinegar for an incredibly aromatic, perfectly crunchy side dish.

### Food Science & Tips
The true game-changer in this video is the culinary science behind when to salt your vegetables: adding salt right at the beginning of the stir-fry process forces the cabbage to release its water, turning your crisp vegetables into a soggy, watery mess. To prevent this, you should cook the cabbage in a slightly generous amount of oil to prevent the temperature from dropping, and only mix in the salt at the very end right before plating. Additionally, Chef James shares a smart prep tip for high-heat cooking: slice your garlic instead of mincing it, as fine garlic paste burns much too quickly in a blazing hot wok.
