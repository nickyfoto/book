# YouTube — 2026-05-27

## Watch First
[How the Electrical Grid Is Being Rebuilt for AI | Bloomberg Primer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8KOYyfZbPzo) is a fascinating look at how the explosive power demands of artificial intelligence are forcing a massive, global overhaul of the electrical transmission system. It serves as an excellent primer on the geopolitical battle for grid dominance and features experimental hardware like Veir's liquid-nitrogen-cooled superconducting cables.
