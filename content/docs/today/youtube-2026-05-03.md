# YouTube — 2026-05-03

## Watch First
If you only watch one thing today, check out [Can a quantum sensor detect your heartbeat from 60 km away?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVTPv4sI_Jc). It is an excellent investigation by Veritasium that breaks down the physics of nitrogen-vacancy diamond magnetometers while thoroughly debunking a sensationalist rumor about CIA heartbeat-tracking technology.

## Highlights by Theme

### News & Business
On the business front, CNBC explores a fascinating side effect of the weight-loss boom in [Why GLP-1s Are Helping The Hair Care Industry](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TvhSxb8qltw): GLP-1 drugs are causing severe hair loss for some patients, sparking a new $1 billion opportunity for the beauty and haircare industry. For corporate strategy, CNBC International offers a compelling profile in [How Rakuten Built a Tech Empire by Breaking the Rules](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fzar4vb03TI), detailing CEO Mickey Mikitani's contrarian choices, such as mandating English for his Japanese workforce to compete globally and pivoting heavily into a virtualized mobile network. 

### Learning & Ideas
The most inspiring talk today is [The Nurse Who Can Smell Parkinson’s | Joy Milne | TED](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oCtLmeo56Jo), detailing how a woman with hereditary hyperosmia smelled changes in her husband's sebum a decade before his diagnosis, which is now leading to a non-invasive diagnostic swab test. In history, the Chinese-language channel LIFEANO CLUB provides a dense but rewarding Q&A session in [【限免】袁Sir翻牌](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVUDeaT7KNk), touching on everything from Southern Song dynasty political factions to why Spain's Francisco Franco refused to help Hitler take Gibraltar during WWII.

### Tech & AI
In Chinese financial commentary, [AI产业已秘密转向！](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cgKUgAJE3cs) notes that AI giants are quietly pivoting away from consumer chatbots toward enterprise AI agents, realizing that B2B tools like Anthropic's Claude and OpenAI's Codex are far more profitable, setting up a new arms race for compute power. As this AI disruption accelerates, a brief [TED Talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4OFjfCGXBJU) highlights that the U.S. workforce isn't prepared, arguing for outcome-based training and tax credits to incentivize worker redeployment rather than mass layoffs.
