**[Boeing Engineers and Technical Workers Reject Proposed Contracts, Authorize Future Strike](https://komonews.com/news/local/boeing-strike-engineers-technical-workers-speea-union-reject-contracts-approve-strike-authorization-october-contarct-expires-negotiations)** · KOMO News

The union membership approved **strike authorization measures**, preparing them to strike if negotiations fail to produce an agreement before their contracts expire in October. This decision sets the stage for potential industrial action, intensifying labor pressure on Boeing as both sides look to resume contract talks.

Seattle Public Utilities (SPU) has launched a **\$6.4 million sewer rehabilitation project** at the intersection of 12th Avenue and Pike Street, sparking outrage among Capitol Hill business owners. **[Two Unrelated Vehicle-on-Pole Collisions Leave Over 10,000 Seattle Customers Without Power](https://www.king5.com/article/news/local/seattle/pole-incidents-weekend-seattle-power-outages/281-25b2cd71-36ba-439a-bf97-55dd11f708fd)** · KING 5

Seattle City Light worked through the weekend to replace damaged infrastructure and restore electrical service to the affected neighborhoods.
