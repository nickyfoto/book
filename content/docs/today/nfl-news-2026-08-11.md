# NFL News — 2026-08-11

## Read First
In the most significant quarterback decision of the preseason, Minnesota Vikings head coach Kevin O’Connell officially named veteran **[Kyler Murray the team’s starting quarterback](https://www.vikings.com/news/kyler-murray-quarterback-starting-2026-nfl-season)** for the 2026 season, ending a highly publicized three-month battle with sophomore signal-caller J.J. McCarthy. This move provides the Vikings with a highly athletic and experienced dual-threat starter under center while allowing the 23-year-old McCarthy more time to develop and fully recover after an injury-shortened rookie campaign.

## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
The league's top rookies are rapidly claiming key roles, headlined by Tampa Bay Buccaneers first-round pick Rueben Bain Jr., who is the lone rookie starter on the team's **[first unofficial depth chart](https://www.buccaneers.com/news/looming-preseason-opener-means-first-bucs-depth-chart-2026)** and was described as a "one-man wrecking crew" in joint practices against the Jets. In Cleveland, rookie wideout Denzel Boston is pushing for a starting role after showcasing his separation and scoring a deep touchdown during **[Day 12 of Browns camp](https://www.clevelandbrowns.com/news/denzel-boston-posts-standout-performance-on-day-12-training-camp-observations)**, while rookie quarterbacks Shedeur Sanders and Fernando Mendoza are poised for heavy reps in their respective preseason openers. Meanwhile, New York Giants No. 5 overall pick Arvell Reese drew rave reviews from defensive coordinator Dennard Wilson after nabbing a tipped interception in MetLife Stadium-prep, and Carolina Panthers cornerback Will Lee III is already competing with the ones, displaying the "confidence" coordinator Ejiro Evero loves **[at the midway point of training camp](https://www.panthers.com/news/three-observations-from-panthers-coordinators-at-mid-way-point-of-training-camp)**. Up front, Las Vegas Raiders' Canadian rookie undrafted tackle Niklas Henning is rapidly finding his footing, shifting to right guard and displaying impressive progress after a stellar **[CFL-to-NFL transition](https://www.raiders.com/news/from-cfl-prospect-to-raiders-lineman-niklas-henning-is-finding-his-footing-in-the-raiders-trenches)**.

### Roster Moves & Free Agency
The Chicago Bears suffered a devastating blow to their secondary as prized free-agent safety **[Coby Bryant underwent knee surgery](https://www.chicagobears.com/news/coby-bryant-to-miss-4-6-months-after-knee-surgery)** and is expected to miss four to six months, projecting his return for mid-December at the earliest. In response to defensive injuries across the league, the Green Bay Packers added depth by **[signing safeties Trey Dean III and Tra Fluellen](https://www.packers.com/news/packers-announce-roster-moves-august-11-2026)** while releasing Drew Biber and Karsen Barnhart, and the Seattle Seahawks signed linebacker Aaron Smith while placing fullback Robbie Ouzts on injured reserve. Meanwhile, front offices were active on the waiver wire as the Atlanta Falcons **[claimed guard Jaeden Roberts](https://www.atlantafalcons.com/news/falcons-claim-offensive-lineman-off-waivers)** and signed tight end Nick Muse, while the Cleveland Browns claimed rookie tight end Dae'Quan Wright and placed cornerback Damarri Mathis on IR. Finally, the New Orleans Saints are turning to rookie fourth-round guard **[Jeremiah Wright to step up](https://www.neworleanssaints.com/news/new-orleans-saints-rookie-guard-jeremiah-wright-poised-for-action-in-joint-practices-preseason-games)** following a season-ending ACL tear to their top backup interior lineman.

### Team Beats
Joint practices erupted in high-stakes physicality and ejections across the league, as a massive 30-player scuffle broke out between the **[Bucs and Jets at One Jets Drive](https://www.buccaneers.com/news/luke-goedeke-on-joint-practice-scuffles-we-are-here-to-play-football)** after Jets' David Bailey called out Bucs' Luke Goedeke, while the Titans-49ers session saw the ejections of tackles Austin Deculus and Dan Moore Jr. following multiple early brawls. On the field, quarterback operations took center stage as a healthy Jalen Hurts showed off his improved speed in a new **[Eagles training camp system](https://www.philadelphiaeagles.com/news/eagles-training-camp-notes-highlights-ahead-of-the-first-preseason-game)** under Vic Fangio, while Falcons signal-caller Tua Tagovailoa delivered his best practice yet under Alex Van Pelt. For teams visiting outside foes, Drake Maye's vertical connections with Romeo Doubs highlighted a **[productive Patriots-Colts joint session](https://www.patriots.com/news/day-14-blogservations-patriots-colts-enjoy-productive-back-and-forth-in-joint-practice)** despite a late interception, while Dallas' linebacker Justin Barron dominated the Rams with three interceptions in a physical matchup that also featured **[Dak Prescott and Matthew Stafford clicking in the red zone](https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/dak-prescott-brian-schottenheimer-recap-rams-joint-practice-it-was-a-battle)**. Finally, injury concerns are lingering over several contenders as Baltimore is holding its breath after elite cornerback Nate Wiggins was carted off, while Green Bay has capitalized on injuries to wideouts by letting Bo Melton shine with the **[Packers' starting offense](https://www.packers.com/news/secondary-sabbatical-shaped-bo-melton-into-better-receiver-aug-11-2026)**.

### League News & Culture
Commercial and rule innovations are redefining the gameday experience, highlighted by a landmark multi-year deal naming **[Athletic Brewing Company the Proud Non-Alcoholic Beer Partner of MetLife Stadium](https://www.newyorkjets.com/news/athletic-brewing-metlife-stadium-jets-giants-announcement-08-11-2026)**, the Giants, and the Jets, which will feature special-edition co-branded 12-packs. Across the league, teams are dramatically upgrading stadium culinary offerings, including New Orleans Saints icon Drew Brees getting set for a halftime induction into the **[Saints' Ring of Honor](https://www.neworleanssaints.com/news/drew-brees-saints-ring-of-honor-october-11-saints-vikings)** on October 11, and the Colts, Patriots, Panthers, and Cardinals introducing major new local food partnerships. Finally, coaches are preparing for a subtle but critical rule adjustment this season, as a newly tweaked Rule 15 Section 1 now allows head coaches to **[delegate the duty of throwing the red challenge flag](https://www.baltimoreravens.com/news/lamar-jackson-will-not-play-preseason-opener-jesse-minter-defensive-play-caller-who-will-throw-challenge-flag-teddye-buchanan-update)** to a designated assistant on their staff.
