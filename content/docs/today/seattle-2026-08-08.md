**[Grand Park 2 Fire Sparks Near Mount Rainier, Forcing Closures](https://komonews.com/news/local/grand-park-2-fire-grows-to-about-200-acres-in-mount-rainier-national-park-sr-410-closed-pierce-county-burn-ban-wildfires-lane-eleanor-grand-park-2-sunrise-white-river)** · KOMO News
**A new wildfire, dubbed the Grand Park 2 Fire, has erupted northeast of Mount Rainier, quickly growing to approximately 200 acres. The National Park Service has closed all access to the popular Sunrise and White River areas from State Route 410. Fire crews are actively battling the timber-burning blaze as a Pierce County burn ban and red flag weather conditions escalate fire dangers across the region.**

**[Tacoma Police Rescue Over 30 Malnourished Cats from Abandoned Boat](https://www.king5.com/article/life/animals/over-30-cats-rescued-from-abandoned-boat-tacoma-beach/281-121b1385-7404-41c8-b891-455d1771e8aa)** · KING 5
**Tacoma Police Department officers, Animal Control, and Forensics successfully rescued over 30 frightened and malnourished cats and kittens discovered on an abandoned sailboat near Owen Beach. The felines were found living in unsafe conditions on the anchored vessel after police investigated reports of a boat with improper lighting. The rescue mission concluded Friday afternoon with 31 of the cats safely transferred to the Humane Society for Tacoma and Pierce County.**

**[Renton Man Charged with Costco Parking Lot Road-Rage Murder](https://komonews.com/news/local/renton-man-charged-with-murder-after-intentional-fatal-motorcycle-crash-with-coworker-costco-headquarter-issaquah-crime-king-county-video-crash-scene)** · KOMO News
**A Renton man is facing murder charges in King County after allegedly intentionally causing a fatal motorcycle crash that killed his co-worker in Issaquah. The suspect admitted to police that his "blood was boiling" after seeing the victim hug a female co-worker in their workplace Costco parking lot earlier that night. Prosecutors are pursuing the case following a review of local surveillance footage of the crash scene.**

**[UW Off-Campus Housing Projects Spark Outrage After Slated Trees Removed](https://komonews.com/news/local/trees-slated-for-preservation-damaged-removed-at-university-of-washington-college-student-housing-projects-greystar-blakeley-village-tree-canopy-tree-action-seattle)** · KOMO News
**Dozens of mature, established trees originally slated for preservation have been damaged or removed at the sites of two new University of Washington off-campus student housing developments. Developer Greystar had previously assured local community members that the historic tree canopy at Blakeley Village and Laurel Village would be retained and incorporated into the projects. However, neighbors and tree advocates are speaking out after finding several trees dead, missing, or severely damaged as construction moves forward.**

**[City Proposes New Policy to Stop Surveillance Pricing in Grocery Stores](https://www.kiro7.com/news/local/new-seattle-policy-aims-block-retailers-using-personal-data-set-grocery-prices/7E66XZ4UZFHDPAG6WZVSGVZMJ4/)** · KIRO 7
**The City of Seattle has introduced a policy proposal designed to block grocery stores and other retailers from using shoppers' personal data to set individualized, dynamic prices. The initiative aims to protect consumer privacy and prevent corporate pricing algorithms from charging different rates to different customers based on personal profile tracking.**
