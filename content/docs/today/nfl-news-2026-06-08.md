# NFL News — 2026-06-08

## Read First
The Atlanta Falcons secured their offensive cornerstone by signing wide receiver Drake London to a staggering four-year, $141 million extension containing $100 million in guarantees, as confirmed in **[REPORT: Kevin Stefanski confirms Drake London extension](https://www.atlantafalcons.com/news/report-kevin-stefanski-drake-london-extension)**.
