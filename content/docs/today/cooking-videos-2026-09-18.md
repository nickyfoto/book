### Food Science & Tips
Martin provides essential buying advice for picking plump, roe-rich autumn crabs—specifically looking for a dark, bulging abdomen—alongside a safe knife technique to calm the crab before thoroughly scrubbing away mud and trimming the gills and stomach. On the flavor chemistry side, he highlights how the amino acids in Puning bean paste interact with the natural glutamic and nucleic acids in crab meat, dramatically amplifying its natural sweetness and umami.
