**[Price Hike or Discount? Nintendo’s Changes to Physical and Digital Game Pricing Have Analysts Split, Too](https://www.ign.com/articles/price-hike-or-discount-nintendos-changes-to-physical-and-digital-game-pricing-have-analysts-split-too)**
Nintendo is shifting its pricing model starting with Yoshi and the Mysterious Book, which will cost $69.99 physically but $59.99 digitally in the U.S. Analysts suggest this is a strategic move to push consumers toward the more profitable digital ecosystem and offset rising manufacturing costs, essentially serving as a stealth price hike for physical collectors.

**[VR Social Game Rec Room Is Shutting Down on its 10th Anniversary](https://www.ign.com/articles/vr-social-game-rec-room-is-shutting-down-on-its-10th-anniversary-our-costs-always-ended-up-overwhelming-the-revenue-we-brought-in)**
Despite reaching over 150 million players, the VR social club Rec Room is officially shutting down on June 1 due to an inability to find a sustainably profitable business model. The developers noted that costs consistently overwhelmed revenue, dealing another major blow to the struggling VR gaming market.

**[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-super-mario-galaxy-movie-review)**
Illumination’s The Super Mario Galaxy Movie is packed wall-to-wall with Easter eggs, deep-cut Nintendo cameos, and gorgeous action sequences. However, the overcrowded roster leaves the film lacking an emotional core or a focused story, making it a fun but somewhat forgettable follow-up to the 2023 hit.
