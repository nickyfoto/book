## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
The rookie contract dominoes are finally falling, headlined by No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza inking a fully guaranteed four-year, $57.2 million deal with the Las Vegas Raiders. According to ESPN's NFL Live crew, the Raiders plan to sprinkle Mendoza into first-team reps but are perfectly content letting veteran Kirk Cousins steer Clint Kubiak's offense while the rookie marinates on the sideline. Over on **[Which rookies will stand out in training camp? | GMFB](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gAfUO1f24ag)**, the panel zeroed in on immediate impact defenders, singling out Bears safety Dylan Theamman as an elite center-fielder and Commanders linebacker Sunny Styles as a freaky athlete ready to dictate Dan Quinn's defense. 

### Free Agency & Trades
The New England Patriots are going all-in on Drake Maye following his near-MVP season, swinging a massive blockbuster trade for A.J. Brown to completely transform their passing attack. In Dallas, Jerry Jones is playing hardball; the Cowboys are forcing wideout George Pickens to prove himself on a $27.3 million franchise tag rather than handing him a long-term extension. In a concerning medical update, Tyreek Hill revealed he still has "no power" in his surgically repaired left knee 10 months after his devastating injury, throwing his effectiveness for the Dolphins into serious doubt in **[Can veteran WR Tyreek Hill overcome injury setbacks amid recovery? | Pro Football Talk | NFL on NBC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tbni1DmpH3A)**. Out in Los Angeles, don't hold your breath for Aaron Donald—the future Hall of Famer is skipping the start of Rams camp and won't return until he's fully convinced his body can handle another grueling run. 

### Game Analysis & Strategy
Bill Barnwell's FTN Almanac breakdown dropped some spicy projections, most notably backing the Houston Texans to boast a top-10 offense behind C.J. Stroud while projecting the Lions' injured secondary to drag down their elite defensive front. Meanwhile, the Cowboys' defensive success revolves around new 34-year-old defensive coordinator Christian Parker, who brings a disguised shell scheme designed to fix an interior run defense that bled points late last season.
