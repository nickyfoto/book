# CNBeta — September 11, 2026

## Top Story
According to [a cnbeta report on Enflame Technology's STAR Market debut](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577482.htm), Chinese domestic AI chipmaker Enflame Technology surged 234% on its first day of trading on Shanghai's STAR Market, turning founders Zhao Lidong and Zhang Yalin into billionaires. Backed by tech giant Tencent as its largest shareholder and primary customer, the AMD-veteran-led firm has developed non-Nvidia ecosystem AI processors despite never generating a profit. The blockbuster listing underscores how domestic capital markets continue to aggressively finance China's semiconductor independence drive amidst ongoing global trade and hardware restrictions.

## Tech & AI
A [cnbeta report on CXMT's Q2 financial performance](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577434.htm) reveals that Chinese DRAM maker CXMT achieved EBIT margins surpassing global giants Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron, as standard DDR5 memory prices soared while international competitors shifted production toward high-bandwidth memory (HBM) for AI servers. In AI IP disputes, [Anthropic accused several Chinese AI laboratories](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577500.htm)—including Alibaba, Moonshot AI, and DeepSeek—of conducting industrial-scale distillation by generating over 150 million interactions with Claude to train domestic models like Qwen and Kimi. Meanwhile, [DeepSeek retired its flagship V4 Pro model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577486.htm) just 32 days after its official launch, replacing its API traffic with the lighter V4.1 Flash architecture to reduce server operating costs while awaiting a massive deployment of 160,000 Ascend 950DT chips from Huawei. In semiconductor foundry developments, [SMIC posted record quarterly revenue exceeding \$3 billion](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577538.htm), fueled by resilient domestic demand for mature-node chips across automotive and IoT sectors.

## Consumer & Devices
Apple's new foldable, the [iPhone Duo](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577448.htm), debuted featuring a 7.8-inch inner OLED screen with a nano-texture anti-reflective layer; however, [its 15,999 RMB (\$1,999) price tag and eSIM-only requirement](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577464.htm) pose immediate adoption hurdles in China, where consumers face established book-style and tri-fold competitors from Xiaomi and Huawei. On the electric vehicle front, Tesla launched the [Model Y All-Wheel Drive Performance in China](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577432.htm) starting at 369,000 RMB (\$52,000), featuring 0–100 km/h acceleration in 3.5 seconds, V2L bi-directional charging, and a CLTC range of 659 km. Meanwhile, struggling EV brand [Neta Auto unveiled a 3 billion RMB restructuring plan](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577484.htm) backed by Taiyi Shenglian to restart production of the Neta X SUV for overseas export markets. Internationally, [Xiaomi faces potential comprehensive regulatory scrutiny in India](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577428.htm) over corporate operational models and overseas royalty payments.

## Gaming
In Shanghai's first lawsuit targeting AI voice duplication, [Mihoyo won a 750,000 RMB ruling against a software developer](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1577438.htm) that extracted voice lines from 63 *Genshin Impact* characters to sell commercial AI voice transformation packs without authorization. On PC platforms, [Ubisoft announced it is streamlining its Steam experience](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1577418.htm) by replacing the mandatory Ubisoft Connect desktop client with lightweight background services, starting with *Prince of Persia: The Lost Crown*. Meanwhile, [Nintendo issued a security advisory for Switch consoles](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1577478.htm) running firmware below version 23.0.0, warning that scanning malicious on-screen QR codes could allow unauthorized code execution.

## Science & Space
Hong Kong and Shanghai-based [Insilico Medicine reported phase IIa clinical data](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1577446.htm) for its AI-discovered idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis drug rentosertib, showing that four weeks of treatment reduced patients' blood proteomic age by up to 3.5 years across multiple biological aging clocks. In academic disputes, [OpenAI faced plagiarism accusations from German mathematician Andreas Thom](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1577450.htm), who alleged that OpenAI's Astra model utilized unpublished ChatGPT conversation logs regarding non-sofic groups to claim credit for solving Gromov's soficity conjecture. In Earth science, [Chile's hyper-arid Atacama Desert experienced rare snowfall](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1577488.htm) following an uncharacteristic cut-off low winter storm system boosted by El Niño conditions.
