# CNBC — 2026-09-01

## Lead Story
Fresh military escalation in the Middle East has sent shockwaves through global markets, with **West Texas Intermediate crude** spiking above **\$90 a barrel** following U.S. strikes against Iranian targets in the Strait of Hormuz. This geopolitical friction sparked a global bond sell-off, driving the benchmark **10-year Treasury yield** to its highest level since January 2025 at **4.788%** and severely pressuring tech-heavy equity averages.

## Markets & Economics
Rising inflation worries spurred by the Middle East war pushed the **U.S. 10-year Treasury yield** to **4.788%**, while Japan's benchmark yield reached a 30-year high of **3%**. In response, Fed Governor **Michael Barr** signaled readiness to raise rates decisively unless inflation begins to moderate toward 2%. High-yield currency desks are closely watching standard indicators like the S&P 500, which technical strategists warned could see severe consolidation if it slips below the **7,500 level**. As investors brace for these macro shifts, experts are analyzing historical indicators, with **[Piper Sandler's James Fish looks ahead to Dell earnings Tuesday](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/31/piper-sandlers-james-fish-looks-ahead-to-dell-earnings-tuesday.html)** highlighting how the AI server cycle may interact with rising capital costs.

## Business & Earnings
**Dell Technologies** led after-hours gainers, surging 10% after posting adjusted EPS of **\$7.04** on **\$46.97 billion** in revenue, handily beating estimates and tripling its full-year AI server revenue projection to **\$74 billion**. **Palo Alto Networks** also beat expectations with **\$3.41 billion** in revenue and adjusted EPS of **\$1.02**, announcing plans to buy agentic AI security startup **Console** to address a \$1 trillion modernization cycle in aging cyber infrastructure. At **Apple**, John Ternus officially took the helm as CEO as highlighted in **[John Ternus prepares to take over as CEO of Apple](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/28/john-ternus-prepares-to-take-over-as-ceo-of-apple.html)**; however, analysts cautioned that rising memory component costs could trigger margin compression of **1.4%** in the coming fiscal year. Meanwhile, camera maker **GoPro** saw its shares skyrocket 40% after announcing a surprise defensive pivot to AI data centers and military defense markets through a merger with **Starman Optical**.

## Investing & Commentary
With the S&P 500 hovering within 2% of its record high, **Jim Cramer** is turning defensive, raising cash in his Charitable Trust to **15%** and exiting **Corning** to protect a **52% average gain** amid worries that data center capital expenditures face political and resource constraints. Concurrently, Cramer argued that **Nvidia** should dramatically scale up its buybacks with a "monster" **half-trillion-dollar** authorization to address the widening discount between its robust growth and stock price performance. Active managers are also eyeing opportunities in the options space, as **Janus Henderson** highlighted that an unusual gap between individual stock volatility and broader index volatility makes writing covered calls on individual companies highly lucrative.
