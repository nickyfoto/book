# Tech Videos — 2026-09-18

## Watch First
The single video most worth watching today is **[Why Diffusion Will Win AI Inference with Inception Co-Founder and CEO Stefano Ermon](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N1rjtDs8blY)** on **No Priors: AI, Machine Learning, Tech, & Startups**. Stanford professor and Inception CEO Stefano Ermon delivers a compelling, mathematically grounded argument that diffusion-based LLMs will displace autoregressive models for production inference because non-sequential coarse-to-fine denoising inherently parallelizes across GPU tensor cores and scales test-time compute far more efficiently.
