## Top Stories
- **Apple Scrambling to Secure Memory Ahead of iPhone 18 Pro Launch**: A global **DRAM shortage** is delaying production of the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro and the foldable **"iPhone Ultra,"** leaving roughly **\$1 billion worth of un-packaged TSMC processors** sitting idle. Although Apple remains confident it can meet initial demand, assembly partners face **highly compressed production schedules** as memory supplies tighten. With lifetime production estimates hitting **200 million units**, the shortage may significantly **extend online wait times** and dry up retail stock post-launch. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/06/apple-scrambling-to-secure-memory/))
- **Apple Boosts Device Trade-In Estimates by Up to 30%**: Apple has **increased trade-in values** across most iPhones, iPads, Macs, and Apple Watches in the U.S. market, with some devices seeing **gains of nearly 30%**. This update brings the **iPhone 16 Pro Max value up to \$720**, Mac mini up to \$480, and Mac Studio up to \$1,305, alongside **new eligibility** for recent Google Pixel, Samsung Galaxy, and OnePlus models. Analysts suspect these aggressive hikes are timed to help consumers manage **anticipated price increases** for the premium iPhone 18 Pro lineup. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/06/apple-raises-trade-in-values/))
- **OpenAI Seeks Dismissal of Apple's Trade Secrets Lawsuit**: OpenAI has filed a federal motion to **dismiss Apple's lawsuit** alleging trade secrets theft, arguing that Apple's complaint is **meritless and fails to identify actual protectable trade secrets**. The filing claims Apple’s lawsuit **mischaracterizes normal industry recruiting practices** and former employee actions, framing their leftover system access as Apple's own security failure. The motion contends OpenAI is building hardware **entirely distinct** from Apple's ecosystem. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/06/openai-asks-judge-to-dismiss-apple-lawsuit/))
- **Urgent macOS Patches Resolve Serious Screen Sharing Flaw**: Apple has unexpectedly released **macOS Tahoe 26.6.1, macOS Sequoia 15.7.9, and macOS Sonoma 14.8.9** to fix a critical vulnerability in the Screen Sharing service. The flaw could allow an attacker on the same network to authenticate and **remotely control a Mac without valid credentials**. Users are urged to **install the updates immediately**, as Apple bypassed normal beta testing cycles to address this severe threat. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/06/apples-latest-macos-updates-address-a-serious-screen-sharing-vulnerability/))
- **ChatGPT Unveils Free Unlimited Text Chats and Smarter Models**: OpenAI is upgrading its ChatGPT experience by making the **GPT-5.6 Luna model the default for free users**, complete with **unlimited text chats** and a new **"Think" button** for deep reasoning. Paid Plus and Pro users will receive a smarter, more factually precise **GPT-5.6 Sol model** that dramatically reduces factual errors in finance, medicine, and law. A new **slider** also allows paid subscribers to choose how much thought ChatGPT puts into each answer. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/06/openai-updating-chatgpt-with-a-smarter-gpt-5-6-sol-and-unlimited-free-chats/))
