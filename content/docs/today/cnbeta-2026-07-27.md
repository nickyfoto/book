# CNBeta — 2026-07-27

## Top Story
Changxin Technology (CXMT), China's leading DRAM manufacturer, debuted on the STAR Market with a record-breaking 66.6 billion RMB ($9.2 billion) IPO, surging over 471% on its first day to reach a 3.3 trillion RMB valuation. According to an **[in-depth cnbeta analysis](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570450.htm)**, this marks a major shift in the global memory market by breaking the oligopoly of Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron. This IPO highlights China's aggressive local government investment strategy yielding a self-sufficient player in the semiconductor supply chain, especially as CXMT secures local DUV lithography machines to push its 3D DRAM production.

## Science & Space

This marks the first time such a transmissible tumor has been found in a freshwater species, though researchers emphasize it poses no threat to humans. 

Back on Earth, **[three NASA astronauts safely returned from the ISS](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1570482.htm)** after a 241-day mission spanning 102 million miles. The crew brought back critical research on cancer treatments and advanced computing materials.
