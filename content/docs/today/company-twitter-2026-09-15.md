# Company@X — 2026-09-15

## Signal of the Day
**Google** introduced **Gemini 3.8 Live** and **Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking**, new voice models capable of real-time reasoning, background task execution, and visual understanding. The release signals a major shift toward integrating continuous background reasoning into production voice agents across consumer apps, developer APIs, and enterprise software.

## Key Announcements

**Google DeepMind** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleDeepMind/status/2099907440422830269#m)
Google DeepMind unveiled **Gemini 3.8 Live** and **Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking**, combining fluid, low-latency dialogue with background reasoning and automated tool execution. The models feature 97-language auto-detection, near real-time visual grounding, and built-in **SynthID** audio watermarking. Rollouts begin immediately across Search Live, the Gemini app, Google AI Studio APIs, and Google Workspace enterprise platforms.

**Waymo** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Waymo/status/2099649529268338883#m)
Waymo executed formal commercial agreements with **Nihon Kotsu Taxi** and **GO** to launch a fully autonomous ride-hailing service in **Tokyo in 2027**. This partnership represents Waymo's first international expansion, marrying its driverless system with Japan's primary taxi dispatch network. The autonomous fleet will be accessible directly through both the Waymo and GO ride-hailing apps.

**Liquid Compute** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ronitrjain/status/2099870726719168737#m)
Liquid Compute emerged from stealth with a **\$15 million seed round** co-led by Chemistry and FirstMark Capital. The company filed applications with the CFTC for DCM and DCO status to establish the first regulated orderbook for trading cash and physically settled **compute derivatives**. It is collaborating with Susquehanna, BGC Group, and Wintermute to facilitate OTC compute trading across neocloud providers, AI labs, and lenders.

**Meta** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/finkd/status/2099997096896274533#m)
Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg outlined the company's AI safety and compute strategy, prioritizing **model alignment and user trust** over unconstrained capability races. Zuckerberg revealed Meta delayed releasing its **Muse** model for several months to conduct internal security red-teaming, while committing the vast majority of its infrastructure to serving production workloads. He argued that alignment will be the primary competitive differentiator for agentic models.

**Factory AI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/matanSF/status/2099906259734654984#m)
Factory AI closed a **\$200 million funding round at a \$5 billion valuation** to scale self-improving software engineering agents in enterprise environments. The platform currently supports hundreds of thousands of engineers across companies like Nvidia, Adobe, T-Mobile, and Palo Alto Networks. Capital will be allocated toward applied AI research, product development, and global enterprise expansion.

**Profound** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/dbabbs/status/2099891650285703616#m)
Profound secured a **\$180 million Series D** co-led by Sequoia Capital and Kleiner Perkins to build an enterprise AI marketing platform. The system introduces "Your AI Marketer" alongside a "Context Manager" that ingests brand collateral, emails, and meetings to autonomously investigate market data and execute campaigns. Profound is expanding its applied AI research group to establish domain-specific benchmarks for marketing workflows.

**Helion Energy** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Dkirtley/status/2099890988852412502#m)
Helion Energy finalized an upsized **\$500 million Series G financing round** with participation from pension, crossover, and sovereign wealth funds. The capital will fund technical milestones required to bring Helion's magnetyl fusion generators onto the electrical grid. CEO David Kirtley emphasized that the oversubscribed round reflects institutional confidence in commercial fusion power delivery timelines.

**Open Source AI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/LiamFedus/status/2099896055030501702#m)
Researchers released **Neon**, an open-source materials science model trained on 1,300 Nvidia H200 GPUs using proprietary experimental lab data. Neon surpassed GPT-6 Astra on specialized materials analysis benchmarks covering superconductors, high-strength magnets, and semiconductor materials. The release underscores how tight feedback loops between physical automation labs and open models can outperform general-purpose frontier models on domain-specific tasks.
