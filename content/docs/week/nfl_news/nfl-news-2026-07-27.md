# NFL News — 2026-07-27

## Read First
The 2026 NFL season has officially kicked off as veterans report to training camps, bringing massive coaching carousels and blockbuster offseason acquisitions into focus. With padded practices looming, the spotlight falls squarely on new systems being installed across the league—from John Harbaugh taking over the Giants to Mike McCarthy leading Aaron Rodgers in Pittsburgh.

## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
Rookie quarterbacks and highly touted prospects are taking center stage as early camp reps dictate depth charts. In Cleveland, newly hired head coach Todd Monken is evaluating a highly anticipated quarterback battle, noting that rookie Shedeur Sanders is showing improved decisiveness as he competes with Deshaun Watson. The Giants are heavily focused on the development of sophomore signal-caller Jaxson Dart as he transitions into passing game coordinator Brian Callahan's NFL system. In Jacksonville, two-way phenom Travis Hunter is back to full health and is expected to feature extensively on both offense and defense for Ryan Coen's squad. Meanwhile, Rams rookie quarterback Ty Simpson is already impressing the coaching staff with his eagerness for immediate feedback during 11-on-11 drills.

### Roster Moves & Free Agency
Front offices continue to churn the bottom of their rosters while managing early camp injuries and securing core talent. The Green Bay Packers locked up defensive tackle Devonte Wyatt with a **[contract extension](https://www.packers.com/news/packers-sign-dt-devonte-wyatt-to-contract-extension-2026)** following his career-best five-sack campaign. The Miami Dolphins fortified their edge rush by adding veteran Clelin Ferrell and waiving Kahlil Saunders, while the Las Vegas Raiders signed Patrick Johnson, Buddy Johnson, and Deven Thompkins. Meanwhile, the PUP list claimed several notable starters across the league: the Lions will open camp without their top safety duo of Kerby Joseph and Brian Branch, and the Giants sidelined Sam Roberts, Roy Robertson-Harris, and Thomas Fidone II. The Colts also placed linebacker CJ Allen and wide receiver Alec Pierce on the Active/PUP list.

### Team Beats
Quarterback dynamics and defensive overhauls are dominating local beats as teams hit the grass. In New England, Patriots head coach Mike Vrabel praised newly acquired A.J. Brown's integration into Todd Downing's offense, while shutting down trade rumors surrounding receiver Kayshon Boutte. The Cardinals are shaking up their depth chart early, with Jacoby Brissett slated to take first-team reps over Gardner Minshew following a sharp showing in practice. Philadelphia's Jalen Hurts is embracing his sixth offensive coordinator in seven years, expressing excitement about building chemistry with Sean Mannion and rookie wideout Makai Lemon. Defensively, the Rams' restructured unit is already turning heads as **[Myles Garrett](https://www.therams.com/news/10-observations-from-day-1-of-2026-rams-training-camp-stetson-bennett-myles-garrett-jaylen-watson-kam-kinchens-cam-lampkin)** debuted in team drills and newly acquired cornerback Trent McDuffie described feeling a "homecoming" vibe with familiar faces in the secondary. In Cincinnati, second-year coordinator Al Golden is integrating blockbuster additions Dexter Lawrence and Jonathan Allen to fix a unit that leaked points in 2025.

### League News & Culture
Off the field, the league's top talents are being recognized, with Fred Warner (#54), Derwin James (#50), and Joe Thuney (#49) landing on the NFL's Top 100 Players list. EA Sports announced that Rams teammates Matthew Stafford and Myles Garrett have both achieved coveted 99 overall ratings in **[Madden NFL 27](https://www.therams.com/news/matthew-stafford-and-myles-garrett-in-99-club-for-madden-nfl-27)**. In branding and facilities news, the Bills unveiled new all-gray **['Nickel City' alternate uniforms](https://www.buffalobills.com/news/buffalo-bills-unveil-new-nickel-city-uniforms-leading-into-the-inaugural-season-of-highmark-stadium)** featuring a royal blue "Charge" helmet, while the Giants announced a massive 93,500-square-foot expansion to their training headquarters. The Browns are also leaning into global expansion, partnering with the Italian Federation of American Football to promote flag football ahead of the 2028 Olympics in Los Angeles.

## Storylines to Watch
As padded practices commence, keep an eye on how high-profile veterans acclimate to completely new locales—specifically Aaron Rodgers leading the Steelers and Mike Vrabel establishing his culture with the Patriots. Additionally, the sheer volume of elite players returning from significant injuries, such as Colts quarterback Daniel Jones (Achilles) and Packers pass-rusher Micah Parsons (ACL), will heavily dictate the balance of power in both conferences.
