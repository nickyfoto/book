# NFL News — 2026-08-24

## Read First
With the final week of the NFL preseason officially underway, coaching staffs across the league are making high-stakes decisions to solidify their starting lineups ahead of the looming 53-man cutdown deadline. No decision carries more weight than Cleveland Browns head coach Todd Monken officially naming **Deshaun Watson** the team's starting quarterback for Week 1, shifting their quarterback competition into the regular season preparation phase.
