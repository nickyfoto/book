# NFL News — 2026-07-23

## Read First
Two massive stadium projects reflect the league's next infrastructure boom. The Chiefs and Panthers both unveiled multi-billion-dollar future homes and renovations, setting the stage for decades of regional economic impact.

## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
The Las Vegas Raiders have officially locked up their entire 2026 draft class, getting all 10 rookies under contract before camp. The group is headlined by **[No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza](https://www.raiders.com/news/raiders-sign-first-round-pick-fernando-mendoza-quarterback-indiana-2026-nfl-draft)**, the National Championship-winning QB out of Indiana who threw for 41 touchdowns last season. Elsewhere, former Jets wideout Braylon Edwards strongly praised New York's draft haul under coach Aaron Glenn. Edwards specifically noted how **[first-round edge David Bailey and mismatch-nightmare TE Kenyon Sadiq](https://www.newyorkjets.com/news/notebook-former-jets-wr-braylon-edwards-knows-from-fresh-starts-kenyon-sadiq)** give the team immediate plug-and-play starters with massive upside.

### Roster Moves & Free Agency
Fringe roster shuffling and injury designations are underway as camps open. The Ravens **[waived undrafted rookie QB Diego Pavia](https://www.baltimoreravens.com/news/diego-pavia-ravens-waive-release-training-camp-2026)** to make room on the 90-man roster for veteran center Ethan Pocic. Multiple teams utilized the active/PUP list to stash recovering contributors: the Dolphins sidelined **[CBs Darrell Baker Jr. and Storm Duck](https://www.miamidolphins.com/news/dolphins-make-roster-moves-x5889)**, the Titans parked **[tackle JC Latham and safety Amani Hooker](https://www.tennesseetitans.com/news/titans-place-tackle-jc-latham-safety-amani-hooker-on-pup-list)**, and the Browns placed **[DT Maliek Collins](https://www.clevelandbrowns.com/news/browns-place-dt-maliek-collins-on-active-pup)** on the list. Meanwhile, in Arizona, QB Jacoby Brissett has **[returned to practice](https://www.azcardinals.com/news/as-cardinals-return-to-training-camp-jacoby-brissett-returns-to-practice)** amid ongoing negotiations for a salary raise.

### Team Beats
Training camp injuries are already forcing schematic pivots across the league. In Carolina, a devastating **[right knee injury to second-year OLB Nic Scourton](https://www.panthers.com/news/dave-canales-nic-scourton-going-to-get-tests-on-right-knee-injury-training-camp)** halted practice and required an MRI. The Panthers also shifted **[Monroe Freeling to right tackle](https://www.panthers.com/news/monroe-freeling-is-a-right-tackle-now-and-was-already-being-recruited-robert-hunt-taylor-moton)** to cover depth issues. In Dallas, beat writers are focused on George Pickens, challenging the receiver to **[prove his consistency](https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/no-3-what-s-the-biggest-thing-pickens-needs-to-prove)** while playing on a $27.3 million franchise tag. Re-imagined defenses are also a major talking point; the Steelers are integrating a hard-hitting safety trio of DeShon Elliott, Jaquan Brisker, and Jalen Ramsey under **[new defensive coordinator Patrick Graham](https://www.steelers.com/news/pre-camp-position-previews-safety)**, while the Packers anticipate deploying Micah Parsons in a NASCAR-style sub-package for **[defensive coach Jonathan Gannon](https://www.packers.com/news/inbox-it-s-good-to-see-bo-melton-back-home-july-23-2026)**.

### League News & Culture
Massive infrastructure projects dominated the business wire today. The Kansas City Chiefs unveiled renderings for a **[fully enclosed, $3 billion stadium](https://www.chiefs.com/news/ein-neu-gedachtes-vermachtnis-die-kansas-city-chiefs-stellen-entwurfe-fur-ihr-neues-stadion-vor-dessen-eroffnung-fur-2031-geplant-ist)** in Kansas, slated to open in 2031. Simultaneously, the Panthers **[extended their naming rights with Bank of America](https://www.panthers.com/news/bank-of-america-and-carolina-panthers-extend-naming-rights-for-bank-of-america-stadium-reinforcing-long-term-commitment-to-charlotte-and-the-carolinas)**, pairing it with a $1.3 billion renovation for their current Uptown Charlotte venue. In broadcasting news, **[Jon Gruden will return to the booth](https://www.buccaneers.com/news/jon-gruden-join-ronde-barber-tv-booth-preseason-bucs-chiefs)** alongside Rondé Barber to call the Buccaneers' preseason game against the Chiefs.
