# NFL News — 2026-07-12

## Read First
As training camps loom across the league, teams are dramatically overhauling their offensive identities for the 2026 season. From the Giants building a bruising, Greg Roman-inspired run game to the Steelers constructing a towering wideout tandem for Aaron Rodgers, offensive scheme shifts and front-office maneuvering are dominating the July news cycle.

### Draft & Prospects
The Steelers are banking on Georgia defensive back Daylen Everette, selected 85th overall, to bring high football IQ to their secondary, a trait lauded by defensive coordinator Patrick Graham in **[Five Fast Facts: Daylen Everette](https://www.steelers.com/news/five-fast-facts-daylen-everette)**. Everette reunites with former Bulldogs Broderick Jones and Darnell Washington in Pittsburgh. Meanwhile, New York's ground game will rely heavily on their 10th overall pick, offensive lineman Francis "Sisi" Mauigoa, to implement their new physical style as detailed in **[20 Questions in 20 Days: What does the new offense look like?](https://www.giants.com/news/2026-nfl-season-jaxson-dart-matt-nagy-brian-callahan-greg-roman-cam-skattebo-john-harbaugh)**.
