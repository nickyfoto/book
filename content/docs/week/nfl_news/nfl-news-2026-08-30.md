# NFL News — 2026-08-30

## Read First
In a seismic shockwave on cutdown Sunday, three-time NFL Defensive Player of the Year **Aaron Donald** announced he is ending his two-year retirement to rejoin the Los Angeles Rams for the 2026 campaign, instantly transforming the NFC title race. Donald's dramatic comeback coincided with the NFL's league-wide deadline to reach the 53-man roster limit, an expedited cutdown process moved up by two days to accommodate the Week 1 international opener in Australia between the Rams and 49ers.
