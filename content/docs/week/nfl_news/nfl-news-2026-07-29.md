# NFL News — 2026-07-29

## Read First
The quarterback carousel is dominating the start of 2026 training camps, primarily driven by injuries and shifting depth charts. The Atlanta Falcons are already shuffling their quarterback room as **[QB Tua Tagovailoa is set to miss time with an injury](https://falcons-frontend.pocket.nfl.com/news/qb-tua-tagovailoa-to-miss-time-with-injury)** while **[Michael Penix Jr. is still working toward full medical clearance](https://falcons-frontend.pocket.nfl.com/news/qb-michael-penix-jr-still-working-towards-full-medical-clearance)**, prompting the team to **[sign veteran Cooper Rush](https://falcons-frontend.pocket.nfl.com/news/falcons-sign-qb-cooper-rush-training-camp)**. Elsewhere, teams are settling into new eras, with **[Daniel Jones looking "really good" in his first Colts practice](https://colts-frontend.pocket.nfl.com/news/daniel-jones-looks-really-good-in-first-practice-of-colts-training-camp)** and **[Kirk Cousins officially opening Raiders camp as QB1](https://raiders-frontend.pocket.nfl.com/news/kirk-cousins-2026-raiders-training-camp-qb1-klint-kubiak-fernando-mendoza)** under Klint Kubiak's offensive plan. 

## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
First-round rookies and young prospects are already carving out major roles as pads begin to come on across the league. In Arizona, **[rookie running back Jeremiyah Love is flashing with the first-unit offense](https://www.azcardinals.com/news/jeremiyah-love-with-cardinals-in-pads-and-camp-aftermath)** under Mike LaFleur, while the Panthers' new duo struck quickly as **[Bryce Young connected with Tetairoa McMillan for massive plays](https://panthers-frontend.pocket.nfl.com/news/play-of-the-day-bryce-young-and-tetairoa-mcmillan-connect-for-two-biggest-plays)**. The Saints are taking a more cautious approach with **[first-round pick Jordyn Tyson](https://www.neworleanssaints.com/news/new-orleans-saints-first-round-pick-jordyn-tyson-set-to-open-training-camp)**, managing a pre-draft hamstring injury as they build up his workload. Up north, the Vikings are integrating **[first-round defensive tackle Caleb Banks](https://vikings-frontend.pocket.nfl.com/news/2026-training-camp-observations-caleb-banks)** into their defensive line rotation while managing a crowded quarterback room where J.J. McCarthy and Kyler Murray are battling for reps.

### Roster Moves & Free Agency
Extensions and defensive line maneuvering defined the transaction wire on day one of camps. The Browns locked down their secondary by signing **[Denzel Ward to a two-year contract extension](https://browns-frontend.pocket.nfl.com/news/denzel-ward-signs-2-year-contract-extension-with-the-browns)**, and the Vikings secured their trench play with a **[Brian O'Neill extension](https://vikings-frontend.pocket.nfl.com/news/brian-oneill-contract-extension-2026)**. In Dallas, defensive anchor **[Quinnen Williams made it clear he "definitely" wants to remain with the Cowboys long-term](https://cowboys-frontend.pocket.nfl.com/news/quinnen-williams-definitely-wants-to-remain-with-cowboys-long-term)**. Meanwhile, a notable era seems to have ended in New Orleans, as **[Taysom Hill shared a farewell statement thanking the Saints and the city](https://saints-frontend.pocket.nfl.com/news/taysom-hill-thank-you-saints-new-orleans-mickey-loomis)**. On the fringes of the roster, the Buccaneers added depth by signing **[Ifeatu Melifonwu and Travis Shaw](https://buccaneers-frontend.pocket.nfl.com/news/bucs-sign-ifeatu-melifonwu-travis-shaw)**, and the 49ers inked defensive linemen **[Quinton Bell and K.J. Henry](https://49ers-frontend.pocket.nfl.com/news/49ers-sign-dl-quinton-bell-dl-k-j-henry-to-one-year-deals-more-moves)**. 

### Team Beats
Innovative practice habits and high expectations are driving early camp storylines. Cardinals coach Mike LaFleur has instituted a unique teaching tool, using **[stadium jumbo screens for instant practice replay feedback](https://www.azcardinals.com/news/mike-lafleur-makes-sure-cardinals-have-instant-practice-feedback-with-video)** to coach up the trenches in real-time. In Chicago, the offense is rapidly evolving as **[Caleb Williams looks to raise his game under Ben Johnson's guidance](https://bears-frontend.pocket.nfl.com/news/under-ben-johnson-s-guidance-caleb-williams-looks-to-raise-game-to-next-level)**. Down in New Orleans, General Manager Mickey Loomis is expressing **[heavy optimism regarding the Saints' young core](https://www.neworleanssaints.com/news/optimism-evident-as-new-orleans-saints-open-training-camp-26)**, outlining a specific vision for how the franchise intends to leap from a six-win team to NFC South contenders while actively negotiating an extension with star receiver Chris Olave. Out in Seattle, the defending champion Seahawks are already seeing the benefits of a full offseason for **[speedy wideout Rashid Shaheed](https://www.seahawks.com/news/wr-rashid-shaheed-ready-for-a-bigger-role-in-first-full-season-with-seahawks)**, whose growing chemistry with Sam Darnold was immediately evident in practice.
