# NFL News — 2026-09-12

## Read First

**New England suffered a major setback ahead of Week 2 by placing star wideout A.J. Brown on Injured Reserve** following an injury in their opener, forcing the Patriots to reshape their receiving corps under head coach Mike Vrabel. Meanwhile, front offices scrambled to secure core offensive talent before Sunday's full slate, **locking in multi-year extensions for Chicago running back D'Andre Swift, Green Bay tight end Tucker Kraft, and Las Vegas tight end Michael Mayer.**

Tight ends also cashed in, as **Green Bay extended Tucker Kraft**—who ranks alongside Rob Gronkowski and Mark Andrews as one of three tight ends since 2000 with 100+ catches and 15+ TDs in his first three seasons—in **[Packers sign TE Tucker Kraft to contract extension](https://www.packers.com/news/packers-sign-te-tucker-kraft-to-contract-extension-sept-12-2026)**, while **Las Vegas signed Michael Mayer to a multi-year deal** in **[Raiders sign TE Michael Mayer to multi-year extension](https://www.raiders.com/news/raiders-sign-te-michael-mayer-to-multi-year-extension-2026-nfl-transactions)**. Meanwhile, **New England added 12-year veteran DT DaQuan Jones** to reinforce its interior defense in **[Patriots Sign Veteran DT DaQuan Jones and Place WR A.J. Brown On IR](https://www.patriots.com/news/patriots-sign-veteran-dt-daquan-jones-and-place-wr-a-j-brown-on-ir)**, while teams across the league used practice squad elevations on veterans like Buffalo's Frank Gore Jr. in **[Bills elevate WR Greg Dortch and RB Frank Gore Jr. for Week 1 at Texans](https://www.buffalobills.com/news/bills-elevate-wr-greg-dortch-and-rb-frank-gore-jr-for-week-1-at-texans)** and Minnesota's Kyle Van Noy in **[Vikings Elevate Kyle Van Noy & DeeJay Dallas for 2026 Week 1 Vs.
