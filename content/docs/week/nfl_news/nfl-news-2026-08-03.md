# NFL News — 2026-08-03

## Read First
Across the league, the "real" football season officially began on Monday as teams finally donned full pads, immediately cranking up the physical intensity in the trenches and pushing roster battles into overdrive. The sudden escalation of contact has already tested team depth charts, with several key figures fighting through early camp injuries as the physical toll of summer ball ramps up.

## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
Monday's padded workouts offered a major showcase for the rookie class, headlined by New York Giants linebacker **[Arvell Reese](https://www.giants.com/news/john-harbaugh-arvell-reese-dj-reader-jermaine-eluemunor-francis-sisi-mauigoa-tyrone-tracy-jr-training-camp)**, the fifth overall pick who dominated in rain-soaked drills and drew massive praise from head coach John Harbaugh and veteran teammate DJ Reader. In Cleveland, Browns rookie defensive tackle **[Mason Graham](https://www.clevelandbrowns.com/news/harold-fannin-jr-and-mason-graham-have-big-days-on-browns-first-day-in-pads-on-day-5-training-camp-observations)** consistently disrupted the offensive front, showing exceptional post-snap instincts that head coach Todd Monken praised. Meanwhile, Panthers first-year boundary cornerback **[Will Lee III](https://www.panthers.com/news/play-of-the-day-tetairoa-mcmillan-vs-will-lee-highlights-a-physical-day)** locked horns with standout rookie wideout **[Tetairoa McMillan](https://www.panthers.com/news/play-of-the-day-tetairoa-mcmillan-vs-will-lee-highlights-a-physical-day)**, a high-stakes daily battle that culminated in McMillan boxing out Lee to haul in an explosive game-winning touchdown catch. Additionally, Cincinnati Bengals fourth-round wideout **[Colbie Young](https://www.bengals.com/news/quick-hits-d-line-keeps-flexing-rookie-wr-colbie-young-s-big-leap)** enjoyed a breakout padded session, utilizing his impressive 33-inch vertical leap to secure a highlight-reel sideline catch from Joe Flacco.

### Roster Moves & Free Agency
Front offices were busy securing stars as the Cleveland Browns locked down their secondary by signing five-time Pro Bowl cornerback **[Denzel Ward](https://www.baltimoreravens.com/news/grant-delpit-denzel-ward-contracts-shemar-stewart-broderick-jones-bengals-browns-steelers-first-week-training-camp)** to a historic two-year, $62.2 million extension to make him the NFL's highest-paid defensive back, while safety **[Grant Delpit](https://www.baltimoreravens.com/news/grant-delpit-denzel-ward-contracts-shemar-stewart-broderick-jones-bengals-browns-steelers-first-week-training-camp)** secured a lucrative three-year deal. In San Francisco, the 49ers bolstered their offense by signing wideout **[Deebo Samuel Sr.](https://www.49ers.com/news/morning-reports/morning-report-welcome-back-deebo)** to a one-year contract, immediately getting him back on the practice field alongside newly signed running back Khalil Herbert. The Baltimore Ravens also made a swift depth addition along their offensive line by signing veteran tackle **[Kendall Lamm](https://www.baltimoreravens.com/news/ravens-signing-offensive-tackle-kendall-lamm)** in the wake of tackle Hakeem Adeniji's sudden personal decision to retire. Meanwhile, the New Orleans Saints were forced to place wide receiver **[Ja'Lynn Polk](https://www.neworleanssaints.com/news/new-orleans-saints-first-padded-practice-of-training-camp-was-intensified-workday)** on the reserve/retired list following his unexpected retirement before ever playing a regular-season snap with the team.

### Team Beats
The first day of padded practices sparked intense battles, notably in Detroit where All-Pros **[Penei Sewell](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/detroit-lions-training-camp-day-5-observations-goff-lacy-joseph-abney)** and **[Aidan Hutchinson](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/detroit-lions-training-camp-day-5-observations-goff-lacy-joseph-abney)** went toe-to-toe in a high-octane one-on-one sequence that showcased why they are the class of the league. In Chicago, the Bears' left tackle competition is heating up as a fully healthy **[Braxton Jones](https://www.chicagobears.com/news/bears-training-camp-report-monday-aug-3)** fends off a challenge from former first-round pick Jedrick Wills Jr.. Red-zone efficiency took center stage in Buffalo, where quarterback Josh Allen repeatedly connected with tight end **[Dalton Kincaid](https://www.buffalobills.com/news/what-we-learned-about-the-bills-on-day-4-of-training-camp-2026)** for multiple scores during rain-soaked team drills, proving Kincaid's targeted offseason preparation is paying off. Over in Atlanta, the Falcons are executing a careful quarterback plan, leaning on Jack Strand and Cooper Rush while first-round pick **[Michael Penix Jr.](https://www.atlantafalcons.com/news/falcons-training-camp-quotes-zachariah-branch-tua-tagovailoa-jack-strand)** remains limited to individual work as he recovers from surgery.

### League News & Culture
Off the field, the Tampa Bay Buccaneers made business history by introducing the franchise's first-ever practice jersey patch via an elevated partnership with **[Fifth Third Bank](https://www.buccaneers.com/longform/buccaneers-fifth-third-bank-introduce-first-ever-practice-jersey-patch)**. Meanwhile, excitement builds for the preseason opener in Canton as Carolina Panthers head coach Dave Canales announced **[Kenny Pickett](https://www.panthers.com/news/training-camp-observations-kenny-pickett-to-start-hall-of-fame-game-panthers-cardinals)** will start the Hall of Fame Game against the Arizona Cardinals. At the same time, Carolina players are feeding off the mentorship of legend **[Luke Kuechly](https://www.panthers.com/news/current-panthers-players-grateful-to-be-close-to-greatness-of-pro-football-hall-of-famer-luke-kuechly-bryce-young)**, whose highly accessible, normal presence around the facility continues to inspire both rookies and veterans to chase greatness.
