# NFL News — 2026-07-08

## Read First
The New York Giants have completely reshaped their identity under new head coach John Harbaugh, earning a rare "A+" offseason grade from NFL.com. Through a franchise-altering trade sending Dexter Lawrence to Cincinnati for the 10th overall pick, aggressive free-agent spending, and a heralded draft class, the Giants have positioned themselves as the NFC's ultimate sleeper team heading into training camp.

## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
Rookie trench players are dominating the pre-camp evaluations across the league. In Detroit, first-round pick Blake Miller is locked in an open competition with veteran Larry Borom for the starting right tackle job as Penei Sewell shifts to the left side, per **[TWENTYMAN: 5 position battles to watch heading into training camp](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/detroit-lions-5-position-battles-2026-nfl-training-camp)**. Meanwhile, the Vikings are high on third-round compensatory pick Caleb Tiernan, whose elite initial quickness and high football IQ make him a prime swing-tackle candidate despite concerns over his arm length, highlighted in **[Vikings Film Breakdown: Studying Rookie Offensive Lineman Caleb Tiernan](https://www.vikings.com/news/caleb-tiernan-offensive-lineman-film-breakdown-review-analysis-2026)**. The Buccaneers are already heaping praise on No. 15 overall pick Rueben Bain Jr., noting the Miami edge rusher is processing NFL schemes well beyond his years. In Philadelphia, all eyes will be on first-round receiver Makai Lemon out of USC, who arrives to help offset a major departure. 

### Roster Moves & Free Agency
The wide receiver carousel spun wildly this offseason, headlined by A.J. Brown departing Philadelphia for the Patriots and Mike Evans leaving Tampa Bay to sign with the 49ers. To reload, the Eagles signed speedsters Hollywood Brown and Elijah Moore, traded for Dontayvion Wicks, and brought in Samori Toure, according to **[Eagles 2026 Training Camp Position Preview: Wide Receiver](https://www.philadelphiaeagles.com/news/eagles-2026-training-camp-position-preview-wide-receiver)**. The Bengals built a terrifying defensive interior by trading the 10th overall pick to the Giants for Dexter Lawrence II, pairing him with newly signed veteran Jonathan Allen. The Falcons also made massive sweeping changes under President of Football Matt Ryan and head coach Kevin Stefanski, overhauling the quarterback room by signing Tua Tagovailoa and releasing Kirk Cousins, detailed in **[Bengals 2026 Opponent Preview: Atlanta Falcons](https://www.bengals.com/news/bengals-2026-opponent-preview-atlanta-falcons)**. The Ravens, meanwhile, hope their $112 million investment in Trey Hendrickson will fix a sluggish pass rush after backing out of a deal for Maxx Crosby.

### Team Beats
Defensive health is the defining storyline in San Francisco, where the 49ers are expecting Dre Greenlaw, Fred Warner (ankle), and Nick Bosa (torn ACL) back at full strength to play under new defensive coordinator Raheem Morris. In Los Angeles, the Chargers' offensive line is searching for a starting left guard in a three-way battle between Jake Slaughter, Trevor Penning, and Kayode Awosika to complete the overhaul required for offensive coordinator Mike McDaniel's scheme, noted in **[What’s the Chargers Biggest Position Battle Ahead of Training Camp?](https://www.chargers.com/news/biggest-question-training-camp-guard)**. Across the country, the Saints are hoping for better injury luck from Pro Bowl center Erik McCoy after Kellen Moore's squad added Travis Etienne Jr. and Tyler Shough to the offense. In the secondary, the Seahawks are hosting a wide-open battle for their third cornerback spot to replace the departed Riq Woolen, featuring a mix of rookies like Julian Neal and veterans like Nehemiah Pritchett. 

### League News & Culture
The Rams and the wider NFL community are mourning the passing of legendary cornerback LeRoy Irvin at age 68; the two-time All-Pro was remembered by Eric Dickerson as an ultimate teammate with an infectious smile, as remembered in **[Remembering Rams legend LeRoy Irvin](https://www.therams.com/news/remembering-rams-legend-leroy-irvin)**. The Colts announced that Adam Vinatieri will take his rightful place in their Ring of Honor during Week 6 against the Titans. On the media front, the Chiefs' in-house production team debuted a trailer for "The Lost Game," a documentary exploring Lamar Hunt's barnstorming basketball team from the Dallas Texans era. 

## Storylines to Watch
The 2026 coaching carousel is already dictating the pace of training camp, with John Harbaugh's culture shock in New York, Kevin Stefanski teaming up with OC Tommy Rees in Atlanta, and Raheem Morris bringing his aggressive defensive scheme back to San Francisco. Additionally, how teams integrated blockbuster defensive line trades—specifically Dexter Lawrence in Cincinnati and Trey Hendrickson in Baltimore—will be a major focus as the pads come on.
