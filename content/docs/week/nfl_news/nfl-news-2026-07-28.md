# NFL News — 2026-07-28

## Read First
Training camps are officially opening league-wide, ushering in highly anticipated quarterback battles and new coaching regimes while front offices simultaneously lock up foundational pieces with massive contract extensions. This marks the true start of the 2026 season as pads come on and theoretical depth charts are finally tested on the gridiron.
