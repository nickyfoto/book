# NFL News — 2026-07-01

## Read First
The 2026 NFL offseason has fundamentally reshaped the league's balance of power, defined by blockbuster edge-rusher trades, drastic coaching changes, and high-profile rookie quarterbacks taking the reins. With training camps fast approaching, the written news cycle highlights a league transitioning from theoretical roster building into actual on-field scheme implementation, setting the stage for several fierce positional battles.

## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
The pressure is squarely on the 2026 quarterback class as they bridge the gap from OTAs to training camp. In Las Vegas, No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza is leaning on his experience transferring in college to digest the playbook, focusing on syncing his footwork with his reads rather than forcing highlight-reel throws, per **[Fernando Mendoza tiene humildad, confianza y una misión clara](https://www.raiders.com/news/fernando-mendoza-tiene-humildad-confianza-y-una-mision-clara)**. Meanwhile, Rams No. 13 overall pick Ty Simpson is utilizing off-field audio scripts to master playcalls as he learns from veterans like Matthew Stafford in Los Angeles, detailed in **[Newcomer update: Ty Simpson](https://www.therams.com/news/rams-2026-newcomer-update-ty-simpson)**. On the defensive side, the Jets' No. 2 overall pick, edge rusher David Bailey out of Texas Tech, is drawing rave reviews for his burst and processing speed in a stand-up role. Teams are also aggressively relying on rookies to patch glaring holes; the Browns drafted tackle Spencer Fano ninth overall to anchor a completely rebuilt offensive line, while the Falcons expect third-round receiver Zachariah Branch's world-class 4.35 speed to immediately impact their vertical passing and return games.

### Roster Moves & Free Agency
This offseason featured staggering defensive line shakeups that completely rewrite the AFC and NFC landscapes. The Browns executed a jaw-dropping trade by sending single-season sack record holder Myles Garrett to the Rams in exchange for 2024 Defensive Rookie of the Year Jared Verse and draft capital, noted in **[Cleveland Browns | 2026 Saints opponent profile](https://www.neworleanssaints.com/news/cleveland-browns-2026-saints-opponent-profile)**. The Ravens nearly pulled off a similar blockbuster for Maxx Crosby before backing out due to knee concerns, pivoting instead to sign Bengals star Trey Hendrickson to a massive four-year, $112 million deal. The Raiders, retaining Crosby, loaded up their defensive unit heavily, signing veteran Kwity Paye, trading for premier nickel corner Taron Johnson, and uniting former Georgia linebackers Quay Walker and Nakobe Dean. In New York, the Jets supplemented their edge draft pick by trading Jermaine Johnson to the Titans for massive nose tackle T'Vondre Sweat and signing free agent David Onyemata. 

### Team Beats
Coaching regimes are aggressively installing new schematic advantages before August arrives. In Los Angeles, new Chargers OC Mike McDaniel is meticulously tweaking Justin Herbert's footwork, placing his left foot forward in the shotgun to save fractional seconds on quick deliveries and protect the franchise quarterback from the immense pressure he faced in 2025, highlighted in **[Why Justin Herbert & Rashawn Slater are Among Top Storylines Ahead of Chargers Training Camp](https://www.chargers.com/news/training-camp-dates-justin-herbert)**. The Browns are navigating a fascinating quarterback battle between Deshaun Watson, returning from an Achilles injury, and second-year passer Shedeur Sanders under new head coach Todd Monken. Down in Carolina, the focus is squarely on timing; newly signed center Luke Fortner is spending the summer adjusting to the specific cadence and physical stature of quarterback Bryce Young, according to **[Bryce Young has a new center, and he and Luke Fortner are learning each other](https://www.panthers.com/news/bryce-young-has-a-new-center-and-he-and-luke-fortner-are-learning-each-other)**. The Cowboys, conversely, are banking on offensive continuity, with Dak Prescott declaring playoffs as "the minimum" now that receiver George Pickens has reported to camp on the franchise tag without a holdout.
