# NFL News — 2026-07-17

## Read First
The integration of reigning Defensive Player of the Year Myles Garrett into the Los Angeles Rams' defensive scheme is the most terrifying offseason development, fundamentally altering the NFC West arms race. Defensive coordinator Chris Shula's primary task in training camp is unleashing Garrett, who boasts 83 sacks over the last five seasons, within the Rams' 3-4 structure alongside new cornerbacks.

## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
This year's rookie class is already making waves in pre-camp workouts. In Tennessee, fourth overall pick Carnell Tate has generated massive buzz with spectacular one-handed grabs, setting up a fascinating receiver battle for the Titans. The Cowboys are hoping 23rd overall pick Malachi Lawrence, who posted elite short-area quickness at the combine, can grow into a major edge-rush role for Dallas's retooled defense **[Role Call: Malachi Lawrence can grow into major role in his rookie year](https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/role-call-malachi-lawrence-can-grow-into-major-role-in-his-rookie-year)**. Meanwhile, the Falcons are plotting how to deploy third-round speedster Zachariah Branch on both offense and special teams, though his integration into the passing game alongside Drake London might take time. 

### Roster Moves & Free Agency
Blockbusters and extensions dominated the spring, highlighted by the Jets locking down Breece Hall with a multi-year extension after his 1,000-yard season **[Jets 2026 Training Camp Preview | Breece Hall Leads a Big 3 in the Backfield](https://www.newyorkjets.com/news/jets-camp-position-preview-2026-running-back-breece-hall-isaiah-davis)**. In Baltimore, the Ravens shored up their center spot by signing veteran **[Ethan Pocic](https://www.baltimoreravens.com/news/ravens-sign-ethan-pocic-veteran-starting-center)** to a one-year, $3 million deal with an additional $1.5 million in incentives. The Panthers quietly put together a monster free agency haul, securing top linebacker Devin Lloyd and edge rusher Jaelan Phillips, before circling back to re-sign versatile offensive lineman **[Brady Christensen](https://www.panthers.com/news/panthers-bring-back-brady-christensen)**. Over in the AFC, the Chiefs aggressively re-tooled their ground game by signing former Super Bowl MVP Kenneth Walker III to a three-year deal.

### Team Beats
Offensive line reshuffling is a massive theme this week, notably in Detroit where the Lions shifted All-Pro Penei Sewell to left tackle to make room for first-round rookie **[Blake Miller](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/2026-detroit-lions-training-camp-preview-offensive-line-sewell-mays-ratledge)** at right tackle. In Arizona, the **[Cardinals Roster Reset: Quarterback](https://www.azcardinals.com/news/cardinals-roster-reset-quarterback)** highlights a muddy three-way dance between veteran Jacoby Brissett, Gardner Minshew, and rookie Carson Beck. Over in Dallas, **[Dak Prescott](https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/dak-prescott-every-season-with-cowboys-is-super-bowl-or-bust)** is embracing a "Super Bowl or bust" mentality alongside new defensive coordinator Christian Parker and dynamic rookie safety Caleb Downs. The Eagles are also navigating a fresh offensive system under new coordinator Sean Mannion, with center Cam Jurgens noting the scheme is designed to allow the line to play fast and attack **[Cam Jurgens is 'excited' for the new-look Eagles offense](https://www.philadelphiaeagles.com/news/cam-jurgens-is-excited-for-the-new-look-eagles-offense)**.

### League News & Culture
The league office cracked down heavily on integrity violations, indefinitely suspending **[Cardinals director of college scouting Ryan Gold](https://www.azcardinals.com/news/nfl-suspends-ryan-gold-cardinals-scouting-veteran)** for participating in sports gambling and leaking confidential 2026 draft information. On the entertainment front, Buccaneers quarterback Baker Mayfield is starring in Season 3 of the Netflix hit **["Quarterback"](https://www.buccaneers.com/news/best-of-baker-mayfield-quarterback-netflix-2026-season-3)**, which showcases his fiery trash talk against the Eagles. 

## Storylines to Watch
The rapid integration of high-profile veterans into new defensive schemes—like Myles Garrett in L.A. and the Devin Lloyd/Jaelan Phillips duo in Carolina—will dictate the early balance of power in the NFC. Additionally, multiple contenders are leaning heavily on young, unproven talent in critical spots, with teams like the Lions and Cowboys banking on rookies to anchor premium positions along the offensive line.
