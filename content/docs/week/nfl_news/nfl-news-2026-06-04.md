# NFL News — 2026-06-04

## Read First
The balance of power in the NFC shifted seismically this week as the Los Angeles Rams acquired reigning Defensive Player of the Year Myles Garrett from the Browns in a blockbuster trade for Jared Verse and three premium draft picks. The move, which rippled across division-rival front offices, gives Los Angeles an unmatched pass-rush anchor while sending shockwaves through the league's offensive line meeting rooms.

## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
The Chargers locked up a crucial piece of their future, officially **[Agreeing to Terms with Akheem Mesidor](https://www.chargers.com/news/agreed-to-terms-akheem-mesidor-2026)**, the No. 22 overall pick who racked up 24 career sacks at Miami and West Virginia. Meanwhile, the **[Dolphins signed first-round CB Chris Johnson and fifth-round TE Seydou Traore](https://www.miamidolphins.com/news/dolphins-sign-johnson-traore)**, securing a key defensive back who tallied six interceptions as a senior at San Diego State. In Atlanta, early reviews of third-round speedster Zachariah Branch are glowing; quarterback Tua Tagovailoa noted that the dynamic rookie **[reminds him of his former teammate Jaylen Waddle](https://www.atlantafalcons.com/news/tua-tagovailoa-jaylen-waddle-zachariah-branch-film-comparison)**, setting high expectations for how the Falcons will deploy his after-the-catch playmaking. 

### Roster Moves & Free Agency
In one of the wildest near-misses in recent memory, the Ravens voided a massive trade for Maxx Crosby due to concerns over his surgically repaired knee, pivoting immediately to **[turn their pass rush into a strength](https://www.baltimoreravens.com/news/ravens-pass-rush-trey-hendrickson-zion-young-mike-green-lamar-jackson-late-for-work)** by signing veteran sack-artists Trey Hendrickson and Calais Campbell. Front offices elsewhere were busy locking down their homegrown talent: the **[Packers signed WR Christian Watson to a contract extension](https://www.packers.com/news/packers-sign-wr-christian-watson-to-contract-extension-june-4-2026)**, while the Steelers extended rising edge rusher Nick Herbig and battering-ram tight end **[Darnell Washington](https://www.steelers.com/news/steelers-sign-washington-x1680)** to five-year deals. Out west, the Seahawks rewarded a breakout second season from edge rusher Derick Hall, handing him an **[extension through 2029](https://www.seahawks.com/news/derick-hall-signed-contract-extension-wednesday-this-place-is-really-special-to-me)**, while 49ers cornerstone **[Trent Williams agreed to a two-year extension](https://www.49ers.com/news/trent-williams-knows-retirement-is-closer-but-hes-not-done-yet)**, though the legendary left tackle admits retirement is "creeping closer."

### Team Beats
Organized Team Activities (OTAs) are generating heavy scheme and personnel buzz across the league. In Dallas, wideout **[George Pickens is absent from voluntary OTAs](https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/george-pickens-absent-from-voluntary-otas-expected-at-mandatory-minicamp)** after signing his franchise tag, though the Cowboys fully expect him at mandatory minicamp. Down in New Orleans, running back Alvin Kamara bucked his usual trend of skipping voluntary workouts, making a **[surprise appearance at Saints OTAs](https://www.neworleanssaints.com/news/morning-break-alvin-kamara-back-at-saints-otas-as-offseason-work-continues-new-orleans-news-headlines-6-4-2026)** amid rumors of front-office uncertainty regarding his future. Several major quarterback shakeups are dominating practice fields: the Broncos are getting an early look at newly acquired weapon Jaylen Waddle, who is **[already building chemistry](https://www.denverbroncos.com/news/there-s-a-crystal-clear-vision-wr-jaylen-waddle-off-to-strong-start-as-broncos-begin-otas)** with Bo Nix; the Jets are integrating Geno Smith as their new alpha quarterback alongside ascending receiver **[Adonai Mitchell](https://www.newyorkjets.com/news/jets-wr-adonai-mitchell-riding-high-in-ota-practices-06-04-2026)**; and in Minnesota, a true quarterback competition is brewing between J.J. McCarthy and recent acquisition Kyler Murray, both of whom are **[learning the offense together](https://www.vikings.com/news/kevin-oconnell-ota-wide-receivers-christian-darrisaw)** under head coach Kevin O'Connell.

### League News & Culture
After a 14-year career that included ten seasons, countless records, and a Super Bowl title in Seattle, Russell Wilson posted an emotional video stepping away from the field to **[take a television role with CBS Sports](https://www.seahawks.com/news/seattle-seahawks-russell-wilson-retirement-tribute)**. Meanwhile, off the field, Chargers star Khalil Mack launched a massive **[foundation scholarship program](https://www.chargers.com/news/khalil-mack-foundation-scholarship-program)** to fund college or trade school for 52 high school students in his home state of Florida. On the front-office side, the Lions made a major splash by hiring longtime former Dolphins GM Chris Grier as a **[personnel executive](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/the-daily-drive-si-predicting-huge-year-for-detroit-in-2026)**.

## Storylines to Watch
The arms race for premium pass-rush talent is defining the offseason, punctuated by the Rams landing Myles Garrett and the Ravens pivoting aggressively to Trey Hendrickson and Calais Campbell after a voided trade. Additionally, wide receiver mobility continues to reshape contender hierarchies—with A.J. Brown heading to New England, Jaylen Waddle adapting in Denver, and Christian Watson securing a long-term bag in Green Bay, front offices are prioritizing elite per-target efficiency over traditional cap restraint.
