# NFL News — 2026-07-31

## Read First
The Dallas Cowboys' aggressive all-in rebuild—trading away Micah Parsons to acquire Kenny Clark, Quinnen Williams, Rashan Gary, and moving up to draft Caleb Downs—sets a new, urgent tone in Jerry Jones's front office. Dak Prescott welcomes the pressure, knowing the organization's historic moves signal that they are tired of falling short in the playoffs and are ready to push their chips to the middle of the table. 

## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
The 2026 rookie class is already flashing major potential across the league. The Texans are fired up over first-round C/G Keylan Rutledge, who flattened linebackers in the run game during an impressive debut. In New England, **[Drake Maye Lights Up Training Camp](https://www.patriots.com/news/day-6-blogservations-drake-maye-lights-up-training-camp)**, aggressively targeting his new weapon A.J. Brown and showing no fear of making tight-window throws against the starting secondary. Browns rookies Denzel Boston and KC Concepcion Jr. are showcasing vertical jump-ball abilities catching deep shots from Shedeur Sanders in a crowded quarterback room. Meanwhile, Dolphins first-round pick Kadyn Proctor is settling into the left guard spot, using his massive 6-foot-7 frame to adapt to the speed of the NFL trenches alongside veterans.

### Roster Moves & Free Agency
The Saints locked down their top target, signing wide receiver Chris Olave to a multi-year contract extension to keep him in New Orleans long-term. The Titans made a significant splash by trading defensive lineman T'Vondre Sweat to the Jets in exchange for Pro Bowl edge rusher Jermaine Johnson II, who boldly declared himself a top-10 player at his position. The Vikings signed veteran hybrid safety **[Jamal Adams](https://www.vikings.com/news/jamal-adams-rebuild-vikings-foundation-2026)**, reuniting him with defensive coordinator Brian Flores to rebuild his confidence and fully utilize his box-safety skills. In minor transactions, the Bills signed **[CB D.J. Miller](https://www.buffalobills.com/news/bills-sign-cb-d-j-miller-to-one-year-deal)**, the Broncos added **[CB Sam Webb](https://www.denverbroncos.com/news/broncos-sign-cb-sam-webb)**, the Raiders brought in **[TE Zack Kuntz](https://www.raiders.com/news/raiders-sign-te-zack-kuntz-2026-nfl-transactions-training-camp)**, and the 49ers added **[TE Josiah Deguara and WR Trenton Irwin](https://www.49ers.com/news/49ers-sign-te-josiah-deguara-wr-trenton-irwin-to-one-year-deals-move-moves)**.

### Team Beats
The quarterback carousel is dominating first-team reps. In Minnesota, Justin Jefferson is adapting as J.J. McCarthy and Kyler Murray split first-team reps in a tight battle. The Steelers' offense, now featuring Aaron Rodgers at quarterback and DK Metcalf at wideout, is showcasing high-level red-zone battles against rookie CB Daylen Everette. Over in Philadelphia, Saquon Barkley is starting from scratch learning a new under-center offense under OC Sean Mannion and run game coordinator Ryan Mahaffey. The Ravens are also transitioning, as Lamar Jackson moves to take more snaps under center in Declan Doyle's new scheme, which is designed to open up deep play-action passing. Out west, Chargers OC Mike McDaniel is praising Justin Herbert for stepping out of his comfort zone and throwing with high anticipation.

### League News & Culture
The tragic passing of Cristina Ulbrich, wife of Falcons defensive coordinator Jeff Ulbrich, has deeply affected the Atlanta organization, with owner Arthur Blank and head coach Kevin Stefanski offering their full support. On a celebratory note, the Panthers are honoring legendary linebacker Luke Kuechly ahead of his **[Pro Football Hall of Fame induction](https://www.panthers.com/news/luke-kuechly-has-left-a-trail-of-small-acts-of-kindness-and-large-ones-panthers-pro-football-hall-of-fame)**, with teammates recalling his anonymous acts of kindness and unmatched preparation. In the virtual world, EA Sports' Madden 27 ratings dropped, revealing Jessie Bates as the top safety at a 94 overall, Chiefs rookie WR Xavier Worthy with a 99 speed rating, and CeeDee Lamb topping the Cowboys' roster at 93.

## Storylines to Watch
The wide receiver contract market continues to hover over camps. While Chris Olave secured his extension, Rams star Puka Nacua is refusing to hold out during negotiations, stating the game is his "safe place" and remaining fully committed to practice. We are also seeing unprecedented quarterback and star-player shifting, with massive veteran names like Aaron Rodgers in Pittsburgh and Kyler Murray in Minnesota shaking up established team identities as padded practices begin to separate the true contenders.
