# NFL News — 2026-06-28

## Read First
The spotlight today falls on Pittsburgh's coaching staff, specifically tight ends coach Robert Kugler. With a coaching foundation forged in part by his father and a recent Super Bowl LX run with the Patriots, Kugler brings a balanced, family-first approach and extensive run-blocking expertise to the Steelers' offensive room.

## Highlights by Theme

### Team Beats
The Steelers offered an inside look at their tight ends coach in **[Get to Know: Robert Kugler](https://www.steelers.com/news/get-to-know-robert-kugler)**, detailing his authentic, accountable, and non-screaming coaching philosophy. Kugler credits his father, former Steelers offensive line coach Sean Kugler, and veteran coach James Campen as his primary mentors, noting that he encourages his players to keep family at the forefront of their decision-making. Prior to joining Pittsburgh, Kugler built a strong resume as an assistant offensive line coach, most recently helping the New England Patriots field a top-ranked offense during their run to Super Bowl LX in 2025. His deep roots in offensive line play also include coaching stints with the Texans and the Panthers, where he fondly recalls a dominant 322-yard rushing performance against the Lions in 2022 as his favorite coaching moment. 

## Storylines to Watch
It is worth monitoring how Kugler's extensive background in offensive line coaching translates to his current role overseeing the tight ends in Pittsburgh. Coming off a stint with a dominant 2025 Patriots offense that ranked first in the AFC and third in the NFL in overall production, his run-game expertise will likely be a major asset to the Steelers' blocking schemes and physical identity.
