# NFL News — 2026-09-15

## Read First
Rams head coach Sean McVay announced that two-time NFL Defensive Player of the Year Myles Garrett will undergo knee surgery and be placed on injured reserve, delivering a massive blow to Los Angeles' pass rush ahead of Week 2. This headline injury caps off an intense opening week where contenders across the league are already forced to test their depth following significant roster shakeups and key starter sidelined notices.
