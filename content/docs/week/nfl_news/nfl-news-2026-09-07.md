# NFL News — 2026-09-07

## Read First
The 2026 NFL regular season kicks off with a heavyweight Super Bowl LX rematch as the defending champion Seattle Seahawks host the New England Patriots, while the San Francisco 49ers and Los Angeles Rams take their fierce divisional rivalry international to Melbourne, Australia. With several rosters reshaped by aggressive offseason trades and key injuries, Week 1 will immediately test a wave of debutant play-callers and rookies thrust into critical roles.

## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
Rookies are taking center stage as teams finalize their opening lineups, none with more hype than Seattle's first-round running back **[Jadarian Price](https://www.seahawks.com/news/what-to-watch-in-the-seahawks-season-opener-against-the-patriots)**, who is tasked with replacing Super Bowl MVP Kenneth Walker III. Despite missing the preseason with a leg injury, Price is fully expected to carry a significant load behind an offensive line returning all five starters. Meanwhile, in Charlotte, the Panthers will start rookie **[Monroe Freeling](https://www.panthers.com/news/monday-brew-getting-a-bonus-day-in-before-long-season-regular-season-week-one-chicago-bears)** at right tackle against the Bears after a trial-by-fire training camp that head coach Dave Canales believes has him primed for his NFL debut. The defensive trenches are also welcoming blue-chip talent, as Panthers second-rounder **[Lee Hunter](https://www.panthers.com/news/rookie-diaries-2026-lee-hunter)** credits All-Pro Derrick Brown with teaching him the technical nuances required to transition from college to the pros. Over in Chicago, rookies like first-round safety **[Dillon Thieneman](https://www.chicagobears.com/news/fantasy-will-become-reality-for-bears-rookies-sunday)** and tight end Sam Roush are ready to fulfill lifelong dreams under coordinator Dennis Allen, showing that youth will be served early and often this season.

### Roster Moves & Free Agency
Roster finalization has triggered a flurry of veteran additions and contract security, highlighted by the New York Giants locking up their core with two-year extensions for **[Jameis Winston and Jon Runyan Jr.](https://www.giants.com/news/jameis-winston-jon-runyan-jr-contract-extension-details-signed-2026-week-1-regular-season)** to keep them in town through 2028. The Broncos have fortified their secondary by signing veteran safety **[Taylor Rapp](https://www.denverbroncos.com/news/broncos-sign-s-taylor-rapp-to-practice-squad)** to their practice squad, a move head coach Sean Payton indicated was highly anticipated due to Rapp's versatility and intelligence. In a corresponding transaction, the Broncos released long snapper Luke Basso to accommodate the veteran safety. Up in Cleveland, the Browns added Georgia product **[Zion Logue](https://www.clevelandbrowns.com/news/browns-sign-dt-zion-logue-to-the-practice-squad)** to their practice squad while terminating Tyreak Sapp's contract. Out in the desert, the Cardinals are optimistic about rookie running back **[Jeremiyah Love](https://www.azcardinals.com/news/jacoby-brissett-one-of-5-cardinals-captains-chosen-for-2026)**, who is progressing well from an ankle injury as Arizona monitors its backfield depth ahead of their opener.

### Team Beats
Position battles and major injury adjustments dominate practice notes as teams enter official game week. The Dallas Cowboys suffered a devastating blow with All-Pro guard **[Tyler Smith](https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/tyler-smith-expected-to-miss-4-6-weeks-with-injury)** set to miss 4-6 weeks after undergoing thumb surgery, forcing Dallas to rely heavily on backup T.J. Bass. Bass, whom the Cowboys secured with a second-round tender, has the **[full confidence](https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/cowboys-have-a-ton-of-confidence-in-t-j-bass-filling-in-for-tyler-smith)** of head coach Brian Schottenheimer after allowing zero sacks last year. The quarterback dynamics are equally compelling in Atlanta, where the Falcons named veteran **[Tua Tagovailoa](https://www.atlantafalcons.com/news/falcons-name-tua-tagovailoa-starting-quarterback-week-1)** the starter while Michael Penix Jr. remains inactive, as head coach Kevin Stefanski prioritizes **[Penix's medical recovery](https://www.atlantafalcons.com/news/kevin-stefanski-details-plan-for-quarterback-position-michael-penix-tua-tagovailoa)** from knee surgery over rushing him back. In Baltimore, head coach Jesse Minter named Jovaughn Gwyn the **[starting center](https://www.baltimoreravens.com/news/jovaughn-gwyn-ravens-starting-center-nnamdi-madubuike-teddye-buchanan-tyler-loop)** following Danny Pinter’s season-ending injury, representing a major transition for the Ravens’ offensive front. Meanwhile, the Packers chose five captains, including Jordan Love and Tucker Kraft, as head coach Matt LaFleur prepares Green Bay to **[face Kyler Murray](https://www.packers.com/news/packers-elect-5-captains-for-2026-season)** and a revamped Vikings front.

### League News & Culture
The league is going global to start the season, as the 49ers and Rams prepare for their historic Melbourne kickoff on Netflix, with the **[Faithful breaking world records](https://www.49ers.com/news/morning-reports/morning-report-2026-captains-announced-faithful-break-world-record-in-melbourne)** in cheerleading dance routines Down Under. In domestic news, the Pro Football Hall of Fame has **[overhauled its selection process](https://www.packers.com/news/inbox-it-s-viewed-as-a-bonus-sept-7-2026)**, cutting the committee in half and putting seniors into the general pool—a move that directly impacts the coaching candidacies of Mike Holmgren and Mike Shanahan. Off the field, stars are making a massive impact, as Vikings running back **[Aaron Jones, Sr.](https://www.vikings.com/news/aaron-jones-military-youth-united-heroes-league-2026)** donated fresh kicks to military youth at the United Heroes League in memory of his parents' service.

## Storylines to Watch
The biggest story is how sophomore quarterbacks Drake Maye and Sam Darnold handle the chess match in Seattle’s hostile environment, especially with the Patriots implementing star receiver A.J. Brown and the Seahawks adjusting to Brian Fleury’s new play-calling wrinkles. Another key narrative is whether first-time center starters like Jovaughn Gwyn in Baltimore and T.J. Bass in Dallas can hold up against premier interior rushers under early-season pressure. Additionally, the anticipation surrounding Mike McDaniel’s creative Chargers offense and its potential to propel Omarion Hampton to a rushing title will keep all eyes on Los Angeles’ home opener.
