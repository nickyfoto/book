# NFL News — 2026-05-26

## Read First
OTAs are officially ramping up across the league, with teams like the defending Super Bowl Champion Seattle Seahawks and the 13-4 Jacksonville Jaguars hitting the field with a strict "new year, new team" mentality. This phase marks the first real look at how heavily-hyped rookies and rehabbing veterans will fit into retooled schemes and updated rosters for the 2026 season.
