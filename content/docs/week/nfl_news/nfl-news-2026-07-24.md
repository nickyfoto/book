# NFL News — 2026-07-24

## Read First
The juxtaposition of the Carolina Panthers unveiling massive Bank of America Stadium renovations and Owner David Tepper boldly declaring them a "playoff team" crashed into a devastating Day 1 training camp reality: a torn ACL for standout outside linebacker Nic Scourton. The loss of Scourton, who tied for the team lead with 5.0 sacks last season, forces immediate scramble mode in Carolina's front office and serves as a brutal reminder of the fragility of offseason optimism.

## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
The top of the 2026 draft class is officially down to business, with Las Vegas Raiders No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza embracing a crowded quarterback room that features veteran Kirk Cousins and Aidan O'Connell in **[Fernando Mendoza enters first training camp ready to learn, listen and lean into the grind](https://www.raiders.com/news/fernando-mendoza-enters-first-training-camp-ready-to-learn-listen-and-lean-into-the-grind)**. In Pittsburgh, **[Steelers sign Allar](https://www.steelers.com/news/steelers-sign-allar)** details how the third-round quarterback inked his four-year rookie deal, bringing his 6-foot-5 frame and "effortless arm" to the facility. Meanwhile, in New York, new Giants head coach John Harbaugh is setting massive expectations for Year 2 quarterback Jaxson Dart, installing run-pass options heavily inspired by his time with Lamar Jackson. Down in Dallas, the Cowboys are anticipating an immediate, defense-altering impact from No. 11 overall pick Caleb Downs, who is expected to serve as a versatile piece for Christian Parker's secondary.

### Roster Moves & Free Agency
The Baltimore Ravens made crucial veteran additions, signing center Ethan Pocic—coming off an Achilles tear—to fill the void left by Tyler Linderbaum in **[Ethan Pocic Enters Center Competition Feeling 'Great'](https://www.baltimoreravens.com/news/ethan-pocic-ravens-center-competition-feel-great-achilles-offensive-line)**, while also reuniting with veteran receiver Chris Moore to bolster the depth chart. The New York Jets are handing the reins back to a familiar face after acquiring Geno Smith from the Raiders, setting up a fierce QB2 battle behind him among Brady Cook, Bailey Zappe, and Cade Klubnik as noted in **[Jets 2026 Training Camp Preview | Geno Smith Returns for a Second Act](https://www.newyorkjets.com/news/jets-camp-position-preview-2026-quarterback-geno-smith)**. Reacting to Scourton's injury, the Panthers brought back UFL Defensive Player of the Year Cam Gill and claimed offensive tackle Ryan Hayes off waivers. The Patriots also tweaked the fringes of their roster, placing rookie WR Jimmy Kibble on injured reserve and signing Boston College TE Jeremiah Franklin.

### Team Beats
New head coaches are wasting no time establishing their cultures as camps open across the league. In Arizona, Mike LaFleur is implementing Sean McVay's highly structured four-day practice blocks and brushing off concerns about star pass rusher Josh Sweat opening camp on the PUP list. Over in New England, Mike Vrabel's first camp is already navigating friction; second-round pick Gabe Jacas is a notable holdout due to contract negotiations, while star corner Christian Gonzalez reported to camp amidst his own ongoing extension talks. In Philadelphia, fresh off a Super Bowl LIX victory, Saquon Barkley is working with new OC Sean Mannion to shift toward a more under-center rushing attack, prompting Barkley to study film with Todd Gurley. Meanwhile, the defending Super Bowl LX champion Seattle Seahawks opened camp with a motivated Sam Darnold, who remains frustrated by his own performance in the title game and is aiming for back-to-back rings. Up in Green Bay, the Packers' defense is bracing for an early-season absence from edge rusher Micah Parsons, who is expected to start the year on the PUP list following December surgery. 

### League News & Culture
The NFL continues its aggressive international push, with fans Down Under prepping for the league's first-ever regular-season game in Melbourne, Australia, featuring the 49ers and Rams in Week 1. Speaking of Los Angeles, **[Rams unveil two new alternate uniforms celebrating the franchise’s championship legacy and iconic history](https://www.therams.com/news/rams-alternate-uniforms-celebrating-franchise-championship-legacy)** showcases two gorgeous throwbacks—the 1951-inspired "Classic Sol" and the "Fearsome White" tribute to the 1960s defensive line—to be featured on primetime this season. In transition news, veteran receiver DeAndre Hopkins isn't officially retiring, but he is spending the opening days of Patriots camp shadowing Mike Vrabel's coaching and personnel staff to explore his post-playing career options.

## Storylines to Watch
The integration of massive new coaching hires and philosophical shifts across the league is the defining theme of the summer. From John Harbaugh installing a Lamar Jackson-esque offense for Jaxson Dart in New York, to Mike LaFleur bringing the McVay system to Arizona, the early days of camp will be defined by how quickly returning rosters adapt to sweeping schematic overhauls. Furthermore, the handling of high-profile absences—from Gabe Jacas holding out in New England to Micah Parsons rehabbing in Green Bay—will dictate the early balance of power across both conferences.
