# NFL News — 2026-05-21

## Read First
In a stunning late-career reunion that instantly reshapes the AFC hierarchy, quarterback Aaron Rodgers has signed with the Pittsburgh Steelers for his final NFL season, teaming up with newly minted Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy. Out West, reigning 2025 NFL MVP Matthew Stafford inked a massive contract extension with the Rams after coming off a career-best 46-touchdown campaign.

## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
Rookie minicamps and OTAs are in full swing, offering the first tangible glimpses of the 2026 draft class. In Tennessee, rookie wideout Carnell Tate dominated Thursday's session, reeling in three touchdowns from a noticeably leaner Cam Ward, who is operating under the new regime of head coach Robert Saleh and offensive coordinator Brian Daboll. Up in Detroit, rookie defensive end Derrick Moore is eager to chase quarterbacks, noting in [Moore excited to play alongside Hutchinson](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/moore-excited-to-play-alongside-hutchinson) that his primary goal is to alleviate double-teams for the star pass rusher. The Chiefs formally introduced their massive rookie haul, headlined by first-rounders Mansoor Delane and Peter Woods, who are already strategizing on how their coverage and pass-rushing skills will complement each other. Finally, the Patriots are developing seventh-round quarterback selection Behren Morton, detailed in [Following in the footsteps nothing new for Behren Morton](https://www.patriots.com/news/following-in-the-footsteps-nothing-new-for-behren-morton), to compete for reps behind Drake Maye and Tommy DeVito. 

### Roster Moves & Free Agency
Big money moved across the league today, starting in Detroit where the front office locked down All-Pro linebacker Jack Campbell with an extension detailed in [Lions sign LB Jack Campbell to contract extension through the 2030 season](https://www.detroitlions.com/news/lions-sign-lb-jack-campbell-to-contract-extension-through-the-2030-season) following his historic 176-tackle campaign. The Cowboys also opened their checkbook, making Brandon Aubrey the highest-paid kicker in NFL history with a four-year, $28 million deal featuring $20 million in guarantees. On the open market, the Ravens aggressively upgraded their pass rush by signing Trey Hendrickson, drawing an unsurprised reaction from his former Bengals teammate Joe Burrow. Minnesota supplemented its offense by bringing in former 49ers standout Jauan Jennings on a one-year deal, analyzed in [Vikings Film Breakdown: Studying New Wide Receiver Jauan Jennings](https://www.vikings.com/news/jauan-jennings-wide-receiver-film-breakdown-review-2026), while the Bears bolstered their special teams by adding veteran return specialist Kalif Raymond.

### Team Beats
Scheme overhauls and depth chart battles are defining the early offseason programming across the league. The Jets have completely revamped their coaching staff under head coach Aaron Glenn, bringing in Frank Reich to run the offense while Glenn takes over defensive play-calling duties himself. Over in Cleveland, an intriguing four-man quarterback rotation is unfolding at OTAs between Deshaun Watson, Shedeur Sanders, Dillon Gabriel, and Green. In Las Vegas, sophomore standout Ashton Jeanty is settling into his role as the feature back, adjusting to new offensive coordinator Klint Kubiak alongside veteran Kirk Cousins and rookie quarterback Fernando Mendoza, as outlined in [After a rookie year spent learning the league, Ashton Jeanty now becomes the steady anchor of the Raiders backfield](https://www.raiders.com/news/ashton-jeanty-steady-anchor-of-the-raiders-backfield-2026-season-nfl). Meanwhile, Panthers head coach Dave Canales brought in veteran Darrell Bevell as associate head coach, specifically leaning on Bevell to demand meticulous execution and tough love.

### League News & Culture
Front office stability was the priority in New York, as the [Giants agree to extension with GM Joe Schoen](https://www.giants.com/news/giants-agree-to-extension-with-gm-joe-schoen). Down South, the Titans are officially entering their farewell season at Nissan Stadium, revamping their radio broadcast with former offensive lineman Ramon Foster to commemorate the building's history, as noted in [Titans Announce 2026 Titans Radio Broadcast Team](https://www.tennesseetitans.com/news/titans-announce-2026-titans-radio-broadcast-team). Looking ahead globally, the NFL's approval of 10 international games for 2027 means teams will no longer be able to protect their home slates from overseas scheduling.

## Storylines to Watch
The Rodgers-McCarthy reunion in Pittsburgh immediately dominates the league's narrative, putting a massive spotlight on whether the aging veterans can script a fairy-tale Super Bowl run in the brutal AFC North. Additionally, the sheer volume of high-profile coaching transitions—from Robert Saleh and Brian Daboll running the Titans to Jim Leonhard installing an aggressive 3-4 defense in Buffalo—will make training camp install periods absolutely critical for contenders trying to hit the ground running.
