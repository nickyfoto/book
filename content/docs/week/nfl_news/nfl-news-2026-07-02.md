# NFL News — 2026-07-02

## Read First
The Denver Broncos have dramatically altered the AFC arms race by acquiring star wide receiver Jaylen Waddle from the Miami Dolphins in a blockbuster trade for first, third, and fourth-round picks. This massive acquisition gives second-year quarterback Bo Nix an elite vertical threat with 4.37 speed to pair opposite Courtland Sutton, signaling that Sean Payton's retooled offense is aggressively preparing for a deep playoff run.

## Storylines to Watch
The offensive arms race in the AFC shows no signs of slowing down, with contending teams prioritizing elite weaponry like Jaylen Waddle and aggressively monitoring the Tyreek Hill market to elevate young quarterbacks. Simultaneously, franchises like the Panthers and Eagles are making massive front-office investments in their defensive fronts, viewing physical dominance in the trenches as the necessary countermeasure to these explosive passing attacks.
