# NFL News — 2026-08-02

## Read First
The ramp-up period is over as teams across the league prepare to don pads on Monday, signaling the true start of evaluations for the 2026 season. The stakes are escalating as injuries begin to shape depth charts, most notably in Carolina where rookie wideout Chris Brazzell's season-ending LCL injury forces the Panthers to adapt early.

## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
Top draft selections are dominating the early days of camp, with 2026 No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza drawing praise for his arm talent and work ethic while adjusting to the Raiders' scheme under Klint Kubiak and Mike Sullivan. In Kansas City, 2026 No. 6 overall pick Mansoor Delane hauled in an interception during team drills despite wearing a yellow non-contact jersey as he recovers from a shoulder injury. Meanwhile, the 2025 class is flexing its muscle; No. 2 overall pick Travis Hunter put on a clinic for the Jaguars by intercepting a pass on defense and immediately following it up with a spectacular contested catch on offense. ### Roster Moves & Free Agency
Teams are tinkering with their depth charts as camp evaluations crystalize. The Lions bolstered their linebacking corps by signing veteran Devin White following a leg injury to Jimmy Rolder. The Steelers made a swap in their secondary, signing safety Rayshawn Jenkins to a one-year deal while releasing Darnell Savage. In San Francisco, the 49ers inked running back Khalil Herbert and DL Titus Leo to one-year deals while waiving cornerback Eli Apple, which you can review in **[49ers Sign RB Khalil Herbert and DL Titus Leo](https://www.49ers.com/news/49ers-sign-rb-khalil-herbert-and-dl-titus-leo-one-year-deals-more-moves)**. Finally, the Saints placed 2024 second-round pick Ja'Lynn Polk on the reserve/retired list after he missed the 2025 season with a shoulder injury. 

### Team Beats
Scheme adjustments are creating high-profile camp battles across the league. In Cleveland, Deshaun Watson is digesting head coach Todd Monken's new offense while competing with second-year quarterback Shedeur Sanders for the starting job. The Rams' offense looks terrifying as Matthew Stafford builds an instant connection with Davante Adams, though Los Angeles is managing a lower-half soreness issue for star defensive end Myles Garrett by giving him scheduled rest days. Over in Philadelphia, the Eagles are experimenting with a "three-headed dragon" in their secondary, testing Cooper DeJean at safety alongside cornerbacks Quinyon Mitchell and Riq Woolen.
