# Simon Willison — 2026-08-17

## Highlight
The standout post of the day is Simon's commentary on **404 Media's physical tracking of a bulk book shipment** directly to an Amazon AI training facility. This gripping real-world investigation exposes the physical-world pipelines and aggressive operations driving **AI training data collection** today.

## Posts

**[We Tracked a Shipment of Rare Books. It Ended at an Amazon AI Training Facility](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/17/we-tracked-a-shipment-of-rare-books-it-ended-at-an-amazon-ai-tra/)** · [Source](https://www.404media.co/we-tracked-a-shipment-of-rare-books-it-ended-at-an-amazon-ai-training-facility/)
Simon highlights an impressive piece of investigative journalism where reporters slipped an **Apple AirTag** into a massive order of 1,000 rare books to see who was behind the purchase. The tracker led them to a VGT3 corner of an **Amazon facility in Las Vegas**, complete with an on-the-nose logo of a dinosaur holding a book. Online forums for Amazon workers subsequently confirmed that this specific corner **destructively scans large volumes of books**. Simon links this to a broader, growing trend of price-insensitive, anonymous buyers scooping up physical books to build proprietary datasets, recalling previous reporting on Anthropic's similar book-scanning practices from 2025.

**[Qwen 3.8 27B scores 52 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/17/qwen-38-27b-scores-52/)** · [Source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/qwen3-8-27b)
Simon flags a major performance milestone for **Qwen 3.8 27B**, which scored a **52 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index**. This score remarkably matches **GPT-5.6 Luna (max)** and sits only a single point behind massive frontier architectures like GLM-5.2 (753B parameters) and DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (1.6B parameters). For developers tracking the rapid evolution of local LLMs, this benchmark confirms that this relatively compact, open-weight model represents a **truly astonishing achievement** in efficiency and intelligence.
