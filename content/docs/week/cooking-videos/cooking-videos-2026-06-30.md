### Food Science & Tips
The traditional Shandong pork tenderloin tutorial offers a fascinating breakdown of the science behind meat tenderization. The chef reveals that soaking the pork slices in a baking soda solution for 20 minutes serves a dual purpose: it tenderizes the proteins and dissolves residual blood, resulting in a beautifully clean, white piece of meat. Furthermore, he expertly demonstrates how vigorously mixing egg whites and starch into the pork creates a protective moisture barrier, ensuring the meat remains incredibly juicy during the high-heat stir-fry.
