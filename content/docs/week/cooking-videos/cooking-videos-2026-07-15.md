### Food Science & Tips
The dumpling masterclass by 老东北美食 offers brilliant kitchen mechanics for balancing texture and flavor in vegetarian fillings. The host strictly warns against squeezing blanched vegetables completely dry with your cheesecloth; retaining a little moisture is critical to prevent the filling from developing a dry, "firewood-like" texture. Furthermore, he advises seasoning the filling with just a touch of Sichuan peppercorn powder rather than heavy, complex blends like 13-spice, ensuring the delicate natural umami of the mushrooms is highlighted rather than entirely masked.
