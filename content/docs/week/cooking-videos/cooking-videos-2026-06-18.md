### Food Science & Tips
James shares a fantastic kitchen tip for preparing raw bean sprouts: always give them a quick blanch rather than just salting them directly, which effectively eliminates their unpleasant raw bean flavor (豆腥味). Meanwhile, Chef Chen offers a brilliant prep tip for tomatoes: after blanching them to remove the skin, always remove the green core, as leaving it in will turn your soup unpleasantly sour.
