### Restaurant & Travel
Take a delicious walk down memory lane with [詹姆士官方專屬頻道](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lHnLjZANluY) in a video where Chef James hosts a fun tasting of iconic Taiwanese snack foods that have been around for over 40 years. He dives into the quirky culinary history of nostalgic treats, such as how a beloved oyster omelet-flavored potato chip was originally intended to be Hiroshima okonomiyaki flavor, alongside other classics like coconut cookies and Kuai Kuai (乖乖). It is a highly entertaining watch if you want to explore Taiwan's rich snacking culture and enjoy some lighthearted food history.
