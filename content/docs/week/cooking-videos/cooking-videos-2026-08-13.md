### Food Science & Tips
Chef James shares a brilliant technique to prevent sticking and elevate flavor: **sear your chicken skin-side down on low heat first** in a hot, lightly greased pan to render its natural fats, creating a beautiful crispy crust and a natural non-stick barrier. He also reveals an aromatic hack—if you are running behind, **pour your Chinese cooking wine around the sizzling outer edges of the pan** at the very end of cooking, allowing the intense perimeter heat to instantly vaporize the alcohol and release a massive wave of fragrance into the meat. For the perfect noodle sauce, Chef Chen explains that the tianmian and soybean pastes must be **fried ("Zha") until the water is completely gone**, signaled by small, uniform bubbles rising to the surface, which removes any raw bitterness and concentrates the paste's natural sugars. Finally, he recommends **rinsing your freshly boiled noodles in cold, pure water** to stop the cooking process, keeping them incredibly bouncy and chewy for a hot day.
