### Food Science & Tips
When working with pre-packaged store-bought yellow noodles, Chef James shares a crucial prep tip: always boil the noodles briefly before introducing them to the wok in order to strip away any unknown, stale oils lingering from the commercial packaging. For the same dish, he also advises cutting water spinach diagonally so its shape mirrors the noodles and the exposed surface area absorbs the savory Shacha sauce much more effectively. If you are experimenting with Thai eggplants for the first time, be aware that their dense, fibrous texture makes them slower to soften in the pan and naturally a bit bitter. Generously salting them for 20 minutes and then washing them thoroughly with clear water before cooking draws out a subtle sweetness and ensures the dish does not become overwhelmingly salty.
