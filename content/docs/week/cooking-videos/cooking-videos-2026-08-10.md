### Recipes & Tutorials
In this delightful recipe tutorial, chef Masa of **[MASAの料理ABC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8TAcQjpFxZI)** details how to build these gorgeous, layered peach cheesecake cups. His standout technique involves **poaching premium Japanese peaches with their skin on** in a mixture of sake, water, sugar, and lemon juice for exactly three minutes, which beautifully preserves the fruit's bite while releasing a gorgeous pastel pink hue into the syrup. After letting the peaches marinate overnight in the fridge, the cups are built with layers of buttery crushed cookies, a luscious sugar-sweetened cream cheese and heavy cream mixture, and the sake-infused peaches prepped in two cuts—small cubes for the structural layers and thin wedges for a picture-perfect top. Masa makes sure to **drizzle the sake-infused poaching syrup** back into the cups for a deep, rice-wine aroma, finishing it off with a striking sprig of fresh rosemary.
