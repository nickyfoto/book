# Cooking Videos — 2026-09-10

## Watch First
If you only have time for one video today, Chef James' **[簡單版的水煮牛](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oRnquRjcfFM)** on **詹姆士官方專屬頻道** is an absolute must-watch masterclass on culinary technique. He breaks down how to transform budget-friendly beef into melt-in-your-mouth tender slices by slicing strictly against the grain, sealing moisture with an oil coat, and gently poaching off the heat before scalding with a fragrant spiced chili-peppercorn oil.

### Food Science & Tips
Understanding meat structure is key to kitchen success, and Chef James (**詹姆士官方專屬頻道**) in **[簡單版的水煮牛](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oRnquRjcfFM)** explains how slicing perpendicular to muscle fibers cuts through tough connective tissue, while locking in liquids with a neutral oil barrier prevents proteins from losing juice during cooking. Complementing this, **老东北美食**'s tutorial **[過年了，教妳醬牛肉好吃的做法](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-eIN11NX79Y)** breaks down beef shank anatomy for braising, showing why choosing yellow cattle shank with thin fascia layers yields a beautifully marbled, gelatinous slice.
