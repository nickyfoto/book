### Recipes & Tutorials
For those looking to master fundamental flavor building, 老东北美食 provides a brilliant, fail-proof guide to scallion oil noodles in **[葱油拌麪，根根爆香！](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l09l9NpHoro)**. The creator shares invaluable sensory kitchen cues—like rendering the scallion greens low and slow until they turn a dark green and make a distinct, crispy rustling sound in the pan—to yield a glossy, umami-packed sauce loaded with light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, and sugar. 

From the same channel, **[雙煎土豆絲餅，好吃停不下來！](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lfjkRIdsE5Y)** is a masterclass in achieving the ultimate crunch for breakfast. It turns just two shredded potatoes and a light dusting of starch into a gorgeously golden, double-pan-fried pancake that you finish with an aromatic hit of traditional spiced salt. 

Finally, Rosalina's Kitchen 蘿潔塔的廚房 brings an appetizing seafood main to the lineup with **[自己做的塔塔醬，健康又美味！](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I9sp5UhJ4hI)**. This recipe is a must-watch if you are looking to pair a tender, juicy pan-seared salmon steak with a bright, refreshing, and crunchy homemade tartar sauce.
