### Food Science & Tips
The Sachima video also doubles as an amazing food science explainer for pastry enthusiasts. The chef provides a brilliant breakdown of five different categories of dough used in Chinese cooking—including leavened, unleavened, warm-water, and oil-based doughs—and details the precise chemical and physical leavening agents that make them work. They also explain the crucial importance of resting the Sachima dough at exactly 40 degrees for three hours; this allows the leavening agents to volatilize properly before frying at a meticulously controlled 160 degrees.
