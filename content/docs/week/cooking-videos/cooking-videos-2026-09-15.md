### Food Science & Tips
Master Chef Chen highlights two vital seafood prep rules: thoroughly scraping off the silvery scale layer and purging the dark blood line along the spine, both of which are non-negotiable to eliminate fishy odors. He also shares a brilliant spice tip—dry-toasting whole star anise, Sichuan peppercorns, cloves, and fennel in a warm skillet or microwave to reactivate essential oils before grinding them into fresh five-spice powder. Over on James’s channel, he notes that seasoning the tomato, onion, and garlic salsa base first allows the harsh raw bite to mellow before gently folding in delicate avocado cubes right before serving to prevent oxidation and browning.
