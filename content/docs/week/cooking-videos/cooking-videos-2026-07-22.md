### Food Science & Tips
The Ox-Tongue Pastry tutorial drops a couple of fantastic baker's secrets, specifically highlighting how the addition of maltose syrup (饴糖) in the dough acts as a humectant to lock in moisture. More importantly, the chef offers a crucial tasting tip: unlike many Western baked goods that are best served warm, traditional Chinese pastries should be lightly covered and allowed to rest at room temperature for a day or two so the crust and filling flavors can perfectly meld together before eating.
