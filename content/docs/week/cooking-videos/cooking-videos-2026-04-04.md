### Food Science & Tips
Two videos offered fantastic, practical kitchen science today. ShangshiKitchen highlighted how dusting beef shanks in seasoned flour before pan-frying serves a dual purpose: it accelerates the Maillard reaction for a deeply flavorful crust, and as the meat simmers, the starch slowly releases to naturally thicken the stew without the need for a clumpy cornstarch slurry. For wok-style cooking, 詹姆士 shared a crucial prep technique: if you want maximum flavor in a stir-fry, you must physically smash your scallions before cooking to crush them, which allows their pungent, aromatic oils to rapidly infuse into your cooking fat.
