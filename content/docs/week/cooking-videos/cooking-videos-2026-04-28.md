### Food Science & Tips
If you struggle with soggy, watery stir-fried greens at home, the chrysanthemum greens video by 老东北美食 is essential viewing. The chef demonstrates a fantastic moisture-control technique: applying a thickening broth to the stems first and adding a cornstarch slurry at the very end, which mimics a blanching effect, locks in the oyster sauce flavor, and keeps the final plate perfectly dry. The video also emphasizes managing oil temperatures, warning cooks not to add the garlic to overly hot oil to prevent it from becoming unpleasantly bitter.
