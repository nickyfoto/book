### Food Science & Tips
During his tasting, Chef James shares a brilliant flavor tip for capturing the robust essence of a traditional pan-fried leek box (韭菜盒) in a lighter format. He explains that by wrapping the classic filling—nutritious, fiber-rich leeks, glass noodles, and dried tofu—directly inside a pan-fried egg and adding **thick soy paste or chili paste**, you get all the satisfying savory flavors without the heavy, oil-soaked dough of a fried wrapper.
