### Recipes & Tutorials
In **[【國宴大師•咖喱鮑魚】](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IxpP0UBtVBY)**, the esteemed culinary masters of the **老饭骨** channel share their **gold-medal-winning Curry Abalone recipe**, which relies on a slow-simmered foundation of old hen, old duck, and cured ham to infuse the naturally flavorless abalone with deep, meaty savoriness. They reveal that **proper abalone prep is key to its final texture**: canned abalone requires a four-to-five-hour pre-steam, while fresh large abalone should be pressure-cooked for 20 minutes. The dish is finished by **reducing the braising liquor over high heat** to draw out the bone marrow and fully concentrate the flavors, followed by a light starch slurry to create a gorgeous, glossy glaze that clings to the **tender, incredibly glutinous abalone**.

### Food Science & Tips
The video highlights a **critical kitchen science rule: never cook curry in an iron wok**, as a chemical reaction between the metal and the spices will turn your beautiful sauce a dark, unappealing black. Instead, home cooks should **always opt for stainless steel or aluminum cookware** to preserve the bright, golden-yellow color of the curry. Additionally, the masters demonstrate that **slow-simmered poultry and cured ham release enough natural sodium and rich glutamates** during the hours of braising that there is absolutely no need to add external seasoning or salt.
