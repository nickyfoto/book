### Food Science & Tips
James explains that his "three-stage oil pouring" method gradually builds heat over cold aromatics like garlic and ginger, releasing a much deeper, sustained aroma without burning the ingredients or letting the oil cool too fast. On the sauce-making front, Master Chen shares a crucial emulsion rule: always stir sweet bean sauce gradually into sesame paste—never the other way around—to prevent the fats from separating and turning grainy. Lastly, Master Chen advises tearing lettuce by hand and simply pouring dressing over the top right before serving, rather than tossing it, which keeps the greens crisp and prevents them from weeping water.
