### Food Science & Tips
There is a brilliant texture manipulation tip in the salted beef video: after the cooked beef shank finishes its long soak, it is rolled extremely tightly in plastic wrap to resemble a sausage. This removes the air and compresses the meat as it cools, resulting in a perfectly firm, springy, and chewy texture that holds its shape beautifully when sliced cold. Additionally, the clam tutorial demonstrates a smart aromatic extraction technique by boiling garlic and chili in water for 30 seconds before adding the clams, ensuring the aromatics fully infuse the steaming liquid.
