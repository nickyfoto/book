### Food Science & Tips
In the Zha Jiang Mian tutorial, the chefs share a brilliant preparation tip: always remove the skin from your pork belly, because leaving it on will cause the rich meat sauce to harden unpleasantly once it cools down. Additionally, the fried shrimp video emphasizes the crucial mechanics of a traditional "Wan Zhi" (bowl sauce) for coating fried foods. A proper sweet-and-sour glaze must be highly concentrated and minimal in volume so that it entirely clings to the exterior of the shrimp without pooling on the plate, which ensures the crust stays shatteringly crisp rather than turning soggy.
