### Food Science & Tips
If you have ever wondered why rich sauces sometimes slip right off your stir-fried meats, this video offers a brilliant breakdown on the science of velveting: using egg white and starch for your initial marinade creates a tacky surface that beautifully grips the final sauce, a method the chef notes is far superior to modern shortcuts that rely heavily on oil. The chef also demonstrates how adding a splash of yellow wine acts as the perfect solvent to break up and disperse thick soybean paste and sugar in a screaming hot wok.
