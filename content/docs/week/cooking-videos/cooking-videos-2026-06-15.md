### Food Science & Tips
The standout kitchen tip of the day comes from the 老东北美食 cold salad video, which solves the common textural problem of unpleasantly dry, stiff tofu skin. By quickly blanching the shredded dry tofu for just half a minute in water mixed with about 3 grams of baking soda (alkali powder), the tofu instantly transforms, yielding a smooth, tender bite that won't taste dry and will perfectly soak up the savory dressing.
