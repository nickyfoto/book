### Food Science & Tips
While demonstrating his egg custard, the chef in the 老东北美食 video drops some excellent technical kitchen wisdom regarding temperature control. He explains that using 40-50°C warm water and pre-heating your steaming plate ensures the eggs cook evenly from the bottom up, while the high steam heat naturally bursts any surface bubbles from whisking to create a perfectly smooth finish.
