### Food Science & Tips
In their braised pork tutorial, [老式東北壇肉嫩如果凍！炖肉香就用老配方！酥爛入味，拌飯香哭了](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sNYatZ4MZbM), the host drops some excellent culinary wisdom regarding oxidation. They explain that simmering the caramel-coated meat in a traditional jar rather than an iron pot is crucial, as the iron will cause the meat to oxidize and turn unappetizingly black over subsequent days. The video also shares a brilliant flavor-building tip: using a mere 2 grams of fermented bean curd sauce is enough to add a rich, fragrant depth and instant color enhancement without overpowering the dish's delicate sweetness.
