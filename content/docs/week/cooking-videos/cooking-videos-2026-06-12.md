### Food Science & Tips
In his sweet and sour ribs tutorial, the host from 老东北美食 highlights a highly practical way for home cooks to trigger the Maillard reaction without the hassle and oil waste of a deep fryer. By carefully pan-searing blanched ribs in a wok over low heat for about four minutes—rotating the pan to ensure even contact—the meat develops a beautifully deep, roasted crust and intense savory aroma before any braising liquids hit the pan.
