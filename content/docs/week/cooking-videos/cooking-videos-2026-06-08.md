### Food Science & Tips
A fantastic culinary tip highlighted in two separate recipes today is the use of Gardenia fruit (黃梔子) as a powerful, natural food dye. Whether you are cracking it open to tint the boiling water for chicken skin or infusing a savory tofu braising liquid, this traditional Chinese ingredient imparts a stunning, appetizing golden-yellow hue without interfering with the dish's fundamental flavor profile.
