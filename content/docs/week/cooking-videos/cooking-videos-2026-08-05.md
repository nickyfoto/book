### Food Science & Tips
In his recipe breakdown, Chef Chen explains the fascinating science behind salted green Sichuan peppercorns, which deliver an intensely fresh, citrusy aroma but carry a much lower level of mouth-numbing heat compared to dried varieties. Crucially, he reveals that these fresh peppercorns act as a natural preservative, allowing the pork belly and green bean gravy to be stored safely in the refrigerator for days. Over in the **[老饭骨](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Ar32umP72Q)** kitchen, the masters highlight a vital technique for braised noodles: after layering the raw noodles on top of the chicken, you must never stir them immediately, allowing them to gently steam and absorb the rising juices without turning into a clumpy, starchy mass.

The team at **[老饭骨](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Ar32umP72Q)** delves into Shandong's chicken-obsessed dining culture, discussing local variations like Linyi stir-fried chicken, Zaozhuang spicy chicken, and the famously tender Dezhou braised chicken.
