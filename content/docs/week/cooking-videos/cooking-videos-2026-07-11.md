Today's watch list is packed with practical kitchen wisdom, starting with a brilliant frying tip from 老东北美食: sprinkling salt directly into hot oil before searing your fish ensures the delicate skin won't stick to the wok. We also get a great lesson in flavor balancing, learning that frying soybean paste removes fishy odors far more effectively than standard cooking wine, while Master Chen shares that the soaking liquid from dried scallops was historically utilized as a powerful, natural umami enhancer long before the invention of MSG.
