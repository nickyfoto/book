### Food Science & Tips
In her fried fish tutorial, 小高姐的 Magic Ingredients demonstrates a brilliant food science trick for creating a shatteringly crisp crust. By combining two tablespoons of white vinegar with baking soda, baking powder, and dry flour, an immediate chemical reaction creates aerating micro-bubbles in the batter. Frying the fish at a high 190°C temperature immediately sets this bubbly crust and evaporates the vinegar entirely, ensuring no sour taste remains in the finished dish.
