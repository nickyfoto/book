### Food Science & Tips
In [鸡肉串](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NzSjPDPcGII), Magic Ingredients dives into the culinary science of fruit-based meat tenderizers. The video explains that unlike kiwi, peach, or pineapple—which contain potent enzymes that can easily strip meat of its elasticity and turn it to mush—pear acts as a remarkably gentle tenderizer that softens chicken breast while maintaining a satisfying, bouncy texture. As a bonus, marinating the chicken with pear for an hour (or overnight) imparts a delightful, subtle fruity sweetness and aroma to the finished skewers.
