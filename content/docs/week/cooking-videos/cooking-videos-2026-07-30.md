### Food Science & Tips
Chef James drops a fantastic piece of practical kitchen science regarding pan dynamics: because long, spindly vegetables like water lotus never lay perfectly flat in a standard pan, they will always heat unevenly if you try to stir-fry them from a raw state. Quickly blanching them ensures uniform heat distribution, and they will continue to gently cook via residual heat once removed from the water. He also notes a vital flavor-building tip: always slightly mash your pickled berries before adding them to the pan, as this releases their uniquely salty, sweet juices and allows you to perfectly balance the dish with just a touch of sugar.
