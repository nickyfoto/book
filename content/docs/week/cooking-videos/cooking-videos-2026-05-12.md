### Food Science & Tips
In the Crispy Sliced Pork video, the chef from 老饭骨 drops some fantastic culinary science, explaining how cutting pork belly on a bias (rather than straight down) ensures the perfect thickness and texture for frying. He also breaks down the physics of wok-frying batter: an overly thick starch coating will become doughy and unappetizing, while a batter with poor starch absorption will simply slip right off the meat into the hot oil.
