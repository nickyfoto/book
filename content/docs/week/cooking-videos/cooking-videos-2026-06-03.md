# Cooking Videos — 2026-06-03

If you only have time for one video today, you absolutely must check out the **[Scallion Focaccia](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uJkUPPu_YPc)** by *小高姐的 Magic Ingredients*. ## Highlights by Theme

### Recipes & Tutorials
Over on the *Costco 好市多* channel, Chef Li Sheng-han shares a hearty **[Soybean Paste Stewed Beef Ribs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5_5-zEnEMrA)**, where he sears fatty American beef ribs to build a crust, uses the rendered tallow to sauté aromatics, and simmers the meat with Korean chili paste and fresh apple to naturally tenderize it. The same channel also features an elegant **[Garlic Thick Soup Braised Red Fish](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WAzcaWhDeYw)**, walking viewers through smart pan management by frying tofu first to establish a flavor base, then braising a beautifully scored whole red tilapia in the residual oil with garlic, ginger, and mushrooms. 

### Food Science & Tips
*小高姐的 Magic Ingredients* drops some excellent baking science in her focaccia video, explaining how using high-gluten flour and an 80% water-to-flour ratio allows the dough to develop strong structure automatically through a simple "stretch and fold" technique, rather than intense kneading. Additionally, in the *Costco 好市多* red fish tutorial, the chef shares great practical tips for checking fish freshness via clear eyes and using two spatulas—one at the head, one at the tail—to safely transfer a delicate whole braised fish to a serving platter without breaking it.

### Everything Else
