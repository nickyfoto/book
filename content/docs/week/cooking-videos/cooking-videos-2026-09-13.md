### Food Science & Tips
Chef Wang Gang highlights critical kitchen science in his tutorial, explaining that pressure-cooking beef brisket in pre-salted water for 35–40 minutes cuts cooking time by more than half compared to a 90-minute braise while driving base seasoning directly into the beef fibers. He also demonstrates how reducing strained beef broth with rendered lard over high heat creates a naturally thick, milky emulsion without requiring a starch slurry. On the prep side, ShangshiKitchen shares an essential tip for acidic sauces: avoid using iron cookware when simmering fresh tomato reductions to prevent a metallic off-flavor, and shock boiled rice noodles in multiple ice-water baths to flush surface starches so they remain bouncy and non-sticky.
