### Food Science & Tips
This week is packed with essential culinary science, starting with MASA's tip to **steam rather than boil pumpkin** to preserve its dry, fluffy texture and concentrate its natural sweetness. On the savory side, James explains that **meat must be massaged with water before adding egg or starch**, as starch creates a protective barrier that blocks the meat from absorbing moisture. He also shares a brilliant claypot trick: **layering wet enoki mushrooms over raw garlic** allows the mushrooms' natural moisture to steam the pot, preventing the garlic from burning without adding a single drop of water.
