## Highlights by Theme

### Recipes & Tutorials
In **[家常豆瓣魚，不用油炸肉嫩更香！老廚的燉魚配方，拌米飯香到跺腳](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3dryzVhvMaY)**, the channel 老东北美食 delivers an absolute masterclass in braising a whole fish. The chef emphasizes a crucial technique of making deep, bone-grazing cuts along the fish to ensure the ginger-scallion marinade and the rich braising liquid penetrate deeply into the meat. After pan-frying the fish until perfectly golden, it is simmered in a highly appetizing sauce built from Pixian doubanjiang, meat broth, sugar for umami, and rice vinegar. The result is a mouthwatering balance of savory, mildly sweet, slightly sour, and spicy flavors that practically demands a side of white rice or steamed buns. 

### Everything Else
If you are looking to step up your kitchen efficiency, the meal prep advice in **[這款百搭 ＃番茄肉醬 一次做好再分裝冷凍，要吃時加熱一下就搞定！](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ixnkD5-IzsQ)** by 詹姆士官方專屬頻道 is incredibly practical. The host shares an easy, highly versatile Italian-style tomato meat sauce recipe inspired by an authentic version he learned from an Italian friend during a student camping trip. What makes this tutorial stand out is his smart tip to leave the salt out of the base recipe; this allows you to make a massive batch, freeze it in convenient portions, and easily adapt the seasoning later whether you are tossing it with pasta or using it as a savory stir-fry base.
