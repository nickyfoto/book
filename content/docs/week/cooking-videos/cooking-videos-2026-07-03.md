### Recipes & Tutorials
**[Garlic Spicy Chicken Feet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6jYqbx40vIM)** by 老东北美食 delivers a mouth-watering tutorial on making the ultimate savory, sticky chicken feet. The chef shares essential kitchen secrets, like washing dry spices (such as star anise and cinnamon) before frying to remove dust, and explains that simmering for 20 minutes yields a satisfying cartilaginous crunch, while a 30-minute braise results in fall-off-the-bone tenderness. He also demonstrates finishing the dish with a fragrant garlic and chili stir-fry, noting that adding a touch of sugar is a crucial rule for braising to boost umami.
