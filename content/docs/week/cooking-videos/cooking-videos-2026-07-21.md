### Food Science & Tips
Master Chen shares an invaluable prep secret for freshwater fish: meticulously scraping away the outer slime and the black film inside the belly is absolutely crucial to eliminating its notoriously muddy flavor. On the vegetable front, the cold cabbage recipe proves that briefly curing raw chopped cabbage with salt for three minutes draws out excess moisture, resulting in a significantly crunchier texture than traditional blanching methods.
