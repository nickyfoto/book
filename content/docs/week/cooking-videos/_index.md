## Week in Review
This week's culinary content was dominated by a masterclass in regional Chinese cooking, with veteran chefs from **老东北美食**, **鲁菜老厨陈宗明**, and **老飯骨** dissecting Shandong (Lu) and Northern classics. ### Food Science & Tips
Science-minded cooks will appreciate **[詹姆士官方專屬頻道](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xYshWrlmmls)**'s breakdown of how salting raw fish skin triggers salt osmosis to draw out volatile, fishy compounds. We also got a masterclass in frying from **[鲁菜老厨陈宗明](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oTvElmYXk8Y)**, who explained that eggplant requires a dual-stage fry to form a protective starch shell and evaporate surface moisture. Lastly, Chef Chen shared a brilliant trick in **[醬燜黃花魚](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T6svxoB8fbw)** of briefly blanching fish in boiling water before braising to remove skin impurities and eliminate muddy odors without the mess of deep-frying.

### Restaurant & Travel
This week offered a rich journey into culinary heritage, starting with **[老飯骨](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=luQ9ykesDDs)** as master chefs recalled their careers at the Great Hall of the People while attempting to preserve vanishing, labor-intensive classics. They also explored Anhui's deeply savory, fermented specialty, Chou Gui Yu (Smelly Mandarin Fish), highlighting its cultural importance in local restaurants across Anqing. Meanwhile, **[詹姆士官方專屬頻道](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3l80XmuOxbM)** took us on a nostalgic trip through Taiwan’s schoolyard history with the iconic, buttery milk powder bun (Naixu bread).
