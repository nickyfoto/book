## Watch First
The crown jewel of the week is Lao Fan Gu's **[Tangshan Peppercorn Pork](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d2_S_8467Xo)**, which reveals state-banquet secrets to transforming humble pork belly into a melt-in-your-mouth centerpiece through dry-fried local mountain peppercorns and patient double-steaming. ## Week in Review
This week was defined by an inspiring revival of regional Chinese heritage techniques, spanning meticulous Shandong banquet classics, Tangshan braised meats, and rustic Dongbei preservation staples. A shared thematic fascination with cinematic food culture also emerged across multiple elite channels, unpacking the comforting wartime lore of "Xu Fu Braised Rice". ### Food Science & Tips
Protein velveting received rigorous analysis, with both Chef Wang Gang and Chef James illustrating that massaging water into lean beef and pork prior to adding starch allows muscle fibers to swell and retain moisture, whereas adding starch too early creates a barrier that locks hydration out. Textural chemistry was equally prominent: Master Chen demonstrated that kneading soaked steamed *mantou* into hand-chopped pork yields an airy meatball that dissolves on the palate without turning dense, while MASA showed that steaming pumpkin rather than boiling it preserves a dry, fluffy crumb and concentrates natural sweetness. On the preservation side, **尚食厨房 ShangshiKitchen** addressed food safety by explaining that vegetable nitrite concentrations stay lowest during the first 48 hours of pickling, making small-batch cabbage consumed within three days the safest option.

### Restaurant & Travel
Culinary history met cinematic storytelling through deep dives into the origins of "Xu Fu Braised Rice" from *Welcome to Dragon Restaurant*, illuminating how the dish sustained orphans during wartime and comparing its caramelized claypot-style execution to formal soup-thickened Shandong *hui* traditions. Viewers also received an unvarnished look at traditional apprenticeship standards in **[Master Chen's Homework Check](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1juJ-UlNekM)**, which documented Master Chen Zongming critiquing his pupil's pork kidney stir-fry and detailing the exhaustive kneading required to prevent voids in authentic Shandong meatballs.
