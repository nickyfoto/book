### Food Science & Tips
Crucial technical advice emerged across multiple channels, such as pre-mixing starch sauces (*qian kou*) to prevent overcooking during flash-frying and combining rendered lard with vegetable oil to enhance seafood sweetness. In soup and stock preparation, chefs emphasized that salting broth too early dehydrates muscle proteins into tough textures and demonstrated that flushing lotus root cavities under water prevents muddy discoloration. Additional smart kitchen hacks included steeping pork tripe in 70°C water for effortless peeling without tightening the meat, as well as freezing cooked crab for an hour to tighten muscle fibers and lock in natural sugars.
