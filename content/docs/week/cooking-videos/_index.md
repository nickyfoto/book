### Food Science & Tips
This week was a goldmine for kitchen science, highlighted by 小高姐的 Magic Ingredients demonstrating how using pear as a gentle marinade tenderizes chicken breast without stripping its elasticity or turning it to mush like kiwi or pineapple does. The channel 老东北美食 demystifies an alkaline tenderizing trick, showing how vigorously kneading beef in a baking soda or lye solution primes the meat fibers to flawlessly absorb moisture. Additionally, Chef Wang Gang provides a must-watch explainer on wok-hei, detailing how heating an iron wok until reddish expands its pores to capture oil and create an instant non-stick surface for perfectly sliding omelettes. For the perfect egg texture, 老东北美食 also reveals that whisking eggs with potato starch and chicken broth allows the mixture to gelatinize at 65-70 degrees Celsius, locking in moisture for a remarkably delicate, juicy bite.
