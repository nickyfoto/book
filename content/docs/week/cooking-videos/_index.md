### Food Science & Tips
This week offered phenomenal insights into ingredient science, particularly Chef James' explanation that simmering tofu causes it to expel water and puff up, allowing it to act like a savory sponge as it cools and shrinks. We also learned the vital importance of "aging" homemade sauces in the refrigerator to let spices deeply meld, alongside a brilliant moisture-control hack from 老东北美食 where salting thick-cut eggplant and cucumbers draws out water for a dramatically crispier final texture. Furthermore, a fantastic tip for preventing oxidation in braised meats is to ditch the iron pot in favor of a traditional jar, keeping the meat beautifully caramelized rather than turning an unappetizing black.

### Restaurant & Travel
The 老饭骨 channel provided fascinating glimpses into the professional restaurant industry this week, highlighting how an Australian-Chinese chef adapts regional Jingzhou flavors for diverse crowds in Melbourne. They also hosted an executive chef from Tangshan who shared the rich local lore behind his signature Huadiao Chicken, a dish whose origins date back to Emperor Taizong boosting troop morale during the Tang Dynasty.
