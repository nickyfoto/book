### Food Science & Tips
The chicken breast tutorial by *詹姆士官方專屬頻道* dives deep into the mechanics of meat fibers, explaining that you must first cut against the grain, and then lightly pound the meat to stretch those fibers out. If you don't break them down, the chef explains, the chicken will violently shrink and toughen as soon as it hits the heat. The video also features great foundational tips, like using off-heat poaching to keep the chicken tender, and shocking blanched spinach in cold water immediately to halt the cooking process and preserve its crispness.
