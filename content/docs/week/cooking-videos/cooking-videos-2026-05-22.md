### Food Science & Tips
If you want a heartwarming back-to-basics refresher on kitchen equipment and practical tips, check out [四伯爷从来没用过微波炉，送给他一台教他如何热菜](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b_dHTL9l2_4) by 美食作家王刚. After gifting his rural uncle a microwave to save him the daily hassle of lighting firewood, Wang Gang shares vital appliance safety rules, emphasizing that metal, stainless steel, and enamel must never go in the microwave. He also demonstrates a fantastic tip for transforming tough, spicy cold beef: by gently reheating it on medium power, the fat melts and the meat significantly softens, though he warns that you should only heat small portions at a time to prevent the meat from drying out and becoming tough again.
