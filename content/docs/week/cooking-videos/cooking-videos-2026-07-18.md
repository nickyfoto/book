### Recipes & Tutorials

### Food Science & Tips
For anyone who loves eggplant but hates how greasy it gets, the short but mighty tip from **[詹姆士官方專屬頻道](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O9qUxS_shNc)** is a complete game-changer. He explains the simple science of preventing eggplants from acting like an oil sponge: tossing the raw vegetable with salt beforehand. The salt draws moisture out of the eggplant, and this active outward flow of water physically prevents the hot oil from seeping deep into the flesh while cooking, drastically cutting down on the greasiness of your final dish.
