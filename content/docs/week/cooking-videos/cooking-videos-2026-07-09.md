### Food Science & Tips
The tomato pork slice tutorial from 老东北美食 offers a fantastic visual guide to meat tenderization, demonstrating how a brief 3-5 minute soak with a small amount of baking soda breaks down the meat fibers and pulls out impurities. The chef also shows how gently poaching the velveted meat—preventing the water from reaching a rolling boil—maintains a perfectly bouncy, slip-down-your-throat texture. Over in the cold hot pot video, a quick but essential tip is adding a splash of vinegar to your blanching water, which yields a much firmer, snappier bite for the shrimp.
