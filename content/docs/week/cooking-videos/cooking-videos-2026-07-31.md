Meanwhile, Shandong cuisine veteran Chen Zongming offers a fantastic lesson in home-style seafood with his upload, **[家常不平常，营养丰富，味透肌理【茼蒿焖鲅鱼】#民间手艺人 #鲁菜老厨陈宗明 #美食 #美食教程 #cooking](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tSr7ql-z99E)**. He demonstrates how to properly prepare Spanish mackerel by scoring the flesh right down to the bone, ensuring the rich braising liquid can deeply penetrate the meat. He braises the fish for about fifteen minutes and brilliantly contrasts the heavy seafood flavor by adding crisp, fresh crown daisy greens at the very last minute so they retain their signature bright aroma without turning mushy.

### Food Science & Tips
In his mackerel tutorial, chef Chen Zongming breaks down the culinary method of neutralizing aggressive fish odors by employing white angelica root, Sichuan peppercorn oil, and a crucial splash of vinegar, which physically carries the fishy scent away as it evaporates in the hot pan. Additionally, the Lao Fan Gu video provides an indispensable tip for home cooks looking to upgrade their pantry: you can slowly fry ginger, scallions, parsley, and shallots in peanut oil to extract a robust scallion oil that stores well and will make absolutely any vegetable or meat dish taste significantly more aromatic.
