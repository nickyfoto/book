### Recipes & Tutorials
In **[Costco终于有带皮五花肉了！红烧肉必须安排｜无水慢炖，软糯不腻｜The Best Way to Cook Costco Skin-On Pork Belly, No-Water Braise](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aq1gpWrUo7E)**, **尚食厨房 ShangshiKitchen** breaks down a complete guide to scallion-scented braised pork belly using Costco's newly available skin-on pork belly cuts. The technique begins by blanching the meat in cold water to tighten its shape and eliminate impurities, followed by a dry sear over high heat to render out excess lard and blister the skin. Rather than adding water, the pork is layered over two large bundles of scallions inside a clay pot and simmered with ginger, star anise, cinnamon, yellow rock sugar, rice wine, soy sauces, and a can of light beer.

### Food Science & Tips
According to **尚食厨房 ShangshiKitchen**, dry-searing the pork belly before stewing forces trapped moisture and rendered fat to pop out of the skin, which allows the skin to braise into an exceptionally soft, gelatinous texture. When selecting your braising liquid, stick strictly to crisp, light beers and avoid bitter styles like IPAs, whose concentrated hop bitterness will ruin the savory balance of the sauce. Additionally, slow-simmering in a traditional clay pot for 60 to 75 minutes allows the fat and aromatics to meld harmoniously, preserving tender meat layers that would otherwise turn mushy in a pressure cooker.
