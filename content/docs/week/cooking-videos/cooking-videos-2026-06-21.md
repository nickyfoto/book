### Food Science & Tips
Today's videos are absolutely packed with brilliant kitchen wisdom, like the pomfret tutorial's deep dive into knife work: scoring your fish at a precise 45-degree angle exposes a wider surface area of the flesh for maximum flavor absorption without weakening the structure of the fish in the pan. We also get a stellar lesson in texture from the one-pot rice video: parboiling your rice for exactly 3 minutes until the hard, raw core is halved ensures a wonderfully chewy, *al dente* bite that won't turn into mush during the final steaming process. Additionally, this video proves that physically tearing vegetables like eggplant or breaking potatoes with force—rather than slicing them cleanly—creates uneven, jagged surfaces that soak up braising liquids far better.
