### Food Science & Tips
James drops a brilliant scientific tip for creating the perfect scallion ginger dipping oil: because salt and sugar cannot dissolve in hot oil, you must mix them directly into the aromatics and allow the natural juices—or a splash of water or rice wine—to dissolve the seasoning before pouring the oil over the mixture. He also explains that pouring the hot oil in two separate stages creates a more complex, layered flavor profile by gently coaxing out the aromatics across varying temperatures. Additionally, for the ultimate chicken flavor, he suggests an advanced kitchen tip: boiling, straining, and chilling the original poaching liquid to soak the cooked chicken in later, rather than diluting the flavor with a plain ice water bath.
