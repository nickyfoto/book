### Food Science & Tips
If you love baking or making cafe-style drinks at home, **[想用抹茶卻不知道該買哪種？](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PDbW8pPvU_4)** by MASAの料理ABC is a wonderfully practical guide to selecting and handling matcha powder. MASA explains the science behind why matcha clumps up in cold liquids—the particles are so incredibly fine that they actually repel water—and demonstrates how to properly dissolve it by mixing the powder with a small amount of liquid first. He also offers a genius, zero-waste kitchen hack: when you finish a bag of matcha, toss some salt or sugar directly into the supposedly "empty" bag and shake it up to create a delicious, homemade matcha seasoning that is perfect for toast, yogurt, or even fried eggs.
