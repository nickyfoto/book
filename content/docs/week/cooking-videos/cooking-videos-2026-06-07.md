### Food Science & Tips
Chef Wang Gang drops some stellar seafood preparation knowledge, explaining that properly bleeding a fish by snipping near the gills will make the fish meat significantly fresher and better tasting. He also demonstrates a phenomenal technique for low-temperature slow cooking: by immediately turning off the heat and letting the fish soak in 95°C water for about 10 minutes, he ensures the delicate skin won't burst, the meat stays unbelievably tender, and the eyes won't bulge out from aggressive boiling. Furthermore, he advises being exceedingly gentle when cleaning around the fish's roe; if the membrane breaks and the roe scatters, it will become tough rather than soft and delicious.
