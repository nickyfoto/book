### Food Science & Tips
Chef James offers a fantastic piece of culinary science regarding how to get tofu to actually absorb your sauce: when tofu is simmered over heat, it expels its internal water and literally puffs up in the pan. As it cools down and shrinks, it acts like a sponge, pulling the savory meat sauce deep into the curds for maximum flavor. Additionally, the Lao Dongbei channel shares a quick, foolproof kitchen hack for pan-frying delicate tofu without resorting to a deep fryer: adding a pinch of salt directly to your cooking oil prevents the tofu pieces from sticking to the wok as you fry them to a gorgeous, golden "tiger skin" crust.
