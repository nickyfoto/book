## Highlights by Theme

### Recipes & Tutorials
The absolute standout recipe today is [特色【龙虾炒鸡】](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vFKKGirZNhA) uploaded by 鲁菜老厨陈宗明, which brilliantly pairs young rooster and live crayfish, braising them together with beer and peanut oil for a rich, dry, and mouth-wateringly spicy finish. The masterful chef emphasizes traditional Shandong cooking philosophy, highlighting the natural umami of the fresh meats without relying on MSG or even extra salt to build flavor. ### Food Science & Tips
Chef Chen Zongming's chicken and crayfish tutorial is an absolute goldmine for practical kitchen butchery and ingredient prep. He demonstrates exactly how to quickly devein a live crayfish by twisting its tail fin to seamlessly pull out the digestive tract, and he explicitly shows how to chop chicken bones perfectly flat to prevent sharp bone splinters from ruining your meal. Furthermore, he expertly explains the importance of blooming aromatics like Sichuan peppercorns in hot oil off the heat, allowing them to gently release their numbing aroma without burning and turning bitter.
