### Food Science & Tips

The video dives into the fascinating science behind these tiny greens, explaining how 3-to-4-day-old sprouts contain 10 to 100 times more active compounds—which convert into the protective compound sulforaphane—than fully grown broccoli. Furthermore, the host walks you through an effortless jar-sprouting technique that requires nothing more than seeds, a cheesecloth-covered jar, and a simple daily rinsing routine.
