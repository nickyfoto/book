### Food Science & Tips
There are some fantastic culinary technique takeaways in today's digest. The scallion pancake tutorial demonstrates the critical importance of temperature control, showing how using 56°C warm water yields a softer dough with distinct layers, and explains how pouring hot oil over a 1:1 mix of large white scallions and small green scallions balances pungent flavor with vibrant visual appeal. Additionally, the tofu tutorial drops a great tip on ingredient mechanics: always opt for firm brine tofu (卤水豆腐) and fry it before braising to seal in the moisture; this prevents the tofu from crumbling in the wok and turns it into a sponge for that savory scallion sauce.
