**Byron Murphy II / "We Have Everything We Need In Order To Be Successful"** · [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ORp8RU4Envw)
Now entering his third season, defensive tackle **Byron Murphy II** is taking on a prominent leadership role in a defensive line room that he proudly declares is packed with versatile "dogs". Murphy revealed that coaches expect more pass-rush productivity from him this year, which is why he is rotating through multiple roles, including the three-technique, nose tackle, and lower alignments. While he noted that playing through double-teams is now "second nature" to him, Murphy admitted that rushing from the nose is a quick, grueling battle because you are almost always fighting through both the guard and center. Murphy was visibly saddened by the news of **Clark's** injury but emphasized that the locker room "has Bud's back" as they adjust. Showing a mature, professional mindset, Murphy shrugged off questions about the skyrocketing defensive tackle market to keep his eyes strictly on training camp and "stacking days".
