# Seahawks Press — 2026-07-29

## Top Takeaway
Veteran defensive lineman Leonard Williams is approaching his 12th NFL training camp with the mindset of a rookie, channeling advice he received from his father-in-law, Hall of Famer Ronnie Lott. 

## Press Conferences & Interviews

**Leonard Williams, Defensive Lineman** · [Seahawks Press Pass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CgRDUhZ1D0Q)
Entering his 12th training camp, Williams shared a fantastic insight into his longevity and mindset, noting that he still leans on advice given to him by his father-in-law, Ronnie Lott, back in his fourth NFL season. Lott told him to "Treat every year like your rookie year". Williams came across as grounded and incredibly focused, emphasizing that despite his established veteran status, he is entering this camp eager to absorb knowledge and improve just as he did when he was younger. It is exactly the kind of open-minded approach you want to hear from a cornerstone defensive player.

## Reading Between the Lines
Having a 12-year veteran explicitly state that he is willing to hit the reset button and learn like a "rookie" is a massive early win for Mike Macdonald and the defensive coaching staff. Williams' open-mindedness sets the perfect tone for a locker room that is currently installing a brand new, highly complex defensive scheme, showing the younger players on the roster that no one is above learning the details.
