# Seahawks Press — 2026-08-23

## Top Takeaway
**Seahawks head coach Mike Macdonald delivered a devastating blow to the roster's depth, confirming that safety Bud Clark suffered a broken ankle that will sideline him for "multiple months," while versatile offensive lineman Mason is also facing a "major, long-term injury".**

## Press Conferences & Interviews

**Mike Macdonald / Head Coach** · **[Seahawks at Titans Postgame Press Conference - Preseason Week 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MgwMJq87aJc)**
**Macdonald didn't sugarcoat the disappointment of the second-half collapse but praised how his "twos" jumped out to a 16-3 lead against Tennessee's starting defense, stating the team definitely took a step forward from last week. He highlighted major strides in pre-play operations, huddle tempo, and physical pass-rushing, but the real headline was the brutal injury news regarding Bud Clark and Mason. Macdonald also lauded running back Jay Wright's explosive touchdown run and drew attention to safety depth under evaluation, specifically name-dropping AJ, Dean Anthony, and Max. He closed with a massive nod of approval to the veterans on the sideline, noting he'd "much rather tell them to woah than go" after they picked up a celebration penalty while hyping up the young guys.**

**Drew Lock / Quarterback** · **[Seahawks at Titans Postgame Press Conference - Preseason Week 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MgwMJq87aJc)**
**Lock brought tons of positive energy, calling the chance to play against the Titans' starting unit "awesome" and expressing deep pride in the second-team offense's fast start. He was hilariously candid about his fourth-down interception, admitting the ball "came out of my hand so bad" on a throw to Cody and thanking the "Zebras" for throwing a flag to give them a free play. Lock confirmed the offensive line "sold it perfectly" on the touchdown pass to Jakard and that the entire unit made a massive emphasis on operation tempo and crisp motions this week. He also showed great leadership by talking about his mentorship of young quarterback Jaylen, encouraging him to "perfect his process" and quickly clear his head after mistakes.**

**Jay Wright / Running Back** · **[Seahawks at Titans Postgame Press Conference - Preseason Week 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MgwMJq87aJc)**
**Wright was visibly grateful to be back on the field after battling his own injuries, showing exactly what he brings to a banged-up running back room. On his jaw-dropping 69-yard touchdown run, Wright stayed disciplined in the A-gap as coached before bouncing to the right and hitting pure green grass. He was quick to credit his wideouts, calling a downfield block on the safety the "finishing block" that fully sprung the run. Macdonald highlighted Wright's work ethic and unique skillset, praising him as a heavier back with "good hands out of the backfield".**

**Jakard / Wide Receiver** · **[Seahawks at Titans Postgame Press Conference - Preseason Week 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MgwMJq87aJc)**
**The second-year receiver is playing with supreme confidence, noting that the game has "slowed down a lot" for him compared to his rookie campaign. He described his long touchdown catch as a "great ball by Drew" on a busted coverage where he saw nothing but green grass. Jakard revealed the second-team offense's gritty locker-room mindset, explaining they treated the Titans' starters as a "nameless faceless opponent". He didn't hold back his praise for his quarterback either, declaring that "Drew's a dog" and is officially putting the entire league on notice this preseason.**

## Injury & Roster Updates
**The Seahawks took severe hits to their depth charts as safety Bud Clark is sidelined for "multiple months" with a broken ankle, and versatile interior lineman Mason (who was filling in at tackle) suffered a major, long-term injury. Furthermore, Rodney Thomas is recovering from hand surgery with a TBD timeline. With Clark out, Macdonald expects a full reevaluation of safety depth, though he was encouraged by what he saw from AJ, Dean Anthony, and Max.**

## Reading Between the Lines
**The heavy focus on "operation" this week clearly paid off, with Macdonald and Lock both praising the huddle breaks and pre-snap motions, signaling that the coaching staff is successfully stamping out the sloppy preseason play. Wright's massive run being sprung by downfield wideout blocking proves the team is buying into the "relentless details" and Macdonald's core "12 is one" team philosophy. Finally, Macdonald's glowing defense of his players' celebration penalty shows he's building a fiery, high-energy culture that isn't afraid to play with serious attitude.**
