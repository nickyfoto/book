# Seahawks Press — 2026-08-27

## Top Takeaway
Seahawks head coach Mike Macdonald officially celebrated a **three-year contract extension for Pro Bowl defensive tackle Leonard Williams**, cementing him as a cornerstone of Seattle’s defense alongside other elite young core defenders. Williams was incredibly candid and emotional about the extension, stating that the Seahawks' family-oriented organization and the city of Seattle have grounded him and **"changed my life"** on and off the field.

## Press Conferences & Interviews

**Leonard Williams / Defensive Tackle** · **[Seahawks Press Pass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cjncM8t7mpE)**
Defensive tackle Leonard Williams was visibly thrilled and reflective after signing a contract extension that locks him in Seattle for a **minimum of three years**. Williams shared that Seattle's unique, family-oriented environment and passionate fan base allowed him to thrive and play some of his best football, even noting that he **"won a Super Bowl here"** and feels completely locked in after an up-and-down career. He spoke glowingly about head coach Mike Macdonald, calling him a unique leader who **"practices what he preaches,"** recalling how Macdonald would be grinding in the weight room at 6:00 a.m. while Williams was receiving early treatment for plantar fasciitis. Having earned All-Pro honors and ESPN's ranking as the **number one interior defensive lineman in the league**, Williams is fully embracing his role on the team's Leadership Council by mentoring the next generation of Seahawks defenders, specifically calling out rookies Byron Murphy and Riley. 

**Mike Macdonald / Head Coach (Press Conference)** · **[Seahawks Press Pass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lni0Qk2xPCc)**
Head Coach Mike Macdonald formally celebrated Williams' new deal, praising him as an **"All-Pro caliber player"** and an **"absolute force"** who synergizes the entire defensive front by absorbing double-teams and freeing up his teammates. Macdonald was passionate when discussing the long-term future, expressing excitement about keeping foundational players like Williams, Devon Witherspoon ("Spoon"), and Jaxon Smith-Njigba ("Jax") locked up for an extended period. He touched on the team's leadership dynamic, referencing the **Leadership Council**—which includes Williams, Jax, Spoon, and Charles Cross—and how a tribute video created by Daniel Stern for his late mentor, OJ Pgrants, showcased OJ's fierce love and legacy on *Hard Knocks*. The coach was supportive but realistic about a rash of training camp injuries, confirming that **safety Bud is out for the season** and facing surgery, while wide receivers Jake Bobo and Cody Thompson ("Tori") are also sidelined.

**Mike Macdonald / Head Coach (Preseason Finale Preview)** · **[Seahawks Press Pass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lAARM4zc_AQ)**
In a business-like session focused on the upcoming preseason matchup against the Chiefs, Coach Macdonald confirmed that the team's **established starters will be held out of the game entirely**. Instead, quarterback **Jaylen will play the entire game**, which Macdonald views as a spectacular starting opportunity for him to go through the process of starting, finding a rhythm, and managing game situations. Macdonald urged fans not to jump to conclusions regarding the upcoming roster cuts, emphasizing that "transaction days" are highly individual and based on health, depth, and special teams needs rather than simple number counting. Despite a depleted running back room that prevented their rookie rusher from getting typical preseason game reps, Macdonald expressed **absolute confidence in the rookie's readiness for Week 1**. He also highlighted the stellar development of younger players like J.R. Singleton, Peely, and Bo, who are "stacking wins" and displaying the high-intensity chasing mentality the coaching staff demands.

## Injury & Roster Updates
The Seahawks suffered a major blow with the confirmation that **safety Bud is out for the season** and will require surgery. Additionally, wide receivers Bobo and Tori are currently down, with Tori ruled out with a long-term injury. On the positive side, defensive tackle Zach Charbonnet's PUP status remains under evaluation with no immediate news, while Coach Macdonald kept Week 1 availability details for Nick Harris (referred to as **"Nicki Mori"**) and others strictly close to the vest.
