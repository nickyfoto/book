# Seahawks Press — 2026-08-21

## Top Takeaway
**Head Coach Mike Macdonald confirmed that rising wide receiver Jake Bobo suffered a serious knee injury during joint practice that early signs suggest will be a long-term setback.**

---

## Press Conferences & Interviews

**Mike Macdonald / Head Coach** · **[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jW63TmgHtCo)**
Macdonald delivered the devastating news that wide receiver Jake Bobo suffered a serious, likely long-term knee injury during joint practice. Hard news emerged on the defensive depth chart as well, with Macdonald stating that **"Noah" (Noah Pritchett) and "mighty" are currently rotating with the first-team defense** as the coaches search for the best secondary combinations. He highly valued the joint session, noting that it provided starters with 40 to 50 physical, controlled reps compared to the mere 20 they would see in a typical preseason game. Macdonald was candid, serious, and deeply supportive of both the injured Bobo and the behind-the-scenes staff who executed the cross-country travel logistics.

**Sam Darnold / Quarterback** · **[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UcMHNawCloM)**
Darnold was visibly affected by Jake Bobo's injury, admitting "it hurts" and praising Bobo as an offensive sparkplug who "brings the juice every single day". On the scheme side, Darnold revealed that coaches Brian Flurry and Jake Peetz are heavily preaching progression discipline, instructing quarterbacks to **trust their eyes and feet and make fast decisions**, including throwing the ball away to play fast. He also praised the running back group's incredible versatility, specifically highlighting how "special" playmaker **Jadarian Price's ability to run inside, outside, and catch passes** changes the offense's look. Darnold's tone was reflective and focused, acknowledging that as defending Super Bowl champions, the team must avoid taking their foot off the gas or becoming complacent.

**Ernest Jones IV / Linebacker** · **[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8iY0xa89264)**
Jones IV established an incredibly high defensive bar, stating that **their standard is "perfection" and that giving up even a single yard "pisses us off"**. He revealed a key roster reality: because established starters do not play much in traditional preseason games, these joint practices are treated as actual game days to get them ready for the regular season opener. Jones also highlighted safety Ty Okada's rapid growth, praising Okada for being more vocal, in command, and playing ahead of the ball. Fired up and intensely focused, Jones IV was candid and left no doubt about the physical, no-nonsense identity of this defense.

**Ty Okada / Safety** · **[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LCRnKYtvXPQ)**
Okada detailed his highlight pick-six from the joint practice, calling it **a great scheme call by Macdonald** that set him up to disguise coverage and make a play on a crossing route. He explained that his growing comfort comes from understanding the "why" behind defensive calls, allowing him to play free instead of just trying to "not make a mistake". Addressing roster competition after Kobe's departure, Okada noted he embraces a "me versus me" mindset to push his performance while holding younger players to a high standard. Okada spoke with a poised, humble, and highly confident tone, showcasing his development as a vocal leader on defense.

**Jadarian Price / Running Back** · **[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trGFczEqVAU)**
Though the footage of his return was brief, Price's reintegration into contact practice is a massive positive for an offense looking to expand its versatility. Teammates and coaches raved about his "special" skill set, emphasizing that **his capability to run inside, hit the edge, and catch screens** brings a different facet to the offense. Macdonald noted that the staff is being "responsible" with Price's usage, keeping him on a ramp-up program rather than giving him "thousand carries" immediately. The tone surrounding Price's integration was highly optimistic, signaling that the team views him as a vital multi-purpose weapon once fully unleashed.
