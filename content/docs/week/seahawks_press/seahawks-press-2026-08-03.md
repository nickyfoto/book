# Seahawks Press — 2026-08-03

## Top Takeaway
Head coach **Mike Macdonald** revealed that **rookie safety Bud Clark is getting extensive reps with the first-team defense** in a trial-by-fire evaluation to see how much of the complex defensive scheme he can process, while right tackle **Abraham Lucas** declared that the starting offensive line’s chemistry and comfort is **"night and day"** compared to where they started last year.

## Press Conferences & Interviews

**Mike Macdonald / Head Coach** · **[Mike Macdonald: "We Have Great Competition Going On In Our Secondary"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a8b9CAlPiiQ)**
Macdonald announced a **fierce, wide-open battle in the secondary**, highlighted by the coaching staff asking a massive amount from rookie safety **Bud Clark** by throwing him into the "fog" of heavy installation with the starters. He also highlighted an unnamed **third-year defensive back** (transcribed as "my app") who has recently **moved inside to nickel** to test his versatility and is firmly in the mix as a kick returner. On offense, Macdonald noted that **230-pound back Emanuel Wilson is flashing surprising downfield receiving talent** up the seam, while newcomer **Zach has been "tremendous" and entirely unintimidated** in carving out his role in the physical running game. Macdonald was highly analytical, collaborative, and relaxed, joke-calling himself an "idiot" when comparing his basketball knowledge to that of the visiting **San Antonio Spurs coaching staff** who came out to talk program building.

**Abraham Lucas / Offensive Tackle** · **[Abraham Lucas Talks The Importance Of Continuity On The Offensive Line](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wJtWu1fUU40)**
Lucas shared that returning the entire starting unit to the line has brought a **massive boost in confidence and execution speed**. He explained that while **"Coach Flurry's" new offense has its differences**, the substantial amount of terminal and conceptual carryover has made running play adjustments significantly easier. Lucas praised guard **Anthony Bradford's constant physical development** next to him and lauded offensive line coach **John Benton ("Coach JB") as an "old-school" details-driven coach** who also happens to be a "kick-ass guitarist" who shreds metal. Throughout the session, Lucas was incredibly candid, cracking jokes about his offseason travels to Italy as a "history nerd" and teasing rookie **Bo Stevens** for racking up ridiculous rookie fines in the offensive line room.
