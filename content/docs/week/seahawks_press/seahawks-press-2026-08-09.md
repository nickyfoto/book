# Seahawks Press — 2026-08-09

## Top Takeaway
**AJ Finley’s emotional interception** at Lumen Field—coming exactly one year after he was carted off the same field with a season-ending injury—stood out as the defining moment of the day. Head Coach Mike Macdonald was thrilled for Finley, who broke down the team huddle at the end of practice, while Finley shared that he is 100% healthy but still training his mental "eyes" to read and react.

## Press Conferences & Interviews

**[Mike Macdonald / Head Coach]** · [Seahawks Press Pass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JoGtfoZUvf0)
Mike Macdonald praised the electric atmosphere created by the 12s at Lumen Field and was incredibly candid when discussing **AJ Finley's "full circle" interception** after a grueling year of ACL rehab. He provided key injury updates, noting that Jerry Price is sidelined with minor **"lower body soreness"** but should return in a matter of days. Macdonald also lauded running back Emanuel Wilson's physical, **"heavy" style of play** and gave a massive shout-out to running backs coach Thomas Hammock for fostering an intensely competitive backfield. The head coach was energetic and clearly fired up about the team's progress, emphasizing that both the run game and run defense are steadily improving by the day.

**[AJ Finley / Safety]** · [Seahawks Press Pass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YD_b3xp4Zuw)
Safety AJ Finley was visibly emotional yet deeply grateful as he detailed his interception on a **quarters-coverage over route** thrown by Sam Howell, exactly one year after tearing his ACL in the very same stadium practice. Finley revealed that staying in Seattle to rehab and being re-signed early in the offseason by the Seahawks meant everything to him. He declared himself **100% physically healthy**, though he admitted he is still fine-tuning the mental aspect of reading and reacting on the field. Finley maintained a humble, team-first tone, stating he will do whatever it takes to get on the field, whether on special teams or as a mentor, while also hyping up rookie Tory Foster as "electric".

**[Emanuel Wilson / Running Back]** · [Seahawks Press Pass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=08B9h6uQQcQ)
Emanuel Wilson showcased quiet confidence, asserting that critics underestimate his speed and emphasizing the **raw power** he brings to the Seahawks' backfield. Wilson expressed his excitement for offensive coordinator Brian Flurry’s **two-back philosophy** and explained how running backs coach Thomas Hammock pushes him hard to run north-and-south rather than east-and-west. While he feels completely gelled with the running scheme, Wilson admitted he is still actively adjusting to the complexities of the passing game in the classroom. He also praised fellow back Jadarian for his exceptional feet and ball-tracking skills.

## Injury & Roster Updates
Jerry Price is currently sidelined with minor **"lower body soreness"** but is expected to return in a matter of days. AJ Warner is working his way back from a minor injury, and while Head Coach Mike Macdonald notes he is not yet where he wants to be, his recovery is progressing well. Elijah is back to full speed and looking highly productive after an injury slowed him down last season, while fellow safety Nick continues his own rehab process. Lastly, there is **nothing new to report** regarding fullback Robbie's status.
