**[From Memory-Hungry HNSW to Quantized SPANN: The Technical Evolution of Pinterest’s Manas Platform](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/09/pinterest-search/)** · Pinterest

To break the memory bottleneck of traditional HNSW graphs, Pinterest evolved its platform toward Quantized SPANN, combining Scalar and Product Quantization with SSD-backed index storage. The key tradeoff involved trading minimal retrieval latency for massive memory cost savings and transitioning to multi-vector models for refined relevance matching. This architecture provides a clear blueprint for teams scaling billion-scale vector search, proving that hybrid disk-vector indices with quantization can cut infrastructure overhead without sacrificing retrieval quality.
