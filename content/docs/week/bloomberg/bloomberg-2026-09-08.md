# Bloomberg — 2026-09-08

## Lead Story
Global financial markets were rattled as Brent crude surged toward \$100 a barrel following US military strikes near Iran’s vital oil export hub at Kharg Island and Houthi drone and missile strikes that forced Saudi Arabia to halt operations at several southern energy facilities. With Strait of Hormuz shipping volumes estimated by Vitol Group to be running at roughly half of pre-war levels, the supply squeeze has reignited global inflation fears and deepened selloffs across international bond and equity markets.
