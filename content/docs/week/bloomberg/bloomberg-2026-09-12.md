# Bloomberg — 2026-09-12

## Lead Story
A drone strike launched from inside Iraq forced Saudi Arabia to shut its critical East-West crude pipeline, disrupting a key bypass around the Strait of Hormuz and triggering a sharp surge in global energy prices. The supply shock pushed California diesel prices to \$9.99 per gallon and sent inflation expectations soaring across major economies. The sudden spike in crude and refined fuels has put intense pressure on G7 central banks, complicating upcoming rate decisions for Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh as markets brace for monetary tightening.
