# Bloomberg — 2026-08-29

## Lead Story
The Trump administration has finalized an agreement securing **exclusive US access to Venezuela’s vast crude reserves**, a pact granted by acting President Delcy Rodríguez that has triggered fierce backlash from both domestic hard-liners and opposition leaders. Coming six months into the US conflict with Iran—which has depressed Persian Gulf crude flows to roughly 40% of prewar levels—the arrangement seeks to anchor Western energy security, although analysts caution that Venezuela's degraded infrastructure will provide little near-term supply relief. European energy majors including Italy’s **Eni SpA** are already mobilizing alongside Venezuelan authorities to rehabilitate the nation's battered oil sector under the expanding US umbrella.
