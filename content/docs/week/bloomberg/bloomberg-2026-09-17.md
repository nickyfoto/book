# Bloomberg — 2026-09-17

## Lead Story
The Federal Reserve unanimously raised interest rates by 25 basis points in its first rate increase in three years, signaling a resolute stance against persistent inflation under Chairman Kevin Warsh. The decision triggered a broad rally across US stocks and Treasuries as investors applauded Warsh's anti-inflation credibility, despite lower oil prices easing immediate cost pressures. President Donald Trump publicly criticized the move, renewing his calls for benchmark interest rates to be slashed to 1% .

## Markets & Economics
