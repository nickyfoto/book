# Bloomberg — 2026-07-21

## Lead Story
The escalating US-Iran conflict has extended to a tenth consecutive day of military strikes, driving up geopolitical risks and disrupting global energy markets as Houthi militants threaten shipping in the Red Sea. The war has cost the US roughly $37.5 billion so far, according to Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, while President Donald Trump downplayed the prospect of near-term diplomatic talks. These persistent hostilities have forced oil tankers to make U-turns, choked traffic through the vital Strait of Hormuz, and stoked inflation fears that pushed US Treasury yields to two-month highs.
