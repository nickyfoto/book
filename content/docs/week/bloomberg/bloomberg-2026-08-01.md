## Lead Story
The escalating conflict in the Middle East is rapidly cascading into global energy markets, highlighted by President Donald Trump weighing new strikes on Iran and a Qatari liquefied natural gas tanker being struck by a projectile in the Strait of Hormuz. The geopolitical turmoil is already knocking critical global refining capacity offline, prompting ExxonMobil and Chevron to warn that high fuel prices will endure regardless of crude movements, ultimately squeezing US consumers who no longer have the buffer of federal tax refunds.
