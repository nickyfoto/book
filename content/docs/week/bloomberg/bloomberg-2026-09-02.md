# Bloomberg — 2026-09-02

## Lead Story
In a US government-led push to revive Venezuela’s energy industry while holding deposed President Nicolás Maduro in a New York jail, US Energy Secretary Chris Wright and acting Venezuelan President Delcy Rodríguez unveiled a sweep of massive energy partnerships. Leading the wave of deals, Chevron Corp. committed \$7 billion over the next five years to more than double its Venezuelan crude output, while Italian giant Eni SpA announced plans to eventually lift its production to 1 million barrels a day. Additionally, GE Vernova Inc. signed a pact to repair large portions of the country’s dilapidated power grid to support the rapid expansion.
