# Bloomberg — Week of 2026-09-05 to 2026-09-11

## Story of the Week
Direct military escalation between the US and Iran in the Strait of Hormuz—marked by tit-for-tat strikes on oil tankers, attacks near Kharg Island, and Houthi strikes on Saudi energy facilities—drastically curtailed Persian Gulf shipping and drove Brent crude past \$107 a barrel. The resulting global energy shock sparked widespread selloffs in sovereign debt, pushed US diesel prices to record highs, and forced major central banks toward hawkish policy pivots.

## Markets & Economics
* **[Global Central Banks Pivot Hawkish Amid Energy-Driven Inflation]** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-10/ecb-lifts-rates-for-second-time-as-iran-war-drives-inflation): The European Central Bank raised its benchmark rate to 2.5% as energy costs flared, while hotter-than-expected US CPI and PPI data cemented expectations for a September Federal Reserve rate hike under incoming Chair Kevin Warsh. Meanwhile, strong wage growth and rising corporate prices pushed the Bank of Japan toward policy normalization, driving the yen to multi-month highs.
* **[Global Bond Selloff Accelerates as US Buybacks Disappoint]** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-10/treasury-buys-less-than-maximum-in-longer-debt-buyback-operation): Escalating energy prices and heavy sovereign borrowing triggered a sharp global bond rout, pushing UK borrowing costs to their highest level since 1998. In the US, benchmark 10-year yields approached 5% after the Treasury’s expanded \$6 billion buyback operation purchased less debt than expected, frustrating investors.
* **[Commodity Supercycle Advances as Copper Hits Record Highs]** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-08/copper-ascends-to-new-heights-on-tight-supply-and-tariff-fears): LME copper surged to record highs above \$14,600 per metric ton on acute inventory shortages and US tariff anxieties. Simultaneously, European natural gas futures spiked past €80/MWh as Middle East supply threats compounded winter heating demand.
* **[Yen Rallies Past 155 Level Amid Currency Market Tension]** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-07/yen-rises-to-strongest-since-february-topping-intervention-rally): The Japanese yen rallied past 155 per dollar toward 2026 highs, fueled by safe-haven flows and government intervention funded through US Treasury sales. The rapid currency movement prompted US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent to issue direct warnings to market speculators.
* **[China Export Surplus Nears \$806 Billion Despite Real Estate Drag]** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-08/china-export-growth-rebounds-as-ai-boom-offsets-extreme-weather): China’s annual trade surplus headed toward \$806 billion as strong global demand for AI tech hardware powered export growth. However, sweeping real estate sales reforms triggered a projected 30% plunge in local government land-sale revenues, worsening regional fiscal debt strains.

## Business & Industries
* **[GE Aerospace Leads Multi-Billion Industrial and Energy M&A Wave]** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-08/ge-to-buy-consolidated-precision-products-for-11-75-billion): GE Aerospace agreed to acquire aerospace castings manufacturer Consolidated Precision Products for \$11.75 billion to secure engine production capacity amid supply bottlenecks. M&A activity also accelerated across banking and energy, headlined by Intesa Sanpaolo investors approving a €35.4 billion takeover of Monte Paschi and Tamarack Valley buying Headwater Exploration for C\$10 billion.
* **[Apple Debuts Foldable iPhone Duo as Tech Industry Races on AI Infrastructure]** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-09/apple-s-rally-pressures-new-ceo-to-deliver-on-foldable-iphone): Apple Inc. launched its \$2,000 foldable "iPhone Duo"—the first major hardware unveiling under new CEO John Ternus—to jumpstart premium smartphone upgrade cycles. Elsewhere in tech, surging AI demand boosted Oracle’s cloud revenues, while US corporate convertible bond issuance reached record highs as tech firms rushed to fund heavy AI capital expenditures.
* **[Global Automakers Restructure Operations Amid Tariffs and Sales Slumps]** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-05/jaguar-land-rover-to-cut-4-000-jobs-as-sales-slide-times-says): Jaguar Land Rover announced 4,000 job cuts across corporate and factory roles as it combats falling global sales, high operational costs, and new US tariffs. Meanwhile, Stellantis committed over €1 billion to manufacture commercial vans in France while selling an idled Toronto facility.
* **[Pharma Sector Plagued by Series of High-Profile Clinical Failures]** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-08/novartis-avidity-drug-del-desiran-fails-in-third-trial-setback-in-a-week): Shares of Novartis AG dropped after the company suffered three major clinical trial failures in a single week, including the flop of a rare disease treatment from its \$12 billion Avidity acquisition. Pipeline setbacks extended across the industry as AstraZeneca’s experimental breast cancer pill failed its Phase 3 study and Novo Nordisk halted two heart disease trials.

## Policy & World
* **[US-Canada Trade Conflict Escalates Before Seeking Diplomatic Relief]** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-08/carney-says-tariffs-on-us-goods-meant-to-protect-canada-not-escalate-conflict): Ottawa imposed tariffs up to 50% on \$27.6 billion of US goods in response to President Trump’s trade policies, prompting Washington to retaliate by barring Canadian firms from federal procurement contracts. Prime Minister Mark Carney later signaled restraint against further US curbs while deepening economic ties with the European Union.
* **[Far-Right AfD Wins Historic Landslide in Eastern German Election]** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-07/merz-moves-to-contain-fallout-from-far-right-rout-in-state-vote): The Alternative for Germany (AfD) captured 44.5% of the vote in Saxony-Anhalt, securing its strongest-ever state election result and dealing a heavy blow to Chancellor Friedrich Merz’s center-right establishment. The victory stoked fears among business leaders over international capital flight and acute labor shortages in eastern Germany.
* **[Ukraine Envoys Meet in Moscow and Kyiv as War Projected to Last Into 2027]** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-08/kyiv-s-allies-see-war-going-into-2027-after-putin-met-us-envoys): US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner held high-level talks with Vladimir Putin in Moscow and Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Kyiv, yielding a temporary pause in airstrikes but no diplomatic breakthrough. Western allies now expect the conflict to persist into 2027, with the Pentagon urging Congress to approve \$1.5 trillion in defense funding to replenish depleted missile stockpiles.
* **[Trump Floats \$5,000 Cash Dividend Proposal Ahead of US Midterms]** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-10/trump-floats-5-000-dividend-to-americans-if-gop-wins-midterms): Addressing the Republican midterm convention in Dallas, President Donald Trump pledged a \$5,000 payout to every adult American if the GOP retains control of Congress. The plan faced immediate pushback over its \$1 trillion-plus price tag, even as energy costs and AI data center grid strain emerged as top midterm campaign issues.

The call aligns with broader critiques highlighting the paradox of AI developers pursuing superintelligence despite internally acknowledging existential threats.
