# Bloomberg — Week of 2026-08-22 to 2026-08-28

## Story of the Week
The abrupt collapse of cross-border trade negotiations ignited an all-out trade war between the United States and Canada, after Washington levied 50% tariffs on \$20 billion of Canadian goods under Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930. Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney retaliated with dollar-for-dollar counter-duties targeting more than 700 American products—including steel, aluminum, and agricultural staples—leaving deeply integrated cross-border automotive and industrial supply chains heavily disrupted. With Washington threatening to double automotive duties and talks fully deadlocked, the standoff marks a structural rupture in North American commerce that is expected to persist well past the US midterm elections.
