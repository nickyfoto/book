# Bloomberg — Week of 2026-08-08 to 2026-08-14

## Markets & Economics

*   **[Australia's RBA Holds Rates Steady and Flags Hawkish Bias]** · [Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/australia-holds-key-rate-to-counter-elevated-inflation-pressures): **The Reserve Bank of Australia kept its benchmark interest rate unchanged at 4.35%, betting that rising unemployment and a cooling housing market will eventually curb elevated prices.** However, the central bank maintained its hawkish stance, warning that **persistent underlying price pressures keep future rate hikes firmly on the table.**
