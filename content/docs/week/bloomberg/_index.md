# Bloomberg — Week of 2026-08-28 to 2026-09-04

## Story of the Week
The escalating military conflict between the US and Iran in the Strait of Hormuz became the week's defining crisis, as direct US airstrikes and Iranian retaliatory attacks drove global energy prices to records and sent shockwaves through fixed-income markets. This severe energy anxiety triggered a historic global bond rout, pushing sovereign yields to their highest averages since 2008 and forcing major shipping carriers to urgently reroute.

## Markets & Economics
* **Carry-Trade Unwind Fuels Sharp Yen Surge** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/carry-trade-exodus-fuels-yen-surge-ahead-of-boj-rate-decision): After slipping past the critical 160-per-dollar threshold and prompting a record \$96.4 billion intervention by Tokyo, the Japanese yen surged to a one-month high as carry trades unwound rapidly. This move was accelerated by hawkish comments from Bank of Japan policymakers who left the door open for back-to-back interest-rate hikes.
* **Hawkish Federal Reserve Remarks Spark Rate-Hike Wagers** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-30/bond-investors-wary-after-warsh-fans-wagers-fed-poised-to-hike): Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh delivered a hawkish debut address at Jackson Hole, discarding forward guidance and warning that policymakers have more work to do to cool persistent inflation. While the comments sparked sharp wagers on a September rate hike and drove Treasuries lower, some fixed-income managers expressed skepticism over whether the central bank is truly prepared to tighten further.
* **Global Sovereign Bond Yields Rise to 2008 Highs** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/why-government-bond-yields-are-rising-and-causing-alarm): Escalating Middle Eastern energy conflicts and persistent fiscal concerns triggered a severe global bond selloff, pushing yields to levels not seen since 2008. In the US, the 30-year Treasury entered its worst stretch since 2006, while Japan’s 10-year yield touched 3% for the first time since 1996.
* **Eurozone Inflation Jumps to Three-Year High** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/euro-zone-inflation-jumps-to-highest-in-almost-three-years): Euro-area consumer price inflation accelerated to three-year peaks in August, reinforcing expectations for a September ECB rate hike. However, a Bloomberg survey indicates economists expect the ECB to implement a final quarter-point hike to 2.5% next week and hold there through 2027, marking a dovish divergence from market bets.
* **Bank of Canada Holds Rates Steady Amid Trade Tensions** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-02/bank-of-canada-holds-rates-at-2-25-as-tariff-war-heats-up): Despite second-quarter GDP growing at an annualized 3.3% rate, the Bank of Canada held its benchmark interest rate steady at 2.25%. Policymakers warned that escalating tariff disputes with the United States threaten domestic expansion and risk reviving inflationary pressures.
* **India's Economy Outpaces Disruptions with 7.8% GDP Expansion** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/india-s-growth-beat-suggests-economy-on-cusp-of-investment-boom): India’s economy expanded by a faster-than-expected 7.8% last quarter, signaling the revival of a long-dormant private investment cycle despite global energy volatility. To manage the resulting heavy capital inflows, the Reserve Bank of India’s net short dollar book climbed to a record \$136.8 billion.

## Business & Industries
* **John Ternus Takes the Reins as Apple CEO** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/apple-s-2-275-gain-under-cook-is-tough-act-for-ternus-to-follow): Hardware engineering chief John Ternus officially succeeded Tim Cook as CEO of Apple on September 1, taking over just ahead of a crucial fall product cycle that includes foldable iPhones and AI smart displays. Outgoing CEO Tim Cook will oversee a phased transition, capping a historic tenure that saw Apple's valuation surge by 2,275%.
* **Nvidia Targets AI Software with \$14 Billion Hugging Face Pursuit** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/audio/2026-09-02/bloomberg-tech-nvidia-nears-hugging-face-deal-podcast): Nvidia is in advanced negotiations to acquire Hugging Face, a leading repository for artificial intelligence models and developer datasets, for approximately \$14 billion. The potential blockbuster acquisition follows Nvidia's separate \$3.5 billion strategic investment in Taiwan’s MediaTek to bolster its consumer and automotive silicon capabilities.
* **Eli Lilly Diversifies Beyond Obesity with \$2.88 Billion Autoimmune Deal** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-31/lilly-to-buy-merida-for-2-88-billion-for-autoimmune-expansion): Eli Lilly agreed to acquire Merida Biosciences for up to \$2.88 billion to expand its therapeutic pipeline into treatments for autoimmune and allergic conditions. The acquisition leverages blockbuster profits from Mounjaro, which also secured expanded FDA approval this week to prevent serious cardiovascular events in high-risk patients.
* **Volkswagen Board Approves Radical Turnaround Plan** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/vw-ceo-still-needs-to-fix-thorniest-problems-after-job-cuts-deal): Volkswagen's supervisory board unanimously endorsed CEO Oliver Blume's sweeping restructuring program, which includes 50,000 additional job cuts, fewer models, and a reduced industrial footprint. Though the agreement successfully defuses a high-stakes showdown with labor, it leaves the unresolved fate of four major German manufacturing plants in question.
* **Shein Shares Plunge 10% in Highly Anticipated Hong Kong Debut** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/shein-s-debut-shows-the-cost-of-ipo-missing-its-growth-peak): Fast-fashion giant Shein fell sharply on its first day of trading on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, closing down as much as 10%. The weak launch underscores investor anxiety over Shein's decelerating growth and rising regulatory hurdles, with its \$26.5 billion valuation representing a steep drop from its \$100 billion peak in 2022.

## Policy & World
* **US Seizes Control of Venezuelan Oil in Geopolitical Power Play** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/as-us-venezuela-oil-deal-takes-shape-here-are-the-key-points): The Trump administration finalized a sweeping 25-year accord granting the US control of 65 billion barrels of Venezuelan crude to replenish the Strategic Petroleum Reserve. The massive deal—which involves a \$7 billion commitment from Chevron and grid-rehabilitation agreements with GE Vernova—has blindsided domestic executives and squeezed Beijing by denying China access to the oil.
* **Bilateral Gridlock Persists in US-Canada Trade War** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-29/where-the-us-canada-trade-war-stands-after-talks-fell-apart): Cross-border trade talks remain deadlocked as Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney maintains a firm stance against escalating US tariff threats. The dispute has intensified with newly active 50% tariffs on Canadian exports severely disrupting small businesses in Vermont, while Canadian provincial leaders publicly mock Washington's rhetoric.
* **European Allies Pressure Greece and Spain for Ukraine Air Defenses** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-09-01/spain-and-greece-face-call-from-eu-allies-to-give-patriot-stockpiles-to-ukraine): As Ukraine warns of a potential winter ground offensive targeting Kyiv and Chernihiv, EU partners are aggressively lobbying Spain and Greece to transfer their stockpiles of Patriot air defense systems. The push comes amid an acute global shortage of PAC-3 interceptors, forcing officials to explore retrofitting older PAC-2 systems.
* **China Blocks G20 Communique as Rare Military Dialogues Open** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/china-commander-for-taiwan-ops-met-us-counterpart-for-first-time): Geopolitical friction peaked as Beijing blocked a G20 joint communique over disputes regarding its massive trade surplus. Concurrently, the Chinese general overseer of Taiwan operations held a historic first-time meeting with US Indo-Pacific Admiral Samuel Paparo in an effort to manage escalating cross-strait tensions.
