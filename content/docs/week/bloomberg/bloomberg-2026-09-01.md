# Bloomberg — 2026-09-01

## Lead Story
The **US launched targeted military strikes** on Iranian infrastructure around the **Strait of Hormuz** after projectiles struck two commercial supertankers late Monday, driving global energy prices and bond yields to dramatic highs. This escalation triggered a **global bond rout**, pushing sovereign yields to levels not seen since **2008** as investors dump longer-maturity debt on fears of resurgent inflation and imminent central bank rate hikes. In the US, the **30-year Treasury yield** entered its worst stretch since **2006**, while Japan’s **10-year yield** touched **3%** for the first time since **1996**, highlighting the severe, interlocking pressure on global monetary policy.
