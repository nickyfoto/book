# Bloomberg — 2026-08-28

## Lead Story
Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh delivered a hawkish debut address at the Jackson Hole economic policy symposium, warning that inflation has failed to cool meaningfully and declaring that policymakers have "work to do" to bring consumer prices back to their firm 2% target. Warsh noted that broad financial conditions can hardly be described as "restrictive" and discarded conventional forward guidance, arguing that economic forecasting remains merely "an aspiration". The remarks triggered an immediate repricing across Wall Street, driving two-year Treasury yields and real yields sharply higher while equities slipped into negative territory as traders priced in a roughly 50% probability of an upcoming interest-rate hike.

## Markets & Economics
* **[Canada Economy Grows 3.3% in Second Quarter, Fastest Since ’23](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-28/canada-economy-grows-3-3-in-second-quarter-fastest-since-23)** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-28/canada-economy-grows-3-3-in-second-quarter-fastest-since-23)): Gross domestic product expanded at an annualized 3.3% rate in the second quarter, rebounding from a yearlong trade-induced slump despite a prolonged Statistics Canada website outage that delayed data access for market participants.
