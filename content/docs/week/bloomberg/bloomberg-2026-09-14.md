# Bloomberg — 2026-09-14

## Lead Story
Calls by artificial intelligence leaders—including Anthropic PBC CEO Dario Amodei and OpenAI President Greg Brockman—to slow frontier model development sparked a global technology selloff, dragging Nasdaq 100 futures down 1.4% and causing SoftBank Group Corp. shares to plunge 13%. Semiconductor and hardware suppliers bore the brunt of the downturn as investors reassessed capital expenditure expectations across the AI supply chain. The market slide highlighted a deepening policy rift, occurring as President Donald Trump dismissed safety concerns as a "hoax" during a live phone call with Nvidia Corp. CEO Jensen Huang.
