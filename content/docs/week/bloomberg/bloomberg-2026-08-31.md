# Bloomberg — 2026-08-31

## Lead Story
The US military resumed direct hostilities with Iran by striking rocket launchers preparing to deploy naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz, triggering retaliatory Iranian missile and drone attacks against US air bases in Jordan and installations in the United Arab Emirates. The sudden military escalation propelled Brent crude higher and drove the 10-year US Treasury yield past 4.75% for the first time since January 2025, sending the S&P 500 lower as rate-sensitive equities slumped. The confrontation threatens renewed shipping bottlenecks through the world's most critical crude corridor while heightening inflation risks for global central banks.
