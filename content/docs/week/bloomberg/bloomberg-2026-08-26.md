# Bloomberg — 2026-08-26

## Lead Story
The trade conflict between the United States and Canada has rapidly escalated into a full-scale trade war, with the Trump administration now threatening to double tariffs on Canada’s critical automotive sector. This move follows Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney's announcement of dollar-for-dollar retaliatory measures against new US import duties. The friction, triggered by the US invoking a rare 50% tariff on Canadian imports under Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930, has prompted immediate regional countermeasures—including Saskatchewan's new 50% tax on US alcohol imports —and revived consumer boycotts of American products in Canadian supermarkets .

## Markets & Economics
* **[Core PCE Muted as Real Spending Flatlines](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/key-us-inflation-gauge-posts-muted-advance-spending-stalls)** (Bloomberg): The Federal Reserve’s preferred inflation metric, the core PCE price index (excluding food and energy), rose a modest 0.2% in July and advanced 3.3% from a year ago, while inflation-adjusted consumer spending stalled. This muted print provides the central bank with critical breathing room to hold interest rates steady as global markets focus on incoming Fed Chair Kevin Warsh’s impending debut speech at the Jackson Hole retreat.
* **[Strait of Hormuz Revenue Deal Drags Oil Lower](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/iran-oman-agree-to-share-strait-of-hormuz-revenue-irgc-says)** (Bloomberg): Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and Omani officials have negotiated an "interim framework" to resume commercial shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, anchored by a joint revenue-sharing agreement on transit fees. This diplomatic breakthrough has dragged global oil prices lower, offering a much-needed reprieve from acute inflation worries even as Russia-Ukraine tensions continue to boil.
* **[Natural Gas Eclipses Oil as Primary European Inflation Risk](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/natural-gas-eclipses-oil-as-key-inflation-risk-for-european-debt)** (Bloomberg): Natural gas has overtaken crude oil as the chief concern for European bond traders, driven by fears that depleted fuel supplies will spark a winter resurgence in consumer price inflation. The anxiety has intensified after Dutch network operator Gasunie warned that the Netherlands will miss its winter storage filling targets, leaving Europe highly exposed to seasonal temperature drops and unexpected pipeline disruptions .
* **[Hot Australian Inflation Sparks Rate Hike Forecasts](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/australia-core-inflation-comes-in-stronger-than-expected-in-july)** (Bloomberg): An unexpected acceleration in Australia's July core inflation has forced economists at Goldman Sachs and Commonwealth Bank to forecast an interest rate hike by the Reserve Bank of Australia as early as September. In response to this persistent global inflation pressure, Australia’s second-largest pension giant has aggressively built its largest overweight position in the Japanese yen in years, betting that markets are significantly underpricing the Bank of Japan's tightening path.

## Business & Industries
* **[Meta Platforms Agrees to Historic \$18 Billion Teen Safety Settlement](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/audio/2026-08-26/breaking-meta-and-states-settle-social-media-harm-case)** (Bloomberg): In a watershed moment for the technology industry, Meta Platforms Inc. has agreed to pay up to \$18 billion to resolve a multi-state lawsuit alleging that Facebook and Instagram were designed to addict teenagers. Crucially, the settlement imposes strict equitable relief—mandating youth-specific accounts, age-gating, parental oversight, and algorithm modifications—marking what regulators describe as a "Big Tobacco" moment that will reshape social media business models.

While the results confirm that the hardware buildout remains a juggernaut, analysts note that the broader market is growing impatient for these heavy infrastructure outlays to deliver tangible corporate productivity gains.
* **[NFL Owners Approve Record \$9.6 Billion Sale of Seattle Seahawks](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/nfl-owners-approve-9-6-billion-seattle-seahawks-sale-to-khoslas)** (Bloomberg): NFL team owners have officially approved the sale of the defending Super Bowl champion Seattle Seahawks to technology billionaires Vinod and Neeru Khosla for \$9.6 billion .
