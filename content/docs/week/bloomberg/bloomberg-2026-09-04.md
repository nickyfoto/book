# Bloomberg — 2026-09-04

## Lead Story
**US nonfarm payrolls rose by 162,000 in August**, handily beating all economist estimates in a Bloomberg survey and keeping the unemployment rate steady at **4.1%**. The blowout report jolted global markets as swap traders boosted the odds of a **September Federal Reserve interest-rate hike**, causing Treasuries to slide and US stock futures to erase early gains. The robust labor data has also re-ignited a high-stakes political battle over monetary policy, with **President Donald Trump** and **Vice President JD Vance** aggressively calling on the central bank to lower interest rates to ease housing and economic pressures.

## Markets & Economics
* **[Yen Surges as Carry Trade Unwind Accelerates](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/carry-trade-exodus-fuels-yen-surge-ahead-of-boj-rate-decision)**: A massive rush to unwind yen-funded carry trades propelled the Japanese currency to a **one-month high** against the dollar, as traders ramped up bets on upcoming Bank of Japan rate hikes. Nomura Securities noted that in an extreme scenario of persistent yen weakness, the central bank could deliver **three consecutive interest-rate hikes** through December.
* **[European Central Bank Projected to Conclude Hikes Next Week](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/economists-see-final-ecb-hike-next-week-in-split-with-markets)**: A Bloomberg survey of economists indicates that the ECB will raise its deposit rate by a quarter-point to **2.5%** next Thursday and hold it there through 2027. This expectation marks a highly dovish split from current market pricing, which is betting on more aggressive tightening.
* **[Strait of Hormuz Conflict Drives Energy Prices to Records](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/us-retail-diesel-hits-record-as-hormuz-russia-crises-stretch-on)**: Growing geopolitical friction between the US and Iran has pushed US retail diesel to a record national average of **\$5.85 a gallon**, raising fears of winter fuel shortages and persistent inflation. While shipowners are drawn back to the route by attractive earnings, European gas storage levels are low, threatening a global supply scramble.
* **[Senegal Downgraded to Junk as Eurobond Restructuring Looms](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/s-p-downgrades-senegal-after-government-unveils-debt-rework)**: S&P Global Ratings has cut Senegal’s credit score further into junk, declaring a default or distressed restructuring of the country's **\$5 billion of eurobonds** is "extremely likely". The sovereign debt crisis is exposing regional lenders, including the West African Development Bank (BOAD) and Africa Finance Corp., to significant credit risk.

## Business & Industries

* **[Credit Score Giants Tumble on US Policy Pushback](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/fico-plunges-21-credit-bureaus-fall-as-pulte-renews-criticism)**: Shares of Fair Isaac Corp. (FICO) plummeted **21%**, dragging down major credit bureaus Equifax and TransUnion, after Federal Housing Finance Agency Director Bill Pulte renewed sharp criticisms of consumer credit-scoring costs. The comments highlight growing regulatory pressure on the highly consolidated industry to curb fees.
* **[Dangote Refinery Eyes Impending \$1.6 Billion IPO](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/dangote-sets-pricing-gets-regulator-s-approval-1-6-billion-ipo)**: Nigerian billionaire Aliko Dangote has secured regulatory approval to launch an initial public offering for his massive oil refinery, Africa’s largest, within the next two weeks. * **[National Stock Exchange of India Receives Long-Awaited IPO Nod](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/india-s-nse-secures-regulatory-approval-for-long-awaited-ipo)**: The National Stock Exchange of India Ltd. has received regulatory approval for a long-delayed initial public offering, clearing a major hurdle for what is expected to be the country's **biggest-ever public listing**.
