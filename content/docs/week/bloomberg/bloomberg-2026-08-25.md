# Bloomberg — 2026-08-25

## Lead Story
The trade dispute between the US and Canada has escalated into a full-blown trade war after Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney matched President Donald Trump’s 50% import tariffs dollar-for-dollar. The Canadian retaliatory package imposes 50% duties on US steel, aluminum, furniture, and dairy products, targeting more than 700 American goods. This direct retaliation responds to US tariffs applied over the weekend on approximately \$20 billion of annual Canadian exports, leaving both sides with no current plans to return to the negotiating table.
