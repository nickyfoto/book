# Bloomberg — 2026-07-24

## Lead Story
US President Donald Trump reconstructed his protectionist wall on Friday, extending a 10% to 12.5% tariff baseline hitting 60 economies under Section 122 of the Trade Act. The administration simultaneously levied new duties over forced-labor claims and threatened fresh tariffs on the European Union in retaliation for a $1 billion fine against Alphabet Inc.'s Google. The sweeping trade actions are already sparking pushback from Asian allies and lawsuits from domestic small businesses, further reawakening global inflation fears.
