# Bloomberg — 2026-08-27

## Lead Story
**[Nvidia Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/nvidia-adds-442-billion-in-second-biggest-ever-stock-surge)** added a historic \$442 billion to its market value on Thursday in the second-largest one-day gain in stock market history, propelled by a blowout sales forecast that has temporarily eased concerns over an AI spending bubble. The tech giant is further tightening its grip on the artificial intelligence sector with a reported **[\$13 billion acquisition of open-weight AI platform Hugging Face](https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/articles/2026-08-27/-13-billion-hugging-face-deal-would-make-nvidia-the-king-of-ai)**, effectively absorbing one of the primary players fighting to keep the AI ecosystem decentralized and open.

## Markets & Economics

*   **[India's Sensex Shaken by 3% 'Flash Crash' During Closing Auction]** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/india-s-sensex-hit-by-flash-crash-during-unpopular-closing-auction)): India’s popular BSE Sensex benchmark briefly plummeted about 3% during its final 20-minute closing auction on Thursday. ## Business & Industries
*   **[Meta Agrees to Historic \$18 Billion Social Media Settlement]** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-27/wendy-s-falls-on-no-buyout-bid-meta-moves-on-settlement-video)): Meta agreed to pay up to \$18 billion to resolve landmark lawsuits from US states accusing the company of hook-and-scroll youth addiction on Facebook and Instagram. The deal mandates major operational changes, including restrictions on scrolling hours for youth and the inability to disable safety settings without parental consent.
*   **[Revolution Medicines Wins US Approval for Landmark \$475K Cancer Pill]** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-27/revmed-s-pancreatic-cancer-pill-priced-above-475k-video)): The FDA approved Rasonque, a breakthrough once-daily drug that targets mutated RAS proteins to extend lives in advanced pancreatic cancer patients. Priced at over \$475,000 per year, Rasonque debuts as one of the most expensive oncology treatments in history.
*   **[Shein Guides Hong Kong IPO Pricing to Midrange at \$26B Valuation]** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/shein-is-said-to-guide-hong-kong-ipo-price-to-middle-of-range)): Fast-fashion giant Shein plans to price its Hong Kong initial public offering near the midpoint of the marketed range, aiming to raise approximately \$1.7 billion (HK\$13.6 billion). The targeted pricing values the retailer at \$26 billion—a steep drop from the \$100 billion valuation it commanded four years ago.
*   **[Swedish Miner Boliden Secures Control of Nexa Resources in \$1.3B Deal]** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/boliden-to-buy-controlling-stake-in-nexa-in-1-3-billion-deal)): Swedish mining group Boliden AB agreed to acquire Votorantim SA's stake in Nexa Resources SA for \$1.3 billion, securing a controlling interest in the zinc and silver producer. The deal marks a major expansion for Boliden, establishing an operational foothold in South America across Peru and Brazil.
*   **[China Vanke Posts \$2.2 Billion Loss as Debt Pressure Mounts]** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/china-vanke-posts-2-2-billion-loss-as-debt-pressure-intensifies)): China Vanke Co.’s first-half losses deepened to 14.95 billion yuan (\$2.2 billion) amid the country’s prolonged real estate slump. The massive deficit escalates the pressure on the developer’s new board as it prepares to face a looming wall of maturing debt.

## Policy & World

*   **[US Pursues Stake in Venezuelan Oil Fields as OPEC Exit Looms]** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/us-in-talks-with-venezuela-to-take-major-stake-in-oil-fields)): The Trump administration is in advanced negotiations with Venezuela to acquire a major ownership stake in the country's oil fields, extending US influence over post-Maduro energy assets. Concurrently, Caracas is closely studying a historic exit from OPEC, which would deliver a major blow to the oil cartel it helped found more than six decades ago.
*   **[Deadly Nepal Flash Flood Knocks Out 8% of Country's Power Grid]** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/nepal-may-turn-to-india-for-power-after-floods-hit-hydro-output)): A catastrophic landslide-triggered flash flood on the China-Nepal border has resulted in over 150 confirmed deaths and left hundreds of others missing. The deluge severely damaged regional hydropower infrastructure, wiping out 8% of Nepal's power generation capacity and forcing the government to negotiate with India for emergency electricity imports.
