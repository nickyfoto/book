# Bloomberg — 2026-07-20

## Lead Story
The US conducted a ninth straight day of airstrikes on Iran in an escalating campaign after three American troops were killed, while Yemen's Houthi militants threatened to impose a maritime blockade on Saudi Arabia's Red Sea traffic. The deepening Middle East conflict is directly bleeding into global markets, holding oil near its highest close since mid-June and pushing US gasoline prices back above the $4-a-gallon mark. This surge in energy costs is simultaneously reigniting inflation anxieties, weighing heavily on both equities and Treasuries as investors seek safety.

## Markets & Economics
* **Hedge Funds Dump Tech at Record Pace** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/goldman-says-hedge-funds-sell-us-tech-stocks-at-record-pace)): Hedge funds pulled back from US technology stocks at a record pace over the past two months. Bearish bets against overall US equities are also surging to an all-time high as anxiety mounts over the staying power of the AI-driven market rally.
* **UK Gilts Spooked by Burnham's Fiscal Comments** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/gilts-fall-as-burnham-s-fiscal-flexibility-comments-spook-market)): UK government bonds extended their retreat after new Prime Minister Andy Burnham indicated he will seek "flexibility" in existing fiscal rules to fund his first budget. The remarks stoked immediate market concerns that his administration will heavily add to Britain's debt load.
* **Chinese Traders Unwind Leveraged Bets** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/chinese-traders-unwind-leveraged-bets-at-fastest-pace-since-2016)): Chinese stock traders exited leveraged positions at the fastest pace since the 2015–2016 market crash. The aggressive deleveraging was triggered by a global equities rout driven by fears that the artificial intelligence rally had become overstretched.
* **Diverging Global Inflation Data** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/canada-inflation-rate-falls-to-2-8-core-measures-ease)): Canada's inflation rate unexpectedly slowed to 2.8% in June, with core measures dropping below 2% for the first time in nearly six years. Conversely, New Zealand's annual inflation accelerated to 4.1% in the second quarter, fanned by soaring domestic fuel prices.
