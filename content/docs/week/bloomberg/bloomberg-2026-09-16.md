# Bloomberg — 2026-09-16

## Lead Story
The Federal Reserve voted unanimously to raise interest rates by a quarter percentage point to a range of 3.75% to 4.00% — its first rate hike since 2023 — and penciled in an additional increase later this year to curb persistent inflation. The hawkish decision, delivered during a record-short press conference by Chair Kevin Warsh, directly defied President Donald Trump's demands for rate cuts to 1% or less and sent the US dollar surging alongside Treasury yields.
