# Bloomberg — 2026-08-07

## Lead Story
The US labor market flashed a surprise warning sign as employers unexpectedly shed **23,000 jobs in July**, defying expectations of an 80,000-job gain and triggering downward revisions to hiring data from previous months. This downbeat payroll print sparked a major rally in **US Treasuries**, sending the two-year yield tumbling as much as nine basis points to 4.15% as traders aggressively dialed back expectations for future Federal Reserve rate hikes. While the headline unemployment rate fell slightly to **4.1%**, the cooling labor demand has led major market participants to declare that near-term central bank rate hikes are now off the table.
