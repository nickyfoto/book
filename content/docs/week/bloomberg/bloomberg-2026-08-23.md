# Bloomberg — 2026-08-23

## Lead Story
High-stakes trade negotiations between the US and Canada have collapsed over discrepancies between verbal agreements and the written text. The breakdown triggers the escalation of a major trade war, with Canada preparing dollar-for-dollar counter-tariffs following Washington's imposition of new **50% import duties** on Canadian products. Former Finance Minister **Chrystia Freeland** warned that the US approach threatens to reduce allies to "vassals" and disrupt critical integrated manufacturing chains like the Detroit-Windsor auto supply line, while Prime Minister **Mark Carney’s** administration sees little chance of restarting talks before the upcoming US midterm elections.
