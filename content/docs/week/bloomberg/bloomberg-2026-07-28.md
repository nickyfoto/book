# Bloomberg — 2026-07-28

## Lead Story
The global semiconductor sector is facing a severe reckoning as AI investment fatigue and fears of intensifying Chinese competition wipe out billions in market value across continents. The Nasdaq 100 is hurtling toward a technical correction, while a deep 10% slide in Asian tech stocks from their June peaks has severely battered regional benchmarks and forced trading halts in South Korea. The deepening rout underscores mounting Wall Street anxiety over the eventual payoff and sustainability of massive, debt-fueled capital expenditures on artificial intelligence infrastructure.
