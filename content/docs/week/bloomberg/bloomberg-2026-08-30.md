# Bloomberg — 2026-08-30

## Lead Story
President Donald Trump announced that the United States will use a sweeping 25-year energy accord to tap Venezuela's 65 billion barrels of crude oil to refill the nation's depleted Strategic Petroleum Reserve. The agreement, negotiated with acting President Delcy Rodríguez following the ouster of Nicolás Maduro, places the Latin American nation's vast resources under US commercial leadership. However, the unprecedented move is drawing warnings over long-term political instability and accusations of modern resource colonialism as Maduro reemerges on social media.
