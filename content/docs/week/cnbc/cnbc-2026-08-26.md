# CNBC — 2026-08-26

## Lead Story
**[Nvidia’s](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/NVDA) stunning 70% revenue growth projection for fiscal year 2028 hijacked the narrative after a Q2 blowout, proving the AI buildout is far from peaking.** The chipmaker posted adjusted EPS of \$2.22 on \$96.22 billion in revenue, handily beating the Street and igniting a 4% extended-trading rally that re-energized the entire technology sector.

## Markets & Economics
The S&P 500 hovered flat as July’s core PCE rose 0.2% monthly and 3.3% annually, matching estimates but failing to quiet hawk chatter ahead of Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's Friday keynote at Jackson Hole. Bond yields held near multi-decade highs, with the 10-year Treasury flat at 4.645% despite Scott Bessent’s aggressive debt buybacks, a program scrutinized in **[Investor Peter Boockvar Sees Merit to Druckenmiller's WSJ Op-Ed Bashing Bessent's Bond Ploys](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/25/investor-peter-boockvar-sees-merit-to-druckenmillers-wsj-op-ed-bashing-bessents-bond-ploys.html)**. Trade tensions intensified after Canada retaliated "dollar-for-dollar" with a 50% tariff on \$20 billion of U.S. goods, detailed in **[Former U.S. Trade Rep. Ron Kirk: Tariffs are a self-imposed tax on American families and businesses](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/26/former-u-s-trade-rep-ron-kirk-tariffs-are-a-self-imposed-tax-on-american-families-and-businesses.html)**. Meanwhile, crude prices fell sharply, with Brent down 2.52% to \$86.35 and WTI dropping 2.17% to \$80.56 as Iran and Oman advanced an independent Hormuz navigational corridor, analyzed in **[the Strait of Hormuz is just one of many choke points being squeezed right now says Jeff Currie](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/26/the-strait-of-hormuz-is-just-one-of-many-choke-points-being-squeezed-right-now-says-jeff-currie.html)**.

## Business & Earnings
**Salesforce** soared 14% post-market on Q2 revenue of \$11.35 billion, boosted by a massive \$2.6 billion strategic gain from its Anthropic stake and the roll-out of "Claudeforce," as CEO Marc Benioff declared "SaaSpocalypse" fears "nonsense" on "Mad Money". To expand their AI footprint, the companies unveiled the "Claudeforce" integration, discussed in **[Anthropic CEO on ‘Saaspocalypse’: We’re not interested in destroying anyone](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/26/anthropic-ceo-on-saaspocalypse-were-not-interested-in-destroying-anyone.html)**. Cyber giant **CrowdStrike** surged 11% on record revenues of \$1.47 billion, with CEO George Kurtz pointing to a post-Mythos boom in AI cybersecurity demand. Identity provider **Okta** surged 19% following a clean top-and-bottom-line beat, while **Meta Platforms** erased a major overhang by securing a \$16.7 billion settlement over youth addiction lawsuits.

## Investing & Commentary
Panmure Liberum's Joachim Klement advised investors that hyperscalers are wasting billions overbuilding data centers, pitching Apple and Dell as the ultimate winners as AI workloads shift to cheaper, edge-based local models. Bernstein re-stoked the crypto bulls, forecasting that U.S. debt debasement will propel Bitcoin to \$300,000 by 2029, a trend expected to supercharge MicroStrategy. Pundits also highlighted shifts among ultra-high-net-worth investors, discussing a major move into equities in **[Family offices shift to stocks: Here’s what to know](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/26/family-offices-shift-to-stocks-heres-what-to-know.html)**.
