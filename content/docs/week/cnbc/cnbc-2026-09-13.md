# CNBC — 2026-09-13

## Lead Story
Crude oil prices surged above \$107 per barrel after Saudi Arabia shuttered its 7-million-barrel-per-day East-West pipeline following drone attacks, compounding global market distress alongside an unprecedented AI safety crisis that sent stock futures tumbling.

## Markets & Economics
U.S. stock futures slid Sunday night—with Dow futures down 179 points (-0.4%), S&P 500 futures losing 0.6%, and Nasdaq-100 futures dropping 1.2%—extending last week's pullbacks as [WTI crude](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/13/oil-price-iran-war-strait-hormuz-saudi-pipeline.html) rose 2.8% to \$102.87 and [Brent crude](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/13/oil-price-iran-war-strait-hormuz-saudi-pipeline.html) jumped 3.1% to \$107.87 per barrel following the Saudi pipeline shutdown. Investors are hyper-focused on Wednesday's Federal Reserve policy meeting, where CME FedWatch futures price in an 86% probability of an interest rate hike following August's 0.3% core CPI increase, alongside upcoming retail sales and housing starts data. In maritime trade, the Breakwave Tanker Shipping ETF (**[BWET](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/13/iran-war-oil-prices-freight-tankers-shipping.html)**) has soared over 3,600% year-to-date as Middle East oil tanker shipping rates jumped nearly 500% amid 140,000+ vessel disruptions and ongoing military conflict in the Strait of Hormuz. On fiscal policy, bipartisan support is growing in Congress to address Social Security's \$30 trillion 75-year shortfall before 2032 by lifting the \$184,500 payroll tax cap on high earners.

## Business & Earnings
Oracle founder Larry Ellison canceled his 10b5-1 trading plan to sell up to 50 million shares worth \$7.5 billion just one day after its disclosure, preserving his 40%+ stake as the company manages AI infrastructure debt and a 23% YTD stock decline. In artificial intelligence, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman confirmed the company will not go public this year due to safety concerns, aligning with Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's manifesto calling on frontier labs to pace model capabilities. Market analyst Jim Cramer warned that a self-imposed AI slowdown could weigh on Nvidia and data center supply chains, noting his Charitable Trust cut exposure to Corning while favoring defensive diversification into Cardinal Health and BNY. Meanwhile, prediction market platforms Kalshi and Polymarket logged record volume—including \$2.3 billion on Kalshi for college football opening day—as they launch midterm election hubs and expand sports partnerships.

## Investing & Commentary
