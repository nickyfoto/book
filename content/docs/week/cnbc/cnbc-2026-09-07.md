# CNBC — 2026-09-07

## Lead Story
**An escalation in the U.S.-Iran war has driven crude prices to a six-week high, with Brent crude hitting \$97.93 a barrel after U.S. forces struck three Iranian oil tankers in response to ballistic missile attacks. This geopolitical flare-up, combined with mounting U.S.-Canada trade tensions, has sent Dow futures tumbling over 300 points as markets brace for renewed inflationary pressures.**

## Markets & Economics
**A historic capital flow reversal is underway as foreign appetite for U.S. debt sours, with **[Deutsche Bank](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/07/treasurys-bonds-investors-deficit-debt.html)** reporting that overseas equity inflows have overtaken fixed income for the first time outside of the GFC. The 10-year Treasury yield is facing a crucial test at 4.8%, a level that strategists warn could trigger broad asset repricing if surpassed, according to **[Miller Tabak & Co.](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/07/us-treasury-yields-markets-scott-bessent.html)**. Simultaneously, **[Japan's foreign reserves](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/07/japan-foreign-reserves-yen-intervention.html)** suffered a record \$80 billion (6.18%) plunge in August due to the finance ministry's aggressive yen-buying interventions. These moves come as traders price in a 60% probability of a Federal Reserve rate hike next week following August's robust payroll expansion, detailed in **[UBS's rate-hike analysis](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/07/ubs-federal-reserve-interest-rate-hike-investing.html)**.**

## Business & Earnings
**In corporate restructuring, luxury automaker **[Jaguar Land Rover](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/07/jaguar-land-rover-jlr-job-cuts-autos.html)** is launching a voluntary redundancy program to cut up to 4,000 jobs over the next two years as part of a £1.7 billion (\$2.3 billion) cost-saving initiative. Meanwhile, Beijing is orchestrating a downsized, tobacco-backed **[360 billion yuan (\$53.6 billion) recapitalization package](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/07/china-state-banks-lenders-insurers-capital-solvency-bankrupt-nim-.html)** for state lenders and insurers, which includes Agricultural Bank of China raising up to 160 billion yuan and China Life receiving 35 billion yuan. Adding to trade headwinds, President Trump announced on social media that Canadian aerospace giant **[Bombardier](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/07/trump-bombardier-cant-sell-in-us-unless-canada-aerospace-giant-builds-there.html)** will be barred from selling in the U.S. unless it establishes domestic manufacturing, just ahead of \$20 billion in Canadian retaliatory tariffs.**

## Investing & Commentary
**Amid volatility, Cohen & Steers strategist Jeff Palma is advising exposure to real assets—such as real estate, infrastructure, and natural resource equities—arguing that a structurally higher neutral rate reflects a resilient U.S. economy, as seen in his **[CNBC video interview](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/07/this-investor-thinks-the-us-economy-is-strong-enough-to-handle-higher-rates.html)**. On the equities front, Morgan Stanley's Joseph Moore reiterated Nvidia (**[NVDA](https://www.tipranks.com/stocks/nvda)**) as his top semiconductor pick with a raised price target of \$300, while BMO Capital reiterated a buy rating and \$119 target on Uber (**[UBER](https://www.tipranks.com/stocks/uber)**) due to its expanding autonomous vehicle platform strategy. Additionally, KeyBanc analyst John Vinh reaffirmed a buy rating and \$400 price target on Marvell Technology (**[MRVL](https://www.tipranks.com/stocks/mrvl)**), pointing to accelerating AI networking demand in its data center business.**
