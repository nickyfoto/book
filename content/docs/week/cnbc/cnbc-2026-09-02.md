# CNBC — 2026-09-02

## Lead Story
The re-escalation of military conflict between the U.S. and Iran in the Gulf has triggered a massive shockwave across global financial markets, sending crude prices surging and driving global bond yields to multi-year highs. Investors are aggressively repricing inflation risk as central bank officials signal willingness to resume rate-hiking cycles.

## Markets & Economics
U.S. stocks snapped a three-day slide with the Dow gaining nearly 300 points, even as the 10-year Treasury yield scaled a multi-year high of 4.818% before easing on Wednesday. The labor market showed clear signs of cooling in the **[ADP Private Payrolls Video](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/02/private-payrolls-rose-by-38000-in-august-fewer-than-expected-adp-reports.html)**, which reported that private hiring grew by just 38,000 in August, missing the 47,000 consensus estimate. In energy markets, West Texas Intermediate (WTI) settled at \$91.78 per barrel and Brent crude jumped over 2% to \$96.59 following U.S.-Iran strikes in the Gulf, though in the **[Energy Secretary Chris Wright Video](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/02/energy-sec-wright-more-than-17-million-barrels-of-oil-transited-through-strait-of-hormuz-on-monday.html)**, the chief noted that a wartime record of over 17 million barrels per day still transited the critical Strait of Hormuz on Monday. Central bank policy remains a wildcard as seen in the **[New York Fed President John Williams Interview](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/02/watch-cnbcs-full-interview-with-new-york-fed-president-john-williams.html)**, where he adopted a wait-and-see approach, urging investors to focus on a resilient, technology-driven economy rather than temporary market volatility.

## Business & Earnings
The technology sector was dominated by massive AI-driven earnings reports: **[Dell Technologies](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/dell-has-more-than-tripled-in-2026-two-analysts-say-it-has-more-room-to-run.html)** surged after lifting its fiscal 2027 revenue forecast to \$192 billion on robust server demand, while **[Snowflake](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/snowflake-snow-q2-earnings-report-2027.html)** rocketed over 20% after-hours on a Q2 top-and-bottom-line beat fueled by its CoCo AI coding agent. In contrast, **[Broadcom](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/broadcom-avgo-q3-earnings-report-2026.html)** slipped after-hours on a soft fourth-quarter revenue guide of \$34.8 billion, though CEO Hock Tan came out swinging on the conference call with a bold projection to double AI revenues to \$230 billion by fiscal 2028. In energy, the **[Chevron CEO Mike Wirth Interview](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/02/chevron-ceo-mike-wirth-additional-production-in-venezuela-will-not-compete-with-u-s-production.html)** highlighted the company’s new \$7 billion investment to more than double Venezuelan crude production to 600,000 barrels per day, assuring that the output will not compete with domestic U.S. production. Additionally, the **[Lowe’s CEO Marvin Ellison Interview](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/02/loweas-ceo-marvin-ellison-on-launch-of-the-building-futures-skilled-trades-coalition.html)** featured the launch of a new coalition to train one million skilled workers, while **[Uber](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/uber-to-cut-10percent-of-workforce-in-bid-to-move-simpler-and-faster.html)** announced plans to slash 10% of its workforce to simplify management structures.

## Investing & Commentary
CNBC's **[Jim Cramer](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/cramer-says-investors-arent-ditching-tech-they-want-cheaper-stocks.html)** emphasized that the tech sell-off is not a rejection of AI, but rather a rotation out of high-multiple names into cheaper plays like Dell and Nvidia as rising bond yields force multiple compression. Meanwhile, options trader **[Tony Zhang](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/time-to-start-fading-caterpillar-stock-says-trader-tony-zhang.html)** warned it is time to fade industrial giant Caterpillar, proposing a bearish put vertical spread on expectations that public resistance to data center construction will dent its AI power backlog. For investors looking for value, Deutsche Bank upgraded **[SiriusXM](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/siriusxm-is-being-overlooked-by-the-street-deutsche-bank-says.html)** to buy with a \$45 price target, highlighting an overlooked exclusive audio advertising partnership with YouTube that could generate \$2 billion in annual incremental revenue by 2029.
