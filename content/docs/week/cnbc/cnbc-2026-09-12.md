# CNBC — 2026-09-12

## Lead Story
Escalating conflict in the Persian Gulf sent WTI crude surging past \$102 a barrel and pushed market odds for a September Federal Reserve rate hike to 87%, as headline August CPI rose 3.4% year-over-year. Meanwhile, AI tech leaders executed a major strategic shift as **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman ruled out a 2026 IPO, backing **Anthropic** CEO Dario Amodei’s proposal to intentionally pace advanced model development.

## Markets & Economics
U.S. equities posted a losing week as the **Dow Jones Industrial Average** fell 1.6%, the **S&P 500** slipped 0.8%, and the **Nasdaq Composite** dropped 0.7%, even as Friday's 1% rally offered brief respite from surging energy costs and Treasury yields. WTI crude spiked over 9% on the week to top \$102 per barrel and Brent crude reached \$108 before settling at \$104.61, driven by drone attacks on Saudi Arabia's 7 million barrel-per-day East-West pipeline and Houthi gains near the Bab el-Mandeb Strait (see **[Oil’s roundtrip back to \$100](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/12/oils-roundtrip-back-to-100-why-china-could-determine-what-happens-next.html)** and **[Trump sees Iran war ending soon](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/12/trump-sees-iran-war-ending-very-soon-oil-prices-then-falling.html)**). Macro pressures mounted as August CPI rose 3.4% year-over-year against 3.1% wage growth, causing real average hourly earnings to fall 0.3% and forcing consumers toward discount retailers (detailed in **[Inflation is outpacing wage growth again](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/12/inflation-is-outpacing-wage-growth-again-squeezing-americans-paychecks.html)**). With the 10-year Treasury yield peaking above 4.95%, futures markets now price an 87% probability of a Federal Reserve rate hike at the upcoming September 15–16 policy meeting under Fed Chair Kevin Warsh (covered in **[Stocks stumble on inflation fears](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/12/stocks-stumble-on-inflation-fears-but-2-names-give-us-reasons-to-stay-bullish.html)**).

## Business & Earnings
In frontier tech, **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman officially ruled out a 2026 IPO, publicly uniting with **Anthropic** CEO Dario Amodei and **Elon Musk** on a proposal to establish independent safety evaluation standards and pace capability scaling (see **[OpenAI rules out IPO this year](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/12/anthropics-amodei-proposes-plan-to-slow-the-pace-of-advancing-ai-capabilities.html)**). **Oracle** founder Larry Ellison abruptly canceled his 10b5-1 trading plan to sell up to 50 million shares valued at \$7.5 billion just 24 hours after disclosing it, maintaining his 40% stake following Oracle's 121% jump in cloud infrastructure revenue (covered in **[Larry Ellison nixes plan to offload stock](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/12/larry-ellison-nixes-plan-to-offload-up-to-7point5-billion-worth-of-oracle-stock-.html)**). Aerospace giant **Boeing** averted a potential strike by reaching a tentative four-year agreement with its 17,000-member SPEEA engineering union, providing an immediate 10% wage increase to keep delayed 737 MAX 10 and 777-9 certification programs on track (detailed in **[Boeing and engineers’ union reach contract agreement](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/12/boeing-and-engineers-union-reach-tentative-contract-agreement.html)**). **GE Aerospace’s** \$11.75 billion acquisition of Consolidated Precision Products at 26 times EBITDA signaled a massive valuation boost for **Berkshire Hathaway’s** Precision Castparts, with analysts pricing the unit near \$100 billion (see **[Buffett’s confidence pays off](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/12/buffetts-confidence-in-troubled-decade-old-acquisition-finally-pays-off.html)**). **General Motors** partnered with Peak Energy to deploy domestic sodium-ion batteries by 2029 for data center energy storage, seeking to leapfrog China's battery dominance while rival **Ford Motor** faces Transportation Department scrutiny over Chinese CATL licensing (analyzed in **[GM plans U.S. battery development](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/12/gm-us-battery-development-ford-china-ties.html)**).

## Investing & Commentary
**Bank of America** reiterated buy ratings on high-conviction ideas, elevating **Microsoft’s** price target to \$600 based on accelerating Azure growth (43% YoY) and touting regional lender **First Horizon** as a defensive yield play alongside **Timken** and **UBS** (see **[Bank of America says these stocks have more room to run](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/12/these-stocks-have-more-room-to-run-bank-of-america-says.html)**). Warning of "eerie parallels" to the fall 2018 market correction driven by surging oil and bond yields, CNBC's Jim Cramer advised taking profits off the table to build cash for high-quality dip-buying, while maintaining top Magnificent Seven ratings on **Meta Platforms** and **Apple** (see **[Cramer: This market has eerie parallels with 2018](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/11/cramer-market-eerie-parallels-2018-what-it-means-for-investors.html)**). Institutional fixed-income investors are tracking an emerging opportunity in insurance-linked securities, as \$20 billion-plus hyperscale AI data center campuses push traditional property coverage limits toward the \$66 billion catastrophe bond market (analyzed in **[Why data centers could be the next big market for catastrophe bonds](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/12/ai-data-centers-catastrophe-bonds-insurance.html)**).
