# CNBC — 2026-09-04

## Lead Story
The blockbusting August jobs report completely shattered consensus estimates by adding 162,000 nonfarm payrolls, triggering a sharp spike in short-term Treasury yields and forcing traders to aggressively dial up the probability of a September rate hike. This unexpected hiring surge was almost entirely driven by women, who accounted for a whopping 98% of the net monthly gains.

## Markets & Economics
Following the stellar labor data, the 2-year Treasury yield surged over 7 basis points to 4.425%, reaching its highest level since January 2025, while the CME FedWatch tool showed the probability of a September rate hike rising to 58%. This economic strength prompted President Donald Trump to issue a sweeping Truth Social ultimatum demanding that Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh slash interest rates or face a complete U.S. trade halt with deficit-harboring nations. At the same time, Allianz Chief Economic Advisor Mohamed El-Erian cautioned in an **[on-air interview](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/el-erian-government-bond-selloff.html)** that the global bond sell-off is far from over as traditional buyers of U.S. debt face domestic and geopolitical pressures. Adding to the market's unease, diesel fuel prices hit record highs—reaching \$5.85 nationally and \$7.70 in California—due to conflict-led refinery shutdowns, raising concerns that shipping costs will continue to feed sticky inflation.

## Business & Earnings
**[Tesla](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/teslas-stock-drops-as-cybercab-update-underwhelms-nhtsa-probe.html)** shares fell 6% after its unstreamed Cybercab launch failed to impress Wall Street, compounded by a new NHTSA safety probe into the vehicle's compliance. Conversely, European auto giant **[Volkswagen](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/volkswagen-job-cuts-tariffs-china.html)** surged nearly 6% as investors welcomed its Future Plan 2030 restructuring, which slashes an additional 50,000 positions to navigate Chinese competition and tariff headwinds. In tech, **[Nvidia](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/nvidia-hugging-face-deal-chips.html)** confirmed its \$12.9 billion acquisition of AI developer hub Hugging Face to safeguard its open-source software moat, reflecting a broader strategic push that has ballooned its equity investments to \$99 billion. Meanwhile, **[Lululemon Athletica](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/stocks-making-the-biggest-moves-premarket-.html)** shares plummeted up to 20% after the firm posted disappointing comparable sales and cut its full-year net revenue forecast to between \$10.35 billion and \$10.5 billion.

## Investing & Commentary
CNBC's Jim Cramer declared a **[Revenge of the Magnificent Seven](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/magnificent-seven-jim-cramer-says-time-to-buy.html)**, arguing that months of underperformance have left tech giants like Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, and Nvidia trading at attractive discounts relative to their future AI returns. In sector calls, Citi urged investors to buy the dip on network provider **[Ciena](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/buy-the-dip-on-this-networking-stock-that-set-to-more-than-double-says-citi.html)**, predicting over 100% upside to a \$65 price target as AI data center interconnects drive optical transport growth. Meanwhile, Deutsche Bank raised its price target on **[Robinhood](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/robinhood-may-pop-on-one-of-latest-crypto-ventures-deutsche-bank-says.html)** to \$136, highlighting that accelerating fees from the newly launched Robinhood Chain are tracking significantly ahead of previous estimates.
