# CNBC — 2026-08-23

## Lead Story
**Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is set to announce a historical "economic D-Day" campaign of coordinated sanctions against Iran, a major geopolitical escalation that has sent U.S. stock futures slipping as investors brace for prolonged economic warfare.** **In an exclusive interview, Bessent warned global allies that they must choose to stand with or against the U.S. as it deploys its "full might" to completely collapse the Iranian economy.**

## Markets & Economics
**U.S. stock index futures are posting modest declines following a challenging week that saw the S&P 500 drop 1.4% and the Nasdaq lose 2% due to skyrocketing global bond yields, with the 30-year Treasury yield scaling to a near 20-year high above 5.3%.** **Investors are heavily focused on newly appointed Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh's upcoming maiden address at the Jackson Hole symposium this Friday, which takes on added weight ahead of Wednesday's July PCE inflation print, expected to show a 3.2% year-over-year increase.** **In a dramatic shift in market leadership, the equal-weighted S&P 500 is currently outperforming the market-weighted index by roughly 3% year-to-date, pushing the popular **[Invesco S&P 500 Equal Weight ETF (RSP)](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/RSP/)** above \$100 billion in assets for the first time as mega-cap tech momentum cools.** **Although David Zervos of Jefferies argues that the Treasury’s tactical long-end buyback program delivers QE-like reflationary support, the persistent threat of an extended Middle Eastern conflict continues to keep market participants on edge.**
