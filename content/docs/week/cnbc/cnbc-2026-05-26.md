# CNBC — 2026-05-26

## Lead Story
Global markets are navigating a delicate geopolitical balancing act as [U.S. forces conduct "self-defense strikes" in Iran](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/26/us-conducts-self-defense-strikes-in-iran-as-trump-seeks-peace-deal.html) amid ongoing peace negotiations under the Trump administration. This fragile diplomacy is keeping energy markets highly volatile, with traders fearing potential toll charges or prolonged closures at the critical Strait of Hormuz.

## Markets & Economics
Despite the geopolitical overhang in the Middle East, U.S. equities are demonstrating remarkable resilience, with the [S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite climbing to fresh closing records](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/26/stock-market-today-live-updates.html). Tech leadership remains firmly intact, highlighted by [Micron Technology surging 18% to cross the $1 trillion market cap threshold](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/26/micron-stock-trillion-market-cap.html) for the first time, fueled by an AI-driven global memory shortage. In the bond market, [Treasury yields slid following the Memorial Day break](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/26/treasury-yields-us-strikes-on-iran-oil-bonds.html), with the benchmark 10-year note falling more than 6 basis points to 4.510% as traders weighed the prospects of an Iran peace deal. Meanwhile across the Atlantic, the [European Central Bank remains steadfastly hawkish](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/26/ecb-villeroy-inflation-iran-war.html), with the Bank of France governor warning they will "do what is necessary" to tame inflation, virtually cementing expectations for a rate hike at the central bank's next meeting.

## Business & Earnings
In the luxury automotive sector, [Ferrari shares tumbled up to 7%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/26/ferrari-stock-shares-luce-electric-vehicle-ev-launch.html) following online backlash over the debut of its [first fully electric vehicle, the 'Luce'](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/05/26/ferraris-electric-ambitions-with-the-luce-launch.html), a five-seater co-designed by Jony Ive that CEO Benedetto Vigna championed heavily on the network today. Corporate governance took center stage as [BP ousted Chairman Albert Manifold](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/26/bp-chair-albert-manifold-removed-conduct-governance-issues.html) over "serious" conduct concerns, while [Dropbox CEO Drew Houston announced he is stepping down](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/26/dropbox-ceo-drew-houston-ashraf-alkarmi.html) to become executive chairman after 19 years leading the cloud pioneer. On the earnings front, [AutoZone is pacing for its worst trading day since March 2020](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/26/autozone-stock-on-pace-for-worst-trading-day-since-march-2020.html) due to margin compression and international growth concerns, despite actually beating Wall Street estimates. Elsewhere, the space economy continues to integrate into commercial transport, with [American Airlines tapping SpaceX’s Starlink](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/26/american-airlines-spacex-starlink.html) to provide in-flight Wi-Fi across more than 500 planes.

## Investing & Commentary
Market strategists are increasingly bullish on defensive and unloved sectors, with [JPMorgan flagging low-volatility, dividend-paying stocks](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/26/jpmorgan-says-its-time-to-buy-unloved-safe-stocks-that-pay-dividends.html) as an attractive entry point amid broader market uncertainty. In the tech space, [Bank of America argues that the shift toward "agentic" AI uniquely benefits Apple](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/26/bank-of-america-says-a-shift-in-ai-is-playing-into-apples-hands.html), positioning the stock favorably heading into its crucial WWDC event on June 8. For investors tracking geopolitical risks, [Piper Sandler warns that the prolonged closure of the Strait of Hormuz](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/26/piper-sandler-says-strait-of-hormuz-to-remain-closed-for-months-and-oil-to-hit-new-highs.html) could propel crude oil to new highs this summer, making tactical energy exposure a critical hedge.
