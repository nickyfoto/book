# CNBC — 2026-07-29

## Lead Story
The Federal Reserve held its key interest rate steady at 3.5% to 3.75% amid a highly divided committee where three regional presidents dissented and pushed for an immediate rate hike. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh offered few forward-looking cues during his press conference, stoking market uncertainty and sending long-dated Treasury yields soaring to their highest levels since 2007.

## Markets & Economics
The U.S. Federal Reserve's decision to maintain rates sparked a stark market reaction, with the [30-year Treasury yield](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/US30Y/) spiking above 5.2% while short-end rates dipped. DoubleLine Capital CEO Jeffrey Gundlach noted that the bond market is essentially telling Warsh to act decisively on inflation if he genuinely wants to hit his 2% target. Elsewhere, oil prices jumped sharply after [Iran launched a surprise ballistic missile attack](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/us-iran-war-hormuz-centcom.html) on U.S. forces, pushing West Texas Intermediate crude up nearly 7% to near $90 a barrel. These geopolitical tensions have exacerbated supply concerns just as the [U.S. Strategic Petroleum Reserve faces operational strain](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/us-strategic-petroleum-reserve-spr-iran-oil-strait-hormuz.html) following historic, massive drawdowns over the past few years.

## Business & Earnings
Mega-cap tech earnings are driving massive post-market moves, with [Microsoft](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/microsoft-msft-q4-earnings-report-2026.html) jumping 7% in extended trading on accelerating Azure growth and [Meta Platforms](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/meta-q2-earnings-report-2026.html) sinking nearly 10% after issuing a soft revenue forecast. Auto giant Ford surged 5% after topping Q2 earnings expectations, with CFO Sherry House highlighting the firm's resilience to recent supply chain headwinds in a [CNBC interview](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/07/28/ford-cfo-sherry-house-talks-earnings-as-stock-pops-in-extended-trading.html). Over in Europe, [UBS](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/ubs-earnings-ai-correction-stocks-swiss-bank.html) CEO Sergio Ermotti posted a 64% surge in pre-tax profit to $3.6 billion and dismissed recent AI-driven market volatility as a "healthy" correction during a Squawk Box Europe appearance. Additionally, Tim Cook is preparing for his final earnings call as [Apple's](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/tim-cooks-last-earnings-call-comes-at-momentous-time-for-apple-.html) CEO just as the company navigates a severe memory chip shortage and rolls out a new iPhone leasing program with Klarna.

## Investing & Commentary
A notable market rotation is underway as investors cycle out of high-flying AI infrastructure plays and into value and defensive sectors like consumer staples and healthcare. Jim Cramer highlighted [Seagate](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/jim-cramer-says-seagate-is-the-key-to-this-market-after-blowout-earnings.html) as the "key to this market" after its picture-perfect earnings, suggesting its performance might be the ultimate test for the beleaguered AI sector's momentum. Meanwhile, Fairlead's Katie Stockton warned there is [still risk to the downside for the Nasdaq 100](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/07/29/theres-still-risk-to-the-downside-for-the-nasdaq-100-says-fairleads-katie-stockton.html), pushing analysts to recommend a pivot to resilient, cash-generating businesses like Costco and ServiceNow.
