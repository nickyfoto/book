# CNBC — Week of 2026-08-28 to 2026-09-04

## Story of the Week
**Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh’s hawkish Jackson Hole warning and a blockbuster 162,000 August payroll print shattered near-term rate-cut hopes, driving the 10-year Treasury yield to a multi-year high of 4.818%. This macroeconomic squeeze was compounded by escalating geopolitical tensions as U.S. military strikes on Iranian targets in the Strait of Hormuz spiked WTI crude above \$91 a barrel. The resulting collision of persistent inflation fears, surging borrowing costs, and energy supply shocks forced a sharp repricing across global equity and bond markets.**

## Markets & Economics
- **Rate Hike Pressures Mount on Hawkish Fed Rhetoric and Surprise Jobs Surge** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/28/treasury-yields-jackson-hole.html): Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh discarded forward guidance at Jackson Hole, warning that stubborn inflation leaves "work to do" and pushing September rate-hike odds up to 55%. The hawkish stance was cemented by a blockbusting August jobs report that added 162,000 nonfarm payrolls—98% driven by women—pushing September rate-hike odds to 58% and prompting a Truth Social warning from President Trump to cut rates or face trade halts.
- **Persian Gulf Escalation Spikes Oil Prices and Triggers Diesel Inflation Worries** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/02/energy-sec-wright-more-than-17-million-barrels-of-oil-transited-through-strait-of-hormuz-on-monday.html): After U.S. forces struck Iranian rocket launchers on Larak Island, West Texas Intermediate surged past \$91 per barrel and Brent crude reached \$96.59, though over 17 million barrels per day continued to transit the Strait of Hormuz. Fuel supply anxiety worsened as refinery shutdowns pushed domestic diesel prices to a record national average of \$5.85, raising shipping costs and feeding sticky consumer inflation.
- **Bond Market Meltdown Drives Borrowing Costs to Highest Levels Since January 2025** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/el-erian-government-bond-selloff.html): Geopolitical friction and hawkish central bank outlooks pushed the 10-year Treasury yield to a multi-year high of 4.818%, while average 30-year fixed mortgage rates surged to 6.87% and Japan's 10-year yield touched a 30-year high of 3%. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent defended U.S. debt management at the G20, but the Treasury was forced to double long-dated bond buybacks to \$4 billion to calm sovereign debt fears as total national debt crossed \$40 trillion.
- **Consumer Divergence Deepens Under Historic \$1.26 Trillion Credit Card Debt Burden** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/29/k-shaped-economy-c-shaped-e-shape.html): The University of Michigan consumer sentiment index tumbled 11% year-over-year as national gas prices at \$4.09 squeezed households, sparking debate over whether the economy is K-shaped, C-shaped, or E-shaped. This stress was mirrored in diverging corporate strategies, as Walmart funneled a \$2.9 billion tariff refund into consumer price cuts, while Home Depot and Target retained their refunds to protect margins.
- **ADP Private Payrolls Reveal Muted Hiring Expansion Ahead of Official Labor Data** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/02/private-payrolls-rose-by-38000-in-august-fewer-than-expected-adp-reports.html): Highlighting a divergence in labor indicators, private hiring grew by just 38,000 in August, missing the consensus estimate of 47,000. However, Fed officials like Governor Michael Barr and New York Fed President John Williams maintained a tight focus on secular economic resilience and technology-driven growth rather than short-term payroll swings.

## Business & Earnings
- **Enterprise AI Giants Surge on Stellar Server and Platform Monetization** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/dell-has-more-than-tripled-in-2026-two-analysts-say-it-has-more-room-to-run.html): Dell Technologies surged 10% after tripling its full-year AI server revenue projection to \$74 billion and lifting its revenue forecast to \$192 billion, while Snowflake rocketed over 20% on a Q2 beat driven by its CoCo AI coding agent. Salesforce climbed 22% on the week after generating strong operating margins and a record \$25 billion buyback, with CEO Marc Benioff dismissing software obsolescence fears, though Broadcom slipped on a soft Q4 guide despite projecting to double AI revenues to \$230 billion by fiscal 2028.
- **Corporate Shakeups and Mega-Deals Reshape Tech, Insurance, and Biotech Landscapes** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/31/end-of-an-era-at-apple-tim-cooks-last-day-as-ceo.html): Tim Cook ended his 15-year tenure as Apple CEO, passing the reins to John Ternus as Apple faces rising memory component costs that could compress margins by 1.4%. In dealmaking, Aon detailed a \$17 billion acquisition of USI Insurance Services, Eli Lilly deployed GLP-1 cash reserves to buy Merida Biosciences for up to \$2.88 billion, and Nvidia confirmed its \$12.9 billion acquisition of open-source AI hub Hugging Face.
- **Auto and Retail Sectors Launch Sweeping Restructurings to Protect Squeezed Margins** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/volkswagen-job-cuts-tariffs-china.html): Volkswagen shares surged 6% on its "Future Plan 2030," which slashes an additional 50,000 positions to navigate Chinese competition and tariff headwinds, whereas Tesla fell 6% after its unstreamed Cybercab launch failed to impress and triggered a new NHTSA safety probe. Elsewhere, Lululemon plummeted up to 20% after cutting its full-year guidance due to disappointing comparable sales, and Goodyear extended its restructuring timeline to address a \$453 million first-half loss and \$7 billion in debt.
- **Social Media Legal Exposure Mounts as OpenAI and Cybersecurity Players Scale Up** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/30/mike-moore-ag-social-media-settlement-big-tobacco.html): Meta agreed to a landmark youth safety settlement totaling \$17–18 billion with state attorneys general, establishing a regulatory framework that litigators aim to apply to competitors. Meanwhile, OpenAI’s ad division reached a \$1 billion annualized run rate, and Palo Alto Networks beat earnings and acquired agentic AI security startup Console as CEO Nikesh Arora warned that \$1 trillion in legacy cyber infrastructure is currently unequipped to handle AI-speed attacks.

## Investing & Commentary
- **Jim Cramer Champions "Revenge of the Magnificent Seven" While Adopting a Tactical Defense** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/magnificent-seven-jim-cramer-says-time-to-buy.html): Cramer declared a buying window for underperforming tech giants like Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, and Nvidia, but simultaneously raised cash in his Charitable Trust to 15% and exited Corning due to data center energy and political constraints. He also urged Nvidia to launch a "monster" half-trillion-dollar buyback program to address the wide discount between its fundamental growth and stock price performance.
- **Wall Street Strategists Warn of Treacherous September Seasonality and Recommend Value Rotations** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/cramer-says-investors-arent-ditching-tech-they-want-cheaper-stocks.html): Citadel's Scott Rubner warned that equity risk-reward is deteriorating as buyback blackouts start around September 12, while analysts noted that rising bond yields are driving healthy multiple compression and rotation into cheaper tech plays like Dell. Concurrently, Janus Henderson highlighted that an unusual gap between individual stock and index volatility makes covered-call writing highly lucrative in the current environment.
- **High-Conviction Sector Bets Focus on Infrastructure, Ag Equipment, and Audio Ad Partnerships** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/buy-the-dip-on-this-networking-stock-that-set-to-more-than-double-says-citi.html): Citi urged buying the dip on networking provider Ciena, predicting over 100% upside to \$65 as data centers expand optical transport, while Baird upgraded Deere with an \$800 price target as rising crop prices lift farmer margins. For value seekers, Deutsche Bank upgraded SiriusXM to Buy on an overlooked YouTube audio ad partnership projected to yield \$2 billion in annual incremental revenue by 2029, and raised Robinhood's target to \$136 on stronger-than-expected Robinhood Chain fees.
