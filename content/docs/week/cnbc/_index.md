# CNBC — Week of 2026-09-05 to 2026-09-11

## Story of the Week
U.S. military strikes disabling three Iranian crude carriers near the Strait of Hormuz effectively closed the strategic waterway, sending Brent crude past \$100 per barrel and national diesel prices to a record \$6.05 per gallon. The sudden energy shock reignited inflation concerns as August wholesale prices jumped 5.4% year-over-year and core CPI accelerated 0.3% month-over-month. Consequently, market expectations for a Federal Reserve rate hike at the September 15–16 meeting surged past 85%, pushing 10-year Treasury yields to 4.94% and 30-year mortgage rates above 7.07%.

## Markets & Economics
* **[U.S.-Iran Conflict Shutters Hormuz, Driving Crude Past \$100 and Diesel to \$6.05]** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/09/oil-prices-today-wti-brent-us-iran-hormuz-attacks.html): Military strikes on Iranian tankers off Kharg Island effectively closed the Strait of Hormuz, causing Brent crude futures to surge past \$101 per barrel and WTI above \$96 per barrel. The physical supply blockade drove national diesel prices to an all-time record of \$6.05 per gallon while OPEC+ kept its output policy unchanged for October.
* **[Inflation Re-accelerates as August PPI and Core CPI Exceed Forecasts]** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/11/consumer-prices-rose-0-point-4-percent-in-august-as-expected-core-inflation-was-higher-than-estimated.html): August wholesale inflation rose 5.4% year-over-year while core CPI accelerated 0.3% month-over-month (3.4% headline YoY), signaling persistent broad-based price pressures. The hotter prints fueled macro anxiety across equity and debt markets despite the Treasury Department expanding long-term buyback operations to \$6 billion.
* **[Treasury Yields Touch Multi-Year Highs as Fed Rate Hike Odds Surpass 85%]** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/07/ubs-federal-reserve-interest-rate-hike-investing.html): Futures market odds for a quarter-point rate hike at the September 15–16 FOMC meeting escalated from 58% to over 85% despite public political pushback from the White House. Fixed income markets repriced rapidly, sending 10-year Treasury yields to 4.94% and average 30-year fixed mortgage rates past 7.07%.
* **[ECB Hikes Rates While Foreign Appetite for U.S. Debt Weakens]** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/07/treasurys-bonds-investors-deficit-debt.html): The European Central Bank enacted a 25-basis-point rate hike as Eurozone energy inflation reached 14.3%. Simultaneously, foreign equity inflows into the U.S. surpassed fixed income for the first time outside the GFC, and Japan's foreign reserves dropped by a record \$80 billion due to aggressive currency intervention.
* **[U.S.-Canada Trade Escalates with Import Bans and Retaliatory Tariffs]** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/09/us-canada-trade-war-import-ban.html): Washington announced import bans on Canadian motorcycles, dairy, and alcohol alongside restrictions barring Bombardier from U.S. sales without domestic manufacturing. The restrictions followed Ottawa implementing retaliatory tariffs on \$20 billion of American exports.
* **[Raw Sugar Rallies 21.5% in Strongest Monthly Advance Since 2010]** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/06/sugar-is-outperforming-the-stock-market-this-year-whats-driving-it.html): Soft commodities posted historic gains as raw sugar climbed 20% year-to-date, driven by a projected 19% drop in EU sugar beet production and El Niño threats across Brazil and Thailand. Brazil's subsidized mills diverted sugarcane into biofuel production as crude topped \$90, further constraining global export availability.

## Business & Earnings
* **[Data Center Land Rush Triggers Power Bottlenecks and State Moratoriums]** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/06/the-real-estate-bet-behind-the-ai-infrastructure-boom.html): Commercial land purchases for data centers surged 79% to \$6 billion in H1 2026, pushing powered land in Northern Virginia past \$8 million per acre. Rapid land deployment faces growing political pushback, including a one-year hyperscale moratorium in New York and pending restrictions across nine additional states.
* **[Oracle, Dell, and Qualcomm Surge on Enterprise AI Infrastructure Demand]** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/09/10/oracle-shares-spike-on-earnings-and-revenue-beat.html): Oracle jumped 7% after cloud infrastructure revenue doubled to \$7.4 billion, while Dell rallied 10% on a \$95 billion server backlog. Additionally, Qualcomm sealed a multi-generational AI infrastructure deal with Amazon AWS featuring \$4 billion in stock warrants.
* **[AI "Model Fatigue" Sets In as Tech Giants Push Point Updates and Wall Street Automation]** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/10/openai-chatgpt-for-financial-services-targets-work-of-junior-bankers.html): Enterprise IT buyers faced release overload as Anthropic, Meta, Google, and OpenAI launched model updates within a single week. Meanwhile, OpenAI deployed "ChatGPT for Financial Services" with Morgan Stanley and Evercore, while Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face for \$12.9 billion.
* **[Major Megadeals and Keynotes Highlight GE Aerospace, Apple, and Berkshire Hathaway]** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/ge-aerospace-to-buy-castings-maker-cpp-for-nearly-12-billion.html): GE Aerospace agreed to purchase engine castings maker CPP for \$11.75 billion, while Apple CEO John Ternus debuted the \$1,999 foldable iPhone Duo. Separately, Berkshire Hathaway CEO Greg Abel detailed a \$10 billion direct stock purchase of Alphabet shares to fund Google's computer infrastructure.
* **[Biopharma Divergence: Trial Failures Slam Novartis as AstraZeneca and J&J Advance]** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/novartis-novn-del-desiran-drug-trial.html): Novartis plummeted nearly 9% and Amgen dropped 10% following Phase 3 trial failures for del-desiran and pelacarsen. Conversely, AstraZeneca published positive Phase 3 COPD trial results and Johnson & Johnson secured FDA approval for its Ottava surgical robotics platform.

## Investing & Commentary
* **[Cramer and Strategists Advocate Pivoting to Energy Infrastructure and Defensive Real Assets]** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/jim-cramer-investors-focused-ai-stocks.html): Jim Cramer urged rotating out of overcrowded AI trades into high-yielding energy infrastructure (Enbridge, Enterprise Products) and defensive value names (BNY, Kimberly-Clark). Concurrently, Cohen & Steers advised allocating toward real assets to navigate higher neutral interest rates.
* **[Wall Street Issues High-Conviction Calls on Tech, FinTech, and Transportation]** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/09/affirm-is-a-high-quality-growth-story-loop-capital-says.html): Morgan Stanley reiterated Nvidia as a top pick (\$300 PT), while Piper Sandler initiated Nvidia (\$300 PT) and AMD (\$600 PT) as Overweight due to persistent GPU shortages. In consumer tech, Loop Capital initiated Affirm with a \$105 target, and Morgan Stanley noted that \$6+ diesel costs enhance the cost savings thesis for Tesla's Semi.
* **[Bullish Soft Commodity Calls and Private Market Portfolio Shifts Take Center Stage]** · [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/06/sugar-is-outperforming-the-stock-market-this-year-whats-driving-it.html): Citi named raw sugar its highest-conviction bullish agricultural trade with a 19-cent price target amid global supply deficits. To capitalize on AI infrastructure trends, BlackRock recommended modifying the standard 60/40 portfolio to a 50/30/20 mix incorporating private market alternatives.
