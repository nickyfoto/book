# CNBC — Week of 2026-06-27 to 2026-07-03

## Story of the Week
A shockingly weak June jobs report severely cooled fears of an overheating economy, essentially pricing out a near-term Federal Reserve rate hike and sparking a massive sector rotation. The U.S. economy added a mere 57,000 jobs while a staggering 720,000 workers exited the labor force, sending the Dow Jones Industrial Average surging nearly 600 points to a record close of 52,900.07 as investors aggressively dumped high-flying semiconductors for cyclical laggards.

## Markets & Economics
* **[Small-cap stocks enjoy best first half since 1991 as AI trade expands]** · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/small-cap-stocks-enjoy-best-first-half-since-1991-as-ai-trade-expands.html): Small-cap equities surged more than 21% year-to-date, marking their best first half in 35 years as the AI capital expenditure boom began trickling down the broader supply chain.
* **[Oil prices retreat amid mixed messaging on peace talks]** · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/oil-prices-brent-wti-crude-trump-iran.html): Brent crude plummeted roughly 21% for its steepest monthly decline since 2020 after the U.S. and Iran reached a ceasefire in Doha, allowing 40 million barrels of Iranian oil to safely exit the Strait of Hormuz.
* **[Japan yen falls to lowest level since 1986]** · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/japan-yen-falls-lowest-level-since-1986-dollar-intervention-risk.html): Driven by wide U.S.-Japan interest rate differentials fueling the carry trade, the Japanese yen sank to a 40-year low of 162.83 against the U.S. dollar, keeping markets on high alert for currency intervention. 
* **[Supreme Court lets presidents fire independent regulators, rules for Trump in FTC case]** · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/29/supreme-court-trump-slaughter-ftc.html): A landmark 6-3 Supreme Court ruling overturned long-standing precedent to grant the President authority to fire independent regulators, clearing a potential path for President Trump's stated intent to remove Federal Reserve Governor Lisa Cook.

## Business & Earnings
* **Comcast Sparks Media Spinoffs:** Comcast shares skyrocketed 23% after the telecommunications giant announced plans to spin off NBCUniversal and its cable networks into a standalone publicly traded company to unlock strategic flexibility.
* **Meta's Cloud Ambitions:** Meta shares jumped up to 11% following reports that the social media giant is building a new cloud-computing business to sell excess AI capacity, a move designed to monetize massive infrastructure investments though it risks diluting lucrative advertising margins.
* **Nike's Turnaround Stalls:** Nike stock tumbled as a 12% revenue drop in Greater China to $1.30 billion overshadowed a fiscal fourth-quarter earnings beat, leaving investors highly cautious over the brand's struggling global position.
* **Anthropic's Export Win:** The Trump administration officially lifted export controls on Anthropic's restricted Mythos 5 and Claude Fable 5 models, ending a tense standoff and allowing the startup to deploy its advanced AI to trusted corporate and federal partners.
* **SpaceX's Meteoric Rise:** Following a historic $2 trillion IPO, SpaceX was officially added to the Nasdaq-100 index, prompting a flurry of immediate stock purchases by congressional lawmakers sitting on powerful financial and armed services committees.

## Investing & Commentary
* **The Shifting AI Trade:** Jim Cramer and top Wall Street analysts argue the AI trade has decisively shifted away from the "Magnificent Seven" hyperscalers, advising investors to buy the dip on hardware suppliers and interconnect providers—like Intel, Micron, and AMD—who are actively capturing the massive data center spending.
* **Safe Havens No More:** Wealth strategists are warning that traditional market safety nets like gold, Treasurys, and the yen are breaking down, as these assets increasingly trade on their own volatile macro fundamentals rather than offering reliable blanket protection during risk-off events.
