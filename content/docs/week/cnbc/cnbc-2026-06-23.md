# CNBC — 2026-06-23

## Lead Story
A vicious [global tech sell-off](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/tech-stocks-sell-off-mag7-samsung-sk-hynix.html) sent shockwaves through international markets, underscored by a massive 10% collapse in South Korea's Kospi as investors aggressively dumped semiconductor and artificial intelligence darlings.

## Markets & Economics
International markets took a severe hit as the rotation out of the "Magnificent Seven" bled into global equities, headlined by historic drops in memory chip giants SK Hynix and Samsung that triggered a [10% plunge in South Korea's Kospi](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/22/stock-market-today-live-updates.html). Concurrently, the U.S. Treasury issued a [60-day sanctions waiver](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/us-iran-oil-sanction-relief-strait-of-hormuz-peace-deal-.html) allowing Iranian oil sales, easing energy supply concerns and unlocking billions in revenue for Tehran as peace talks progress. Domestically, the [S&P manufacturing PMI](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/factory-job-cuts-reported-around-financial-crisis-and-covid-levels-for-june-sp-says.html) printed better than expected at 55.7, but underlying data revealed factory job cuts running at their highest levels since 2009 amid escalating costs. Adding to the geopolitical momentum, the U.S. Senate advanced an [Iran war powers resolution](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/thune-congress-vote-trump-iran-deal.html) to end hostilities, though Republican leaders are demanding a formal vote on any final deal to review potential sanctions relief.

## Business & Earnings
[FedEx](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/fedex-fdx-q4-2026-earnings.html) delivered a strong fiscal fourth quarter, posting adjusted earnings of $6.31 per share on $25.01 billion in revenue in its final reporting period before the FedEx Freight spinoff. [Oracle](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/oracle-ai-job-cuts-layoffs-21000.html) disclosed it shed 21,000 jobs, or nearly 13% of its workforce, over the past year to offset massive $55.7 billion capital expenditures for AI data centers. [SpaceX](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/spacex-stock-tech-sell-off.html) continues to face intense selling pressure, shedding 16% on Monday and wiping out $400 billion in market value, even as it taps banks for a [massive $25 billion debt sale](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/spacex-debt-bond-market-ipo.html). Finally, [Alphabet](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/alphabet-verizon-dow-djia.html) is set to replace Verizon in the Dow Jones Industrial Average, solidifying megacap tech's heavy influence on the blue-chip index.

## Investing & Commentary
Despite the violent tech shakeout, Fundstrat strategist Mark Newton sees the [Magnificent 7 pullback as a prime buying opportunity](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/magnificent-7-stock-decline-offers-buying-opportunity-fundstrat.html), advising investors to look for relative strength rather than indiscriminately chasing weakness. [Goldman Sachs](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/goldman-sachs-picks-best-hedges-for-a-rate-shock-scenario.html) is recommending investment-grade bond puts and long-dated payer options as the most effective hedges against a renewed rate shock under Fed Chair Kevin Warsh. For those looking to diversify away from the AI momentum trade, Evercore ISI highlighted ["negative beta" stocks](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/try-evercore-isis-negative-beta-stocks-to-diversify-away-from-ai-.html) like Mondelez International and Exxon Mobil to weather broader market corrections.
