# CNBC — 2026-07-14

## Lead Story
The standout macroeconomic event of the day is the unexpectedly cool June inflation report, which saw the consumer price index fall 0.4% for its sharpest monthly drop since April 2020. This data massively lowered the market's odds of a July Fed rate hike, shifting Wall Street's focus to a highly anticipated September cut.

## Markets & Economics
Headline inflation [fell 0.4% in June](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/consumer-price-index-inflation-report-june-2026.html), dragging the annual rate down to 3.5% and sending Treasury yields significantly lower. The core index was flat on the month, easing concerns about persistent consumer price pressures. Despite the softer data, newly appointed Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh emphasized in his [congressional testimony](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/watch-fed-chairman-kevin-warsh-testify-live-to-house-financial-services-committee.html) that the central bank remains resolute in restoring price stability across the board. Meanwhile, oil prices [surged over 2%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/oil-prices-today-brent-wti-hormuz-trump-toll-iran.html) (with Brent topping $85 a barrel) after President Trump announced plans to reinstate a naval blockade of Iranian ports and floated a controversial [20% shipping toll](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/13/trump-iran-hormuz-strait-charge-reimburse.html) on cargo passing through the Strait of Hormuz. 

## Business & Earnings
Wall Street's megabanks dominated the start of earnings season, with [Goldman Sachs](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/GS/) and [JPMorgan Chase](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/JPM/) posting record quarterly hauls driven by an AI-fueled "capex super cycle" that boosted equities trading and capital markets activity. Goldman reported a blowout $20.98 EPS on $20.34 billion in revenue, sending its stock up 7%. On the flip side, [IBM](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/IBM/) suffered a historic plunge of 25% after [warning of a massive Q2 miss](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/ibm-warns-second-quarter-earnings-fell-short-of-expectations.html) as clients diverted IT spend away from software and toward AI servers and memory. Additionally, [Lucid](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/LCID/) stock collapsed 40% before the EV maker [strongly denied rumors](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/lucid-stock-lcid-bankruptcy-report.html) it was considering going private or filing for bankruptcy.

## Investing & Commentary
Tech investor Chamath Palihapitiya sounded the alarm on corporate ["tokenmaxxing"](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/chamath-palihapitiya-ai-tokenmaxxing.html), warning that soaring AI consumption costs are slipping past CFOs and will soon dent enterprise earnings. Meanwhile, Jim Cramer dismissed concerns about broader AI market froth, arguing that cheap multiples on financial giants and robust corporate earnings make today's environment fundamentally different from the 2000 dot-com bubble. For defensive plays amidst the volatility, Todd Gordon flagged [Datadog](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/red-hot-ai-cloud-data-stock-now-priced-for-perfection-says-todd-gordon.html) as a technical breakout candidate, though he cautioned the stock is currently "priced for perfection".
