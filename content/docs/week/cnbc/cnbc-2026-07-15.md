# CNBC — 2026-07-15

## Lead Story
The AI capital expenditure supercycle officially crowned its unexpected winners—Wall Street megabanks—as Morgan Stanley posted record revenue on a 69% equities trading surge, while legacy tech giant IBM suffered its worst day on record, plummeting 25% following a sharp pivot in enterprise spending from software to hardware.

## Markets & Economics
Wholesale inflation [unexpectedly declined 0.3%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/wholesale-inflation-june-2026-.html) in June, driven by a sharp drop in gasoline prices that offered continued relief on the price front. In Washington, Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh defended his independence during [Senate testimony](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/watch-fed-chairman-kevin-warsh-testify-live-before-senate-banking-committee.html), while New York Fed President John Williams stated that inflation has [likely peaked](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/new-york-fed-president-williams-says-inflation-has-peaked-rates-well-positioned.html), projecting a drop to 3.25% by year-end. Meanwhile, U.S. forces launched [fresh strikes against Iran](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/oil-prices-today-brent-wti-hormuz-blockade.html) and reinstated a naval blockade in the Strait of Hormuz, sending Brent crude above $85 a barrel. Overseas, China reported a [sluggish 4.3% Q2 GDP growth](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/china-gdp-retail-sales-investment-june-.html), missing expectations and marking its weakest pace of expansion since 2022.

## Business & Earnings
Morgan Stanley joined JPMorgan Chase and Goldman Sachs in reporting a [blowout quarter](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/morgan-stanley-ms-earnings-q2-2026-.html), posting $3.46 in EPS on $21.35 billion in revenue. On the semiconductor front, ASML [hiked its full-year sales forecast](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/asml-2q-earnings-ai-chips-orders.html) to between 43 billion and 45 billion euros on ferocious AI chip demand, while Anthropic began lining up investor meetings ahead of a [potential mega-IPO](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/anthropic-ipo-banks-investor-meetings.html). In healthcare, Johnson & Johnson [beat Wall Street estimates](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/johnson-johnson-jnj-q2-earnings.html) with $25.31 billion in sales and raised its full-year outlook despite weakness in its MedTech unit. In massive M&A news, Stripe and Advent International submitted a [joint $53 billion offer](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/stripe-advent-offer-to-buy-paypal-for-more-than-53-billion-reuters.html) to acquire PayPal for $60.50 per share.

## Investing & Commentary
Warren Buffett criticized the current market environment, warning that it is "tough to find values when everybody is [preferring gambling](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/warren-buffett-on-the-market-today-its-tough-to-find-values-when-everybody-is-preferring-gambling.html)" while confirming he [initiated Berkshire's massive stake](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/warren-buffett-tells-cnbc-he-initiated-berkshire-hathaways-investment-in-alphabet.html) in Alphabet. Meanwhile, Jim Cramer demanded ["cold hard" proof](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/jim-cramer-needs-proof-ai-paying-off-.html) that heavy AI investments are actually paying off for corporate customers, cautioning that the recent Big Tech rally may be detached from fundamentals. UBS analysts remain extremely bullish on the AI hardware trade, [raising their price target on AMD](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/one-ai-chipmaker-is-a-double-in-three-months-ubs-sees-more-gains-ahead.html) to $700 ahead of the chipmaker's upcoming AI event.
