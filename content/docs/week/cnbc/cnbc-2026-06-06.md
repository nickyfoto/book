# CNBC — 2026-06-06

## Lead Story
A massive unwind in semiconductor stocks snapped the S&P 500's nine-week winning streak, triggered by Broadcom's earnings miss, a hot jobs report pushing the 10-year Treasury yield above 4.5%, and an impending deluge of tech equity offerings.

## Markets & Economics
The relentless "crash up" in chip stocks violently reversed on Friday, sending the **[VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/06/wall-streets-fear-gauge-punches-back-as-the-crash-up-in-chip-stocks-finally-reverses.html)** tumbling nearly 10% at its lows and triggering the biggest single-day spike in the VIX since March. Investors rotated aggressively out of tech and into lagging sectors like health care and financials, with names like Eli Lilly and Wells Fargo ending the week in the green. Further pressuring market liquidity is a looming tidal wave of equity supply: **[SpaceX](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/06/here-are-3-forces-that-drove-a-whirlwind-week-for-the-stock-market.html)** is planning a $75 billion stock sale at a $1.8 trillion valuation, Anthropic filed for an IPO at a $965 billion valuation, and Alphabet announced an $85 billion stock offering to fund its AI buildout. 

## Business & Earnings
Berkshire Hathaway's new CEO Greg Abel is moving fast, initiating a $6.8 billion acquisition of homebuilder Taylor Morrison and a $10 billion discounted investment in **[Alphabet's AI infrastructure](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/06/abel-goes-his-own-way-with-new-berkshire-investments-including-billions-for-ai.html)**. In European telecoms, a Bouygues-led consortium inked a massive **[$23.44 billion deal to acquire SFR](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/06/bouygues-led-consortium-signs-23point44-billion-deal-to-buy-sfr-from-altice-france.html)** from Altice France. Meanwhile, Chinese automakers like BYD and Geely are actively exploring North American partnerships and joint ventures to bypass fierce U.S. tariffs and establish a manufacturing footprint.

## Investing & Commentary
Bank of America named TSMC, Yum China, and Aramark as "compelling" buys, highlighting TSMC's structural AI tailwinds and **[Aramark's new hyperscaler contracts](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/06/five-stocks-to-buy-now-per-bank-of-america.html)** for data center services. Despite Bitcoin bleeding assets and dropping 16% this week, a new decentralized "hyperliquid" crypto asset (HYPE) is bucking the trend, with **[new spot ETFs from Bitwise and 21shares](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/06/bitcoin-price-crash-crypto-hype-hyperliquid-etfs.html)** pulling in roughly $150 million due to a unique token buyback model. Jim Cramer cautioned that the broader market's bull run could easily be derailed by the sheer volume of impending megacap stock issuances soaking up all available investor demand.
