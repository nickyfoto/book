# CNBC — 2026-09-08

## Lead Story
Escalating Middle East hostilities between the U.S. and Iran pushed international benchmark Brent crude above \$99 per barrel, igniting inflation fears and triggering a broad stock market sell-off. The spike in energy costs dragged the Dow Jones Industrial Average down 1.2% while pushing the 10-year Treasury yield above 4.8% as Wall Street reassesses Federal Reserve interest rate expectations ahead of crucial inflation prints.

## Markets & Economics
**[Wall Street closed lower](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)** across the board as the Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 1.2% (down nearly 630 points), the S&P 500 fell 0.6%, and the Nasdaq Composite slipped 0.3% amid climbing energy costs. Benchmark **[10-year Treasury yields](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/us-treasury-yields-bonds.html)** briefly topped 4.80% to hit 4.8063% while 30-year yields rose to 5.2708%, tracking **[Brent crude's rally](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/oil-prices-today-brent-wti-hormuz-iran-war.html)** toward \$99 a barrel and WTI crude topping \$94 a barrel. Fixed-income markets are bracing for Thursday's U.S. Producer Price Index (projected at +0.4% month-over-month) following August's blowout 162,000 payrolls gain, with fed funds futures now pointing to a ~60% probability of an interest rate hike at the September 15-16 FOMC meeting. Internationally, **[China's trade surplus expanded](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/china-exports-imports-august-trade-rebalance-demand-surplus-.html)** to \$119.09 billion as exports surged 25% year-over-year while imports grew 28.2% (missing 30% forecasts), even as **[HSBC hiked its S&P 500 target](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/hsbc-just-hiked-its-price-target-for-the-sp-500-heres-what-the-bank-expects.html)** to 8,100 on corporate earnings resilience.

## Business & Earnings
**[Qualcomm shares jumped 4%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/qualcomm-amazon-data-center-infrastructure-deal.html)** after sealing a multi-generational AI infrastructure pact with Amazon Web Services that included issuing Amazon warrants for 25 million shares worth \$4 billion, boosting Qualcomm's push toward a \$15 billion data center revenue target. In aerospace and defense, **[GE Aerospace agreed to acquire](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/ge-aerospace-to-buy-castings-maker-cpp-for-nearly-12-billion.html)** castings maker Consolidated Precision Products for \$11.75 billion to bolster its engine supply chain. Biopharma saw major volatility as **[Novartis plunged nearly 9%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/novartis-novn-del-desiran-drug-trial.html)** following Phase 3 clinical trial failures for its muscle-wasting drug del-desiran and cholesterol treatment pelacarsen, which knocked rival Amgen down nearly 10%. Meanwhile, **[AstraZeneca released strong Phase 3 data](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/astrazeneca-copd-drug-tozorakimab-shows-positive-late-stage-trial-results.html)** for its COPD biologic tozorakimab, reinforcing peak annual sales expectations above \$5 billion, while European AI startup **[Mistral raised \$3.5 billion](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/mistral-ai-funding-valuation-samsung.html)** led by Samsung at a \$24 billion valuation.

## Investing & Commentary
CNBC's Jim Cramer urged investors to look beyond concentrated AI data center trades, highlighting actionable value in aerospace (**[GE Aerospace](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/GE)**), fintech (**[Robinhood](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/HOOD)**, **[Affirm](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/AFRM)**), and energy infrastructure (**[Enbridge](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/ENB)**, **[Enterprise Products Partners](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/EPD)**). On Wall Street, **[UBS upgraded Lockheed Martin](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/earnings-upside-for-this-defense-giant-isnt-being-appreciated-by-investors-says-ubs.html)** to Buy with a \$674 price target, pointing to 38% free cash flow growth through 2030, while **[Pivotal Research initiated SpaceX](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/spcx-is-a-buy-based-on-one-bet-pivotal-research-says.html)** at Buy with a \$220 price target, betting on Starship reusability breakthroughs. Meanwhile, **[RBC initiated Shake Shack](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/rbc-says-this-fast-casual-stock-down-50percent-from-its-highs-is-set-to-rise.html)** at Outperform with an \$89 price target, citing margin expansion from lower beef prices, and analysts noted that data center REITs like **[Equinix](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/these-dividend-stocks-could-catch-a-tailwind-from-data-center-pushback.html)** are gaining pricing power due to local development pushback.
