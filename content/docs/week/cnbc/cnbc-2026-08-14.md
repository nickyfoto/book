# CNBC — 2026-08-14

## Lead Story
Despite high-level C-suite instability marked by the sudden exits of key revenue and operating executives this week, **OpenAI** finance chief **Sarah Friar** reassured shareholders that the company's **annualized revenue run-rate has hit \$40 billion**, driven by a massive acceleration in the high-margin enterprise segment which now represents the majority of its business. 

## Markets & Economics
Markets closed the week on mixed footing following Thursday's record-setting close of **7,798.99** for the **S&P 500**. In economic data, investors digested a major miss on **July retail sales**, which **unexpectedly plunged 0.6%** against the 0.1% monthly expansion projected by economists, discussed on-air in **[Retail sales unexpectedly fell in July](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/14/retail-sales-unexpectedly-fell-in-july.html)**. While the weak print raises valid concerns about a broader consumer slowdown following last week's soft payrolls report, Goldman Sachs analyst Lizzie Dove pointed out in **[High-income tourism is 'off the charts,' says Goldman Sachs’ Lizzie Dove](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/14/high-income-tourism-is-off-the-charts-says-goldman-sachs-lizzie-dove.html)** that travel-spending momentum remains exceptionally resilient among wealthier cohorts. Further macro perspective was offered in **[WSJ's Greg Ip: The euphoria around U.S. economic prospects isn't translating to job creation](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/14/wsjs-greg-ip-the-euphoria-around-u-s-economic-prospects-isnt-translating-to-job-creation.html)**, which highlighted the stark disconnect between buoyant corporate sentiment and sluggish domestic hiring. Geopolitical risks also pressured bonds, sending the **10-year Treasury yield up to 4.661%** after Washington threatened Iran with **"never-before-seen" economic isolation** and a continuous naval blockade in the Strait of Hormuz.

## Business & Earnings
In corporate coverage, Disney CEO **Josh D'Amaro** sat down with Julia Boorstin from the **D23 Expo**, expressing solid confidence in the company's trajectory and the strength of its parks division last quarter, even as the entertainment giant continues to manage industry-wide margin pressures, ongoing cost-cutting, and broadcast license scrutiny from the FCC. Meanwhile, Brinker International CEO **Kevin Hochman** detailed how Chili's aggressive **\$10.99 value campaign** has been a critical engine for driving higher diner volumes and anchoring the restaurant chain's impressive rebound story. In fintech, Corgi Invest CEO **Nico Laqua** joined *ETF Edge* to discuss how the venture-backed startup has deployed **AI to accelerate written regulatory filings**, allowing it to launch an unprecedented **197 niche ETFs** in record time to challenge industry giant BlackRock. Finally, semiconductor equipment heavyweight **Applied Materials** slid 5% in midday trading after its Q2 print (\$3.50 adjusted EPS on \$9.12 billion in revenue) failed to satisfy lofty near-term buy-side expectations despite a robust 25% year-over-year surge in sales.

## Investing & Commentary
The high-flying memory sector remains highly strategic, with **SanDisk upgraded to overweight at JPMorgan with a \$2,250 price target** on the strength of its margin-boosting, long-term customer agreements. This structural shift away from traditional memory cycles is also expected to benefit **Micron**, which is projected to generate a scorching **\$50 billion in free cash flow** for fiscal year 2026. In major institutional moves, Q2 regulatory filings revealed Warren Buffett's **Berkshire Hathaway boosted its Alphabet position by 83% to \$37.9 billion**, while David Tepper's **Appaloosa Management** trimmed select memory names during the quarter to double down on megacap tech giants like Amazon and Meta.
