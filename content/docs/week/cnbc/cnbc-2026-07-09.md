# CNBC — 2026-07-09

## Lead Story
The escalating conflict in the Middle East dominated trading desks as President Trump declared the U.S.-Iran ceasefire "over" following a fresh wave of military strikes. However, markets found their footing after Trump later noted that Tehran had reached out to make a deal, signaling a potential diplomatic off-ramp to the crisis.

## Markets & Economics
Energy markets were highly reactive to the geopolitical whiplash, pushing **[Brent crude to $78.82 and WTI to $74.29 a barrel](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/oil-rises-as-iran-us-tensions-raise-concerns-over-supply-disruptions-.html)** as tanker traffic in the Strait of Hormuz slowed to a crawl. While geopolitical fears loomed, **[Wall Street is largely betting](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/wall-street-is-betting-trump-doesnt-want-to-disturb-the-stock-market.html)** that Trump will avoid any prolonged escalation that could derail the broader stock market's momentum. In Asia, South Korea's Kospi officially fell into bear territory, shedding more than 5% on Wednesday as investors sharply rotated out of crowded AI chipmaker trades. Back in the U.S., Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh unveiled five new task forces aimed at overhauling central bank operations, notably tapping **[tech heavyweights like Marc Andreessen](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/fed-task-force-member-chairman-kevin-warsh-ai.html)** to evaluate AI's long-term impact on economic productivity. Domestically, the housing market remains frozen; **[June home sales disappointed](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/07/09/june-home-sales-disappoint-as-prices-reach-an-all-time-high.html)**, dropping 2.4% while median existing-home prices hit an all-time high of $440,600.

## Business & Earnings
Corporate earnings were a mixed bag, with **[PepsiCo missing profit estimates](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/pepsico-pep-q2-2026-earnings.html)** as North American consumers tightened their belts in response to rising gas prices and broader inflation. On the semiconductor front, **[Micron shares surged 7%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/micron-stock-us-chipmaking.html)** after the company unveiled a massive $3 billion strategic investment to bolster the U.S. chip supply chain, lifting the broader hardware sector. In a major healthcare stumble, **[AstraZeneca shares plunged 9%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/astrazeneca-stock-trial-heart-drug-pharma.html)** after its highly anticipated heart disease drug, Wainua, failed to meet its primary endpoint in a late-stage clinical trial. Meanwhile, **[Goldman Sachs scored a major victory](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/goldman-sachs-asset-management-deals-verizon-lockheed-martin.html)** by securing $70 billion in retirement asset management deals with Verizon and Lockheed Martin, accelerating its push for stable, recurring revenue. The tech sector is also bracing for the massive $29 billion U.S. Nasdaq debut of South Korean memory giant **[SK Hynix](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/meet-sk-hynix-the-trillion-dollar-chipmaker-debuting-on-us-markets-.html)** this Friday, a major liquidity test for the AI memory boom.

## Investing & Commentary
For investors looking to hedge Middle East risks, Bank of America named **[Chevron as its top pick](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/bank-of-americas-no-1-way-to-play-the-strait-of-hormuz-closing-again.html)**, citing the oil major's lack of regional exposure and high earnings torque to rising crude prices. On the technology front, Palo Alto Networks CEO Nikesh Arora cautioned that **[AI token pricing must fall 90%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/palo-alto-ceo-arora-ai-pricing.html)** for widespread enterprise adoption to become a viable reality. Despite some near-term sector volatility, bulls remain heavily invested in big tech; a massive **[$24 million options trade in the QQQ](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/a-huge-trade-just-happened-on-the-nasdaq-100-bulls-are-taking-notice.html)** signals high conviction that the Nasdaq-100 will hit fresh all-time highs by the end of July.
