# CNBeta — 2026-05-29

## Top Story
According to [a cnbeta report on Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563976.htm), Huawei's new "Tau Law" and 3D packaging technologies are significant technical breakthroughs, but they pose no immediate threat to TSMC's dominance. Huang noted that TSMC has nearly a decade of advanced packaging experience, while [TSMC executives emphasized that energy efficiency, not pure compute, is the ultimate bottleneck for future AI chips](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563996.htm).

## Tech & AI

In the enterprise space, [Amazon shut down an internal AI leaderboard](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564012.htm) after employees gamified the system by generating unnecessary prompts to climb the ranks, wasting valuable compute resources. 

Meanwhile, Elon Musk's recent tweets claiming a short-term 180-day data center lease with SpaceX contradict [SpaceX's IPO filings](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564066.htm), which detailed a multi-year $15 billion commitment to xAI, raising red flags for investors ahead of the $1.8 trillion offering. 

Finally, [Dell's stock surged 32%](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564080.htm) driven by a 757% spike in AI server revenue, though analysts warn [skyrocketing memory prices](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564078.htm) will severely impact PC and server margins in upcoming quarters.

## Consumer & Devices

Conversely, [Tesla's FSD safety claims face internal skepticism](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564006.htm), as data labelers report the system frequently fails basic tasks and researchers accuse executives of relying on misleading crash statistics. ## Gaming
California passed the [Protect Our Games Act](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1564040.htm), requiring publishers of digital games released after 2027 to maintain offline playability or offer refunds when servers shut down, marking a massive legislative win for game preservation. 

[Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1564062.htm) is reportedly launching on October 23, skipping a day-one Game Pass release while allegedly arriving on the Nintendo Switch 2. 

Despite a significant price hike, the [Steam Deck OLED sold out in North America within 24 hours](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1564036.htm), prompting Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney to [mock Gabe Newell's yacht habits](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1564018.htm) in response to the price surge.
