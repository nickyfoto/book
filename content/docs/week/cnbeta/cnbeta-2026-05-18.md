# CNBeta — 2026-05-18

## Top Story
According to a [cnbeta report on Nvidia's H200 shipments](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562834.htm), the Chinese government is actively blocking domestic companies from purchasing the advanced AI chips, despite the US Commerce Department approving the exports. This move aims to force Chinese tech giants like Alibaba, Tencent, and ByteDance to rely on domestic alternatives like Huawei, severely impacting Nvidia's projected revenue. This marks a significant escalation in the US-China tech war, signaling that Beijing is now retaliating by utilizing its own market access as a weapon against American semiconductor dominance.

## Tech & AI
A [cnbeta report on AI video generation](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562784.htm) highlights a major shift in the global AI race, with Chinese models like Kuaishou's Kling and ByteDance's Seedance 2.0 now outperforming US counterparts in quality and creative freedom. Creators note these models handle complex movements better and have fewer restrictive content filters. 
In the foundational model infrastructure space, [China Telecom launched commercial "Token Plans"](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562750.htm) starting at just 9.9 RMB per month for 10 million tokens, targeting both developers and everyday users by integrating domestic models like DeepSeek V3.2. 
On the semiconductor front, [TSMC is already laying the groundwork for its 1nm process](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562826.htm), preparing new facilities for mass production by 2030 to maintain its lead over Samsung. 
Meanwhile, [Apple is reportedly losing its bargaining power in DRAM negotiations](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562842.htm), as memory manufacturers prioritize hyperscalers building AI data centers, shifting Apple's strategy from demanding the lowest prices to simply securing a stable supply. 
In legal tech news, [Elon Musk lost his lawsuit against Sam Altman and OpenAI](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562856.htm), clearing a major hurdle for OpenAI's highly anticipated IPO.

## Consumer & Devices
An [insight into Apple's chip strategy](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562782.htm) reveals the company is using "defective" A18 Pro chips—ones with five working GPU cores instead of six—to power its new $599 MacBook Neo. This binning strategy allows Apple to recycle less-than-perfect silicon for entry-level devices while protecting its margins. 
In the education hardware market, [sales of e-ink study tablets have crashed by 90%](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562792.htm) in the first quarter of 2026, as consumers shift back to LCD screens and budget-friendly devices amidst a broader electronics slump. 
For audiophiles, [leaked images of Sony's 10th-anniversary "ColleXion" headphones](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562838.htm) show a sleek gradient design and a $649 price tag, positioning the WH-1000XX model as a premium luxury accessory.

## Gaming

The patent, which covered touchscreen mechanics for throwing capture items, was deemed an obvious combination of existing gameplay concepts. 
PC gamers will see massive improvements in load times, as [Forza Horizon 6 cuts boot times by 95%](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1562800.htm) using Microsoft's new Advanced Shader Delivery technology.

## Science & Space
A [rumored acquisition by BYD](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562820.htm) suggests the Chinese EV giant is in talks to buy the Alpine F1 team and Renault's engine factory. If successful, BYD would become the first Chinese automaker to enter Formula 1. 
In aerospace, [SpaceX is preparing for the first test flight of its upgraded Starship V3](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562822.htm), a critical milestone as the company gears up for its IPO. However, the launch comes amidst controversy, as [OSHA investigates a recent worker fatality](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562862.htm) at the Starbase facility in Texas.
