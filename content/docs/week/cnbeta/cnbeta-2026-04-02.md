# CNBeta — 2026-04-02

## Top Story
**[Artemis II mission successfully launches for a historic lunar flyby](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1556232.htm)**, marking humanity's first return to the moon's vicinity in 54 years. Despite overcoming pre-launch delays and executing a flawless translunar injection, the four astronauts are currently dealing with a broken toilet fan and bizarre software glitches with Microsoft Outlook on their Surface Pro devices. For the first time, NASA is also allowing the crew to bring their personal iPhones on board to record their 10-day journey.

## Tech & AI
Microsoft is aggressively pushing its proprietary AI capabilities to reduce reliance on OpenAI, launching the **[MAI-1-preview foundation model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556348.htm)**, trained on nearly 15,000 NVIDIA H100 GPUs using a Mixture-of-Experts architecture. They also rolled out a suite of multimodal tools, including the **[MAI-Transcribe-1 speech model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556346.htm)**, which claims top accuracy across 25 languages, and the upgraded **[MAI-Image-2 generator](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556350.htm)**, which has climbed into the top 3 on the Arena leaderboard.

In China, domestic GPU makers are rapidly filling the void left by US sanctions, **[capturing 41% of the local AI accelerator server market](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556296.htm)**. Huawei leads the domestic pack, shipping over 810,000 AI chips to account for half of the local brand volume, significantly eroding NVIDIA's once-dominant 55% market share. Meanwhile, Moore Threads has emerged as a major player, ranking second in a **[top 10 list of 2026 Chinese AI chip companies](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556268.htm)**.

Google's upcoming **[open-source Gemma 4 model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556306.htm)** is rumored to feature a 120B parameter MoE architecture. However, the tech giant is facing severe academic backlash after the authors of the RaBitQ algorithm accused Google's **[TurboQuant paper of plagiarism and academic misconduct](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556238.htm)**.

The Ministry of Commerce addressed Meta's rapid $2 billion acquisition of the Chinese-founded AI startup Manus, noting in a **[recent press conference](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556314.htm)** that the talent acquisition must fulfill all statutory procedures and comply with Chinese law.

Quantum computing may shatter modern encryption much sooner than anticipated, with recent studies showing that just **[10,000 atomic qubits could crack RSA](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556336.htm)**, posing an imminent threat to global cybersecurity and cryptocurrency architectures.

## Consumer & Devices
In a heartfelt internal letter marking **[Apple's 50th anniversary](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556228.htm)**, CEO Tim Cook hinted that the company's next blockbuster product will emerge at the "intersection of hardware, software, and services". Leaked molds for the **[iPhone 18 Pro](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556320.htm)** show a 35% smaller Dynamic Island, utilizing under-display Face ID components and a variable aperture camera, though rumors suggest the **[black color option will remain absent](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556330.htm)**.

China is implementing a strict **[new national standard for power banks](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556308.htm)** to mitigate thermal runaway risks, mandating smart lifecycle tracking systems to monitor battery cell voltage, current, and temperature over time.

Huawei's aggressive consumer push is yielding results as it **[secures the #2 spot in China's PC market](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556272.htm)**, achieving 11% market share and outpacing Lenovo's growth rate in Q4 2025.

## Gaming
Contrary to traditional console lifecycles, **[PS5 prices are trending upward](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1556242.htm)**, with Sony raising the price of the disc version to $650 five years after launch. Elsewhere in hardware, the **[Steam Deck 2 is reportedly delayed to 2028 or 2029](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1556340.htm)** due to ongoing global memory and NAND flash shortages.

Blizzard is recruiting for an **[unannounced AAA open-world shooter](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1556304.htm)** using Unreal Engine, which insiders suggest is set in the StarCraft universe and could debut at BlizzCon 2026.

Nintendo's formidable legal team took a hit as the US Patent and Trademark Office **[revoked its crucial character summoning patent](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1556248.htm)**, which could impact its ongoing copyright lawsuits against clone games.

## Science & Space
SpaceX is seeking a **[historic IPO valuation exceeding $2 trillion](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556364.htm)** ahead of potential roadshows, a figure that would make it more valuable than Meta or Tesla.

To address the massive energy and space demands of AI data centers, Hitachi and Mitsui O.S.K. Lines are partnering to **[convert retired ships into floating data centers](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556358.htm)**, utilizing seawater for cooling.

If you're wondering why we haven't heard from extraterrestrials, a new EPFL study suggests **[alien signals may have already passed Earth](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1556288.htm)**, indicating humanity needs to observe broader galactic regions over much longer timeframes.

## Also Noted
**[HSBC India forced all user passwords to uppercase](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556250.htm)**, inadvertently revealing to security experts that the bank stores passwords in plaintext or reversible formats.

**[Amazon is in talks to acquire Globalstar](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556280.htm)**, a move that could give the tech giant control over the satellite infrastructure powering Apple's emergency SOS features.

**[US tech giants are aggressively purchasing Chinese power transformers](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556318.htm)**, driving a 182% surge in exports to the US to satisfy the massive energy demands of AI data centers.

**[Stellantis is negotiating with Leapmotor](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556234.htm)** to utilize an idle Canadian assembly plant for EV production following Canada's tariff agreements with China.

**[Russian users are entirely cut off from App Store payments](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556334.htm)** and Apple subscriptions as new sanctions block mobile carrier billing.

# CNBeta — 2026-04-02 (中文版摘要)

## Top Story
**[阿耳忒弥斯2号任务成功发射并进行历史性的绕月飞行](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1556232.htm)**，标志着人类54年来首次重返月球附近。尽管克服了发射前的延误并完成了完美的变轨，四名宇航员目前却面临着厕所风扇损坏 以及Surface Pro设备上Microsoft Outlook出现离奇软件故障的问题。这也是NASA首次允许宇航员携带个人iPhone登船，以记录他们为期10天的旅程。

## Tech & AI
微软正积极推进其专有AI大模型以减少对OpenAI的依赖，推出了基于混合专家架构（MoE）并在近15000块NVIDIA H100 GPU上训练的 **[MAI-1-preview基础模型](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556348.htm)**。他们还推出了一系列多模态工具，包括号称在25种语言中准确率最高的 **[MAI-Transcribe-1语音模型](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556346.htm)**，以及在Arena排行榜上跻身前三的升级版 **[MAI-Image-2图像生成器](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556350.htm)**。

在中国，本土GPU制造商正迅速填补美国制裁留下的空白，**[占据了当地AI加速服务器市场41%的份额](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556296.htm)**。华为领跑国内阵营，出货量超81万张AI芯片，占本土品牌总出货量的一半，大幅削弱了英伟达曾经高达55%的市场主导地位。同时，摩尔线程作为一匹黑马，在 **[2026中国AI芯片公司Top10榜单](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556268.htm)** 中位列第二。

传闻谷歌即将开源的 **[Gemma 4模型](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556306.htm)** 将采用120B参数的MoE架构。然而，该科技巨头目前正面临严厉的学术界反弹，此前RaBitQ算法的作者指控谷歌的 **[TurboQuant论文存在抄袭和学术不端行为](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556238.htm)**。

中国商务部在 **[近期的例行新闻发布会](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556314.htm)** 上回应了Meta斥资20亿美元快速收购由中国团队创立的AI初创公司Manus一事，指出此类人才收购必须履行法定程序并遵守中国法律。

量子计算击溃现代加密体系的时间可能比预期的要早得多，最新研究表明，仅仅 **[1万个原子量子比特就可能破解RSA](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556336.htm)**，这对全球网络安全和加密货币架构构成了迫在眉睫的威胁。

## Consumer & Devices
在庆祝 **[苹果成立50周年](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556228.htm)** 的一封真挚的内部信中，CEO库克暗示公司的下一个爆款产品将诞生于“硬件、软件和服务的交汇点”。泄露的 **[iPhone 18 Pro](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556320.htm)** 模具显示，灵动岛面积缩小了35%，这得益于屏下Face ID组件和可变光圈摄像头的设计，不过有传闻称 **[黑色版本可能将继续缺席](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556330.htm)**。

中国正在实施严格的 **[充电宝新国标](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556308.htm)** 以降低热失控风险，强制要求配备智能全生命周期管理系统，长期监测电芯的电压、电流和温度。

华为在消费市场的激进扩张取得成效，**[拿下了中国PC市场第二的位置](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1556272.htm)**，实现11%的市场份额，且在2025年第四季度的增速超过了联想。
