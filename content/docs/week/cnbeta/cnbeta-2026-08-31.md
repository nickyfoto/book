## Tech & AI
Silicon alliances and memory architectures are rapidly consolidating around next-generation artificial intelligence workloads. A [cnbeta report on Nvidia's \$3.5 billion investment in MediaTek](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575762.htm) reveals that the two chipmakers are co-developing the NVLink Fusion ecosystem to integrate third-party custom XPUs directly into high-bandwidth datacenter fabrics alongside upcoming NVHBM architectures. Concurrently, China's memory sector logged a strategic milestone as [CXMT commenced risk production of HBM3E memory](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575764.htm), targeting 1.2 TB/s bandwidth across 8-layer and 12-layer stacks to narrow the technological gap with South Korean incumbents. Meanwhile, [Samsung is developing an 8-layer HBM4E module tailored for Nvidia's Rubin Ultra GPUs](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575706.htm), scaling back layer counts to maximize yields while pushing transmission speeds to 18 Gbps. 

Physical AI and humanoid robotics are shifting from showfloor spectacle to grueling industrial verification. An analysis of [Nvidia's deepening bet on Chinese robotics](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575634.htm) highlights how Santa Clara is embedding its Thor SoCs and GR00T foundation models into Chinese hardware leaders like Unitree and Agibot. This supply chain interdependence persists even as [escalating US regulatory restrictions threaten to bifurcate global robotics markets](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575688.htm), with Chinese OEMs capturing 86% of global humanoid shipments in the first half of 2026 through massive manufacturing scale. On the factory floor, [field trials at the Foton Cummins engine plant](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575700.htm) demonstrate that dual-arm and bipedal robots have achieved sub-four-minute material handling cycles, though industry leaders caution that long-term actuator reliability and positive return on investment remain unresolved hurdles.

The economic rules governing web data extraction and commercial AI monetization are undergoing a severe realignment. Web infrastructure giant [Cloudflare announced it will block hybrid AI crawlers by default starting September 15](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575710.htm), directly targeting Google's AI Overviews and ending the thirty-year covenant of free web scraping in exchange for search referral traffic. On the commercial front, [OpenAI's ChatGPT advertising business surged to an annualized run-rate of \$1 billion](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575750.htm) within 200 days of testing, establishing a vital monetization pillar ahead of its planned public listing. 

Cybersecurity and intellectual property risks in generative AI reached a boiling point. Anthropic issued emergency alerts after [infostealer malware compromised active Claude user session tokens](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575632.htm), prompting the company to terminate logins and purge payment cards as syndicates siphoned API compute for underground proxy markets. In parallel, [Apple filed explosive forensic evidence in its trade secret litigation against OpenAI](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575802.htm), alleging that former senior engineer Liu Chang synchronized proprietary power schematics to a retained MacBook and trained automated AI agents to execute circuit simulations.

## Consumer & Devices
China's hyper-competitive automotive sector is confronting a reckoning over abbreviated product lifecycles. A comprehensive [cnbeta investigation into the hidden costs of "quick-build" vehicles](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575624.htm) details how compressing vehicle development timelines to 12–18 months and substituting physical proving grounds with software simulations compressed automaker operating margins to 1.5% and stranded over 600 new 2026 models in a post-launch "death valley". In response, Chinese regulators promulgated [mandatory national standards for L3 and L4 autonomous driving](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575724.htm), strictly penalizing deceptive marketing claims while imposing mandatory driver takeover monitoring and minimum-risk fallback maneuvers. 

Domestic hardware giants demonstrated financial endurance amid global supply chain reconfigurations. Defying ongoing export curbs, [Huawei posted H1 2026 revenues of 467.8 billion yuan](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575718.htm), climbing 9.55% year-over-year behind a record 121.4 billion yuan R&D expenditure and more than 80 million active HarmonyOS 6 devices. Concurrently, [major PC manufacturers including HP, Asus, and Acer are shifting laptop assembly back to mainland China](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575746.htm), discovering that persistent logistics bottlenecks and overheads in Southeast Asia elevated production costs by an average of \$9 per notebook compared to Chinese manufacturing hubs.

In the EV retail landscape, Tesla moved to capture cost-conscious buyers across Greater China. The automaker rolled out a [stripped-down budget Model 3 in Hong Kong and Macau](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575648.htm) priced under 180,000 yuan (HK\$205,000), slashing ambient lighting, ventilated seating, and acoustic trim while retaining a 572-kilometer WLTP range to lower the purchase barrier against regional electric competitors.

Sony Interactive Entertainment is steering PlayStation toward post-hardware monetization. Sony executives confirmed that [the PS5 has entered the latter phase of its lifecycle](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1575658.htm), refocusing corporate resources on extracting recurring digital revenue from its 125 million monthly active users rather than subsidizing aggressive console discounting. Defending its transition away from physical discs, [Sony argued in California federal court that consumers cannot logically "own" digital games](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1575664.htm), maintaining that storefront transactions represent non-exclusive software licenses rather than absolute property transfers. 

Meanwhile, physical trade event security broke down at Europe's flagship expo. Several independent game developers suffered severe equipment losses as [burglars ransacked multiple exhibition booths at Gamescom 2026](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1575672.htm), stealing development laptops containing unreleased playable code and sensitive studio assets.
