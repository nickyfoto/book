# CNBeta — 2026-04-30

## Top Story
Microsoft is radically shifting its operating system strategy, with a **[CNBeta report](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560704.htm)** detailing CEO Satya Nadella's pledge to return to Windows fundamentals, simplify setups, and reduce intrusive ads. This top-down directive aligns with revelations from senior engineers that **[Windows 11 is ditching web wrappers in favor of 100% native apps](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560750.htm)** to cut memory usage and latency. As the platform celebrates hitting **[1.6 billion monthly active devices](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560788.htm)**, this pivot marks a major win for PC users globally who have long criticized the system's sluggish components.

## Tech & AI
The AI infrastructure boom continues as Big Tech prepares to spend over $700 billion this year, creating a severe **[Samsung memory shortage that could last until 2027](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560772.htm)**. The rapid data center expansion is even impacting local economies, with **[Texas data centers outbidding housing projects for electricians](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560768.htm)**. Meanwhile, OpenAI is overhauling its compute strategy, **[abandoning the massive $500 billion "Stargate" joint venture](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560780.htm)** in favor of leased compute and bilateral deals. In court, Elon Musk openly admitted that **[xAI used OpenAI's models to train Grok](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560800.htm)**, confirming the industry's open secret of model distillation. On the hardware side, AMD and Intel jointly released the **[ACE whitepaper for x86 matrix acceleration](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560720.htm)** to vastly improve AI inference, while Intel revealed **[critical data for its 18A-P node](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560726.htm)**, boasting an 18% power reduction at identical performance levels.

## Consumer & Devices
A controversial end of an era is here for laptop enthusiasts, as **[Lenovo abandons the independent magnesium alloy roll cage](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560718.htm)** that has defined ThinkPad P-series durability for 20 years. In the smartphone and wearables space, rumors suggest the upcoming **[AirPods Ultra will feature IR cameras](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560786.htm)** to feed Apple's Visual Intelligence, while **[some iPhone 17 Pro and Air units are suffering from severe charging issues](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560798.htm)** after their batteries are fully drained. There are also radical shifts rumored for Samsung, as its **[Galaxy Book lineup may ditch Windows 11 to pre-install Android 17](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560762.htm)**, aiming for tighter ecosystem integration. Finally, for EV markets, **[JAC Motors officially plans to invest in Huawei's Yinwang](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560756.htm)** smart driving unit, further consolidating Huawei's influence over the auto sector.

## Gaming
In a win for AI upscaling, **[NVIDIA's DLSS 4.5 crushed AMD's FSR 4.1 in a massive blind test](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1560680.htm)**, though players noted FSR's significant generation-over-generation improvements. Microsoft's multi-platform gamble is paying off handsomely, with **[13 Xbox first-party games selling over 100,000 copies on PS5](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1560784.htm)**. As for hardware fixes, Valve's highly anticipated **[Steam Controller utilizes TMR sensors to eliminate stick drift](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1560682.htm)**, a technology that **[GuliKit is also adapting for the upcoming Switch 2 Joy-Cons](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1560806.htm)**.

## Science & Space

In cosmology, a new analysis of dark energy data suggests the **[universe may only have a lifespan of 33.3 billion years](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1560696.htm)**, challenging the theory of eternal inflation. Closer to home, environmental researchers warn that **[common goldfish are causing systemic destruction to freshwater ecosystems](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1560776.htm)** when thoughtlessly released into the wild.
