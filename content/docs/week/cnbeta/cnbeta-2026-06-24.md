# CNBeta — 2026-06-24

## Top Story
OpenAI and Broadcom have unveiled "Jalapeño," OpenAI's first custom-designed AI inference chip. According to a [cnbeta report](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1566684.htm), the ASIC took only nine months from design to tape-out, a record-breaking pace aided by OpenAI's own models participating in the layout and circuit design. A separate [deep dive](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1566698.htm) notes that the chip will be deployed by late 2026, marking a major step towards OpenAI's goal of becoming a full-stack AI company capable of driving down inference costs for its advanced models and upcoming AI agents.

## Tech & AI
A [report on TSMC's advanced process pricing](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1566656.htm) indicates the foundry is raising prices by 5% to 10% across all 7nm and below nodes, driven by relentless AI demand. 

A new [breakdown of China's autonomous driving national standards](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1566706.htm) details strict requirements for L3 and L4 systems, including a mandate to alert drivers 10 seconds before requiring human intervention and strict rules against aggressive lane changes. 

At Nvidia's annual meeting, Jensen Huang declared that "useful AI has arrived, and it is profitable," pointing out that clients are now building "AI factories" rather than just buying servers, as covered in this [Nvidia meeting summary](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1566700.htm). 

Trump administration export controls on Anthropic have caused collateral damage, severely impacting the US NSA which was heavily relying on Anthropic's "Mythos" model for cybersecurity testing, according to a [cnbeta article on the ban's fallout](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1566638.htm). A related [lawsuit by Legion](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1566648.htm) shows clients suing the US government over the sudden cutoff of the Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models, calling the damage irreparable. 

[Alibaba has sued the US government](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1566640.htm) for "arbitrary and capricious" actions after the Pentagon added the tech giant to a list of Chinese military-linked enterprises, which Alibaba claims has caused severe reputational harm.

## Consumer & Devices
An [Omdia forecast](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1566652.htm) predicts global smartphone average selling prices will surge 21% to $565 by 2026, primarily driven by skyrocketing DRAM and NAND flash memory costs. 

Tesla is reportedly bringing a [longer, six-seat version of the Model Y](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1566636.htm) to the US market by late 2026, potentially to fill the void left by the discontinued Model X SUV. 

Chinese office suite WPS faced massive user backlash and a [comment from Weibo's CEO](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1566646.htm) after users discovered the software was forcing cache files onto the system's C drive, prompting discussions about the reality of prioritizing commercial monetization over the narrative of "domestic alternatives". 

High-end gamers are frustrated with [RTX 5090 quality control issues](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1566678.htm), with teardowns showing excessive thermal paste spilling over the PCB while leaving the GPU die barely covered, causing massive overheating and fan noise.

## Gaming
Rockstar confirmed that [GTA6 physical editions will lack a disc](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1566666.htm) to prevent early leaks, providing only a download code for single-player content. Meanwhile, retail leaks suggest the console versions will feature [both performance and quality modes](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1566674.htm), though a stable 60fps remains questionable. 

A [Steam market study](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1566670.htm) found that games disclosing the use of generative AI suffer a 40% to 60% drop in estimated sales and receive significantly fewer reviews, highlighting strong gamer resistance. 

A resurfaced interview reveals [Shigeru Miyamoto's changing views on Zelda dungeons](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1566672.htm), noting that traditional linear labyrinths take massive development time and may no longer provide the immersive fun modern players expect.
