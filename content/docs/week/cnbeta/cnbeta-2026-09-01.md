# CNBeta — September 1, 2026

## Top Story
On September 1, 2026, Apple officially completed a historic leadership transition as John Ternus stepped in as CEO, while Tim Cook shifted to Executive Chairman after a 15-year tenure that deeply tied Apple's fate to China’s manufacturing ecosystem. According to [cnbeta reports](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575838.htm), Cook leaves a double-edged legacy: while he built an unparalleled supply chain, the strict manufacturing standards effectively served as a training ground for fierce domestic rivals like Huawei and Vivo, leaving Ternus to face a brutal market share battle and complex local AI compliance hurdles in his first week.

## Tech & AI
AI development continues its breakneck speed with significant open-source and proprietary releases. A [cnbeta report on DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575922.htm) showcases China's rising AI prowess, as the new 305B experimental multimodal model adds vision capabilities that allow it to challenge Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.8 in agentic tasks. This comes as Anthropic introduces [Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575974.htm), which lower token costs while reducing false security warnings, and OpenAI asserts its upcoming [Astra AI model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575976.htm) has passed critical cybersecurity thresholds. Meanwhile, the paradigm of local AI processing gains traction as Perplexity launches [Hybrid Compute](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575948.htm), allowing users to split tasks so that sensitive data is handled locally on Apple Silicon Macs using models like Qwen 3.6 or Gemma. The AI shift is also redefining CS education; a [controversial course at Nanjing University](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1575850.htm) requires students to self-fund their DeepSeek-V4-Flash tokens to break free from "zero-cost thinking" and build strict quality and cost awareness in modern software engineering. In corporate tech developments, automotive lighting giant Xingyu has triggered a massive ESG storm after abruptly firing or forcing out over 100 fresh graduates. According to [reports detailing the backlash](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575914.htm), the laid-off graduates bypassed standard labor disputes by directly reporting the company to its prestigious European clients, prompting [Volkswagen China](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575856.htm) and Mercedes-Benz to initiate formal supply chain compliance audits, hitting the supplier right where it hurts.

## Consumer & Devices
Flagship smartphones in China are facing a coordinated wave of price hikes from Huawei, Xiaomi, and Meizu, driven by an unsustainable rise in memory and advanced node chip costs. As discussed in a [cnbeta report on mobile price hikes](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575866.htm), TSMC's 2nm wafer pricing has surpassed \$30,000, while storage chip costs have surged 200%, forcing brands to raise prices across the board. In the automotive tech sector, Tesla Shanghai’s six-seater flagship [Model Y L has faced heavy criticism](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575846.htm) from owners reporting severe rear suspension sagging, causing tire wear and chassis scrape risks after long, loaded trips. On the networking front, TP-Link has unveiled its first consumer Wi-Fi 8 router, the [Archer 8 Ultra](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575936.htm), promising up to 19Gbps wireless bandwidth, though its US release remains shadowed by its inclusion on the Pentagon's military list. Additionally, according to [analyses of NVIDIA's \$3.5 billion investment in MediaTek](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575842.htm), the partnership will accelerate MediaTek’s transition from chip design to system-level design while extending NVIDIA’s rack-scale ecosystem into custom silicon. Meanwhile, lifestyle brand Dyson is venturing into dental care with the [\$499 CameraJet smart toothbrush](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575930.htm), which uses an integrated 100k-pixel camera and real-time AI to identify tooth gaps and blast them with mouthwash.

## Gaming
Console and PC gaming are seeing interesting shifts. While Valve’s ecosystem thrives as [Steam Deck verified titles pass the 30,000 mark](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1575830.htm), Sony is facing a quiet rebellion from indie developers. According to a [Digital Foundry report on Sony's disc-less strategy](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1575876.htm), small and medium-sized publishers are deeply resentful of PlayStation's push to phase out physical media by 2028, leading many to refocus their priorities on Nintendo's upcoming "Switch 2" where physical media remains viable. Meanwhile, the hunt for the *GTA 6* leaker is intensifying, with [Take-Two filing sealed Discord subpoenas](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1575878.htm) to track down the identities of several key suspects without tipping them off. On the graphics technology front, modders have managed to run leaked builds of [NVIDIA’s next-generation DLSS 5](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1575882.htm) on older RTX 20 and 30 series graphics cards, proving that AI-driven rendering can be backported despite official hardware limitations.

## Science & Space
China's private space industry reached another milestone as Galactic Energy successfully executed the debut launch of its [Pallas-1 reusable liquid rocket](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1575854.htm) from the Jiuquan launch center, establishing a solid-liquid dual propulsion fleet to target LEO constellation deployments. This coincides with NASA's monumental launch of the [Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1575858.htm) aboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy; built around a refurbished 2.4-meter military spy satellite mirror, the flagship observatory will scan the cosmos 1,000 times faster than Hubble to unlock the secrets of dark energy and map billions of galaxies. Closer to Earth, a Danish-led study of the [WWI submarine wreck UC-30](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1575946.htm) revealed that the sunken German vessel is still actively leaking toxic TNT explosives into the North Sea, prompting fresh European discussions on underwater hazardous waste remediation.
