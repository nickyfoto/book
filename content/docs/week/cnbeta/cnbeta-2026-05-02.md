# CNBeta — 2026-05-02

## Top Story
According to a [CNBeta report on Tesla's Canadian pricing](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561072.htm), the starting price of a Model 3 has plummeted by 50% to 39,490 CAD (roughly 198,000 RMB), making it significantly cheaper in Canada than in its domestic Chinese market. This massive market disruption is driven by Tesla shifting its Canadian vehicle supply to the Shanghai Gigafactory, capitalizing on China's low production costs and a recent China-Canada tariff agreement that dropped import taxes on Chinese-made EVs to 6.1%. This pricing strategy vividly illustrates how Chinese manufacturing advantages are actively reshaping global EV competition and Western market dynamics.

## Tech & AI
Apple has officially dropped its long-held net-cash-neutral target, fueling rumors of an impending mega-acquisition in the AI space, as noted in an [analysis of Apple's Q2 earnings call](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561062.htm). Analysts point to the AI startup Perplexity as a prime candidate. Interestingly, Perplexity's CEO recently praised Apple hardware in a [keynote about their Personal Computer AI agent](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561080.htm), explicitly calling the Mac mini the best and most accessible local deployment platform for their tools. 

A groundbreaking [Harvard medical study on AI triage](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561090.htm) proved that an LLM-based AI system dramatically outperformed human doctors in emergency room diagnostic accuracy. The AI system achieved a 67% accuracy rate with limited triage data and 82% with detailed records, significantly beating the 50-55% and 70-79% accuracy ranges of human doctors.

In the AI assistant space, OpenAI's ChatGPT now supports direct login to the [OpenClaw project](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560988.htm), allowing Plus and Pro users to execute complex autonomous agent tasks without paying for extra API tokens. This open-ecosystem approach marks a stark contrast to Anthropic's recent decision to strictly block OpenClaw from using Claude subscription quotas.

On the semiconductor front, China's Lisuan Tech announced the [May 20 launch of its homegrown 7G100 GPU](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561012.htm). Built on a 6nm process using a fully self-designed TrueGPU architecture and instruction set, the GPU successfully bypasses third-party IP licensing, insulating it from geopolitical tech sanctions.

## Consumer & Devices
Dreame Technology's founder showcased a working prototype of a [modular smartphone](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561006.htm) where both the camera module and the entire back cover can be magnetically detached and swapped. This design allows users to seamlessly upgrade imaging capabilities or strip the phone down for an ultra-lightweight daily carry.

Marking the end of an era, China Mobile officially shut down [Monternet (移动梦网)](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561036.htm), the pioneering 2G/WAP portal that once boasted nearly 100 million monthly active users during the feature phone era. The final core services went offline on April 30, closing a major chapter in early Chinese mobile internet history.

During the May Day holiday, Hangzhou deployed China's first full squad of [LLM-powered traffic management robots](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561000.htm). Dubbed "Hangjing Zhixing", these 15 robots are stationed at popular tourist intersections to provide instant route guidance and politely correct pedestrian traffic violations, proving highly effective at maintaining order.

## Gaming
Following the colossal success of *Black Myth: Wukong*, Tencent has increased its stake in developer [Game Science](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1561020.htm) to 24%, becoming the studio's sole external investor. Sources claim Tencent will continue to respect the studio's creative independence as founder Feng Ji remains the largest shareholder with a 38.76% stake.

Microsoft announced that **[Forza Horizon 6](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1561040.htm)** is now available for pre-load ahead of its May 19 release. The highly anticipated racing game is set in Japan and features the series' largest map ever, including a Tokyo area five times the size of FH5's Guanajuato.

Legendary Chinese fighting game player "Xiaohai" (Zeng Zhuojun) successfully defended his championship title in *Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves* at [EVO Japan 2026](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1561094.htm). His dominant performance secured him a direct qualification for the 2026 Esports World Cup.

## Science & Space
China's DAMPE ("Wukong") satellite has successfully [decoded cosmic ray acceleration mechanisms](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1561008.htm) after 10 years in orbit. By analyzing data from 18.5 billion high-energy particles, the research team directly observed common bulge structures in particle energy distribution, proving the existence of cosmic ray acceleration sources near Earth.

A new study challenges the long-held belief that high atmospheric oxygen alone created [prehistoric giant insects](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1561088.htm). Researchers found that tracheal tubes in insect flight muscles take up less than 1% of the space, meaning ancient "griffinflies" had plenty of evolutionary room to increase oxygen delivery without hitting a structural ceiling, suggesting other factors drove their massive size.

## 头条新闻

## 科技与人工智能

在AI助手领域，OpenAI的ChatGPT现在支持直接登录[OpenClaw项目](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1560988.htm)，允许Plus和Pro用户无需额外购买API Token即可执行复杂的自主智能体任务。这种开放生态的做法与Anthropic近期严格封杀OpenClaw使用Claude订阅额度的决定形成了鲜明对比。

在半导体方面，中国的砺算科技宣布将于[5月20日发布其国产7G100 GPU](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561012.htm)。该GPU基于6nm工艺，采用完全自研的TrueGPU架构和指令集，成功绕过了第三方IP授权，从而免受地缘政治技术制裁的影响。

## 科学与太空
中国“悟空”(DAMPE)暗物质粒子探测卫星在轨10年后，成功[破解了宇宙射线加速机制](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1561008.htm)。通过分析185亿个高能粒子的数据，研究团队首次直接观测到粒子能量分布中共同的鼓包状结构，证实了地球附近存在宇宙射线加速源。

一项新研究挑战了长期以来的观点，即高大气氧含量是造就[史前巨型昆虫](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1561088.htm)的唯一原因。研究人员发现，昆虫飞行肌肉中的气管小管仅占据不到1%的空间，这意味着远古的“狮鹫蜓”有充足的演化空间来增加氧气输送而不会触碰结构天花板，这表明是其他因素促成了它们庞大的体型。
