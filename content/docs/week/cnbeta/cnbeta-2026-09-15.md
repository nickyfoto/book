# CNBeta — September 15, 2026

## Top Story

According to **[a CNBeta deep dive into Apple's 15-year foldable journey](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578058.htm)**, Apple has officially entered the foldable market with the launch of the iPhone Duo, priced from 15,999 RMB (~\$2,199) and featuring Samsung-supplied OLED panels paired with ultra-thin flexible glass. While hardware specifications like its 11.3mm closed thickness and 254g weight lag behind Chinese competitors, market analysts expect the device to capture nearly 25% of global foldable shipments in its first year by leveraging iOS 27 to enforce responsive app layout standards across its dual-screen ecosystem. This landmark release highlights a shift where operating system enforcement and developer compliance, rather than hinge engineering alone, dictate the next phase of the foldable smartphone industry.

## Tech & AI

Silicon and model architectures saw major updates across the ecosystem today. **[MediaTek's announcement of the Dimensity 9600 Pro](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578074.htm)** showcases the industry's rapid adoption of TSMC's 2nm node, utilizing over 33 billion transistors and a dual super-core layout to deliver a 17% single-core CPU performance gain and double INT4 AI computing capacity. On the software and application layer, **[a report on DeepSeek Harness](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578046.htm)** highlights the release of an official desktop client operating via a custom protocol for improved security, while **[a viral essay by DeepSeek's CUDA operator lead](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578082.htm)** reflects on how autonomous AI agents are rapidly outperforming human engineers in writing low-level GPU kernel code.

In real-time voice interaction, **[Google introduced Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578144.htm)**, an audio-to-audio model capable of executing multi-step logic and code debugging in the background while sustaining natural, uninterrupted voice dialogue. On the financial side, **[ByteDance's H1 2026 financial report](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578114.htm)** revealed a 30% revenue increase driven by TikTok and international expansion, though net profit margins contracted to ~16.7% as the company secured a record \$30 billion bank loan to fund capital-intensive AI infrastructure. Meanwhile, **[an evaluation by the Korea Semiconductor Industry Association](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578128.htm)** indicates that Chinese chipmakers YMTC and CXMT have narrowed the technical gap with global leaders to 1–3 years, advancing 270-layer 3D NAND and hybrid bonding for next-generation HBM production.

## Consumer & Devices

Performance leaks and market responses dominated device headlines today. **[First Geekbench benchmarks for Apple's M6 Mac mini](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578134.htm)** reveal a 2nm 12-core CPU achieving a 24% boost in single-core and a 48% increase in multi-core performance over M4, alongside GPU neural accelerators that quadruple local AI inference speeds. For Chinese iPhone users, **[a CNBeta report on iOS 27](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578056.htm)** details a long-awaited Clock app update that automatically syncs alarms to statutory makeup workdays during national holidays. Seeking to counter Apple's entry into foldables, **[Samsung is accelerating the launch of the Galaxy Z TriFold 2](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578136.htm)** alongside a companion rollable smartphone to defend its dominance in luxury form factors.

In automotive news, **[a viral account of a Tesla Cybercab ride in Austin](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/funny/1578084.htm)** illustrated current autonomous navigation limitations when a 10-minute trip stretched into a 70-minute cross-city detour because the driverless fleet is restricted from entering highways or crossing railway tracks. Additionally, **[Tesla revised its official Model YL owner manual](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578062.htm)** to reduce ground clearance and overall height specs by 3–4mm following customer complaints regarding rear suspension sagging.

Gaming developments featured hardware iterations and strategy shifts. **[Nintendo's official map release for The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1578032.htm)** revealed enlarged regions like the Ice Cavern, while **[online debate surfaced over visual comparisons to Genshin Impact](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1578040.htm)** due to both titles sharing real-world European medieval architectural inspirations. On the spatial computing front, **[a hands-on review of Valve's Steam Frame headset](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1578036.htm)** praised its 469g lightweight chassis and Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 multi-screen streaming, though frequent system crashes underline that the software remains in early testing. Furthermore, **[industry leaks regarding Xbox Game Pass](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1578116.htm)** indicate Microsoft plans to remove day-one first-party game releases and cloud streaming from standard tiers in early 2027 while trimming subscription prices by \$3 to stem user churn.

## Science & Space

Commercial spaceflight and domestic robotics made major strides today. **[A progress update on SpaceX's Starship](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1578140.htm)** targets September 22 for Flight 14, attempting to reach orbit and deploy 26 Starlink V3 satellites in Starship's first operational payload mission. Meanwhile, **[Chinese travel portal Ctrip listed tickets for Virgin Galactic spaceflights](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578090.htm)** priced at 5.1 million RMB (~\$720,000) for a 2027 suborbital journey, with over 680 seats already sold.

In field robotics, **[China's quadrupedal bionic firefighting robot "Fire Tianlang"](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578052.htm)** completed its first live-fire exercise, navigating structural ruins and enduring 500°C temperatures for up to two minutes to conduct close-range reconnaissance. In hydrology, **[geologists in Xinjiang discovered substantial underground water reserves](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1578048.htm)** beneath the Taklimakan Desert, which will be desalinated at experimental stations to irrigate green shelterbelts and reinforce sand containment barriers.
