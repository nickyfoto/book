# CNBeta — 2026-06-03

## Top Story
DeepSeek is reportedly finalizing a staggering 50 billion RMB (approx. $6.9 billion) initial funding round, pushing its post-money valuation to between 350 billion and 400 billion RMB. According to [a cnbeta report on the funding](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564552.htm), founder Liang Wenfeng will personally inject 20 billion RMB, with Tencent and CATL contributing 10 billion and 5 billion RMB respectively. This massive capital aggregation highlights the intense focus on securing a domestic champion in China's AI ecosystem, especially as the national integrated circuit fund is also in talks to participate.

## Tech & AI
Tencent is quietly advancing plans to embed an AI agent directly into WeChat, initiating compliance processes before a phased public rollout. A [cnbeta report on WeChat's AI](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564510.htm) notes that the agent will allow users to seamlessly access millions of mini-programs for tasks like ordering coffee, though regulatory approval for its 1.4 billion users remains a bottleneck. 

Meanwhile, U.S. attempts to curb China's computing advancements are facing practical limits, as Arm CEO Rene Haas stated in an [interview covered by cnbeta](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564504.htm) that it is nearly impossible to ban AI CPU exports to China. He noted regulators cannot easily establish specific performance and bandwidth thresholds for CPUs like they do for GPUs. This localized push is also reshaping the global memory market, where Agentic AI data center demands pushed the global NAND market to $46 billion in Q1 2026, with a [cnbeta report detailing YMTC's rise](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564546.htm) showing the Chinese manufacturer's market share surging by 246% year-over-year to 13%,.

On the software front, [Microsoft's new Windows 11 strategy](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564540.htm) aims to make the OS an "agent-native" battleground for AI developers, integrating multi-model local execution and enterprise-grade security containers,. The company is even testing new form factors, such as a [wearable AI badge prototype](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564596.htm) equipped with a camera and fingerprint sensor to provide AI agents with real-time environmental context.

## Consumer & Devices
Apple's entry-level strategy is paying off, with a [cnbeta update on Mac shipments](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564584.htm) revealing the company doubled its 2026 MacBook Neo shipment target from 5 million to 10 million units,. The $599 A18 Pro-powered laptop has driven a record number of first-time Mac buyers, forcing Windows competitors like Dell to aggressively counter-market their XPS 13 models.

In the foldable space, a [cnbeta leak of Samsung dummies](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564528.htm) reveals two distinct designs for its next generation: a shorter, wider Galaxy Z Fold8 and a taller, premium Fold8 Ultra featuring a triple-camera module. The new models reportedly boast near-creaseless inner displays and a reduced weight of 201 grams. 

For high-end power users, the upcoming ARM-based [Nvidia RTX Spark N1X laptops](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564574.htm) will likely start at a hefty $2,900 (around 19,631 RMB). The premium price tag gets buyers a 20-core Grace CPU and a 6144-CUDA-core RTX 5070 designed to outmuscle MacBooks in local AI processing and gaming. Conversely, legacy action-camera maker GoPro is in deep trouble; a [cnbeta report on the company's financials](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564520.htm) notes a severe drop in revenue and warns operations could cease within a year, largely exacerbated by AI-driven spikes in memory chip costs.
