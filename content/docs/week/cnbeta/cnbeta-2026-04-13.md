# CNBeta — 2026-04-13

## Top Story
According to **[a report on enterprise AI](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557792.htm)**, Anthropic's annualized recurring revenue (ARR) has crossed the $30 billion mark, surpassing OpenAI's $25 billion for the first time since the ChatGPT boom. This shift highlights a massive surge in enterprise AI adoption, specifically driven by venture-backed startups favoring Claude's capabilities. Consequently, OpenAI is distancing itself from Microsoft's control and deepening its ties with Amazon Web Services to secure more computing power and regain enterprise market dominance **[OpenAI Distances from Microsoft](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557854.htm)**.

## Tech & AI
The global AI compute shortage is intensifying, with **[GPU rental prices soaring by 48%](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557740.htm)** and causing severe service outages for leading AI providers like Anthropic. Meanwhile, the hardware landscape could face a major shakeup as **[Dell and HP stocks jumped](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557850.htm)** amid rumors that Nvidia is negotiating to acquire a major PC manufacturer.

In the semiconductor space, a **[White House advisor warned](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557808.htm)** that China's chip design capabilities are now only 1.5 to 2 years behind the US, noting that Huawei is rapidly catching up and plans to export its Ascend AI chips soon. Facing extreme DRAM shortages and rising costs, **[Qualcomm is partnering with China's Changxin Memory Technologies (CXMT)](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557782.htm)** to develop custom mobile DRAM solutions set for mass production in late 2026.

On the software front, Elon Musk's X platform has globally launched **[XChat](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557794.htm)**, a standalone encrypted messaging app that integrates the Grok AI model and natively supports simplified Chinese for mainland users. Additionally, Microsoft is testing a new **[OpenClaw-style enterprise agent for Copilot](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557852.htm)** that can execute multi-step tasks across M365 applications.

## Consumer & Devices
Apple is ramping up production for its upcoming releases, reportedly increasing the **[first-generation MacBook Neo production to 10 million units](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557838.htm)** powered by the A18 Pro chip. Apple is also making strides in its foldable device development, utilizing **[new optical adhesive materials to solve the persistent screen crease problem](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557836.htm)** by better managing stress distribution.

Microsoft is finally addressing long-standing user complaints by allowing users to **[skip forced updates during Windows 11 setup](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557844.htm)**, enabling faster access to the desktop. Furthermore, a recent Windows 11 update has officially **[removed the 30-year-old 32GB formatting limit for FAT32 drives](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557734.htm)**. However, consumers looking for new Windows hardware will face higher costs, as a global memory shortage has driven Microsoft to **[significantly raise the prices across its entire Surface Pro and Surface Laptop lineup](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557862.htm)**.

## Gaming
Internal friction at Microsoft is making headlines as an **[ex-Bethesda executive criticized the company](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1557832.htm)**, claiming Bethesda was "damaged and mistreated" post-acquisition and now feels "inauthentic". Microsoft is also facing subscription headwinds, with an Xbox executive admitting that **[Xbox Game Pass has become too expensive](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1557848.htm)** for players, prompting internal discussions on pricing and structure adjustments.

On the hardware side, a recent survey indicates that **[40% of core gamers are willing to pay over $699 for the PlayStation 6](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1557748.htm)**, showing high price tolerance for next-gen performance. Meanwhile, the modding community achieved a remarkable feat by getting a **[native port of The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess running at 120 FPS on an iPhone 17](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1557772.htm)**.

## Science & Space
In orbital technology, Canadian firm Kepler and Sophia Space have established the **[world's largest orbital compute cluster](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557754.htm)**, utilizing Nvidia edge processors to handle AI inference directly in space without relying on terrestrial data centers. Competing in the aerospace sector, Amazon unveiled its **[Leo aircraft satellite antenna](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557846.htm)**, directly challenging SpaceX's Starlink for dominance in the in-flight Wi-Fi market.

In astrophysics, a new study reveals that deep, high-pressure **[magma oceans could independently generate strong magnetic fields on super-Earths](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1557732.htm)**, potentially acting as crucial shields to sustain life-friendly environments.
