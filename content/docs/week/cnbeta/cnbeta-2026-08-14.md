# CNBeta — August 14, 2026

## Top Story
According to [a cnbeta report on DeepSeek Harness](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573150.htm), the Chinese AI pioneer has officially launched the developer preview of "Harness," a highly anticipated open-source agentic execution system under the MIT license, alongside the release of its V4 Pro model. This move represents a tectonic shift in AI commercialization, moving away from selling raw API tokens and toward delivering verifiable, complex, multi-step task outcomes. Led by a former Jane Street quant researcher, the Harness framework adopts an "everything is a plugin" philosophy, enabling developers to build stable, transparent, and highly customizable AI agents that can directly operate terminal environments and external APIs.

## Tech & AI
**Artificial Intelligence remains the undisputed battleground as major players adjust their strategies for the Chinese market.** A [cnbeta report on Apple's Chinese AI model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573208.htm) reveals that Apple is training a custom, proprietary AI model specifically for China in collaboration with Alibaba's Qwen and Baidu, attempting to clear domestic regulatory hurdles and counter fierce competition from local rivals like Huawei. Simultaneously, a [cnbeta report on Tencent and Manus](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573156.htm) indicates that Tencent is set to become the largest shareholder of Chinese AI developer Manus, purchasing Meta's stake for approximately \$2 billion after the Chinese government pressured Meta to undo its initial acquisition. Meanwhile, a [cnbeta report on Microsoft's China strategy](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573220.htm) shows how the Redmond giant is shifting focus from selling Windows and Office to helping Chinese businesses like Shein expand globally by providing Azure cloud and OpenAI model access. Supporting this massive surge in domestic AI computing, a [cnbeta report on SMIC's Q2 earnings](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573210.htm) highlights the Chinese foundry giant topping \$3 billion in quarterly revenue for the first time, leading to price hikes for its wafers due to relentless demand for AI-related hardware.

## Consumer & Devices
**Hardware innovation and mobility are seeing deep structural realignments, particularly as Chinese supply chains prove difficult for global tech to replicate.** A [cnbeta report on the foldable iPhone Ultra](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573152.htm) reveals that Apple's upcoming 7.76-inch foldable flagship is suffering from low production yields and component shortages, leading the company to prioritize the US market and delay international rollouts. At the same time, a [cnbeta report on Pony.ai and Uber](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573230.htm) highlights a massive European expansion of 2,000 Robotaxis across five cities, powered by Pony.ai's Chinese-engineered autonomous software which costs less than a quarter of Waymo's equivalent hardware. Geopolitical trade policy is also squeezing legacy automakers: a [cnbeta report on Ford's Lincoln strategy](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573214.htm) notes that Ford will stop exporting Chinese-made Lincoln Nautilus SUVs to the US by 2030 to avoid staggering 52.5% import tariffs, relocating production back to America. Meanwhile, on the telecom front, a [cnbeta report on MasOrange and Huawei](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573236.htm) indicates that Spain's largest mobile operator has paused its plans to phase out Huawei 5G equipment, citing Ericsson's failure to meet key technical and delivery criteria.

## Gaming
**The gaming industry is witnessing monumental sales milestones and long-awaited lifestyle features.** A [cnbeta report on Pokémon: Pokobia](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573228.htm) shows Nintendo's stock surging 7% after the Switch 2 title crossed 5 million in sales in just four months, establishing itself as the platform's second-highest selling game. In the AAA space, a [cnbeta report on GTA 6 pre-orders](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573158.htm) estimates that Rockstar’s upcoming title has generated over 25 million pre-orders, securing roughly \$2 billion in revenue to fully cover its development costs before official marketing even begins. Lastly, a [cnbeta report on Minecraft's sitting feature](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573162.htm) details a nostalgic milestone for the sandbox community as the official release of a "Cushion" item finally allows characters to sit naturally after 17 years of player requests.

## Science & Space
**Fascinating scientific breakthroughs are bridging the gap between local aerospace innovation and interplanetary history.** A [cnbeta report on bionic fly-fish SkyX](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1573216.htm) highlights a young entrepreneurial team from Hunan that has developed a bionic cross-medium vehicle capable of seamlessly transitioning between flying in the air and diving underwater. Meanwhile, a [cnbeta report on Martian meteorite NWA 13441](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1573264.htm) describes the discovery of a rare deep Martian rock that successfully fills a 2-billion-year gap in Mars’s geological history, shedding light on the planet's early volcanic and magmatic activity.

# CNBeta 每日简报 — 2026年8月14日

## 今日焦点 (Top Story)
根据 [cnbeta 报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573150.htm)，中国AI先锋 DeepSeek 正式上线了备受瞩目的开源智能体执行系统“Harness”的开发者预览版（采用 MIT 协议），并同步发布了 V4 Pro 官方正式版。此举标志着 AI 商业化的重大转折：从单纯售卖 API Token 转向交付可验证的、复杂的多步骤任务结果。Harness 团队由前 Jane Street 量化研究员领衔，推崇“一切皆插件”的设计哲学，使开发者能够构建高度稳定、透明且可定制的 AI 智能体，直接操作本地终端和外部 API。

## 科技与人工智能 (Tech & AI)
**人工智能依然是各大巨头在中国市场博弈的核心，战略调整动作频频。** 据 [cnbeta 关于苹果中国 AI 模型的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573208.htm) 透露，苹果正与阿里巴巴（通义千问）和百度合作，为中国市场训练专属的生成式 AI 模型，以应对复杂的本土监管并抗衡华为等本土企图。与此同时，另一篇 [cnbeta 关于腾讯收购 Manus 的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573156.htm) 指出，在中国监管部门干预 Meta 的收购后，腾讯将联合红杉中国、真格基金出资约20亿美元回购 Meta 持有的 Manus 股份，使腾讯成为这家中国 AI 独角兽的最大股东。在企业出海方面，[cnbeta 关于微软中国战略的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573220.htm) 显示，微软正将重心转向服务 Shein 等中国出海企业，通过 Azure 云服务为其提供合规的海外数据管理和 OpenAI 独家模型通道。而为这些庞大本土 AI 算力提供底层支撑的 [cnbeta 关于中芯国际Q2财报的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573210.htm) 则指出，受国内 AI 芯片代工需求激增推动，中芯国际单季营收首次突破 30 亿美元，并已在第一季度和第三季度连续上调代工价格。

## 消费电子与移动出行 (Consumer & Devices)
**硬件创新和全球出行版图正在经历深度重构，中国供应链的绝对优势让海外企业难以忽视。** 根据 [cnbeta 关于折叠屏 iPhone Ultra 的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573152.htm)，由于 2nm 工艺芯片、全新铰链及柔性屏的组装良率面临瓶颈，苹果首款 7.76 英寸折叠屏旗舰初期产能严重受限，首批现货将优先供应美国市场，多国零售商已确认首发缺货。与此同时，[cnbeta 关于小马智行与 Uber 合作的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573230.htm) 透露，双方计划在欧洲 5 个城市内部署超过 2000 辆 Robotaxi，得益于中国成熟的供应链，小马智行第七代自动驾驶套件及整车成本已压低至 Waymo 的四分之一。地缘政治摩擦也在重塑传统汽车产业：[cnbeta 关于福特林肯品牌战略的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573214.htm) 指出，为规避高达 52.5% 的中国进口关税，福特决定自 2030 年起停止向美国出口中国制造的林肯航海家（Lincoln Nautilus），将相关车型的生产迁回美国本土。但在 5G 基础设施上，欧洲清退中企设备仍举步维艰，[cnbeta 关于西班牙 MasOrange 的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573236.htm) 表明，由于爱立信未能达到技术和交付要求，这家西班牙最大运营商已决定暂缓全面替代华为 5G 设备的计划。

## 游戏动态 (Gaming)
**游戏行业迎来了里程碑式的销售神话，以及令老玩家泪目的情怀更新。** 据 [cnbeta 关于《宝可梦：波可比亚》的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573228.htm) 显示，这款 Switch 2 平台新作上市仅四个月销量便突破 500 万份，提振任天堂股价大涨 7%。而在 3A 巨作领域，[cnbeta 关于《GTA6》预售的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573158.htm) 透露，该作预售量预计已达到惊人的 2500 万套，尚未正式开启营销便已带来约 20 亿美元收入，提前收回了高昂的开发成本。最后，[cnbeta 关于《我的世界》更新的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1573162.htm) 带来了一个温暖的消息，官方推出的全新物品“Cushion（坐垫）”终于实现了玩家 17 年来“想在游戏里正常坐下”的夙愿，无需再依赖模组或卡位。

## 科学与太空 (Science & Space)
**前沿科学探索正连接起地方高精尖装备制造与数十亿光年外的时空奥秘。** 一篇 [cnbeta 关于空水跨介质飞行器的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1573216.htm) 介绍了湖南一支平均年龄仅 25 岁的年轻团队自研的仿生飞鱼“SkyX”飞行器，它能无缝切换空中飞行与水下潜行模式，具有极高的科研和应急应用前景。而在天文学领域，[cnbeta 关于火星陨石 NWA 13441 的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1573264.htm) 宣布，科学家通过一块含有原始化学特性的罕见火星深层陨石，成功填补了 Mars 地质历史上近 20 亿年的空白。
