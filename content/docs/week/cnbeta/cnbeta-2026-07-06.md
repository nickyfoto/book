# CNBeta — 2026-07-07

## Top Story
Apple is grappling with a severe supply chain breach after **[a data leak from India's Tata Electronics exposed extensive details about the unreleased iPhone 18 Pro](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567978.htm)**. The leak highlights the security vulnerabilities of Apple's expanding "Make in India" initiative, and has already spurred rapid prototyping among copycat manufacturers in Shenzhen's Huaqiangbei electronics hub **[(read more)](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1568054.htm)**. Concurrently, another leak reveals that Apple's first foldable device, the **[iPhone Ultra, is entering mass production this month](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1568002.htm)**.

## Tech & AI
Tencent has officially launched its new **[Hunyuan Hy3 AI model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1568050.htm)**, which adopts a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture with 295 billion parameters and claims to rival larger flagship models in reasoning and coding tasks. In the AI startup ecosystem, a former Huawei "Genius Youth" publicly criticized **[DeepSeek's sluggish and unprofessional interview process](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1568118.htm)**, claiming he was falsely accused of cheating during a coding test. Over in the US, Elon Musk's xAI has completed its brand restructuring, officially **[merging into SpaceX under the new name SpaceXAI](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1568092.htm)** to leverage the aerospace company's vast infrastructure and data centers. The AI hardware market also saw temporary jitters after rumors circulated that **[Nvidia's next-gen Kyber NVL144 server rack architecture is delayed until 2028](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1568006.htm)** due to PCB manufacturing bottlenecks, though **[Nvidia quickly denied any changes to its roadmap](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1568076.htm)**.

## Consumer & Devices
Tesla has rolled out a **[long-wheelbase version of the Model Y, the Model Y L, across the US, Puerto Rico, and the UAE](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567982.htm)**. The 6-seat family SUV aims to directly compete with traditional automakers in the mid-size SUV segment **[(read more)](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1568068.htm)**. On the computing front, an Apple executive detailed why the **[Mac mini has become a massive hit in the local AI era](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1568034.htm)**, crediting its localized processing capabilities and Apple Silicon for efficiently handling Agent-based workloads. For upcoming smartphones, a leaked image of the **[iPhone 18 Pro motherboard reveals the A20 Pro chip](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1568108.htm)** alongside a Qualcomm 5G modem, while Samsung's upcoming **[Galaxy S27 Pro is slated to use the Exynos 2700 chip](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1568088.htm)** in most global markets instead of Qualcomm's Snapdragon. Furthermore, **[Intel is reportedly planning a dual-side power delivery system for its 14A Gen2 (14A2) process](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1568062.htm)** to rival TSMC and Samsung's 1.4nm advancements.

## Gaming
Sony's controversial decision to **[end physical game disc production by 2028](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1567976.htm)** has ignited a massive debate over digital ownership and triggered a surge of interest in jailbreaking the PlayStation 5. The move has drawn sharp criticism from industry experts and former executives, who warn that players are **[at risk of losing access to their purchased games if accounts are banned or servers go offline](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1568124.htm)**. In other industry news, Microsoft is continuing its massive restructuring, **[laying off roughly 1,600 employees within the Xbox division](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1568072.htm)** as part of a broader 4,800-person workforce reduction, while simultaneously **[appointing Minecraft veteran Helen Chiang as Xbox's first Chief Operating Officer](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1568096.htm)**.

## Science & Space
China's **[Tianwen-2 probe has successfully reached its target asteroid 2016HO3](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1568004.htm)** after a 400-day journey, coming within 20 kilometers to begin its scientific observation and preparation for sample return. Deep beneath the Earth, scientists have discovered a **[bizarre reversal in the flow of molten iron in the outer core under the Pacific Ocean](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1568036.htm)**, challenging traditional models of planetary magnetic field generation. Additionally, a new study introduces a **[3D "laser micro-stirring" technique that enables the additive manufacturing of high-entropy alloys](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1567990.htm)**, paving the way for complex aerospace components.
