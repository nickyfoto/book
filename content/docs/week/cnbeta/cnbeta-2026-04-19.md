# CNBeta — 2026-04-19

## Top Story
According to **[a cnbeta report](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558780.htm)**, NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang strongly criticized US AI chip export bans on China, calling the assumption that American companies will inevitably lose market share to local Chinese competitors a "loser mentality". Huang argued that isolating China will force it to develop an entirely separate technology stack, fundamentally harming the global reach of the US tech ecosystem and warning that AI innovation should not be artificially fractured.

## Tech & AI
In the AI sector, a major **[cnbeta report on DeepSeek](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558768.htm)** indicates the Chinese AI startup is seeking its first external funding of $300 million at a $10 billion valuation, despite previous claims of self-sufficiency. Meanwhile in the US, **[OpenAI is experiencing an executive exodus](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558826.htm)**, with three top executives departing on the same day as the company prepares to shut down its Sora video generation tool due to high costs and declining usage.

On the hardware side, an **[ASML earnings update](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558812.htm)** reveals that memory chip EUV orders have surpassed logic orders for the first time, driven by surging demand for AI infrastructure and HBM chips. This aligns with a broader **[global DRAM shortage](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558784.htm)** that has caused prices to skyrocket, prompting **[Samsung to phase out mature LPDDR4/4X production](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558786.htm)** by year-end to focus entirely on high-margin LPDDR5/5X and future LPDDR6 memory.

In networking, the IETF is reviewing a proposed **[IPv8 protocol draft](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558818.htm)** that would provide 184 billion addresses while remaining 100% backwards compatible with IPv4, aiming to solve the slow adoption rate of IPv6. A striking demographic trend was also noted in a **[survey on Gen Z university graduates](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558810.htm)**, which shows they harbor the highest levels of anxiety and hostility toward AI, fearing the technology will eliminate entry-level jobs and destroy their career prospects.

## Consumer & Devices
The ongoing memory shortage is taking a toll on product roadmaps, with an **[Apple supply chain report](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558832.htm)** suggesting that the upcoming M5 Mac Studio and OLED touchscreen MacBook Pro will be delayed to late 2026 and 2027, respectively.

In the PC hardware space, an **[Intel Core Ultra 5 250K Plus review](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558788.htm)** shows the new $199 chip edging out the AMD Ryzen 5 9600X in multi-threaded productivity tasks, though the AMD chip remains highly competitive in power efficiency. To combat AMD's dominance in gaming cache, **[Intel is reportedly developing a 288MB bLLC flat cache](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558794.htm)** for its Nova Lake desktop processors, aiming to brute-force a performance advantage over AMD's stacked 3D V-Cache. Meanwhile, enthusiasts eyeing the **[AMD Ryzen 9 9950X3D2](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558790.htm)** will need serious cooling, as its Package Power Tracking (PPT) limit reportedly hits 250W, driving temperatures near 100°C under load.

For software and services, **[Windows 11's File Explorer is getting a speed boost](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558800.htm)** and native support for more archive formats. In China, travelers face ticketing hurdles as **[12306 blocks third-party platforms](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558822.htm)** like Ctrip and Fliggy from automated train ticket booking ahead of the May Day holiday.

## Gaming
Early details on the next console generation are surfacing, with **[PS6 performance rumors](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1558850.htm)** indicating a 3x boost in rasterization over the PS5 and ray-tracing capabilities potentially rivaling Nvidia's future RTX 5090.

In the broader industry, a **[game developer survey](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1558814.htm)** reveals that 44% of professionals are considering leaving the industry due to AI-induced layoffs and creative burnout, making AI a contentious topic. Taking a stance against this trend, handheld console maker **[Panic has banned AI-generated games](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1558776.htm)** from its Playdate Catalog, prohibiting AI use in art, music, and text.

Nostalgia is also taking a hit as the popular **[World of Warcraft private server Turtle WoW](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1558770.htm)** announced its shutdown following a cease-and-desist order from Blizzard. Finally, racing fans got a treat with a **[Forza Horizon 6 gameplay reveal](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1558774.htm)** showcasing 35 minutes of action across Tokyo's diverse urban and rural landscapes.

## Science & Space
**[Blue Origin's New Glenn rocket](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1558828.htm)** successfully recovered its first-stage booster in a major milestone for reusability. However, a subsequent **[New Glenn mission failure](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1558852.htm)** saw the rocket's upper stage place an AST SpaceMobile satellite into a sub-optimal orbit, resulting in the total loss of the BlueBird 7 payload.

In deep space, **[NASA turned off Voyager 1's LECP instrument](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1558842.htm)** to conserve the probe's rapidly depleting power supply, nearly 49 years after its launch. Astronomers also identified **[Exoplanet L 98-59 d](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1558844.htm)** as a rare "sulfur magma world," potentially representing an entirely new class of rocky planets. Closer to Earth, an **[AI telemetry study](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1558846.htm)** tracking global ocean floating algae revealed massive explosive growth over the past two decades, driven by climate change and coastal runoff.

# CNBeta — 2026-04-19 (中文版)

## 头条新闻 (Top Story)
据 **[cnbeta报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558780.htm)**，NVIDIA首席执行官黄仁勋强烈批评美国对华AI芯片出口禁令，称认为美国公司必然会把市场份额输给中国本土竞争对手的假设是一种“失败者心态”。黄仁勋指出，孤立中国只会迫使其开发出一套完全独立的底层技术栈，这最终将严重损害美国技术生态的全球影响力，并警告不应人为割裂AI领域的创新。

## 科技与人工智能 (Tech & AI)
在AI领域，一篇关于 **[DeepSeek的cnbeta报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558768.htm)** 指出，尽管之前声称资金充裕，这家中国AI初创公司正寻求以100亿美元的估值进行首轮3亿美元的外部融资。同时在美国，**[OpenAI正经历高管离职潮](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558826.htm)**，三名核心高管同日离职，且公司正准备关闭因成本高昂和使用率下降的Sora视频生成工具。

在硬件方面，一份 **[ASML财报更新](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558812.htm)** 显示，受AI基础设施和HBM芯片需求激增的推动，存储芯片的EUV订单量首次反超逻辑芯片。这与更广泛的 **[全球DRAM短缺](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558784.htm)** 趋势一致，短缺导致价格飙升，促使 **[三星计划在年底前逐步停产成熟的LPDDR4/4X](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558786.htm)**，将产能全面转向高利润的LPDDR5/5X及未来的LPDDR6内存。

在网络技术方面，IETF正在审议一份提议的 **[IPv8协议草案](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558818.htm)**，该草案将提供1844亿个地址，同时保持对IPv4的100%向下兼容，旨在解决IPv6普及缓慢的问题。此外，一项 **[关于Z世代大学毕业生的调查](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558810.htm)** 揭示了一个引人注目的社会现象：这一群体对AI的焦虑和敌意最深，担心该技术会消除初级白领岗位并破坏他们的职业前景。

## 消费电子与设备 (Consumer & Devices)
持续的内存短缺正在影响产品路线图，一份 **[苹果供应链报告](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558832.htm)** 暗示，即将推出的搭载M5芯片的Mac Studio和OLED触摸屏MacBook Pro将被迫分别推迟至2026年底和2027年发布。

在PC硬件领域，一份 **[Intel酷睿Ultra 5 250K Plus评测](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558788.htm)** 显示，这款售价199美元的新处理器在多线程生产力任务中微弱优势领先AMD锐龙5 9600X，不过AMD在能效比上依然极具竞争力。为了对抗AMD在游戏缓存方面的统治地位，**[据传Intel正在为其Nova Lake桌面处理器开发288MB的bLLC大平层缓存](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558794.htm)**，旨在通过面积暴力堆叠以抗衡AMD的3D V-Cache。与此同时，关注 **[AMD锐龙9 9950X3D2](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558790.htm)** 的硬核玩家将需要强大的散热支持，因为据称其封装功耗(PPT)极限达到250W，满载时温度逼近100℃。

在软件服务方面，**[Windows 11的文件资源管理器迎来了速度提升](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558800.htm)**，并原生支持了更多压缩包格式。在中国，旅客在五一假期前面临购票障碍，因为 **[12306屏蔽了第三方平台](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1558822.htm)** (如携程、飞猪) 的自动化抢票和出票功能。

## 游戏 (Gaming)

在更广泛的行业中，一份 **[游戏开发者调查](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1558814.htm)** 显示，由于AI引发的裁员潮和创造力受损，44%的受访者正在考虑离开游戏行业，这也让AI成为行业内的争议焦点。掌机制造商Panic对此作出了明确表态，**[Panic禁止了AI生成的游戏](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1558776.htm)** 登陆其Playdate应用商店，限制在美术、音乐和文本中使用生成式AI。
