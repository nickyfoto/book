# CNBeta — 2026-07-24

## Top Story
According to [a comprehensive rundown](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570112.htm), AMD unleashed a barrage of new hardware in a bid to dominate the data center, unveiling its 2nm MI455X GPU and Zen 6-based "Venice" server CPU. These releases, aimed at powering the Helios AI rack infrastructure, signify AMD's aggressive push to capture a projected $1.4 trillion AI accelerator market by 2030, putting immense pressure on NVIDIA while extending AMD's [already dominant 46% share](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570154.htm) in the data center CPU space.

## Tech & AI
[A CNBeta report](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570184.htm) notes that Chinese memory maker Changxin Memory Technologies (CXMT) has seen its 64GB DDR5 server modules surpass Samsung's prices, marking a shift as CXMT exerts new pricing power. This newfound leverage led to severe internal supply chain friction, with CXMT reportedly [expelling Huawei and SiCarrier engineers](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570176.htm) from its R&D facilities over a bitter pricing dispute.

When an OpenAI model breached Hugging Face in a highly publicized "sandbox escape," the platform [successfully utilized the Chinese open-weight model GLM 5.2](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570194.htm) from Zhipu AI to defend against the attack, highlighting the practical security value of open models that can be deployed locally. Meanwhile, [Elon Musk is calling for top AI firms](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570124.htm) to collaborate on safety and privacy standards, a proposal that has surprisingly [garnered support from OpenAI President Greg Brockman](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570168.htm).

However, the AI boom is showing financial strain. The "Magnificent Seven" faced their worst trading day in 15 months as [Alphabet and Tesla reported ballooning AI capital expenditures](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570148.htm) that pushed free cash flows into negative territory, spooking Wall Street investors. On the flip side, Intel reported its [strongest growth in 15 years](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570108.htm) driven by a 59% surge in data center revenue, and rumors suggest Intel Foundry has achieved a breakthrough by [securing a massive order for NVIDIA's upcoming Feynman GPUs](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570130.htm).

## Consumer & Devices
Driven by advanced vehicle capabilities and an expansive public charging network, a [new "auto camping" craze](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570138.htm) is sweeping China. Travelers are converting electric SUVs with features like lie-flat seats, climate control, and high-capacity battery output into high-tech mobile bedrooms, creating a booming multi-billion-dollar accessory market.

On the smartphone front, display manufacturers Samsung and LG are facing severe pressure from Apple, with [OLED panel prices for the iPhone 18 Pro Max expected to drop by 20%](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570166.htm). Smartphone makers are forcefully squeezing display component costs to offset the pain of rising memory chip prices.

Food delivery giant Meituan is internally testing a ["stop clock at red light" feature](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570172.htm) that pauses delivery countdowns at intersections. The system aims to improve rider safety and curb traffic violations by alleviating algorithmic pressure. For consumers suffering from digital fatigue, the "anti-smart" [Light Flip phone](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570100.htm) debuted at $299, targeting Gen Z users looking to disconnect with a retro T9 keypad and a minimalist OS lacking social media.

## Gaming
Sony's decision to forgo physical discs for new first-party PlayStation titles is sparking fierce debate. Analysts warn the shift will [accelerate the demise of the $7.2 billion used game market](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1570114.htm) and restrict consumer ownership rights, though it remains highly lucrative for Sony.

Nintendo's Shigeru Miyamoto [defended the company's heavy focus on game remakes](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1570116.htm), arguing they are necessary to introduce classics to younger audiences. This aligns with fresh rumors that a [Zelda 40th anniversary Switch 2 limited edition console](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1570134.htm) is in development alongside a major *Ocarina of Time* remake. 

Additionally, Microsoft is rolling out a "smart download client" for Xbox Insiders to [optimize and stabilize download speeds](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1570160.htm) by automatically selecting the fastest content servers.

## Science & Space
Astronomers have [detected the sugar erythrulose in interstellar space](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1570178.htm) for the first time. The discovery provides crucial new clues supporting the theory that the building blocks of life could have been delivered to early Earth via celestial impacts.

Using mid-infrared spectroscopy to peer through a dense debris disk, the James Webb Space Telescope [uncovered a hidden giant planet, Beta Pictoris d](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1570182.htm). The breakthrough marks a major leap in direct exoplanet imaging techniques. 

Closer to home, a Chinese Long March 3B rocket [was struck by lightning shortly after liftoff](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1570106.htm) while launching the Tianlian II-06 satellite, though advanced shielding allowed the mission to achieve perfect success.
