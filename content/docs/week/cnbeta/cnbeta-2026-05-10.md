# CNBeta — 2026-05-10

## Top Story
According to **[a cnbeta report on Anthropic's controversial data gathering](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561972.htm)**, the AI startup's internal "Project Panama" involved purchasing, destructively scanning, and then pulping millions of physical books to train its Claude models. This aggressive approach highlights the extreme lengths and legally gray "fair use" tactics AI companies are employing to acquire high-quality training data without securing direct licensing from publishers. 

## Tech & AI
**[Meta's massive bet on generative AI](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561950.htm)** is reportedly causing internal friction, as the company implements strict workplace tracking via its "Model Capability Initiative" to train internal AI models, while simultaneously cutting 8,000 jobs to help fund a $600 billion infrastructure build-out. This aligns with broader industry trends, where **[US tech sector unemployment hit 3.8% in April](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561958.htm)** due to AI-driven workforce restructuring and shifting hiring priorities that favor senior AI talent over entry-level developers. 

On the hardware side, Nvidia and Intel are deepening their ties, highlighted by **[Jensen Huang receiving an honorary doctorate at CMU](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561996.htm)**, as the two giants collaborate on custom Xeon processors and next-gen consumer SoCs. Meanwhile, **[SMIC founder Richard Chang argues](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562000.htm)** that China's semiconductor industry doesn't need to strictly focus on 2nm or 3nm nodes to be successful, noting that mature processes make up over 80% of the global market demand. 

Domestically, **[Alibaba is integrating its Tongyi Qianwen AI into Taobao](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561964.htm)**, transforming the e-commerce experience from traditional keyword searches to conversational AI agents that can handle browsing, price comparisons, and ordering.

## Consumer & Devices
Apple is heavily infusing AI into its upcoming operating systems, with **[Safari 27 expected to automatically organize browser tabs](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561980.htm)** using on-device machine learning to group related topics. This will coincide with **[macOS 27's "Liquid Glass" UI updates](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561978.htm)**, which will refine transparency and shadow effects to improve overall readability. 

For photographers, Increment Labs has launched the **[LMW-V1 light meter watch](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561952.htm)**, a retro-styled, aluminum-bodied wearable designed to help users constantly practice manual exposure estimation even when they aren't carrying a camera. 

In a controversial ecosystem move, **[Google's new reCAPTCHA rules](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561960.htm)** now require Android users to have Google Play Services installed to pass human verification, effectively locking out users of de-Googled custom ROMs like GrapheneOS from basic web access.

## Gaming
A report on **[Microsoft's new Xbox CEO Asha Sharma](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1561994.htm)** highlights the internal chaos she faces, including a corporate mandate for 30% profit margins that has led to console shortages, Game Pass price hike attempts, and a controversial shift toward multi-platform releases. 

Meanwhile, **[Sony is proactively messaging PS4 users](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1561976.htm)**, urging them to upgrade to a PS5 ahead of the highly anticipated launch of *GTA 6*, signaling to many that Rockstar's marketing machine is about to ramp up. 

In a lighter gaming and toy update, **[Lego officially changed its 99-year age limit](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1561968.htm)** on packaging to "100+" to honor legendary broadcaster Sir David Attenborough's 100th birthday.

## Science & Space

In a separate space hazard study, researchers warn that **[destroying an Earth-bound asteroid](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1561984.htm)** could inadvertently create a hazardous debris cloud around the Moon, potentially triggering Kessler Syndrome in lunar orbit. 

On Earth, ecologists have discovered that **[beaver dams turn rivers into massive carbon sinks](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1561992.htm)**, capturing up to ten times more CO2 than unaffected areas. Furthermore, researchers tracking avian cognition found that **[parrots use specific names](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1561990.htm)** to identify individual humans and animals, showcasing highly advanced social communication.
