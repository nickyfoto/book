# CNBeta — September 12, 2026

## Top Story
According to **[a CNBeta report on AI industry leaders](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577648.htm)**, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, Elon Musk, and OpenAI CEO Sam Altman have made a rare joint call to throttle the pace of frontier AI development, warning that recursive self-improvement and autonomous agent swarms pose critical existential risks. This call comes as **[25 Fields Medalists, led by Terence Tao, published an open letter](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1577568.htm)** accusing OpenAI and Anthropic of undermining mathematical research by treating millennium prize problems as PR benchmarks and allegedly training on unreleased academic drafts. The brewing backlash highlights a pivotal conflict as tech giants race toward multi-trillion-dollar valuations while facing unprecedented ethical and safety resistance from top scientific minds.

## Tech & AI
OpenAI has officially unbundled its developer tools, with **[a CNBeta analysis of OpenAI's Codex strategy](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577572.htm)** detailing the launch of a cloud-hosted Agents API that provides "Codex as a Service" for autonomous workflows. This managed harness approach directly contrasts with DeepSeek Harness's modular, open-source plugin ecosystem.

In China's AI unicorn landscape, **[Moonshot AI launched its Kimi K2.8 model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577580.htm)** featuring a 1-million-token context window open to all membership tiers to lower inference costs as the firm targets \$2 billion in annual recurring revenue ahead of a Hong Kong IPO. Meanwhile, Moonshot AI issued an official statement **[refuting false rumors regarding its founder and employees](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577584.htm)** and confirming it has reported the misinformation to police authorities. Concurrently, **[DeepSeek initiated gray-box testing for voice conversations](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577634.htm)** on its mobile application, introducing four distinct voice personas alongside its lightweight DeepSeek V4.1 Flash multimodal architecture.

On the hardware modification front, **[Shenzhen Suqiao Technology introduced a modded RTX 5090](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577618.htm)** with VRAM expanded to 96GB, offering local LLM developers a \$3,888 alternative to Nvidia's \$16,000 enterprise GPUs despite conflicting GDDR6X specs listed on Alibaba. Elsewhere, **[Google faced open-source license scrutiny](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577626.htm)** after startup Minitap accused Google's Artemis AI mobile automation project of directly copying code from its "mobile-use" repository without proper attribution.

## Consumer & Devices
Apple officially kicked off global pre-orders for the **[iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577632.htm)** featuring 2nm A20 Pro processors, narrowed Dynamic Islands, and variable aperture cameras. High consumer demand and initial 65% manufacturing yields for the foldable **[iPhone Duo led to massive scalper markups](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577570.htm)**, with secondary market asking prices surging up to 59,999 RMB. Apple executives acknowledged that while **[leaks revealing the iPhone Duo's aspect ratio were unfortunate](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577564.htm)**, the company successfully kept its nano-texture display processing a secret prior to launch.

In wearable technology, **[Apple detailed the Watch S11 chip](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577630.htm)**, which inherits its 2nm architecture from the A20 Pro to drive 60x denser heart-rate sampling and offline Audio Intelligence. This launch comes as Apple CEO John Ternus **[addressed privacy concerns surrounding AI smart glasses](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577582.htm)**, emphasizing user consent in response to public backlash against rival wearables.

Market intelligence from Counterpoint Research on **[foldable display panel shipments](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577562.htm)** predicts a 23% overall market expansion in 2026, driven primarily by Apple's entry, which is expected to capture 27% of annual panel volume. In Chinese consumer dining, Chinese restaurant chain Xibei saw its **[store count halved from 400 to 203 locations](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1577576.htm)** over the past year following a viral pre-cooked food controversy with influencer Luo Yonghao that severely damaged brand trust.

## Gaming
Blizzard Entertainment made major waves at BlizzCon 2026, **[officially announcing Diablo V for 2029](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1577658.htm)** set a century after the fourth installment in a completely fallen Sanctuary, alongside **[an open-world StarCraft shooter for 2030](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1577660.htm)** focusing on Terran infantry combat, and the immediate release of **[a 30+ hour Warcraft III: Reforged DLC](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1577662.htm)** exploring the rise of the Forsaken.

In platform rights, **[Sony's legal defense against a digital game ownership lawsuit](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1577592.htm)**—which argued that rational consumers understand digital purchases are merely revocable licenses—was challenged by consumer advocates compiling over 30 instances where Sony explicitly claimed players "own" digital titles. Meanwhile, **[OpenAI developer Varrma took down his AI RuneScape clone](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1577628.htm)** after receiving a cease-and-desist notice from publisher Jagex regarding his Codex-generated browser game *Elderwood*.

## Science & Space
In neuro-AI research, scientists successfully **[connected a fruit fly brain simulation directly to ChatGPT](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1577574.htm)**, converting biological neural firing spikes into natural language prompts and allowing the simulated insect connectome to communicate with an LLM.

In space exploration, **[NASA withdrew funding for the EnVision mission radar](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1577610.htm)** due to domestic budget cuts, prompting the European Space Agency to independently build the VenSAR replacement and delay launch to 2032.

In physical science, physicists **[recreated 2,000°C+ superionic ice in a laboratory](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1577616.htm)**, confirming a hexagonal close-packed crystal structure that explains the unusual off-center magnetic fields of Uranus and Neptune. Meanwhile, astronomers identified **[COSMOS-z3.1-A, the most distant protocluster](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1577602.htm)**, formed when the universe was only 2.1 billion years old.
