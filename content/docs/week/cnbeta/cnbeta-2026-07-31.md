# CNBeta — 2026-07-31

## Top Story
The Wall Street Journal reported that [Tesla is exploring a sale or spin-off of its China operations](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571092.htm) to clear regulatory hurdles for a potential future merger with SpaceX. However, Elon Musk quickly took to social media to label the rumor as "fake news". This highlights the intense geopolitical scrutiny surrounding Musk's dual roles, especially as [SpaceX is reportedly actively purging all Chinese supply chain components](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571090.htm) and personnel from its manufacturing ecosystem.

## Tech & AI
Chinese AI giant DeepSeek is aggressively scaling up its compute capacity, reportedly [planning a massive 1-gigawatt AI data center in Inner Mongolia](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571076.htm) to compete directly with Silicon Valley leaders. The company also rolled out its [DeepSeek-V4-Flash model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571132.htm), which surprisingly outperformed its own Pro preview version in AI Agent capabilities, proving that lower API costs do not necessarily mean compromised performance. In the semiconductor space, [TSMC is developing an EMIB-like advanced packaging technology](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571146.htm) to defend against Intel and relieve severe AI chip bottlenecks. Meanwhile, China's domestic supply chain hit a major milestone as [Dinglong launched the country's first fully independent high-end photoresist production line](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571154.htm), securing a crucial node in semiconductor manufacturing. On the gig-economy front, [Meituan is rolling out a "wait at red light" feature](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571152.htm) in 20 cities that pauses delivery countdowns at intersections, marking a significant algorithmic shift to prioritize rider safety over speed.

## Consumer & Devices
Apple's Q3 earnings revealed strong iPhone sales, but executives warned that [severe supply constraints on advanced chips and memory](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571150.htm) will squeeze margins going into the holiday quarter. Driven by skyrocketing 2nm and memory costs, analysts predict a massive [$250 to $300 price hike for the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro series](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571160.htm). In the GPU market, [AMD is notifying partners of a minimum 10% price increase on Radeon GPUs](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571156.htm) starting in August due to rising GDDR6 costs, while scalpers have already pushed [RTX 5080 retail prices higher than the RTX 5090's original MSRP](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571112.htm). In mobile hardware, [Transsion unveiled a conceptual smartphone with a true 0mm bezel](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571126.htm), entirely eliminating visible black borders around the display. Conversely, [Black Shark has completely shut down its official smartphone after-sales service channels](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571148.htm), marking the definitive end for the once-popular gaming phone brand.

## Gaming
The physical media debate continues to heat up as [Sony's CFO confirmed the company is moving forward with ending physical disc production](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571142.htm), acknowledging player frustration but insisting the all-digital transition is necessary. Industry analysts warn this strategy is short-sighted, arguing that [abandoning physical discs is "digging its own grave"](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1571110.htm) by eliminating the lowest-barrier entry point for new PlayStation users. In a related hardware struggle, [Xbox reported a staggering $1.7 billion loss for FY2026](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1571166.htm) following a 29% plunge in console sales. On the software side, [leaked amiibo listings suggest a Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake is launching on November 12, 2026](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1571096.htm), positioning it to clash directly with the launch window of GTA 6.

## Science & Space
NASA's Curiosity rover has discovered a [massive "honeycomb" polygon landscape on Mars](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1571054.htm), offering the largest-scale evidence yet of ancient, cyclic water flows on the Red Planet. Closer to home, a [discarded SpaceX Falcon 9 upper stage is projected to crash into the Moon on August 5](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1571124.htm), providing researchers a unique opportunity to study lunar impact plumes and subsurface geology. Back on Earth, a breakthrough in genetic recombination mapping has revealed [two "ghost ancestors" in modern human DNA](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1571140.htm), proving that the human evolutionary tree is an entangled web of migrations and interbreeding stretching back 1.8 million years.
