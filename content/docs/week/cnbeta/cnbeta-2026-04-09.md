# CNBeta — 2026-04-09

## Top Story
A **[report from CNBeta](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557216.htm)** reveals that Chinese memory maker Changxin Memory Technologies (CXMT) has begun mass production of 12-layer High Bandwidth Memory (HBM). This critical breakthrough enables CXMT to enter the high-end AI hardware supply chain, narrowing the manufacturing capability gap with Korean giants Samsung and SK Hynix to less than three years. The company is reportedly dedicating 20% of its total DRAM capacity to HBM production to meet domestic AI demands and is seeking a $4.2 billion IPO to further expand.

## Tech & AI
The US Federal Communications Commission (FCC) is proposing a comprehensive **[ban on Chinese laboratories](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557166.htm)** testing electronic devices for the US market, a move that could severely disrupt the global electronics supply chain since 75% of such products are currently tested in China. In the AI model race, ByteDance has launched **[Seeduplex](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557188.htm)**, a native full-duplex voice model integrated into its Doubao app, achieving simultaneous listening and speaking to drastically reduce interruption errors. Meanwhile, Meta has released its highly anticipated **[Muse Spark (Avocado)](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557190.htm)**, a closed-source, native multimodal reasoning model spearheaded by Alexandr Wang, marking a strategic pivot from its traditional open-source approach. On the infrastructure side, China is leveraging its massive renewable energy grid in the west to generate **[low-cost AI tokens](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557218.htm)**, with domestic inference costs dropping to a fraction of overseas competitors. Furthermore, a massive leak has exposed **[Nvidia's N1 SoC motherboard](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557246.htm)**, confirming the chip giant's ambitious push into the Windows on Arm (WoA) laptop market to challenge Intel and AMD.

## Consumer & Devices
Tesla is reportedly developing a **[new compact SUV in China](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557268.htm)** that will be significantly cheaper than the Model 3, aiming for a mass-market price point with a smaller battery and a lighter 1.5-ton chassis. This comes as the Chinese auto market continues its rapid EV transition, with **[March sales data](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557252.htm)** showing BYD's Song dominating the charts at number one, followed by Tesla's Model Y. In the smartphone sector, the **[iPhone 17 Pro Max](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557162.htm)** reigned supreme as the world's top-selling smartphone, capturing 5% of global sales. Looking ahead, Apple is currently testing a **[miniaturized Dynamic Island](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557280.htm)** for the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro by moving Face ID components beneath the screen, though a final decision has yet to be made.

## Gaming
Gamers eagerly awaiting *Grand Theft Auto 6* might face a staggered release, as **[new rumors suggest](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1557138.htm)** Rockstar will launch the multiplayer mode a month after the single-player campaign, with PC players forced to wait 12 to 18 months. On the racing front, the newly revealed map for **[Forza Horizon 6](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1557134.htm)** features routes inspired by real-world Japanese landmarks, including the iconic Mount Haruna—famous as Mount Akina in the *Initial D* series. In quirkier news, the release of the Nintendo Switch 2 has revived a bizarre 2017 trend: **[players are licking the new cartridges](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1557136.htm)** and discovering they still taste incredibly bitter, thanks to a non-toxic chemical coating designed to prevent children from swallowing them.

## Science & Space
NASA has released a stunning batch of photos from the **[Artemis 2 mission](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1557258.htm)**, showcasing breathtaking views of an "Earthset" behind the moon's rugged far side and a total solar eclipse captured from deep space. Further out in the cosmos, astronomers have discovered a **[supermassive black hole binary](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1557240.htm)** in the galaxy Mrk 501 that is projected to merge within the next 100 years, offering a rare real-time glimpse into cosmic evolution.

# CNBeta — 2026-04-09

## Top Story
据 **[CNBeta 的一篇报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557216.htm)** 披露，中国存储芯片制造商长鑫存储(CXMT)已正式量产 12 层高带宽内存(HBM)。这一关键突破使长鑫存储具备了进入高端 AI 硬件供应链的能力，将其与韩国巨头三星和 SK 海力士在制造能力上的差距缩短至不到三年。据报道，该公司正将其 20% 的 DRAM 总产能投入 HBM 生产，以满足国内 AI 需求，并计划通过 42 亿美元的 IPO 进一步扩张。

## Tech & AI
美国联邦通信委员会(FCC)正提议 **[全面禁止中国实验室](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557166.htm)** 对进入美国市场的电子设备进行准入测试，此举可能会严重扰乱全球电子供应链，因为目前 75% 的此类产品在中国进行测试。在 AI 模型竞赛中，字节跳动推出了 **[Seeduplex](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557188.htm)**，这是一款集成在豆包 App 中的原生全双工语音大模型，实现了边听边说的同步交互，大幅降低了误打断率。同时，Meta 发布了备受期待的 **[Muse Spark (Avocado)](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557190.htm)**，这是一个由 Alexandr Wang 牵头开发的闭源原生多模态推理模型，标志着该公司从传统的开源路线发生了战略转向。在基础设施方面，中国正利用西部庞大的可再生能源网络生成 **[低成本的 AI Token](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557218.htm)**，使国内的推理成本降至海外竞争对手的一小部分。此外，一次大规模泄露曝光了 **[Nvidia 的 N1 SoC 主板](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557246.htm)**，证实了这家芯片巨头正雄心勃勃地进军 Windows on Arm (WoA) 笔记本市场，以挑战英特尔和 AMD。

## Consumer & Devices
据报道，特斯拉正在开发一款 **[全新的中国产紧凑型 SUV](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557268.htm)**，该车型将比 Model 3 便宜得多，旨在通过更小的电池和仅 1.5 吨的轻量化底盘主打大众市场。与此同时，中国汽车市场继续快速向电动化转型，**[3 月份销量数据](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557252.htm)** 显示比亚迪宋占据榜首，特斯拉 Model Y 紧随其后。在智能手机领域，**[iPhone 17 Pro Max](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557162.htm)** 依然是全球最畅销的智能手机，占据了全球总销量的 5%。展望未来，苹果目前正在为即将推出的 iPhone 18 Pro 测试一种 **[“迷你灵动岛”](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557280.htm)** 设计，通过将 Face ID 组件移至屏幕下方来缩小开孔，不过最终决定尚未敲定。
