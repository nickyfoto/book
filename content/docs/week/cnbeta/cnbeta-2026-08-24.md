# CNBeta — August 24, 2026

## Top Story
According to [a cnbeta report on Huawei's upcoming flagship chip](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574664.htm), the Mate 90 series will debut a Kirin processor utilizing "logic folding" technology (based on "Tao's Law"), achieving an equivalent transistor density that rivals TSMC’s 3nm process. This domestic breakthrough allows China's semiconductor supply chain to bypass advanced lithography restrictions and establishes a roadmap to reach 1.4nm-equivalent densities by 2031. The development marks an epochal shift in China's hardware ecosystem, signaling a significant step toward chip self-sufficiency and a direct challenge to Apple's dominance.

## Tech & AI
A [cnbeta report on Xiaomi's Xuanjie O3 SoC](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574710.htm) reveals a powerhouse 3nm mobile chip with 24 billion transistors and a ten-core all-big-core CPU architecture that surpasses Apple's A19 Pro in GeekBench 6.5 multi-core performance. Crucially, the SoC [pioneers support for LPDDR6 memory in partnership with China's CXMT](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574748.htm), elevating domestic semiconductor capabilities and breaking the historical monopoly of foreign memory makers. Meanwhile, US AI leaders face changing tides: [Anthropic's high-priced Fable 5 model is seeing cold reception](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574628.htm) as enterprise users shift to lower-cost, high-performance models like OpenAI's GPT-5.6. Amid this shifting landscape, AI platform [Hugging Face is reportedly exploring a sale at a \$13 billion valuation](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574672.htm), following Stripe's \$8 billion acquisition of model router OpenRouter. On the clinical front, Alibaba's [DAMO Academy published a Nature Medicine paper on its DAMO LiON liver cancer AI](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574706.htm), which successfully caught 15 missed cases of 1-centimeter micro-tumors during a real-world hospital trial, improving diagnostic sensitivity by 11.5%.

## Consumer & Devices
According to [a cnbeta report on Apple's upcoming lineup](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574636.htm), the tech giant is set to announce its September launch event, debuting the iPhone 18 Pro series alongside its first-ever foldable, the iPhone Ultra. As [Mark Gurman notes](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574634.htm), with China commanding over 50% of global foldable shipments, winning status-seeking Chinese buyers is critical, even as [rising memory and chip costs threaten to push Pro pricing up by \$100](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574630.htm). In contrast, the ambitious ecosystem expansion of smart appliance firm Dreame Technology has ended in a spectacular retreat; [a deep-dive into Dreame's phone and car divisions](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574736.htm) reveals that its 1,000-person automotive team has collapsed to just 100, its 3-million-RMB concept cars are gathering dust in a basement, and its smartphone division was quietly dissolved. Meanwhile, Samsung is preparing its [Galaxy S27 Ultra with an iPhone-like horizontal camera matrix](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574640.htm), potentially powered by a next-gen Exynos 2700 2nm chip that [reportedly beats Qualcomm's Snapdragon 8 Gen 6 Pro in internal efficiency testing](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574756.htm). Lastly, EV maker Ideal Auto showcased its [refreshed flagship pure-electric minivan, the Ideal MEGA](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574738.htm), capturing attention with its striking solid-white retro 'pie' wheel covers and upgraded dual-motor output of 413kW.

However, as [players question whether CyberLeek actually has the full game](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1574678.htm), the hacker has successfully monetized the hype by launching a self-titled cryptocurrency that generated \$15 million in volume on day one, prompting parent company Take-Two to obtain a [court order demanding Discord hand over the identities of up to 100,000 users](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1574694.htm) from related channels. Lastly, following Game Science's 15-minute gameplay demo of *Black Myth: Zhong Kui*, [foreign media praised the game's breathtaking photorealistic environments](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1574648.htm) but warned that optimizing its heavy Unreal Engine 5 build for consoles will pose a massive challenge for the developer.

## Science & Space
China's ambitious lunar exploration program faced a sudden setback as authorities announced that the [Chang'e 7 south pole exploration mission cannot meet launch conditions](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1574638.htm) and has been postponed from its highly anticipated 2026 window. In a major milestone for reproductive biology, [Japanese scientists successfully cloned fertile female mice from male mouse cells](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1574742.htm) by using CRISPR gene-editing to completely delete the Y chromosome, triggering the embryos to differentiate into female XX mammals. Furthermore, a genetic study published in Taxon has [unveiled a hidden new species of Baobab tree, Adansonia bozy, in Madagascar](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1574654.htm), which was previously misclassified as another species, a discovery that will alter local conservation funding and species threat levels.

# CNBeta 每日精选 — 2026年8月24日

## 头条新闻
根据 [CNBeta关于华为全新旗舰芯片的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574664.htm)，即将发布的 Mate 90 系列将首发搭载采用“逻辑折叠”技术（基于“韬定律”）的全新麒麟芯片。这一本土半导体领域的史诗级突破，为国内芯片设计企业规避先进制程受限风险、突破技术瓶颈提供了一条全新的可行路径，使其晶体管等效密度一举比肩台积电第一代 3nm 工艺，并规划在 2031 年达到 1.4nm 制程同等水平。此举标志着中国芯片自研迈出关键一步，也将直接对海外硬件巨头在华市场地位发起有力挑战。

## 技术与人工智能
一篇 [关于小米新一代自研玄戒O3旗舰SoC的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574710.htm) 揭示了这款采用 3nm 工艺、拥有 240 亿晶体管的性能怪兽，其十核全大核的 CPU 架构在 GeekBench 6.5 多核测试中超越了苹果 A19 Pro。更为瞩目的是，该芯片 [通过与国内存储龙头长鑫存储（CXMT）合作，行业首发支持 LPDDR6 移动内存](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574748.htm)，打破了此前由海外三巨头垄断新一代低功耗内存首发的局面。与此同时，美国 AI 头部阵营面临市场重塑：[Anthropic 旗舰模型 Fable 5 因高昂定价遭遇冷遇](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574628.htm)，企业用户正转向性价比更高的 OpenAI GPT-5.6 或其自家的低成本模型。在这场大模型退烧潮中，开源模型协作平台 [Hugging Face 传出正评估买家兴趣，寻求至少 130 亿美元估值的收购意向](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574672.htm)。而在医学人工智能前沿，阿里巴巴 [达摩院在《自然·医学》发表关于肝癌诊断 AI 模型 DAMO LiON 的研究成果](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574706.htm)，该模型在真实医院临床试验中辅助医生抓出 15 例原本被漏诊的 1 厘米左右微小肿瘤，敏感性提升 11.5%。

## 消费与设备
根据 [关于苹果发布会本周官宣的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574636.htm)，苹果预计于 9 月 9 日集中亮相 iPhone 18 Pro 系列及旗下首款折叠屏 iPhone Ultra。彭博社知名记者 [麦克·古尔曼指出](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574634.htm)，中国市场占据了全球折叠屏出货量的半壁江山，赢得追求眼球设计与身份象征的中国高端用户对苹果至关重要，哪怕 [整机物料成本上涨正推高 iPhone 18 Pro 系列的起售价约 100 美元](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574630.htm)。相比之下，明星清洁电器独角兽追觅科技的“无边界”扩张却迎来了戏剧性收缩：[一份对追觅手机和造车业务的深度调查](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574736.htm) 证实，其曾经接近千人的造车团队已裁撤至百人留守，价值数百万的展车在地下车库蒙灰，手机自研团队也已全员离职。同时，三星正在紧锣密鼓地准备其 [Galaxy S27 Ultra 旗舰，其横向大矩阵镜组撞脸 iPhone 17 Pro Max](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574640.htm)，并且搭载的 Exynos 2700 2nm 芯片 [在内部能效测试中已跑赢骁龙 8 至尊版 Gen 6 Pro](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574756.htm)。此外，新能源车企理想汽车也首次亮出了 [新一代纯电旗舰理想 MEGA 实车](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574738.htm)，其封闭式白色“大饼”轮圈极其吸睛，动力也升级至双电机综合功率 413 千瓦。

## 科学与航天
中国探月工程四期遭遇突发推迟。载人航天工程办公室证实，本着万无一失原则，[嫦娥七号南极环境与资源勘查任务不满足发射条件](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1574638.htm)，无法在今年预定窗口实施，这让刚刚于文昌完成器箭垂直转运的航天迷颇感意外。在生殖生物学领域，[日本山梨大学成功完成了世界首例“雄性克隆雌性”小鼠实验](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1574742.htm)，科学家通过 CRISPR 技术精准剔除雄性小鼠细胞内的 Y 染色体，使其自然分化为具备生育能力的 XX 雌性克隆个体。此外，发表于植物分类学期刊《Taxon》的一项基因研究 [在马达加斯加确认了猴面包树新物种 Adansonia bozy](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1574654.htm)，该物种此前被归为 A. za 的一部分，这一独立的身份认定将全面改写当地的物种濒危评估和保护资金流向。
