# CNBeta — 2026-08-28

## Top Story
According to [a cnbeta report on China's upcoming road traffic safety legal revision](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575366.htm), China is drafting landmark regulations that hold automobile manufacturers and importers directly liable for traffic violations committed while an autonomous driving system is actively engaged. By shifting the legal burden of proof onto automakers—who possess complete vehicle sensor and telematics data—the law aims to curb deceptive marketing that misleads drivers into treating L2 driver assistance as hands-off autonomy. This regulatory shift establishes an aggressive global benchmark for product liability as automakers worldwide race to deploy higher levels of automated driving.

## Consumer & Devices
Runaway component inflation is fundamentally reshaping the consumer hardware market.
