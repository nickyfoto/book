# CNBeta — 2026-07-19

## Top Story
The Chinese robotics industry is experiencing an unprecedented boom, with billions in funding pouring into startups led by top-tier veterans from Huawei, DJI, and Li Auto. According to a [cnbeta report on China's humanoid robot surge](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569432.htm), the country is drastically compressing the hardware development cycle—achieving in three years what took the EV industry a decade—and driving down production costs to a fraction of US counterparts by leveraging its highly integrated local supply chain. 

## Tech & AI
Leaks suggest the [DeepSeek V4 "full blood" edition](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569446.htm) is launching imminently with dynamic pricing strategies like peak/valley billing, maintaining its "price butcher" status while offering Opus-level reasoning performance. Meanwhile, a [cnbeta report on Alibaba's AI ambitions](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569458.htm) reveals the company has open-sourced the SAIL software stack for its proprietary "Zhenwu" AI chips to attract developers away from Nvidia's dominant CUDA ecosystem. The domestic AI market is so heated that Moonshot AI had to [pause new subscriptions for its Kimi K3 model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569498.htm) just three days after release, as skyrocketing user requests pushed compute clusters to their absolute limits. In the semiconductor space, a [report on CXMT's DDR6 pivot](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569492.htm) shows the Chinese memory maker is aggressively upgrading its manufacturing processes with packaging substrate maker Haesung DS to get a head start on next-generation memory architecture. This comes as [China takes three spots in the global top 10 foundries](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569426.htm), with SMIC, Hua Hong, and Nexchip dominating mature process nodes while TSMC focuses on advanced AI chips.

## Consumer & Devices
Samsung is betting heavily on a new form factor with the upcoming Galaxy Z Fold 8, which a [cnbeta leak on Samsung's foldable production](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569442.htm) claims will be the first Android "wide-fold" device to compete directly with Huawei's Pura X Max. Production plans target a massive 2.8 million units, positioning it as the main driver for the Z series. In the PC world, [Apple abandoned plans for an M2 Extreme Mac Pro](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569474.htm), scrapping a dual-model strategy that would have retained Intel processors for specific high-end peripheral compatibility. For EVs, following widespread user complaints of battery bulging and ruptures, a [report on GAC Aion's battery issues](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569466.htm) notes the automaker has extended battery warranties to 8 years or 300,000 kilometers specifically for its S series models, which are highly popular in the ride-hailing sector. Additionally, Samsung has officially [responded to the Galaxy S26 Ultra red screen issue](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569436.htm), confirming it is a software-related color balance bug triggered by strong ambient light and promising an upcoming patch.

## Gaming
Tensions are reportedly high within Xbox leadership regarding Game Pass, with a [cnbeta report on Xbox executive discontent](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1569470.htm) detailing internal frustrations that day-one releases cannibalize direct sales and devalue premium titles. Elsewhere, [Obsidian canceled a secret new IP](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1569452.htm) internally codenamed "Michigan," shifting resources to develop a brand new *Fallout* game instead. In digital storefront news, Sony is notifying users in multiple regions that the [PlayStation Store will transition to local currency billing](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1569450.htm), finally paving the way for regional pricing standardizations instead of relying on heavily fluctuating USD conversions.

## Science & Space
Following a delayed Starship launch, a [report on SpaceX's market valuation](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569462.htm) highlights that the company's implied market cap has plunged by an astounding $1 trillion from its mid-June peak, triggering massive paper profits for short sellers. On the bioengineering front, [Harvard scientists turned a silicon chip into a DNA factory](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1569484.htm), using precise electrical currents to facilitate enzyme-based DNA synthesis without relying on hazardous organic solvents. Meanwhile, an analysis of a deep-sea worm's jaws revealed a unique combination of proteins and high concentrations of metal ions, leading researchers to [propose a new material classification called "biological metal"](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1569486.htm).
