# CNBeta — 2026-05-13

## Top Story
**[Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang joins Trump on Air Force One for China visit](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562294.htm)** signals a potential breakthrough in the stalled export of high-end H200 AI chips to Chinese clients. According to **[a related cnbeta report](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562328.htm)**, the sudden addition of Huang to the presidential delegation has sparked optimism among major Chinese cloud computing and server companies who have been waiting for deliveries. This high-stakes diplomatic and commercial maneuver could reshape the global AI hardware supply chain and test the boundaries of US-China tech cooperation.

## Tech & AI
A **[cnbeta report on Anthropic's new funding](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562276.htm)** reveals that the AI startup is negotiating to raise $30 billion at a staggering $900 billion pre-money valuation to cover escalating computing infrastructure costs. Meanwhile, in the ongoing OpenAI trial, **[Sam Altman testified that Elon Musk wanted complete control](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562298.htm)** of the company and even suggested passing it to his children, claiming Musk only wants to work at companies he can completely dominate. In a related AI privacy development, **[Mark Zuckerberg announced an "Incognito Mode"](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562378.htm)** for Meta AI chats featuring end-to-end encryption, ensuring that neither Meta nor third parties can read the temporary conversations. On the hardware front, a **[report on skyrocketing memory prices](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562388.htm)** notes that NAND and DRAM contract prices have surged over sixfold and fourfold respectively since September 2025 due to massive demand from AI data centers. Furthermore, rumors suggest **[Apple may use Intel's 18A-P process](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562386.htm)** to manufacture its future M7 chips, leveraging Intel's advanced packaging technologies to balance performance and power consumption.

## Consumer & Devices
Google has effectively transformed its Android platform into a hardware framework for its AI ambitions, launching the **[Googlebook PC platform](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562292.htm)** aimed directly at Apple's MacBook Neo. These new premium laptops will run the ChromeOS-Android hybrid "Aluminum OS" and feature chips from Intel, Qualcomm, and MediaTek to power the new **[Gemini Intelligence agent features](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562318.htm)**. In the smartphone market, Sony unveiled the **[Xperia 1 VIII with a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562280.htm)**, dropping its signature continuous optical zoom for a fixed 48-megapixel 70mm telephoto lens to improve low-light performance. For content creators, Canon officially launched the **[EOS R6 V mirrorless camera](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562372.htm)**, which incorporates a built-in active cooling fan to support continuous 7K video recording. Meanwhile, the long-delayed **[Trump phone is finally shipping this week](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1562354.htm)**, arriving with a transparent plastic case and gold braided cable, though claims of 600,000 pre-orders remain highly exaggerated.

## Gaming
Microsoft appears to be rethinking its physical media strategy, with reports suggesting the **[next-generation Xbox "Project Helix" will be completely digital](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1562360.htm)**. However, a related initiative codenamed **[Project Positron](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1562352.htm)** might allow players to convert their physical game discs into digital licenses. In the VR space, Valve's upcoming **[Steam Frame headset appeared on Qualcomm's website](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1562322.htm)**, confirming it will be powered by the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chip and potentially outperforming the Meta Quest 3. Nintendo is bracing fans for a price hike by offering a **[Switch 2 "choose your game" bundle](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1562324.htm)**, allowing buyers to select a free digital title like *Mario Kart: World* before the console's base price increases to $499 in September.

## Science & Space
In a major breakthrough for deep space travel, **[NASA's experimental MPD ion engine passed a critical test](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1562288.htm)**, operating at 120 kilowatts—25 times more powerful than current electric thrusters—which could drastically shorten travel times for crewed Mars missions when paired with a nuclear reactor. At CERN, researchers successfully created the **[first antimatter qubit](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1562286.htm)** by manipulating single antiproton spins, opening a new era of precision measurements to test the fundamental symmetries of the universe. Astronomers utilizing the James Webb Space Telescope have mapped the **[most detailed cosmic web over 13.7 billion years](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1562348.htm)**, revealing how galaxies evolved within this massive framework of dark matter and gas. Back on Mars, NASA's **[Curiosity rover finally shook off a stubborn 13-kilogram rock](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1562350.htm)** that had been stuck to its drill mechanism for several days.
