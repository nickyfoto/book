# CNBeta — 2026-07-17

## Top Story
According to a [CNBeta report on Moonshot AI's new Kimi K3 model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569244.htm), the Chinese startup has sent shockwaves through the US artificial intelligence sector by offering top-tier performance at a fraction of the cost of premium American AI systems. This aggressive pricing strategy and formidable capability are prompting US industry leaders to question the sustainability of high-cost AI subscription models and raising alarms that China is rapidly closing the gap in frontier AI development.

## Tech & AI
A [recent investment disclosure by Kairun Shares](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569240.htm) revealed that Chinese AI foundational startup DeepSeek has reached a staggering post-money valuation of up to 351 billion RMB (approx. $52 billion) following its latest funding round. Conversely, on the public markets, [Zhipu AI saw its shares plummet by 28.49%](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569292.htm), wiping out 177.5 billion HKD in market value as its first batch of restricted shares unlocked and investors re-evaluated the company's profitability amidst fierce open-source competition.

In the West, a critical glitch in an [OpenAI Codex update unexpectedly deleted user $HOME directories](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569252.htm) in macOS and Linux systems when its "full access mode" bypassed sandbox protections. Meanwhile, [Microsoft is developing an internal AI security tool code-named "Project Percept"](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569272.htm) that dynamically routes tasks between models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Microsoft to hunt for software vulnerabilities while significantly undercutting competitors' operational costs. Tensions between the two tech giants are also flaring, as [Apple issued legal warnings to former employees now at OpenAI](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569280.htm), demanding they preserve data amid a high-stakes lawsuit accusing them of stealing commercial hardware secrets.

## Consumer & Devices
In the electric vehicle sector, a [sweeping update to Chinese national standards](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569288.htm) will double the mandatory reliability test mileage for pure EV and hybrid vehicles to 30,000 kilometers, fully aligning EV testing thresholds with traditional combustion engine cars to weed out low-end manufacturing. Sales data for the first half of 2026 saw [SAIC overtake BYD in total auto sales](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569262.htm) within China's top ten automakers, though Chinese brands continue to globally dominate the top EV sales charts. As domestic competition intensifies, [Xpeng is actively evaluating potential EV manufacturing sites in Germany](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569260.htm) to bypass European tariffs and expand its international footprint.

In smartphones, despite aggressive marketing from Android brands, an impressive [87% of US iPhone buyers this past quarter upgraded from an older Apple device](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569312.htm)—a three-year high that underscores Apple's unyielding ecosystem stickiness. Contrastingly, [Samsung's mobile division might face its first-ever quarterly operating loss](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569284.htm) due to skyrocketing memory chip prices eroding the profit margins of devices like the Galaxy S26.

In aerospace, after securing over $85 billion in the largest IPO in history, [SpaceX's Starship V3 second launch attempt was abruptly aborted](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569224.htm) right after engine ignition due to Raptor engine failures. Meanwhile, researchers using China's FAST telescope have discovered the [first critical evidence that low-energy cosmic rays originate from star-forming regions](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1569290.htm) like the Orion Nebula.
