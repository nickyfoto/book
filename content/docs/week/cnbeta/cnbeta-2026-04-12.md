# CNBeta — 2026-04-12

## Top Story
According to a **[report on banned NVIDIA shipments](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557602.htm)**, a Chinese firm has been importing an estimated 630 million RMB worth of embargoed NVIDIA H100 and H200 AI GPUs. The hardware was found in Supermicro and Dell servers, highlighting ongoing loopholes in U.S. export controls despite strict regulations and recent arrests tied to smuggling. This shadow market underscores the immense desperation for cutting-edge computing power in China's AI ecosystem, with NVIDIA noting that several smuggling attempts have already led to prosecutions.

## Tech & AI
A **[report on Samsung's 2nm process](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557606.htm)** reveals that low yields (initially around 20% last year) may force Qualcomm to shift all its upcoming Snapdragon 8 flagship orders exclusively to TSMC. Although Samsung has allegedly improved its yield to 60%, it still falls short of Qualcomm's 70% threshold. Meanwhile, NVIDIA's new RTX 50 series introduces Neural Texture Compression (NTC), which a **[recent test detailing RTX neural texture compression](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557620.htm)** shows can reduce VRAM usage by up to 85%. This AI-driven technology decodes textures in real-time using Tensor cores, making 8GB graphics cards viable for much longer without sacrificing visual quality. In the domestic AI race, the newly open-sourced Minimax 2.7 is showing impressive performance on par with Claude Opus, noted in a **[domestic AI roundup](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557612.htm)**. The Chinese developer community is now eagerly awaiting the rumored late-April release of DeepSeek V4, which will natively support domestic AI hardware systems. Further west, UK financial regulators are assessing the cybersecurity risks of Anthropic's unreleased Claude Mythos Preview model, as covered in a **[report on UK AI regulation](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557638.htm)**, after the model autonomously discovered thousands of software vulnerabilities.

## Consumer & Devices
A **[smartphone sales tracker](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557592.htm)** shows that the iPhone 17 series completely dominates the Chinese market with over 27.21 million units sold by the 14th week of 2026. For comparison, the top domestic premium phone, Huawei's Mate 80 series, sold 5.08 million units, while Xiaomi's 17 series reached 4.44 million. Extensive **[benchmarks of 2026 desktop CPUs](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557614.htm)** declare AMD's Ryzen 7 9850X3D the undisputed champion of gaming performance, while Intel's Core Ultra 7 270K Plus dominates single-threaded workloads. A supplementary **[AMD X3D processor review](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557656.htm)** highlights that the Zen 5-based 9850X3D outpaces older Zen 3 chips by roughly 64% in 1080p gaming. Elsewhere, the Red Magic 11 Pro series has been permanently delisted from 3DMark rankings following a **[benchmark cheating investigation](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557598.htm)**. Reviewers found the phone bypassed safety limits when detecting the public benchmark app, leading to dangerously high surface temperatures exceeding 50°C.

## Gaming
Nintendo is actively hunting down internal leakers using a "canary trap" strategy, according to **[reports on Nintendo's internal probe](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1557634.htm)**. The company is intentionally distributing slightly altered information to different departments to trace the source of recent Switch 2 and unannounced game leaks. In the PC space, the world's largest World of Warcraft classic private server, Turtle WoW, is officially shutting down after a **[federal court ruling](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1557654.htm)**. A California judge issued a permanent injunction following an eight-month legal battle initiated by Blizzard. Finally, following in the footsteps of its predecessor, a **[box office update](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/movie/1557636.htm)** reveals the *Super Mario Galaxy Movie* has rocketed past $600 million globally in just ten days.

## Science & Space
An American university research team has created a highly safe, fireproof battery design, detailed in a **[report on Kevlar zinc batteries](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557600.htm)**. Using Kevlar nanofiber hydrogels to suppress dendrite growth, the zinc-ion batteries achieved 900 test cycles without degradation, presenting a cheap and safe alternative for energy storage. Meanwhile, scientists have genetically engineered a strain of algae that secretes limonene to capture microplastics from water, according to a **[study on microplastic removal](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1557630.htm)**. The algae binds with the hydrophobic plastic particles to form easily removable clumps. Up above, China successfully launched a satellite internet test satellite aboard a Jielong-3 rocket, noted in a **[launch summary](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1557608.htm)**. This mission will test direct-to-cell satellite communication and inter-satellite networking to pave the way for China's upcoming "Space Net" megaconstellation.

# CNBeta — 2026-04-12 (中文版)

## 焦点新闻 / Top Story
根据一份**[关于NVIDIA被禁芯片走私的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557602.htm)**，一家中国企业进口了价值约6.3亿元人民币的NVIDIA H100和H200等受管制的AI GPU。这些硬件被发现搭载于超微和戴尔的服务器中，突显出尽管有严格的出口管制和近期与走私相关的逮捕行动，美国的禁令依然存在漏洞。这一灰色市场反映了中国AI生态系统对尖端算力的极度渴望，NVIDIA也指出目前已有多起走私未遂人员被起诉。

## 科技与人工智能 / Tech & AI
一份**[关于三星2nm工艺的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557606.htm)**指出，由于良率偏低（去年下半年仅为20%），高通可能会将下一代骁龙8旗舰处理器的订单全部转交给台积电。尽管据称三星已将良率提升至60%，但仍未能达到高通70%的标准要求。与此同时，NVIDIA的全新RTX 50系列引入了神经纹理压缩技术（NTC），一项**[详细测试RTX神经纹理压缩的报告](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557620.htm)**显示，该技术最高可将显存需求降低85%。这项由AI驱动的技术利用Tensor核心实时解码纹理，使8GB显卡能在不损失画质的情况下显著延长使用寿命。在国内AI竞赛中，新开源的Minimax 2.7表现出色，性能几乎接近Claude Opus，这在一篇**[国产AI盘点](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557612.htm)**中被重点提及。中国开发者社区正翘首以待传闻于4月下旬发布的DeepSeek V4，该模型将原生支持国产AI硬件系统。而在西方，英国金融监管机构正在评估Anthropic未发布的Claude Mythos Preview模型带来的网络安全风险，一份**[关于英国AI监管的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557638.htm)**提到，该模型此前自主发现了数千个软件漏洞。

## 消费与设备 / Consumer & Devices
一份**[智能手机销量追踪报告](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557592.htm)**显示，iPhone 17系列在中国市场占据绝对主导地位，截至2026年第14周累计销量突破2721万台。相比之下，国产高端旗舰销量冠军华为Mate 80系列售出508万台，小米17系列售出444万台。全面的**[2026桌面CPU跑分天梯榜](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557614.htm)**宣告AMD锐龙7 9850X3D成为无可争议的游戏性能王者，而Intel的酷睿Ultra 7 270K Plus则在单线程性能上登顶。一篇补充的**[AMD X3D处理器评测](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557656.htm)**强调，基于Zen 5架构的9850X3D在1080p游戏中的平均帧率比旧款Zen 3芯片高出约64%。另一方面，由于**[跑分作弊调查](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557598.htm)**，红魔11 Pro系列已被3DMark永久移出排名榜单。测试人员发现，该手机在检测到公开版跑分软件时会无视系统安全限制，导致表面温度飙升至危险的50℃以上。

## 科学与太空 / Science & Space
美国一所大学的研究团队发明了一种高度安全的防火电池，这在一篇**[关于凯夫拉锌电池的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1557600.htm)**中有详细说明。通过使用凯夫拉纳米纤维水凝胶抑制枝晶生长，这种锌离子电池实现了900次循环而无性能损失，为储能领域提供了一种廉价且安全的替代方案。同时，科学家通过基因改造使一种藻类能够合成柠檬烯，从而去除水中的微塑料，相关内容见于一项**[去除微塑料的研究](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1557630.htm)**。这种藻类能与疏水性的塑料颗粒结合，形成易于分离的絮状团块。在航天领域，中国使用捷龙三号火箭成功发射了一颗卫星互联网技术试验卫星，详情见**[发射摘要](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1557608.htm)**。此次任务将测试手机直连卫星和星际组网等关键技术，为中国即将建设的“太空天网”巨型星座铺路。
