# CNBeta — August 4, 2026

## Top Story
According to [a cnbeta report on DeepSeek's V4 Flash update](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571566.htm), China's rising AI star DeepSeek has supercharged its lightweight "Flash" model, enabling it to outscore its own "Pro" flagship on several benchmarks and stand on par with global tier-one models. This astonishing performance leap was achieved via advanced post-training optimization and the rollout of "DeepSeek Harness," a powerful, unreleased agent tool that equips the model with search capabilities, code execution, and context management. By narrowing the capability gap with frontier systems while maintaining its ultralow price point, DeepSeek's latest release has once again redefined the competitive "killing zone" for the entire global AI industry.

## Tech & AI
The global AI landscape is experiencing intense debates over open-source supremacy, corporate overreach, and legal warfare. In a notable [interview with Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571572.htm), the open-source pioneer argued that China is winning the AI race through its collaborative and open ecosystem, projecting that Chinese AI tools will catch up to US frontier labs by late 2026 or 2027. From a corporate standpoint, [Palantir CEO Alex Karp offered a "Marxist" critique](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571554.htm) of developers of closed LLMs, claiming they are trying to "seize the production materials" of their partners by using their proprietary industry experience to build monopolies. This friction is also manifesting in intense litigation: [Apple filed a motion for a preliminary injunction against OpenAI](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571638.htm) and two former Apple engineering and design leaders, Chang Liu and Tang Yew Tan, to prevent them from sharing trade secrets to aid OpenAI's consumer hardware ambitions. In response, [OpenAI published internal chat logs](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571630.htm) to expose holes in Apple's claims, demonstrating that any residual system access was caused by Apple's own administrative IT errors rather than corporate espionage. On the hardware architecture front, [SK Hynix and SanDisk co-published an OCP standard for High Bandwidth Flash (HBF)](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571590.htm), establishing a new 3D NAND-based storage tier between HBM and traditional SSDs that can deliver up to 3TB/s bandwidth to optimize large-scale AI servers.

## Consumer & Devices
Chinese manufacturers are aggressively advancing across smart mobility and semiconductor supply chains. Xiaomi has made a strategic shift into family-centric transportation, unveiling its [Kunlun technical architecture and two Pengcheng series EREV SUVs](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571588.htm) that bring massive, highly adjustable cabin spaces to Chinese households at a highly competitive price point. In memory tech, China's semiconductor giant [CXMT has entered LPDDR6 risk production](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571670.htm) with a new mobile memory chip achieving speeds up to 12.8 Gbps. This milestone comes as [major PC brands HP, ASUS, and Acer begin using CXMT DRAM](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571626.htm) in select entry-level laptops, though they are taking a cautious approach to avoid alienating dominant suppliers like Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron. Meanwhile, China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology announced the [first mandatory safety standard for L3 and L4 autonomous driving](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571650.htm), enforcing system safety and manufacturer liabilities beginning July 1, 2027. This regulatory push occurs as Tesla's deployment of [FSD v14 Lite on legacy HW3 computers](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571576.htm) triggers widespread complaints, with owners reporting system crashes and ECU thermal warnings exceeding 90°C.

## Gaming
Rising component costs are starting to squeeze hardware platforms and game developers alike. Internal leaks concerning Valve's next-generation [Steam Frame VR headset indicate a potential $1,500 price tag](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1571586.htm)—far exceeding original estimates due to Qualcomm SoC price hikes and global memory shortages. This hardware constraint aligns with a biting critique from [Hardware Unboxed warning that 8GB VRAM graphics cards are bottlenecking PC gaming](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1571592.htm), as developers are forced to drastically degrade low-preset texture details to maintain compatibility. For a nostalgic chuckle, retro gaming archives revealed that the [original 2004 GameCube launch of Animal Crossing](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1571608.htm) caused a massive panic, with furious parents calling Nintendo's hotlines because they mistook the motion of the clay Haniwa (burial clay) ornaments for something adult-oriented.

## Science & Space
Humanity's exploration of the universe continues to yield fascinating discoveries, while terrestrial research challenges old health dogmas. The European Space Agency's [Euclid Telescope has identified 31 ancient quasars](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1571546.htm) from the universe's reionization era, more than doubling known quasars at redshifts of 7 or higher in its first year of wide-field surveys. Closer to home, a peer-reviewed study analyzing a [meteorite that crashed into a New Jersey home](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1571548.htm) confirmed the presence of liquid salt and amino acids, illustrating that prebiotic chemistry can develop in CM carbonaceous chondrites. In dietary science, a randomized controlled trial from the [University of Toronto disputed low-fat dairy recommendations](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1571550.htm), revealing that whole milk and full-fat dairy consumption did not elevate weight or lipids, which supports the "Dairy Matrix Hypothesis" that whole-food structures mediate absorption. Lastly, NASA's plan to deploy Tesla's [unmodified Cybertruck as the launch escape vehicle for Crew-13](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1571662.htm) has raised eyebrows, given the electric pickup's 11 NHTSA safety recalls compared to the heavily armored military MRAPs historically used.

# CNBeta — 2026年8月4日

## 头条新闻
根据[CNBeta关于DeepSeek V4 Flash更新的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571566.htm)，中国人工智能新星DeepSeek对其轻量级“Flash”模型进行了史诗级升级，使其跑分甚至超越了自家的“Pro”旗舰模型，并以极低的成本直逼全球第一梯队模型。这一惊人的性能飞跃主要得益于极高质量的后训练（post-training）优化，以及首次引入的未发布智能体工具“DeepSeek Harness”，该工具为模型配备了搜索引擎、代码运行环境并能高效管理项目上下文。在保持极低价格的同时，DeepSeek成功拉近了与前沿大模型的差距，再次重新定义了全球AI行业的“斩杀线”。

## 技术与人工智能
全球AI硬件与人才版图正处于剧烈动荡之中。在接受[Hugging Face首席执行官克莱芒·德朗格的专访](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571572.htm)时，这位开源倡导者指出，中国正通过其开放、协作的开源生态系统赢得AI竞赛，并预测中国AI工具将在2026年底或2027年追平美国前沿实验室的水平。而在企业端，[Palantir首席执行官亚历克斯·卡普则对大模型开发商提出了“马克思主义式”的犀利批评](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571554.htm)，警告称许多构建闭源LLM的实验室正试图通过榨取企业合作伙伴的行业经验和知识产权来“夺取生产资料”，建立竞争壁垒。这种日益紧张的关系在司法层面也得到了体现：[苹果公司正式向法庭提起初步禁令申请](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571638.htm)，要求禁止OpenAI及两名前苹果核心技术骨干（Chang Liu与Tang Yew Tan）访问或使用苹果的商业秘密，以此遏制ChatGPT母公司进军消费硬件领域的步伐。针对此项诉讼，[OpenAI迅速公开了双方的邮件与iMessage聊天记录进行反击](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571630.htm)，直指苹果“打错算盘”，并证明所谓的“残留访问权限”实际上反映了苹果自身离职管理系统的IT漏洞，而非员工恶意窃密。在存储硬件架构方面，[SK海力士与闪迪正式联合发布了HBF（高带宽闪存）首个标准规范](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571590.htm)，该标准由开放数据中心组织OCP发布，通过3D NAND芯片堆叠构建了介于HBM和传统SSD之间的中间层方案，可实现高达3TB/s的带宽，从而大幅降低AI推理和数据处理的服务器成本。

## 消费与电子设备
中国智能硬件与新能源车企正在加速技术演进并重塑市场格局。小米在家庭智能出行领域错峰出牌，正式发布了[“昆仑技术架构”以及旗下“澎程”系列的两款增程SUV](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571588.htm)，这标志着小米从追求极致性能的纯电轿跑SU7转向主打“智能可变大空间”的家庭SUV，旨在凭借极致性价比和米家互联生态捕获更广泛的家庭用户群。在半导体领域，中国存储巨头[长鑫存储正式进入LPDDR6风险量产阶段](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571670.htm)，其推出新一代移动内存芯片最高运行速度达12.8 Gbps。与此同时，[惠普、华硕、宏碁等PC大厂已开始在部分入门级笔记本中克制地采用长鑫内存芯片](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571626.htm)，以保障在当前全球供应极度紧张的卖方市场中的供应链韧性。在行业监管方面，工信部正式发布了[中国首部L3/L4自动驾驶系统的强制性国家标准](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571650.htm)，该标准建立了全生命周期安全保障和人机交互规范，拟于2027年7月1日起实施。然而，智能驾驶的硬件妥协也带来痛点：特斯拉全面推送的[针对HW3老电脑的FSD v14 Lite精简版模型遭到车主集中吐槽](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571576.htm)，由于HW3仅有8GB内存和八分之一的带宽，高负载运行导致主板温度飙升至90℃以上，频繁触发ECU过热警告并罢工。

## 游戏
硬件供应链的通胀和技术门槛正在直接制约游戏行业。根据行业爆料，由于高通骁龙8 Gen 3芯片涨价以及AI数据中心引发的全球存储短缺，[Valve的下一代VR头显Steam Frame基础配置预计售价将飙升至1500美元](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1571586.htm)。硬件限制同样引发了游戏画质退步，[知名技术频道Hardware Unboxed指出，8GB显存的广泛普及正在持续拖累整个PC游戏业的技术进步](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1571592.htm)，由于大批玩家仍停留在8GB门槛，开发商不得不通过粗暴削减低画质预设下的纹理细节来妥协兼容性。对于复古游戏玩家来说，一则趣闻显示[初代《动物森友会》在2004年发售时曾引发家长投诉潮](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1571608.htm)，许多美国家长因文化差异将游戏中笑眯眯的传统日式“埴轮”土偶误认作不雅的成人向物品，导致任天堂客服热线一度被愤怒的家长打爆。

## 科学与太空
在浩瀚的宇宙探索与微观的生命科学领域，今日均迎来了重大突破。欧空局的[欧几里得空间望远镜在其首年观测中一举发掘出31座红移大于7的远古类星体](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1571546.htm)，使这一极早期超大质量黑洞的已知数量翻了一倍以上，为揭开黑洞与早期星系协同演化提供了宝贵的“时间胶囊”。在太阳系起源研究方面，权威报告证实[此前坠落于新泽西居民楼的陨石内部保存了完整的咸水液体与氨基酸痕迹](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1571548.htm)，这颗极罕见的CM1/2碳质球粒陨石有力印证了原始小行星在地球生命诞生前夕输送生命前体物质的关键作用。在营养学领域，多伦多大学的一项对照实验[对传统的“低脂乳制品更健康”建议提出质疑](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1571550.htm)，证实摄入全脂乳制品并不会增加体重或体脂率，并提出了“乳制品基质假说”，强调应综合考量天然整体食物中各营养成分的协同效应。此外，NASA宣布计划采用曾遭遇11次安全召回的特斯拉[Cybertruck纯电皮卡作为Crew-13任务的发射台逃生与紧急撤离车辆](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1571662.htm)，该举措因Cybertruck缺乏军用重型防地雷反伏击车（MRAP）的防护力，且存在油门踏板卡死等潜在故障风险而引发外界对宇航员安全保障的担忧。
