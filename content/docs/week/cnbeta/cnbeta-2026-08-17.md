# CNBeta — August 17, 2026

## Top Story
According to **[a cnbeta report on Unitree's STAR Market listing](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573628.htm)**, Chinese robotics leader Unitree Technology is set to officially go public on Shanghai's Sci-Tech Innovation Board on August 19, 2026, launching with a staggering initial market valuation of approximately 60.99 billion RMB (~\$8.4 billion). This landmark listing coincides with the company's debut of **[Unitree's groundbreaking 'Superman' robot](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573566.htm)**, which breaks human running and jumping barriers by leaping 2 meters high and reaching a peak speed of 12.66 m/s. Unitree's dual milestones of a public listing and a record-shattering athletic breakthrough demonstrate that China's humanoid robotics sector is rapidly transitioning from capital-intensive laboratory research into an aggressive, commercialized, and globally competitive phase.

## Tech & AI
In cutting-edge model deployments, **[DeepSeek's new peak-and-valley pricing](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573514.htm)** went into effect, marking a rare shift to computational congestion pricing where API rates double during peak daytime hours to optimize server traffic. Simultaneously, **[Alibaba's launch of the 'Happy Shrimp' AI music model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/music/1573588.htm)** brings end-to-end, natural language music generation to both domestic and international markets, addressing long-standing track misalignment and vocal noise issues. Extending smart capabilities to desktop workflows, **[ByteDance's Doubao rolling out remote PC control](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573584.htm)** allows mobile users to direct files and operate computer applications seamlessly from their smartphones.

In enterprise hardware, **[SINKER introducing the first domestic 48GB DDR5 server memory](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573506.htm)** utilizing advanced ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT) DRAM dies marks a significant step for China's localized semiconductor supply chain, providing high-bandwidth performance for AI training and memory databases. This localized alternative is extremely critical given a severe **[German DDR5 memory prices surging fivefold](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573498.htm)** compared to July 2025, driven by global AI demand which is pushing low-budget PC building into crisis.

Globally, AI power structures continue to clash; **[Anthropic's CEO defending safety regulations](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573556.htm)** ahead of their massive \$2 trillion IPO argues that smart, tiered rules protect open-weight and smaller developers by placing stricter burdens on frontier giants. At the same time, **[OpenAI's president addressing executive departures](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573626.htm)** dismissed concerns over high-level turnover as standard lifecycle phases, following the abrupt departure of key corporate heads. Highlighting consumer pushback against forced AI labeling, **[an open-source tool cracking Claude's SynthID-Text watermarks](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573528.htm)** went viral on GitHub, gaining 11k stars by removing invisible Unicode characters and altering token sampling statistical patterns to sanitize output text.

## Consumer & Devices
Supply chain costs are rapidly translating to consumers as **[Apple's impending global price hikes for the iPhone 17 series](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573536.htm)** threaten to raise global prices by up to 1,000 RMB (~\$140) by the end of August, duplicating a memory-inflation-driven price increase implemented in Japan in July. This price strain is expected to worsen as **[leaked details of Apple's high-cost A20 processor](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573642.htm)** indicate a switch to TSMC's advanced 2nm GAA process, driving up next-generation iPhone 18 Pro retail prices by \$250 to \$300 despite massive efficiency gains. Undeterred, **[Foxconn's massive sign-on bonuses for iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Fold production](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573606.htm)** have reached up to 8,800 RMB (\$1,230) in Zhengzhou, as the manufacturer aggressively builds up manpower for Apple's upcoming flagship line and its highly anticipated first foldable device.

In the smart vehicle market, **[the 2027 BMW X3 integrating cloud-based LLM assistants](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573568.htm)** will make its national debut at the Chengdu Motor Show, bringing advanced voice intelligence powered by Cloud LLMs directly to drivers via an OTA update. For computer buyers, **[a leak of Lenovo's MacBook Neo-targeting IdeaPad Vibe specs](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573652.htm)** exposes highly customizable configurations, leveraging AMD's Ryzen AI 300/400 processors with up to 50 TOPS NPU, though sacrificing some baseline memory to keep entry-level pricing highly competitive.

Meanwhile, **[BlackBerry's successful automotive-driven business turnaround](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573526.htm)** marks a historic milestone as the legendary mobile giant turns cash-flow positive after fifteen years, successfully pivoting to QNX vehicle operating systems and Secusmart enterprise encryption. Additionally, **[the upcoming HMD 2760 Flip adopting Android Go](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1573580.htm)** signals a departure from KaiOS, offering users a retro-style 4G flip phone equipped with Android 13 Go to access the full Google Play Store.

Meanwhile, **[French physicist Étienne Klein being stripped of his PhD](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1573530.htm)** has rocked European academia, after a meticulous investigation revealed that nearly two-thirds of his 1999 doctoral dissertation was systematically plagiarized from other authors, including Albert Camus.
