# CNBeta — 2026-05-04

## Top Story
In a move demonstrating massive supply chain leverage, **[Apple is reportedly hoarding the global supply of LPDDR5 memory](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561188.htm)**, locking in long-term contracts to secure capacity and stabilize the pricing of its upcoming iPhone 18 Pro models. Analysts suggest that by keeping the base iPhone 18 Pro at 8999 RMB while memory costs surge globally, Apple is forcing Chinese Android manufacturers into a corner; many are considering abandoning their "Ultra" flagship models entirely rather than operating at a loss or raising prices.

## Tech & AI
The global AI landscape is seeing rapid shifts as a new **[DeepSeek-TUI tool, dubbed the "DeepSeek version of Claude Code",](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561238.htm)** goes viral on GitHub, offering a terminal-based programming agent optimized for DeepSeek's V4 model. Meanwhile, a security engineer exposed critical vulnerabilities in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems by **[spending just $12 to pollute AI information sources](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1561180.htm)**, successfully tricking major chatbots into declaring him the 2025 world champion of a board game through a fake website. In China's entertainment sector, an **[AI micro-drama boom is severely impacting traditional production chains](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/movie/1561200.htm)**, with AI-generated videos costing around $30 per minute and drastically reducing opportunities for human actors. On the domestic hardware front, the highly anticipated **[Huawei Kirin 9050 chip](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561192.htm)** is rumored to debut in the upcoming Mate 90 series with an 8-core design pushing CPU clock speeds past 3GHz, marking a new milestone for Chinese semiconductor self-sufficiency. Finally, ByteDance's popular AI assistant **[Doubao is testing paid subscription tiers](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561222.htm)** priced up to 500 RMB per month for advanced productivity tasks like video generation and data analysis, though daily usage will remain free.

## Consumer & Devices

Apple's upcoming **[iPhone 18 Pro series will feature a significantly smaller Dynamic Island](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561248.htm)**, reducing the screen cutout width by roughly 35% for improved media viewing and gaming experiences. In software updates, **[Microsoft is deprecating the Edge browser's sidebar app list](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561218.htm)** to simplify the interface and push its Copilot integration. The tech giant also hastily **[removed a support document suggesting 32GB RAM is the new baseline for gaming](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1561226.htm)** following community backlash over Windows 11's notoriously high memory consumption.
