# CNBeta — 2026-07-20

## Top Story
The global AI race is reaching a new inflection point as Chinese tech giants aggressively push open-source frontier models. A [report on Moonshot's Kimi K3 and Alibaba's Qwen3.8](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569588.htm) details how these models are outperforming many American counterparts and committing to open-source development. This strategy has triggered a fierce debate in Silicon Valley, with an [OpenAI executive suggesting](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569622.htm) the US might need to weaponize regulatory uncertainty to counter China's "AI communism," a remark that drew sharp criticism from proponents of open competition.

## Tech & AI
SpaceX has reportedly placed a [massive $52 billion order with Foxconn](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569502.htm) for 13,000 Nvidia GB300 AI server racks, which will dramatically expand its Colossus datacenters' training capabilities. 

Moving away from total reliance on Nvidia, a [leak of GitHub code suggests Anthropic is testing AMD GPUs](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569560.htm) to systematically diversify its computing infrastructure. 

Chinese AI unicorn DeepSeek is seeing its true valuation surface, with [recent indirect investments placing its worth around 330 to 350 billion RMB](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569556.htm) as it prepares for further funding rounds. 

Nvidia is deepening its global footprint by [partnering with Japanese giants like SoftBank to build a "Sovereign AI" infrastructure](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569570.htm) and an industrial robotics network.

## Consumer & Devices
The [Chinese smartphone market saw Huawei and Apple surge in Q2 2026](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569600.htm), with Huawei claiming a 23% market share and Apple climbing to 18%, defying an overall 2% market decline. 

These consumer device makers are facing severe headwinds, however, as an [AI-driven memory chip shortage causes prices to skyrocket](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569514.htm), forcing PC and smartphone brands to raise retail prices and delay product lines. 

In Apple news, code in the latest iOS 27 developer beta [references multiple battery health states](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569624.htm), fueling speculation about a dual-battery design for an upcoming foldable iPhone.

## Gaming
High memory costs are hitting gamers hard, with Nvidia reportedly [suspending its GeForce RTX 50 SUPER series indefinitely](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569516.htm) due to the exorbitant cost of 3GB GDDR7 memory modules. 

Sony has filed a [patent for a modular controller featuring a touchscreen](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1569542.htm), suggesting the PlayStation 6 could allow players to hot-swap joysticks and interact with mobile notifications directly from their gamepad. 

The emulation scene is advancing rapidly, as a [PS5 emulator called SharpEmu successfully booted Ghost of Yōtei on PC](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1569618.htm), marking a major milestone for early-stage PlayStation 5 development.

## Science & Space
A recent Chinese robotics combat event went viral after a [humanoid robot continued to fight despite losing its head](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1569562.htm), prompting Elon Musk to remark on the entertainment value of robot brawls. 

Japanese researchers have developed the [EleTac bionic soft gripper with built-in cameras](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1569628.htm), allowing robots to "see" and "feel" object deformations internally without relying on external sensors.
