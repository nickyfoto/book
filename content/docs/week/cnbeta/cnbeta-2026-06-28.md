# CNBeta — 2026-06-28

## Top Story
According to a [cnbeta report on Jefferies' forecast](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567086.htm), global memory prices are expected to surge by up to 50% in Q3 and another 40% in Q4, fundamentally reshaping the cost of consumer electronics. Driven by insatiable AI server demand, the shortage has forced giants like Apple and Microsoft to hike prices, but as [another cnbeta article notes](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567140.htm), it represents an "existential crisis" for smaller hardware startups whose 8GB DRAM costs skyrocketed from $35 to $300. In response, [Apple is reportedly lobbying the US government](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567158.htm) to allow DRAM supplies from China's CXMT to secure capacity. Apple is also [accelerating its transition to TSMC's 1.4nm process](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567134.htm) to avoid further AI-driven capacity squeezes on the 2nm node.

## Tech & AI
Asian startups are capitalizing on US export restrictions. As noted in a [cnbeta report on new security models](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567080.htm), China's 360 and Japan's Sakana AI have launched "Mythos-like" AI models, positioning them as sovereign alternatives to Anthropic's restricted tools. Concurrently, [Austria is urging the EU to bring Anthropic into Europe](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567142.htm) with capital support and market access to prevent the bloc from being cut off from frontier AI developments. Meanwhile, [Anthropic's latest user data report](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567106.htm) reveals intimate details about global workers, showing how high-income professionals consume significantly more tokens while engaging in complex tasks. In enterprise AI, [Ford is rehiring veteran engineers](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567152.htm) after its automated AI quality control systems failed to catch production flaws, opting to pair human experts with AI tools. Furthermore, [Microsoft has officially launched Azure Linux 4.0](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567160.htm) as a free, open-source distribution tailored specifically for cloud workloads and Azure infrastructure.

## Consumer & Devices
A [CCTV investigation into tech review "hidden rules"](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567120.htm) exposed how smartphone manufacturers cheat benchmarks using special reviewer-only firmware and cloud-controlled performance boosts. In PC hardware, [NVIDIA's 16-pin connector melting issue has resurfaced on the RTX 5090](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567088.htm), with severe burn-outs reported despite newer ATX 3.1 safety standards. The memory crisis is also hurting consumers directly, as [UK Apple dealers are reportedly demanding extra payments](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567122.htm) from users who already fully paid for M5 Max MacBook Pros before the recent price hikes. On the software side, [WPS Office faced massive backlash](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567096.htm) for secretly hoarding gigabytes of cache data on the C-drive, prompting the company to promise customizable installation paths in July. Finally, [Android 17 will introduce a native split-screen game mode](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567072.htm) for foldable phones, turning the bottom half into a customizable virtual gamepad.

## Gaming
The console market is facing severe headwinds. According to a [cnbeta report on May console sales](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1567082.htm), PlayStation hit a 25-year low and Xbox had its worst month ever following recent price hikes, leaving the relatively affordable Nintendo Switch 2 as the sole winner. The situation may worsen in the next generation, as [the PS6's bill of materials is reportedly nearing $1,000](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1567102.htm), raising fears of unprecedented retail pricing. Digital ownership is also under fire, with [PlayStation permanently deleting over 500 purchased movies](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1567124.htm) from user accounts due to expired StudioCanal licenses. Additionally, [Xbox has reportedly frozen new Game Pass contracts](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1567138.htm), signaling the end of its aggressive "Day One" spending spree for third-party titles.
