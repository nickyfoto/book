# CNBeta — 2026-05-23

## Top Story
**[DeepSeek slashed its V4 Pro model API pricing to just 25% of its original cost](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563374.htm)**, turning a temporary promotional discount into the permanent standard rate. This aggressive move drops the price to 3 RMB per million input tokens for cache misses and 6 RMB for outputs, making it highly competitive for developers integrating AI into programming tools. This ensures developers can freely deploy the model in various applications without cost concerns, further shaking up the competitive landscape for frontier AI models.

## Tech & AI
In the semiconductor space, **[Wingtech Technology is suing Dutch chipmaker Nexperia for 8 billion RMB](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563400.htm)** (about $1.18 billion) over ongoing control disputes following Dutch export restrictions. At TSMC, record AI-driven profits are clashing with internal cost-cutting, as **[rumored bonus reductions have furious TSMC employees discussing "Samsung-style" strikes](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563438.htm)**, highlighting the growing tension in the global AI supply chain. Meanwhile, **[Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang issued a stern warning to Supermicro regarding export compliance](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563436.htm)**, demanding zero ambiguity in navigating US restrictions on AI servers bound for restricted markets. Nvidia also feels vindicated on its memory strategy; **[the company's CFO mocked rivals caught off guard by soaring HBM and DDR prices](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563440.htm)**, noting Nvidia locked in capacity with suppliers well ahead of the current AI-driven memory shortage. On the regulatory front, **[the US Trade Representative confirmed the Trump administration is still weighing semiconductor import tariffs](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563404.htm)** to incentivize domestic manufacturing.

## Consumer & Devices
Apple's aggressive pricing strategy is paying off in China, where **[the iPhone 17 series saw a massive sales surge following a 1000 RMB official price cut](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563412.htm)**, pushing total active units in the region past 30 million. Looking ahead, leaks suggest the **[iPhone 18 Pro will drop its popular 'Hermes Orange' color for Burgundy and Black](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563416.htm)**, while featuring **[the narrowest bezels in iPhone history and the 2nm A20 Pro chip](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563410.htm)**. Ahead of WWDC 2026, **[Apple has quietly set up a "genai.apple.com" subdomain](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563442.htm)**, signaling major Apple Intelligence integrations across its upcoming OS updates. Elsewhere, **[Google's newly teased AI glasses received a lukewarm reception](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563406.htm)**, with critics noting the Samsung-partnered hardware lacks a unique selling point or ecosystem synergy to genuinely challenge Meta's dominant Ray-Ban wearables.

## Gaming
Microsoft's racing flagship is accelerating rapidly, as **[Forza Horizon 6 approached 5 million global sales in its first week](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1563396.htm)**. The title is seeing an even split in revenue between Steam and Xbox ecosystems, with Chinese players making up a notable 13% of the Steam user base. In hardware news, **[an open-source HID Remapper update now allows the Steam Controller to function across platforms like the Switch and Xbox](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1563392.htm)**, though Valve is currently investigating a serious hardware issue where **[the new Steam Controller charger's exposed pins can cause dangerous electrical shorts](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1563420.htm)**.

## Science & Space
SpaceX successfully launched its **[first Starship V3 on Flight 12](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1563418.htm)**, completing crucial payload deployments and a suborbital trajectory before the booster was lost over the Gulf of Mexico. The extensively redesigned system is seen as a critical milestone for SpaceX's upcoming massive $75 billion IPO. China's space program also hit a milestone with the unveiling of the **[Shenzhou-23 crew, featuring China's first female astronaut from Hong Kong](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1563428.htm)**. They will bring nine key experiments to the Tiangong space station, including **[the first in-orbit dynamic testing of perovskite solar cells and consecutive "second generation" space rice cultivation](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1563402.htm)**.
