# CNBeta — 2026-06-29

## Top Story
According to a [cnbeta report on Ford's internal assessments](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567196.htm), Ford CEO Jim Farley admitted that the automaker's R&D system is 25 years behind China's electric vehicle industry. He acknowledged that Ford cannot beat BYD in EV cost due to BYD's aggressive vertical integration, noting that BYD's battery costs are 30% lower than what Ford pays CATL. To catch up, Farley stated Ford must completely re-architect its motors, gearboxes, and inverters to reduce total battery load by 30%. 

## Tech & AI
A [cnbeta report detailing DeepSeek's new DSpark architecture](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567238.htm) reveals how the company boosted inference speeds by up to 85% via speculative decoding, lowering token delivery costs rather than leaping in raw intelligence. Concurrently, the [DeepSeek V4 official mid-July launch](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567234.htm) will introduce a peak/valley pricing mechanism, doubling API costs during high-traffic hours.

[Meta is heavily restricting employee access to Claude and Codex](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567240.htm) due to fears of "model distillation," actively preventing third-party AI outputs from accidentally contaminating Meta's own internal training datasets.

China's domestic AI capabilities continue to rise globally, as [Zhipu's open-weight GLM-5.2 model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567164.htm) has reportedly matched Anthropic's advanced Mythos model in cybersecurity tasks and vulnerability detection. In the hardware space, [Baidu's AI chip division Kunlunxin](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567228.htm) is unusually requiring its IPO investors to purchase AI chips worth 3 to 7 times the value of their equity investment. 

Meanwhile, [Tencent has secured a massive $2.94 billion memory chip supply deal](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567202.htm) with domestic manufacturer Changxin Memory Technologies (CXMT) to secure DRAM for its servers and AI workloads.

## Consumer & Devices

Despite these premium additions, the [entry-level iPhone 18e will reportedly remain locked to a 60Hz display](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567222.htm), with Apple holding back ProMotion 120Hz screens from lower-tier models until at least 2028.

Tencent is currently beta testing a [standalone payment app named "TenPayGo"](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567204.htm) specifically aimed at foreigners visiting China, which streamlines mobile payments through international bank cards without requiring cash.

Electric vehicle "soft-close doors" are drawing heavy criticism following a [report documenting dozens of finger-crushing injuries](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567236.htm), exposing a critical lack of anti-pinch safety standards during the final locking phase of modern auto doors.

A prolonged heatwave across Europe has triggered a [surge in sales of Chinese cooling appliances on AliExpress](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1567216.htm), with portable air conditioners from domestic brands completely selling out across local European warehouses.
