# CNBeta — 2026-05-26

## Top Story
According to **[a cnbeta report on the Three-Body Universe](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563630.htm)**, former CEO Xu Yao has been executed for the 2020 poisoning of founder Lin Qi. The company released a statement declaring that "justice is served," marking the end of a dramatic corporate saga involving the globally recognized sci-fi IP. This concludes a shocking case where Xu tested poisons in a makeshift lab before successfully administering lethal doses disguised as probiotic pills.

## Tech & AI
The AI hardware boom continues to reshape global markets. A massive surge in TSMC's stock has propelled Taiwan to become the **[world's fifth-largest stock market](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563688.htm)**, driven by its 42% weighting on the local index. Similarly, memory maker **[Micron joined the $1 trillion market cap club](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563708.htm)** as its HBM supply for 2026 is fully sold out. Meanwhile, Nvidia is preparing for the next phase of agentic AI, having delivered its **[first Vera CPU to partners like OpenAI and Anthropic](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563662.htm)**. 

On the software and enterprise side, AI implementations are facing turbulent realities. **[Starbucks halted an AI inventory pilot](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563706.htm)** after the tool began "hallucinating" imaginary syrup bottles, opting to return to manual counting. Over at Meta, **[surviving employees report shattered morale](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563676.htm)** following recent layoffs, as remaining staff are forcefully reassigned to AI data labeling roles without promotion prospects. Looking ahead, OpenAI is preparing for future risks, opening a **[highly paid position for a security researcher](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563666.htm)** to study the consequences of AI models autonomously training better versions of themselves.

## Consumer & Devices
Ferrari's stock took a 6.3% dive following the unveiling of **[Luce, its first pure electric vehicle](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563696.htm)**, with investors and fans questioning if the $640k EV strays too far from the brand's core DNA. PC gamers are getting a cleaner software experience as Nvidia officially **[retires the 20-year-old GeForce Control Panel](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563714.htm)** in favor of its unified new NVIDIA App. On the CPU front, CPU-Z has quietly added support for the unannounced **[AMD Ryzen 7 7700X3D](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563640.htm)**, essentially confirming the upcoming budget-friendly gaming chip.

## Gaming
Digital Foundry successfully transformed a **[PS5 into a 5K PS3 emulator](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1563644.htm)** by running Linux, though CPU bottlenecks remain for heavy titles. In Japan, a cultural clash erupted over **[Forza Horizon 6](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1563642.htm)**, with players protesting the ability to destroy traditional rice fields in-game, despite shrines and cherry blossoms being indestructible. In a rapid correction, Activision apologized after its anti-cheat system falsely banned a **[disabled Call of Duty player](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1563694.htm)** who relies on a mouth-operated QuadStick controller.

## Science & Space
Japan successfully ground-tested a **[Mach 5 scramjet engine](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563698.htm)**, taking a crucial step toward 2-hour Tokyo-to-LA commercial flights envisioned for the 2040s. A new demographic study predicts a severe **[accelerating global population collapse](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1563650.htm)**, projecting human extinction by 2415 due to plummeting birth rates. In space operations, the Pentagon and SpaceX are clashing over pricing, as the military heavily utilizes **[Starlink on kamikaze drones in Iran](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563692.htm)**.

## 头条新闻

## 科技与人工智能
AI硬件热潮继续重塑全球市场。受台积电在本地指数中42%权重的推动，其股价暴涨使台湾跃升为 **[全球第五大股票市场](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563688.htm)**。同样，存储芯片制造商 **[美光加入了万亿美元市值俱乐部](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563708.htm)**，其2026年的HBM供应已全部售罄。同时，英伟达正在为下一阶段的代理式AI做准备，已向OpenAI和Anthropic等合作伙伴交付了其 **[首款Vera CPU](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563662.htm)**。

在软件和企业端，AI的落地正面临现实的动荡。**[星巴克叫停了一项AI库存试点](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563706.htm)**，因为该工具开始对不存在的糖浆瓶产生“幻觉”，公司决定回归人工盘点。在Meta，**[幸存的员工报告称士气崩溃](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563676.htm)**，近期的裁员后，留下的员工被强制重新分配到没有晋升前景的AI数据标注岗位。展望未来，OpenAI正在为未来的风险做准备，开放了一个 **[高薪安全研究员职位](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563666.htm)**，以研究AI模型自主训练出更好版本所带来的后果。
