# CNBeta — 2026-09-08

## Top Story
According to [a cnbeta report on Huawei's Mate XT2](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1576872.htm), Huawei launched its new trifold flagship featuring the Kirin 9050 Pro, the company's first core processor in six years with fully self-developed CPU, GPU, NPU, and modem architectures. Developed using a proprietary design methodology called "Tao Law" to circumvent US supply restrictions, the chip runs 30-billion parameter AI models on-device.
