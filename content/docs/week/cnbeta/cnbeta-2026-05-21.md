# CNBeta — 2026-05-21

## Top Story
**[Nvidia concedes the Chinese high-end AI chip market to Huawei](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563182.htm)** as U.S. export controls tighten, while simultaneously facing a **[new ban on its RTX 5090D v2 consumer GPUs](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563240.htm)** entering China. This marks a critical inflection point in the global semiconductor war, as Nvidia's absence accelerates China's push for a self-sufficient, localized AI ecosystem and independent computing infrastructure.

## Tech & AI
**[Tencent's new OS-level AI assistant "Marvis" has officially launched](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563204.htm)**, capable of deep system control and cross-device coordination across Windows, Mac, and Android. In the hardware race, **[Nvidia's CEO is betting heavily on "Agentic CPUs" with the new Vera chip](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563174.htm)**, projecting $20 billion in CPU sales this year and opening a potential $200 billion market for AI agent execution. Similarly, **[Intel is strictly enforcing an "A0 to production" mandate](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563196.htm)** to fix historic yield issues ahead of its 14A node launch in 2028. Meanwhile, **[Meta is laying off roughly 8,000 employees](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563246.htm)** to funnel resources into its AI ambitions. In the startup space, **[Anthropic expects its first profitable quarter](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563172.htm)** as it **[negotiates to adopt Microsoft's custom Maia chips](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563256.htm)** to reduce reliance on Nvidia. On the regulatory front, **[President Trump has delayed signing an AI executive order](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563266.htm)** over fears it could slow U.S. AI development and give an edge to China.

## Consumer & Devices
The **[OLED screens for Apple's upcoming MacBook Pro have hit mass production](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563220.htm)**, with Samsung Display reportedly achieving a 95% "golden yield" for the complex tandem two-stack panels. Targeting the local AI market, **[AMD unveiled the Ryzen AI Halo workstation for $4,000](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563160.htm)** alongside the **[Ryzen AI Max+ 400 PRO APU](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563162.htm)**, claiming it is the world's first x86 processor to support 300-billion parameter models. Elsewhere, **[Flipper Devices teased the Linux-based Flipper One](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563250.htm)** for network hackers and tech enthusiasts, and **[Philips introduced an innovative dual-sided business monitor](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563264.htm)** designed for customer-facing environments.

## Gaming
**[Valve is fiercely fighting a New York lawsuit](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1563206.htm)**, arguing that CS2 loot boxes are no more akin to gambling than buying baseball cards or Cracker Jack prizes. Over at Microsoft, **[Xbox has hired industry analyst Matthew Ball](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1563210.htm)** to help save its struggling console business, even as insiders reveal **[EA was the primary roadblock that killed the Xbox Game Pass family plan](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1563212.htm)**. In a testament to protracted development cycles, **[several real-world Miami buildings referenced for GTA 6 have been demolished](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1563208.htm)** before the highly anticipated game has even been released.

## Science & Space
**[SpaceX's IPO prospectus reveals a massive $4.9 billion loss](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563232.htm)** driven largely by xAI's data center investments, with Elon Musk retaining 85% voting power and positioning himself to become the world's first trillionaire. Back on Earth, **[Meituan has officially normalized its low-altitude drone delivery network](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1563222.htm)**, deploying its fourth-generation M-Drone 4L Winch and smart M-Port 3 landing hubs across multiple Chinese cities. Meanwhile, a **[new study of quasar data challenges the standard cosmological model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1563164.htm)**, suggesting the universe may exhibit a "cosmic dipole anomaly" and is not truly symmetric.
