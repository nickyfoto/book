# CNBeta — 2026-07-26

## Top Story
NVIDIA is aggressively securing the future of artificial intelligence infrastructure with two jaw-dropping financial maneuvers. According to a [report on NVIDIA's $500 billion partnership with SK Group](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570388.htm), the company is embarking on a massive initiative to secure SK Hynix's next-generation HBM memory supply and build out global AI factories. Meanwhile, a separate [CNBeta report details a planned $250 billion guarantee](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570408.htm) by NVIDIA to help OpenAI fund a colossal 10-gigawatt data center. These moves underscore how the astronomical scale of AI capital expenditures is pushing NVIDIA to underwrite the debt of its most crucial partners and secure its supply chain at an unprecedented scale.

## Tech & AI
OpenAI is [lobbying the US government to restrict Chinese open-source models](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570368.htm) while publicly supporting open weights, a dual strategy that is [drawing intense industry criticism](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570360.htm). Tech leaders like Elon Musk and Jensen Huang oppose these bans, with [Musk predicting China could eventually lead the AI sector](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570382.htm) due to its massive electricity generation capabilities. Model "distillation" has also become a heavily debated topic, as [detailed in a report on the industry's new obsession](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570396.htm), sparking fierce arguments between open-source advocates and major closed-source labs over IP theft and national security. 

On the domestic front, [DeepSeek's planned 10 billion RMB funding round has suddenly been paused](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570356.htm) due to reported internal dissatisfaction over leaked communications regarding the first round of financing. Concurrently, Moonshot AI celebrated the success of its Kimi K3 model with a [leaked internal event carrying the bold slogan "To the Moon"](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1570362.htm).
