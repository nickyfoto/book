# CNBeta — 2026-06-26

## Top Story
AI-driven memory chip shortages are fundamentally disrupting the consumer electronics market, triggering unprecedented price hikes across major brands. According to a [cnbeta report on Apple's price adjustments](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1566886.htm), the company has raised prices on 14 hardware products, including Macs and iPads, citing a massive surge in memory demand from AI data centers. This cost inflation is a systemic industry issue; as noted in a [cnbeta report on Lenovo's market forecast](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1566944.htm), the era of cheap storage is likely over, with memory price increases expected to become the "new normal" through the end of the decade. The ripple effects are already being felt globally, pushing [Microsoft to hike Xbox console prices](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1566882.htm) and causing [Apple's Asian supply chain stocks to plummet](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1566964.htm) as hardware cost inflation spooks investors.
