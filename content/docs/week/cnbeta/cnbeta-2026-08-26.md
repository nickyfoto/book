# CNBeta — August 26, 2026

## Top Story
According to **[a cnbeta report on OpenAI's Jalapeño AI chip](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574986.htm)**, OpenAI's first custom silicon, co-developed with Broadcom, has shattered performance benchmarks and energy-efficiency metrics against NVIDIA's flagship processors. Designed in a record nine months utilizing a self-improving loop driven by OpenAI’s own "GPT-Astra" (GPT-6) model, the chip poses a direct threat to NVIDIA’s historically unassailable CUDA moat by allowing OpenAI to custom-tailor hardware to its workloads. This breakthrough marks a pivotal milestone where advanced AI models are now actively design-engineering their own successor hardware, potentially rewriting the rules of the global semiconductor supply chain.

## Tech & AI
Chinese AI laboratories and semiconductor players are demonstrating unprecedented commercial and technical momentum. A **[cnbeta report on DeepSeek's financial performance](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575026.htm)** reveals the startup generated 475 million RMB (\$70.7 million USD) in the first seven months of 2026—a spectacular nine-fold surge over its entire 2025 revenue—driven by the global popularity of its cost-effective open-source V4 models. Simultaneously, **[cnbeta confirms Zhipu AI's secret 'Ox Alpha' model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575056.htm)** was the mysterious, high-performing model that topped the OpenRouter programming and agent leaderboard; Zhipu is officially releasing its weights under a name inspired by the Chinese film *牛来*. On the hardware front, **[cnbeta reports on ChangXin and Yangtze Memory's expansion](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574980.htm)** show that China's top memory makers are aggressively boosting capacity toward a 50% self-sufficiency target by 2027/2028, valuing "Apple certification" as a premium validation of their technology over volume supply. This domestic hardware progression is underscored by **[a performance test of ChangXin DDR5 memory](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575060.htm)** which demonstrated stable operation at 8000MT/s with performance nearly identical to SK Hynix's flagship M-Die. Meanwhile, mobile giant Xiaomi is advancing its silicon autonomy; a **[cnbeta leak of the Xiaomi 18 Fold](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575020.htm)** reveals the upcoming foldable will debut the "Xuanjie O3" AI flagship SoC, hitting a record 5.22 million AnTuTu benchmark points and introducing global-first LPDDR6 support.

## Consumer & Devices
Apple's autumn hardware strategy is taking clear shape. A **[cnbeta report on Apple's upcoming September 9 event](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575116.htm)** reveals the Cupertino giant will unveil the iPhone 18 Pro series alongside its highly anticipated book-style foldable, the "iPhone Ultra". However, this premium folding strategy faces early friction; **[a cnbeta survey on foldable iPhones](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575088.htm)** shows that 75% of US adults are uninterested in a foldable model, largely put off by a rumored \$2,000+ price tag. Meanwhile, Apple's desktop lineup is getting an aggressive silicon boost as **[cnbeta details the new M6 Mac mini](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574964.htm)**, which steps up to a 2nm M6 chip with double the neural cores to achieve a 4.8x boost in local AI processing over the M4. In a rare customer-friendly move, **[cnbeta highlights Apple's free Mac mini upgrades](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575090.htm)**, showing that Apple is automatically upgrading pending M4 orders to the new M6 model for free. On the Android side, Samsung is moving toward battery density innovations; **[cnbeta's Galaxy S27 CAD leaks](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575130.htm)** suggest that while standard model aesthetics remain unchanged, it will feature a much larger 4,900mAh silicon-carbon negative electrode battery to dramatically extend daily runtime. Conversely, wearable software in China's youth market is facing severe backlash; **[cnbeta's coverage of the Xiaotiancai watch controversy](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1575080.htm)** reveals the leading kids' smartwatch maker issued a public apology and scrubbed subjective weight ratings after a healthy 167cm, 50.5kg girl was labeled "overweight" by its unscientific BMI algorithm.

## Gaming
Open-source and platform-level shifts are redefining the gaming landscape. A **[cnbeta report on W4 Games' B-round funding](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1575028.htm)** details how the commercial developer behind the open-source Godot Engine secured an \$18 million funding round led by Tencent, signaling a joint effort to heavily expand Godot’s footprint across Asian corporate and indie game studios. In triple-A development, Rockstar Games is navigating the fallout of a massive blockchain-hosted security breach; **[cnbeta highlights Rockstar's GTA 6 apology](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1575086.htm)**, where the developer apologized to fans, expressed heartbreak over the leaks, and confirmed a long-awaited extended gameplay reveal for August 28 ahead of its November 19 release. Finally, console gaming is entering a smoother post-physical era: as **[cnbeta reports on Xbox's disc-to-digital feature](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1575104.htm)**, Microsoft is launching a preview feature allowing users to insert a physical game disc once to permanently register a digital license on their accounts, a massive consumer-friendly preservation effort that stands in contrast to Sony's recent move to phase out physical PlayStation discs.

## Science & Space
Space exploration and robotics are advancing at an accelerating pace. According to **[a cnbeta report on SpaceX's Louisiana Starport](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1575006.htm)**, SpaceX has committed \$100 billion to construct the world's largest launch site in Louisiana, planning over a dozen launchpads to support a staggering 30 Starship launches per day. This massive expansion is forcing structural resource reallocation; as **[cnbeta reports on SpaceX ending Florida Starlink missions](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1575042.htm)**, the aerospace leader is phasing out East Coast Falcon 9 Starlink launches to channel engineering talent toward rapid Starship turnaround. Back on Earth, leg-based robotic speed has surpassed human limits; **[cnbeta details Tiangong Ultra's record run](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574990.htm)** at the World Humanoid Robot Games in Beijing, where the 1.8-meter-tall humanoid sprinted 100 meters in an astonishing 8.86 seconds—comfortably beating Usain Bolt's human record of 9.58 seconds—while also setting humanoid world records in the 400-meter run and high jump. Meanwhile, looking toward sustained deep space infrastructure, **[a cnbeta report on NASA's lunar traffic management](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1575076.htm)** details a new "string of pearls" orbital queue algorithm designed to prevent spacecraft collisions as multiple landers and supply ships begin arriving at the Gateway space station in 2027.

# CNBeta 中文版 — 2026年8月26日

## 头条新闻
根据**[cnbeta关于OpenAI“辣椒”(Jalapeño)芯片的报道](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1574986.htm)**，OpenAI与博通联合研发的首颗自研ASIC推理芯片“Jalapeño”取得了重大性能突破，其功耗仅为700瓦（仅为英伟达GB300 1400W的一半），却在开源大模型上跑出了每秒1459个Token的惊人速度。该芯片的研发进程极具革命性，OpenAI通过其自有的“GPT-Astra”(GPT-6)模型在流片室担任“最强辅助”，仅用九个月便完成了传统18至36个月的开发周期，实现了“AI设计芯片、芯片跑AI”的自我进化闭环。这一技术飞跃不仅大幅降低了OpenAI的数据中心运营成本，更通过直击应用痛点的极速推理性能，对英伟达构筑多年的CUDA软件生态护城河发起了实质性挑战。
