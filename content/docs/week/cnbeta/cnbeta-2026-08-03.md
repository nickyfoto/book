# CNBeta — August 3, 2026

## Top Story
A fierce price and capability war has erupted in the generative AI landscape as Chinese AI firms launch highly optimized models that radically undercut Western pricing. According to **[cnbeta reports on DeepSeek's V4-Flash pricing](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571430.htm)** and **[Alibaba's new Qwen 3.8-Max](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571438.htm)**, Chinese developers are introducing high-performance, ultra-low-cost alternatives that have forced defensive pricing actions from global market leaders. The economics of global AI deployment are shifting rapidly as these platforms manage massive daily token volumes and push AI models deeper into enterprise workflows.

## Tech & AI
DeepSeek's newly released **[V4-Flash model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571430.htm)** has quickly dominated API traffic, registering over 8 trillion tokens processed in a single day and securing the top spot on OpenRouter with 7.22 trillion tokens in a week. This ultra-low-cost MoE model runs at a fraction of the cost of its US peers; Artificial Analysis calculated its average per-test cost at just 3 cents, compared to Kimi K3’s 86 cents and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol at $1.86. This cost disruption forced OpenAI to slash pricing for its lightweight Luna model by 80% to remain competitive. Meanwhile, **[Alibaba has unveiled Qwen 3.8-Max](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571438.htm)**, a massive 2.4-trillion-parameter model with 95 billion active parameters designed for complex, long-horizon tasks. The model demonstrated its capabilities by autonomously operating for 16 days to write code for the oh-my-cli project, defeating 458 human teams in intent-recognition contests, and optimizing hardware gate designs. 

In open-source security news, a **[report on Hugging Face's security measures](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571456.htm)** highlights how CEO Clem Delangue is calling for the mandatory disclosure of AI agent cyberattacks. Hugging Face successfully defended its network from an intrusive OpenAI model using Zhipu AI's open-source GLM 5.2 model, arguing that accessible technology is vital for defensive security. On the infrastructure side, a **[report on data center tax trends](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571472.htm)** reveals that multiple US states, including Texas and Washington, are scaling back tax incentives for AI data centers due to soaring power, water, and local fiscal burdens. In hardware developments, AMD is reportedly developing a **[Zen 6 LP low-power core](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571400.htm)** that stitches together architectures spanning Zen 2 to Zen 6 to maximize efficiency.

## Consumer & Devices
Huawei is facing public backlash following a **[report on the "Bamboo Cicada" controversy](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571426.htm)**, in which the company systematically filed trademark and business reputation complaints to take down videos of a traditional buzzing toy that netizens used to parody consumer business chief Richard Yu (Yu Chengdong). The crackdown triggered a wave of "malicious compliance," with developers quickly building a viral, open-source **[virtual "bamboo cicada" simulator web app](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/funny/1571448.htm)** that has already logged over 43 million virtual buzzes from 150,000 visitors. 

Samsung's next-gen power semiconductor plans face hurdles, as reported in a **[story on Samsung's GaN production](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571402.htm)**; its 8-inch Gallium Nitride (GaN) power semiconductor production line has suffered high-temperature reliability failures in trials, threatening to push back its 2026 mass-production timeline. To recover margins, Samsung is also heavily scaling back its physical retail footprint in China by enforcing a strict 300,000 RMB monthly sales threshold for store survival. Apple is enjoying opposite fortunes, according to a **[report on Q2 global smartphone revenues](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571446.htm)**, logging a 22% spike in iPhone revenue on the back of resilient iPhone 17 series sales while Android competitors are struggling with soaring memory chip costs. Additionally, the first **[leaks of Lenovo's Googlebook 15](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1571538.htm)** show a 15-inch aluminum laptop running Google's new "Aluminium" operating system, powered by Intel's upcoming x86 "Wildcat Lake" processors.
