# CNBeta — 2026-06-02

## Top Story
A crucial loophole in US export controls has been closed, directly impacting Chinese tech firms and their overseas operations. According to **[a new Commerce Department directive](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564394.htm)**, the US has explicitly banned the export of advanced AI chips to any overseas subsidiaries whose actual headquarters belong to entities inside China. This plugs a major gap left when the Biden administration's broader AI diffusion rules were paused, which previously allowed Chinese companies to acquire high-end compute power by establishing shell companies and using third-party data centers abroad. 

## Tech & AI
Nvidia is expanding its footprint in the Windows on Arm and AI agent ecosystem. **[Early benchmark leaks for Nvidia's RTX Spark (N1X) chip](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564414.htm)** show it matching the multi-core performance of Apple's M3 Max, confirming serious momentum for its 20-core architecture. The processor claims to run all Windows 11 applications through native Arm support or the "Prism" translation layer. Furthermore, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang stated that the **[Vera CPU will become the company's primary growth driver](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564426.htm)** over GPUs, anticipating the immense computational demands of autonomous "agentic AI" ecosystems.

Microsoft launched a massive wave of AI initiatives, including the new **[MAI-Thinking-1 reasoning model and MAI-Code-1](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564474.htm)**, positioning itself to serve enterprise clients with its own commercially viable models without purely relying on OpenAI. The tech giant also unveiled **[Project Solara](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564468.htm)**, a new Android-based operating system designed for "agent-first" hardware devices like smart badges and desktop hubs, alongside **[Scout](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564458.htm)**, an autonomous enterprise AI agent for Microsoft 365 that manages workflows continuously in the background. 

On the regulatory and market fronts, Anthropic has **[confidentially filed for an IPO](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564384.htm)**, heating up the race against OpenAI to secure increasingly scarce public capital. Concurrently, **[President Trump signed a scaled-back AI executive order](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564484.htm)** designed to balance national security reviews for frontier models with aggressive economic innovation.

## Consumer & Devices
Apple's much-rumored foldable "iPhone Ultra" is progressing, with **[prototypes featuring a liquid metal hinge](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564430.htm)** reportedly sent to global carriers for network compatibility testing. Hardware fragmentation across regions also continues: the **[Chinese version of the iPhone 18 Pro will feature a smaller battery](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564482.htm)** (4056mAh) compared to the US version (4288mAh) because it must retain the physical SIM card tray. To deal with thermals in future devices, Apple has patented a method to **[use mineral oil inside iPhone camera modules](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564472.htm)** as a liquid heat sink to manage the extreme temperatures generated by large image sensors and shape-memory alloy actuators.

To combat the dramatic rise of AI deepfake voice scams, Google is rolling out a background **[“Scam Call Detection” feature](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564460.htm)** on Android devices. The system uses a silent "digital handshake" via RCS to verify if a call is actually originating from the trusted contact's real device, instantly warning users if the signal is missing. 

## Gaming
Sony is facing serious headwinds in its software ecosystem, with **[PlayStation first-party game sales plunging by 30 million units](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1564416.htm)** over the last four years. Market analysts blame the dramatic decline on a heavy, resource-draining pivot toward live-service games and the costly acquisition of Bungie. In the handheld space, ASUS announced the **[ROG XBOX Ally X20 handheld console](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1564410.htm)**, an upgraded device featuring the AMD Z2 Extreme chip, an OLED display, and highly durable anti-drift joysticks. Meanwhile, a recent ESRB rating suggests **[Minecraft will be getting a native release on the upcoming Nintendo Switch 2](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1564408.htm)**.

## Science & Space
SpaceX is aggressively preparing for what could be the **[largest IPO in history](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564450.htm)**, targeting a $1.75 trillion valuation to raise at least $75 billion. The company is reportedly leveraging its dominant market position by **[negotiating with underwriters to slash fees](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1564438.htm)** below 0.75%. 

In aviation, NASA's experimental **[X-59 quiet supersonic aircraft is scheduled to break the sound barrier](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564442.htm)** later this month. The mission aims to prove that a radically redesigned airframe can mitigate sonic booms, potentially paving the way to lift the US ban on commercial supersonic flights over land. In the computing sector, IBM announced a massive **[commitment of over $10 billion to quantum computing](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1564428.htm)** over the next five years, aiming to deliver a large-scale fault-tolerant quantum computer by 2029.
