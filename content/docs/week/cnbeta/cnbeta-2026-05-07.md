# CNBeta — 2026-05-07

## Top Story
Elon Musk has officially announced the integration of his AI startup xAI into SpaceX, renaming the combined entity to SpaceXAI. According to a **[CNBeta report on the merger](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561646.htm)**, this massive $1 trillion valuation deal is driven by the realization that Earth's power grids cannot support the gigawatt-scale energy demands of future AI, prompting a radical shift toward building orbital AI data centers powered by solar energy.

## Tech & AI
OpenAI's ambitions for in-house silicon are facing hurdles, as detailed in a **[report on the Broadcom partnership](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561710.htm)**; Broadcom is reportedly demanding that Microsoft purchase 40% of the $180 billion project's output to secure financing. Meanwhile, the company continues to iterate on software, launching three new real-time voice models, including GPT-Realtime-2, to enable seamless "think-while-listening" capabilities, per **[OpenAI's latest release notes](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561706.htm)**.

In the AI infrastructure race, **[Arm predicts a massive data center revenue boom](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561662.htm)**, locking in over $2 billion in orders for its new 136-core AGI CPUs designed to complement GPU deployments. Microsoft is aggressively reshaping Windows 11, walking back forced AI integrations by **[reducing Copilot's visibility in system apps](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561634.htm)** to appease frustrated users and refocusing Copilot on enterprise clients. The company is also **[testing a Windows 10-style smaller taskbar](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561636.htm)** and developing a **[Low Latency Profile feature](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561700.htm)** to temporarily boost CPU frequencies and drastically cut UI response times.

## Consumer & Devices
Apple is grappling with overwhelming demand for its aggressively priced $599 MacBook Neo, prompting the company to **[double A18 Pro chip orders](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561688.htm)**. However, due to rising chip and memory costs, an **[industry analysis suggests Apple might scrap the $599 entry-level model](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561690.htm)** altogether, effectively raising the base price to $699. 

Looking ahead to future wearables, a **[leak reveals Apple is testing camera-equipped AirPods Pro](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561708.htm)**, integrating miniature cameras into the stems to feed spatial and visual data directly to Siri. In the mobile space, **[Qualcomm announced the Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 and 4 Gen 5](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561692.htm)**, targeting mid-range smartphones with enhanced AI imaging and improved UI smoothness. On the automotive front, **[Lexus unveiled the TZ](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/tech/1561668.htm)**, a three-row electric SUV equipped with a 95.82kWh battery and DIRECT 4 electronic all-wheel drive.

## Gaming
The highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto VI* continues to dominate headlines, with **[rumors suggesting the game's file size could hit 200GB](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1561652.htm)**, posing a significant storage challenge for standard PS5 owners. This aligns with Take-Two's CEO confirming the game's historically massive budget, as noted in a **[report on GTA 6's development costs](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1561672.htm)**, which analysts estimate at up to $2 billion. 

Nintendo surprised fans by **[announcing a new Star Fox game for the Switch 2](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1561630.htm)**, featuring 4v4 online dogfights and a planned release this June. Meanwhile, Microsoft is overhauling its gaming ecosystem, teasing a **[unified Xbox dashboard across consoles, handhelds, and PCs](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/game/1561704.htm)** to reduce fragmentation and improve the overall player experience.

## Science & Space
Astronomers using the James Webb Space Telescope have discovered that LHS 3844 b is a **[dark, airless super-Earth resembling Mercury](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1561694.htm)**, with a surface likely covered in basaltic rock and completely devoid of atmospheric gases. In geophysics, scientists analyzing satellite data have **[observed a "whiplash effect" in major earthquakes](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1561674.htm)**, where the ground transiently rebounds in the opposite direction after an abrupt fault stop, presenting severe structural engineering challenges. Neuroscientists have also uncovered that the brain's **[primary sensory cortex is deeply involved in decision-making](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/science/1561626.htm)**, challenging the traditional view that decisions are solely processed in higher-order frontal brain regions.
