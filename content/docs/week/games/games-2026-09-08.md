# Gaming News — 2026-09-08

## Top Story
Nintendo officially pulled back the curtain on its long-awaited **[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-remake-fully-revealed)**, launching exclusively for **Nintendo Switch 2 on November 5, 2026**. Built from the ground up with an overhauled 3D aesthetic, modern quality-of-life upgrades (dedicated jump button, free camera, motion aiming, instant boot toggling), and full voice acting, the N64 classic is also getting a special **Zelda 40th Anniversary Switch 2 console** on October 29.

## News & Reviews

**[Valheim 1.0 Review: The King of Survival Crafting Hits Full Release](https://www.ign.com/articles/valheim-review)** · IGN  
Iron Gate’s Norse survival hit has officially exited Early Access after nearly six years, delivering an expansive **1.0 release** complete with eight distinct biomes, refined combat mechanics, and a dramatic conclusion in the Deep North. IGN praises it as a **cozy warrior's paradise** and a genre-defining masterpiece when sailing and building with friends. It stands as an essential playthrough for survival craft fans seeking satisfying progression and memorable boss fights.

**[Game of Thrones RTS 'War for Westeros' Streamlines Systems for Beginners](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-war-for-westeros-developers-are-planning-for-a-flood-of-game-of-thrones-fans-who-have-never-played-an-rts)** · IGN  
Developer PlaySide is designing its real-time strategy title **War for Westeros** specifically to welcome Game of Thrones fans who have never played an RTS. The game eliminates tedious worker micromanagement with a **"set and forget" resource system**, allowing players to focus on tactical battles with iconic heroes like Jon Snow, Arya Stark, and Tywin Lannister. It offers a uniquely approachable entry point into faction warfare without sacrificing strategy depth.

**[Archetype Entertainment Showcases Sci-Fi RPG 'Exodus' for 2027](https://www.ign.com/articles/exodus-five-things-you-need-to-know)** · IGN  
Former BioWare veterans at Archetype Entertainment unveiled a deep look at **Exodus**, an ambitious sci-fi action-RPG arriving April 7, 2027, centered around the heartbreaking effects of **time dilation**. Players guide traveler Jun Aslan alongside companions—including "Awakened" intelligent animals like a sniper octopus—combining tactical cover shooting with Celestial powers and morally heavy choices. With its cinematic squad mechanics and branching dialogue trees, it’s shaping up to be a premier space opera.

**[Call of Duty Adds Doom Slayer for Black Ops 7 Halloween Event](https://www.ign.com/articles/call-of-duty-adds-doom-slayer-skin-as-part-of-new-halloween-update)** · IGN  
Activision is bringing id Software's iconic **Doom Slayer** to *Call of Duty: Black Ops 7* as a playable operator starting September 24 in 'The Haunting' update. The crossover includes both *Doom: The Dark Ages* armor and classic 1993 Doomguy skins alongside signature weapons like the BFG, Crucible blade, and Super Shotgun. It delivers a gory, fan-favorite send-off for Black Ops 7 right before *Modern Warfare 4* releases in October.

**[SteelSeries Aeon Pro Review: High-End Tech Meets Infinite Battery Swapping](https://www.ign.com/articles/steelseries-aeon-pro-review)** · IGN  
SteelSeries has debuted the \$260 **Aeon Pro controller**, integrating the hot-swappable "infinite battery" system previously featured in its Arctis Nova Pro headsets. Outfitted with Hall-effect thumbsticks, two-stage Hall-effect triggers, four back paddles, dual USB-C multi-system switching, and an OLED screen, it offers top-tier hardware for PC and Xbox gamers. Despite the high cost, its luxury build and instant battery swapping make it a standout pro gamepad.

**[Stellar Blade Developer Clarifies Stance on AI After Video Backlash](https://www.ign.com/articles/shift-up-cto-clarifies-stellar-blade-developers-stance-on-ai-after-blood-rain-music-video-controversy)** · IGN  
Following fan backlash over an AI-generated music video for *Stellar Blade: Blood Rain*, Shift Up CTO DongKi Lee clarified that AI is strictly an **"experimental tool"** to support developer craft rather than replace human artists. Lee assured players that all official game content remains human-made and that future promotional tests will be separated from actual game development. The response underscores ongoing industry debates regarding AI experimentation versus human craftsmanship.

**[Sega President Embraces Stream-Watching Fans Who Don't Play Games](https://www.ign.com/articles/segas-president-says-theres-been-an-increase-in-fans-who-dont-play-its-games-but-that-doesnt-mean-they-arent-gamers)** · IGN  
Sega COO Shuji Utsumi noted a major rise in fans who follow franchises like *Yakuza* and *Sonic* through YouTube, streams, and film adaptations without playing the games themselves. Utsumi declared that Sega considers all passionate followers to be **"gamers,"** driving expanded merchandise lines in cosmetics and lifestyle apparel. It highlights how transmedia strategies are reshaping traditional gaming demographics and commercial reach.

**[Toho Confirms King Ghidorah for 'Godzilla Minus Zero' Sequel](https://www.ign.com/articles/godzilla-minus-zero-trailer-confirms-king-ghidorah)** · IGN  
Toho dropped the first trailer for **Godzilla Minus Zero**, confirming that three-headed monster King Ghidorah will battle Godzilla in the November 6, 2026 theatrical sequel. Helmed again by Oscar-winner Takashi Yamazaki, this marks the first Japanese feature filmed natively for **IMAX 70mm**. The Kaiju brawl promises a massive, cinematic continuation of the acclaimed 2023 blockbuster.
