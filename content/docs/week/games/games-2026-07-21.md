# Gaming News — 2026-07-21

Former *Forza Horizon* director Mike Brown has traced Microsoft's devastating wave of Xbox layoffs directly to the failure of Game Pass to hit the massive subscriber targets required to justify the company's aggressive spending. As Xbox undergoes what CEO Asha Sharma calls its most significant restructure, the reality of a changing subscription landscape is hitting the industry hard.

## News & Reviews

**[Splatoon Raiders Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/splatoon-raiders-review)** · IGN
Nintendo's first *Splatoon* spinoff completely sticks the landing as an incredibly well-designed action RPG, earning a stellar 9/10. The single-player campaign forces you to expertly manage new gadgets and scrappy weaponry against challenging Salmonid hordes, making it one of the absolute best games on the Switch 2 so far.

**[The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Songs of the Past Reveal Date Confirmed](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-songs-of-the-past-reveal-date-confirmed)** · IGN
CD Projekt Red confirmed that the highly anticipated third expansion for *The Witcher 3*, titled *Songs of the Past*, will be officially revealed on August 25 at gamescom 2026. Co-developed with Fool's Theory, the 2027 DLC will act as a prequel of sorts to *The Witcher 4* and will arm Geralt with a mysterious new sword.

**[Just 7 PlayStation Games Have Sold More Than 100,000 Physical Copies in the U.S. So Far This Year](https://www.ign.com/articles/just-7-playstation-games-have-sold-more-than-100000-physical-copies-in-the-us-so-far-this-year)** · IGN
Amid massive backlash over Sony's plan to kill physical game discs starting in 2028, sobering new U.S. sales data underscores exactly why the publisher isn't changing its mind. Physical copies have essentially flatlined in the U.S. market, with digital margins proving far too lucrative for Sony to ignore in the current economy.

**[Lara Croft’s Acrobatics Were So Important to Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis That Actress Alix Wilton Regan Started Rock-Climbing to Get Into Character](https://www.ign.com/articles/lara-crofts-acrobatics-were-so-important-to-tomb-raider-legacy-of-atlantis-that-actress-alix-wilton-regan-started-rock-climbing-to-get-into-character)** · IGN
Crystal Dynamics is making Lara Croft's iconic mobility a major focus of the upcoming *Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis*, bringing back her classic cartwheels, handstands, and swan dives. Actress Alix Wilton Regan even took up rock climbing to physically embody the confident, highly capable movement of 1996-era Lara.

**[2026 Is Great For PC Gaming If You Already Have Hardware](https://www.ign.com/articles/2026-is-great-for-pc-gaming-if-you-already-have-hardware)** · IGN
The AI-driven RAM crisis has caused PC hardware prices to skyrocket, with even the entry-level Steam Machine now costing over a thousand dollars. Fortunately, developers seem to be reacting to the shortage by releasing remarkably well-optimized games, making this a great year for PC gamers who are willing to ride out the crisis on their current rigs rather than trying to upgrade.
