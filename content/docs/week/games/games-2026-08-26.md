## News & Reviews

**[Metro 2039: Handcrafted and Lethal](https://www.ign.com/articles/metro-2039-the-first-hands-on-preview)** · IGN
In our first hands-on preview, the post-apocalyptic sequel from 4A Games blows us away with its gorgeous, tension-spiking gameplay that refuses to hold your hand and requires rigorous gas mask filter management. Rebuilt from the ground up on a fully ray-traced engine, the game focuses heavily on the consequences of war—a theme deeply influenced by the developer's real-world survival of Russia's invasion of Ukraine. Set to land in February 2027, this slow-burn masterpiece is shaping up to be an absolute must-play for fans of immersive, high-stakes survival shooters.

**[Xbox Announces Disc-to-Digital Option While PlayStation Abandons Discs](https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-is-letting-players-digitize-their-physical-games-starting-next-week-for-select-users)** · IGN
Starting next week on August 31, Microsoft is rolling out a consumer-friendly feature allowing Xbox Insiders to digitize physical Xbox One and Series X discs directly into their digital library. This brilliant move stands in stark contrast to PlayStation's controversial decision to discontinue physical game discs entirely by January 2028, earning Microsoft massive goodwill among physical media collectors. While Xbox CEO Asha Sharma remains cagey on whether the next-gen "Project Helix" will have an optical drive, this transition plan ensures physical game libraries are preserved.

**[Fable’s Albion Delivers Exquisite World-Building and British Wit](https://www.ign.com/articles/fable-after-watching-a-full-quest-i-couldnt-be-more-excited-to-play-it-gamescom-2026)** · IGN
A 15-minute live gameplay presentation of *Fable* at Gamescom 2026 showed off Bowerstone's stunning, hand-crafted architecture and a quest full of Playground Games' signature British humor. Players will navigate class-free combat with swords, bows, and flashy magic like floating "magical blades" and electric whips while making story-shaping moral choices. The game is shaping up to be a brilliant, highly responsive modern continuation of the beloved franchise.

**[The Duskbloods Network Test Proves This Bizarre FromSoftware-Nintendo Collab Actually Works](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-duskbloods-preview-hands-on-fromsoftware-switch-2-exclusive)** · IGN
After spending eight hours in the network test, this Switch 2 exclusive feels like "Bloodborne meets Kirby Air Ride's City Trial," dropping players into a grotesque, gothic playground to hunt, gather power-ups, and clash in chaotic PvPvE matches. Directed by Hidetaka Miyazaki to cater to players who normally find high-intensity PvP too daunting, the game utilizes a built-in rubber-banding mechanic and last-second comeback tools to emphasize friendly, multiplayer fun.

**[Star Wars: Zero Company Reaches for Greatness but Hits a PC Performance Wall](https://www.ign.com/articles/star-wars-zero-company-review)** · IGN
Co-developed by former XCOM 2 and Star Wars Jedi: Survivor veterans, this tactical turn-based shooter delivers an outstanding, blaster-focused Clone Wars campaign with exceptional characters and writing. However, our technical testing revealed an Unreal Engine 5 "nightmare" on PC, plagued with microstutters, CPU bottlenecks, and crashes that frequently wipe out end-of-mission progress. While the core tactics and aggression-based systems are a triumph, PC players will want to tweak settings heavily or wait for post-launch optimization patches.

**[Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 Beta Hits Switch 2 This Weekend](https://www.ign.com/articles/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4s-beta-will-be-playable-on-switch-2-for-free-this-weekend)** · IGN
For the first time in over a decade, Call of Duty returns to Nintendo hardware with a free open beta of *Modern Warfare 4* starting August 28, offering Switch 2 players exclusive gyro and Joy-Con mouse controls. The full game launches October 23, but physical buyers should take note: it's a "code in a box" release with no physical game card, as the massive 100GB+ game size exceeds standard Switch 2 cartridge limits.

**[Silent Hill Producer Confirms Konami Wants Annual Franchise Releases](https://www.ign.com/articles/silent-hill-producer-says-konami-wants-to-release-a-new-game-in-the-series-every-year)** · IGN
Producer Motoi Okamoto revealed that Konami is aiming to release a new *Silent Hill* game every single year, with multiple unannounced projects currently in exploration. To avoid player fatigue, the publisher plans to break away from traditional American settings, as seen in the upcoming *Silent Hill f* set in 1960s Japan. This is part of a broader corporate push by Konami to aggressively revitalize its classic franchises, including *Metal Gear* and *Castlevania*.
