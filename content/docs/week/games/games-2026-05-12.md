## News & Reviews

**[Forza Horizon 6 Dev Bans Pirate Who Leaked Gameplay Footage Until the Year 10,000](https://www.ign.com/articles/forza-horizon-6-dev-bans-pirate-who-leaked-gameplay-footage-until-the-year-10000-but-faces-uphill-battle-plugging-the-leak)**
Playground Games isn't messing around with leakers, handing out hardware bans until the year 9999 for players caught accessing the pre-release build of *Forza Horizon 6*. Despite early gameplay hitting social media, hype remains sky-high, and the title is currently sitting pretty as the second top-selling game by revenue on Steam ahead of its official May 19 launch.

**[Sega Cancels Mysterious 'Super Game’ 5 Years After It Was Announced](https://www.ign.com/articles/sega-cancels-mysterious-super-game-5-years-after-it-was-announced-amid-pull-back-from-underperforming-free-to-play)**
Sega is ditching its "Super Game" initiative, canceling the project after five years as the company pulls back from its struggling free-to-play strategy. Instead of chasing a massive $634 million lifetime sales target with a live-service ecosystem, developers have been shuffled over to "Full Game" teams to focus on beloved franchise reboots like *Crazy Taxi* and *Jet Set Radio*.

**[Lies of P Sequel 'Enters Full Production Phase’](https://www.ign.com/articles/lies-of-p-sequel-enters-full-production-phase)**
Neowiz has confirmed that the sequel to the phenomenal 2023 soulslike *Lies of P* has officially moved out of prototyping and into full production. The developers noted that narrative immersion is their core competency, proving that they intend to double down on the original's fantastic storytelling as they look to expand their global IP footprint.

**[Dead Space Producer Doubts Dead Space 4 Will Ever Be Made](https://www.ign.com/articles/dead-space-producer-doubts-dead-space-4-will-ever-be-made-the-numbers-just-arent-there)**
If you're holding your breath for *Dead Space 4*, you might want to stop, as former series producer Chuck Beaver states the numbers "just aren't there" to justify it. Due to massive modern production budgets, Beaver estimates a new entry would need to sell around 15 million copies to be viable—a steep hurdle that likely explains EA's cold feet despite the recent remake's critical success.

**[The Sinking City 2 Preview: A Promising but Inconsistent Eldritch Horror Adventure](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-sinking-city-2-preview-a-promising-but-inconsistent-eldritch-horror-adventure)**
Frogwares' *The Sinking City 2* takes a big swing by dropping its predecessor's tedious open-world elements for a denser, more focused survival horror experience. While the revamped investigation board mechanics and deep Lovecraftian lore are huge upgrades, the game is currently held back by clunky, inconsistent third-person combat that feels loose and janky.

**[Former Tekken Lead Katsuhiro Harada Sets Up Own Company, VS Studio](https://www.ign.com/articles/former-tekken-lead-katsuhiro-harada-sets-up-own-company-vs-studio)**
Fighting game legend Katsuhiro Harada is striking out on his own, forming VS Studio in partnership with SNK Corporation. Following his departure from Bandai Namco over ongoing creative frustrations, Harada promises this new Tokyo-based venture will lean on his 31 years of experience to craft games that push "beyond tradition".
