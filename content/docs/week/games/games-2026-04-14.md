# Gaming News — 2026-04-14

## Top Story
Microsoft is reportedly rethinking its Game Pass strategy, with rumors swirling that this year's *Call of Duty* might be pulled as a day-one release. Following reports that Xbox leadership finds the subscription service "too expensive for players," this massive shift away from day-one releases for their biggest shooter was allegedly a primary factor behind the recent Game Pass price hikes.

## News & Reviews

**[Mouse: P.I. for Hire Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/mouse-pi-for-hire-review)** · IGN
*Mouse: P.I. for Hire* is a gorgeous black-and-white boomer shooter that nails the 1930s cartoon aesthetic, but completely misses the mark on noir storytelling. While the retro gunplay feels solid enough to carry the 12-hour campaign, the non-stop cheese puns and the ludonarrative dissonance of playing a mass-murdering private eye keep it from achieving its true potential.

**[Sony Announces Bloodborne and Zelda Movie Updates](https://www.ign.com/articles/bloodborne-r-rated-animated-movie-in-development-with-jacksepticeye-producing)** · IGN
In a massive CinemaCon reveal, Sony Pictures announced an R-rated animated *Bloodborne* film produced by PlayStation Productions and YouTuber JackSepticEye. On top of that, Sony confirmed that filming for Nintendo's live-action *The Legend of Zelda* movie has officially wrapped ahead of its May 7, 2027 theatrical release.

**[Windrose Sets Sail to Huge Early Access Success](https://www.ign.com/articles/new-pirate-game-windrose-sets-sail-today)** · IGN
The PvE pirate survival game *Windrose* (formerly known as *Crosswind*) is off to a phenomenal start out of the gates. With a concurrent peak of over 25,000 players and a 90% "Very Positive" rating on Steam, its brutal "soulslite" combat and rewarding base-building mechanics are proving to be a massive hit. 

**[Valor Mortis Preview: A Grotesque Napoleon Soulslike](https://www.ign.com/articles/valor-mortis-preview-a-soulslike-where-you-die-again-and-again-in-napoleons-army)** · IGN
*Valor Mortis* is shaping up to be an incredibly brutal, horrifying first-person Soulslike set within the corrupted ranks of Napoleon's Grande Armée. Hands-on previews revealed satisfying parry-heavy combat and dual-wielding pistol action, though the demo's punishing difficulty was so high that almost no one actually finished it.

**[Overwatch Season 2 Ushers in a New Era](https://www.ign.com/articles/new-hero-sierra-returning-post-match-accolades-and-a-map-rework-everything-new-coming-to-overwatch-season-2)** · IGN
*Overwatch* is officially dropping the "2" and launching its massive Season 2 "Reign of Talon" storyline alongside a brand new recon-focused DPS hero named Sierra. The update also brings a much-needed rework to the Antarctic Peninsula map to smooth out flanking routes, and finally restores the beloved post-match accolades system.

**[Bethesda Addressing Starfield PS5 Crashes](https://www.ign.com/articles/bethesda-responds-to-starfield-ps5-crashing-issues)** · IGN
Microsoft's highly anticipated rival console launch of *Starfield* on the PS5 has been unfortunately marred by progression-breaking crashes and save issues over its opening weekend. Thankfully, Bethesda Game Studios has publicly acknowledged the problems and is aiming to deploy a hotfix later this week to stabilize the Settled Systems.
