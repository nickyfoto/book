# Gaming News — 2026-07-04

## Top Story
PlayStation's hit samurai epic is officially getting the anime treatment, with Crunchyroll revealing the first character art for the *Ghost of Tsushima Legends* series at Anime Expo. Set to release exclusively on Crunchyroll in 2027, the adaptation by Aniplex features character designs by *Afro Samurai* creator Takashi Okazaki, bringing the game's mystical multiplayer mode into stunning animated life.

## News & Reviews

**[Solo Leveling: Beyond the System Movie Announced](https://www.ign.com/articles/solo-leveling-beyond-the-system-movie-announced-at-anime-expo)** · IGN
The smash-hit anime *Solo Leveling* is bypassing a traditional third season for now, instead continuing its story with a brand-new feature film titled *Solo Leveling: Beyond the System*. Announced at Anime Expo by English voice actor Aleks Le, the A-1 Pictures project will pick up exactly where season two left off, ensuring fans get to see Sung Jinwoo's overpowered hunter antics on the big screen.

**[Mr. Terrific TV Show in the Works](https://www.ign.com/articles/mr-terrific-tv-show-in-the-works-with-pilot-from-the-sandman-alum)** · IGN
DC Studios is secretly cooking up a TV spin-off for the genius superhero Mr. Terrific, following his cinematic debut in the upcoming *Superman* movie. Showrunner Allan Heinberg, known for his work on Netflix's *The Sandman*, is already attached to steer the project. While the DCU is currently licking its wounds after *Supergirl* pulled disappointing box office returns, actor Edi Gathegi is expected to reprise his role as the tech-savvy hero for this new series.

**[Young Washington Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/young-washington-review)** · IGN
Angel Studios takes a shot at the historical epic with *Young Washington*, chronicling the early frontier blunders of America's first president during the French & Indian War. While it boasts solid battle scenes and an earnest performance by William Franklyn-Miller, it ultimately feels like a superficial TV movie that skips the deeper moral complexities of the era. Our verdict: It's a sincere but stunted origin story wrapped in standard action hero tropes.

**[The Devil’s Rain Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-devils-rain-review)** · IGN
Severin Films has dropped a beautiful new 4K release of the 1975 paranormal kitsch-fest *The Devil’s Rain*, starring Ernest Borgnine and William Shatner. The movie drops you right into its Satanic lore with a confusing plot, but it completely redeems itself with fantastic, gooey practical effects and an unforgettable melting climax. It falls short of greatness due to a frustrating script, but genre fans will undoubtedly appreciate this wild 1970s time capsule.
