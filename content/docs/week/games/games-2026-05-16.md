# Gaming News — 2026-05-16

## Top Story
Microsoft's gaming division has officially rebranded itself to an all-caps "XBOX" under the leadership of new CEO Asha Sharma. Following a social media poll where almost 65% of fans voted for the capitalized version, the change has already taken effect across XBOX's main X account and executive profiles, marking a loud, nostalgic shift for the platform.

## News & Reviews

**[Mixtape is Here to Stay]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/mixtape-publisher-refutes-claim-that-expired-music-licenses-will-cause-the-game-to-be-delisted)
Annapurna Interactive has firmly shot down rumors that its coming-of-age masterpiece *Mixtape* will eventually be delisted due to expiring music licenses. The development team paid extra to secure perpetual licenses for the game's phenomenal soundtrack, meaning this 10/10 game won't suffer the same digital death as *Alan Wake* or *Rock Band 4*. This is a massive win for game preservation and a huge relief for anyone worried about losing access to such a stylish hit. 

**[Splatoon Raiders Gets a July Release Date]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/best-deals-for-may-16-2026)
Nintendo's first single-player-focused entry in the splatter-shooter franchise, *Splatoon Raiders*, is officially dropping on July 23. Pre-orders are live now for the physical Switch 2 edition, launching alongside three brand new amiibo featuring Frye, Shiver, and Big Man. It's a bold new direction for the multiplayer-heavy franchise, and definitely one to lock in if you're a Switch 2 owner.

**[First Look at Metal Sonic as Sonic 4 Wraps]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/sonic-the-hedgehog-4-director-shares-behind-the-scenes-look-at-metal-sonic-as-filming-wraps)
Director Jeff Fowler celebrated the end of filming for *Sonic the Hedgehog 4* by dropping a behind-the-scenes look at a life-sized Metal Sonic. The film, which introduces Kristen Bell as the voice of Amy Rose, is set to hit theaters on March 19, 2027. After the massive success of the third movie, the introduction of both Metal Sonic and Amy proves Paramount is fully committed to fleshing out its cinematic Sega universe.
