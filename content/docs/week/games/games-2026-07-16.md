# Gaming News — 2026-07-16

## Top Story
Saber Interactive's newly appointed Chief Business Officer accidentally let slip that the studio is targeting a massive 2028 lineup, which allegedly includes the highly anticipated *Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic Remake* and an unannounced *Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 3*. While Saber PR quickly tried to downplay the comments as "broad" and "not official," this is the first positive sign of life the troubled *KOTOR Remake* has had in years.

## News & Reviews

**[New Marvel's Wolverine Trailer Reveals Lady Deathstrike](https://www.ign.com/articles/marvels-wolverine-trailer-features-first-look-at-lady-deathstrike)** · IGN
Insomniac Games has dropped an action-packed new look at *Marvel's Wolverine*, confirming that the iconic villain Lady Deathstrike will be giving Logan a run for his money. The trailer also teases an unlikely team-up with Sabretooth ahead of the brutal PS5 exclusive's September 15, 2026 launch. It looks unabashedly bloody, and thankfully, a physical disc version has been confirmed.

**[Sony’s Anti-Disc Stance Sparks Massive Backlash and Sold-Out Physicals](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-special-ps5-only-physical-revered-edition-of-mortal-shell-2-has-sold-out-and-fans-are-sending-the-news-sonys-way-as-they-continue-to-push-for-a-u-turn-on-killing-discs)** · IGN
Sony's controversial decision to stop printing physical PlayStation game discs by 2028 continues to dominate industry conversations and frustrate fans. While GameStop's CEO Ryan Cohen bizarrely claimed that the move is "totally irrelevant" because video game sales make up less than 12% of his business, core gamers are proving physical media isn't dead by instantly selling out the PS5-exclusive physical *Revered Edition* of *Mortal Shell 2*. Sadly, analysts still insist Sony's digital margins are too lucrative for a reversal.

**[Game Freak Steps Out of Pokémon's Shadow With Beast of Reincarnation](https://www.ign.com/articles/beast-of-reincarnation-is-game-freaks-best-chance-to-break-out-of-pokmons-shadow-final-preview)** · IGN
If you thought Game Freak only made *Pokémon*, their ambitious new action-RPG *Beast of Reincarnation* is here to prove you wrong. Blending the parry-heavy combat of *Sekiro* with the atmospheric sci-fi storytelling of *NieR:Automata*, this 30-to-40-hour adventure feels incredibly fresh. Releasing August 4 for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC, the combat system alone—which tasks you with juggling protagonist Emma's blade arts alongside her wolf companion's magical abilities—makes it an absolute must-play.

**[God of War Laufey Star Dismisses "Totally Unbothered" Backlash](https://www.ign.com/articles/god-of-war-laufey-star-deborah-ann-woll-totally-unbothered-by-online-hate-surrounding-the-game)** · IGN
A vocal minority has been whining about Kratos taking a backseat to his late wife Faye in the upcoming PS5 exclusive *God of War Laufey*, but actress Deborah Ann Woll isn't breaking a sweat. Woll stated she's "totally unbothered" by the toxic discourse because she knows the game is "great". Given that the magical swordplay looks fantastic, the haters are likely going to miss out when it drops in 2027.

**[Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced Cuts Hidden Blade Combat](https://www.ign.com/articles/assassins-creed-black-flag-resynced-director-reveals-why-ubisoft-ditched-edwards-hidden-blade-combat)** · IGN
Fans noticed Edward Kenway's hidden blade combat is missing from the brilliant *Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced*, and director Richard Knight admitted it was cut so the team could prioritize the core dual cutlass combat system. However, the community is so engaged with the remake that one lucky pirate managed to accidentally stumble upon a top-secret Community Treasure Hunt chest containing the original Explorer Outfit before Ubisoft even dropped a clue.

**[Blood of Dawnwalker's 30-Day Timer Won't Actually End Your Game](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-blood-of-dawnwalker-30-day-time-limit)** · IGN
Time limits in RPGs usually stress players out, but Rebel Wolves is clarifying how the 30-day countdown works in *The Blood of Dawnwalker*. Time is treated as a currency for completing main objectives; if you simply ignore the rescue mission and let your in-game family die, the game doesn't end—it just branches into a much darker, equally valid storyline.
