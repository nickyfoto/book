## News & Reviews

**[Capcom’s Onimusha: Way of the Sword is a 10/10 Masterpiece](https://www.ign.com/articles/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-review)
After a 20-year hiatus, Capcom has revived its samurai series with near-universal critical acclaim and a perfect score. Taking cues from *Sekiro’s* rhythmic parry-heavy combat and *God of War's* cinematic action, the game follows a highly expressive Miyamoto Musashi through Kyoto. It is a gorgeous, challenging, and surprisingly funny triumph that runs beautifully on the Steam Deck.

**[Hands-On with Exodus: The Spiritual Successor Mass Effect Fans Have Been Waiting For](https://www.ign.com/articles/exodus-plays-exactly-how-a-new-mass-effect-should-ign-hands-on-preview)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/exodus-plays-exactly-how-a-new-mass-effect-should-ign-hands-on-preview)
Developed by Archetype Entertainment and narrative-led by BioWare legend Drew Karpyshyn, our first look at *Exodus* confirms it is channeling pure classic *Mass Effect* vibes. The game boasts robust third-person combat where you slow time to command companion biotic and tech abilities, combined with a deep "Paladin/Immortal" morality system. Your romance choices are fully open to all genders with tasteful, reserved sex scenes—though sadly, you cannot romance Salt the Awakened Octopus.

**[Lego Skylines Preview: A Cozy, Relaxing City-Builder We Can’t Wait to Play](https://www.ign.com/articles/lego-skylines-hands-on-preview-its-a-cozy-more-relaxing-city-builder)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/lego-skylines-hands-on-preview-its-a-cozy-more-relaxing-city-builder)
*Lego Skylines* strips away the stressful, punishing logistics of traditional city-builders in favor of a gentler, more relaxing aesthetic. While you won’t build brick-by-brick, you’ll construct a living city using pre-fabricated structures designed from real Lego configurations. It strikes a wonderful balance between sandbox creativity and light simulation problem-solving that is perfect for cozy gaming sessions.

**[Halloween: The Game is a Jank-Filled Nightmare in Need of Serious Polish](https://www.ign.com/articles/halloween-the-game-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/halloween-the-game-review)
Illfonic's new asymmetrical survival horror game has plenty of excellent ideas and a blast of a killer mode as Michael Myers, but its early build is currently a buggy, frustrating mess. Between broken animations, hilarious NPC AI, audio bugs, and outright game crashes on PS5, the title is incredibly rough around the edges ahead of its launch patch. Unless you are a diehard Michael Myers fan, you’ll definitely want to wait for updates.

**[Hideki Kamiya Tells Xbox CEO He’s Ready to Make Scalebound](https://www.ign.com/articles/hideki-kamiya-tells-xbox-ceo-hes-ready-to-make-scalebound-when-she-is)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/hideki-kamiya-tells-xbox-ceo-hes-ready-to-make-scalebound-when-she-is)
Bayonetta creator Hideki Kamiya has publicly called on new Xbox CEO Asha Sharma to resurrect the canceled dragon action-RPG *Scalebound*. Although his new studio Clovers is busy working on *Okami 2*, Kamiya insists he is one hundred percent serious about bringing his beloved, canceled Xbox-exclusive back to life if Microsoft wants to make it happen.

**[Bungie Subleasing Bellevue HQ Following Brutal Layoffs](https://www.ign.com/articles/bungie-confirms-plans-to-sublease-parts-of-its-enormous-bellevue-headquarters-following-mass-layoffs)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/bungie-confirms-plans-to-sublease-parts-of-its-enormous-bellevue-headquarters-following-mass-layoffs)
Reflecting the sobering reality of its current size, Bungie is subleasing over 210,000 square feet of its massive Bellevue headquarters. The developer cited its transition to a remote-friendly, "digital-first" model, though the move inevitably follows its third brutal round of layoffs in three years, which cut 292 employees in June.

**[Wonder Woman Casting Rumors Heat Up for Adria Arjona](https://www.ign.com/articles/wonder-woman-casting-rumors-heat-up-as-fans-suspect-james-gunn-secretly-picked-adria-arjona-for-the-role)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/wonder-woman-casting-rumors-heat-up-as-fans-suspect-james-gunn-secretly-picked-adria-arjona-for-the-role)
