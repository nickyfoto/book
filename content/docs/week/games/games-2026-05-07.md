# Gaming News — 2026-05-07

## Top Story
Nintendo shadow-dropped the reveal of a ground-up Switch 2 remake of *Star Fox 64* and detailed a new pricing policy that sets the digital version at $49.99 and physical at $59.99. It's a surprisingly affordable entry for the upcoming console, though fans and even the original franchise creator are already offering mixed reactions to the game's art direction and the fact that this is the fourth time Nintendo has remade or reimagined the exact same story.

## News & Reviews

**Mixtape is a 10/10 Masterpiece (But You Can't Stream It)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/mixtape-review)
Beethoven & Dinosaur's coming-of-age title *Mixtape* just earned a glowing 10/10 review for delivering a brilliantly constructed, nostalgic journey that effortlessly captures teenage friendships and aimless afternoons. However, the game famously lacks a streamer mode because its licensed soundtrack—featuring artists like Devo and Smashing Pumpkins—is deeply intertwined with the narrative and level design. Streamers will have a tough time monetizing VODs, but this is a must-play experience that shouldn't have its musical soul compromised by replacing its iconic tracks.

**Xbox Teases Project Helix and Weighs Exclusivity Changes** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/next-gen-console-project-helix-headlines-first-xbox-game-dev-show-later-today)
Microsoft's first "Xbox Game Dev Update" show focused heavily on getting developers on board with its upcoming next-gen console, codenamed Project Helix, which utilizes a custom AMD SoC and is co-designed to shape the future of rendering and simulation. While we won't get full consumer details until "later this year," new Xbox boss Asha Sharma is reportedly evaluating the brand's approach to exclusive games, treading carefully to win back core fans without losing the massive revenues seen from bringing titles to PlayStation.

**Aliens: Fireteam Elite 2 Announced for Summer** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/aliens-fireteam-elite-2-announced-pc-ps5-xbox)
Cold Iron Studios is bringing us *Aliens: Fireteam Elite 2* on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S this summer, upping the co-op bug-hunting chaos to a four-player squad. Along with full cross-play, the sequel introduces a fully customizable Specialist class that lets you mix abilities, and dedicated Horde mode maps featuring increasingly difficult Xenomorph waves and better rewards.

**Civilization VII Reverses Course on Divisive Age Transitions** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/civilization-vii-update-will-finally-let-players-stay-as-one-civ-through-all-ages-as-take-two-boss-admits-we-got-it-wrong)
Following a mixed reception and dwindling player counts, Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick admitted they "got it wrong" with *Civilization VII*'s mandatory Age Transitions. Arriving May 19, the massive, free "Test of Time" update will finally allow players to stay as one civilization from Antiquity all the way to the Modern Age using new Syncretism and Affirmation mechanics. It's a huge pivot that shows Firaxis is willing to listen and fundamentally tweak the core mechanics to appease their veteran playerbase.

**GTA Online Promises "Exciting" Summer Update Ahead of GTA 6** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/with-gta-6-due-out-in-november-rockstar-teases-exciting-new-update-for-gta-online-this-summer)
With *GTA 6* speeding toward its November 19, 2026 release, Rockstar has teased an "exciting new update" for *GTA Online* this summer. While fans are speculating whether this will be a massive send-off for Los Santos or a bridging event to the new game, Take-Two has historically kept older online games running, so we may see Rockstar continue to support this massive cash cow alongside the new release.

**Paranormal Activity: Threshold Cancelled** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/new-paranormal-activity-game-from-the-mortuary-assistant-developer-has-been-cancelled)
Solo developer Brian Clarke, the mind behind the terrifying *The Mortuary Assistant*, has unfortunately had to cancel his upcoming found-footage horror game *Paranormal Activity: Threshold*. Paramount reportedly wouldn't offer an extension on development time, forcing Clarke to scrap the project rather than release a compromised, subpar experience. It's a massive shame, as his tense, atmospheric style was a perfect fit for the haunted house franchise.
