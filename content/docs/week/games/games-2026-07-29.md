# Gaming News — 2026-07-29

## Top Story
**Battlefield 6 Success Brings EA CEO $38 Million Payday While Devs Pay the Price**
EA CEO Andrew Wilson is pocketing an $8 million raise, bringing his total fiscal 2026 compensation to a staggering $38.6 million thanks to the massive success of *Battlefield 6*. It’s a bitter pill for the industry to swallow, considering EA laid off an undisclosed number of developers across all Battlefield studios just months after the game broke sales records. 

## News & Reviews

**[The Relic: First Guardian Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-relic-first-guardian-review)** · IGN
If you're looking for the next great action RPG to scratch that Soulslike itch, keep looking, because *The Relic: First Guardian* is a shoddy and broken mess. Our review highlights bland character models, a jittery camera, and an overstuffed roster of over 75 bosses where you'll frequently die to cheap mechanics or bugs that completely break the encounters. It barely clears the bar with its punchy weapon skills, but everything else is unacceptable.

**[Halo: Campaign Evolved Launches With Immediate Mod Madness](https://www.ign.com/articles/halo-campaign-evolved-modders-have-already-added-iron-man-carl-johnson-from-gta-and-more)** · IGN
*Halo: Campaign Evolved* is officially out now for PS5, Xbox Series X/S, and PC, bringing a stunning Unreal Engine 5 remake of the original 2001 classic alongside a brand-new space combat story arc. In true PC gaming fashion, modders have wasted absolutely no time ripping into the code; you can already play the campaign as Iron Man or *GTA: San Andreas* protagonist Carl Johnson. 

**[Xbox Investigates Physical Disc DRM Outage](https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-promises-to-investigate-reports-of-service-outage-preventing-players-from-accessing-physical-games)** · IGN
A major service outage recently locked Xbox players out of their own physical game discs, proving that gamers' fears about a digital-only future are completely justified. Xbox Chief Technology Officer Scott Van Vliet promised a full post-incident review, acknowledging that a single point of failure shouldn't ruin your night and that disc-based entitlement checks are meant to work offline by design.

**[Dragon Age Trilogy Remaster Deemed 'Impossible'](https://www.ign.com/articles/biowares-former-dragon-age-boss-says-a-mass-effect-style-remaster-trilogy-is-pretty-much-impossible)** · IGN
Don't hold your breath for a *Mass Effect*-style remastered collection of the original *Dragon Age* trilogy. Former BioWare boss Mark Darrah crushed our hopes by confirming that practically no one left at the studio—or the broader industry—understands the antiquated Aurora Engine that the games were built on.

**[GTA Online Disables In-Game Gambling in Australia](https://www.ign.com/articles/gta-online-in-game-gambling-suddenly-disabled-in-australia)** · IGN
Rockstar has unexpectedly blocked players in Australia from accessing the gambling minigames inside *GTA Online's* Diamond Casino. It's a bizarre move considering the casino update launched seven years ago, though it seemingly follows new local minimum classification guidelines targeting simulated gambling in video games.
