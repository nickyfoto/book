# Gaming News — 2026-07-17

## Top Story
Bethesda Game Studios has laid out a massive roadmap for its core franchises, officially confirming that *Fallout 5* is in preproduction alongside a brand new *Fallout* project from Obsidian Entertainment. The studio is also currently working on highly anticipated remasters for *Fallout 3* and *Fallout: New Vegas*, while *The Elder Scrolls 6* remains the team's primary development focus for the foreseeable future.

## News & Reviews

**Grand Theft Auto 6 Tracking Toward $5.2 Billion Launch** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/gta-6-predicted-to-make-up-to-52-billion-in-sales-by-end-of-launch-week-after-securing-an-estimated-260-million-via-preorders)
*Grand Theft Auto 6* is projected to generate up to $5.2 billion by the end of its launch week, fueled by a staggering estimated $260 million in global digital preorders. The highly anticipated sequel is slated for November 19, 2026, and already boasts what analysts call the strongest and most in-demand preorder campaign ever observed in the industry.

**Castlevania: Belmont’s Curse Preview** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/castlevania-belmonts-curse-might-make-the-long-wait-for-a-new-2d-castlevania-worth-it-ign-preview)
The veteran team behind *Dead Cells* is reviving 2D *Castlevania* with *Belmont's Curse*, a direct follow-up to the events of *Dracula's Curse*. The game features fluid combat, an incredible whip-grappling mechanic, and a fresh protagonist in Trevor Belmont’s daughter, Rose, making it a highly promising return for the legendary franchise.

**God of War Show Paused Due to Injury** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/god-of-war-show-filming-paused-after-kratos-actor-ryan-hurst-tears-bicep-filming-stunt)
Filming for the live-action *God of War* series on Prime Video is temporarily on hold after Kratos actor Ryan Hurst suffered a severe bicep tear during a stunt. The injury is significant enough to require surgery, which could potentially push the show's production back into 2027 depending on his recovery time.

**Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced Hits 3 Million** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/assassins-creed-black-flag-resynced-new-game-plus-confirmed-as-ubisoft-marks-3-million-copies-sold)
Ubisoft is riding high on the success of its *Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced* remake, which just surpassed 3 million copies sold across PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S just a week after launch. To celebrate the milestone, developers confirmed that a highly requested New Game Plus mode is currently in the works to enhance replayability.

**Marathon Game Director Departs Bungie** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/marathon-game-director-joseph-ziegler-is-leaving-bungie)
Joseph Ziegler, the game director for Bungie's upcoming extraction shooter *Marathon*, has officially left the studio to pursue new opportunities. Assistant game director Del Chafe III will step up to lead the project alongside creative director Julia Nardin as the game approaches its Season 3 launch this September.

**Wolverine Trailer Faces Physical Media Backlash** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/marvels-wolverine-trailer-flooded-with-comments-as-physical-disc-backlash-against-playstation-continues)
Insomniac Games dropped an exciting new cinematic trailer for *Marvel's Wolverine*, but the community response was instantly hijacked by angry PlayStation fans. Gamers flooded the comments section to protest Sony's controversial decision to kill off physical disc production for PlayStation consoles by 2028.
