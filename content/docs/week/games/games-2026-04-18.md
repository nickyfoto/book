## News & Reviews

**[Resident Evil Requiem Mercenaries Mode Potentially Leaked]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/resident-evil-requiem-mercenaries-rumors-heat-up-after-alleged-leak)
Dataminers have reportedly uncovered high-octane, ticking-clock music tracks in *Resident Evil Requiem*'s files, heavily suggesting the return of the beloved, arcade-style Mercenaries mode. Capcom has already confirmed a mysterious new "mini-game" is coming to the hit survival horror title this May, adding serious credibility to the rumors.

**[Crimson Desert Adding Difficulty Settings in Hefty Update]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/crimson-deserts-next-major-patch-adds-difficulty-settings-and-inventory-tabs-next-week)
Pearl Abyss is rolling out a massive new patch next week for its breakout hit *Crimson Desert*, which recently crossed an impressive 5 million copies sold in less than a month. Alongside graphical improvements and much-needed inventory tabs, the brutal open-world game will introduce easy, normal, and hard difficulty modes to better accommodate all player skill levels.

**[Magic: The Gathering Details Strixhaven Mechanics]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/magics-secrets-of-strixhaven-study-guide-all-of-the-sets-new-mechanics-explained)
*Magic: The Gathering* is returning to the wizarding school of Strixhaven with a slew of fresh, game-changing mechanics. Key additions include "Prepare," which lets players attach powerful instant or sorcery effects directly to creature cards, alongside distinct strategic keywords like "Repartee" and "Opus" for each of the magical colleges.

**[Antonio Margheriti & The Jungles of Doom Review]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/antonio-margheriti-the-jungles-of-doom-review)
Severin Films has dropped a beautiful new 4K UHD box set of Antonio Margheriti’s “Indiana Jonesploitation” trilogy, a wonderfully schlocky triple-slice of 1980s Italian cinematic knock-offs. While the films suffer from dodgy English captioning and predictable flaws, the spectacular location shoots, Bond-inspired action sequences, and the actors' undeniable charm make this an enthusiastic, fun collection.

**[Kevin Feige Defends Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom Casting]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/kevin-feige-on-casting-robert-downey-jr-as-doctor-doom-its-our-universe)
Marvel boss Kevin Feige has weighed in on the controversial return of Robert Downey Jr. to the MCU as Victor Von Doom, citing the narrative freedom of the multiverse. Feige boldly stated that because Downey played the universe's most iconic hero, it only makes sense for him to portray its most iconic villain in *Avengers: Doomsday*.
