# Gaming News — 2026-06-02

## Top Story
Sony's State of Play dropped a megaton announcement: *God of War Laufey* is the next chapter in the iconic action franchise, but this time, Kratos is sitting out. Instead, players will take control of his late wife, Faye, as she battles her way through the mythological afterlife known as the Everywhen using both brutal agility and magic.

## News & Reviews

**[Insomniac's Wolverine is Brutal, Bloody, and Single-Player Focused](https://www.ign.com/articles/marvels-wolverine-the-big-interview-with-insomniac-on-sonys-unapologetically-violent-ps5-exclusive)**
*Marvel's Wolverine* finally got a massive gameplay reveal at State of Play, showcasing an "unapologetically violent," M-rated linear action game. Insomniac confirmed it won't be an open-world sandbox like *Spider-Man*, but a globe-hopping, story-driven adventure featuring co-op finishers with Jean Grey and an uneasy competitive alliance with Sabretooth. For players who prefer less carnage, the studio has included an accessibility toggle to turn off the gore entirely.

**[Control Resonant Secures a September Release at a Refreshing Price](https://www.ign.com/articles/control-resonant-release-date-announced-in-new-trailer-costs-60)**
Remedy Entertainment's highly anticipated sequel, *Control Resonant*, arrives on September 24 for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC. You'll play as Jesse Faden's tortured brother, Dylan, fighting supernatural threats that have consumed Manhattan. In a rare win for our wallets, the standard edition will cost $59.99—bucking the industry trend of $70 AAA releases.

**[Rayman Legends Retold Reimagines a Platforming Masterpiece](https://www.ign.com/articles/rayman-legends-retold-reimagines-ubisofts-classic-platformer-with-3d-visuals-includes-rayman-origins-enhanced)**
Ubisoft is bringing back one of the best 2D platformers of the last decade, fully rebuilt in the Snowdrop engine with stunning 3D visual environments while keeping the tight 2D action intact. *Rayman Legends Retold* drops on October 1 for $39.99, featuring new 3D flight segments, returning local co-op, fully voiced cutscenes, and brand-new musical levels.

**[Star Fox Remake on Switch 2 Plays Like 'A New Hope' With Animals](https://www.ign.com/articles/star-fox-hands-on-preview-like-a-new-hope-for-the-franchise)**
The upcoming *Star Fox* remake for the Switch 2 isn't just a fresh coat of paint; it's a massive, cinematic overhaul of the beloved *Star Fox 64*. With expanded voice acting, crisp 60 fps gameplay during missions, and a reworked script that heavily leans into the crew's camaraderie and rivalry, it's shaping up to be the definitive Lylat Wars experience when it launches on June 25.

**[Until Dawn 2 Haunts the PS5 Next Year](https://www.ign.com/articles/until-dawn-2-revealed-at-playstation-state-of-play)**
Firesprite has officially revealed *Until Dawn 2*, shifting the interactive horror franchise from a snowy mountain cabin to a tropical setting. Slated for a 2027 release, the sequel follows a crew of fake paranormal investigators who stumble into real horrors, complete with the return of the life-or-death Butterfly Effect choices.

**[Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis Delayed to 2027](https://www.ign.com/articles/it-looks-like-tomb-raider-legacy-of-atlantis-is-delayed-to-2027)**
Lara Croft's acrobatics will have to wait a little longer, as Amazon listings leaked a February 10, 2027 release date for *Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis*. While no official reason was given, dodging the massive launch window of *GTA 6* late this year makes a lot of commercial sense for the upcoming remake of the 1996 classic.
