# Gaming News — 2026-06-23

## Top Story
Valve is officially defending the hefty $1,049 starting price tag for its newly launched Steam Machine console, flat-out refusing to sell the hardware at a loss like traditional console manufacturers. This bold move comes amid rising component costs and signals Valve's steadfast commitment to an open PC gaming ecosystem, rather than locking players into a closed platform to recoup hardware losses.

## News & Reviews

**Fallout Season 3 Goes to Snowy Colorado** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/fallout-season-3-todd-howard-teases-new-things-never-seen-before-in-the-games)
Bethesda's Todd Howard confirmed that the highly anticipated third season of the *Fallout* TV series will explore post-apocalyptic Colorado, a snowy region never before seen in the mainline video games. With filming kicking off this month in Los Angeles, fans can expect significant and surprising expansions to the official franchise canon.

**Lords of the Fallen 2 Delayed to 2027** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/lords-of-the-fallen-2-delayed-to-2027)
CI Games has officially pushed *Lords of the Fallen 2* out of its Fall 2026 release window, delaying the dark fantasy RPG to early 2027. The delay gives the development team time to integrate feedback from Souls-like veterans and strategically helps the game dodge a notoriously crowded holiday release calendar.

**Bungie Rejected a Destiny Dating Sim** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/bungie-developers-repeatedly-pitched-a-destiny-dating-sim-but-leadership-rejected-it)
In a wild piece of trivia, former Bungie developers revealed they repeatedly pitched a *Destiny* dating simulator inspired by *Dream Daddy*. Despite a dedicated pitch deck with projected metrics from romance game designers, leadership gave a "very hard no," feeling the player base wasn't interested in romance or silliness.

**Destiny 2's Heartbreaking End** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/warframe-dev-says-no-one-is-celebrating-as-destiny-2-support-comes-to-a-heartbreaking-end)
The gaming community is still reeling after Sony pulled the plug on *Destiny 2* support, an abrupt end that even caught much of Bungie's staff by surprise. Rival developers at Digital Extremes expressed their heartbreak over the news, noting that the live-service space is much healthier with healthy competition and that "no one is celebrating" the loss of such a towering franchise.

**Marathon Unveils New PvE Mode** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/marathon-confirms-new-pve-mode-release-date-season-3-begins-september-2026)
Looking to win over skeptical players, Bungie's extraction shooter *Marathon* is launching an experimental new PvE mode called "Vault Breaker" on July 21st. Players will infiltrate the Cryo Archive to extract "Vault Data" for unique progression upgrades, giving players a lower-risk way to explore raid-like endgame maps ahead of Season 3's launch on September 22nd.

**Star Wars: Galactic Racer Preview** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/star-wars-galactic-racer-the-first-preview)
Lucasfilm's collaboration with *Burnout* veterans at Fuse Games is shaping up to be an absolute blast. *Star Wars: Galactic Racer* perfectly combines lightning-fast arcade racing, aggressive takedowns, and an unforgiving roguelite loop, making it the brilliant spiritual successor to *Episode 1: Racer* we've been waiting for.
