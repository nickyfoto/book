# Gaming News — 2026-06-11

## Top Story
Microsoft's Xbox division is facing a massive "reset" under newly installed CEO Asha Sharma, who just warned staff of hard truths and impending layoffs as the brand struggles with trailing hardware console sales. While new Chief Strategy Officer Matthew Ball remains a "strategic optimist" about the platform's future, players should brace for Xbox to double down on major core franchises like *Halo* and *Gears of War* while smaller studios potentially face the chopping block in the coming months.

**[Star Trek: Shadow Frontier — Paramount Says 'The Goal Is for Bloober to Make the Best Star Trek Game of All Time’](https://www.ign.com/articles/star-trek-shadow-frontier-paramount-says-the-goal-is-for-bloober-to-make-the-best-star-trek-game-of-all-time)** · IGN

**[1666: Amsterdam Developer Admits Using AI Assets and Apologizes, Says They’ll Now Be Replaced](https://www.ign.com/articles/1666-amsterdam-developer-admits-using-ai-assets-and-apologizes-says-theyll-now-be-replaced)** · IGN
Panache Digital Games faced swift and heavy backlash after observant players spotted AI-generated art in the newly released playable prologue of *1666: Amsterdam*. The studio has publicly apologized for the oversight, promising that human-made versions will replace the AI assets in an upcoming update and assuring players that the final 1.0 release will be completely free of AI generation.

**[Stranger Than Heaven Interview: Director Says Tupac is a Good Idea and Confirms Yakuza Story Connections](https://www.ign.com/articles/stranger-than-heaven-interview-director-says-tupac-is-a-good-idea-and-confirms-yakuza-story-connections)** · IGN
RGG Studio’s upcoming spin-off, *Stranger Than Heaven*, is making waves for casting the likeness of the late legendary rapper Tupac Shakur to star alongside Snoop Dogg's character in early 20th century Japan. Executive Director Masayoshi Yokoyama aggressively defended the controversial decision, confirming they secured estate approval to honor the rapper's legacy, and emphasized that real voice actors—not AI—will respectfully bring the character to life.

**[Crimson Desert Hits New Huge Sales Milestone Less Than 3 Months After Launch](https://www.ign.com/articles/crimson-desert-hits-new-huge-sales-milestone-less-than-3-months-after-launch)** · IGN
Pearl Abyss's massive open-world action RPG, *Crimson Desert*, has shattered expectations by selling over 6 million copies in just 83 days. Over 80% of these sales were driven by North American and European markets, cementing the South Korean developer's status as a global powerhouse as they prepare a highly anticipated summer DLC roadmap.

**[Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis Makes a 30-Year-Old Adventure Feel Brand New](https://www.ign.com/articles/tomb-raider-legacy-of-atlantis-the-first-preview)** · IGN
Arriving on February 12, 2027, the *Legacy of Atlantis* remake modernizes Lara Croft's 1996 classic debut with beautifully fluid, acrobatic movement and an innovative Spider-Man-style grapple gadget. The game perfectly bridges the gap between old-school environmental exploration and modern combat, complete with slick bullet-time mechanics and a terrifyingly cinematic T-Rex chase sequence.

**[EA Sports College Football 27 Preview: Defense Wins Championships](https://www.ign.com/articles/ea-sports-college-football-27-preview)** · IGN
The upcoming *College Football 27* is completely revamping defensive gameplay with a predictive "Look for Work" system, smart zones, and incredibly realistic hand-fighting between receivers and cornerbacks. Hardcore fans will also be thrilled that Dynasty Mode is receiving a massive overhaul, introducing dynamic AD Expectations and a strategic points system for facility upgrades and NIL deals to ensure building your legacy is harder than ever.
