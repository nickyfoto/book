# Gaming News — 2026-07-28

## Top Story
Double Fine Productions, the beloved studio behind *Psychonauts*, has tragically laid off over 25% of its workforce just weeks after separating from Xbox. The 90-person team was forced to downsize in order to financially sustain its newly regained independent status, highlighting the harsh realities of the current industry landscape even for veteran development houses.

## News & Reviews

**[After 15 Years, Resident Evil 5 Has Finally Been Dethroned as The Series’ Best-Selling Game](https://www.ign.com/articles/after-15-years-resident-evil-5-has-finally-been-dethroned-as-the-series-best-selling-game)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/after-15-years-resident-evil-5-has-finally-been-dethroned-as-the-series-best-selling-game)
Capcom's spectacular *Resident Evil 2* remake has officially overtaken *Resident Evil 5* to become the survival horror franchise's top-selling game of all time, shifting an impressive 19.75 million copies. The remake's ongoing momentum, bolstered by the success of subsequent modern releases like the *Resident Evil 4* remake and *Resident Evil Requiem*, proves that reimagining classic horror titles with contemporary gameplay is a massively lucrative long-term strategy. 

**[The Vast Majority of Capcom’s 2026 Game Sales Have Been Digital](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-vast-majority-of-capcoms-2026-game-sales-have-been-digital)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-vast-majority-of-capcoms-2026-game-sales-have-been-digital)
In a statistic that is making physical media collectors sweat, Capcom revealed that a staggering 93.3% of its Q1 2026 game sales were digital. While PC accounts for the bulk of this figure at 60.4%, the overwhelmingly digital tilt adds fuel to the fire regarding Sony's controversial plan to completely cease PlayStation physical disc production by January 2028.

**[Square Enix Dev Explains the Reality of Final Fantasy 6, 8, and 9 Remakes](https://www.ign.com/articles/square-enix-dev-yoshi-p-says-final-fantasy-6-8-and-9-remakes-would-probably-have-to-be-released-in-four-or-even-five-installments)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/square-enix-dev-yoshi-p-says-final-fantasy-6-8-and-9-remakes-would-probably-have-to-be-released-in-four-or-even-five-installments)
If you're holding out hope for a *Final Fantasy 7*-style treatment for *Final Fantasy 6*, you might need to adjust your expectations. Director-producer Naoki Yoshida stated that remaking massive classic titles like *FF6*, *8*, or *9* with modern fidelity would require breaking them into four or even five separate, full-scale installments. This echoes previous developer sentiments that a true *FF6* remake could take upwards of 20 years to complete due to its staggering narrative depth and massive cast.
