# Gaming News — 2026-05-11

## Top Story
Nintendo officially announced a massive $50 price hike for the upcoming Switch 2, bringing the console to $499.99 in the U.S. amid rising component costs. To soften the blow and maintain momentum with frustrated investors, Nintendo president Shuntaro Furukawa confirmed multiple unannounced Switch 2 games are slated to be revealed and released in the second half of this fiscal year.

## News & Reviews

**[Forza Horizon 6 Suffers Disastrous Leak as Steam Preload Files Are Made Available Without Encryption](https://www.ign.com/articles/forza-horizon-6-suffers-disastrous-leak-as-steam-preload-files-are-made-available-without-encryption)** 
Microsoft dropped the ball big time, accidentally pushing an unencrypted Steam preload for *Forza Horizon 6* that leaked 155GB of game files online. This massive blunder resulted in the hotly anticipated racer hitting piracy and streaming sites well ahead of its May 15 early access date.

**[Stellar Blade 2 Won’t Be Published by Sony, Potentially Hinting at Multiplatform Launch](https://www.ign.com/articles/stellar-blade-2-wont-be-published-by-sony-potentially-hinting-at-multiplatform-launch)** 
Shift Up is taking the reins for *Stellar Blade 2*, opting to self-publish the sequel instead of relying on PlayStation. This move strongly hints at a multiplatform launch for the highly anticipated follow-up, aiming to maximize sales and lean into their newfound first-party service model.

**[Xbox Game Pass for Discord Nitro Subscribers Officially Announced](https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-game-pass-for-discord-nitro-subscribers-officially-announced)** 
Discord and Xbox officially revealed a "starter edition" of Xbox Game Pass bundled at no extra cost for Discord Nitro subscribers. The tier offers 10 hours of cloud gaming and a rotating library of over 50 classic games, including heavy hitters like *Doom Eternal*, *Fallout 4*, and *Stardew Valley*.

**[007 First Light Will Run at 60fps on PS5 Pro](https://www.ign.com/articles/007-first-light-will-run-at-60fps-on-ps5-pro)** 
IO Interactive is smoothing out *007 First Light*'s framerate issues just in time for its May 27 launch. The slick James Bond shooter is now confirmed to run at a buttery 60fps on the PS5 Pro, with a similar performance mode also available for the base PS5.

**[Crimson Desert Adds Surprise Claw Machine Mini-Game and Lets Pet Dogs Attack Enemies as Part of Update 1.06.00](https://www.ign.com/articles/crimson-desert-adds-surprise-claw-machine-mini-game-and-lets-pet-dogs-attack-enemies-as-part-of-update-10600)** 
Pearl Abyss continues its wild streak of weekly updates for *Crimson Desert* with patch 1.06.00. Alongside much-needed endgame resource extraction features, the patch randomly drops in a fully functional claw machine mini-game and finally allows you to command pet dogs to attack your enemies.

**[Directive 8020 Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/directive-8020-review)** 
Supermassive Games kicks off the second season of *The Dark Pictures Anthology* with *Directive 8020*, an excellent space horror adventure that channels *Alien* and *The Thing*. It's a solid 10-hour experience with sharp writing, branching paths, and a new active stealth system, earning it high praise for narrative horror fans despite some minor audio issues.
