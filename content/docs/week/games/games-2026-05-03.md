# Gaming News — 2026-05-03

## Top Story
Sony is preparing to pay out $7.85 million in refunds to PlayStation gamers following preliminary approval of an antitrust class-action lawsuit. If you bought digital versions of games via the PlayStation Store between April 2019 and December 2023 that were previously available as retail vouchers, you may be automatically eligible to receive PSN store credit.

Bungie isn't planning on abandoning its critically acclaimed new extraction shooter anytime soon, with creative director Julia Nardin confirming the team has a roadmap to evolve *Marathon's* narrative over "the next few years". Despite the game's brutal 9/10 launch mechanics, Bungie stresses that every future season will act as a welcoming entry point so new runners can jump into the mysteries of Tau Ceti IV at any time.
