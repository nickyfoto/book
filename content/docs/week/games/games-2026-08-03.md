# Gaming News — 2026-08-03

## Top Story
Microsoft has officially increased the price of Xbox Series X/S consoles by more than a third in Europe and the UK, leaving fans stunned at the massive hikes. The Xbox Series X disc model has spiked by up to 48% to an eye-watering £669.99/€799.99, which Microsoft blames on soaring component and memory costs.

**[Microsoft Leaks Hint at Xbox 360 Emulation on PC and Handhelds](https://www.ign.com/articles/microsoft-reportedly-working-to-bring-xbox-360-games-to-pc-and-project-helix)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/microsoft-reportedly-working-to-bring-xbox-360-games-to-pc-and-project-helix)
A leaked Microsoft document reveals plans to allow developers to bring classic Xbox 360 titles to PC, handhelds, and the upcoming "Project Helix" hardware. The feature will reportedly rely on Microsoft's Xbox PC emulator so developers won't have to perform any additional engineering, though the gradual rollout isn't expected until between 2027 and 2028. This is an incredibly exciting development for backwards compatibility and retro preservation, especially as the industry continues to careen toward an all-digital future.

**[Nintendo Direct Broadcast Announced for Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave](https://www.ign.com/articles/nintendo-direct-announced-focused-on-fire-emblem-fortunes-weave)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/nintendo-direct-announced-focused-on-fire-emblem-fortunes-weave)
Nintendo has announced a dedicated 20-minute Direct broadcast to air on Tuesday, August 4, delivering new details on its highly-anticipated RPG, *Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave*. Set to launch on September 17, 2026, as a Switch 2 exclusive, the tactical RPG features turn-based combat centered around a championship called the Heroic Games. This presentation marks the first major gameplay deep dive for the next-gen title, giving fans an exciting look at what is easily one of the console's most important upcoming releases.

**[Shift Up CEO Defends Stellar Blade Sequel K-Pop Video from AI Backlash](https://www.ign.com/articles/shift-up-ceo-defends-stellar-blade-blood-rain-ai-music-video-against-player-backlash)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/shift-up-ceo-defends-stellar-blade-blood-rain-ai-music-video-against-player-backlash)
Shift Up CEO Kim Hyung Tae has responded to intense player backlash over an AI-generated promotional music video starring Evie, the protagonist of the upcoming *Stellar Blade* sequel, *Blood Rain*. Kim defended the video by arguing that the AI merely rendered and presented the character based on existing, human-made assets rather than creating her from scratch. However, fans remain deeply frustrated, pointing out that the "AI slop" distorts Evie's appearance and actively diminishes the quality of the team's exceptional hand-crafted artistry.

**[Ex-Designer Reveals Rockstar Killed Midnight Club 5 in Favor of GTA](https://www.ign.com/articles/rockstar-games-killed-midnight-club-5-because-it-wasnt-as-big-as-gta-ex-designer-claims)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/rockstar-games-killed-midnight-club-5-because-it-wasnt-as-big-as-gta-ex-designer-claims)
Former Rockstar senior designer Kris Roberts revealed that a fifth *Midnight Club* game, which had prototyped locations like London, was canceled in early development because it wasn't as massive as *Grand Theft Auto*. Roberts explained that the racing series was treated like a "red-headed stepchild" by the New York office, causing remaining staff to move onto projects like *Red Dead Redemption*. This internal shift effectively killed the dormant franchise, leaving Rockstar locked in a GTA-and-Red-Dead-only release loop for the past fifteen years.

**[Paralyzed Rust Player Sues Police for $176 Million After Tragic Swatting Incident](https://www.ign.com/articles/rust-fan-sues-police-after-swatting-incident-leaves-him-paralyzed)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/rust-fan-sues-police-after-swatting-incident-leaves-him-paralyzed)
Axeel Melendez, a 23-year-old *Rust* player who was shot and paralyzed by police during a swatting incident, is suing law enforcement for $176 million. Despite Melendez calling 911 two days prior to warn officers about potential swatting threats from a malicious hacker, deputies still stormed his bedroom window and opened fire when they mistook his legally owned shotgun for a threat. The tragic, life-altering case highlights the horrific and growing dangers of online gaming harassment and swatting.
