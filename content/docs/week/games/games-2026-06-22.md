**[Zelda Fan Ditches Ocarina of Time Unreal Engine Remake After a Decade of Work](https://www.ign.com/articles/zelda-fan-ditches-ocarina-of-time-unreal-engine-remake-after-a-decade-of-work)** · IGN

The creator cited Nintendo's recently announced official Switch 2 remake of the classic as the primary reason, fearing inevitable legal action from Nintendo's notorious ninjas.

**[Bethesda Boss Todd Howard Saw Marvel’s Blade in May, Says Arkane 'Doing a Really, Really Great Job’](https://www.ign.com/articles/bethesda-boss-todd-howard-saw-marvels-blade-in-may-says-arkane-doing-a-really-really-great-job)** · IGN
