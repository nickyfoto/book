# Gaming News — 2026-06-03

## Top Story
The internet is still buzzing over Sony’s surprise State of Play reveal of *God of War Laufey*, a daring new entry that replaces Kratos with his late wife Faye. By jumping into a timeline that runs parallel to the 2018 reboot, Santa Monica Studio is taking a massive, exciting risk, ditching the heavy Leviathan Axe combat for Faye's agile, magic-infused swordplay through the afterlife.

## News & Reviews

**[Stuntman Series Returns With Hollywood Blockbusters](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-stuntman-series-returns-after-almost-20-years-with-fast-furious-back-to-the-future-and-more)** · IGN
Saber Interactive is finally reviving the cult classic PS2 driving franchise with *Stuntman: Hollywood*, bringing high-octane vehicular action back to PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC. The ultimate hook this time around is a partnership with Universal Pictures, letting you tear up movie sets with fully licensed vehicles like K.I.T.T. from *Knight Rider* or the iconic time machine from *Back to the Future*. It's a massive win for racing fans who have waited nearly twenty years for a proper comeback.

**[Marvel's Wolverine Skips Collector's Edition Amid Release Date Hype](https://www.ign.com/articles/from-19-inches-of-venom-to-nothing-theres-no-marvels-wolverine-collectors-edition-and-insomniac-fans-dont-know-why)** · IGN
Insomniac Games has confirmed that *Marvel's Wolverine*, dropping on September 15, 2026, won't be getting a lavish physical Collector’s Edition. While fans are understandably bummed they won’t be dropping cash on a gnarly Logan statue like they did with *Spider-Man 2*'s 19-inch Venom, the game itself is shaping up to be a glorious, unapologetically violent adventure that trades an open-world checklist for a tightly paced, *Uncharted*-style structure. 

**[Control Resonant Release Date Locks in a Stacked September—With a Catch](https://www.ign.com/articles/control-resonant-has-a-2-day-early-access-through-the-digital-deluxe-edition-but-only-on-ps5)** · IGN
Remedy's mind-bending sequel *Control Resonant* officially launches on September 24, throwing itself right into a brutal release window packed with heavy hitters. However, there's some ugly platform exclusivity involved: only PS5 players who buy the Digital Deluxe Edition will get a 48-hour early access period, leaving PC and Xbox fans entirely in the dark. It's exactly the kind of anti-consumer FOMO cash-grab that is frustrating fans and alienating players who are already selective with their wallets.

**[Phantom Blade Zero Delays Launch to Dodge the September Bloodbath](https://www.ign.com/articles/phantom-blade-zero-delay-moves-launch-to-late-october-2026)** · IGN
Developer S-Game has wisely chosen to delay its highly anticipated action-RPG *Phantom Blade Zero* from September 9 to October 29, 2026. With massive titles like *Wolverine* and *Control Resonant* turning September into an absolute battle royale to beat *GTA 6*'s looming November launch, this 50-day breather gives the team time for critical visual and environmental polish without getting crushed by the competition.

**[Persona 5 Royal Headlines June Xbox Game Pass Lineup](https://www.ign.com/articles/persona-5-royal-headlines-xbox-game-pass-june-2026-wave-1)** · IGN
Microsoft is beefing up its subscription service this month, with the legendary RPG *Persona 5 Royal* hitting Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass on June 9. Wave 1 of June's lineup also includes the boxing title *Undisputed* and the floating island survival game *Solarpunk*, giving gamers plenty of variety before the upcoming Xbox Games Showcase drops its major announcements.

**[PS2 Classics Are Finally Returning to PlayStation Plus](https://www.ign.com/articles/sony-confirms-ps2-games-coming-to-the-playstation-plus-classics-catalog-over-the-next-few-months)** · IGN
Sony is injecting some genuine nostalgia into the PlayStation Plus Premium tier this summer by adding three beloved PS2 bangers to the vault. Players can dive into the quirky cult-classic rhythm game *Gitaroo Man* starting June 16, followed by Midway's psychic shooter *Psi-Ops: The Mindgate Conspiracy* on July 28, and Capcom’s demon-slashing *Onimusha: Dawn of Dreams* on August 25.
