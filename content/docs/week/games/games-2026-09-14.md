# Gaming News — 2026-09-14

## Top Story
Valve has officially launched its standalone wireless VR headset, the **[Steam Frame](https://www.ign.com/articles/steam-frame-review)**, starting at \$1,059 for the 256GB base model. Powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chip running native ARM SteamOS via Proton, the high-end hardware prioritizes 6GHz wireless PCVR streaming despite price increases driven by the global memory crisis.

## News & Reviews

**[Steam Frame Review: Valve's Premium Wireless VR Headset Takes Flight](https://www.ign.com/articles/steam-frame-review)** · IGN
Valve's Steam Frame delivers an exceptionally light 440g design with rear-mounted battery balance and rock-solid 6GHz wireless PCVR streaming. While standalone gaming performance on the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 processor is modest and drains the 21.6Whr battery in under two hours, IGN praised the device as a stellar virtual monitor replacement for PC gamers. At \$1,059, the steep price tag reflects ongoing RAM supply shortages, but its comfort and open-platform versatility set an impressive benchmark for VR enthusiasts.

**[Blizzard Addresses Diablo 4 Expansion Future Following Diablo 5 Reveal](https://www.ign.com/articles/blizzard-insists-there-are-many-reasons-to-keep-playing-diablo-4-as-fans-debate-end-of-expansions-and-diablo-5-in-2029)** · IGN
Following BlizzCon's reveal of Diablo 5 for a 2029 release, Blizzard confirmed no further expansions will be developed for Diablo 4. The news triggered community backlash over previously promised annual expansions, though game directors insist ongoing seasonal content and the upcoming Amazon class will keep the game vibrant. Development for Diablo 5 is now in full production, leaving Diablo 4 focused primarily on seasonal storytelling in the interim.

**[Fortnite Kingdom Hearts Collaboration Lands Sora Skin This Week](https://www.ign.com/articles/fortnite-kingdom-hearts-collaboration-including-sora-skin-lands-this-week)** · IGN
Epic Games officially confirmed that Kingdom Hearts is joining Fortnite, debuting keyblade wielder Sora as an Outfit alongside social media teases for Riku, Kairi, and Roxas. The crossover arrives as part of Season Override, which has already added major gaming icons like Sonic, Crash Bandicoot, and Mega Man. The new cosmetics drop later this week, offering Disney and Square Enix fans plenty of fan service while Kingdom Hearts 4 continues development.

**[Stephen Root Confirms Grand Theft Auto 6 Role Ahead of Rockstar Announcement](https://www.ign.com/articles/first-major-cast-member-confirms-appearance-in-gta-6-seemingly-going-off-rockstars-script)** · IGN
Character actor Stephen Root revealed he voices drug smuggler Brian Heder in Grand Theft Auto 6, becoming the first major cast member to publicly discuss his role. Root noted that fans correctly identified his distinct voice from Trailer 2, where his character offers protagonist Jason a place to stay in exchange for favors. The unexpected slip gives fans a rare glimpse into Rockstar's tightly guarded cast list ahead of the game's November 19, 2026 launch.

**[Lies of P Sequel Name "Wonders of O" Revealed in Trademark Filing](https://www.ign.com/articles/lies-of-p-trademark-appears-to-spell-out-sequels-name)** · IGN
Developer Neowiz has registered a trademark for "Wonders of O," heavily pointing to the official title of the Lies of P follow-up. Building on the original game's post-credits tease, fans expect "O" to represent The Wizard of Oz, sending protagonist Dorothy into a dark, corrupted Soulslike environment. With the original game and its Overture expansion earning widespread praise, expectations are high for Neowiz's next twisted fairy tale.

**[Netflix Announces Crazy Taxi Movie, Sonic Animated Series, and RGG Studio Project](https://www.ign.com/articles/live-action-crazy-taxi-movie-new-sonic-animated-series-and-stranger-than-heaven-adaptation-heading-to-netflix)** · IGN
Netflix and Sega announced three new adaptations: a live-action comedy Crazy Taxi movie, an upper pre-school Sonic animated series, and a live-action crime drama based on RGG Studio's upcoming Stranger Than Heaven. The Crazy Taxi film is being written by The Naked Gun reboot writers Dan Gregor and Doug Mand, with the Sonic film producers on board to produce. This major partnership underscores Netflix's aggressive push into gaming adaptations alongside projects for Diablo, Gears of War, and Assassin's Creed.

**[Bungie Delays Marathon Update and Shifts Focus Toward Permanent PvE Content](https://www.ign.com/articles/marathon-delays-next-major-update-puts-focus-on-pve-and-moves-away-from-seasonal-content-amidst-bungie-struggles)** · IGN
Bungie has pushed Marathon's next major update to December 8 while moving away from traditional seasonal models to focus on permanent PvE modes and social spaces. Following player retention struggles after launch and widespread internal layoffs, the studio is overhauling progression systems to revitalize the shooter. The course correction represents a critical effort by Bungie to rebuild trust with action fans.
