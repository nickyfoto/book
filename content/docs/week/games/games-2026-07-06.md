# Gaming News — 2026-07-06

## Top Story
Microsoft is initiating the most significant restructure in Xbox history, laying off 3,200 employees and spinning off major studios like Double Fine and Compulsion Games. This desperate "reset" from CEO Asha Sharma reflects an incredibly unhealthy gaming business that's lost its way and is abandoning its diverse studio portfolio to double down on massive, established franchises.

## News & Reviews

**[The Xbox Restructuring Bloodbath](https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-boss-asha-sharma-announces-3200-layoffs-including-1600-today-with-4-studios-leaving-for-new-management-read-the-email-to-staff-in-full)** · IGN
Microsoft is shedding one-fifth of its gaming workforce and drastically changing the fate of multiple first-party studios to stop its financial bleeding. While Double Fine and Compulsion Games will regain their independence, Undead Labs and Ninja Theory are being sold to undisclosed buyers to finish *State of Decay 3* and *Senua*. Sadly, Arkane Lyon's future is in severe jeopardy, leaving *Marvel's Blade* in limbo, and Bethesda is narrowing its focus almost entirely to mega-franchises like *Fallout* and *The Elder Scrolls*, effectively putting *Starfield* on ice.

**[Over 115,000 Gamers Petition PlayStation to Keep Discs](https://www.ign.com/articles/dont-kill-the-disc-independent-retailer-launches-petition-calling-on-playstation-to-keep-physical-games-gets-over-115000-signatures)** · IGN
Sony's heavily criticized decision to kill off physical disc production for PlayStation consoles by 2028 has sparked a massive consumer backlash. An independent retailer's petition has already garnered over 115,000 signatures, arguing that an all-digital future ruins game preservation and destroys thousands of retail jobs. Despite the uproar, industry analysts believe Sony is unlikely to reverse course since digital storefronts provide a drastically higher profit margin for the company.

**[Nintendo Switch 2 Gets Replaceable Batteries in Europe, Switch 1 Retires](https://www.ign.com/articles/new-nintendo-switch-2-announced-for-europe-this-fall-will-be-slightly-heavier-but-with-user-replaceable-batteries)** · IGN
Due to impending European Union legislation, Nintendo is updating the Switch 2 and its controllers to feature user-replaceable batteries starting this fall. Because these strict new regulations take effect in mid-February 2027, Nintendo has opted to completely withdraw the original Switch 1 family of consoles from the European market rather than update them. This marks the definitive end of an era for the original Switch in Europe, though it should still be widely available through the end of 2026.

**[Palworld 1.0 Finally Gets a Launch Time](https://www.ign.com/articles/palworld-dev-offers-approximate-release-time-for-10-update-says-players-do-not-need-to-wipe-their-data-but-they-really-should)** · IGN
Pocketpair's monster-catching phenomenon is graduating from Early Access with its massive 1.0 update launching around July 10 at 12:30 PM Japan time, which translates to July 9 in the US. The developers teased a staggering 27 pages of patch notes, heavily overhauling the game's mechanics and adding fresh content. While a data wipe isn't strictly mandatory, the developers highly recommend that returning tamers start a fresh character to get the best possible experience.

**[Meccha Chameleon Achieves 15 Million Sales](https://www.ign.com/articles/meccha-chameleon-hits-an-astonishing-15-million-sales-teases-collaboration-with-famous-japanese-star-and-fans-have-theories-for-who-it-might-be)** · IGN
The indie paint-and-seek multiplayer sensation *Meccha Chameleon* just crossed a staggering 15 million copies sold on Steam less than a month after its release. Built by a two-person team in just two months with zero marketing, the game has been a massive hit with content creators. The developers are now teasing an upcoming collaboration with a "famous Japanese star," leaving fans enthusiastically speculating on who the mystery guest will be.

**[Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered Gets a True Physical Switch 2 Release](https://www.ign.com/articles/elder-scrolls-iv-oblivion-remastered-nintendo-switch-2-where-to-buy)** · IGN
In a refreshing win for physical media advocates, *The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered* is dropping on the Switch 2 with the entire base game on an actual physical cartridge rather than a download code. Preorders for the $59.99 Physical Deluxe Edition are currently live, giving players a modernized version of the 2006 classic packed with the *Shivering Isles* and *Knights of the Nine* expansions. Grab it quickly, as major retailers like Best Buy and Amazon are already selling out of their allocations.
