# Gaming News — 2026-06-04

## Top Story
The critically acclaimed *Elden Ring* is officially heading to the Nintendo Switch 2 on August 28, 2026, alongside a new DLC pack for other platforms. The new *Tarnished Edition* will bundle the base game, the *Shadow of the Erdtree* expansion, new weapons, and the ability to customize Torrent with armor skins. Given the game's staggering 30 million copies sold worldwide, being able to take FromSoftware's dark fantasy masterpiece on the go is undeniably huge news for the upcoming console.

**[Persona 6 Images Leak, Only to Be Removed by Sega](https://www.ign.com/articles/persona-6-images-leak-only-to-be-removed-by-sega)** · IGN
Grainy concept art featuring new characters and a green "P6" logo has been aggressively scrubbed from the internet via copyright DMCA takedowns by Sega. **[Helldivers 2 Dev Confirms Roguelite Systems, Called Planet Warfronts, as Part of Big Changes Coming to the Game](https://www.ign.com/articles/helldivers-2-dev-confirms-roguelite-systems-called-planet-warfronts-as-part-of-big-changes-coming-to-the-game)** · IGN
Arrowhead is rolling out massive dynamic progression changes to *Helldivers 2*, highlighted by a new roguelite "Planet Warfronts" system that clusters missions into strategic zones like Defend or Behind Enemy Lines. The developers are also introducing 1-to-3-week Galactic War Campaigns and overhauling ship upgrades with brand-new, customizable vessels, ensuring the chaotic spread of democracy is deeper than ever.

**[PlayStation Promises 'Many More Stories to Tell’ With Kratos in Future, After God of War Laufey](https://www.ign.com/articles/playstation-promises-many-more-stories-to-tell-with-kratos-in-future-after-god-of-war-laufey)** · IGN
Following a bizarre social media spat with Domino's Pizza UK over *God of War Laufey* starring Faye instead of Kratos, Sony Santa Monica has stepped in to reassure the community. Studio head Cory Barlog and director Ariel Lawrence confirmed that Kratos remains the figurehead of the franchise, promising "many more stories to tell" featuring the iconic god killer and noting that *Laufey* is meant to be an expansion of the universe, not a departure. 

**[IO Interactive Won’t Self-Publish Future James Bond Games; Amazon Will Have More Control](https://www.ign.com/articles/io-interactive-wont-self-publish-future-james-bond-games-amazon-will-have-more-control)** · IGN
After *007 First Light* achieved record-breaking success with 1.5 million copies sold in its first 24 hours and $150 million in generated revenue, IO Interactive confirmed it will not self-publish its sequels. Amazon Game Studios, which financially backs the franchise following its acquisition of MGM, will now handle publishing on future *James Bond* titles and may have a larger creative say to create synergy with upcoming films and TV shows.

**[Valve Reaffirms Steam Machine Summer Launch](https://www.ign.com/articles/valve-reaffirms-steam-machine-summer-launch)** · IGN
Despite hardware market woes and previous delays, Valve has confirmed its new Steam Machine and Steam Frame gaming PCs are still on track for a release this summer. The company is currently prepping its Verified program for developers to ensure a smooth UI path for the new hardware, though PC gamers should brace for potentially higher price tags following the recent Steam Deck price hike.

**[Ace Combat 8 Is a Very Grounded Game Set in the Skies | IGN Preview](https://www.ign.com/articles/ace-combat-8-is-a-very-grounded-game-set-in-the-skies-ign-preview)** · IGN
Hands-on time with *Ace Combat 8* proves it is shaping up to be a stunning, politically charged dogfighter where social media-style propaganda directly fuels the narrative and player perception. With jaw-dropping weather effects that actively impact gameplay mechanics—like ice forming on your plane in clouds—and incredibly satisfying tactical freedom, it looks like a monumental step forward for the beloved franchise.
