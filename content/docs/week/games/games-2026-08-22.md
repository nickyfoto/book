## News & Reviews

**[‘Hoping for a Huge October Surprise, But Not Holding My Breath’ — Arc Raiders Players Torn After Embark Delays Future Expeditions Until Early 2027](https://www.ign.com/articles/hoping-for-a-huge-october-surprise-but-not-holding-my-breath-arc-raiders-players-torn-after-embark-delays-future-expeditions-until-early-2027)** · IGN
**Embark Studios has announced a significant pause** to its post-launch roadmap for the hit extraction shooter *Arc Raiders*, delaying future Expeditions and weekly Trials until early 2027 to address community feedback regarding player progression and accessibility. While fans are disappointed to see the game's primary progression system temporarily removed, developers are promising a massive overhaul that tackles player pain points like tight departure windows and reward structures. To make up for the downtime, those who have completed all Expeditions will receive a Zenith outfit, while partial Caravan progress will be replaced with Ermal Vouchers.

**[The Sinking City 2 Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-sinking-city-2-review)** · IGN
Frogwares' Lovecraftian horror sequel ***The Sinking City 2* is an unapologetic, over-the-shoulder tribute to Capcom's modern *Resident Evil* remakes**, exchanging the original's investigation focus for tense gunplay and resource scrounging. Set in a beautifully damp and decrepit 1920s Arkham, the game's atmosphere and rewarding clue-board system shine, even if the puzzles are slightly too easy and it curiously drops the sanity mechanic entirely. It is a highly recommended experience for cosmic horror enthusiasts looking for a creepy, well-crafted ride.

**[Metal Gear Solid 4: The Flawed Masterpiece Kojima Didn’t Want to Make](https://www.ign.com/articles/metal-gear-solid-4-the-flawed-masterpiece-kojima-didnt-want-to-make)** · IGN
After being trapped on the PlayStation 3's notoriously difficult Cell processor architecture for nearly two decades, Hideo Kojima's linear, cinematic masterpiece ***Metal Gear Solid 4: Guns of the Patriots* is finally playable on modern hardware** thanks to the upcoming *Master Collection Vol. 2*. This return offers a perfect opportunity for gamers to re-evaluate one of the most ambitious, messy, and uncompromisingly sincere titles in gaming history, which stands as a stark contrast to modern, focus-tested live-service projects. The game's absolute dedication to linear storytelling and player embodiment represents an era of risk-taking that triple-A gaming has largely left behind.

**[Ultra Graphics Settings Are a Scam: Why High Is Good Enough, if Not Better, for Most Players](https://www.ign.com/articles/ultra-graphics-settings-are-a-scam-why-high-is-good-enough-if-not-better-for-most-players)** · IGN
**Playing games on maxed-out "Ultra" settings is mostly marketing hype** that leaves massive performance on the table. Testing across demanding modern titles like *Cyberpunk 2077* and *Alan Wake 2* proves that dropping settings to "High" or "Medium" with balanced upscaling delivers a huge boost in framerates with almost no visible visual trade-offs during actual gameplay. Hardware reviewers argue that these hyper-intensive graphics settings are designed to sell hardware and screenshots rather than enhance actual immersion.
