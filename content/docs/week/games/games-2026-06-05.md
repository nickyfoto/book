# Gaming News — 2026-06-05

## Top Story
Square Enix dropped an absolute bombshell at Summer Game Fest, revealing *Final Fantasy VII Revelation* as the epic conclusion to the remake trilogy, set to launch simultaneously across all platforms in Spring 2027. Promising their most expansive open world yet, players will seamlessly skydive from the Highwind airship, explore new regions like Wutai, and customize combat with the new FITS outfit-based job system. With Vincent Valentine and Cid Highwind fully playable, this is gearing up to be a monumental RPG finale.

## News & Reviews

**[PlatinumGames Takes Over TMNT: The Last Ronin](https://www.ign.com/articles/teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles-the-last-ronin-is-in-development-at-platinumgames)**
Following the shutdown of the original project at Black Forest Games, Paramount has tapped legendary action studio PlatinumGames to develop *Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: The Last Ronin*. The AAA title aims to marry top-tier Japanese action with Western storytelling, delivering a dark, vengeance-fueled narrative that could finally give TMNT fans the hardcore game they deserve.

**[Resident Evil Veronica Officially Announced for 2027](https://www.ign.com/articles/resident-evil-veronica-announced-coming-2027)**
Capcom has officially confirmed *Resident Evil Veronica*, a full remake of the beloved *Code Veronica*, is slated for a 2027 release. The debut footage showed Claire Redfield in a Parisian apartment, confirming fans will finally get the highly anticipated reimagining of her horrific journey alongside Chris and Wesker.

**[Alien: Isolation 2 Revealed at Summer Game Fest](https://www.ign.com/articles/first-alien-isolation-2-footage-revealed-at-summer-game-fest-2026)**
A full decade after the terrifying original, Sega and Creative Assembly finally debuted the first footage for *Alien: Isolation 2*. Moving beyond claustrophobic corridors, the sequel follows a new protagonist across both the interiors and exteriors of Kurosaki Station, promising a smarter Xenomorph and even slimmer chances of survival.

**[Control Resonant Shifts to High-Octane Melee Action](https://www.ign.com/articles/control-resonant-hands-on-preview)**
Our hands-on preview of *Control Resonant* confirms that the sequel successfully pivots the franchise from a shooter into a frantic, open-ended melee action game. Featuring heavily customizable weapon loadouts, incredible aerial traversal mechanics, and surreal shifting environments as Dylan Faden escapes The Oldest House, Remedy is cooking up an incredibly potent combat evolution.

**[Gothic 1 Remake: Review in Progress](https://www.ign.com/articles/gothic-1-remake-review)**
Our early verdict on the *Gothic 1 Remake* shows that while it brilliantly upgrades the classic 2001 RPG's visuals, voice acting, and controls, its unforgiving core gameplay remains largely untouched. It's the definitive way to experience the game's ahead-of-its-time worldbuilding, but players must still wrestle with shallow combat mechanics and frustrating pacing issues.

**[Guild Wars 3 Revealed with Fall 2027 Beta](https://www.ign.com/articles/guild-wars-3-officially-revealed-beta-starts-fall-2027-for-arenanets-next-mmorpg)**
ArenaNet officially unveiled *Guild Wars 3*, an action-style MMORPG set 1,000 years before the original game. Featuring a momentum-based combat system and marking the franchise's first foray onto consoles, this huge announcement comes with the excellent news that it will remain subscription-free when it enters beta in Fall 2027.

**[Black Ops 7 Gets Classic Mode](https://www.ign.com/articles/black-ops-7s-new-classic-mode-seems-to-fix-a-lot-of-issues-players-have-with-the-game)**
*Call of Duty: Black Ops 7* just launched a "Black Ops Classic" limited-time mode that completely strips out modern omnimovement, sliding, and stims. By reverting the mechanics to feel more like *Black Ops 1* and *2* across throwback maps like Nuketown and Firing Range, the community is praising the mode for finally putting the focus back on pure gun skills.

**[Palworld 1.0 Arrives in July](https://www.ign.com/articles/palworld-is-finally-getting-its-full-10-release-coming-in-july)**
After dominating the gaming conversation in early access, developer Pocket Pair has confirmed that the massive *Palworld 1.0* update will officially launch across all platforms on July 10.
