# Gaming News — 2026-07-27

## Top Story
Physical copies of *Grand Theft Auto 6* in Japan will actually just be a box with a digital download code that expires 170 days after the game's November 19, 2026, launch date. This controversial move by Rockstar bypasses strict Japanese consumer protection laws but has added massive fuel to the ongoing anger over the death of physical media.

**[Critics of Sony’s Decision to Kill Physical Discs Call on Gamers to Boycott PlayStation for a Week Next Month, but Will Anyone Listen?](https://www.ign.com/articles/critics-of-sonys-decision-to-kill-physical-discs-call-on-gamers-to-boycott-playstation-for-a-week-next-month-but-will-anyone-listen)** · IGN

As someone who loves hunting for physical game deals, it is incredibly disheartening to see corporations push so hard to strip true ownership away from players.

**[Final Fantasy 14 Will Be The Biggest Game on Nintendo Switch 2 by Install Size, and Take Up Almost Half of the Console’s Entire Memory](https://www.ign.com/articles/final-fantasy-14-will-be-the-biggest-game-on-nintendo-switch-2-by-install-size-and-take-up-almost-half-of-the-consoles-entire-memory)** · IGN
If you plan to play *Final Fantasy 14* on the Nintendo Switch 2 this August, you better start clearing space or buying a massive SD card, because the MMO will demand a staggering 117.2GB of storage. This eye-watering install size consumes nearly half of the new console's 256GB internal memory and easily dethrones *Final Fantasy 7 Rebirth* as the largest game on the platform. While the download thankfully includes all five previous expansions, players will undoubtedly need even more space when the upcoming *Evercold* expansion drops next January.

**[Now Xbox Is Down for Some, Microsoft Says It’s Working to Fix a Major Outage Affecting Library, Sign-In, and Game Launch](https://www.ign.com/articles/now-xbox-is-down-for-some-microsoft-says-its-working-to-fix-a-major-outage-affecting-library-sign-in-and-game-launch)** · IGN
Microsoft is currently scrambling to resolve a severe Xbox Live outage that is preventing players from signing into profiles, viewing their game libraries, and launching digital titles. Coming right before the full launch of *Halo: Campaign Evolved*, this badly-timed server crash gives physical media advocates even more ammunition following a similar PlayStation Network outage last week. It is a stark reminder of the inherent flaws in an all-digital ecosystem where you can completely lose access to your owned games the second a server goes down.

**[Ubisoft Gives Free PC Copies to Xbox Owners of Select Games](https://www.ign.com/articles/ubisoft-gives-free-pc-copies-to-xbox-owners-of-select-games)** · IGN
In a fantastic pro-consumer move, Ubisoft and Xbox have partnered up to give Xbox console owners free PC copies of select titles, including *Assassin's Creed Mirage*, *Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora*, and *Skull and Bones*. As long as your accounts are linked, these games will automatically populate in your Ubisoft Connect PC library by next week. It is a brilliant perk for anyone transitioning from console to PC, and a great excuse to revisit some huge open-world games on stronger hardware.

**[Call of Duty: Black Ops Fans Discover Cut Zombies Content Over 15 Years After Release](https://www.ign.com/articles/call-of-duty-black-ops-fans-discover-cut-zombies-content-over-15-years-after-release)** · IGN
Over a decade and a half after its original release, dedicated fans have dug into the recent PS4 port of *Call of Duty: Black Ops* to discover an unreleased, bird's-eye view tower defense Zombies mode. This crude but fascinating prototype let players place turrets to stop undead hordes, and was likely abandoned in favor of the cult-classic Dead Ops Arcade minigame. It is an incredible piece of gaming history that showcases just how much Treyarch experimented with the beloved Zombies formula back in its golden era.
