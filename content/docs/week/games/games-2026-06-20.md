# Gaming News — 2026-06-20

## Top Story
The Xbox gaming division is reportedly facing another major restructuring, with leaks suggesting that beloved development studios like Ninja Theory, Double Fine, and Compulsion are in serious danger of being shut down. While mainline giants like *Halo* and *Gears of War* remain safe, projects like *Forza Motorsport* and *The Outer Worlds* have unfortunately been cancelled entirely as Microsoft resets its core strategy.

## News & Reviews

**[Epic Games Store Roadmap Promises 5x Faster Boot Times, User Reviews, and More](https://www.ign.com/articles/epic-games-store-roadmap-promises-5x-faster-boot-times-user-reviews-and-more)** · IGN
Epic Games has revealed a massive 12-month roadmap for its PC storefront, promising a ground-up launcher rebuild that aims to deliver a 5x faster cold start. Along with these essential speed boosts, the platform will finally add long-requested features like user-written reviews, third-party patch notes, and publisher-funded coupons. It's a much-needed overhaul for a platform that has historically struggled to achieve feature parity with its main competitor, Steam.

**[Halo: Campaign Evolved Split-Screen Co-Op on PS5 Requires PlayStation Plus for Both Players](https://www.ign.com/articles/halo-campaign-evolved-split-screen-co-op-on-ps5-requires-playstation-plus-for-both-players)** · IGN
In a baffling move that is already alienating the community, playing local split-screen couch co-op in *Halo: Campaign Evolved* on the PS5 will require two active PlayStation Plus subscriptions linked to Microsoft accounts. Fans are understandably outraged, as the original 2001 release offered this foundational couch co-op experience entirely for free, turning what should be a nostalgic triumph into a frustrating paywall.

**[Sea of Remnants Is Like a Puppet-Punk Persona at Sea | IGN Preview](https://www.ign.com/articles/sea-of-remnants-is-like-a-puppet-punk-persona-at-sea-ign-preview)** · IGN
*Sea of Remnants* is shaping up to be a stunning open-world RPG arriving on PS5, PC, and mobile later this year, cleverly blending *Assassin's Creed Black Flag* naval exploration with *Persona*-style turn-based combat. The game utilizes a charming, hand-crafted wooden puppetry aesthetic that recalls stop-motion films like *Coraline*, pairing its unique art style with dynamic weather and massive kraken boss fights.

**[Out of Words Is an Artistic, Handmade Breath of Fresh Air | IGN Preview](https://www.ign.com/articles/out-of-words-is-an-artistic-handmade-breath-of-fresh-air-ign-preview)** · IGN
Launching in early 2027, *Out of Words* is a meticulously handmade stop-motion co-op platformer where players must work together, akin to recent multiplayer hits like *It Takes Two*. The game follows two adolescents thrust into the mystical world of Vokabulantis, promising an emotional 10-hour adventure built entirely in the real world with physical mediums like clay and moss.

**[Ubisoft Co-Founder Claude Guillemot Dead at 69 After Plane Crash in France](https://www.ign.com/articles/ubisoft-co-founder-claude-guillemot-dead-at-69-after-plane-crash-in-france)** · IGN
The gaming industry mourns the loss of Claude Guillemot, one of the five founding brothers of Ubisoft, who passed away tragically in a twin-engine plane crash in western France at the age of 69. Guillemot was the CEO and chairman of the Guillemot Corporation, and his visionary early work helped build Ubisoft into the global powerhouse behind mega-franchises like *Assassin's Creed* and *Rayman*.
