# Gaming News — 2026-07-19

## Top Story
Microsoft's Xbox executives have clarified their shifting exclusivity strategy, assuring players that more console exclusives are coming alongside heavy-hitters like *Gears of War: E-Day* and *Clockwork Revolution*. However, leadership warned fans not to "overly fixate" on expecting all future single-player games to remain exclusive as they evaluate releases on a case-by-case basis.

## News & Reviews

**[Xbox Clarifies Future Exclusivity Strategy](https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-says-more-exclusives-are-coming-but-tells-players-not-to-overly-fixate-on-single-player-games)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-says-more-exclusives-are-coming-but-tells-players-not-to-overly-fixate-on-single-player-games)
Xbox executives Matthew Ball and Matt Booty have promised that *Gears of War: E-Day* and *Clockwork Revolution* won't be the platform's only exclusive titles, despite a recent push for multiplatform releases. Booty emphasized that they will evaluate exclusivity for each franchise individually and that single-player games won't automatically be locked to the Xbox ecosystem. This strategy clarification arrives as Xbox continues to navigate massive structural changes, including 3,200 total recent and upcoming staff layoffs.

**[Game Devs Embrace "Jimothy" the Viral Raccoon](https://www.ign.com/articles/world-of-warcraft-and-runescape-fans-want-official-jimothy-support-after-game-devs-show-love-for-viral-raccoon)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/world-of-warcraft-and-runescape-fans-want-official-jimothy-support-after-game-devs-show-love-for-viral-raccoon)
The internet's latest obsession, a uniquely proportioned raccoon named Jimothy, has caught the attention of major game developers across the industry. The studios behind *Squirrel With a Gun*, *World of Warcraft*, and *Old School RuneScape* have all created tribute renders or quick skin mods highlighting the viral critter. While Jimothy isn't officially playable in these titles just yet, passionate fans are already petitioning for developers to implement him as an official battle pet and crossover mount.

**[Fall Asleep to the Chaos of Nostalgic Call of Duty Lobbies](https://www.ign.com/articles/call-of-duty-ambience-website-lets-you-fall-asleep-to-nostalgic-black-ops-and-mw2-lobbies)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/call-of-duty-ambience-website-lets-you-fall-asleep-to-nostalgic-black-ops-and-mw2-lobbies)
A new website appropriately named "Call of Duty Ambience" is offering a highly specific dose of nostalgia by streaming the chaotic, trash-talking voice chats of classic *Modern Warfare 2*, *Black Ops*, and *Black Ops 2* lobbies 24/7. Created by app-maker Mike Wing, the site is wrapped in an old Xbox 360 dashboard theme and acts as unconventional white noise for gamers longing for the aggressive, lawless social elements of early 2010s multiplayer gaming.
