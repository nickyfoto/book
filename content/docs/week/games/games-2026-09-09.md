# Gaming News — 2026-09-09

## Top Story
Nintendo dominated headlines with its September 2026 Direct broadcast, unveiling major upcoming Switch 2 titles including *Metroid Ravenous* (a 2D sequel to *Metroid Dread*) and *Kirby and the World Beyond*, alongside rapid sellouts for the Zelda 40th Anniversary Switch 2 console.

## News & Reviews

**[Metroid Ravenous Announced for Switch 2](https://www.ign.com/articles/metroid-ravenous-announced-a-side-scrolling-sequel-to-metroid-dread)** · IGN
Nintendo and MercurySteam announced *Metroid Ravenous*, a 2D side-scrolling sequel to *Metroid Dread* launching exclusively for Nintendo Switch 2 on January 28, 2027. The game finds Samus stranded on a mysterious moon where she must feed on enemies to survive, accompanied by standard, special, and amiibo physical editions. This unexpected follow-up brings back *Dread*'s tense exploration and combat while pushing Samus's story forward.

**[Huge Steam Leak Exposes Persona 6 and Kingdom Hearts 4 Achievements](https://www.ign.com/articles/apparent-steam-leak-reveals-persona-6-and-kingdom-hearts-4-achievements-unannounced-games)** · IGN
A massive Valve API flaw led to achievement lists leaking on Exophase for highly anticipated titles, revealing plot points for *Persona 6* and unannounced Disney worlds for *Kingdom Hearts 4*. The leak also unveiled unannounced Steam ports and titles like *Professor Layton vs. Phoenix Wright: Ace Attorney*, a new Nickelodeon game, and a Telltale *Walking Dead* collection. It gives gamers an unexpected look at major upcoming releases, though Valve has yet to officially resolve the backend slip-up.

**[No Law Hands-On Preview: A Worthy Cyberpunk Immersive Sim](https://www.ign.com/articles/no-law-looks-like-the-deus-ex-successor-ive-been-waiting-for)** · IGN
Developer Neon Giant showcased *No Law*, a dense sci-fi immersive sim set in the vibrant, reactive metropolis of Port Desire that feels like a true spiritual successor to *Deus Ex*. Players control ex-military agent Grey Harker, using cybernetic superjumps, stasis grenades, stealth, and branching emergent mechanics to tackle missions with total freedom. Built by a 24-person team without generative AI, it stands out as one of the most promising tactile action games on the horizon.

**[No Man's Sky Cosmos Update Lets Players Own Space Stations and Reach Stars](https://www.ign.com/articles/no-mans-sky-10th-anniversary-cosmos-update-actually-updates-space-lets-players-finally-reach-stars)** · IGN
Hello Games launched the massive 10th-anniversary *Cosmos* update for *No Man's Sky*, overhauling deep space with orbital base-building, salvageable space hulks, and player-owned space stations. Players can now form multiplayer Alliances and undertake a perilous trek to reach the actual sun at the center of their star system. It transforms empty space into a living ecosystem, proving Hello Games' decade-long commitment to expanding its universe.

**[The Uprising Review: Andrew Garfield Leads a Visceral Peasants' Revolt](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-uprising-review-andrew-garfield)** · IGN
Paul Greengrass directs *The Uprising*, a historical drama centered on England's 1381 Peasants' Revolt featuring a moving performance by Andrew Garfield as a grieving farmer turned rebel. IGN praised the film's immersive hand-held realism and strong cast performances, though noted its jarring digital aerial shots and an overly Hollywoodized ending. Ultimately, it offers a gritty, thought-provoking slice of history that action fans and history buffs will appreciate.

**[Tianji: Shadow of the Ancients Brings Tang Dynasty Martial Arts to HD-2D RPGs](https://www.ign.com/articles/hands-on-tianji-shadow-of-the-ancients)** · IGN
Developer Justdan revealed *Tianji: Shadow of the Ancients*, an Unreal Engine 5 RPG inspired by Square Enix's HD-2D style but rooted in Tang Dynasty lore and Qi energy combat. The game features amnesiac protagonist Shangguan Shuo, fast-paced turn-based battles with 2x speed toggles, deep skill trees, and gorgeous ink-wash cutscenes. Coming in 2027 to Steam, Switch, Switch 2, and PS5, it looks like a fresh, stylish alternative for turn-based tactical fans.

**[Apple Unveils Foldable iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro at Event](https://www.ign.com/articles/apple-announces-foldable-iphone-duo)** · IGN
Apple officially entered the foldable market with the \$1,999 iPhone Duo, featuring a 5.4-inch outer cover that unfolds into a 7.6-inch display with zero visible crease and an A20 Pro chip. Alongside it, Apple debuted the \$1,199 iPhone 18 Pro with a physical shutter 48MP camera and faster AI performance, plus new AirPods 5 and Apple Watch Series 12. For mobile gamers and tech enthusiasts, the Duo promises high-end thermal vapor chamber cooling and enhanced screen real estate.

**[Onimusha: Way of the Sword Gets Official Prequel Webtoon](https://www.ign.com/articles/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-is-getting-an-official-prequel-webtoon-with-new-chapters-every-saturday)** · IGN
Following the launch of the critically acclaimed 10/10 masterpiece *Onimusha: Way of the Sword*, Capcom partnered with Laurel Pursuit for an official prequel webcomic titled *Fated Rivalry*. The free-to-read Webtoon series follows Sasaki Ganryu's obsession with defeating Miyamoto Musashi after acquiring an Oni Gauntlet. New chapters release every Saturday, providing canon backstory that feeds directly into the main Edo-period game.
