# Gaming News — 2026-06-25

## Top Story
Grand Theft Auto 6 preorders are officially live at $80 for the Standard Edition, but Rockstar Games has ignited a massive controversy by confirming the physical release will only include a digital download code rather than a game disc. While industry analysts point out this is a calculated move to kill the second-hand market and strictly control game pricing, fans are furious—especially after the preorder launch arrived without the highly anticipated Trailer 3.

## News & Reviews

**Sony Confirms 'Significant' Bungie Layoffs** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/bungie-announces-layoffs-as-it-admits-destiny-2-fell-short-of-expectations)
In a brutal blow to the studio, PlayStation CEO Hermen Hulst confirmed heavy layoffs impacting most of the Destiny team and some Marathon developers. Bungie openly admitted that Destiny 2 fell short of expectations, leaving the future of the once-titan franchise looking remarkably bleak as the studio downsizes.

**Microsoft Hikes Xbox Series X/S Prices** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/microsoft-announces-significant-price-rises-for-xbox-series-x-and-s-2tb-model-discontinued)
Thanks to an ongoing component crisis heavily fueled by the AI boom, Microsoft is jacking up console prices starting August 1. The 512GB Series S jumps by $100 to $499.99, while the 1TB Series X will now cost a staggering $799.99, pricing many gamers out of the current console generation.

**Deltarune Chapter 5 Shatters Records, Chapter 6 Due in 2027** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/deltarune-chapter-6-will-release-in-2027-chapter-5-shatters-concurrent-player-records-on-steam)
Toby Fox's indie juggernaut continues to dominate, with the newly released Chapter 5 shattering its previous Steam record by hitting over 290,000 concurrent players. Fox also confirmed the story isn't over, slating Chapter 6 for a 2027 release as development smoothly continues.

**Beast of Reincarnation Preview** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/beast-of-reincarnation-is-a-stunning-action-rpg-with-a-tactical-twist-ign-preview)
Game Freak is stepping away from Pokémon to deliver a stunning post-apocalyptic action-RPG featuring an excellent sword-swinging protagonist and her magical dog companion, Koo. The tactile, companion-assisted combat and slick traversal mechanics look absolutely incredible, making this a must-watch title ahead of its August 4 release.

**DC Announces Three New Animated Shows** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/absolute-batman-joker-anime-krypto-animated-series-announced)
DC Studios unveiled a stacked slate of animated projects, including an adult adaptation of the highly acclaimed Absolute Batman comic run. We're also getting DC's first-ever anime, Joker: Laugh Riot, and a new kid-friendly Krypto series, expanding DC's television footprint in a big way.
