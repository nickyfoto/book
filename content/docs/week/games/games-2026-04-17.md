# Gaming News — 2026-04-17

## Top Story
The 2026 BAFTA Game Awards crowned Sandfall Interactive's *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33* as Best Game, dominating the evening with additional wins for Best Debut Game and Best Performer in a Leading Role. The critically acclaimed RPG fended off heavy hitters like *Ghost of Yōtei* and *Kingdom Come: Deliverance II*, cementing its place as an instant classic in the genre. 

## News & Reviews

**[Slay the Spire 2 Review-Bombing](https://www.ign.com/articles/slay-the-spire-2-review-bombing)**
Mega Crit's highly anticipated *Slay the Spire 2* was hit by a massive wave of review bombing, tanking its Steam rating to "Mixed" after the controversial version 0.103.2 update dropped yesterday. Players were furious over sweeping balance changes and UI adjustments, serving as a harsh reminder of how volatile the Early Access development cycle can be for both fans and developers.

**[Samson Coming To Consoles This Fall](https://www.ign.com/articles/samson-coming-to-consoles-this-fall) & [Samson Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/samson-review)**
Liquid Swords’ 90s-set open-world brawler *Samson* earned a dismal 4/10 review from us due to extreme bugs, clunky combat, and repetitive missions that made clearing its in-game debt a miserable slog. Despite the deeply flawed PC launch, the developer has detailed a heavy patch schedule and confirmed the game is headed to consoles this fall. 

**[Diablo 4: Lord of Hatred’s New Endgame](https://www.ign.com/articles/diablo-4-lord-of-hatreds-new-endgame-everything-you-need-to-know-ign-first)**
Blizzard is completely overhauling the *Diablo 4* endgame experience in the upcoming *Lord of Hatred* expansion with a new "War Plans" playlist system designed to seamlessly chain activities like Helltides and Nightmare Dungeons. It also introduces a grueling, unending new horde mode called "Echoing Hatred," and even brings fishing into the ARPG mix.

**[Final Fantasy XIV Patch 7.5 Live Letter Summary](https://www.ign.com/articles/final-fantasy-xiv-patch-75-live-letter-summary-trailer-breakdown-new-dungeon-next-alliance-raid-kefka-ultimate-and-more)**
Square Enix unveiled the roadmap for FFXIV's April 28 patch, introducing the highly complex, beast-catching Beastmaster Limited Job, and a snow-covered Garlemald dungeon called The Clyteum. The live letter also heavily teased that Final Fantasy VI's Kefka will be the boss of the game's next grueling Ultimate Raid. 

**[Game Pass Rumor Details](https://www.ign.com/articles/game-pass-rumor-details)**
Rumors are swirling that Xbox is exploring a new, cheaper Xbox Game Pass tier that would exclusively feature Microsoft's first-party titles. According to reports, the new Xbox boss, Asha Sharma, has noted that the current subscription model has "become too expensive for players," prompting a potential massive shakeup for the service.

**[Galaxies Spring Showcase 2026: Everything Announced](https://www.ign.com/articles/galaxies-spring-showcase-2026-everything-announced)**
The Galaxies Showcase brought the heat with seven world premieres, delivering deep dives into upcoming bangers like the SAO action RPG *Echoes of Aincrad* and Supermassive's new sci-fi horror *Directive 8020*. We also got a look at *Kitaria Fables 2* and the brutal 50v50 shooter *Hell Let Loose: Vietnam*.
