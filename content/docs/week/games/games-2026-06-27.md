# Gaming News — 2026-06-27

## Top Story
In a stark reminder that digital ownership is mostly an illusion, Sony announced it will be deleting over 550 previously purchased Studio Canal movies from PlayStation users' accounts this September without offering refunds. The move is already sparking major backlash and reigniting fierce debates over consumer rights and what it actually means to "own" a digital product in the modern era.

## News & Reviews

**[GTA 6 Disc Debunked & Scalper Madness](https://www.ign.com/articles/no-there-is-no-gta-6-physical-disc-coming-out-at-launch-or-the-months-after-those-reports-and-social-media-posts-are-incorrect)** · IGN
Despite viral rumors, Rockstar is strictly sticking to a digital-only launch for *GTA 6*, meaning the physical edition will just be a code in a box with no plans for discs. Hilariously, that hasn't stopped eBay scalpers from successfully flipping pre-orders for inflated prices, preying on uninformed gamers who don't realize there's absolutely no shortage of digital codes.

**[Street Fighter 6 Devs Talk 10-Year Plan and Tifa](https://www.ign.com/articles/street-fighter-6-devs-have-no-current-plans-to-make-any-major-adjustments-to-system-mechanics-evo-2026)** · IGN
At Evo 2026, *Street Fighter 6* developers clarified they hope to support the game for a full 10 years, though they shot down competitive hopes for immediate changes to the controversial Drive system mechanics. They also teased that upcoming DLC character Tifa will utilize a unique *Final Fantasy VII* Materia mechanic, while hyping up the franchise's first Filipino fighter, Yasmine.

**[Steam Machine Resurgence Meets Immediate Scalping](https://www.ign.com/articles/steam-machine-scalpers-on-ebay-are-selling-reservations-at-double-the-price-valve-is-asking-for)** · IGN
Valve recently opened up reservations for its pricey new $1,049 Steam Machine, utilizing a randomized queue intended to be a fair alternative to traditional pre-orders. Unfortunately, the system failed to stop resellers, as eBay is currently flooded with confirmed reservations flipping the console hybrid for up to $2,899.

**[Invincible VS Devs Want Scorpion Crossover](https://www.ign.com/articles/invincible-vs-devs-are-really-interested-in-a-mortal-kombat-crossover)** · IGN
The team behind the fighting game *Invincible VS* confirmed at Evo that they are extremely interested in bringing in guest characters from the *Mortal Kombat* franchise. With Omni-Man already appearing in *MK1*, a crossover feels well within the realm of possibility, and the developers are explicitly naming Scorpion as a guest they would jump to include.

**[FNaF 3 Movie Taps Horror Veteran Scribe](https://www.ign.com/articles/five-nights-at-freddys-3-enlists-it-and-annabelle-writer-for-its-script)** · IGN
Blumhouse is apparently looking to inject some fresh blood into the notoriously poorly reviewed *Five Nights at Freddy's* cinematic universe by enlisting *It* and *Annabelle* writer Gary Dauberman for the third installment's script. While the first two films raked in massive box office hauls despite terrible critic scores, hopefully, Dauberman can finally elevate the horror elements for older audiences.
