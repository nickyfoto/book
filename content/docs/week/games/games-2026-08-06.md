## News & Reviews

**[Switch 2 Sales Cool Off as Global Memory Shortage Triggers September Price Hike](https://www.ign.com/articles/switch-2-sales-down-year-on-year-after-2025s-explosive-launch-though-nintendo-insists-adoption-still-compares-favorably-to-original-switch)** · IGN
Nintendo's latest financial results show Switch 2 sales fell by over a third to 3.82 million units this quarter, though the company insists adoption still compares well to the original console. However, a massive global memory shortage has completely sold out 2027 DRAM and HBM production, causing hardware prices to spike across the industry. As a result, Nintendo has announced an official Switch 2 price increase starting next month, meaning right now is the best time to pick up a console.

**[Capcom Unfazed by the Looming Death of Physical Game Discs](https://www.ign.com/articles/death-of-physical-games-will-not-have-a-significant-impact-on-sales-resident-evil-publisher-capcom-says)** · IGN
As Sony gears up to completely eliminate game discs for new PS5 games starting in January 2028, physical media's days are officially numbered. However, *Resident Evil* and *Monster Hunter* publisher Capcom remains entirely unbothered by the shift, revealing that digital downloads already account for a staggering 90% of their total unit sales. This underscores a massive industry-wide migration to digital storefronts, even as console manufacturers experiment with digital licensing transfers.

**[Onimusha: Way of the Sword Perfects the Balance of Brutality and Style](https://www.ign.com/articles/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-the-final-preview)** · IGN
In our final hands-on preview, Capcom's upcoming revival *Onimusha: Way of the Sword* beautifully balances challenging, Soulslike boss fights with incredibly stylish and accessible combat against standard enemies. The game redefines the series' classic "Soul Absorption" mechanic, turning it into a tense, competitive tug-of-war where giant bosses can steal souls to power themselves up. It's a fresh, deeply satisfying evolution that makes combat feel uniquely dynamic and cinematic.

**[Warrior Cats Books Snag a Major Console and PC RPG Adaptation](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-warrior-cats-books-are-finally-getting-a-major-video-game-adaptation)** · IGN
Longtime fans of Erin Hunter’s iconic YA book series *Warriors* are finally getting the official, large-scale adaptation they have been waiting over two decades for. Developed by TRAILMARK Games, *Warrior Cats: Clans of the Forest* is a turn-based RPG arriving on Steam and major consoles, including the Switch 2, this fall. The game will let players build their own custom feline, join a legendary Clan, and cross paths with beloved characters from the books.

**[The Secret of Weepstone Captures Tabletop D&D Magic on PC](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-secret-of-weepstone-preview)** · IGN
This gorgeous, monochrome 3D dungeon crawler completely stole the show in our hands-on demo with its pen-and-ink aesthetics and tabletop-style digital dice rolls. The game features a brilliant take on permadeath by rewarding you with "Mortal Favors"—unique passive abilities unlocked when a party member dies. It's a delightfully unforgiving adventure where even sending your fighter down a castle latrine to check on running water can result in an embarrassing, permadeath demise.

**[Crossfire Developer That's No Moon Hit by Staff Layoffs](https://www.ign.com/articles/layoffs-hit-crossfire-developer-thats-no-moon)** · IGN
Independent developer That’s No Moon has unfortunately become the latest studio hit by layoffs, letting go of an undetermined number of staff, including environmental and technical artists. Founded in 2021 by veterans from Naughty Dog and Infinity Ward, the studio recently debuted its ambitious AAA narrative-driven action-adventure *Crossfire* at Summer Game Fest 2026. It is a sobering reminder of the ongoing restructuring struggles plaguing the gaming industry.
