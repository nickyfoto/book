# Gaming News — Week of 2026-08-15 to 2026-08-21

## Story of the Week
The highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto 6* dominated headlines this week as a massive security breach by hacker group **'CyberLeek'** leaked gameplay videos and the complete map to promote a cryptocurrency memecoin. Take-Two Interactive swiftly responded with a massive legal campaign, **subpoenaing Microsoft and Discord** to hunt down the perpetrators. Meanwhile, the game's colossal hype reached absurd new heights as the US Army's 9th Engineer Battalion reportedly got at least **20 soldiers to reenlist** in exchange for a four-day pass to play the game on launch day.

## Top Stories

- **Kingdom Hearts 4 Delivers Spectacular Extended Gameplay and a Late 2027 Release Window** · [Source](https://www.ign.com/articles/kingdom-hearts-4-gets-extended-gameplay-trailer-featuring-playable-mickey-donald-and-goofy-d23-2026)
Square Enix stole the show at Disney's D23 Expo with a four-minute extended gameplay trailer showcasing traversal mechanics in Quadratum, a first look at Pixar's *Coco* world, and playable Mickey, Donald, and Goofy. Series leads Tetsuya Nomura and Tai Yasue firmly guaranteed a smooth development process and a **100% locked-in release window for late 2027** with no delays. The hype was so intense that it propelled the *Kingdom Hearts HD 1.5+2.5 ReMix* compilation to the **second spot on Steam's US best-sellers list** amid an ongoing franchise sale.

- **The Impending Death of Discs Sparks a PlayStation Boycott as Physical Sales Plunge** · [Source](https://www.ign.com/articles/no-disc-no-buy-comments-dominate-sonys-phantom-blade-zero-state-of-play-livestream-as-planned-playstation-boycott-nears)
Outraged gamers are organizing a week-long economic and gameplay blackout under the banner **"No Disc No Buy"** to protest Sony's plans to phase out PlayStation disc production by 2028. This backlash coincides with US physical game sales crashing to an all-time July low of just **\$85 million**—the worst retail software performance since tracking began in 1995. Despite the retail collapse and online pushback, Sony remains unfazed, pointing to a massive **82% digital download ratio**.

- **FromSoftware Reveals Switch 2 Exclusive 'The Duskbloods' but Network Test Overwhelms Servers** · [Source](https://www.ign.com/articles/fromsoftware-sincerely-apologizes-for-the-duskbloods-network-test-server-issues-ends-first-slot-early)
Helmed by Hidetaka Miyazaki, FromSoftware officially announced *The Duskbloods*, an ambitious, board game-influenced multiplayer PvPvE action game heading **exclusively to the Nintendo Switch 2**. However, the game's first closed network test hit a major roadblock as servers buckled under the load, forcing the developers to **prematurely end the session** and issue a sincere apology. The team is actively upgrading stability for the title, which is scheduled to release **later this year** and feature intense phase-based, 8-player matches.

- **Live-Service Dreams Falter as Riot Cancels 2XKO and Guerrilla Reboots Horizon Spinoff** · [Source](https://www.ign.com/articles/riot-to-end-active-development-for-2xko-in-december-2026-online-play-to-remain-unaffected)
In a major blow to multiplayer ambitions, Riot Games announced it is **shutting down active development** on its *League of Legends* fighting game, *2XKO*, this December due to unsustainable player retention. Meanwhile, Guerrilla Games has reportedly sent its cartoony multiplayer spinoff, *Horizon Hunters Gathering*, **back to the drawing board** to strip out live-service elements and focus on a smaller-scope co-op story mode following negative playtest feedback. These setbacks highlight the industry's ongoing retreat from chasing the ever-elusive live-service gold rush.

- **Immersive Sim Legend Warren Spector Announces Retirement After 44 Years** · [Source](https://www.ign.com/articles/deus-ex-system-shock-and-thief-developer-legend-warren-spector-announces-retirement)
Warren Spector, the visionary creator behind iconic immersive sims like *Deus Ex*, *System Shock*, and *Thief*, has officially announced his **retirement from active game development** at age 70. Spector noted that the modern gaming business has changed and is "just not as much fun" anymore, though he plans to lecture, write books, and practice piano. While he leaves the door slightly open for a future return, his retirement marks the end of a legendary career that **fundamentally shaped player agency** in gaming.

- **Double Fine Regains Independence and IP Rights Post-Xbox Split** · [Source](https://www.ign.com/articles/double-fine-owns-ip-and-publishing-rights-for-all-of-its-games-following-xbox-split-psychonauts-studio-confirms)
Tim Schafer confirmed that Double Fine has successfully **clawed back the IP and publishing rights** for all of its catalog games, including *Psychonauts* and *Brutal Legend*. Fans can now directly support the studio's independent future, which kicks off with a **Kickstarter for its *Amnesia Fortnight 2026* game jam**. This move gives the beloved studio a solid development runway as they enter a new independent era.
