# Gaming News — Week of 2026-09-05 to 2026-09-11

## Story of the Week
Sony unexpectedly canceled its partnership on Hideo Kojima's next-gen action-espionage title *Physint* over budget concerns and missed deadlines following the disappointing sales performance of *Death Stranding 2*. In a massive industry shakeup, Microsoft stepped in to finance the project and secure Xbox as the official publisher. The high-profile pickup gives Xbox a major prestige win while extending its creative partnership with Kojima Productions across gaming, film, and television.

## Top Stories
**[Nintendo Unveils Ocarina of Time Remake & Switch 2 Lineup](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-remake-fully-revealed)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-remake-fully-revealed)
Nintendo headlined the week with a double-header Direct broadcast, officially revealing a ground-up remake of *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* for Nintendo Switch 2 launching November 5, 2026, alongside a Zelda 40th Anniversary console. The showcase also introduced *Metroid Ravenous* (a 2D sequel to *Metroid Dread*) for January 2027 and offered a first look at *Kirby and the World Beyond*. Additionally, System Update 23.0.0 released for Switch 2, adding TV-mode Variable Refresh Rate (VRR) support and console-to-console data transfers.

**[GTA 6 Confirms Launch Details Amid Developer Legal Battles](https://www.ign.com/articles/gta-6-developer-rockstar-says-it-will-defend-itself-vigorously-from-union-claims-as-employment-tribunal-over-the-firing-of-31-workers-gets-underway)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/gta-6-developer-rockstar-says-it-will-defend-itself-vigorously-from-union-claims-as-employment-tribunal-over-the-firing-of-31-workers-gets-underway)
Rockstar Games confirmed *Grand Theft Auto 6* will launch digitally worldwide at local midnight on November 19, sparking heated community debate over realistic survival mechanics like mandatory gas station refueling. Meanwhile, Rockstar defended itself before a UK employment tribunal against union claims regarding 31 terminated developers, alleging staff leaked confidential material on Discord. The company also revealed extreme anti-drone security measures at its offices, while Miami Beach city commissioners approved a \$3 million marketing campaign tied to the game.

**[Marvel’s Wolverine Reviews Highlight Blood-Soaked Action](https://www.ign.com/articles/marvels-wolverine-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/marvels-wolverine-review)
Ahead of its September 15 release, previews and reviews for Insomniac's PS5-exclusive *Marvel's Wolverine* showcased its raw, uncensored violence and introspective story following Logan's Weapon X roots. Insomniac cleared up viral internet confusion by clarifying that the purple sword-wielding villain is Lady Deathstrike rather than Psylocke. While critics lauded the visceral claw combat and character animation, reviews noted that repetitive arena fights and limited progression hold back its 15-hour campaign.

**[Double Fine Reclaims Independence Following Xbox Restructuring](https://www.ign.com/articles/no-one-puts-double-fine-out-of-business-except-for-me-tim-schafer-explains-how-he-kept-double-fine-afloat-after-leaving-xbox)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/no-one-puts-double-fine-out-of-business-except-for-me-tim-schafer-explains-how-he-kept-double-fine-afloat-after-leaving-xbox)
Following its sudden divestment during Microsoft's summer restructuring, studio head Tim Schafer announced that Double Fine has officially reclaimed its independent status. Despite losing a quarter of its workforce in the transition, the team shadow-dropped *Keeper* on PS5 and launched a Kickstarter to crowdfund its next title under the Double Fine Action Labs label. Schafer reiterated his support for subscription platforms like Game Pass while reflecting on navigating the industry's four-year economic downturn.

**[Capcom Scores Day-One Hit with Onimusha: Way of the Sword](https://www.ign.com/articles/as-onimusha-way-of-the-sword-sells-over-1-million-day-one-capcom-says-its-focusing-on-re-activating-ips-that-havent-had-a-new-title-launch-recently)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/as-onimusha-way-of-the-sword-sells-over-1-million-day-one-capcom-says-its-focusing-on-re-activating-ips-that-havent-had-a-new-title-launch-recently)
Capcom scored another major success as action RPG *Onimusha: Way of the Sword* sold over one million copies on day one, receiving a perfect 10/10 review from IGN for its combat and narrative. The milestone pushed total franchise sales past 10 million and prompted Capcom to focus heavily on reviving long-dormant classic IPs. To expand the game's lore, Capcom partnered with Laurel Pursuit to launch an official prequel Webtoon series titled *Fated Rivalry*.
