# Gaming News — Week of 2026-08-28 to 2026-09-04

## Story of the Week
Rockstar Games dominated headlines with a massive rollout of **Grand Theft Auto 6** details, locking in a November 19, 2026 launch date that has already amassed 4.5 million pre-orders. The studio revealed Vice City doubles the size of Los Santos inside a massive open world three times larger than *Red Dead Redemption 2*, which will feature a dynamic "criminal profile" morality meter that can brand a character as permanently irredeemable. Despite technical experts warning that a 60fps console version is extremely unlikely, ninety-nine new screenshots and real-world architectural discoveries have fans more excited than ever for the upcoming crime epic.

## Top Stories

**Sony's Digital Future Ignites Backlash as Studio Settles Landmark \$7.85 Billion Class-Action Lawsuit** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/playstation-claims-digital-games-are-not-really-owned-by-players-in-response-to-class-action-lawsuit)
PlayStation is facing intense backlash after Sony Japan published a strict customer harassment policy to defend against toxic player protests regarding plans to end physical disc production by January 2028. In response to an ongoing consumer class-action lawsuit, Sony legally argued that digital buyers do not own games but merely purchase revocable software licenses. Meanwhile, a federal court granted preliminary approval for a monumental \$7.85 billion antitrust settlement compensating US users for Sony's monopoly over digital game purchases.

**Nintendo's Next-Gen Offense: Switch 2 Price Hike, Major Upgrades, and Next Week's Double Direct** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/best-reviewed-nintendo-switch-2-games)
Nintendo is officially raising the base MSRP of the Switch 2 by \$50 to a \$500 baseline, sparking a rush on existing retail inventory. The platform holder softened the blow by releasing a massive free update adding an exclusive eight-player split-screen "CameraPlay" mode to *Mario Kart 8 Deluxe*, while developers showcased stellar next-gen ports like *Stellar Blade: Complete Edition*. Hype is set to surge even further with two newly announced Direct broadcasts, including a Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary showcase and a general Direct.

**Capcom’s Onimusha: Way of the Sword Resurrects Capcom's Samurai Legacy to Universal Acclaim** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-review)
Capcom has delivered a perfect 10/10 masterpiece with *Onimusha: Way of the Sword*, reviving its dormant samurai franchise after a 20-year hiatus. Starring Miyamoto Musashi, the game has earned glowing reviews for its Sekiro-style rhythmic parrying, expressive combat, and deeply emotional storyline. Action purists have hailed the title as a spectacular triumph that runs beautifully on the Steam Deck.

**Accidental Flight: EA Motive's Leaked Iron Man Gameplay Stuns Marvel Fans** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/ea-motives-iron-man-game-reveal-didnt-go-as-planned-but-the-leak-has-marvel-fans-more-excited-than-ever)
Pre-alpha gameplay footage of EA Motive's single-player *Iron Man* game leaked online, showcasing Tony Stark flying past skyscrapers, firing arm lasers, and battling S.H.I.E.L.D. helicarriers. Rather than panicking, EA leaned into the blunder with a cheeky viral response to what turned out to be an incredibly positive reception from fans. The leak has completely laid to rest lingering community fears that the standalone superhero adventure had been quietly shelved while Motive assisted with *Battlefield*.

**12TB Steam Leak Exposes a Decade of Valve's Archives and Playable History** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/a-playable-build-of-an-unreleased-scream-game-has-surfaced-thanks-to-the-steam-leak)
A massive security breach exposed an endpoint containing approximately 12TB of Steam platform data spanning from 2003 to 2013. Archivists have already uncovered unreleased beta builds, prototype map layouts, and cut dialogue from Valve classics like *Left 4 Dead*. Most surprisingly, the leak unzipped a fully playable, unreleased 4v1 asymmetrical *Scream* game developed by Fatshark, which fans have already successfully run via VPNs.

**The Blood of Dawnwalker Shines as Game Developers Continue to Face Deep Instability** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-blood-of-dawnwalker-review)
Former *Witcher 3* developers at Rebel Wolves scored a stellar 9/10 with their innovative day/night vampire RPG, *The Blood of Dawnwalker*. However, this critical win stands in stark contrast to the rest of the industry, where Bit Reactor furloughed 80% of its staff just weeks before launching its highly successful, 9/10-rated strategy game *Star Wars Zero Company*. Similarly reflecting industry shrinkage, Bungie confirmed it is subleasing 210,000 square feet of its Bellevue headquarters following multiple brutal layoff rounds.
