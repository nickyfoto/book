# Gaming News — 2026-06-10

## Top Story
The Xbox division is facing serious turbulence, with CEO Asha Sharma confirming in a brutal internal memo that Microsoft is planning "significant" layoffs, marketing budget cuts, and a major reassessment of its exclusivity and portfolio strategies. The restructuring comes amid a severe hardware component crisis—with console storage parts expected to cost five times more than last fall—as the company eyes a Holiday 2027 launch for its next-generation console, Project Helix.

## News & Reviews

**[Gears of War: E-Day Details Unveiled](https://www.ign.com/articles/gears-e-day-devs-on-gears-lore-game-length-new-movement-mechanics-and-ai)** · IGN
The Coalition is shaking up the formula for Marcus and Dom's prequel, ditching the classic shaky-cam roadie run for new traversal mechanics and larger flanking paths. Expect a hefty 14-plus hour campaign—the studio's longest ever—that carefully respects the series' expanded lore while introducing us to the doomed city of Kalona.

**[Alien: Isolation 2 Explores the Great Outdoors](https://www.ign.com/articles/we-wanted-to-match-the-original-and-go-beyond-alien-isolation-2s-director-on-improving-a-survivor-horror-icon)** · IGN
Creative Assembly's highly-anticipated sequel takes the terror of the xenomorph outside the claustrophobic corridors of a space station and onto the storm-ravaged wilderness of LV-921. Director Al Hope promises that the same smart, systemic AI that made the original a nightmare will return, ensuring the stalker continues to adapt to your every move.

**[Resident Evil Veronica Confirmed as Third-Person Action](https://www.ign.com/articles/resident-evil-veronica-is-a-third-person-game-capcom-confirms)** · IGN
Despite a first-person teaser, Capcom's upcoming Resident Evil Veronica remake will strictly use a third-person perspective, following in the successful footsteps of the RE2 and RE4 remakes. The team is also heavily re-evaluating the controversial handling of villain Alfred Ashford's cross-dressing storyline to provide a more nuanced, deeper dive into the character's mental state.

**[Ubisoft Closes More Studios Amid Restructuring](https://www.ign.com/articles/ubisoft-closing-2-more-studios-amid-further-cuts-and-job-losses)** · IGN
The bleeding continues at Ubisoft as the publisher shutters its Winnipeg and Belgrade studios, impacting up to 380 positions worldwide. This latest cost-cutting measure follows a brutal start to 2026 for the publisher, which has already seen multiple studio closures, mass layoffs, and project cancellations globally.

**[Fable's Life Sim Shines in New Preview](https://www.ign.com/articles/fable-let-me-choose-whether-a-talking-pig-should-live-or-become-lunch-ign-preview)** · IGN
Playground Games' Fable reboot looks absolutely gorgeous in action and features a robust "game within a game" where your moral choices heavily impact town reputation and property management. From buying the local pub to sparing a talking pig from the butcher, Albion is looking like an incredibly deep social and economic sandbox.

**[Nintendo Sets $80 Physical Price for New Fire Emblem](https://www.ign.com/articles/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-sets-standard-physical-price-at-80)** · IGN
If you want Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave on a cartridge, prepare to pay a premium. Nintendo is officially pricing the standard physical Switch 2 release at $79.99, a full $10 higher than its digital counterpart, setting a frustrating new precedent for physical game collectors.
