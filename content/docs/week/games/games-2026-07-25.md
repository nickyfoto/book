# Gaming News — 2026-07-25

## Top Story
Santa Monica Studio confirmed that the next Kratos-led *God of War* game will arrive after the newly dated *God of War Laufey* (launching February 16, 2027) and will connect directly to her storyline. It's a massive relief for fans who criticized the recent *Laufey* gameplay reveal and were worried about the Ghost of Sparta stepping out of the spotlight.

**[Astartes 2 Gets a December Release Window](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-most-anticipated-warhammer-40000-animation-ever-finally-has-a-release-date)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-most-anticipated-warhammer-40000-animation-ever-finally-has-a-release-date)
Games Workshop finally confirmed that *Astartes 2*, the highly anticipated official sequel to the best *Warhammer 40,000* fan animation ever made, will drop this December on Warhammer TV. The original animation heavily inspired the look and feel of *Space Marine 2*, making this essential viewing for 40k fans.

**[Final Fantasy 7 Revelation Tackles Open World Fatigue](https://www.ign.com/articles/final-fantasy-7-director-kept-open-world-fatigue-in-mind-as-new-remake-chapter-promises-bigger-world-than-rebirth)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/final-fantasy-7-director-kept-open-world-fatigue-in-mind-as-new-remake-chapter-promises-bigger-world-than-rebirth)
Director Naoki Hamaguchi promises that while the world in the upcoming *Final Fantasy 7 Revelation* is even bigger than *Rebirth*, the team is being extremely careful to avoid industry-wide "open world fatigue". The third and final chapter will offer players proper guidance so they know exactly what to do and where to go without feeling lost in the massive world.

**[Rockstar Co-Founder Dan Houser Defends Physical Media](https://www.ign.com/articles/rockstar-co-founder-says-if-people-want-physical-game-releases-then-companies-should-provide-it)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/rockstar-co-founder-says-if-people-want-physical-game-releases-then-companies-should-provide-it)
Amidst the ongoing debate about the death of game discs, Rockstar Games co-founder Dan Houser stated that if players want physical media, companies should be providing it. This sentiment arrives hot on the heels of Sony's plans to end physical disc production in 2028 and the controversial decision for *GTA 6* to ship as a digital code in a box.
