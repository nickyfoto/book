# Gaming News — 2026-05-22

## Top Story
Bungie is reportedly planning "significant" layoffs and officially winding down support for *Destiny 2* this June, effectively ending a 12-year era to focus entirely on their upcoming extraction shooter, *Marathon*. This sudden pivot has sparked a bitter review-bombing war on the *Marathon* Steam page between disgruntled *Destiny* veterans and hopeful Bungie defenders.

## News & Reviews

**[Assassin's Creed: Black Flag Resynced Promises Seamless Piracy](https://www.ign.com/articles/why-ubisoft-returned-to-black-flag-theres-great-value-with-the-classic-assassins-creed-experience)** 
Ubisoft Singapore is overhauling its beloved 2013 pirate adventure to deliver a seamless open world without loading screens and vastly expanded underwater exploration. While the *Resynced* remake brings back Matt Ryan as Edward Kenway and introduces revamped combat and stealth, technical constraints mean DLCs like *Freedom Cry* won't be returning.

**[Aliens: Fireteam Elite 2 Embraces Four-Player Chaos](https://www.ign.com/articles/aliens-fireteam-elite-2-the-first-preview-ign-first)** 
Dropping this summer, *Aliens: Fireteam Elite 2* upgrades the co-op formula by finally letting four players squad up against smarter, deadlier Xenomorphs. A brand new a la carte "Specialist" class lets you mix and match unlocked weapons and abilities, giving you more creative ways to survive horrifying new threats like the six-armed, player-pinning Harbinger.

**[Crimson Desert Drops Massive Quality-of-Life Update](https://www.ign.com/articles/crimson-desert-update-10800-adds-a-pond-to-your-camp-a-cute-baby-wyvern-as-a-pet-and-a-lot-more)** 
Pearl Abyss is keeping the momentum going for *Crimson Desert* with Update 1.08.00, introducing a pond for your camp, a baby Wyvern pet, and firearms for Kliff. Best of all, they finally added a feature allowing you to silence tied-up outlaws, putting a merciful end to the incessant yapping that formerly ruined bounty hunting missions.

**[Final Fantasy 11 Exploring New Expansions 24 Years Later](https://www.ign.com/articles/final-fantasy-11-team-explores-adding-new-areas-as-player-count-explodes)** 
A highly successful *Final Fantasy 14* crossover event has completely revitalized Square Enix's 2002 MMORPG, stabilizing its player count far above development expectations. Thanks to this unexpected population boom, director Yoji Fujito revealed the team is actively investigating how to free up server IDs to potentially add brand new areas and storylines to Vana'diel.

**[Riot Games Denies Vanguard Anti-Cheat is Bricking PCs](https://www.ign.com/articles/riot-games-says-it-would-not-and-cannot-use-vanguard-anti-cheat-to-brick-pcs-after-rumors-spread)** 
After a tongue-in-cheek tweet sparked widespread rumors, Riot Games officially clarified that their Vanguard anti-cheat does not and cannot "brick" normal player PCs. While their latest updates render physical Direct Memory Access (DMA) cheating hardware into useless paperweights, standard PC components and software remain completely unharmed.

**[Total War and Dawn of War IV Devs Call a Truce](https://www.ign.com/articles/dawn-of-war-iv-dev-doesnt-see-total-war-warhammer-40000-as-direct-competition-reckons-fans-will-play-both-games)** 
With *Dawn of War IV* arriving on September 17, 2026, developer King Art Games says they don't see Creative Assembly's upcoming *Total War: Warhammer 40,000* as direct competition. Instead, they believe "a rising tide lifts all boats," noting their upcoming RTS hits a manageable middle-ground in combat scale that differs greatly from *Total War*'s massive grand strategy formula.
