# Gaming News — 2026-04-02

## Top Story
In a heartbreaking revelation, the game director behind the canceled *The Last of Us Online* confirmed the ambitious multiplayer spin-off was "almost 80% complete" when Sony pulled the plug. Vinit Agarwal described the cancellation as "soul crushing," revealing he only found out 24 hours before the public announcement as Naughty Dog forcibly shifted all resources back to single-player titles like *Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet*.

## News & Reviews

**Switch 2’s GameChat Gets Paywalled** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/remember-the-switch-2s-mysterious-c-button-nintendo-is-trying-to-tempt-you-with-platinum-points-and-user-icons-just-to-press-it-now-gamechat-is-a-premium-feature)
Remember the Switch 2's mysterious "C Button"? Nintendo has officially moved its GameChat feature behind the paid Nintendo Switch Online subscription as of April 1, 2026. In a desperate bid to get players to actually use it, they are tossing out 100 Platinum Points if you boot it up before April 22, but locking basic voice chat behind a sub remains an archaic, anti-consumer Nintendo classic.

**Capcom Drops OG Resident Evil Trilogy on Steam... With DRM** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/capcom-adds-all-3-og-resident-evil-games-to-steam-but-theres-a-catch)
Surprise! The original 1996 *Resident Evil*, *Resident Evil 2*, and *Resident Evil 3: Nemesis* are finally on Steam, currently bundled for just $14.97. Unfortunately, Capcom couldn't help themselves and slapped the dreaded DRM on these decades-old classics, completely breaking Steam Deck compatibility and frustrating the modding community that has kept these games alive.

**Dead by Daylight 2 Isn't Happening** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/were-not-going-to-do-a-dead-by-daylight-2-developer-kills-possibility-of-a-sequel)
If you've been waiting for a ground-up sequel to *Dead by Daylight*, don't hold your breath. Behaviour Interactive's executives stated they are absolutely "not going to do a DBD 2," citing the risk of leaving dedicated players and their massive investments behind. Instead, they'll keep updating the 10-year-old original to ensure it stays current and accessible.

**PUBG Chases Fortnite's Crown With New PvE Mode** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/pubg-has-its-sights-set-on-fortnite-as-it-aims-to-become-a-global-cultural-icon)
*PUBG: Battlegrounds* is desperate to become a massive entertainment platform rather than just a battle royale. Their latest swing is "Xeno Point," a sci-fi co-op PvE roguelite where you fight alien bosses using your classic PUBG loadouts alongside an upcoming *Payday* mode. It’s a clear pivot to capture the expansive *Fortnite* audience, but diluting their pure, tense battle royale formula is a massive risk for the 9-year-old shooter.

**Steam Gives Its Storefront a Face Lift** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/steam-unveils-new-look-storefront-with-new-sections-and-higher-resolution-game-art-to-make-it-even-easier-for-you-to-find-new-games)
Valve is currently beta-testing a much-needed visual refresh for the Steam storefront to pull everything together cohesively. Look forward to higher-resolution game art, infinite scrolling, and dedicated sections for discounted Wishlist titles and DLC. Not everyone is thrilled, however, with some developers complaining the interface feels far too much like a cluttered mobile app store.

**Marvel MaXimum Collection Hits the Arcades** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/marvel-maximum-collection-first-impressions-x-men-arcade)
Limited Run Games has successfully compiled iconic 90s Marvel arcade brawlers, preserving original difficulty spikes while modernizing the experience with cheats, rewind, and unlimited credits. Anchored by the legendary *X-Men Arcade*, it features responsive controls and excellent SNES/Sega Genesis side-by-side versions, but questions still linger over how well its rollback netcode will handle 6-player online co-op.

**Songs of Glimmerwick Preview: A Magical Stardew** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/songs-of-glimmerwick-is-a-stylish-stardew-valley-ish-rpg-set-in-a-harry-potter-esque-world-ign-preview)
If you want the cozy vibes of *Stardew Valley* set inside a magical academy, *Songs of Glimmerwick* is shaping up to be your next big obsession. The upcoming RPG swaps out generic tool use for an *Ocarina of Time*-style flute mechanic to cast spells and tend crops, all wrapped in breathtaking isometric art inspired by Cartoon Saloon films.
