**[Hardware Prices Facing Existential Crisis]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/opinion-hardware-price-increases-have-video-games-facing-an-existential-crisis)
IGN dropped a hard-hitting opinion piece on the grim reality of skyrocketing console prices, noting that unlike previous generations, current gaming hardware is actually getting *more* expensive over time. If this trend continues with $600 base Xboxes and $900 PS5 Pros, the industry risks pricing out the next generation of gamers entirely, threatening the future of the medium.

**[Dead by Daylight Overhauling Matchmaking]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/dead-by-daylight-devs-say-matchmaking-rework-is-a-re-imagination-of-how-the-game-is-played)
As *Dead by Daylight* approaches its 10th anniversary, Behaviour Interactive is completely reimagining its matchmaking system. The developers want to move away from binary, hyper-competitive modes and instead properly reward players for altruistic, narrative-focused plays that create the chaotic horror moments the game is known for.

**[WW1 Gallipoli Preview]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/ww1-gallipoli-thrust-me-into-one-of-the-worlds-most-violent-conflicts-ign-preview)
If you're tired of arcade shooters, the upcoming *WW1 Gallipoli* looks to deliver brutal, team-based realism. The game uses photogrammetry to accurately recreate trenches and forces players to rely on authentic mechanics like bipod machine-gun setups and careful teamwork rather than lone-wolf run-and-gun tactics.

**[Outbound Preview]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/outbound-preview-an-open-world-van-life-crafting-adventure-that-understands-the-value-of-taking-life-slow)
Looking for something cozy? *Outbound* puts you in an electric camper van for a laid-back, open-world crafting adventure that releases on April 23. Players will scour the countryside for materials, upgrade their van's engine, and explore a stress-free environment to build a highly customizable mobile home.
