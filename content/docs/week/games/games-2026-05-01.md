# Gaming News — 2026-05-01

## Top Story
Developer Focus Entertainment has officially extended live support for *Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2* into a third year after the action game hit a massive milestone of 12 million players. Year 3 will roll over unused Year 2 content for free, and an updated roadmap promises highly requested perk loadouts, new playable classes, and grueling new challenges for the most dedicated Space Marines.

## News & Reviews

**[Total War: Warhammer 40,000 Devs Reveal Huge Battle Maps](https://www.ign.com/articles/total-war-warhammer-40000-devs-reveal-huge-battle-maps-planets-and-region-scale)**
Creative Assembly gave us a glimpse into *Total War: Warhammer 40,000*, showcasing massive, "honking" battle maps and entirely destructible environments. Players will fight across densely packed hive cities and desolate wastelands, clearing out forests and leveling buildings to drastically change line of sight strategies. 

**[Subnautica 2 Release Date Announcement Forces Indie Devs to Scramble](https://www.ign.com/articles/subnautica-2-release-date-announcement-forces-outbound-dev-to-bring-its-game-launch-forward)**
*Subnautica 2* is officially hitting early access on May 14, sending immediate shockwaves through the indie release calendar. The highly anticipated survival sequel's sudden date reveal forced cozy base-builder *Outbound* to bump its own launch forward to dodge competing with the leviathan.

**[Oblivion Remastered on PC is Still an Abandoned Mess](https://www.ign.com/articles/tech-experts-say-the-elder-scrolls-iv-oblivion-remastered-still-suffers-from-issues-that-plagued-it-at-launch)**
If you were hoping Bethesda had fixed the PC port of *The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered*, think again. The game hasn't received a patch since July 2025, leaving players stuck with degrading performance, memory leaks, and stuttering that make it borderline unplayable despite its massive sales success.

**[GTA 6 Won't Feature Real-World Product Placement](https://www.ign.com/articles/dont-expect-product-placement-in-gta-6-the-ceo-of-take-two-says-it-wont-do-real-world-brand-partnerships-because-all-the-brands-are-made-up)**
Despite being poised as the biggest entertainment launch ever, *GTA 6* won't be selling out to real-world brand partnerships. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick confirmed the decision, stating that players have a sixth sense for what's real, and placing actual brands into Rockstar's fiercely satirical, fictionalized world would ruin the game's purity.

**[Battlefield 6 Players Divided Over Season 3 Battle Pass Pre-Orders](https://www.ign.com/articles/battlefield-6-players-battle-pass-pre-orders)**
EA is stirring the pot by allowing *Battlefield 6* players to pre-order the Season 3 battle pass before they even know what's in it. This aggressive monetization tactic is raising eyebrows since even notoriously greedy franchises like *Call of Duty* avoid battle pass pre-orders, but some fans are perfectly happy to throw their leftover coins at it for the early unlocks.

**[Kingdom Come: Deliverance 2 Devs Face AI Backlash](https://www.ign.com/articles/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-devs-answered-fan-questions-on-reddit-and-inevitably-most-were-about-ai)**
Warhorse Studios recently held a Reddit AMA that quickly devolved into a train wreck as fans grilled the devs over their use of generative AI. After allegedly firing a human translator to cut costs, the studio leaned on PR boilerplate to dodge brutal questions, ultimately claiming they are in the process of hiring "actual humans" for their next project.
