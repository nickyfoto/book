# Gaming News — 2026-09-12

## Top Story
Blizzard Entertainment kicked off BlizzCon 2026 with a blockbuster opening ceremony featuring major game reveals across its legendary franchises. Headline announcements were led by the surprise reveals of **Diablo 5**—promising a dark post-apocalyptic return in 2029—and a new boots-on-the-ground open-world **StarCraft** shooter set for 2030.

## News & Reviews

**[Diablo 5 & StarCraft Open-World Shooter Lead BlizzCon 2026](https://www.ign.com/articles/diablo-5-announced-release-date-2029)** · IGN
Blizzard officially announced *Diablo 5* for a Spring 2029 launch, setting the dark RPG a century after *Diablo 4* in a fully post-apocalyptic Sanctuary where the Lord of Terror has already won. The sequel brings back returning classes like the Demon Hunter and Monk alongside the new, disease-spreading Plague Knight, while reintroducing procedural world generation and a 2027 development roadmap. Meanwhile, the *StarCraft* franchise is dramatically shifting genres into an open-world shooter led by *Far Cry* veteran Dan Hay, aiming for a firm 2030 release date to deliver a visceral, boots-on-the-ground perspective against massive Zerg threats like 10-foot-tall Hydralisks.

**[World of Warcraft Forever & Warcraft 3 'Forsaken Kingdom' Campaign Debut](https://www.ign.com/articles/blizzards-big-blizzcon-2026-opening-ceremony-mic-drop-is-world-of-warcraft-forever)** · IGN
Blizzard capped its ceremony by unveiling *World of Warcraft Forever*, a new "Classic Plus" MMO launching November 4 that expands year one of WoW with three new zones, over 1,000 quests, nine dungeons, and a new playable race. In a massive surprise for strategy fans, Blizzard also shadow-dropped *Forsaken Kingdom*, a 30-hour standalone campaign for *Warcraft 3 Reforged* featuring a MOBA-inspired Lordaeron prologue, a quest-driven Forsaken story, and the new Forsaken Paladin hero unit. IGN praised the campaign's ambitious design and nostalgic "Definitive Edition" visual mode while noting some uneven graphics in cutscenes.

**[Marvel's Wolverine Review & Insomniac's Scent Trail Debate](https://www.ign.com/articles/wolverine-developer-insomniac-says-logans-scent-trail-isnt-always-on-amid-fart-gas-toggle-requests)** · IGN
Insomniac's PlayStation 5 exclusive *Marvel's Wolverine* earned a 6/10 review from IGN, which praised its strong character work but noted that its slick combat wears thin well before the conclusion. Online controversy erupted after critics dubbed Logan's red tracking scent trail "fart gas" and requested an accessibility toggle to turn it off completely. Insomniac's James Stevenson clarified that the scent trail isn't always active and only appears when Logan tracks specific story clues, collectibles, or scents, maintaining that it reflects canon mutant powers rather than constant hand-holding.

**[Xbox Takes a Big Gamble on Kojima's Physint After Sony Drops Project](https://www.ign.com/articles/physint-could-be-xboxs-biggest-gamble-yet)** · IGN
Microsoft scored a major PR victory by funding Hideo Kojima's upcoming action-espionage title *Physint* after Sony dropped the project over missed deadlines and budget concerns. While securing the spiritual successor to *Metal Gear Solid* boosts Xbox's prestige lineup, investing millions during internal restructuring and lay-offs creates significant morale and financial risks if the title fails to become a massive commercial success.

**[Level-5 Boss Apologizes Following Generative AI Showcase Backlash](https://www.ign.com/articles/level-5-boss-addresses-ai-backlash-after-fans-hit-out-at-game-reveal-showcase)** · IGN
Developer Level-5 faced intense fan backlash after viewers spotted generative AI background art and inconsistent character models during its recent showcase for *Professor Layton and the Curious Village*, *Yo-Kai Watch 2*, and *DecaPolice*. CEO Akihiro Hino issued an apology, explaining that genAI was utilized to streamline 3D asset conversion and reduce development cycles from five years to two, while reassuring fans that scenarios and character designs remain human-made.

**[Heroes of the Storm Revived with First New Hero in Six Years](https://www.ign.com/articles/heroes-of-the-storm-gets-first-new-character-in-six-years)** · IGN
Blizzard surprised MOBA players by announcing Xal'atath, the primary antagonist of WoW's Midnight expansion, as the first new hero added to *Heroes of the Storm* since 2020. Official content support ended in 2022, but Blizzard executives revealed that strong community engagement with Xal'atath's release could lead to bigger updates and additional heroes in the future.

**[Na Hong-Jin's Sci-Fi Epic 'Hope' Launches Historic US Theatrical Run](https://www.ign.com/articles/how-a-vfx-controversy-propelled-the-strange-journey-of-na-hong-jins-sci-fi-blockbuster-hope)** · IGN
Director Na Hong-jin's alien action-thriller *Hope* expanded into 1,500 US theaters via Neon—marking the widest release for a Korean film since *Parasite*—and grossed over \$1 million on its first day. Despite polarizing Cannes reviews regarding its motion-captured CGI aliens starring Michael Fassbender and Alicia Vikander, the intense \$137-f-bomb spectacle has generated massive fan interest.

**[Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World Preview Highlights 500-Beast Roster](https://www.ign.com/articles/dragon-quest-monsters-the-withered-world-has-500-beasts-to-collect-and-theyre-all-super-weird-ign-preview)** · IGN
Square Enix's upcoming monster-collecting RPG *Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World* boasts a staggering roster of over 500 collectible creatures, synthesis fusion mechanics, and returning protagonists Bianca and Nera from *Dragon Quest V*. IGN praised Akira Toriyama's vibrant art style and unique wrangler abilities, noting that accessibility camera settings help mitigate motion sickness caused by the rolling world map.
