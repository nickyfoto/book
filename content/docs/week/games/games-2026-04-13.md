# Gaming News — 2026-04-13

## Top Story
Hackers known as ShinyHunters have breached Rockstar Games' data through a third-party application, demanding a ransom and threatening to leak sensitive company information ahead of the launch of *Grand Theft Auto 6*. Rockstar has actively downplayed the breach, insisting that only "non-material" company info was accessed and that development on their highly-anticipated open-world crime epic remains entirely unimpacted.

## News & Reviews

**Pragmata Review** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/pragmata-review)
Capcom's *Pragmata* delivers a brisk, 12-hour campaign that feels ripped straight out of the Xbox 360 era, focusing on highly satisfying, gimmick-driven combat rather than deep storytelling. The core loop—using real-time grid-based hacking to expose robot weak spots while blasting away with punchy weapons—is incredibly engaging and easily makes up for the somewhat generic sci-fi plot. This is definitely worth a playthrough for anyone who misses punchy, straightforward, old-school action shooters.

**New Metro Sequel Revealed as 'Metro 2039'** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/new-metro-sequel-reportedly-set-for-reveal-at-microsoft-xbox-show-this-week-after-major-gameplay-leak-over-the-weekend)
Following significant gameplay leaks over the weekend, reports indicate that developer 4A Games will officially unveil *Metro 2039* at an upcoming Xbox showcase. It has been six years since *Metro Exodus* wrapped up its incredible survival-horror journey, making this the perfect time to gear up for a return to Dmitry Glukhovsky's haunting subterranean apocalypse.

**Fable Reaffirms Fall 2026 Release Window** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/fable-social-media-account-reiterates-fall-2026-release-window-amid-rumors-of-an-internal-delay-to-avoid-gta-6)
Despite swirling rumors that Microsoft pushed Playground Games' *Fable* reboot internally to avoid clashing with the upcoming behemoth *Grand Theft Auto 6*, the official social media accounts have stepped in to confidently restate the "Autumn 2026" release plan. We should get definitive answers regarding Microsoft's exact schedule during the upcoming Xbox Games Showcase on June 7, but for now, expect a holiday trip to Albion.

**Marathon Players Debate Raid Difficulty** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/marathon-players-are-coming-up-with-ideas-for-how-to-get-more-people-into-its-ultra-hardcore-raid)
Bungie's *Marathon* is facing an intense community debate regarding its ultra-hardcore endgame raid, "Cryo Archive," which requires a hefty 5,000-credit loadout just to enter. Players are currently suggesting features like free weekly loadouts to make the elaborately designed, high-stakes mode more accessible to casual players rather than exclusively catering to the elite extraction shooter crowd.

**Pickmon Rebrands to 'Pickmos' Following Clone Backlash** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/after-widespread-criticism-for-being-a-pokemon-clone-pickmon-developer-announces-name-change-to-better-align-with-our-brand-identity-and-lore)
After intense criticism that its monster designs blatantly plagiarized *Pokémon* and fan-made creations, developer PocketGame has changed the game's title to *Pickmos*. While the studio claims the name change represents a "grand Cosmos," it doesn't appear they have actually altered the controversial, heavily derivative in-game creature designs, further frustrating the gaming community.
