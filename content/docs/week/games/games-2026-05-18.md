# Gaming News — 2026-05-18

## Top Story
Sony is officially rolling back its strategy to bring massive single-player games to PC. During a company town hall meeting, PlayStation studio business CEO Hermen Hulst affirmed that major upcoming narrative-driven titles like *Ghost of Yotei* and *Marvel's Wolverine* will remain permanent console exclusives to drive PS5 hardware sales. 

## News & Reviews

**[PlayStation Plus Prices Are Going Up Again](https://www.ign.com/articles/sony-announces-playstation-plus-price-increases-due-to-ongoing-market-conditions)** · IGN
Just as we march closer to the launch of *GTA 6*, Sony is hiking the price of PlayStation Plus subscriptions starting May 20, vaguely blaming "ongoing market conditions". The 1-month and 3-month tiers will see jumps of $1 and $3 respectively, adding yet another annoying squeeze to your gaming budget. With Microsoft recently cutting Xbox Game Pass prices, this move feels particularly disconnected from the current market reality.

**[GTA 6 Budget Reaches Astounding Highs as Release Details Clarify](https://www.ign.com/articles/gta-6-release-date-about-18-months-behind-original-target-take-two-boss-reveals-suggesting-spring-2025-was-once-the-goal-internally)** · IGN
Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick revealed that *Grand Theft Auto 6* is currently running about 18 months behind its original internal target of Spring 2025. The long-awaited sequel carries an estimated $1 to $1.5 billion budget, dwarfing practically every other AAA video game budget in existence. Despite the massive delays and astronomical costs, Zelnick sounds firmly confident that the game will indeed hit its locked-in November 19, 2026 launch date.

**[Lego Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/lego-batman-legacy-of-the-dark-knight-review)** · IGN
TT Games is back with an 8/10 plastic pastiche that seamlessly mashes together iconic scenes from Batman's cinematic history. The combat borrows heavily from Rocksteady’s *Arkham* series and feels great, though the lack of difficulty makes encounters more of a chaotic joyride than a true tactical challenge. The open-world Gotham is absolutely packed with collectibles and clever puzzles, cementing it as one of the best Lego titles in years. 

**[Subnautica 2 Players Debate the Value of Immortal Fish](https://www.ign.com/articles/subnautica-2-dev-gives-players-hope-amid-the-games-first-hot-topic-following-launch)** · IGN
*Subnautica 2* has launched in early access with a strict "no-kill" philosophy for its aquatic life, leading to heated debates among players who are tired of being defenseless against hostile alien wildlife. Unknown Worlds developers admitted the topic is being heavily debated internally and promised that better tools to deter predators will arrive in future updates.

**[Terraria Celebrates 15 Years With Promises of More Updates](https://www.ign.com/articles/terraria-marks-15-years-and-70-million-players-with-a-promise-to-continue-updates-beyond-146-and-crossplay-patch)** · IGN
Re-Logic’s beloved sandbox adventure has crossed an astronomical 70 million copies sold across its impressive 15-year lifespan. Proving the "final update" joke will never die, the developers just confirmed that content drops and updates will continue well beyond the upcoming 1.4.6 and crossplay patches.

**[Final Fantasy 7 Rebirth Switch 2 Preorders Go Live With Promo Card](https://www.ign.com/articles/final-fantasy-7-rebirth-switch-2-preorders-free-magic-the-gathering-card)** · IGN
The highly anticipated Nintendo Switch 2 port for *Final Fantasy 7 Rebirth* drops on June 3, and physical preorders have officially popped up at retailers like Amazon UK. Early buyers will score a highly limited, borderless Zack Fair *Magic: The Gathering* card featuring variant art by Tetsuya Nomura.
