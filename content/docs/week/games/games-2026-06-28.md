ArcSystem Works took the stage at Evo 2026 to discuss their highly anticipated fighting game, *Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls*, revealing they have entirely reworked Magneto to focus heavily on his gravity and debris manipulation rather than traditional beam attacks. The developers noted that they studied actual Marvel comic panels to ensure each attack perfectly captures the iconic Master of Magnetism, cementing him as a major threat on the battlefield.
