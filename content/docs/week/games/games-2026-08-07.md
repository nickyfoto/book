# Gaming News — 2026-08-07

## Top Story
Take-Two has officially confirmed that Rockstar Games' highly anticipated **Grand Theft Auto 6** remains firmly on track for its November 19, 2026 release date, with an exclusive timed "Extended Look" premiering on Netflix on August 27. Publisher Take-Two is actively defending the game's controversial \$80 standard pricing and digital-only launch, calling it an "incredible bargain" that is priced to over-deliver on value.

## News & Reviews

**The Death of Discs? Nintendo Reaffirms Physical Media as Sony Prepares to Kill PS5 Discs** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/nintendo-says-physical-games-still-account-for-385-of-its-software-sales-following-sonys-decision-to-kill-ps5-discs)
Nintendo has confirmed that boxed physical games still account for a substantial 38.5% of its software sales, signaling that the Mario maker has no plans to abandon physical cartridges anytime soon. This stands in stark contrast to Sony's widely criticized decision to completely discontinue physical PS5 disc production starting in January 2028. Meanwhile, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick defended the digital-only launch of *GTA 6*, comparing physical game discs to vinyl records, though he refused to completely rule out a physical release down the line. 

**Marvel Tōkon: Fighting Souls PC Port Overwhelmed by PSN Outrage and Performance Issues** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/marvel-tokon-stumbles-to-mixed-steam-reviews-as-miserable-pc-port-complaints-prompt-response-from-arc-system-works)
Arc System Works' highly anticipated fighter *Marvel Tōkon: Fighting Souls* has stumbled onto Steam with a disappointing 44% "Mixed" user rating at launch. While players are praising the core gameplay as incredible, they are absolutely hammering publisher Sony's mandatory PSN account-linking requirement, which has effectively blocked the game in over 100 countries. Severe framerate drops on standard rigs, rollback netcode spikes, and a complete lack of Linux and Steam Deck compatibility have further soured the launch experience.

**Big Walk Review: A Heartwarming, Communication-Fueled Masterpiece** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/big-walk-review)
IGN has published its review for House House’s *Big Walk*, a co-op-only puzzle adventure that uses proximity-based voice chat to turn communication itself into a brilliant gameplay mechanic. Controlling goofy, stack-of-balls characters with googly eyes, players are dropped into a gorgeous open world to solve brilliant teamwork-oriented challenges and naturally develop their own visual ciphers. The review lauds the game as a stellar hanging-out experience where relationships are the ultimate superpower, though it notes minor frustration regarding save files being strictly tied to the host player.

**Fortnite Season 4 Override Keeps Sprites and Teases Legendary Gaming Crossovers** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/fortnite-is-keeping-sprites-for-next-season-a-gaming-themed-crossover-expected-to-feature-sonic-persona-and-tetris)
Epic Games has confirmed that its wildly popular, Pokémon-like "Sprites" will remain a core part of *Fortnite* in Chapter 7 Season 4: Override, which is scheduled to launch on August 20. The new season will introduce five community-designed Sprites—including the ammo-boosting Bullet Sprite and the loot-grabbing Dumpster Dive Sprite—alongside a customizable Sprite Garden experience. Additionally, sneaky dataminers have discovered in-game files pointing to massive upcoming crossovers with *Sonic the Hedgehog*, *Persona*, *Pac-Man*, and *Tetris* later this month.

**Sadie Sink Discusses Her Secret MCU Debut as Jean Grey in Spider-Man** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/sadie-sink-finally-discusses-secret-spider-man-role-teases-conversations-with-marvel-for-whats-next)
Following the record-shattering box office debut of *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*, actress Sadie Sink has finally opened up about her highly classified role as Jean Grey. Sink admitted she knew nothing about the powerful telepath prior to meeting with Kevin Feige, but instantly viewed the flawed, complex antagonist role as a complete "no-brainer". She also teased that she has already had exciting conversations with upcoming *X-Men* director Jake Schreier about where her character's backstory and struggles will lead in the future.

**Target Reportedly Slicing Open Pokémon Card Boxes to Defeat Scalpers** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/target-is-slicing-open-pokmon-card-boxes-before-theyre-sold-in-a-bid-to-deter-scalpers-fans-say)
Target is reportedly taking extreme measures to combat bulk resellers by directing employees to slice the shrink wrap off new Pokémon card boxes in stores and during online fulfillment. While this packaging removal makes no difference to actual collectors who want to open the cards, it heavily devalues the items for scalpers who flip sealed boxes for a profit. Most fans have reacted positively to the move, though some warn it could risk damaging stock or artificially drive up the price of surviving sealed boxes.
