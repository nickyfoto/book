# Gaming News — 2026-08-21

## Top Story

## News & Reviews

**[FromSoftware Apologizes as The Duskbloods Network Test Hits Server Wall](https://www.ign.com/articles/fromsoftware-sincerely-apologizes-for-the-duskbloods-network-test-server-issues-ends-first-slot-early)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/fromsoftware-sincerely-apologizes-for-the-duskbloods-network-test-server-issues-ends-first-slot-early)
FromSoftware has officially apologized and prematurely ended the first closed network test session for its highly anticipated PvPvE action title, *The Duskbloods*. Gamers who managed to snag coveted codes for the Nintendo Switch 2 exclusive were left staring at a "failed to log in" error screen as servers buckled under the load. While server issues are a disappointment, the developer is actively working on stability upgrades ahead of the game's scheduled release later this year.

**[Double Fine Regains IP and Publishing Rights Post-Xbox Split](https://www.ign.com/articles/double-fine-owns-ip-and-publishing-rights-for-all-of-its-games-following-xbox-split-psychonauts-studio-confirms)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/double-fine-owns-ip-and-publishing-rights-for-all-of-its-games-following-xbox-split-psychonauts-studio-confirms)
Double Fine Productions CEO Tim Schafer confirmed that the studio has successfully clawed back the IP and publishing rights for all its games following their split from Xbox. Gamers can now directly support the studio's independent future and extend its development "runway" by purchasing catalog titles like *Psychonauts* and *Brutal Legend*. To kick off this independent era, the studio has launched a Kickstarter for its *Amnesia Fortnight 2026* game jam.

**[Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 and Blue Eye Samurai Lead Netflix’s Packed Anime Slate](https://www.ign.com/articles/netflix-reveals-packed-anime-slate-full-announcement-rundown-trailers-and-images)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/netflix-reveals-packed-anime-slate-full-announcement-rundown-trailers-and-images)
Netflix stole the show at Anime NYC by dropping high-octane trailers and release windows for its premier animated game and comic adaptations. The English trailer for *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2* revealed an October 20, 2026 release date, while *Blue Eye Samurai* received a January 2027 window for Season 2 and an official greenlight for its third and final season. With reboots like WiT Studio's *The One Piece* also slated for early 2027, gamers and anime fans have an incredibly packed lineup to look forward to.

**[The Blood of Dawnwalker Will Skip 60fps on Consoles](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-blood-of-dawnwalker-won-t-run-at-60fps-on-console-and-fans-arent-happy)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-blood-of-dawnwalker-won-t-run-at-60fps-on-console-and-fans-arent-happy)
Developer Rebel Wolves has disappointed console gamers by confirming that its highly anticipated dark action-RPG, *The Blood of Dawnwalker*, will not feature a 60fps mode. Even on high-end hardware like the PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X, the 14th-century vampire adventure will top out at a 40fps Balanced mode targeting dynamic 4K. With the game's precise swordplay mechanics relying heavily on smooth performance, many fans are already voicing plans to switch to PC ahead of the September 3 release.

**[Stray Dog: nobody cares Wins Over Gamers on Social Media](https://www.ign.com/articles/soulslike-game-where-you-play-as-a-sword-wielding-dog-goes-viral-because-well-youre-a-dog-wielding-a-sword)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/soulslike-game-where-you-play-as-a-sword-wielding-dog-goes-viral-because-well-youre-a-dog-wielding-a-sword)
Indie developer Parry Frames Studio has caught lightning in a bottle with *Stray Dog: nobody cares*, an upcoming Soulslike that has gone viral on social media. Players control a stray hound wielding a sword in its jaws on a brutal, parry-heavy quest to rescue its captured knight master. Following the sudden explosion of wishlist support, the developer—who previously received an Epic Games Megagrant—reassured fans that they have the technical chops to deliver a high-quality action game.

**[Veteran Actor Confirms Casting in Naughty Dog's Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet](https://www.ign.com/articles/intergalactic-the-heretic-prophet-cast-expands-with-uncharted-the-last-of-us-veteran)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/intergalactic-the-heretic-prophet-cast-expands-with-uncharted-the-last-of-us-veteran)
Industry veteran Robin Atkin Downes confirmed he is back working with Naughty Dog for their highly secretive, upcoming sci-fi title *Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet*. The actor posted a clip of himself in performance-capture gear, suggesting active production is underway on the studio's most expensive and ambitious game to date. Notably, the bulk of this project’s development team transitioned over only after Naughty Dog officially cancelled its long-gestating *The Last of Us Online* multiplayer project.
