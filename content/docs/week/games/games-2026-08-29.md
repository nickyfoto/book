# Gaming News — 2026-08-29

## Top Story
**Rockstar Games** has officially confirmed that **Grand Theft Auto 6** will launch on November 19, 2026, completely free of microtransactions and generative AI. While the studio is skipping real-money purchases for its core campaign, it is offering a \$99.99 Ultimate Edition that packages exclusive vehicles, weapons, cosmetic sets, and access to select in-game shops.

## News & Reviews

**[The Witcher 4 Will Have a Physical Edition Even if Discs Go Away, CD Projekt Red Confirms](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-witcher-4-will-have-a-physical-edition-even-if-discs-go-away-cd-projekt-red-confirms)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-witcher-4-will-have-a-physical-edition-even-if-discs-go-away-cd-projekt-red-confirms)
CD Projekt Red joint-CEO Michał Nowakowski confirmed that **The Witcher 4** will still receive a boxed physical release filled with tangible collectibles and "feelies," even if Sony ceases manufacturing physical PlayStation discs in January 2028. Although the studio acknowledged it cannot control platform holder manufacturing policies, the team insisted on preserving the unboxing ritual for physical collectors. This stance offers huge relief to traditional media enthusiasts who feared Sony's impending digital shift would wipe out boxed RPG releases entirely.

**[EA Motive’s Iron Man Game Reveal 'Didn't Go as Planned,' But the Leak Has Marvel Fans More Excited Than Ever](https://www.ign.com/articles/ea-motives-iron-man-game-reveal-didnt-go-as-planned-but-the-leak-has-marvel-fans-more-excited-than-ever)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/ea-motives-iron-man-game-reveal-didnt-go-as-planned-but-the-leak-has-marvel-fans-more-excited-than-ever)
Early pre-alpha footage of **EA Motive's Iron Man** leaked online showing Tony Stark soaring past skyscrapers and taking down enemies across S.H.I.E.L.D. helicarriers, prompting EA to lean into the blunder with a cheeky viral response. The unexpected gameplay reveal put an end to persistent community fears that the single-player project had been quietly canceled while Motive assisted on *Battlefield*. Enthusiastic fan reactions to the flight mechanics, armor customization, and combat have turned an accidental leak into a massive wave of organic hype.

**[Dragon’s Dogma 2: Dark Arisen Will Send You On a Chilly Loot Grind | gamescom 2026](https://www.ign.com/articles/dragons-dogma-2-dark-arisen-will-send-you-on-a-chilly-loot-grind-gamescom-2026)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/dragons-dogma-2-dark-arisen-will-send-you-on-a-chilly-loot-grind-gamescom-2026)
Capcom debuted an extensive 90-minute gamescom demo of **Dragon’s Dogma 2: Dark Arisen**, introducing the frigid northern frontier of Norgan, interactive blizzard hazards, and gruesome encounters like a dragon that vomits zombies. Expansion director Kenta Kinoshita detailed a new *Destiny*-style progression loop where players uncover unidentified relics across the snowfields and decode them at camp to prepare for 12 increasingly brutal Lost Rites dungeons. The verdict is overwhelmingly positive for action RPG fans, showing Capcom doubling down on organic open-world friction, environmental tactics, and deep character builds.

**[We Played State of Decay 3 and It’s in Good Shape Despite the Xbox Upheaval | gamescom 2026](https://www.ign.com/articles/we-played-state-of-decay-3-and-its-in-good-shape-despite-the-xbox-upheaval)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/we-played-state-of-decay-3-and-its-in-good-shape-despite-the-xbox-upheaval)
IGN went hands-on with an early alpha build of **State of Decay 3** at gamescom 2026, reporting that the zombie survival sequel is thriving despite Undead Labs changing ownership amid the broader "Xbox reset". The demo highlighted significantly punchier third-person gunplay, map-wide infestation networks with multi-stage hearts to destroy, and an expanded survivor trait system where unique habits directly impact base resources. The showing proves the project is progressing smoothly toward a closed playtest in late 2026 and a planned 2027 launch on Xbox Series X|S, PS5, and PC.

**[IGN’s Top-Reviewed Nintendo Switch 2 Games](https://www.ign.com/articles/best-reviewed-nintendo-switch-2-games)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/best-reviewed-nintendo-switch-2-games)
Celebrating just over a year since the console's debut, IGN highlighted its top-reviewed Nintendo Switch 2 first-party releases scoring an 8/10 or above. The cream of the crop remains the 10/10 masterpiece **Donkey Kong Bananza**, hailed as a groundbreaking 3D platformer successor to *Super Mario Odyssey*, backed by upgraded editions of *Tears of the Kingdom* and *Breath of the Wild*. High-scoring standouts like *Splatoon Raiders* (9/10), *Pokemon Pokopia* (9/10), and *Metroid Prime 4: Beyond* (8/10) demonstrate that the platform has quickly established an enviable software lineup.

**[Fibbage Card Game Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/fibbage-card-game-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/fibbage-card-game-review)
Jackbox Games has made the jump from screens to the tabletop with the standalone **Fibbage Card Game**, adapting its classic digital party bluffing formula into a boxed card format. IGN gave the physical release high praise for its 300 varied prompt cards and clever mechanics that reward genuine wit over shock value, noting only a slight drawback in players potentially recognizing each other's handwriting. It is an easy recommendation for board game nights, successfully translating a decade-old party staple into an accessible physical format that works for groups of all experience levels.
