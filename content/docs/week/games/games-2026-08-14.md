**[Kingdom Hearts Is a Billion-Dollar Franchise, Disney Declares Ahead of Hotly Anticipated D23 Panel](https://www.ign.com/articles/kingdom-hearts-is-a-billion-dollar-franchise-disney-declares-ahead-of-hotly-anticipated-d23-panel)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/kingdom-hearts-is-a-billion-dollar-franchise-disney-declares-ahead-of-hotly-anticipated-d23-panel)

**[Xbox’s Separation from Compulsion Games Seems Complete as Studio Is Listed as the Publisher of South of Midnight on Steam](https://www.ign.com/articles/xboxs-separation-from-compulsion-games-seems-complete-as-studio-is-listed-as-the-publisher-of-south-of-midnight-on-steam)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/xboxs-separation-from-compulsion-games-seems-complete-as-studio-is-listed-as-the-publisher-of-south-of-midnight-on-steam)
In a quiet but significant move, *South of Midnight* developer Compulsion Games has reclaimed its publishing title on Steam, signaling that its separation from Xbox Game Studios is complete. This spin-out is part of Xbox's massive, layoff-driven "reset" that also saw Double Fine, Undead Labs, and Ninja Theory regain their independence. While some of these other studios still show Xbox as their Steam publisher for now, this change represents a massive victory for Compulsion's publishing autonomy as they carve out their own independent path.

**[25 Years of Mafia Video Games: An Oral History](https://www.ign.com/articles/25-years-of-mafia-video-games-an-oral-history)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/25-years-of-mafia-video-games-an-oral-history)
To celebrate the franchise's 25th anniversary, 2K has launched *Mafia: The Omertà Collection*, bundling all definitive editions alongside a free 60fps console update for *Mafia: Definitive Edition* on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. Hangar 13 developers took a trip down memory lane, discussing the series' evolution from its naive PC roots in Brno to its modern transition to Unreal Engine for *The Old Country*. Despite past structural reboots and shifting player expectations around open-world elements, the team remains deeply grateful for a passionate community that has kept this narrative-driven crime franchise self-sustaining.

**[Lanterns Series Premiere Review: Restoring Faith in James Gunn’s DCU](https://www.ign.com/articles/lanterns-series-premiere-review-restoring-faith-in-james-gunns-dcu)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/lanterns-series-premiere-review-restoring-faith-in-james-gunns-dcu)
The series premiere of HBO's *Lanterns* has delivered a stellar win for James Gunn's DCU, crafting a grounded, *True Detective*-style murder mystery set in rural Nebraska. The episode is powered by the electric, highly hostile chemistry between Kyle Chandler's paranoid, veteran Hal Jordan and Aaron Pierre's stoic John Stewart. While Green Lantern purists expecting a high-budget cosmic spectacle might be disappointed, those who embrace its gritty, character-driven tone are in for a fantastic ride.

**[Grand Theft Auto 2 – The Legacy Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/grand-theft-auto-2-the-legacy-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/grand-theft-auto-2-the-legacy-review)
Revisiting *Grand Theft Auto 2* highlights critical mechanical improvements over its predecessor, including a smoother driving camera, auto-aim assists, a ten-point health bar, and a church-based save system. However, its retro-futuristic Anywhere City setting and clunky, tank-like on-foot controls make it a difficult game to love today. Arriving right before the 3D revolution spearheaded by *Driver*, *GTA 2* stands as a polished but stylistically mismatched relic that was ultimately a dimension short of the future.

**[Wingspan Pocket Card Game Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/wingspan-pocket-card-game-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/wingspan-pocket-card-game-review)
Elizabeth Hargrave has successfully condensed her multi-million-selling bird ecology board game into a fast-paced, highly portable 15-minute card game. By removing complex food dice and making double-sided cards act as both food resources and birds, *Wingspan Pocket* retains the satisfying engine-building strategies of the original with a fraction of the rules overhead. Though it introduces a bit more luck and less overall strategic depth, its massive gains in convenience make it a stellar addition to any tabletop shelf.

**[Toy Story 3 Will Be Delisted on Xbox Just Weeks Before Launch of New Remaster](https://www.ign.com/articles/toy-story-3-will-be-delisted-on-xbox-just-weeks-before-launch-of-new-remaster)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/toy-story-3-will-be-delisted-on-xbox-just-weeks-before-launch-of-new-remaster)
The beloved 2010 movie tie-in *Toy Story 3* is being pulled from the digital Xbox store on September 29, 2026. This move prepares the way for the October 15 launch of *Toy Story 3: Complete Edition*, a remaster from Atari and Digital Eclipse that brings upgraded graphics, resolution, and previously exclusive content to modern consoles. While players who already own the original can still play it, those looking to save some cash should grab the \$9.99 version on Xbox before they are forced to pay for the new remaster's bells and whistles.
