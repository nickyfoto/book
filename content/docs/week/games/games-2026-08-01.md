## News & Reviews

**[Palworld TCG Prices Skyrocket as Scalpers Profit](https://www.ign.com/articles/palworld-tcg-prices-skyrocket-as-ebay-scalpers-profit-off-of-card-game-launch)** · IGN  
The official *Palworld* trading card game just launched, and naturally, scalpers are already ruining the market for fans. Extremely rare cards like the Gold Soul Cattiva have sold for up to $4,000 on eBay, with booster cases hovering around $1,800. It's a massive financial success for Pocketpair, but a total nightmare for everyday collectors looking to play.

**[AI Is Making Games Worse, And For What?](https://www.ign.com/articles/ai-is-making-games-worse-and-for-what)** · IGN  
The tech industry's obsession with AI is driving up hardware prices across the board, funneling memory production to data centers and causing a brutal RAM crisis. In exchange for these increasingly expensive PC builds, gamers are being fed divisive AI features like DLSS 5, which essentially redraws frames to the point of looking like mobile game ads, alongside useless in-game AI copilots.

**[Hololive Dreams Uses RPG Mechanics to Create a Giant Rhythm Game](https://www.ign.com/articles/hololive-dreams-rhythm-rpg-mechanics)** · IGN  
VTuber fans have a new obsession with *Hololive Dreams*, a rhythm game layered with RPG elements that recently launched on PC and mobile. Featuring 54 playable virtual talents and over 150 songs, the game has players building a virtual theme park and completing quests, which gives non-rhythm players a great incentive to jump into the gameplay loop.

**[Spider-Man: Brand New Day Beats Avengers: Endgame Box Office Record](https://www.ign.com/articles/spider-man-brand-new-day-beats-avengers-endgame-record-to-claim-biggest-domestic-opening-day-of-all-time)** · IGN  
*Spider-Man: Brand New Day* just claimed the biggest domestic opening day of all time with $168 million, narrowly beating out *Avengers: Endgame*. The film's incredible success comes despite a high-quality bootleg leaking across social media on release day. For comic fans, the movie is a must-watch as it introduces Sadie Sink as a darker, dangerous version of Jean Grey, officially kicking off the MCU's bold reboot of the X-Men.
