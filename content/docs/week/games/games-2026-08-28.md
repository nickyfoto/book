# Gaming News — 2026-08-28

## Top Story
**Rockstar Games** officially pulled back the curtain on **Grand Theft Auto 6**, confirming that its playable world is three times larger than *Red Dead Redemption 2* and that Vice City itself doubles the size of *GTA 5's* Los Santos. The blockbuster crime epic has already surpassed **4.5 million pre-orders** generating nearly \$450 million in revenue ahead of its November 19, 2026 release on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S.

## News & Reviews

**[How the GTA 6 Map Size Stacks Up to Other Rockstar Games Like RDR2 and GTA 5](https://www.ign.com/articles/how-the-gta-6-map-size-stacks-up-to-other-rockstar-games-like-rdr2-and-gta-5)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/how-the-gta-6-map-size-stacks-up-to-other-rockstar-games-like-rdr2-and-gta-5)
Rockstar revealed that GTA 6's map is three times larger than *Red Dead Redemption 2*, with Vice City and its surrounding suburbs measuring 11 times bigger than Los Santos while packing hundreds of fully enterable interiors. Head of development Rob Nelson explained that the studio built directly upon RDR2's technological breakthroughs to craft its most dense, dynamic, and distraction-heavy open world to date. For gamers, this promises a generational leap in immersion and exploration when Leonida opens up this fall.

**[After GTA 6: Extended Look Showed Rockstar’s Game Running at 30FPS, Tech Experts Say a 60FPS Console Version Is 'Extremely Unlikely’](https://www.ign.com/articles/after-gta-6-extended-look-showed-rockstars-game-running-at-30fps-tech-experts-say-a-60fps-console-version-is-extremely-unlikely-and-genuinely-challenging-even-on-pc)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/after-gta-6-extended-look-showed-rockstars-game-running-at-30fps-tech-experts-say-a-60fps-console-version-is-extremely-unlikely-and-genuinely-challenging-even-on-pc)
Following Rockstar’s 27-minute Extended Look broadcast, Digital Foundry’s technical analysis concluded that GTA 6 is strictly tuned around a locked **30fps target** at an internal 1440p resolution on base PlayStation 5 hardware. The experts emphasized that clawing back enough CPU and GPU frame budget to hit 60fps on current consoles is virtually impossible without gutting the world's simulation density, and even high-end PCs will face serious CPU bottlenecks down the road. If you've been holding out hope for a high-framerate performance mode on PS5 or Xbox Series X, it's time to settle in for a cinematic 30fps presentation.

**[Gallipoli Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/gallipoli-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/gallipoli-review)
BlackMill Games released *Gallipoli*, the fourth and visually superior chapter in its World War 1 shooter franchise, transporting players to the brutal clashes between the British and Ottoman Empires along the Middle Eastern front. IGN praised the title's uncompromising commitment to historical accuracy, highlighting how its dust mechanics, deadly artillery strikes, and realistic weapon suppression force squad coordination and instill authentic battlefield panic. The review’s clear verdict is that *Gallipoli* is one of the finest squad-based tactical shooters around—a must-play for tactical purists tired of run-and-gun arcade shooters.

**[NBA 2K27 Review So Far](https://www.ign.com/articles/nba-2k27-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/nba-2k27-review)
IGN dropped its early review-in-progress for *NBA 2K27*, commending the vastly improved, responsive defensive mechanics and realistic logic-based Dunk Meter that properly rewards rim protection. However, the early verdict sours considerably off the hardwood: the 11-chapter *Fire and Concrete* narrative falls flat, while the new *MyCareer Eras* feature enforces a punitive 60-overall rating reset across separate eras, making microtransactions feel more aggressive than ever. Hoopers will love the balanced on-court action, but the franchise's stubborn pay-to-progress grind remains an exhausting barrier.

**[Xbox CEO Says Project Helix Includes a 'Family of Devices’](https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-ceo-says-project-helix-includes-a-family-of-devices)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/xbox-ceo-says-project-helix-includes-a-family-of-devices)
During a gamescom 2026 interview, Xbox CEO Asha Sharma disclosed that Microsoft's next-generation hardware project, codenamed **Project Helix**, represents a full "family of devices" rather than a standalone console. Previously announced with native PC game compatibility and rumored for a holiday 2027 arrival, Helix appears poised to introduce unconventional form factors as Microsoft navigates shifting hardware business models. This development indicates that Microsoft's upcoming hardware push will attempt to bridge console and PC gaming far more aggressively than current platforms.
