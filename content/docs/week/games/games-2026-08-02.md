# Gaming News — 2026-08-02

## Top Story
Legendary game creator Hideo Kojima has weighed in on the biggest pop culture event of the year, sharing a glowing review of *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*. The mastermind behind *Metal Gear Solid* and *Death Stranding* praised the film's fresh, emotionally resonant take on a lonely Peter Parker, while acknowledging the immense difficulty of striking a balance between a filmmaker's creative vision and blockbuster audience expectations.

## News & Reviews

**[Spider-Man: Brand New Day Nears Historic Box Office Record](https://www.ign.com/articles/spider-man-brand-new-day-comes-close-to-passing-avengers-endgame-with-second-biggest-box-office-debut-of-all-time-at-927-million-globally)** · IGN
Tom Holland's highly anticipated MCU return is an absolute juggernaut, raking in an estimated $927 million globally during its opening weekend. This impressive haul gives it the second-largest box office debut in cinematic history, trailing just a couple of million dollars behind *Avengers: Endgame* domestically, and firmly sets up 2026 to be a colossal year for Marvel.

**[Tom Holland Teases Live-Action Miles Morales MCU Debut](https://www.ign.com/articles/tom-holland-says-spider-man-5-is-unclear-teases-he-knows-when-miles-morales-will-join-the-mcu)** · IGN
While the development status of a potential *Spider-Man 5* remains "unclear" for now, star Tom Holland confidently teased that he knows when fan-favorite character Miles Morales will finally cross over into the live-action MCU. Marvel boss Kevin Feige recently hinted that Morales likely wouldn't appear on screen until after Sony concludes its animated *Spider-Verse* saga, meaning the wheels are actively turning behind the scenes.

**[The MCU Still Doesn't Know What to Do With the Hulk](https://www.ign.com/articles/what-the-heck-is-marvel-doing-with-the-hulk-anyway-spider-man-brand-new-day)** · IGN
Despite delivering an incredibly fun and brutal action sequence, the Hulk's supporting appearance in *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* once again ignores established character continuity. Bruce Banner's sudden, unexplained reversion to his Savage Hulk persona highlights a frustrating narrative mess caused largely by Universal Pictures' long-standing refusal to partner with Marvel on standalone Hulk films.

**[Wicked Star Ariana Grande Steps Back From Public Eye](https://www.ign.com/articles/wicked-star-ariana-grande-to-take-a-step-back-from-visibility-amid-endless-ongoing-public-scrutiny)** · IGN
Following intense, ongoing public scrutiny regarding her health and appearance, *Wicked* star Ariana Grande announced she is taking a "step back from visibility" after completing her Eternal Sunshine tour. The superstar has consequently exited the upcoming West End musical *Sunday in the Park with George*, though she is still slated to appear in future film projects like *Focker-in-Law* and an animated Dr. Seuss adaptation.
