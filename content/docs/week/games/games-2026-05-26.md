# Gaming News — 2026-05-26

## Top Story
Bungie has shocked the gaming world by announcing the discontinuation of live-service support for *Destiny 2* after its final major update this coming June. This decision was reportedly kept secret from the vast majority of Bungie staff until the public announcement, leaving fans and developers alike reeling. The studio is now pouring its remaining resources into its upcoming extraction shooter, *Marathon*.

## News & Reviews

**[007 First Light Review So Far](https://www.ign.com/articles/007-first-light-review)** · IGN
The highly anticipated *007 First Light* is shaping up to be the best James Bond game since *GoldenEye*, featuring a beautifully immersive world and robust gadget-driven gameplay. The IO Interactive title leans into aggressive brawling and run-and-gun combat rather than pure stealth, creating a distinctly Bond-flavored experience. Strangely, the game also features a cameo from TikTok star Khaby Lame, doing his signature deadpan reaction gesture when Bond asks for directions.

**[Alan Wake and Control 'Should Have Sold More,' New Remedy CEO Says](https://www.ign.com/articles/alan-wake-and-control-should-have-sold-more-new-remedy-ceo-says-as-he-accepts-skepticism-of-his-former-ea-background)** · IGN
Remedy's newly appointed CEO, Jean-Charles Gaudechon, recently acknowledged that beloved critical darlings like *Alan Wake* and *Control* simply "should have sold more". The studio hopes that leaning into cross-media adaptations, including their partnership with Annapurna for film and TV, will finally help these iconic franchises reach a much larger, mainstream audience.

**[PlayStation Delists and Shuts Down Servers for Destruction AllStars](https://www.ign.com/articles/playstation-delists-and-shuts-down-servers-for-destruction-allstars)** · IGN
Sony has abruptly delisted and shut down the servers for *Destruction AllStars*, a PS5 launch window live-service game that suffered a highly mixed reception upon release. The sudden closure happened without prior warning to players, reflecting yet another major stumble in PlayStation's ongoing live-service push.

**[Warhammer 40,000: Chaos Gate - Deathwatch Interview Reveals Fresh Details](https://www.ign.com/articles/warhammer-40000-chaos-gate-deathwatch-interview-reveals-fresh-details)** · IGN
Complex Games has spilled new details on *Warhammer 40,000: Chaos Gate - Deathwatch*, a massive strategy sequel focusing on the xenox-killing Space Marine mashup faction. The game boasts a huge variety of units, from Leman Russ tanks to the Astra Militarum, as players battle across six distinct enemy factions, including Orks and T'au.

**[Paradox Interactive Making LEGO City Skylines Spinoff, Ratings Board Leak Suggests](https://www.ign.com/articles/paradox-interactive-making-lego-city-skylines-spinoff-ratings-board-leak-suggests)** · IGN
A Korean ratings board listing has prematurely revealed that Paradox Interactive is publishing a *Cities: Skylines* LEGO spin-off. While fans love the thematic fit of a brick-based city builder, many are understandably skeptical about its technical state given the ongoing performance woes and missing features of *Cities: Skylines 2*.
