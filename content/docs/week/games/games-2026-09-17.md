# Gaming News — 2026-09-17

## News & Reviews

**[Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 Campaign Trailer Reveals Mads Mikkelsen as Main Villain](https://www.ign.com/articles/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-campaign-trailer-reveals-death-stranding-star-mads-mikkelsen)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-campaign-trailer-reveals-death-stranding-star-mads-mikkelsen)
Activision unveiled the campaign trailer for *Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4* during Xbox's Tokyo Game Show 2026 broadcast, revealing *Death Stranding* actor Mads Mikkelsen as primary antagonist Archer. The story picks up with Task Force 141 disbanded and Captain Price on the run following General Shepherd's assassination, as Ghost and Gaz hunt down their former captain while attempting to stop Archer from acquiring a catastrophic world-altering weapon. Advertised as the "epic conclusion" to the rebooted sub-franchise, the game launches on October 23, 2026, marking a monumental turning point for Call of Duty fans.

**[Hot Wheels Infinite Rush Review — A Glossy Open-World Racer That Lacks Spark](https://www.ign.com/articles/hot-wheels-infinite-rush-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/hot-wheels-infinite-rush-review)
Developer Milestone has overhauled the franchise with four distinct open-world islands in *Hot Wheels Infinite Rush*, shifting away from the life-sized indoor diorama tracks that made previous entries standout hits. IGN scored the racer a mediocre 6/10, criticizing its bland environment design, missing pop-culture cars, and the removal of play-worn vehicle damage details. While four-player split-screen and custom track AI are welcome upgrades, the generic open-world structure sacrifices the zany charm that arcade racing fans expect.

**[Twitch CEO and Take-Two Outline GTA 6 Impact and Future Support for GTA Online](https://www.ign.com/articles/twitch-boss-says-game-companies-are-right-to-avoid-competing-with-gta-6-for-attention-expects-gta-online-2-next-year)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/twitch-boss-says-game-companies-are-right-to-avoid-competing-with-gta-6-for-attention-expects-gta-online-2-next-year)
Twitch CEO Dan Clancy urged competing game studios to avoid launching alongside *GTA 6* this November, predicting an initial viewership spike followed by an even bigger surge when Rockstar launches its next multiplayer iteration in 2027. Meanwhile, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick reassured shareholders that the original *GTA Online* will continue to be supported post-launch, pointing to Take-Two's history of maintaining legacy online titles alongside newer releases. With *GTA 6* focused exclusively on single-player at launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, Rockstar is taking a deliberate approach to ensure single-player polish before rolling out its online ecosystem.

**[Former Naughty Dog Director Bruce Straley Criticizes Modern AAA Games as 'Boring'](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-last-of-us-game-director-finds-modern-aaa-games-boring-says-hes-not-excited-by-god-of-war-laufeys-gameplay)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-last-of-us-game-director-finds-modern-aaa-games-boring-says-hes-not-excited-by-god-of-war-laufeys-gameplay)
Bruce Straley, former game director on *The Last of Us* and *Uncharted 4*, stated in an interview that modern AAA games have become "boring" due to a lack of creative risk-taking. Straley cited *God of War Laufey* as an example of impressive technical polish that fails to innovate on core gameplay mechanics. Having departed Naughty Dog due to severe burnout, Straley is now leading indie studio Wildflower Interactive and developing the unconventional project *Coven of the Chicken Foot*.

**[Lollipop Chainsaw 2 Back2Back Announced for PS5 with Roller-Skating Juliet Starling](https://www.ign.com/articles/lollipop-chainsaw-2-back2back-announced-with-juliet-starling-now-on-skates)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/lollipop-chainsaw-2-back2back-announced-with-juliet-starling-now-on-skates)
Dragami Games has officially announced *Lollipop Chainsaw 2 Back2Back*, bringing back zombie-slaying cheerleader Juliet Starling on PS5 in 2027. The sequel relocates Juliet to "Jaredwood"—a film industry metropolis where she slices through corrupted zombies on roller-skates and fights movie-parody bosses like Zorbie. Neither original director Suda51 nor writer James Gunn are involved in the project, leading to mixed fan reactions over early gameplay footage.

**[The Blood of Dawnwalker Preview: How Vampiric Hunger Mirrors BG3's Dark Urge](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-blood-of-dawnwalkers-hunger-is-a-dark-urge-to-match-baldurs-gate-3)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-blood-of-dawnwalkers-hunger-is-a-dark-urge-to-match-baldurs-gate-3)
Ex-CD Projekt Red developers are crafting *The Blood of Dawnwalker*, an action RPG where protagonist Coen transforms into a vampire each night and battles overwhelming bloodlust. If players let Coen's health drop too low, normal dialogue options are overridden with "Give in to the hunger," causing him to impulsively drain key quest-giving NPCs. Coupled with a strict 30-day countdown timer to save Coen's family, the game's systemic approach to vampirism delivers moral tension reminiscent of *Baldur's Gate 3*'s Dark Urge origin.

**[Brandon Sanderson Rules Out Finishing George R.R. Martin's Game of Thrones Books](https://www.ign.com/articles/brandon-sanderson-pushes-back-on-suggestions-he-could-finish-george-rr-martins-game-of-thrones-books)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/brandon-sanderson-pushes-back-on-suggestions-he-could-finish-george-rr-martins-game-of-thrones-books)
Bestselling fantasy author Brandon Sanderson confirmed in an exclusive interview that he would decline any offers to finish George R.R. Martin's *A Song of Ice and Fire* series if Martin passes away before completing it. Sanderson explained that his optimistic tone is incompatible with Martin's gritty style, while urging fans to remember the human author behind delayed releases. The statement reinforces Martin's own assertion that no contingency plans exist for another writer to complete *The Winds of Winter*.
