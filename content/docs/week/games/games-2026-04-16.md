## News & Reviews

**[Assassin’s Creed: Black Flag Remake Reportedly Set for July Release](https://www.ign.com/articles/assassins-creed-black-flag-remake-reportedly-set-for-july-release)**
Rumors are swirling that the highly anticipated pirate remake, *Assassin’s Creed: Black Flag Resynced*, will officially launch on July 9. Ubisoft hasn't explicitly confirmed the date yet, but a recent European PEGI rating suggests we are getting very close to setting sail once again.

**[Battlefield 6 2026 Roadmap Confirmed](https://www.ign.com/articles/battlefield-6-2026-roadmap-confirmed)**
EA and BF Studios are trying to win back players by rolling out a massive 2026 roadmap that promises the return of persistent servers, larger maps, and naval warfare. Arriving this July, Season 4 will finally introduce aircraft carriers and water-based vehicles on a reimagined Wake Island map to deliver the "all-out warfare" the community has been begging for.

**[Starfield Nintendo Switch 2 Spotted](https://www.ign.com/articles/starfield-nintendo-switch-2-spotted)**
Bethesda's massive space RPG might be making the jump to Nintendo's newest console, as a Taiwan ratings board listing just leaked a Switch 2 version of *Starfield*. While Bethesda hasn't formally announced it, a Switch 2 release fully aligns with Microsoft's recent strategy of porting former exclusives to other platforms.

**[Replaced Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/replaced-review)**
*Replaced* offers a dazzling 2.5D sci-fi dystopia with jaw-dropping 16-bit pixel art that gorgeously updates classic retro adventures. Unfortunately, as stunning as the world is, the 11-hour adventure is frequently dragged down by unresponsive combat controls and tedious, uninspired sidequests that derail the pacing.

**[Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 Sales Update](https://www.ign.com/articles/warhammer-40000-space-marine-2-sales-update)**
The Emperor protects, and clearly the players do too, as *Space Marine 2* has blown past expectations by hitting a massive 12 million players. With the game still performing incredibly well, Saber Interactive is actively pushing out new content updates while quietly chipping away at *Space Marine 3*.

**[Ex-Sony Exec Comments on Game Pass](https://www.ign.com/articles/ex-sony-exec-comments-on-game-pass)**
Former PlayStation boss Shawn Layden took a shot at Xbox Game Pass following a leaked Microsoft memo indicating the service has become "too expensive" for players. Layden suggested the subscription model is failing despite Microsoft's best efforts "to will this into health," calling for an industry-wide "post mortem".
