# Gaming News — 2026-06-12

## Top Story
Newly-installed Xbox CEO Asha Sharma has issued a blunt internal memo warning of a 3% profit margin, hinting at significant structural changes across the gaming division. Analysts predict this massive corporate reset will inevitably result in prestige studio closures and the introduction of ad-supported tiers for Game Pass to balance the budget.

## News & Reviews

**[Persona 6 Officially Announced for Modern Consoles](https://www.ign.com/articles/everything-we-know-about-persona-6)** · IGN
After years of rumors, Atlus has officially unveiled Persona 6 at the latest Xbox Games Showcase, confirming its green aesthetic and a Day One launch on Xbox Game Pass. The highly-anticipated RPG will take players to modern-day Japan to balance school life and occult urban legends, completely moving away from PlayStation exclusivity by launching simultaneously on PC, PS5, and Xbox.

**[Nintendo Might Only Win $30k in Palworld Lawsuit](https://www.ign.com/articles/nintendo-may-only-win-30000-payout-in-pokemon-legal-battle-with-palworld-developer-pocketpair-ip-expert-says)** · IGN
IP experts predict a massive letdown for Nintendo in its patent infringement lawsuit against Pocketpair. Because Pocketpair quickly patched Palworld to alter catching mechanics, Nintendo's potential damages are severely limited and may max out around $30,000—chump change compared to their hefty litigation costs. This news comes just ahead of Palworld 1.0, which is set to officially launch on July 10.

**[Final Fantasy 7 Revelation Director Tackles Game Streaming and Minigames](https://www.ign.com/articles/final-fantasy-7-revelation-director-says-gameplay-streams-pose-a-huge-challenge-to-rpgs)** · IGN
Director Naoki Hamaguchi expressed concerns that players watching story-heavy RPGs on streams might feel satisfied without playing, pushing the team to add massive amounts of player choice in Final Fantasy 7 Revelation. Slated for spring 2027, the conclusion to the remake trilogy will also directly address Rebirth's minigame fatigue by making them purely optional for cosmetic rewards and allowing players to completely skip them.

**[FromSoftware Free to Make Games "Without Excessive Interference"](https://www.ign.com/articles/elden-ring-director-insists-fromsoftware-is-free-to-make-the-games-it-wants-to-teases-unannounced-titles)** · IGN
Despite activist investor Oasis Management Company heavily pressuring parent company Kadokawa for higher returns, Elden Ring creator Hidetaka Miyazaki assured fans that FromSoftware maintains an excellent creative environment. Miyazaki confidently stated they are working harder than ever on announced and unannounced titles without external micromanagement, easing fears that the studio might be forced into mobile games or rushed releases.

**[Resident Evil Veronica Remake Will Adjust Series Timeline](https://www.ign.com/articles/resident-evil-veronica-producer-says-capcom-will-adjust-the-story-to-fit-series-timeline)** · IGN
Capcom's Yoshiaki Hirabayashi confirmed the highly anticipated 2027 Resident Evil Veronica remake will restructure its story to more cohesively fit within the complex lore established by recent franchise remakes. The development team aims to preserve fan-favorite core elements while putting players back in Claire Redfield's shoes to survive the T-virus outbreak on Rockfort Island.

**[Assassin’s Creed Black Flag Resynced Expands Edward's Story](https://www.ign.com/articles/assassins-creed-black-flag-resynced-exclusive-edward-and-caroline-cutscene-ign-first)** · IGN
Original lead writer Darby McDevitt returned for the comprehensive remake of Assassin's Creed Black Flag, focusing on fleshing out Edward Kenway's tragic relationship with his wife, Caroline. The remake adds extensive new cutscenes to showcase Edward's sweeter side and naive ambitions before he ultimately turned to a life of piracy.
