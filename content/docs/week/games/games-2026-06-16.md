## News & Reviews

**[Major Helldivers 2 Patch Adds New Galactic Campaigns](https://www.ign.com/articles/major-helldivers-2-patch-adds-new-galactic-campaigns-new-control-center-terminal-and-a-lot-more)** · IGN
Arrowhead has dropped a massive 6.3.0 update that introduces structured Galactic Campaigns, a brand new Control Center terminal, and sweeping balance changes. The patch critically reduces enemy durable damage, decreases the exosuit cooldown to 8 minutes, and finally allows divers to reload while dodge-diving. This is exactly the kind of robust, meta-shifting update we've been begging for to keep the interstellar war fresh.

**[EA Sports UFC 6 Review So Far](https://www.ign.com/articles/ea-sports-ufc-6-review)** · IGN
The latest entry in the MMA franchise delivers an impressive roster of legendary fighters and a deeply immersive "The Legacy" career story mode, though its new "Flow State" mechanic feels slightly gimmicky and out of place. Despite straying a bit from strict simulation realism, the brutal combat and engaging Hall of Legends interactive museum make it a highly compelling fight.

**[Don't Nod Warned It Will Run Out of Funds This Year](https://www.ign.com/articles/life-is-strange-creator-dont-nod-warned-it-will-run-out-of-funds-this-year)** · IGN
