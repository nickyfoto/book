# Gaming News — 2026-09-13

## Top Story
Blizzard stole the show at BlizzCon 2026 by officially confirming that ***Diablo V*** is in full production, targeting a 2029 release. Set 100 years after *Diablo 4* in an apocalyptic Westmarch where Diablo has conquered Sanctuary, the ambitious sequel promises a return to procedural overworlds and classic RPG itemization.

## News & Reviews

**[Diablo 5 Confirmed in Full Production for 2029 Release Target](https://www.ign.com/articles/diablo-5-director-confirms-game-is-already-in-full-production)** · IGN
Blizzard confirmed that *Diablo V* has moved past early development and into full production for a targeted 2029 launch, jumping 100 years ahead into a dark future where Diablo reigns supreme over Westmarch. The sequel is bringing back procedural overworlds alongside dungeons, expanding the class roster beyond any prior entry with returning favorites like the Demon Hunter and Monk as well as new additions like the Plague Knight. Game Director Joe Shely noted that the team aims to build the game alongside player feedback leading into 2027 while maintaining full ongoing support for *Diablo 4*.

**[World of Warcraft: Forever Announced as Classic+ Alternative to WoW 2](https://www.ign.com/articles/world-of-warcraft-forever-wow-2-unlikely)** · IGN
Blizzard unveiled *World of Warcraft: Forever* at BlizzCon 2026, a Classic+ expansion launching November 4 that revitalizes original Azeroth with updated lighting, 1,000+ new quests, and fresh raid sizes like the 10-player Barrow Deeps. Developers Kris Zierhut and Mike Nuthals explained that 25 years of continuous engine and server upgrades make a standalone *WoW 2* unnecessary when the current engine can host new lore and social features like dynamic player campfires. This Classic+ approach offers veteran players a modernized nostalgic experience without splitting the community or resetting two decades of world building.

**[Diablo 4 Geralt Skin Sparks Backlash Ahead of Switch 2 Port and Netflix Show](https://www.ign.com/articles/hdiablo-netflix-series-blizzcon-news-interview)** · IGN
BlizzCon 2026 brought major *Diablo 4* updates, including an upcoming Netflix animated series, a Nintendo Switch 2 port launching September 15, and Season 15 "Hell's Legacy" introducing 20 new bosses. However, a new Geralt of Rivia Barbarian skin sparked immediate backlash because it requires preordering *The Witcher 3: Songs of the Past* expansion specifically on Battle.net, leaving Steam players locked out. Despite the launcher restriction debate, CD Projekt confirmed more *Witcher* content is coming to *Diablo 4* in 2027 alongside *Cyberpunk 2077* arriving on Battle.net.

**[Overwatch Devs Reaffirm Tracer as Mascot Alongside New Vampire Hero](https://www.ign.com/articles/overwatch-devs-confirm-that-yes-tracer-is-still-the-games-mascot)** · IGN
Blizzard developers confirmed at BlizzCon 2026 that Tracer remains the official mascot of *Overwatch*, reaffirming her iconic status despite recent crossover promotions highlighting other roster members. The team also detailed Hero 54, "Doctrine," a vampire-themed Zimbabwean scientist with deep ties to Doomfist, and teased a new Omnic character coming as Hero 55. The new additions demonstrate Blizzard's ongoing push to evolve hero design and expand character lore as the roster reaches 54 heroes.

**[Shaun the Sheep: The Beast of Mossy Bottom Review — Stop-Motion Horror Homage](https://www.ign.com/articles/shaun-the-sheep-the-beast-of-mossy-bottom-review)** · IGN
Aardman Animations delivers another winner with *Shaun the Sheep: The Beast of Mossy Bottom*, a silent-comedy homage to classic Universal monster films hitting theaters September 18. IGN awarded the stop-motion film high praise for its clever visual gags, Frankenstein-inspired plotline involving mutated farm pumpkins, and extraordinary handcrafted animation. It stands out as a charming, family-friendly Halloween release that animation nerds and comedy fans alike will thoroughly enjoy.

**[Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother Review — Mahershala Ali Shines in Devout Crime Drama](https://www.ign.com/articles/your-mother-your-mother-your-mother-review)** · IGN
Director Bassam Tariq and Oscar-winner Mahershala Ali team up for *Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother*, an intense, action-tinged crime drama about a devout hitman attempting to secure breast milk for his infant daughter after his wife's death. Arriving in theaters September 25, IGN highlighted Ali’s devastating performance and the film's sharp, darkly comedic commentary on modern American religious extremes. Though some details of the protagonist's moral code feel under-explained, the movie stands as a thoughtful, visually striking antihero character study.
