# Gaming News — 2026-07-02

## Top Story
Sony dropping physical discs for all new PlayStation games starting in January 2028 is easily the most staggering and offensive industry shift of the week. This anti-consumer move basically abandons game preservation and ownership, sparking widespread backlash from furious fans, historians, and even parody tweets from brands like KFC and Domino's Pizza.

## News & Reviews

**[Onimusha: Way of the Sword Release Date Moved Forward](https://www.ign.com/articles/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-release-date-moved-forward)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-release-date-moved-forward)
Capcom's highly anticipated action-adventure sequel *Onimusha: Way of the Sword* is coming earlier than expected, bumping its release date up to September 4, 2026, for PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2. A playable demo is available now, and securing save data from it will unlock a special Kubi Akari charm in the full game. This title marks the franchise's first mainline entry in two decades, and our recent hands-on sessions prove it is shaping up to be a massive win for Capcom.

Slated to launch in the first half of next year, the concluding chapter of the remake trilogy could introduce fully-fledged narrative expansions to keep players hooked. Fans are already speculating that the DLC could adapt spinoff material like *Dirge of Cerberus* or *Crisis Core* to make the ultimate definitive package.

**[Silent Hill: Townfall Continues To Revitalize the Series](https://www.ign.com/articles/silent-hill-townfall-continues-to-revitalize-the-series-in-new-horrifying-ways-ign-preview)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/silent-hill-townfall-continues-to-revitalize-the-series-in-new-horrifying-ways-ign-preview)
*Silent Hill: Townfall*, arriving on September 24 for PS5 and PC, looks incredibly promising as it leans heavily into suspenseful, slow-paced first-person stealth. Developed by No Code, the game tasks you with using a CRTV handheld radio to track horrific enemies through walls and navigate an abandoned, fog-covered Scottish port town. It is an atmospheric thriller that beautifully captures the core tenets of the franchise's legendary survival horror.

**[Obsidian Entertainment Reportedly NOT at Risk of Closure](https://www.ign.com/articles/avowed-the-outer-worlds-and-fallout-new-vegas-developer-obsidian-entertainment-reportedly-also-at-risk-of-closure-amid-xbox-reset)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/avowed-the-outer-worlds-and-fallout-new-vegas-developer-obsidian-entertainment-reportedly-also-at-risk-of-closure-amid-xbox-reset)
Despite terrifying rumors that Xbox's ongoing internal "reset" put *Fallout: New Vegas* developer Obsidian Entertainment on the chopping block, reporters have clarified that the studio is actually being kept safe. With *Avowed* and *The Outer Worlds 2* previously missing commercial targets, fans were rightfully worried about the talented RPG team's future. Thankfully, it seems Xbox is holding onto them, even as the broader games industry braces for further Microsoft layoffs.

**[College Football 27 Review So Far](https://www.ign.com/articles/college-football-27-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/college-football-27-review)
*College Football 27* steps onto the field with major overhauls to both pre-play defensive adjustments and Dynasty mode management. The new Dynasty Points and NIL deal mechanics make running a team far more complex and menu-heavy than before, completely transforming the recruitment phase. While muscle memory might betray you on defense at first, playing the newly added tight end position in Road to Glory is undeniably a blast.
