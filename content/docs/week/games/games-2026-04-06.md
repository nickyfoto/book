**[Life is Strange: Reunion Delivers a Proper Ending]**(https://www.ign.com/articles/life-is-strange-reunion-review)

Our 2026 review praises the tighter narrative and excellent new mechanics, such as Chloe's argument-winning "talk back" feature, giving this decade-long story the poignant and highly replayable wrap-up it deserves. 

**[Overwatch Redesigns Anran's 'Baby Face' After Backlash]**(https://www.ign.com/articles/we-moved-away-from-that-baby-face-overwatch-reveals-updated-look-for-anran-following-fan-backlash)
Blizzard has officially updated the look of the new fire fan-wielding hero, Anran, after fans and her own voice actor complained about the game's female cast suffering from "Same Face Syndrome". The new design gives her a more mature and striking appearance, moving away from her initially innocent look to better match her fierce, confident personality as a natural-born leader.

**[God Save Birmingham Brings Zombies to the Middle Ages]**(https://www.ign.com/articles/god-save-birmingham-preview-medieval-zombie-survival-pax-east)
If standard survival games are getting stale for you, this upcoming medieval title tasks you with surviving a highly-lethal zombie outbreak in a historically accurate 14th-century Birmingham. Instead of punching trees to build forts, you'll be scavenging for period-accurate tools, barricading existing derelict shanties, and strategically lopping off limbs with pitchforks and axes.

**[Crimson Desert Players Are Running Out of Things to Kill]**(https://www.ign.com/articles/after-hundreds-of-hours-some-crimson-desert-players-are-complaining-that-the-world-has-become-too-peaceful-because-theyve-basically-killed-all-the-enemies)
Completionists sinking over 100 hours into *Crimson Desert* are running into a bizarre endgame problem: the map is getting too peaceful because cleared enemy camps simply don't respawn. Desperate players are now committing in-game crimes just to trigger fights with city guards, prompting the community to beg Pearl Abyss for a Nemesis-style system or a way to reset forts.

**[Lucas Pope Hesitant to Talk About New Games Due to AI]**(https://www.ign.com/articles/return-of-the-obra-dinn-dev-says-ai-makes-him-hesitant-to-talk-about-new-projects)
The legendary indie developer behind *Papers, Please* and *Return of the Obra Dinn* says he's holding back on discussing his current projects out of fear that his ideas will "get slurped up by AI" or directly copied. Pope also admitted to feeling the pressure of living up to his previous massive hits, noting he wants to ensure his next project doesn't drag down his legacy.

**[Double Dragon Creator Yoshihisa Kishimoto Passes Away]**(https://www.ign.com/articles/yoshihisa-kishimoto-creator-of-double-dragon-and-renegade-dies-at-64)
The gaming industry has lost a true titan: Yoshihisa Kishimoto, the creator of iconic, genre-defining beat-em-ups like *Double Dragon* and *Renegade* (*River City*), has died at age 64. His son confirmed the news on social media, thanking fans worldwide for understanding and celebrating his father's foundational contributions to gaming.
