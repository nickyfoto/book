## News & Reviews

**[Helldivers 2 x Warhammer 40,000 Warbond Detailed](https://www.ign.com/articles/helldivers-2-x-warhammer-40000-castellans-creed-legendary-warbond-fully-revealed-weapons-and-armor-shown-off-release-date-confirmed)** · IGN
Arrowhead has fully revealed the Castellan’s Creed Legendary Warbond, bringing Cadian Kasrkin-inspired gear to the galactic front lines on August 12. Complete with a bolt pistol, meltagun stratagem, and heavy armor, this crossover is exactly the injection of grimdark firepower democracy needs.

**[Star Wars: Galactic Racer Preview Shows Roguelite Promise](https://www.ign.com/articles/star-wars-galactic-racer-campaign-preview)** · IGN
Forget a standard racing campaign—Fuse Games is turning *Star Wars: Galactic Racer* into a high-stakes CarPG roguelite. Our four-hour hands-on session revealed a shockingly deep progression system where every choice and crash matters, making this much more than just a nostalgia trip.

**[The Elder Scrolls Online Content Delayed Following Layoffs](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-elder-scrolls-online-content-delayed-to-2027-following-xbox-layoffs)** · IGN
In the wake of Xbox's recent restructuring, ZeniMax Online Studios has pushed major *ESO* content, including the Crimson Veldt trial and combat refresh, into 2027. The team insists the MMO is here to stay, but the delay is meant to give the developers the runway they need to hit the right quality marks.

**[Thimbleweed Park 2 Announced for 2028](https://www.ign.com/articles/thimbleweed-park-2-announced-for-pc-mac-and-linux-by-monkey-island-creator)** · IGN
Ron Gilbert and the original team are back to deliver *Thimbleweed Park 2*, bringing more pixel-art point-and-click mystery to PC, Mac, and Linux in early 2028. Coming off the massive success of *Return to Monkey Island*, this is a must-wishlist for old-school adventure game fans.

**[Dungeons & Dragons Officially Crosses Over With World of Warcraft](https://www.ign.com/articles/dungeons-dragons-world-of-warcraft-revealed-includes-every-playable-wow-species-and-class)** · IGN
Wizards of the Coast is bringing Azeroth to the tabletop this November with a massive *World of Warcraft* expansion for D&D. Featuring every playable WoW species and class, plus dungeons like Icecrown Citadel, it’s a brilliant mashup that tabletop fans and MMO veterans alike should be hyped for.

**[Call of Duty: Black Ops 2 Ports Plagued by Lobby Hacker](https://www.ign.com/articles/call-of-duty-black-ops-2-has-a-hacker-thats-making-the-game-unplayable-for-some)** · IGN
The recently launched PS4 and PS5 ports of *Black Ops 2* are being terrorized by an exploiter who is resetting stats and breaking classes. If you see a user named "C Isn't Sharp" in your lobby, back out immediately to save your account from being bricked.
