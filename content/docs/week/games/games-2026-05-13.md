# Gaming News — 2026-05-13

## News & Reviews

**[Star Wars: Fate of the Old Republic Promises a Tighter RPG Experience]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/star-wars-fate-of-the-old-republic-developer-teases-games-length-and-development-time)
Game director Casey Hudson has assured fans that *Star Wars: Fate of the Old Republic* won't be another bloated, 200-hour slog. The spiritual successor to *KOTOR* aims to offer branching storylines for replayability while keeping the main campaign focused so players can actually finish it. This lean development approach is a massive relief for gamers suffering from open-world fatigue, with Hudson promising a release before 2030.

**[Mina the Hollower Shapes Up as a Brilliant Zelda-Bloodborne Hybrid]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/mina-the-hollower-preview-bloodborne-zelda)
Yacht Club Games' upcoming adventure *Mina the Hollower* is looking incredible, blending punishing but rewarding combat with classic 8-bit *Link's Awakening* aesthetics. The core "Hollowing" burrow mechanic adds deep tactical evasion to the gameplay, while the Gothic setting of Tenebrous Isle is packed with secrets, dynamic weapons, and a brilliant risk-reward economy. It officially drops on May 29, and retro action fans absolutely need to keep this on their radar.

**[PS Plus May Lineup Drops Two Massive Open-World Hits]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/playstation-plus-game-catalog-for-may-includes-star-wars-outlaws-and-red-dead-redemption-2)
PlayStation Plus Extra and Premium subscribers are getting a massive injection of blockbuster content this month, headlined by *Star Wars Outlaws* and Rockstar's legendary *Red Dead Redemption 2*. The stellar May lineup also includes *Bramble: The Mountain King*, *Flintlock: The Siege of Dawn*, and the Souls-lite *Enotria: The Last Song*, making it an incredibly strong month for the subscription service.

**[Overwatch Heroes are Officially Dropping into Fortnite]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/overwatchs-most-iconic-heroes-join-fortnite-this-week)
Epic Games is pulling off another massive crossover as iconic *Overwatch* heroes Tracer, Mercy, Genji, and D.Va make their way to the *Fortnite* island. A new cinematic trailer confirms the crossover will be a major component of Chapter 7 Season 2's final act, potentially even bringing over classic maps like King's Row to the battle royale.

**[Housemarque's PS5 Exclusive Saros Stumbles at Launch]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/ps5-exclusive-saros-gets-off-to-lukewarm-start-with-around-300000-copies-sold-according-to-analyst-data)

Despite the slow start and a niche market, the game is seeing fantastic daily active user retention, proving that the players who bought the challenging action title are thoroughly hooked.
