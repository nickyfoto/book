# Gaming News — 2026-09-10

## Top Story
In a massive industry shakeup, Sony unexpectedly canceled its partnership on Hideo Kojima's next-gen action-espionage title *Physint*, prompting Microsoft to step in and secure Xbox as the official publisher for the spiritual successor to *Metal Gear Solid*. The high-profile pickup gives Xbox a major prestige win and extends its creative relationship with Kojima Productions across gaming, film, and television.

## News & Reviews

**[Marvel’s Wolverine Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/marvels-wolverine-review)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/marvels-wolverine-review)
Insomniac Games' *Marvel’s Wolverine* delivers vicious, blood-soaked claw combat and outstanding character animation, paired with a surprisingly nuanced, introspective narrative for Logan. However, the combat mechanics lack long-term progression and wear thin across its 15-hour campaign due to repetitive arena encounters and a lack of puzzle variety. While it stands as a solid Weapon X entry backed by cinematic flair, the core gameplay loop fails to fully match its narrative strength.

**[Final Fantasy 7 Revelation Hands-On Preview](https://www.ign.com/articles/final-fantasy-7-revelation-hands-on-preview-best-in-class-combat-but-needs-to-stay-focused-why-we-love-ff7)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/final-fantasy-7-revelation-hands-on-preview-best-in-class-combat-but-needs-to-stay-focused-why-we-love-ff7)
Hands-on time with *Final Fantasy VII Revelation* showcases best-in-class evolved combat with Cid and Vincent joining the party, high-flying open-world traversal via the Highwind, and a breathtaking reimagining of Wutai. However, the demo raised concerns over potential open-world map bloat and constant UI interruptions from assistant MAI. Separately, director Naoki Hamaguchi addressed fan backlash regarding the single-disc PS5/Xbox physical release requiring a mandatory internet download, maintaining that splitting the game across multiple discs would have compromised visual fidelity.

**[Rockstar Faces Employment Tribunal Over Fired GTA 6 Workers](https://www.ign.com/articles/gta-6-developer-rockstar-says-it-will-defend-itself-vigorously-from-union-claims-as-employment-tribunal-over-the-firing-of-31-workers-gets-underway)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/gta-6-developer-rockstar-says-it-will-defend-itself-vigorously-from-union-claims-as-employment-tribunal-over-the-firing-of-31-workers-gets-underway)
Rockstar Games appeared before a UK employment tribunal to contest claims from the IWGB union that 31 developers working on *GTA 6* were unlawfully blacklisted and terminated for union organizing. Rockstar strongly rejects the claims, maintaining that the staff members were dismissed for gross misconduct after leaking confidential development information in a Discord channel. Meanwhile, limited-edition *GTA 6* DualSense controllers caused massive online storefront queues and instant eBay scalping ahead of the game's November 19 launch.

**[Wardogs Surpasses 1 Million Sales on Day One of Early Access](https://www.ign.com/articles/wardogs-surpasses-1-million-copies-sold-on-day-one-blows-past-300k-peak-concurrent-players-on-steam)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/wardogs-surpasses-1-million-copies-sold-on-day-one-blows-past-300k-peak-concurrent-players-on-steam)
Bulkhead's new all-out warfare FPS *Wardogs* blew past one million copies sold and surged past 300,000 peak concurrent players on Steam during its very first day in Early Access. Blending tactical mil-sim combat with accessible large-scale shooter action, the \$39.99 PC title has emerged as one of the biggest multiplayer hits of the year. A full 1.0 release is targeted for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S in 2028.

**[Nintendo Switch & Switch 2 System Update 23.0.0 Released](https://www.ign.com/articles/nintendo-switch-and-switch-2-system-update-2300-released-here-are-the-full-patch-notes)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/nintendo-switch-and-switch-2-system-update-2300-released-here-are-the-full-patch-notes)
Nintendo launched system update 23.0.0, introducing TV-mode Variable Refresh Rate (VRR) support for Switch 2 consoles alongside a required dock firmware update. The firmware update adds full console-to-console data transfers for Switch 2 and enables temporary online license authentication when virtual game cards cannot be transferred offline. Additional tweaks include per-TV sound settings, Portuguese text-to-speech accessibility, and background music toggles for Mii editing.

**[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake Preorders Discounted](https://www.ign.com/articles/zelda-ocarina-of-time-remake-preorder-discount)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/zelda-ocarina-of-time-remake-preorder-discount)
Physical preorders for *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* remake on Switch 2 are already marked down by \$10 at Amazon and Walmart to \$59.88, matching digital pricing ahead of its November 5 launch. The first physical printing features limited-edition textured foil cover art and contains the complete game directly on the cartridge. Meanwhile, former Nintendo art director Takaya Imamura suggested that Link's divisive new visual model reflects Shigeru Miyamoto's affinity for traditional puppet drama aesthetics.

**[Ace Combat 8 Prequel Live-Action Series "Hour Zero" Revealed](https://www.ign.com/articles/ace-combat-8-hour-zero-live-action-prequel-trailer)** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/ace-combat-8-hour-zero-live-action-prequel-trailer)

Starring Josh Holloway, Brandon Routh, and Tamlyn Tomita, the episodes will stream exclusively on IGN starting September 15. Holloway praised the game's visceral cockpit combat and advised players to maintain relentless aggression during dogfights.
