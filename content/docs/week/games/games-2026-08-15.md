# Gaming News — 2026-08-15

## Top Story
Square Enix’s highly anticipated **Kingdom Hearts 4** stole the spotlight at Disney’s D23 Expo, locking in a late 2027 release window and revealing a surprise Disney+ animated series in development. Series creator Tetsuya Nomura and co-director Tai Yasue confirmed smooth development while showcasing traversal mechanics in the modern city of Quadratum alongside a first look at Pixar's *Coco* as an explorable world.

## News & Reviews

**[Creator Matt Groening Teases The Simpsons: Hit & Run "Coming Back in Some Form"](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-simpsons-hit-run-coming-back-in-some-form-creator-matt-groening-says)** · IGN
During a panel at D23, franchise creator Matt Groening let slip that the beloved 2003 Grand Theft Auto-inspired classic is making some sort of return. Although current series showrunner Matt Selman immediately tried to downplay the comment, the tease has reignited massive fan hope for a modern remaster or remake of the legendary title. This possible revival would end a grueling 23-year drought for fans who have long clamored for the return of one of the greatest licensed games in history.

**[The Handheld Gaming PC Market Faces a Looming Price Crisis Due to Skyrocketing RAM Costs](https://www.ign.com/articles/i-love-handhelds-but-im-worried-the-ram-crisis-is-going-to-kill-them)** · IGN
Skyrocketing memory costs are pushing the prices of flagship gaming handhelds to eye-watering extremes, with the new MSI Claw 8 EX AI+ launching at \$1,799 and the Lenovo Legion Go 2 hitting \$1,999. Even Valve’s highly popular Steam Deck recently saw its minimum price climb to \$789, leaving Asus' Xbox Allies as the only remaining reasonable deals on the market. With major memory manufacturers already booked out through 2027, this ongoing RAM crisis threatens to stifle the growth and accessibility of the entire portable PC gaming ecosystem.

**[Fortnite's Star Wars: Smuggler's Gambit Connects Disneyland to Your Living Room](https://www.ign.com/articles/star-wars-smugglers-gambit-lets-you-keep-helping-hondo-ohnaka-in-fortnite-after-you-leave-disneyland)** · IGN
Arriving on August 16, this new Unreal Engine-powered PvE project expands Disneyland's *Millennium Falcon: Smuggler's Run* theme park ride into a cooperative multiplayer online game. Players can take on three distinct character classes—Pilot, Gunner, or Engineer—to battle stormtroopers and beasts while executing heist missions. Best of all, linking your Epic and MyDisney accounts will unlock a special, mysterious crossover experience on the physical ride itself.

**[Marvel Studios Unveils Young, Wolverine-Free Cast for Upcoming X-Men Reboot](https://www.ign.com/articles/x-men-cast-confirmed-to-include-inde-navarette-as-rogue-adam-driver-as-mister-sinister-maya-boyd-as-storm-and-more-d23-2026)** · IGN
Marvel has officially announced the core cast for its May 2028 mutant reboot, starring Inde Navarrette as Rogue, Christopher Abbott as Professor Charles Xavier, and Adam Driver as the villainous Mister Sinister. Crucially, the film will completely omit Wolverine and Magneto, allowing the MCU to avoid retreading old ground and instead focus on young, misunderstood mutant outcasts. Director Jake Schreier teased that the script draws deep inspiration from classic Chris Claremont and modern Jonathan Hickman comic runs to give the team a fresh cinematic direction.

**[Why Grand Theft Auto 6 Needs to Bring Back GTA 4's Grit and Sincerity](https://www.ign.com/articles/i-hope-gta-6-borrows-a-lot-from-gta-4)** · IGN
A retrospective replay of *Grand Theft Auto 4* highlights why Niko Bellic’s grimy, grounded Liberty City journey remains the franchise's best story. While *GTA 5* perfected blockbuster action, its cartoonish protagonists lacked the raw, sincere humanity that Niko brought to the table. Revisiting *GTA 4*'s weightier driving physics and platonic bonding mechanics suggests a blueprint that Rockstar's upcoming *GTA 6* would do well to integrate.

**[Peacock’s Live-Action Dungeon Crawler Carl Adaptation Could Make or Break the LitRPG Genre](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-dungeon-crawler-carl-tv-series-could-make-or-break-future-litrpg-book-adaptations)** · IGN
Peacock's upcoming TV adaptation of the LitRPG phenomenon *Dungeon Crawler Carl* has cast beloved audiobook narrator Jeff Hays to voice Princess Donut, a huge win for the series' loyal fanbase. However, the decision to produce the series as a CGI-heavy live-action show rather than a cartoon poses extreme visual and auditory risks that could alienate fans. As the genre's first major Hollywood transition, this high-stakes adaptation could either pave the way for future LitRPG screen projects or kill industry interest entirely.
