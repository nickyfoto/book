# Gaming News — 2026-08-10

## News & Reviews

**[No Man’s Sky Celebrates 10 Years with Mind-Blowing Stats and New "Cosmos" Update Tease](https://www.ign.com/articles/after-a-decade-of-no-mans-sky-players-have-explored-less-than-1-of-the-planets)** · IGN
Hello Games' Sean Murray marked the landmark 10th anniversary of *No Man's Sky* by revealing that players have explored **less than 1%** of the game’s 18 quintillion procedurally generated planets since its rocky 2016 launch. Murray expressed deep gratitude to the community for helping claw back Steam reviews from "overwhelmingly negative" to "very positive" and teased the game’s next major free update, titled **Cosmos**. This shows that Hello Games is nowhere near done with their space explorer, even as they actively cook up their next planet-sized fantasy adventure, *Light No Fire*.

**[Rockstar Gackt Sparks Dirge of Cerberus Remake Hype as Final Fantasy VII Spinoffs Converge](https://www.ign.com/articles/final-fantasy-fans-convinced-dirge-of-cerberus-remake-on-the-way-after-gackt-posts-photo-in-genesis-cosplay)** · IGN
Japanese rock legend Gackt sent fans into a frenzy by posting a video of himself cosplaying as the iconic character Genesis while playing his song *Redemption*, the official theme song for *Dirge of Cerberus*. This viral moment coincides with *Final Fantasy VII Revelation* director Naoki Hamaguchi confirming that the upcoming game will tie all FF7 spinoffs, including *Dirge of Cerberus* and *Crisis Core*, **directly into the ending**. If you are a lore enthusiast, this heavily suggests a Vincent Valentine-focused remake or a massive story convergence is on the horizon.

**[Atlus Producer Wada Declares Persona 1 and 2 Remakes Are Projects "We Have to Do"](https://www.ign.com/articles/atlus-producer-says-i-think-we-have-to-do-persona-1-and-2-remakes)** · IGN
Kazuhisa Wada, producer for P-Studio, expressed strong personal backing for remakes of *Persona 1* and *Persona 2*, calling them projects the studio **"should definitely do"**. While not an official greenlight, it’s the most promising update fans have received for the '90s classics, especially as Atlus wraps up *Persona 4 Revival* for its February 2027 release. This shows Atlus is keenly aware of the fans' burning desire to see their foundational JRPGs brought up to modern, slick standards.

**[Large-Scale FPS Wardogs "Hits Different" in Sizzling Closed Alpha Playtest](https://www.ign.com/articles/wardogs-hits-different-and-could-be-the-next-big-fps-those-who-managed-to-get-into-the-recent-private-playtest-say)** · IGN
Bulkhead’s brand-new 100-player tactical shooter, which plays like a hybrid of *Battlefield* and *Arma*, has been showered with praise following a major weekend playtest. Playtesters fell in love with its deliberate, non-twitchy movement, lethal gunplay, and a **clever loadout economy** that lets you reward helpful teammates with direct tips of in-game cash. By bypassing influencer feedback to build a shooter for purists, Bulkhead might actually have a massive breakout hit on its hands.

**[Cozy Farming Sim Fields of Mistria 1.0 Delivers Anime-Infused Perfection](https://www.ign.com/articles/fields-of-mistria-review)** · IGN
Farming sim *Fields of Mistria* has officially launched its 1.0 update, presenting a gorgeous pastel pixel art world that beautifully updates the cozy nostalgia of early *Harvest Moon* and *Animal Crossing*. The game shines with its **highly reactive townspeople**, whose shared histories and lively dialogue make Friday nights at the local inn feel incredibly authentic. Despite minor movement bugs and early-game inventory cramps, this is an absolute must-play for cozy gaming fans.

**[Arc System Works Rescues Marvel Tokon PC Port with Lightning-Fast Hotfix](https://www.ign.com/articles/arc-system-launches-marvel-tokon-pc-update-and-some-players-are-saying-its-fixing-most-of-their-issues)** · IGN
Just four days after *Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls* stumbled into "Mixed" Steam reviews due to terrible stuttering and high CPU utilization, Arc System Works has delivered a massive performance-saving hotfix. The patch successfully reduces CPU strain and fixes severe frame drops related to shader compilation, rapidly turning player sentiment around. It's a masterclass in swift developer response, proving the team is fully committed to keeping their **4v4 tag-team brawler** in fighting shape.
