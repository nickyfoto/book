# Gaming News — 2026-07-08

## Top Story
Obsidian Entertainment is reportedly canceling *Avowed 2* to shift its focus entirely onto a brand-new *Fallout* game led by Josh Sawyer. This shocking pivot follows a massive corporate reset at Xbox and thousands of internal layoffs, but it finally gives diehard fans hope for the first mainline, single-player *Fallout* title in over a decade.

## News & Reviews

**[Microsoft Reportedly Pivoting Back to Xbox Exclusivity]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/microsoft-reportedly-plans-to-make-more-of-its-best-games-console-exclusive-so-people-have-a-reason-to-buy-an-xbox-so-what-could-skip-playstation)
After admitting its multiplatform video game strategy has failed to work out, Microsoft is reportedly planning to make more of its best games console-exclusive again to give gamers a tangible reason to buy an Xbox. It's a massive shift from their recent release strategy, begging the question of whether major heavy-hitters like *The Elder Scrolls 6* or the next mainline *Halo* will skip the PlayStation 5 entirely.

**[Palworld Hits 40 Million Players Ahead of 1.0 Launch]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/palworld-hits-an-incredible-40-million-players-25-years-after-early-access-launch-and-with-10-right-around-the-corner)
Pocketpair’s breakout monster-taming hit *Palworld* has reached a mind-blowing milestone of 40 million players just in time for its big 1.0 launch on July 10. Despite early warnings on its Steam page that the retail price might increase, the developers have decided to keep the game priced at a very reasonable $29.99 as a massive "thank you" to the community for its runaway success.

**[Assassin’s Creed Black Flag Resynced Review]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/assassins-creed-black-flag-resynced-review)
Ubisoft Singapore’s full remake of the beloved 2013 pirate adventure is bigger and better in all the ways that matter. It boasts a stunning Anvil engine visual upgrade, a more discovery-focused map, modernized freerunning, and overhauled combat that introduces heavy attack finishers and instant takedowns. Earning high praise in its review, the remake confidently brings Edward Kenway's swashbuckling journey into the modern era and remains a top-tier *Assassin's Creed* experience.

**[Doom: The Dark Ages - Revelations DLC Review]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/doom-the-dark-ages-revelations-review)
The *Revelations* DLC for *Doom: The Dark Ages* is already shaping up to be an essential, must-play expansion that brilliantly injects the hyper-mobility of *Doom Eternal* back into the game. By swapping out the Shield Saw for the versatile new Chain Spear and giving the Slayer his quick dash ability back, id Software has completely changed the flow of combat to make demon-slaying encounters more creative, agile, and expressive.

**[Destiny 2 Support Winds Down Following Bungie Layoffs]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/destiny-2-players-get-one-last-surprise-from-bungie-developer-reveals-limited-support-plans)
Bungie has confirmed that *Destiny 2* will receive only severely limited support moving forward, stepping away from notable content updates to focus primarily on server maintenance and critical crash fixes. Following hundreds of layoffs and internal reports that a massive *Destiny Infinity* relaunch was scrapped, the studio is now shifting its high-investment focus entirely toward its upcoming extraction shooter, *Marathon*.

**[Nintendo Switch 2 Leak Reveals The Crew Motorfest]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/nintendo-switch-2-leak-reveals-the-crew-motorfest-with-physical-launch-via-game-key-card)
A credible leak points to Ubisoft releasing a Nintendo Switch 2 edition of the open-world racer *The Crew Motorfest* this October. To keep the price down to an attractive $49.99, the game will reportedly get a physical release via a cheaper "Game-Key Card," requiring players to download the full game digitally despite having a physical box on their shelf.

**[Mario Kart Tour Shuts Down Without an Offline Version]** · [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/nintendo-confirms-mario-kart-tour-shutdown-after-7-years-no-offline-version-offered)
After seven years of successful service on mobile phones, Nintendo is officially shutting down the servers for *Mario Kart Tour* on September 29. Unlike *Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp*, Nintendo is tragically not planning a standalone offline version, meaning several unique tracks tied to the mobile game will soon become entirely unplayable.
