# Gaming News — 2026-06-07

## Top Story
The Xbox Games Showcase dropped massive bombshells today, most notably locking in *Gears of War: E-Day* for an October 6, 2026 release date while crushing any hopes of cross-platform play. Despite recent rumors sparked by a Walmart placeholder listing, Xbox explicitly confirmed that the highly anticipated Marcus Fenix prequel—along with inXile's *Clockwork Revolution*—will be a strict Xbox console exclusive and will not be coming to the PS5. 

## News & Reviews

**[Fable Secures a Release Date and a Marvel Villain](https://www.ign.com/articles/fable-trailer-reveals-release-date-hayley-atwell-as-its-villain)** · IGN
Playground Games' *Fable* reboot is officially launching on February 23, 2027. A brand new trailer also revealed that MCU star Hayley Atwell will play the game's grief-driven villain, Isabel, alongside a tease for the return of original antagonist Jack of Blades. It looks like Albion is in great hands.

**[Spyro Returns in A Realm Beyond](https://www.ign.com/articles/spyro-a-realm-beyond-announced-features-true-dragon-flight)** · IGN
Toys for Bob is finally bringing back everyone's favorite purple dragon in Spring 2027 with *Spyro: A Realm Beyond* for PC, PS5, Xbox Series X/S, and Nintendo Switch 2. The major new mechanic shaking up the formula is "true dragon flight," allowing players to seamlessly transition to flying at any time rather than just gliding around the environment.

**[Persona 6 is Real and Coming to Game Pass](https://www.ign.com/articles/persona-6-announced-coming-to-xbox-game-pass)** · IGN
It's been a full decade since *Persona 5*, but Atlus has finally confirmed that *Persona 6* is on the way for PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S. While it currently lacks a release date, the highly-anticipated RPG will feature a new supernatural story in modern-day Japan and will be available to Xbox Game Pass subscribers on day one.

**[Monster Hunter Wilds Confirmed for Switch 2](https://www.ign.com/articles/monster-hunter-wilds-confirmed-for-nintendo-switch-2)** · IGN
Capcom confirmed via a brief social media post that its hit action-RPG *Monster Hunter Wilds* is officially in development for the Nintendo Switch 2. This is fantastic news that bridges the gap for Nintendo fans who had to sit out the initial PC and current-gen console release. 

**[Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 Revives DMZ Mode](https://www.ign.com/articles/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4s-gritty-extraction-mode-dmz-gets-first-gameplay)** · IGN
*Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4* is doubling down on the extraction shooter genre by bringing back DMZ as a premium third mode. Launching on October 23, the mode promises a much grittier tone, dynamic weather, stealth systems, and new team-revive mechanics that look to seriously rival games like *Escape from Tarkov*.
