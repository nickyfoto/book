## News & Reviews

**[Crimson Desert Gets Major Quality-of-Life Update in Patch 1.02.00](https://www.ign.com/articles/crimson-desert-gets-more-storage-space-a-headgear-visibility-option-and-even-a-toggle-for-previous-movement-controls-in-patch-10200)**
Pearl Abyss is rolling out massive improvements for *Crimson Desert* a mere two weeks after launch, expanding private storage up to 1000 slots and adding a much-requested "Movement Controls" toggle so you no longer have to mash the sprint button to get around. With new fast travel tweaks, headgear visibility options, and even new armor for your camp cats, this patch proves the developers are reacting to player feedback at a blistering pace.

**[Will Starfield Ever Get a Cyberpunk-Style Renaissance?](https://www.ign.com/articles/will-starfield-ever-get-a-cyberpunk-style-renaissance)**
Bethesda's April 7th *Free Lanes* update is finally bringing "Cruise Mode" for manual space travel and a new wretched hive of a space port called Anchorpoint to *Starfield*. While it's a generous drop that attempts to address the game's space simulation woes, don't expect a top-to-bottom *Cyberpunk 2.0* overhaul—this is still very much an RPG hardcoded into the quirky, segmented framework of the Creation Engine, warts and all. 

**[Mass Effect Prime Video Series Faces Rewrites for "Non-Gamers"](https://www.ign.com/articles/mass-effect-prime-video-series-amazon-tv-boss-reportedly-requested-rewrites-to-make-it-more-appealing-to-non-gamers)**
Amazon's pricey *Mass Effect* adaptation is reportedly on the verge of a series order, but Amazon TV boss Peter Friedlander has requested script rewrites to make the sci-fi drama appeal more to a mainstream, "non-gamer" audience. We can only hope they take a cue from Amazon's own *Fallout* show's stellar execution and keep the universe's canon intact without stripping away what makes BioWare's sci-fi epic so compelling.

**[Riot Designer Discusses Riftbound TCG's Future and Upcoming Unleashed Set](https://www.ign.com/articles/riftbound-tcg-interview-with-riot-senior-designer-jon-moormann-at-pax-east)**
Riot's wildly popular *League of Legends* trading card game, *Riftbound*, is gearing up for its third set, *Unleashed*, launching in English on May 8. At PAX East, Senior Designer Jon Moormann teased new Jungle-themed gameplay mechanics like "Ambush" and "Hunt," while acknowledging the development team's approach to the oppressive Chaos meta decks ahead of the game's first-ever banlist.

**[Daemons of the Shadow Realm Premiere Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/daemons-of-the-shadow-realm-premiere-review-episode-1)**
Crunchyroll's latest debut from *Fullmetal Alchemist* creator Hiromu Arakawa is a masterclass in economic storytelling and worldbuilding, earning favorable comparisons to the gripping pilots of *Attack on Titan* and *Lost*. It flawlessly pivots from quiet idyllic village life to kinetic, brutal supernatural action, raising fascinating questions and establishing a complex mythology that makes it an absolute must-watch this season.
