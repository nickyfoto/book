# Gaming News — 2026-07-12

## Top Story
Obsidian Entertainment's director is firing back at critics spreading misinformation about the studio's talent pool following recent, massive Xbox layoffs. Amidst the restructuring, reports indicate that the studio has also pivoted from an *Avowed* sequel to focus on a brand-new *Fallout* project.

## News & Reviews

**[Obsidian Defends Its DNA After Xbox Restructure](https://www.ign.com/articles/obsidian-director-blasts-people-running-their-mouths-about-how-the-studio-has-changed-following-xbox-layoffs)** · IGN
Following Microsoft's sweeping cuts of 1,600 Xbox employees, Obsidian director Brandon Adler took to LinkedIn to slam "cold take artists" claiming the studio has lost its touch. Adler insists the core DNA of the team remains intact, noting that the leads behind classics like *New Vegas* and *Pillars of Eternity* are still driving the ship. Interestingly, reports suggest the studio has canceled *Avowed 2* to shift focus toward a new *Fallout* game led by Josh Sawyer.

**[Black Ops 1 and 2 Ports Feature Cross-Gen PlayStation Matchmaking](https://www.ign.com/articles/call-of-duty-black-ops-1-and-2-support-crossplay-between-ps4-and-ps5-activision-confirms)** · IGN
Activision has confirmed that the recently released, bare-bones PS4 and PS5 ports of *Call of Duty: Black Ops 1* and *2* do support crossplay matchmaking between the two platforms, easing some player concerns. However, tech experts remain baffled by the low frame-rate caps and poor resolution of these $39.99 re-releases. Despite the criticism regarding their quality, both classic shooters have quickly surged to the top of the PlayStation Store's best-selling charts.

**[First Look at Avengers: Doomsday Roster Revealed](https://www.ign.com/articles/avengers-doomsday-concept-art-reveals-first-look-at-more-than-20-returning-heroes)** · IGN
Marvel showcased new concept art at Bilibili World 2026, giving fans a sneak peek at Robert Downey Jr.'s shiny Doctor Doom mask alongside the X-Men, Thunderbolts, and Fantastic Four. While we're still waiting for a proper trailer, the leaked artwork proves the MCU is gearing up for an absolutely massive crossover event when the film drops on December 18, 2026.

**[Yelena Belova Teased for Spider-Man: Brand New Day](https://www.ign.com/articles/kevin-feige-teases-appearance-from-florence-pugh-as-yelena-belova-in-spider-man-brand-new-day)** · IGN
Kevin Feige has strongly hinted that Florence Pugh's Yelena Belova will make a cameo appearance in the upcoming *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* before she fully returns in *Avengers: Doomsday*. The highly anticipated web-slinging sequel, which releases in just a few weeks on July 31, is already stacked with heroes like Hulk, The Punisher, and a rumored new incarnation of Jean Grey.
