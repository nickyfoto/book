# Gaming News — 2026-06-06

## Top Story
Telltale Games has finally committed to a 2027 launch window for *The Wolf Among Us 2* on PC and consoles, ending nearly a decade of chaotic development. To pave the way and ease the painfully long wait, a comprehensive remaster of the critically acclaimed 13-year-old original game is dropping this holiday season with updated visuals and bonus content.

## News & Reviews

**[007 First Light Dev IO Interactive Says It’s Now Sold 3 Million Copies](https://www.ign.com/articles/007-first-light-dev-io-interactive-says-its-now-sold-3-million-copies-tracking-well-above-our-forecasts-at-this-point)** · IGN
IO Interactive's *007 First Light* is a massive critical and commercial success, selling an impressive 3 million copies in under two weeks. IGN awarded the game a 9/10, noting it delivers the ultimate James Bond fantasy, and IOI's CEO confirmed sales are pacing well above internal forecasts despite a massive reported development budget. The studio also unveiled an extensive year-one roadmap packed with free updates like a photo mode, New Game+, and story DLC starring Lenny Kravitz's pirate king character Bawama.

**[Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ First Details Tease a Feedback-Driven Extraction Experience](https://www.ign.com/articles/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-dmz-first-details-tease-a-feedback-driven-extraction-experience)** · IGN
Activision is bringing back DMZ mode for *Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4*, promising a deeply persistent and feedback-driven PvPvE extraction shooter experience. Launching on October 23, 2026, the revamped mode drops players into the Korean peninsula border zone of Hajin, featuring dynamic environmental weather effects, story missions, and a highly customizable Forward Operating Base. It's a clear signal that Infinity Ward is serious about cementing their foothold in the extraction genre after mixed reception to their earlier beta tests.

**[Final Fantasy 7 Revelation Director Confirms New Voice Actor for Sephiroth](https://www.ign.com/articles/final-fantasy-7-revelation-director-confirms-new-voice-actor-for-sephiroth)** · IGN
The final chapter in the *Final Fantasy 7* remake trilogy is dropping in Spring 2027, but the One-Winged Angel will sound a bit different. Travis Willingham is officially taking over the English voice role of Sephiroth, replacing Tyler Hoechlin, who was unavailable for the project. In more good news for players burnt out by *Rebirth*, the developers have tweaked the game's infamous mini-games, making them entirely optional for earning crucial combat rewards while still preserving the original game's core DNA.

**[Star Trek: Shadow Frontier Game Reveal Trailer Explores Trek’s Darker Side](https://www.ign.com/articles/star-trek-shadow-frontier-game-reveal-trailer-explores-treks-darker-side-ign-live-2026)** · IGN
Bloober Team has officially revealed *Star Trek: Shadow Frontier*, an ambitious, single-player psychological thriller aiming for a 2027 release on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X/S. The mature-rated action-adventure game stars Michelle Forbes returning as the iconic Ro Laren, who finds herself stranded in a chilling starship graveyard. If you've been waiting for a dark, high-budget AAA *Star Trek* game to shake up the franchise, this horror-tinged narrative looks incredibly promising.

**[Hitman Classic Trilogy Remastered Revealed With 2027 Launch Window](https://www.ign.com/articles/hitman-classic-trilogy-remastered-revealed-with-2027-launch-window)** · IGN
IO Interactive is partnering with Saber Interactive to launch *Hitman Classic Trilogy Remastered* in 2027 for PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X/S. Bundling *Codename 47*, *Silent Assassin*, and *Contracts*, this remaster features overhauled visuals, new character models, an instant classic graphics toggle, and a photo mode. It's the perfect way for newer fans brought in by the recent *Hitman* trilogy to experience the brutal, puzzle-like stealth sandbox origins of Agent 47.

**[Fortnite Live Event Stuns Fans as The Rock’s Fate Is Revealed](https://www.ign.com/articles/fortnite-live-event-stuns-fans-as-the-rocks-fate-is-revealed-and-ben-starrs-character-is-unmasked-in-shock-twist)** · IGN
Epic Games absolutely knocked it out of the park with the "Shattered" live event that concluded the latest *Fortnite* season. In a dramatic narrative twist, The Foundation (played by Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson) was defeated and frozen solid, while his ally The Visitor was shockingly unmasked as the legendary lore villain Geno, voiced by Ben Starr. This massively reshuffles the game's long-running storyline as the newly reforged island map gears up for a fresh season.
