# Gaming News — 2026-07-23

## Top Story
Nintendo is finally taking us back to our favorite virtual vacation destination with *Nintendo Switch Sports Resort*, bringing back the beloved Wuhu Island from the Wii days. The game is poised to avoid its predecessor's lackluster launch by offering 12 sports out of the box and finally taking full advantage of the Joy-Con's gyro capabilities for a much-improved motion experience.

## News & Reviews

**[Halo: Campaign Evolved Review](https://www.ign.com/articles/halo-campaign-evolved-review)** · IGN
Rebuilt in Unreal Engine 5, this remake is an impressive technical test that thankfully retains the series' classic, satisfying combat calculus. While the glossy new visual sheen arguably alters the original game's melancholic atmosphere, the addition of three brand-new prequel missions starring Master Chief and Sergeant Johnson adds a couple of hours of highly welcome, fresh cooperative action. 

**[Microsoft Launches Test for Ad-Supported Xbox Game Streaming](https://www.ign.com/articles/microsoft-launches-test-for-ad-supported-xbox-game-streaming-promises-the-ads-will-not-interrupt-the-flow-of-gameplay)** · IGN
Microsoft is rolling out a test for Xbox Insiders that allows users to play supported Xbox cloud games for free with ads, bypassing the need for a Game Pass subscription. Players will sit through two minutes of ads prior to a 1-hour gaming session—a massive industry shift designed to offset skyrocketing hardware costs and an increasingly tough console market.

**[Warhammer 40,000 Warbond Confirmed for Helldivers 2](https://www.ign.com/articles/okay-so-we-now-know-1-weapon-coming-to-helldivers-2-as-part-of-the-warhammer-40000-warbond-and-i-think-we-can-guess-a-few-others)** · IGN

A teaser officially revealed the iconic Bolt Pistol, and fans are eagerly crossing their fingers for Chainswords, Lasguns, and Astra Militarum-style armor to properly squash the Tyranid-like Terminids.

**[Disgaea Creator Slams Sony for Scrapping Discs](https://www.ign.com/articles/disgaea-creator-slams-sony-for-scrapping-discs-says-playstation-might-as-well-go-all-the-way-and-scrap-consoles-too)** · IGN
The outrage over Sony killing off physical PS5 game discs starting in 2028 continues to grow, with *Disgaea* creator Sohei Niikawa slamming out-of-touch executives, stating if they eliminate discs they "might as well eliminate the hardware too". Compounding the PR nightmare, reports claim Sony heavily anticipated this blowback and issued its strictest social media guidelines ever to gag their developers from commenting on the controversy.

**[Marvel’s Wolverine: Deep Cuts SDCC Panel](https://www.ign.com/articles/marvels-wolverine-deep-cuts-sdcc-panel-live-report-comic-con-2026)** · IGN
Insomniac Games took the stage at San Diego Comic-Con to reveal highly anticipated details on their unapologetically violent PS5 exclusive. The panel promises a deep dive into the game's story and characters, showcasing co-op combat with Jean Grey and a fresh look at Lady Deathstrike.
