# Gaming News — 2026-06-30

## Top Story
Microsoft's gaming division is reportedly bracing for a "bloodbath" as it considers shutting down multiple studios—including Arkane and Undead Labs—which could lead to the cancelation of highly anticipated titles like *Marvel's Blade* and *State of Decay 3*. While Hideo Kojima's Xbox-published horror game *OD* is reportedly safe, this aggressive corporate "reset" has both fans and developers rightfully on edge.

## News & Reviews

**[Civilization 7 Review Update](https://www.ign.com/articles/civilization-7-review-update)** · IGN
Firaxis dropped the 1.4 Test of Time update for *Civilization 7*, attempting to address player feedback by reverting some mechanics to feel more like older entries in the franchise. Unfortunately, replacing age-specific objectives with simplistic point races for victories feels like a step backward, leaving the late game feeling hollow despite some much-needed quality-of-life improvements to the UI.

**[Splatoon Raiders Preview: Turns Out It’s Kind of a Roguelite](https://www.ign.com/articles/splatoon-raiders-preview-turns-out-its-kind-of-a-roguelite)** · IGN
Nintendo is finally giving fans the robust single-player *Splatoon* experience they've been begging for with *Splatoon Raiders*, a roguelite that tasks you with clearing out Salmonids and gathering loot in a mech piloted by Big Man. With persistent progression, deep build customization, and chaotic four-player co-op, this looks like an absolute blast to play.

**[GTA 6 Developers Ask Rockstar for Union Recognition](https://www.ign.com/articles/gta-6-developers-ask-rockstar-for-union-recognition-and-theyre-prepared-to-go-to-the-uk-government-if-necessary)** · IGN
Ahead of the monolithic launch of *Grand Theft Auto 6*, Rockstar developers in the UK have applied for voluntary recognition of the IWGB Game Workers Union to fight for pay transparency and fairer crunch practices. Rockstar claims it offers a world-class environment, but workers are prepared to go to the UK government's Central Arbitration Committee if the studio refuses to voluntarily acknowledge their collective bargaining rights.

**[ESA Claims Private Minecraft Servers Are 'Illegal' and 'Piracy'](https://www.ign.com/articles/esa-claims-private-minecraft-servers-are-illegal-and-piracy-in-stop-killing-games-hearing)** · IGN
In a bizarre defense against a "Stop Killing Games" bill in California, the Entertainment Software Association (ESA) claimed that private *Minecraft* servers are "illegal" and a form of "piracy". This is blatantly false, as *Minecraft* officially encourages and verifies community servers on its own website, exposing the ridiculous lengths lobbyists will go to avoid preserving dead video games.

**[Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game Has Me Excited to Get into the Lab](https://www.ign.com/articles/avatar-legends-the-fighting-game-ign-preview)** · IGN
*Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game* looks stunning and plays exceptionally fast, utilizing a four-button combat system that centers around a risk-reward "Flow" state mechanic. With an intricate support character system and fighters zooming across the screen, this is shaping up to be a shockingly deep brawler that fighting game fans will absolutely want to master.
