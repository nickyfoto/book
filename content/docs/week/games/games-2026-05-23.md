**[Warhammer 40,000 Is an Accessibility Battle](https://www.ign.com/articles/warhammer-40000-is-an-accessibility-battle)**
For disabled gamers, the intense physical demands of tabletop *Warhammer 40,000*—from rolling dice to assembling units—present massive accessibility barriers not found in digital adaptations like *Space Marine 2*. However, Games Workshop is taking a positive step by announcing pre-painted official terrain pieces, which could eventually ease the entry barriers for all players.
