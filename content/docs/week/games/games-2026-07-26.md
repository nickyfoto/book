# Gaming News — 2026-07-26

## Top Story
The upcoming *Resident Evil* movie adaptation from *Barbarian* director Zach Cregger is taking heat from fans over its snowy, modern setting, but Cregger insists these creative liberties are essential to translating the horror to the big screen. He emphasized that his goal isn't a direct adaptation of the video games' stories, but rather a "love letter" that captures the stressful, resource-starved survival horror experience of playing the classic titles.

**[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom Figures Up for Preorder](https://www.ign.com/articles/preorder-hasbro-the-legend-of-zelda-figures)** · IGN

These are practically guaranteed to sell out fast, so lock yours down quickly if you want them for your gaming shelf.

**[Saros Delivers a Must-Play Bullet-Hell Experience](https://www.ign.com/articles/saros-review)** · IGN
The new PS5 exclusive *Saros* from Housemarque is a major success, seamlessly blending fluid sci-fi action with punishing bullet-hell mechanics. IGN's review highlights the game as a brilliant test of skill that feels like a "rhythmic dance" packed with a punchy arsenal. It's easily one of the biggest PS5 titles of the year and absolutely worth a spot in your library.

**[Dragon Quest I & II HD-2D Remake is a Triumph](https://www.ign.com/articles/dragon-quest-12-hd-2d-remake-review)** · IGN
Scoring a stellar 9/10, this ambitious HD-2D double pack beautifully revamps the classic RPGs for a modern audience. The second game in particular received massive story expansions, making this the definitive, magical way to experience the conclusion of the retro trilogy.

**[MTG's Top 12 Commander Precons Ranked](https://www.ign.com/articles/magic-the-gatherings-top-12-most-powerful-commander-precons-of-all-time)** · IGN
If you are looking to get into *Magic: The Gathering's* highly popular Commander format, a new ranking breaks down the 12 most powerful preconstructed decks of all time. Standouts include the terrifying five-color *Draconic Domination* deck, the heavily disruptive *Mutant Menace* from the *Fallout* crossover, and the highly synergistic *Food and Fellowship* from *The Lord of the Rings*.

**[TMNT Enters a Wild New Comic Era](https://www.ign.com/articles/idw-teases-an-exciting-new-era-for-teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles-comic-con-2026)** · IGN
IDW is setting the stage for a massive 2027 for the *Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles* with writer Matthew Rosenberg taking the reins starting with issue #25. Building on Gene Luen Yang's mythology-heavy run, Rosenberg plans to tap into his New York roots to turn the Bronx and Pelham into a chaotic, 24-hour battleground for the brothers.
