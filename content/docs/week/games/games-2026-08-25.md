## News & Reviews

**[Gears of War: E-Day Campaign Preview is "Nice"](https://www.ign.com/articles/gears-of-war-e-day-campaign-hands-on-preview-to-quote-marcus-fenix-nice)** · IGN
The first hands-on campaign preview of the prequel reveals that Marcus Fenix and Dom Santiago are returning to their gritty, chainsaw-revving roots in their classic COG gear. The gameplay shifts away from *Gears 5’s* massive open-world spaces in favor of tighter, multi-path streets in Kolona with optional secondary objectives, while introducing a new weapon mod system and tactical gear buffs. It looks to be the perfect blend of classic cover-shooter aggression and fresh playstyle customization that series veterans have been clamoring to play.

**[Ubisoft Reveals Single-Player Turn-Based Spin-off Rainbow Six Tactics](https://www.ign.com/articles/rainbow-six-tactics-announced-pc-ps5-xbox-series-2027)** · IGN
Ubisoft Paris is developing a single-player turn-based tactics game featuring 15 fan-favorite Operators from *Siege* commanded from afar by *Vegas 2* protagonist Paul "Six" Bishop. Set for a 2027 release on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S, the game marks the franchise's first single-player entry since 2008 and will feature optional permadeath, destructible cover, and deep "breach planning" mechanics. For gamers, this is a massive genre shift that successfully translates the high tension of tactical shooter coordination into turn-based precision.

**[The Witcher 3 Remastered Arrives Next Month as a Free Upgrade](https://www.ign.com/articles/the-witcher-3-remastered-launches-september-free-upgrade-for-all-owners)** · IGN
CD Projekt Red surprised fans at Gamescom ONL by announcing *The Witcher 3 Remastered* will launch on September 29, 2026, as a free upgrade for all PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S owners. This remastered version brings massive visual enhancements like path tracing alongside tweaked combat, cross-platform mod support, and a native Nintendo Switch 2 edition. It is the ultimate excuse to dive back into Geralt’s shoes before *The Witcher 4* eventually launches, which has now been confirmed to be targeting a 2028 release window.

**[Final Fantasy VII Revelation Teases Submarine Combat and Giant Weapons](https://www.ign.com/articles/final-fantasy-7-revelations-gets-new-trailer-at-gamescom-2026-showing-off-submarine-combat-weapons-and-more)** · IGN
A thrilling new trailer for the third part of the remake trilogy debuted at Gamescom, showcasing underwater submarine combat, Highwind airship navigation, and spectacular looks at the Ruby, Emerald, and Ultima Weapons. The trailer also highlights the game's new job-based "FITS" playstyle system and gives fans a taste of Vincent Valentine's gunslinging combat and Galian Beast transformation. It is an incredibly hype showcase that sets expectations high for its simultaneous multiplatform release in Spring 2027.

**[Path of Exile 2 Receives Official December Launch Date](https://www.ign.com/articles/path-of-exile-2-release-date-sets-10-launch-in-december-2026)** · IGN
Grinding Gear Games announced that the full 1.0 release of its highly anticipated free-to-play action-RPG, *Path of Exile 2*, will officially launch on December 11, 2026. The announcement came alongside a new trailer that blended gameplay with cinematic footage, promising players a complete build just in time for the holidays. Action-RPG fans can now prepare for what is bound to be a major genre competitor on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S, with global registration already open on their website.

**[GTA 6 Subpoena Battle Heats Up Between Take-Two and Discord](https://www.ign.com/articles/gta-6-leak-subpoena-discord-says-it-will-evaluate-the-validity-and-scope-before-responding)** · IGN
The legal battle over the *GTA 6* leaks has escalated after a federal court granted Take-Two's requests to subpoena Discord, Microsoft, and X to unmask the identity of the "CyberLeek" account. Discord has responded that they have not been served yet and will evaluate the validity and scope of the subpoena before responding, amid growing concerns from privacy advocates that Take-Two is targeting entire server communities. Meanwhile, the leaker continues to drop gameplay videos to promote a bizarre cryptocurrency scheme just days before Netflix's official timed-exclusive gameplay reveal this Thursday.

**[Mass Effect-Style RPG Exodus Launches on April 7, 2027](https://www.ign.com/articles/exodus-release-date-revealed-the-mass-effect-like-rpg-is-coming-in-april)** · IGN
Archetype Entertainment has confirmed its ambitious spacefaring RPG, *Exodus*, will release in April 2027 on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S. A new trailer debuted a companion named Salt, a hyper-intelligent sniper octopus piloting a mech suit, showcasing combat heavily inspired by *Mass Effect*. Led by veteran BioWare writer Drew Karpyshyn and featuring actor Matthew McConaughey, this is shaping up to be a premier sci-fi blockbuster centered on realistic time dilation effects.

**[Review: Sony's PlayStation 27" Gaming Monitor is a Decent, PS5-Matching Entry](https://www.ign.com/articles/sony-playstation-27-gaming-monitor-review)** · IGN
Sony's new \$349.99 monitor is designed to perfectly match the PS5 and features a Fast IPS QHD panel alongside a convenient foldaway DualSense controller charging hook. IGN's review praises its factory color accuracy and seamless PlayStation integration but finds the 1000:1 contrast ratio average and the 350-nit brightness middle-of-the-road. While it is a solid, hassle-free display for dedicated console players, PC-centric gamers can find better HDR and ergonomics elsewhere for under \$350.
