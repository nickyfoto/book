# Gaming News — 2026-07-03

## Top Story
Sony’s sudden, aggressive push to kill off physical PlayStation game discs by January 2028 is dominating the industry conversation, sparking major backlash from preservationists and the gaming community. While the IGN audience has overwhelmingly rejected an all-digital future (90% against it), Sony is already retraining its disc production staff in Austria, and competitors like Xbox are seizing the opportunity to tout *Halo: Campaign Evolved*'s physical release as a major selling point.

## News & Reviews

**[Suicide Squad Developers Disillusioned by Live-Service Push](https://www.ign.com/articles/suicide-squad-kill-the-justice-league-developers-wanted-to-quit-the-industry-after-the-game-was-released)** · IGN
Two former Rocksteady developers have opened up about the grueling, seven-year development cycle of *Suicide Squad: Kill the Justice League*. The heavy pressure from Warner Bros. to maximize replayability and player engagement through spreadsheets burned the team out, leading the developers to leave the AAA space entirely to create an indie RPG deckbuilder.

**[Cyberpunk 2077 Hits 40 Million Copies Sold](https://www.ign.com/articles/a-great-foundation-for-our-upcoming-projects-in-this-universe-cd-projekt-hails-cyberpunk-2077-as-it-hits-an-incredible-40-million-copies-sold)** · IGN
CD Projekt Red has successfully completed one of the greatest redemption arcs in gaming history, announcing today that *Cyberpunk 2077* has reached an astonishing 40 million units sold. Co-CEO Michał Nowakowski celebrated the milestone, noting it serves as a strong foundation for future projects, including the upcoming *Cyberpunk 2* (Project Orion) which will feature both Night City and a brand new location described as "Chicago gone wrong".

**[Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced Global Release Times](https://www.ign.com/articles/assassins-creed-black-flag-resynced-global-release-and-preload-times-confirmed)** · IGN
Edward Kenway is ready to set sail once again on July 7, with pre-loads for the much-anticipated remake already live on Xbox Series X/S and coming soon to PC and PS5. The original's multiplayer and modern-day first-person segments have been totally axed to focus entirely on Caribbean exploration, and console players can dive in right at midnight local time.

**[Danganronpa 2x2 Delayed to 2027](https://www.ign.com/articles/danganronpa-2x2-delayed-to-early-2027)** · IGN
Fans hoping to return to Hope's Peak Academy this year will have to wait, as Spike Chunsoft has officially delayed the *Danganronpa 2: Goodbye Despair* remaster to early 2027. To cushion the blow, the development team revealed "Slayhem Mode," an entirely remixed storyline with different victims, culprits, and tricks that adds roughly 20% more content to the base game.

**[Captain America's Marvel Rivals Skin Breaks the Internet](https://www.ign.com/articles/marvel-rivals-fans-think-the-new-captain-america-skin-is-a-mod-but-its-actually-just-his-rod)** · IGN
NetEase Games launched a 4th of July "Seaside Sentinel" swimsuit skin for Captain America in *Marvel Rivals*, and it's leaving players incredibly distracted. Despite initial shock and accusations from fans that the incredibly revealing cosmetic was a fan-made mod, the beefy mankini skin is 100% official and available in the store right now alongside beachwear for Loki and White Fox.
