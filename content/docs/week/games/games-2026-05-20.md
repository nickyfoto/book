# Gaming News — 2026-05-20

Sony has officially announced a massive State of Play showcase slated for June 2, 2026, which will run for over 60 minutes. The presentation will feature a fresh, highly anticipated look at Insomniac’s *Marvel's Wolverine* ahead of its September release, alongside updates on a slate of other upcoming PS5 titles. 

## News & Reviews

**[Detroit: Become Human Dev Quantic Dream Kills Live Service Game 3 Months After Early Access Launch](https://www.ign.com/articles/detroit-become-human-dev-quantic-dream-kills-live-service-game-3-months-after-early-access-launch-insists-star-wars-eclipse-continues-as-planned)**
In another brutal blow to the live-service market, Quantic Dream has pulled the plug on its 3v3 MOBA *Spellcasters Chronicles* just three months into Early Access. The game suffered from disastrously low player counts, peaking at just 888 players on Steam, and its cancelation will unfortunately result in a round of studio layoffs. However, the studio insists that development on the highly anticipated *Star Wars Eclipse* remains entirely unaffected.

**[Subnautica 2 Outlines Changes Coming to the Game in Response to No Killing Fish Debate](https://www.ign.com/articles/subnautica-2-outlines-changes-coming-to-the-game-in-response-to-no-killing-fish-debate)**
*Subnautica 2* has had a bumpy Early Access launch, battling backlash over a wildly overreaching EULA (which is now being adjusted) and community frustration regarding predatory fish. While developer Unknown Worlds firmly stated they will not add lethal weapons to the game to preserve its focus on vulnerability, they promised upcoming patches to fix aggressive predator behavior and improve player mitigation tools. 

**[Open-World Lord of Rings RPG and New Kingdom Come Game Confirmed in Development at Warhorse Studios](https://www.ign.com/articles/open-world-lord-of-rings-rpg-and-new-kingdom-come-game-confirmed-in-development-at-warhorse-studios)**
Warhorse Studios, the team behind the stellar *Kingdom Come: Deliverance* franchise, is officially developing an “open world Middle-earth RPG” based on *The Lord of the Rings*. Because they described it as a Middle-earth RPG, it might fall outside the traditional timeline of Tolkien's core books, but injecting Warhorse's penchant for deep, story-driven mechanics into a fantasy setting is incredibly promising. The studio also confirmed a new *Kingdom Come* adventure is in the works.

**[Embracer 'Actively Exploring’ External Partnerships for the Likes of Saints Row, Deus Ex, TimeSplitters, and More](https://www.ign.com/articles/embracer-actively-exploring-external-partnerships-for-the-likes-of-saints-row-deus-ex-timesplitters-and-more)**
There is finally a glimmer of hope for some of gaming's best dormant franchises. Embracer Group is spinning off a new business unit, Fellowship Entertainment, to actively explore external development partnerships for IPs like *Deus Ex*, *Saints Row*, *Legacy of Kain*, and *TimeSplitters*. While no specific games were announced, this means studios outside the messy Embracer umbrella could finally breathe life back into these beloved series.

**[Ubisoft Promises New Assassin’s Creed, Far Cry, and Ghost Recon Games All by March 2029](https://www.ign.com/articles/ubisoft-promises-new-assassins-creed-far-cry-and-ghost-recon-games-all-by-march-2029)**
Ubisoft's latest financial report confirmed an aggressive roadmap aiming to launch new mainline titles for *Assassin’s Creed*, *Far Cry*, and *Ghost Recon* by the end of March 2029. Specifically, the witchcraft-themed *Assassin's Creed Hexe* is slated to drop sometime in 2027. Conspicuously absent from their pipeline updates, however, were long-lost projects like *Beyond Good and Evil 2* and the *Splinter Cell* remake. 

**[Hellraiser: Revival Continues to Look Absolutely Disgusting in New Gameplay Trailer](https://www.ign.com/articles/hellraiser-revival-continues-to-look-absolutely-disgusting-in-new-gameplay-trailer)**
Saber Interactive's *Hellraiser: Revival* is shaping up to be a deliciously gory survival horror experience. A new trailer highlighted the Genesis Configuration—an evil puzzle box that functions almost like a supernatural gravity gun, allowing players to hurl rusty spikes at enemies or harness open flames for destructive attacks. The developers confirmed they are embracing the franchise's extreme violence without censorship, aiming for maximum shock value.
