# iPhone 18 Pro Pre-Orders Launch Alongside CEO AI Strategy and Scientific Breakthroughs — 2026-09-12

## Highlights
Today’s Apple ecosystem news centers on the official launch of iPhone 18 Pro pre-orders, which saw the Apple Store go down briefly before strong initial demand pushed shipping estimates for select Pro Max configurations into October. Alongside the retail surge, newly appointed Apple CEO John Ternus delivered his first keynote vision—dubbed the "Ternus Doctrine"—framing the iPhone as a context-aware "intelligent personal hub" anchored by on-device Apple Silicon and Private Cloud Compute. Meanwhile, Apple’s machine learning research team published SimpleDesign, a generative AI model for protein co-design, underscoring the company's expanding AI scope across consumer devices, operating systems, and fundamental scientific research.

## Top Stories
- **[iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max Pre-Orders Live as Delivery Dates Slip into October](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/12/iphone-18-pro-available-to-pre-order/)**: Apple has officially opened pre-orders for the flagship iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max across more than 65 countries starting at \$1,199 and \$1,299 respectively. Both handsets introduce the A20 Pro chip, a variable aperture main camera system, a smaller Dynamic Island capable of showing up to three Live Activities simultaneously, and upgraded vapor chamber cooling. Within hours of pre-orders opening at 5:00 a.m. PT, shipping dates for several 256GB and 512GB iPhone 18 Pro Max configurations slipped into mid-October, reflecting heavy early demand. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/12/iphone-18-pro-available-to-pre-order/))

- **[The 'Ternus Doctrine': New Apple CEO John Ternus Stakes Out Personal AI Strategy](https://www.macsparky.com/blog/2026/09/the-ternus-doctrine/)**: In his opening keynote address as Apple CEO, John Ternus articulated his core philosophy for Apple's placement in artificial intelligence, defining the iPhone as an "intelligent personal hub". Ternus asserted that personal AI becomes transformative when it integrates deeply with a user's routines, calendar, and relationships, emphasizing local Apple Silicon execution and Private Cloud Compute to safeguard user privacy. The strategy marks a clear attempt to differentiate Apple's ecosystem from third-party cloud chatbots by capitalizing on hardware-level integration and local user context. ([MacSparky](https://www.macsparky.com/blog/2026/09/the-ternus-doctrine/))

- **[U.S. iPhone 18 Pro Max Uses Qualcomm Modem While Global Models Feature In-House C2](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/12/iphone-18-pro-max-us-model-difference/)**: Updated technical documentation reveals that while the iPhone 18 Pro uses Apple's custom third-generation C2 cellular modem worldwide, the iPhone 18 Pro Max in the United States continues to utilize a Qualcomm Snapdragon modem. Apple's in-house C2 modem adds mmWave 5G support, faster upload speeds, and 15% better power efficiency over the prior C1X, but existing supply agreements with Qualcomm likely account for the U.S. Pro Max exception. The foldable iPhone Duo and non-U.S. Pro models maintain the C2 modem globally as Apple progresses toward full cellular independence. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/12/iphone-18-pro-max-us-model-difference/))

- **[Apple Machine Learning Team Unveils 'SimpleDesign' Protein AI Model](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/11/apple-researchers-unveil-simpledesign-a-new-ai-model-for-protein-design/)**: Apple researchers published a landmark study introducing SimpleDesign, a generative AI model designed for the joint co-design of protein amino acid sequences and continuous 3D structures. Building on their prior SimpleFold framework, SimpleDesign eliminates multi-stage tokenization autoencoders in favor of end-to-end training directly in data space using flow-matching techniques on corrupted sequence-structure pairs. Benchmark evaluations showed SimpleDesign achieving competitive or superior results compared to multi-stage protein generators while significantly streamlining model complexity. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/11/apple-researchers-unveil-simpledesign-a-new-ai-model-for-protein-design/))

- **[iPhone Duo Software Includes 'Shift Left Edge' Setting for Case Compatibility](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/12/iphone-duo-fake-bezel-case-compatibility/)**: Hands-on exploration of Apple's upcoming foldable iPhone Duo software uncovered a dedicated display toggle called 'Shift Left Edge'. When activated, the setting inserts an artificial black border along the left side of the outer display, shifting on-screen elements away from the folding hinge. Designed specifically to accommodate protective cases whose lips might otherwise obscure outer screen edges, the setting showcases Apple's software adaptations for its new foldable form factor ahead of its October release. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/12/iphone-duo-fake-bezel-case-compatibility/))

- **[Major U.S. Carriers Launch Up to \$1,200 Trade-In Deals for iPhone 18 Pro](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/12/best-carrier-deals-iphone-18-pro/)**: AT&T, T-Mobile, and Verizon have launched extensive pre-order incentives offering up to \$1,200 in bill credits with eligible smartphone trade-ins for the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max.
