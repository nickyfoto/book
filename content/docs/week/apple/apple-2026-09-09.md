# Apple ‘Surprise and Shine’ Keynote: iPhone Duo Foldable, iPhone 18 Pro, and Watch Series 12 Unveiled — 2026-09-09

## Highlights
Today’s Apple "Surprise and Shine" keynote marked a monumental shift for the Apple ecosystem under new CEO John Ternus, headlined by the debut of Apple’s first foldable smartphone, the iPhone Duo. Alongside the Duo, Apple introduced the flagship iPhone 18 Pro series powered by the 2nm A20 Pro chip and a variable aperture camera system, refreshed the Apple Watch lineup with an all-new Health Sensing System, and brought Active Noise Cancellation to the standard AirPods tier. The company also released Release Candidate builds across all major operating systems, setting the stage for public rollouts on September 14.

## Top Stories
- **[Apple Unveils Foldable iPhone Duo Starting at \$1,999](https://www.macstories.net/news/iphone-18-pro-and-iphone-duo-the-macstories-overview/)**: Apple officially entered the foldable market with the iPhone Duo, featuring a 5.4-inch outer cover screen that unfolds into a 7.6-inch inner display. Powered by the 2nm A20 Pro chip and Apple's custom C2 cellular modem, the device includes dual 48MP Fusion cameras, a custom nano-texture matte folding screen, and support for the USB-C Apple Pencil. Pre-orders open October 16 starting at \$1,999 for 256GB, with general availability launching October 23 running iOS 27.1. ([Source](https://www.macstories.net/news/iphone-18-pro-and-iphone-duo-the-macstories-overview/))

- **[iPhone 18 Pro Lineup Features Variable Aperture Camera and 2nm A20 Pro Chip](https://www.macworld.com/article/2953687/iphone-18-pro-max-2026-release-date-design-specs-price.html)**: The iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max introduce a 48MP Fusion Main camera with a laser-cut 6-blade variable mechanical aperture (f/1.48 to f/4) for precise depth of field and 50% better low-light capture. Driven by TSMC's 2nm A20 Pro processor and a redesigned vapor chamber with 3x surface area, the Pro models deliver up to 40% higher sustained performance and up to 45 hours of video playback on the Pro Max. Pre-orders begin Saturday, September 12 starting at \$1,199 for the Pro and \$1,299 for the Pro Max, shipping September 18. ([Source](https://www.macworld.com/article/2953687/iphone-18-pro-max-2026-release-date-design-specs-price.html))

- **[Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 Debut All-New Health Sensing System](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/09/apple-watch-series-12-announced-all-day-hr-sensor/)**: Driven by the S11 chip, the new Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 feature a redesigned sensor array enabling continuous 5-second heart-rate tracking, 24x higher-frequency HRV sampling, and a daily Readiness score. Both watches introduce Audio Intelligence capabilities including 15-second Live Rewind transcripts, automated Siri Recap summaries, and Sound Recognition. The Series 12 brings back a ceramic case option alongside new aluminum and titanium finishes, while the Ultra 4 expands battery life to 50 hours, with pre-orders open today starting at \$399 and \$799 respectively. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/09/apple-watch-series-12-announced-all-day-hr-sensor/))

- **[AirPods 5 Bring Active Noise Cancellation to \$129 Entry-Level Tier](https://www.macworld.com/article/3230518/5-really-cool-things-about-the-new-airpods-5.html)**: Apple updated its open-ear earbud lineup with AirPods 5, integrating Active Noise Cancellation into the base \$129 model via a new multi-port acoustic architecture that eliminates up to 50% more external noise. A \$149 version adds a wireless charging case, on-stem volume control, and extended battery life. Both models are available for pre-order today and ship on Friday, September 18. ([Source](https://www.macworld.com/article/3230518/5-really-cool-things-about-the-new-airpods-5.html))

- **[iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate Release Candidates Live; App Store Submissions Open](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=k1mtkt1k)**: Following the event, Apple seeded Release Candidate builds for iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS Golden Gate, watchOS 27, visionOS 27, and tvOS 27 ahead of official public releases on Monday, September 14. Developers can now download the Xcode 27 RC and submit updated apps taking advantage of Siri AI, Foundation Models, and new App Store product page preview tools. ([Source](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=k1mtkt1k))
