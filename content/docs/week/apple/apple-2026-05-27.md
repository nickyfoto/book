# Apple Ecosystem Digest: Future iPhones, Anti-Theft Tech, and Satellite Shifts — 2026-05-27

Today's news paints a vivid picture of Apple's future, from rumors of a quad-curved iPhone 19 Pro to tangible leaks of third-party cases for the highly anticipated foldable iPhone. ## Top Stories
- **[Amazon to Acquire Apple’s Globalstar Stake](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/27/amazon-apple-globalstar-deal/)**: Amazon is acquiring Apple's satellite provider, Globalstar, in an $11.57 billion deal that involves absorbing Apple's 20% equity stake. Amazon has promised to maintain support for iPhone and Apple Watch satellite features (like Emergency SOS and Find My) via its expanded low Earth orbit network.
- **[Apple Developing Instant Anti-Snatch Lock](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/27/apple-anti-theft-iphone-feature/)**: Code reveals Apple is working on a new security feature that uses the iPhone's accelerometer and paired Apple Watches to detect when a phone is quickly snatched from a user's hand. The device will automatically lock and activate Stolen Device Protection to prevent thieves from accessing sensitive data while the screen is unlocked.
- **[First Cases for Apple’s Foldable iPhone Surface](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/27/ases-for-foldable-iphone-surface-online/)**: Accessory maker iFunSmart has listed the first protective cases for Apple's anticipated foldable iPhone, corroborating rumors of a slim profile with a two-lens rear camera plateau. The device is widely expected to launch alongside the iPhone 18 lineup in fall 2026 for around $2,000.
- **[Apple Publishes Guide for Confusing Creator Studio Icons](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/27/apple-document-to-help-tell-studio-apps-apart/)**: Following the introduction of the Creator Studio subscription bundle, Apple has issued a dedicated support document to help users distinguish between the new subscription app icons and the older standalone purchase icons. The new icons controversially feature homogenized Liquid Glass designs that replace the classic, distinct looks of apps like MainStage.
- **[Trade-In Values Increased for iPhone, Mac, and iPad](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/27/apple-updates-trade-in-values-may-2026/)**: Apple has quietly boosted its trade-in estimates for most current hardware, raising the top-end iPhone 16 Pro Max offer from $685 to $695. Most Android trade-in values, however, saw slight reductions.
- **[Apple Wallet Digital IDs Expand to Arkansas](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/27/apple-wallet-digital-id-support-expands-to-arkansas/)**: Arkansas has become the 14th U.S. state to allow residents to add their driver's licenses and state IDs to Apple Wallet. Additionally, Atlanta's MARTA Breeze Card is now fully supported in Apple Wallet with Express Transit functionality.
