# Apple Daily Digest — 2026-07-26

## Highlights
Apple's hardware roadmap is coming into sharper focus today, with leaks indicating significant upcoming additions ranging from a water-resistant iPad mini to privacy-focused AI smart glasses debuting in 2027. Meanwhile, an all-new "Apple Upgrade" device leasing program is set to launch this Tuesday, representing a major shift in how customers will finance their ecosystem devices.

## Top Stories
- **Apple Planning to Unveil Privacy-Focused Smart Glasses**: Apple is reportedly targeting WWDC 2027 to reveal its highly anticipated AI smart glasses, allowing developers time to create third-party apps ahead of a late-2027 release. The company is actively prototyping hardware and software privacy features, including a potential camera system that feeds visual data to AI tools but strictly prohibits photo or video recording. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/26/apple-smart-glasses-wwdc-2027-report/))
- **'Apple Upgrade’ Program Launching on Tuesday**: A new device leasing program in partnership with Klarna is expected to go live on July 28, completely replacing new sign-ups for the existing iPhone Upgrade Program. It will feature 24-month terms for iPhones and Watches, and 36-month terms for iPads and Macs, offering lower monthly payments without bundling AppleCare+. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/26/apple-upgrade-program-launching-tuesday/))
- **Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4: What to expect**: The upcoming Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 models will forgo major design changes in favor of internal speed improvements and health-tracking updates. These wearables are expected to feature the first true processing performance upgrade since 2023, though a rumored Touch ID addition seems increasingly unlikely. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/26/apple-watch-series-12-and-ultra-4-what-to-expect/))

**Apple’s First Water-Resistant iPad to Launch Later This Year** ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/25/first-water-resistant-ipad/))
Apple's next iPad mini is poised to be the first tablet in the lineup to feature water resistance, potentially arriving by October. To protect against liquid ingress, Apple has reportedly developed a novel vibration-based speaker system that utilizes flat panels instead of traditional speaker grilles. Combined with a new OLED display and a major processing leap to either an A19 or A20 Pro chip, this sealed design could make the mini a robust competitor to waterproof e-readers like the Kindle Paperwhite. This piece is essential reading for anyone tracking the iPad mini's transformation into a significantly more premium device.
