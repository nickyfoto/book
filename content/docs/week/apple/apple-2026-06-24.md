# Apple Daily Digest: Prime Day Steals, Foldable iPhone Progress, and iOS 27 Features — 2026-06-24

## Highlights
Today's news is dominated by aggressive Amazon Prime Day discounts across Apple's hardware lineup, presenting some of the best buying opportunities of the year before anticipated price hikes. Meanwhile, developers are digging into major new Siri AI and HomeKit capabilities in the latest iOS 27 betas.

## Top Stories
- **[Foldable iPhone 'Ultra' Set for Production in July Despite Hinge Issues](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/24/foldable-iphone-ultra-set-for-production-july/)**: Apple's highly anticipated foldable device is reportedly on track for a September release as supply chain sources indicate recent 3D-printed hinge durability tests have been successful. Most assembly tolerance and noise issues have been officially resolved, setting the stage for Apple's biggest launch of the year.
- **[Best Prime Day Apple deals 2026: Big savings on iPad, AirPods, Macs, Apple Watch](https://www.macworld.com/article/2391988/best-apple-prime-day-deals-2026-june-24.html)**: Amazon Prime Day has brought all-time low prices across the ecosystem, notably dropping the recently released AirPods Max 2 to just $399. Shoppers can also find the M5 MacBook Air for $949, the Apple Watch SE 3 for an unprecedented $199, and steep discounts on the iPad mini and M5 MacBook Pro line.
- **[iOS 27 gives the Camera app on iPhone a brand new mode](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/24/ios-27-gives-the-camera-app-on-iphone-a-brand-new-mode/)**: The iOS 27 Camera app introduces a new "Siri" mode that centralizes visual intelligence features. This allows users to simply point their iPhone at objects like restaurant storefronts or event flyers to instantly pull up reviews, translate text, or add calendar events directly from the viewfinder.

- **[Google Lowering Play Store Fees and Allowing Alternative Payments Worldwide](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/24/google-play-store-fee-change/)**: In a move that could indirectly impact Apple's ongoing legal battles, Google is implementing major worldwide changes to its Play Store, including allowing alternative payment systems and reducing developer fees to between 10% and 25%. These changes, rolling out initially in the US, UK, and EEA, are a direct result of Google's settlement with Epic Games.

Despite the excitement surrounding its $399 Prime Day price tag, this critical review highlights the missed opportunities in the newly released AirPods Max 2.
