# WWDC Anticipation Builds Alongside Foldable iPhone and MacBook Neo Milestones — 2026-06-02

## Highlights
WWDC is just days away, and the rumor mill is in overdrive with leaks pointing to macOS 27 being named "Big Bear" and major Siri AI enhancements powered by Google's Gemini coming in iOS 27. Meanwhile, Apple's hardware roadmap is coming into focus with the MacBook Neo shattering sales records and credible reports detailing a revolutionary liquid metal hinge for the highly anticipated foldable iPhone.

## Top Stories
- **Leaker: Foldable 'iPhone Ultra’ Will Feature Liquid Metal Hinge**: Prototypes of Apple's first foldable iPhone have reportedly reached global carriers for testing, featuring a highly durable liquid metal hinge. This significant engineering milestone, over 15 years in the making, suggests the device is on track for a potential September launch alongside vapor chamber cooling capabilities. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/02/foldable-iphone-will-feature-liquid-metal-hinge/))
- **MacBook Neo Outsold Every Other Mac in Its Debut Quarter**: The newly launched $599 MacBook Neo shipped an impressive 1.1 million units globally in its first three weeks, outselling both the new M5 MacBook Air and M5 MacBook Pro. This massive surge confirms Apple's successful expansion into the budget laptop market, forcing rivals like Dell to aggressively alter their pricing strategies with the $599 XPS 13. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/02/macbook-neo-outsold-every-other-mac/))
- **2026 Apple Design Awards Winners Announced**: Ahead of next week's WWDC, Apple has unveiled the 12 winners of its prestigious Apple Design Awards, celebrating excellence in app and game development. Standouts include "grug" for Delight and Fun, "Primary: News in Depth" for Social Impact, and the immersive Vision Pro experience "NBA: Live Games & Scores" for Innovation. ([MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/news/2026-apple-design-awards-winners-announced/))
- **iPhone 18 Pro leak points to smallest battery gain since 2020**: Leaked capacity figures indicate the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro will see a very minor battery size increase, clocking in at 4,288 mAh for the U.S. eSIM-only version and 4,056 mAh for the physical SIM version. Despite the small physical increase of under two percent, users can still expect improved real-world battery life thanks to the efficiency of the rumored A20 Pro 2nm chip. ([Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/3154305/iphone-18-pro-leak-points-to-smallest-battery-gain-since-2020.html))
- **Apple facing congressional pressure over closure of first unionized US store**: Forty members of the U.S. Congress, along with Maryland Governor Wes Moore, have formally urged Apple CEO Tim Cook to reconsider the closure of the Towson Town Center retail store. Lawmakers are accusing Apple of union-busting and retaliatory practices, demanding that the company offer unionized workers the same transfer rights outside a 50-mile radius as non-union employees. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/01/apple-facing-congressional-pressure-over-closure-of-first-unionized-us-store/))
