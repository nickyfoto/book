# Apple Prepares for 'Surprise and Shine' Keynote Amid Major Mac Silicon and AI Hardware Moves — 2026-08-29

## Highlights
Apple is gearing up for its September 9 "Surprise and Shine" event, where anticipation centers on the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro lineup and the debut of the foldable iPhone Ultra. At the same time, the Mac lineup has taken a dramatic leap forward with the sudden launch of the M6-powered Mac mini alongside powerhouse M5 Max and M5 Ultra Mac Studio workstations. Between roadmaps for upcoming AI smart glasses, CarPlay ecosystem expansions, and streaming price hikes, Apple's hardware and platform strategies are moving at an aggressive pace.

## Top Stories
- **Apple Announces September 9 'Surprise and Shine' Event**: Apple has officially scheduled its annual fall event for Wednesday, September 9, at Apple Park, led by regional taglines including "Surprise and Shine" across primary English-speaking markets. The presentation is expected to introduce the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, and Apple's inaugural foldable device—tentatively dubbed the iPhone Ultra—while base iPhone 18 models are held back until spring 2027. Early reports indicate testers are largely impressed by the foldable device's pocketable hinge and iPad-style layouts, despite the notable absence of a telephoto camera lens. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/29/top-stories-apple-surprise-and-shine-event-invites/))
- **Mac mini and Mac Studio Refresh Debuts M6 and M5 Ultra Silicon**: Apple refreshed its desktop lineup with the launch of the new Mac mini featuring either the M6 chip or M5 Pro, alongside high-end Mac Studio models equipped with M5 Max and M5 Ultra processors. The M6 introduces a 12-core CPU, a 12-core GPU, up to 170GB/s unified memory bandwidth, and Apple's first dual 16-core Neural Engine architecture for up to 4× faster AI execution. The flagship Mac Studio brings PCIe Gen 6 SSD architecture, Thunderbolt 5 with 120Gb/s transfer rates, Apple's N1 wireless chip delivering Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6, and up to a 36-core M5 Ultra CPU. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/29/top-stories-apple-surprise-and-shine-event-invites/))
- **First-Generation Apple AI Smart Glasses Slated for 2027**: Apple is developing its first pair of consumer AI glasses designed around Visual Intelligence and audio playback rather than heads-up visual displays. Engineered in-house across acetate frames in several classic profiles, the eyewear uses onboard cameras and open-ear speakers to feed Siri environmental context and real-world translations without standard photo or video capture capabilities. The product is anticipated to be previewed at WWDC 2027 before shipping in the fall alongside Apple's 20th-anniversary all-glass iPhone. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/29/apple-first-ai-smart-glasses-feature-release-date-roundup/))
- **Meta AI Becomes Fourth Conversational Chatbot on Apple CarPlay**: The latest iOS release of the Meta AI client has gained integration with Apple CarPlay, joining ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Grok in the vehicle dashboard interface. Enabled under Apple's conversational app frameworks ahead of native Siri AI updates scheduled for iOS 27, the rollout is currently undergoing account-gated testing. This addition highlights how CarPlay continues to expand third-party voice assistants while drivers wait for system-level Apple Intelligence updates. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/29/apple-carplay-gaining-a-new-ai-chatbot-app-before-siri-ai-arrives/))
- **Apple TV and Apple One Subscription Rates Increase**: Apple implemented a subscription rate increase for its Apple TV streaming platform, elevating monthly pricing by \$2 to \$14.99 and annual plans by \$20 to \$119 in the United States. The adjustment also pushed the Apple One Individual bundle up to \$21.95 per month, with matching price adjustments taking effect across Brazil, Chile, and Mexico. This marks the fourth subscription rate revision for the streaming service since its inception. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/29/top-stories-apple-surprise-and-shine-event-invites/))
