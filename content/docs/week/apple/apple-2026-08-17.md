- **[Critical macOS Screen Sharing Vulnerability Actively Exploited to Mine Cryptocurrency](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/17/macos-screen-sharing-flaw-exploited/)**: Security regulators and researchers have confirmed that a high-severity vulnerability affecting macOS’s default Screen Sharing port (5900) is being actively exploited in the wild. Attackers are successfully gaining root access on unpatched systems and installing Monero crypto miners. Although Apple has addressed this authentication issue with "improved state management" in macOS Tahoe 26.6.1, macOS Sequoia 15.7.9, and macOS Sonoma 14.8.9, users are strongly urged to apply these updates immediately and disable screen sharing when not in use. Under the new commitment, Apple will remove "possibly discouraging symbols and wording" from its tracking prompts in Germany, granting third-party developers more freedom to explain the business value of personalized advertising and combine the request with GDPR-mandated consent.
