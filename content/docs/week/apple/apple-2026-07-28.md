# Apple Daily Digest — 2026-07-28

## Highlights
Today's news is heavily anchored by Apple's strategic financial moves, notably the launch of its new Klarna-backed Apple Upgrade leasing program and the company briefly hitting a historic $5 trillion market cap. We also see significant momentum in the smart home space with impending new hardware on the horizon, and fresh details emerging around the highly anticipated "MacBook Ultra" and upcoming iOS 27 developer features.

## Top Stories
- **Apple Retires iPhone Upgrade Program for Klarna-Backed Leases**: Apple has officially launched Apple Upgrade, a new leasing model replacing the decade-old iPhone Upgrade Program. The program extends to Macs, iPads, and Apple Watches, offering lower base payments but removing bundled AppleCare+ and eliminating device ownership at the end of the term. Apple also clarified that missed payments will not trigger an iOS 27 "Restricted Mode" device lockout, putting earlier rumors to rest. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/28/apple-upgrade-program-klarna/))

- **Apple Just Became a $5 Trillion Company**: Apple's market capitalization briefly surpassed $5 trillion during intraday trading as its stock climbed past $340. This historic valuation makes Apple only the second publicly traded company to reach this threshold, allowing it to once again overtake Nvidia as the world's most valuable company. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/28/apple-just-hit-5-trillion-market-cap-for-first-time/))

- **All-New Apple Home Hub Reportedly Launching as Soon as October**: Apple is preparing to release an all-new Siri AI-powered smart home hub featuring a 7-inch display, facial recognition, and a hybrid tvOS/watchOS interface. Available in tabletop and wall-mounted versions, it will serve as the centerpiece of Apple's renewed smart home push. Updated, AI-ready versions of the Apple TV 4K and HomePod mini are also expected to debut this fall alongside the new hub. ([Bloomberg via MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/28/apple-home-hub-as-soon-as-october/))

- **MacBook Ultra OLED Touch Panel to Be Exclusively Supplied by Samsung**: Samsung Display will be the exclusive supplier of OLED touch panels for Apple's radically redesigned "MacBook Ultra". LG reportedly could not meet the volume or substrate efficiency requirements for the large tandem OLED screens, leaving Samsung as the sole provider. This lack of supplier competition is expected to drive up manufacturing costs, which may ultimately be passed on to consumers when the laptop launches in early 2027. ([The Elec via 9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/28/touchscreen-decision-may-push-macbook-ultra-price-even-higher/))

- **Apple Shares 'Ted Lasso' Season 4 Trailer**: Apple TV+ released the first trailer for the fourth season of *Ted Lasso*, which will premiere on August 5. The new season shifts focus to a second-division women's soccer team, with fan-favorite characters returning. At the Los Angeles premiere, incoming Apple CEO John Ternus and current CEO Tim Cook affirmed their ongoing commitment to building momentum for the Apple TV+ entertainment division. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/28/ted-lasso-season-four-trailer/))

## Articles Worth Reading

**iOS 27: All the New Phone and FaceTime Features** ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-27-phone-facetime/))
The upcoming iOS 27 update introduces subtle but powerful utility enhancements for communication apps. Notably, the Phone app gains an on-device Apple Intelligence feature called Call Context, which intelligently pulls relevant reservation or confirmation details from Mail when you dial businesses. FaceTime is also adding Dual Capture for iPhone 17 models, allowing users to stream video from both front and rear cameras simultaneously during calls.

**Claude chats exposed in Google searches – another endorsement of Apple’s privacy approach** ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/28/claude-chats-exposed-in-google-searches-another-endorsement-of-apples-privacy-approach/))
A major privacy blunder by Anthropic left hundreds of shared Claude AI conversations exposed and indexable via Google search. The leaked chats included highly sensitive data, such as private cryptocurrency keys and legal transcripts, highlighting the severe risks of server-side AI processing. This incident starkly underscores the value of Apple's Private Cloud Compute and on-device processing approach, which ensures user data is never retained or exposed to third parties.

**Here’s everything new for Apple Wallet in iOS 27** ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/28/heres-everything-new-for-apple-wallet-in-ios-27/))
Apple is positioning the Wallet app as a comprehensive hub with several new additions in iOS 27. Users will be able to scan and import physical passes using visual intelligence, manually create custom passes, and track recurring Apple Card subscriptions through a dedicated calendar view. The update also integrates bill-splitting capabilities via Apple Cash directly from receipt scans, making it faster to settle transactions.

**iOS 27 includes smarter Find My location sharing for Apple Watch users** ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/28/ios-27-smarter-find-my-location-sharing-apple-watch/))
For users who frequently leave their iPhones at home while wearing an Apple Watch, iOS 27 offers a much-needed fix to the Find My network. Location sharing will now intelligently switch to use the Apple Watch's location when the paired iPhone is no longer nearby, ensuring friends and family see accurate, real-time tracking. Additionally, watchOS 27 consolidates all Find My features into a single, unified app with a new full-screen map interface.
