# Apple Ecosystem Daily Digest — 2026-08-02

## Highlights
Today's Apple news cycle is dominated by hardware rumors and supply chain dynamics, alongside the usual cadence of software updates. While enthusiasts eagerly await imminent hardware releases like the foldable iPhone Ultra and camera-equipped AirPods, Apple is reportedly wrestling with severe MacBook Air supply constraints stemming from the broader AI memory crisis. 

## Top Stories
- **[MacBook Air Faces Major Supply Shortages](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/02/macbook-air-reportedly-facing-major-supply-shortages-due-to-ai-driven-memory-crisis/)**: The AI datacenter boom has shifted global memory supply chains, causing significant shortages and price hikes for consumer-grade HBM2 DRAM and SSDs. As a result, Apple is struggling to keep the M5 MacBook Air in stock, a situation exacerbated by the company prioritizing production of the entry-level M5 MacBook Pro ahead of upcoming M6 refreshes this fall. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/02/macbook-air-reportedly-facing-major-supply-shortages-due-to-ai-driven-memory-crisis/))

References to the B790 model have already surfaced in iOS 27 beta 2 code, suggesting Visual Intelligence features could reach users' ears this year. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/02/apple-ai-smart-glasses-to-serve-as-health-companion/))
