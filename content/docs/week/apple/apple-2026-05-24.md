# WWDC 2026 Rumors Heat Up: iOS 27 and watchOS 27 Details Emerge — 2026-05-24

## Highlights
Today's news is heavily focused on the rapidly approaching WWDC 2026 keynote, with a flood of leaks painting a clear picture of what to expect from Apple's next major operating system updates. The overarching theme is refinement and customization, as Apple prepares to deliver major AI improvements across the board, alongside much-needed interface redesigns for core apps like Camera and Settings. 

## Top Stories
- **iOS 27 Camera UI Redesign**: Apple is reportedly overhauling the Camera app in iOS 27 to allow for full UI customization and deeper Visual Intelligence integration. Users will soon be able to choose the exact placement of controls like flash, timer, and exposure, catering directly to professional workflows. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/23/new-camera-app-and-photos-editing-features-coming-to-ios-27/))
- **watchOS 27 Features & Health Coach Delays**: The next major Apple Watch update will heavily prioritize performance, stability, and enhanced heart-rate tracking, though the ambitious "Project Mulberry" AI health coach has reportedly been delayed. Users can also expect a new "Modular Ultra" variant watch face and upcoming satellite features like Apple Maps via satellite. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/24/watchos-27-rumored-features/))
- **AirPods Settings Revamp in OS 27**: Addressing years of feature bloat, Apple is completely redesigning the cluttered AirPods settings menu across iOS 27, iPadOS 27, and macOS 27. The new interface promises to be far more streamlined, making it easier for users to manage complex capabilities like hearing aid functions and head gestures. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/24/report-ios-27-to-revamp-the-airpods-settings-ui/))
- **System-Level Google Cast in the EU**: In response to the European Union’s Digital Markets Act, Apple may allow iOS 27 users in the EU to set third-party streaming protocols, such as Google Cast, as their default alternative to AirPlay. This would allow native iOS integration for smart speakers without requiring AirPlay or Bluetooth support. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/24/ios-27-google-cast-third-party-streaming-integration-eu/))
- **Major Boosts to Genmoji and Image Playground**: Following a lackluster visual debut in iOS 18, Apple's on-device image generation models are receiving a significant quality upgrade in iOS 27. The company is also expected to expand Image Playground to support third-party AI models beyond OpenAI's ChatGPT. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/24/apple-image-playground-and-gemoji-to-get-major-visual-improvements/))
