**CarPlay connection issues? This might explain why** ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/07/carplay-connection-issues/))
This article dives into a frustrating issue for drivers: random Apple CarPlay connection drops that occur in the exact same physical locations. Rather than an issue with the iPhone, cable, or vehicle, experts point to localized radio frequency interference, particularly in the 2.4GHz band, from broadcast antennas or point-to-point microwave links. Because CarPlay relies on a continuous wireless connection to project navigation and media, these invisible "dead zones" can temporarily break the link. This insightful explainer can save drivers hours of unnecessary troubleshooting and costly dealer visits.
