# Apple Ecosystem Daily: September 9 "Surprise and Shine" Keynote Set, M6 Silicon Architecture Unveiled, and Apple TV Price Hikes — 2026-08-28

## Highlights
With Apple’s **"Surprise and Shine" fall keynote** officially scheduled for September 9, anticipation is mounting for the debut of the **iPhone 18 Pro**, an ultra-premium **foldable iPhone**, and the public rollout of **iOS 27**. Amid the impending leadership transition of incoming CEO **John Ternus** taking the helm on September 1, Apple is simultaneously navigating subscription price hikes across **Apple TV** and **Apple One**, new 2nm **M6 silicon** architecture developments, and claim procedures for the **Siri AI class action settlement**. Today's news landscape balances major forward-looking platform roadmap revelations with deep operational retrospectives on **Tim Cook's 15-year executive legacy** and growing concerns over modern Mac infostealer security threats.

## Top Stories
- **[Apple Sets September 9 "Surprise and Shine" Event as John Ternus Prepares First Keynote](https://tidbits.com/2026/08/28/apples-surprise-and-shine-event-set-for-9-september-2026/)**: Apple has officially announced that its annual fall hardware showcase will take place on **Wednesday, September 9, 2026**, at Apple Park under the tagline "Surprise and Shine". Marking incoming CEO **John Ternus’s first product launch** following Tim Cook's transition to executive chairman on September 1, the event is set to headline the **iPhone 18 Pro**, the company’s first **foldable iPhone**, the **Apple Watch Series 12**, and the **Apple Watch Ultra 4**. In a major strategic realignment, Apple is splitting its product cycle to focus September entirely on premium flagships, deferring the standard **iPhone 18** and **iPhone Air 2** to spring 2027. ([TidBITS](https://tidbits.com/2026/08/28/apples-surprise-and-shine-event-set-for-9-september-2026/))
- **[Apple Hikes Subscription Rates for Apple TV and Apple One Individual Plans](https://www.macworld.com/article/3222857/apple-tv-just-got-another-price-increase.html)**: Apple has raised the standalone monthly price of **Apple TV from \$12.99 to \$14.99** (\$119 annually) for subscribers in the U.S., Brazil, Chile, and Mexico, representing the service's fourth rate increase since launching at \$4.99 in 2019. Consequently, the **Apple One Individual** bundle has increased from \$19.95 to \$21.95 per month, while Family and Premier tiers remain unchanged after seeing their own price bumps in July. The price adjustment arrives as Apple expands its programming expenditure on major sports broadcasting deals and hit series like **Dark Matter** and **Ted Lasso**. ([Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/3222857/apple-tv-just-got-another-price-increase.html))
- **[M6 Architecture Details Surface: Base Apple Silicon Adopts Three-Tier CPU Cores](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/28/apples-new-m6-adds-feature-previously-exclusive-to-high-end-chips/)**: Detailed technical specifications of Apple's newly introduced **M6 chip** reveal that the company has migrated its three-tier CPU core architecture down to an entry-level processor for the first time. Built on **TSMC’s cutting-edge 2nm process**, the 12-core base chip incorporates two top-tier "super cores," four performance cores, and six efficiency cores alongside a **Dual 16-core Neural Engine** with dedicated GPU Neural Accelerators. Apple will reportedly bypass dedicated M6 Pro and M6 Max iterations to accelerate the development of the **M7 processor family** for 2027, relying on M5 Pro, Max, and Ultra silicon for high-end systems in the interim. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/28/apples-new-m6-adds-feature-previously-exclusive-to-high-end-chips/))
- **[Siri AI Delay Class Action: Claim Submission Timeline and Payout Structure Finalized](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/28/apple-siri-ai-settlement-claims-timing/)**: New court filings regarding Apple's **\$250 million settlement** over delayed Apple Intelligence Siri capabilities propose that eligible U.S. customers can begin submitting claims on **September 21, 2026**, with the window closing on **December 21, 2026**. The lawsuit covers purchasers of the **iPhone 15 Pro and iPhone 16** lineups who bought devices between June 10, 2024, and March 29, 2025, offering baseline compensation of \$25 per device that may rise up to \$95 depending on overall claim participation. A final fairness hearing before the presiding judge is scheduled for **February 24, 2027**, meaning approved disbursements will commence next year. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/28/apple-siri-ai-settlement-claims-timing/))
- **[iPhone 18 Pro Pre-Orders Anticipated to Kick Off on Saturday, September 12](https://www.macworld.com/article/3222711/iphone-18-pro-pre-orders-later-than-usual.html)**: Supply chain reports indicate that customer pre-orders for the **iPhone 18 Pro**, **Apple Watch Series 12**, and **Apple Watch Ultra 4** will launch on **Saturday, September 12**, breaking Apple's customary post-keynote Friday timeline. Apple is intentionally circumventing Friday, September 11, echoing an identical one-day postponement observed in 2015 for the iPhone 6S on the anniversary of the 9/11 attacks. Sources also report that pre-orders may go live at **midnight PT** rather than 5:00 a.m. PT, while orders for the foldable **iPhone Ultra** could be staggered separately into late September. ([Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/3222711/iphone-18-pro-pre-orders-later-than-usual.html))
