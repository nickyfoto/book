# WWDC 2026 Keynote Unleashes Siri AI, iOS 27, and macOS Golden Gate — 2026-06-08

## Highlights
Today’s WWDC 2026 keynote marked a historic transition for Apple, showcasing the highly anticipated debut of "Siri AI" alongside the rollout of iOS 27, macOS Golden Gate, and other major software updates. The event also carried immense emotional weight as Tim Cook delivered his final keynote address as CEO, officially passing the baton to John Ternus ahead of the fall hardware releases. 

## Top Stories
- **Apple Reveals New AI Architecture Built Around Google Gemini Models**: Apple officially introduced Siri AI, a profoundly more capable assistant underpinned by a new architecture co-developed with Google using Gemini foundation models. The updated assistant boasts enhanced conversational depth, system-wide integration, a standalone app, and advanced on-device and Private Cloud Compute capabilities. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/08/apple-reveals-new-ai-architecture/))
- **Apple’s Most Powerful On-Device AI Now Requires iPhone 17 Pro or iPhone Air**: While iOS 27 brings numerous Apple Intelligence features to existing devices, Apple's most advanced on-device AI models require a minimum of 12GB of unified memory. This strict requirement limits features like expressive voices and advanced dictation to upcoming hardware such as the iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone Air, and Macs with at least an M3 chip. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/08/most-powerful-on-device-ai-now-requires-iphone-17-pro-or-air/))
- **Apple Announces macOS Golden Gate**: Apple revealed macOS 27, officially named "Golden Gate," which focuses heavily on performance optimizations and Liquid Glass design refinements, including a highly requested transparency slider. The update also officially ends support for all Intel-based Macs, cementing the complete transition to Apple silicon. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/08/apple-announces-macos-golden-gate/))
- **iOS 27 Hints at Foldable iPhone With App Resizability Push and New Framework Strings**: The first iOS 27 developer beta includes code-level references—such as "foldState" and "angleDegrees"—pointing directly to an upcoming foldable iPhone. Apple also heavily emphasized responsive, resizable app layouts during the Platforms State of the Union, setting the stage for a device rumored to be the "iPhone Ultra". ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/08/ios-27-hints-at-foldable-iphone-with-app-resizability-push/))
- **New Siri AI Features Won’t Initially Be Available in EU and China**: Apple confirmed that the new Siri AI experiences will not be available on iPhones or iPads in the European Union or China at launch. Apple explicitly blamed the EU's Digital Markets Act for the delay, citing privacy and security concerns with third-party interoperability requirements. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/08/siri-ai-not-available-eu-china/))

## Articles Worth Reading

**Apple Passwords Can Now Automatically Fix Weak and Compromised Passwords With Agentic AI** ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/08/apple-passwords-can-now-automatically-fix-passwords-with-agentic-ai/))
Apple's Passwords app is gaining a highly useful agentic AI feature in iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate. Instead of merely warning users about compromised credentials, the app can now use Safari and Apple Intelligence to autonomously navigate to a website, log in, generate a strong password, and update the account on the user's behalf. This zero-friction approach to security represents a major leap in practical AI application, allowing users to secure vulnerable accounts securely in the background.

**Tim Cook Delivers Farewell Remarks at His Last Apple Keynote as CEO** ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/08/tim-cook-delivers-remarks-at-his-last-keynote/))
With his transition to Executive Chairman slated for September 1, Tim Cook utilized the WWDC 2026 keynote to deliver an emotional farewell to the developer community. Cook reflected on his 15-year tenure as CEO, stating that witnessing what developers create with Apple's tools has been a constant reminder that "imagination has no limits". Incoming CEO John Ternus will take the helm just in time for the iPhone 18 Pro launch, making this event the end of a highly influential era in Apple's history.

**Apple Watch Series 9 Mistakenly Left Off watchOS 27 Compatibility List** ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/08/apple-watch-series-9-mistakenly-off-watchos-27-list/))
Following the WWDC keynote, Apple's official website initially caused panic by omitting the Apple Watch Series 9 from the watchOS 27 compatibility list. Apple later clarified this was an error, confirming the Series 9 is fully supported alongside the Ultra 2. However, watchOS 27 still represents the largest single-year cull in Apple Watch history, permanently dropping support for the Series 6, 7, 8, the original Apple Watch Ultra, and the second-generation SE.
