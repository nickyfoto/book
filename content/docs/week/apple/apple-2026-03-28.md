# Apple Releases iOS 26.4, Discontinues Mac Pro, and Sets WWDC 2026 Dates — 2026-03-28

## Highlights
Today's news is dominated by the release of iOS 26.4, which brings a slew of new features like the AI-driven Playlist Playground, while simultaneously marking the end of an era with the official discontinuation of the Intel-era Mac Pro. Meanwhile, developers can finally mark their calendars for WWDC 2026, and rumors are heating up about Apple's most significant iPhone redesigns in history. 

## Top Stories
- **iOS 26.4 Released to the Public**: Apple has officially rolled out iOS 26.4, delivering Apple Music's AI-generated Playlist Playground, video podcast enhancements with automatic bitrate adjustments, and individual payments for Family Sharing members. The update also addresses keyboard accuracy complaints and introduces eight new emoji. ([Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/3096159/ios-26-4-features-apple-music-emoji-podcasts-reminders.html))
- **Mac Pro Officially Discontinued**: Apple has quietly killed off the Mac Pro workstation, alongside its infamous $700 wheel kit. The tower had not been updated in three years, and its high-end professional role has effectively been usurped by the more affordable Mac Studio. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/28/top-stories-mac-pro-discontinued/))
- **WWDC 2026 Scheduled for June**: Apple's Worldwide Developers Conference will take place from June 8 to June 12 as a hybrid event. After a series of delays, the company has confirmed that significant AI advancements will be a major focal point of this year's keynote. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/28/top-stories-mac-pro-discontinued/))
- **Two New Apple Apps on the Horizon**: Apple is preparing to launch a consolidated 'Apple Business' app this April for streamlined device and employee management. Furthermore, the company is reportedly testing a dedicated Siri app boasting ChatGPT-like functionality, which is expected to ship alongside iOS 27 later this year. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/28/apple-releasing-two-all-new-iphone-apps/))
- **Historic iPhone Revamps in the Works**: Apple's hardware engineering chief John Ternus is reportedly overseeing a massive pipeline of iPhone redesigns. The upcoming roadmap includes a highly anticipated foldable model expected this year, followed by an edge-to-edge 20th-anniversary model slated for 2027. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/28/top-stories-mac-pro-discontinued/))
