# Apple Daily Digest — 2026-07-30

## Highlights
Today's news is dominated by Apple's historic Q3 2026 earnings report, which marked the strongest June quarter ever with $109.4 billion in revenue and Tim Cook's final earnings call before John Ternus takes the helm in September. Meanwhile, rumors are intensifying around the iPhone 18 Pro's upcoming September launch, and lawmakers are ramping up bipartisan pressure on Apple's supply chain decisions regarding Chinese memory chips.

## Top Stories
- **Apple Reports 3Q 2026 Results: $29.8B Profit on $109.4B Revenue**: Apple reported record-breaking June quarter revenue of $109.4 billion, driven by strong iPhone, Mac, and Services sales. This also marked CEO Tim Cook's final earnings call, where he warned of increasing supply constraints for the upcoming September quarter and confirmed his transition to executive chairman on September 1. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/30/apple-3q-2026-earnings/))
- **Apple faces bipartisan Senate pushback over plans to buy Chinese memory chips**: US senators are urging Apple to commit to not purchasing memory chips from Chinese suppliers CXMT and YMTC, citing national security concerns. The pressure comes as Apple attempts to navigate a "100-year flood" on memory pricing that recently forced the company to raise retail prices on Macs and iPads. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/29/apple-faces-bipartisan-senate-pushback-over-plans-to-buy-chinese-memory-chips/))
- **Apple Upgrade vs iPhone Upgrade Program: Here are the key differences**: Apple has launched a new leasing initiative called "Apple Upgrade," which officially replaces the discontinued iPhone Upgrade Program. Unlike its predecessor, the new program operates as a true lease without mandatory AppleCare and extends to Macs, iPads, and Apple Watches, though users do not own the device at the end of the term without a buyout fee. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/30/apple-upgrade-vs-iphone-upgrade-program-here-are-the-key-differences/))
- **iPhone 18 Pro: Twelve Changes Coming to Apple’s Next Flagship**: With the iPhone 18 Pro expected to debut next month, rumors point to twelve major changes, including a larger battery, a smaller Dynamic Island, and a 2nm A20 Pro chip featuring new Wafer-Level Multi-Chip Module (WMCM) packaging. The device may also introduce a variable aperture camera lens, resembling DSLR capabilities. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/30/reasons-to-wait-iphone-18-pro/))
- **Fortnite Returns to iPhone in Brazil as Epic Games Store Launches**: Fortnite has returned to the iPhone in Brazil via the launch of the Epic Games Store, utilizing Apple's mandated support for alternative app marketplaces. Epic Games continues to criticize Apple's 9-step installation process and the 5% Core Technology Commission, arguing these measures are designed to thwart competition. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/30/brazil-epic-games-store/))
