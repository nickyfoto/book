# WWDC 2026 Showcases Siri AI, Apple Foundation Models, and Cross-Platform Evolution — 2026-06-09

## Highlights
Apple's WWDC 2026 announcements center heavily on "Siri AI" and the third generation of Apple Foundation Models, marking a pivotal shift toward deep, system-level agentic AI integration. While the updates to iOS 27, macOS 27 Golden Gate, and visionOS 27 offer substantial quality-of-life improvements like a customizable "Liquid Glass" UI, it's the privacy-first, on-device intelligence that truly defines this year's software roadmap.

## Top Stories
- **[iOS 27 and iPadOS 27 Unveiled](https://www.macstories.net/news/ios-and-ipados-27-the-macstories-overview/)**: The new mobile operating systems bring the highly anticipated Siri AI, powerful new child safety tools, and an adjustable Liquid Glass interface. Notably, the update introduces natural language event creation in Calendar and AI-powered bill splitting in Apple Wallet. 
- **[macOS 27 Golden Gate Refines the Mac Experience](https://www.macstories.net/news/macos-27-golden-gate-the-macstories-overview/)**: Apple's newest macOS focuses on unifying the desktop experience with edge-to-edge sidebars, a consistent corner radius for windows, and an AI-infused Siri that resides directly within Spotlight. It also allows for iPhone Mirroring window resizing and introduces a highly requested pull-to-refresh gesture. 
- **[watchOS 27 Introduces Dynamic Interface Changes](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/08/watchos-27-features-dynamic-app-grid-new-and-more/)**: The Apple Watch gains a new dynamic app grid, a single-tap gesture for navigating the Smart Stack, and an untethered Workout Buddy that no longer requires a paired iPhone nearby. Apple has also completely unified its tracking tools into a single, map-centric Find My app. 
- **[visionOS 27 Enhances Immersive Computing](https://www.macstories.net/news/visionos-27-the-macstories-overview/)**: Siri AI integrates into spatial computing via a floating 3D orb, while a new feature allows users to turn standard panoramic photos into fully immersive Environments. Vision Pro users will also benefit from curved application windows and eye-aware notifications that expand simply by looking at them. 
- **[Siri AI Will Not Launch in the EU for Mobile Devices](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/09/eu-says-decision-not-to-launch-siri-ai-in-europe-is-apples/)**: The European Commission confirmed that Apple chose to withhold Siri AI from the EU market on iOS and iPadOS to avoid interoperability requirements under the Digital Markets Act. European users will, however, have access to Siri AI on the Mac, Apple Watch, and Vision Pro.

## Articles Worth Reading

**[The Third Generation of Apple’s Foundation Models and AFM Core Advanced](https://www.macstories.net/linked/the-third-generation-of-apples-foundation-models-and-afm-core-advanced/)** 
Apple has revealed fascinating architectural details about its new family of five foundation models. The standout is AFM 3 Core Advanced, a massive 20-billion-parameter on-device model utilizing a sparsely activated architecture. Impressively, Apple managed to fit this onto devices with 12GB of RAM by storing the model in flash memory (NAND) rather than forcing it entirely into active memory (DRAM), making routing decisions per prompt. This technological leap promises multimodal text, image, and audio capabilities natively on M-series devices and the upcoming iPhone 17 Pro.

**[Apple’s New AI Models Contain 'None' of Google’s Gemini Assistant](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/09/apples-new-ai-contains-no-gemini/)** 
Apple executives quickly clarified the exact nature of their collaboration with Google regarding Apple Intelligence. Craig Federighi emphasized that Apple’s Foundation Models use absolutely no Google client-side code, Gemini models, or Google Search infrastructure. Instead, Apple trained its models with proprietary data and refined them using distillation outputs from Gemini frontier models. For complex reasoning, Apple also securely extended its Private Cloud Compute to Nvidia GPUs hosted on Google’s cloud, utilizing "ambiguous confidential compute" to ensure user data cannot be accessed.

**[All the Liquid Glass Changes in macOS Golden Gate](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/09/macos-golden-gate-liquid-glass/)** 
Following significant user feedback and controversy regarding the Liquid Glass design introduced in macOS Tahoe, Apple is refining the aesthetic in Golden Gate. The update introduces a highly requested slider allowing users to adjust the UI's translucency from heavily frosted to clearer, improving overall text legibility. Additionally, Apple has tightened the corner radius of app windows, implemented edge-to-edge sidebars without unnecessary shadows, and restored color to sidebar icons, drastically improving the consistency and usability of the Mac interface.
