# Apple OS 26.4 Launch, Standalone Siri Leaks, and Apple Business — 2026-03-24

## Highlights
Today's news is dominated by a massive wave of software updates across the Apple ecosystem, headlined by the widespread release of iOS 26.4, macOS Tahoe 26.4, and iPadOS 26.4. Beyond the immediate software drops, rumors are swirling about a massive AI overhaul that will transition Siri into a standalone chatbot in iOS 27, and Apple has officially introduced "Apple Business" to centralize device management and usher in a new era of advertising on Apple Maps.

## Articles Worth Reading

**[iOS 26.4 Features: Everything New in iOS 26.4](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-26-4-features/)**
iOS 26.4 is a robust release that fundamentally shifts how users interact with several core apps. The update introduces "Playlist Playground," allowing users to generate customized Apple Music playlists utilizing generative text-based prompts. Podcast creators also gain significant new capabilities with HTTP Live Streaming (HLS) support for video podcasts, giving users the option to seamlessly toggle between watching and listening. For security, Stolen Device Protection is now enabled by default, ensuring enhanced protection by requiring biometric authentication for sensitive actions. The release also ships with WebKit updates in Safari that include a new Keyboard Lock API, answering a major request for web-based gaming and remote desktop developers.

**[iOS 26.4 Adds Two New Features to CarPlay](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/24/ios-26-4-adds-two-carplay-features/)**
With iOS 26.4, CarPlay receives notable dashboard upgrades, including a new Ambient Music widget that provides curated playlists for sleep, chill, and productivity without requiring an Apple Music subscription. Even more impactful is the new support for voice-based conversational chatbot apps, allowing developers of AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude to bring hands-free capabilities directly to the vehicle dashboard. This paves the way for a much more intelligent driving experience, teasing the potential of what Apple might deliver with its own rumored Siri chatbot in iOS 27.

**[WhatsApp working on Liquid Glass overhaul for its voice message player](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/24/whatsapp-working-on-liquid-glass-overhaul-for-its-voice-message-player/)**
Third-party developers are actively continuing to embrace Apple's evolving UI design language. The latest iOS beta for WhatsApp reveals an upcoming adoption of Apple's "Liquid Glass" visual style for its voice notes player. This floating, translucent interface will allow users to continue listening to voice messages seamlessly while navigating the app, replacing the current static top banner with a more native, iOS 26-aligned aesthetic.
