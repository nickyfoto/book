# Foldables, Radically Redesigned Watches, and Value Re-evaluations Take Center Stage — 2026-08-09

## Highlights
**Today’s Apple ecosystem highlights center on major leaks and future roadmaps that hint at a profound hardware evolution across both iOS and watchOS devices**. **From third-party accessory leaks of the upcoming foldable "iPhone Ultra" to Bloomberg’s revelations of a fundamental Apple Watch design overhaul, Apple is actively looking to revitalize its core product lineups**. **Concurrently, discussion around memory-shortage-induced price hikes and the historic longevity of standard flagship models highlights a shifting landscape for consumer purchasing decisions**.

## Top Stories
- **Apple Explores Radical Apple Watch Rethink, Circular Displays, and a Premium Ceramic Return**: **Apple is actively re-evaluating the Apple Watch lineup to counter growing competition from screenless smart fitness trackers and smart rings like the Oura Ring**. **The industrial design team is considering display-free models, new sizes, and even circular screens, alongside the return of a highly anticipated premium ceramic casing option for the Series 12 as soon as this year**. **This major overhaul marks a potential departure from the rectangular form factor used since the wearable's inception, although any radical screen redesigns are likely several years away from shipping**. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/09/apple-watch-rethink/))
- **Foldable 'iPhone Ultra 3' Display Upgrades Already Highlighted in Long-Term Roadmap**: **Even before Apple officially announces its first-ever foldable smartphone next month, the company is reportedly mapping out a multi-generation roadmap extending to the "iPhone Ultra 3" for release as early as 2028**. **Bloomberg reports that while the second-generation foldable in 2027 will likely reuse the original model's screen, the third-generation version is slated to feature slightly larger inner and cover displays**. **The first-generation foldable iPhone is expected to launch with a 7.8-inch inner display, a 5.5-inch cover screen, the A20 chip, and a price tag starting around or above \$2,000**. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/09/iphone-ultra-3-rumored-to-feature-larger-displays/))
- **iPhone 17 Projecting to Shatter a 15-Year-Old Flagship Longevity Record**: **Since the next-generation iPhone 18 is not expected to debut until spring 2027, the current iPhone 17 is on track to spend approximately 18 months (~550 days) as Apple's top mainline flagship**. **This extended cycle will surpass the previous record of 15.7 months (477 days) set by the iPhone 4 between 2010 and 2011**. **While standard flagship replacements face a long stretch, Apple's Pro lineup will continue on an annual release cycle alongside next month's foldable iPhone Ultra launch**. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/09/iphone-17-availability/))
- **Global Memory Crisis Price Hikes Pose Value Challenges for Apple Hardware**: **In the wake of steep price hikes earlier this summer that saw the iPad Air jump to \$749 and the M5 MacBook Pro rise to \$1,999, questions are surfacing about whether Apple can sell existing designs without upgrading their core specifications**. **Analysts argue that cheap yet meaningful additions, such as 120Hz displays on the iPad Air or miniLED screens on the iMac, could help justify the increased costs to hesitant consumers**. **Without such updates, buyers may increasingly look toward the used market or delay upgrading altogether**. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/09/apple-price-hikes-better-value-and-more-features/))
- **Scrapped Apple Upgrade White-Glove Feature Would Have Shipped Pre-Loaded iPhones**: **Years ago, Apple contemplated a "white-glove" setup feature for its Apple Upgrade Program that would deliver a user's annual phone replacement pre-loaded with their data via an iCloud backup**. **Although this would make the upgrading process nearly seamless, the feature never shipped due to obvious privacy concerns regarding data transfer, alongside logistical issues like slightly outdated backup data at the time of delivery**. **However, Apple's recently introduced "Presto" system, which updates in-box iPhones wirelessly, demonstrates how in-store technology has evolved to handle seamless retail updates**. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/09/this-scrapped-apple-upgrade-feature-wouldve-made-setup-much-more-seamless/))
