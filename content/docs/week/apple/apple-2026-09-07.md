# Transitions, Leaks, and Triumphs: Apple Prepares for a Landmark Keynote — 2026-09-07

## Highlights
With Apple’s highly anticipated **"Surprise and Shine" event only two days away**, the ecosystem is buzzing with dramatic leadership transitions, strategic services expansion, and major hardware leaks. **John Ternus’s formal debut as CEO** is accompanied by aggressive moves to monetize the App Store, prompting longtime executive Phil Schiller to step aside. Meanwhile, deep beta code leaks have peeled back the curtain on upcoming Apple Watch wellness features and **professional-tier iPhone camera controls**.

## Top Stories
- **John Ternus Takes the Spotlight as Tim Cook Opts Out of September Keynote**: Following his transition to Executive Chairman on September 1, **Tim Cook will not feature in the upcoming September 9 event video**. Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reports that Apple carefully orchestrated this move to **establish newly appointed CEO John Ternus as the sole face** of the foldable iPhone and future hardware. While Cook will still attend the screening in person, excluding him from the presentation prevents his presence from undermining the transition. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/07/tim-cook-wont-appear-apple-sept-9-event-video/))
- **App Store Monetization Push Sparks Executive Shake-up and Phil Schiller's Exit**: Apple is exploring aggressive new ways to boost App Store margins and increase recurring Services revenue, which has recently seen growth slow down and **U.S. commission revenue drop 18% since the start of 2026**. This strategic drive, spearheaded by new CEO John Ternus and services chief Eddy Cue, reportedly led to **Apple Fellow Phil Schiller stepping down** from running the platform. Schiller reportedly opposed the moves, fearing they would create an antagonistic relationship with developers and further irk global governments already investigating Apple's market dominance. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/07/apple-squeeze-more-revenue-from-app-store/))
- **Beta Code Leaks Uncover Major Hardware and Health Features for Apple Watch Series 12**: WatchOS 27 and iOS 27 beta code findings from forum member "pdfu" have revealed **four major developments for the Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4**. The leak reveals an all-new standalone **"Readiness" app for recovery metrics**, richer data infrastructure for strength workouts, and a new "Sunstone4p0" optical sensor for more reliable heart tracking in low-signal scenarios. Additionally, code tied to the T8320 processor suggests **NAND storage will be doubled to 128GB** from previous models. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/07/leak-hints-at-four-new-apple-watch-features/))
- **Upcoming iOS 27 Camera Code Points to Professional-Grade Manual Controls**: Hidden iOS 27 beta code has exposed five advanced Camera app features, including exposure scopes (waveforms and histograms), **focus peaking, highlight warnings, and repeated self-timer shots**. The code also details an **aperture-based image correction system** designed to smoothly adjust lens corrections depending on the active aperture setting. This finding strongly aligns with expectations that the upcoming **iPhone 18 Pro Max will feature a variable aperture main camera**. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/07/five-iphone-18-pro-camera-features-seemingly-leak/))
- **Xiaomi Steals a March with "18 Fold" Wide Foldable Launch Ahead of Apple's Event**: Xiaomi has officially announced its first wide-format foldable smartphone, the 18 Fold, just two days before Apple’s expected foldable iPhone debut. The 18 Fold features a wide-folding book-style design with Leica-developed cameras and **Xiaomi’s in-house 3nm Xring O3 chip**. Priced starting at approximately \$1,638, the device sets an **aggressive competitive baseline** as Apple prepares to enter the foldable market with a highly anticipated device expected to exceed \$2,000. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/07/xiaomi-announces-foldable-iphone-rival/))
- **Apple TV+ Strongholds Comedy Dominance with 20 Creative Arts Emmy Wins**: Apple TV+ has dominated the Creative Arts Emmy Awards with a **record-setting 20 wins**, placing it ahead of major competitors HBO Max (16 wins) and Netflix (13 wins). A substantial part of this success belongs to the breakout comedy **"Widows Bay," which earned 8 wins** and is positioned for a sweep of the comedy categories. Other winning Apple titles include *Pluribus* (4 wins), *Margo's Got Money Troubles* (3 wins), *Palm Royale* (3 wins), and *Mr. Scorsese* (1 win). ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/07/apple-tv-leads-emmys-awards-widows-bay/))

## Articles Worth Reading

**A Foldable Mac Is Years Away, but Apple Is Still Working on It** ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/07/a-foldable-mac-is-years-away-but-apple-is-still-working-on-it/))
This article offers a compelling look at the long-term future of folding Macs and the **eventual shift from physical to soft keyboards**. The author argues that while Apple's immediate hybrid folding Mac/iPad plans may have been put on the back burner, an all-screen foldable is an eventual certainty as display and interface technologies mature. The piece highlights key Apple patents designed to solve the discomfort of glass keyboards, including **flexible displays that deform under finger pressure**, haptic engines for key-press feedback, and **electrostatic charges that simulate physical key textures** on a flat screen. It is a highly engaging read for enthusiasts interested in how Apple plans to bridge the gap between virtual touch interfaces and physical typing comfort.

**Apple Invites Developers to Submit Browser Interoperability Ideas for Interop 2027** ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/07/apple-invites-developers-to-submit-browser-interoperability-ideas-for-interop-2027/))
Apple's WebKit team has formally invited the web development community to submit proposals for Interop 2027, the collaborative multi-browser initiative aimed at **standardizing web technologies across different browsers**. Running annually since 2022 alongside Google, Microsoft, Mozilla, and others, the project focuses on fixing specific interoperability pain points. This article details the guidelines for a successful proposal, highlighting that submissions must be **specific, impactful, stable, and covered by automated Web Platform Tests**. It is an essential read for web developers looking to understand the standardization process and directly influence the features prioritized by major browser engines.

**Intego ONE Now Protects Your iPhone Too – and There’s 50% Off in September** ([Source](https://www.macworld.com/article/3227428/intego-one-iphone-app.html))
Macworld reviews Intego's expansion of its security suite to iOS, providing an educational breakdown of how iPhone security operates. The review stresses that because of **iOS's tight sandboxing architecture, this is not a traditional antivirus scanner** but rather a network and configuration audit tool. The app adds VPN protection for insecure public Wi-Fi, connection security checks, and **device setting audits to flag missing passcodes or jailbreak modifications**. It is worth reading for Apple enthusiasts seeking a realistic understanding of iPhone vulnerability vectors and how companion apps can help secure network connections.
