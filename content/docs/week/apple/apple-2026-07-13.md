# Apple Daily Digest: OS 27 Public Betas Arrive as Apple Sues OpenAI — 2026-07-13

## Highlights
Today's news is dominated by the massive rollout of Apple's OS 27 public betas, officially bringing the highly anticipated Siri AI to eager iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch users. Behind the scenes, the ecosystem is shaken by intense industry competition, highlighted by Apple capturing a record 20% global smartphone share despite market downturns, and a blockbuster trade secret lawsuit filed against OpenAI over hardware ambitions. 

## Top Stories
- **[iOS 27 and iPadOS 27 Now Available to Public Beta Testers](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/13/apple-seeds-ios-27-public-beta-1/)**: Apple has officially launched its public beta program for the OS 27 generation, granting users early access to iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27 Golden Gate, watchOS 27, and visionOS 27. These major updates bring the overhauled, conversational Siri AI to compatible devices, alongside Liquid Glass design refinements and significant under-the-hood performance enhancements. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/13/apple-seeds-ios-27-public-beta-1/))
- **[Report: Apple’s OpenAI Lawsuit Threatens iPhone Rival Plans](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/13/openai-lawsuit-threatens-plans/)**: Apple is taking aggressive legal action against OpenAI, alleging trade secret theft tied to the AI company's consumer hardware ambitions, which are being developed in partnership with former Apple design chief Jony Ive's io Products. The lawsuit reveals that over 400 former Apple employees now work at OpenAI, and the ongoing litigation is already reportedly slowing OpenAI's recruitment and hardware roadmap. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/13/openai-lawsuit-threatens-plans/))
- **[Apple’s Steady iPhone Pricing Pays Off as Rivals Scramble](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/13/apples-steady-iphone-pricing-pays-off/)**: Despite an ongoing memory chip shortage that caused a 4% year-over-year decline in the overall global smartphone market, Apple captured a record 20% share in the second quarter. This impressive performance is largely attributed to a robust iPhone 17 upgrade cycle and Apple's strategy to keep iPhone pricing steady while its competitors were forced to raise theirs. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/13/apples-steady-iphone-pricing-pays-off/))
- **[Touchscreen MacBook Pro Rumours: Release date, OLED, specs and latest leaks](https://www.macworld.com/article/2931833/touchscreen-macbook-pro-m6-design-processor-specs-release.html)**: Apple's long-rumored touchscreen MacBook Pro is facing potential delays, with supply chain constraints pushing the expected launch from late 2026 to early 2027. The radically redesigned laptop is anticipated to feature a tandem OLED display, a hole-punch or Dynamic Island camera cutout, and will bypass the M6 chip generation entirely to utilize M5 Pro and M5 Max chips instead. ([Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/2931833/touchscreen-macbook-pro-m6-design-processor-specs-release.html))
- **[Report: Apple Agreed to Intel Chips Amid White House Tariff Talks](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/13/apple-agreed-intel-chips-trump-tariff-talks/)**: Apple faced heavy pressure from the Trump administration to utilize Intel's fabrication plants as a condition for receiving semiconductor tariff exemptions. The unprecedented arrangement will reportedly see Intel manufacturing select chips for future Macs and iPhones, despite recent financial and production struggles in Intel's foundry business. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/13/apple-agreed-intel-chips-trump-tariff-talks/))
