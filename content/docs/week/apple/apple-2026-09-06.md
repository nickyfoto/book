# Apple Ecosystem Strategic Shifts, Hardware Rumors, and Software Milestones — 2026-09-06

## Highlights
Today’s Apple ecosystem landscape is defined by major strategic crosscurrents: potential monetization shakeups in the App Store that are already causing executive shuffles, alongside a highly complex and premium future for the Mac and iPhone lineups. While Apple is pushing forward with ambitious 20th anniversary iPhone and OLED 'MacBook Ultra' designs, supply-chain realities and development scrap heaps remind us of the physical constraints of folding displays. Simultaneously, the broader ecosystem is celebrating important software milestones and utility deals, offering developers and power users exceptional value.

## Top Stories
- **App Store Revenue Changes Considered as Phil Schiller Steps Aside**: Apple is reportedly exploring strategic changes to the App Store to raise profit margins and secure additional recurring revenue under CEO John Ternus and services SVP Eddy Cue. This monetization push reportedly led to the departure of longtime Apple Fellow Phil Schiller from his role overseeing the App Store, as he feared such aggressive moves would further alienate developers and government regulators. Potential proposals include automating the manual app review process to cut costs or restructuring developer fees, such as charging premium rates for developers or implementing traffic-based subscriptions for large apps. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/06/apple-might-soon-make-app-store-changes-to-raise-revenue-increase-margins-report/))
- **Omdia Report Hints at Steep Price Premium for Redesigned 'MacBook Ultra'**: According to a new Omdia report, Apple will keep its current miniLED display panels in production through 2028, signaling that the upcoming OLED MacBook Pro redesign will likely debut as a premium 'MacBook Ultra' with a significant price premium. While older miniLED models are expected to stay in the lineup to maintain a lower entry price around \$2,499, the new OLED-equipped MacBook Ultra models could start as high as \$2,999. The premium MacBook Ultra is rumored to introduce an OLED touchscreen, a thinner chassis, and a Dynamic Island to the Mac lineup. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/05/macbook-ultra-pricing-lineup-rumor/))
- **Apple Reportedly Abandons Foldable MacBook and Large OLED Laptop Plans**: In a major shift in its display roadmap, Apple has scrapped plans for a larger OLED MacBook Pro and has abandoned the development of its rumored foldable MacBook. The foldable MacBook was expected to feature a massive 16 to 18-inch screen when fully unfolded. Despite these high-end project cancellations, Apple remains committed to its long-term strategy of transitioning its standard MacBook lineup to OLED displays over time. ([iLounge](https://www.ilounge.com/news/mac/macbook-pro/plans-for-new-macbook-models-scrapped))
- **Upcoming 20th Anniversary iPhone Redesign Rumored for Next Year**: As the iPhone approaches its 20th anniversary next year, Apple is rumored to be developing a major hardware redesign. The milestone device is expected to feature a curved glass design, representing the most significant visual and structural change since the launch of the iPhone X. This milestone anniversary comes two decades after Steve Jobs first introduced the revolutionary original iPhone back in 2007. ([iLounge](https://www.ilounge.com/news/iphone/upcoming-20th-anniversary-iphone-could-be-the-next-big-design-since-the-iphone-x))
- **Foldable iPhone Production Restricted by Assembly and Quality Constraints**: Apple's highly anticipated foldable iPhone is reportedly facing severe assembly challenges, limiting current prototype production to just a couple hundred units per day. These tight constraints are driven by Apple's strict control and high-quality standards. As a result of these manufacturing hurdles, consumer supply is expected to be extremely limited when the device eventually launches. ([iLounge](https://www.ilounge.com/news/iphone/foldable-iphone-supply-may-be-limited))
- **iPadOS 27 Multitasking Critiqued for Forcing Complex Windowing Systems**: While iPadOS 26 introduced a powerful new windowing system, critics note that iPadOS 27 continues to frustrate casual users by eliminating simple multitasking toggles. Classic features like Split View and Slide Over are now buried within the complex windowing layer, making layout alignment annoying and prone to accidental triggers. Commentators suggest that Apple should introduce distinct 'Classic' and 'Pro' multitasking modes to allow casual users to easily use simple split-screen layouts without Stage Manager's overhead. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/05/ipados-27-still-needs-simpler-multitasking-heres-what-id-like-to-see/))
