# Apple Daily Digest: AI Accessibility, Leadership Shakeups, and Fortnite's Return — 2026-05-19

## Highlights
Today’s news is dominated by Apple's proactive integration of Apple Intelligence into its accessibility suite, offering profound quality-of-life updates like eye-tracking for wheelchairs and system-wide generated subtitles. Behind the scenes, a major hardware leadership reorganization aims to accelerate Apple's product pipeline, while Epic Games reignites its feud with Apple by bringing *Fortnite* back to the global App Store.

## Top Stories
- **[Apple Previews AI-Powered Accessibility Features](https://www.macstories.net/news/apple-intelligence-infused-accessibility-features-promise-greater-flexibility-and-power/)**: In advance of Global Accessibility Awareness Day, Apple showcased upcoming features like natural language for Voice Control, more descriptive VoiceOver, and eye-controlled wheelchair navigation for Vision Pro. These tools lean heavily on the on-device processing of Apple Intelligence.
- **[Hardware Leadership Shakeup Under Johny Srouji](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/19/apple-shaking-up-product-design/)**: Chief Hardware Officer Johny Srouji is reorganizing product design teams to better align in-house silicon development with product creation. The move transfers product design oversight to Shelly Goldberg and Dave Pakula, aiming to accelerate the launch of future devices.
- **[Fortnite Returns to iOS Globally](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/19/fortnite-returns-to-app-store-worldwide/)**: Epic Games has reinstated *Fortnite* to the App Store worldwide, excluding Australia. CEO Tim Sweeney indicated this move is the start of a "final battle" against Apple's commission fees, anticipating that global regulators will soon force Apple to drop the so-called "Apple Tax".
- **[Google I/O Previews AI Competition](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/19/google-io-2026-roundup/)**: Google's I/O event unveiled Gemini 3.5 Flash, new AI search capabilities, and teased upcoming Android XR audio glasses. The announcements offer a strong look at the AI and hardware competition Apple will face as it heads into WWDC next month.
- **[Apple Acquires Avatar Startup Animato](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/19/apple-acquires-animato/)**: Apple has quietly secured a talent and IP deal with Animato, a startup known for creating virtual avatars for video chats and AI tutoring. The move aligns with Apple's ongoing investments in digital avatar technology like Memoji and Vision Pro Personas.
- **[Apple Retail Turns 25](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/19/first-apple-stores-opened-25-years-ago/)**: Apple is celebrating a quarter century of its brick-and-mortar retail operations, which originally launched on May 19, 2001, in Virginia and California. Despite initial skepticism, the stores have grown into a massive global success, generating some of the highest revenue per square foot in the industry.
