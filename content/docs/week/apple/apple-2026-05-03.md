# Apple Daily Digest: Smart Glasses, Ultra Audio, and Hardware Shifts — 2026-05-03

## Highlights
Today's news highlights Apple's aggressive push into next-generation wearables and AI, countered by strategic retreats in struggling hardware categories. While rumors heat up around AI-powered smart glasses and camera-equipped "AirPods Ultra," disappointing sales figures have reportedly caused Apple to reconsider its ultra-thin smartphone and premium iPad strategies. Simultaneously, iOS 27 promises deeper Siri integration directly into the native Camera app, signaling that Apple Intelligence will increasingly become a core component of everyday user experiences. 

## Top Stories
- **Apple's AI Smart Glasses Targeting Meta Ray-Bans**: Apple is developing a pair of AI-driven smart glasses designed to compete directly with Meta's offerings. The upcoming wearable will feature dual camera modules: a high-resolution lens for capturing photos and video, and a secondary, lower-resolution camera dedicated entirely to interpreting hand-gesture-based inputs. ([iLounge](https://www.ilounge.com/news/apple/smart-glasses-from-apple-to-integrate-hand-gesture-recognition))

- **'AirPods Ultra' Rumored to Include Cameras**: A new, higher-tier addition to Apple's audio lineup, tentatively branded "AirPods Ultra," is reportedly in development. Positioned above the current AirPods Pro 3 (which retail for $249), this new model will command a higher price tag and notably integrate built-in cameras, pushing Apple's wearable ecosystem into new ambient sensing territories. ([iLounge](https://www.ilounge.com/news/airpods-pro/refreshed-airpods-to-be-above-the-airpods-pro-line))

- **Poor iPhone Air Sales Halt Industry Ultra-Thin Plans**: The ultra-thin smartphone trend may be ending before it truly begins, as Apple has struggled to move units of the iPhone Air, seeing just over 700,000 unit activations. This underwhelming market response might lead to the cancellation of the iPhone Air 2, and the ripple effect has reportedly caused competing brands to scrap their own ultra-thin smartphone designs. ([iLounge](https://www.ilounge.com/news/iphone/competition-abandoning-ultra-thin-smartphone-plans-due-to-poor-sales-for-iphone-air))

- **iOS 27 to Bring Visual Intelligence to Camera App**: With the upcoming release of iOS 27, Apple is expanding Apple Intelligence deeper into its native applications. The Camera app will receive a dedicated Siri mode that works across photo, video, portrait, and panorama settings, leveraging Visual Intelligence to enhance accessibility and environmental understanding. ([iLounge](https://www.ilounge.com/news/apple/camera-app-getting-new-features-with-revamped-siri-in-ios-27))

- **iPad Ultra Canceled and M-Series Strategy Shifts**: Following years of softer-than-expected sales for the iPad Pro—including recent shipment cuts for the M4 model—Apple has reportedly discarded its plans to release a massive "iPad Ultra". Furthermore, the launch of the highly anticipated iPad 12 featuring Apple Intelligence has been pushed back by several months due to tough year-over-year revenue comparisons for the tablet division. ([iLounge](https://www.ilounge.com/news/ipad/ipad-ultra-plans-potentially-scrapped))

- **Apple Dominates Global Satellite Smartphone Market**: Apple maintains a commanding lead as the top manufacturer of satellite-capable smartphones globally, a trend it kickstarted by partnering with Globalstar for the iPhone 14's Emergency SOS feature. Market projections estimate that satellite-capable devices will account for roughly 46% of all global smartphone shipments by the year 2030. ([iLounge](https://www.ilounge.com/news/apple/apple-ahead-in-satellite-smartphone-market))
