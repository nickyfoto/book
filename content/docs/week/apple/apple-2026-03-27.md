# Apple Discontinues the Mac Pro, Unveils WWDC 2026 Dates, and Disrupts the PC Market — 2026-03-27

## Highlights
Today's news is dominated by the end of an era as Apple officially discontinues the Mac Pro, marking a complete shift toward the Mac Studio for high-end professional workflows. Concurrently, Apple announced the dates for WWDC 2026, where we expect a massive, AI-focused overhaul to Siri in iOS 27. Meanwhile, the newly launched $599 MacBook Neo is already causing shockwaves across the PC industry due to its impressive A18 Pro chip performance, aggressive pricing strategy, and unparalleled vertical integration.

## Top Stories
- **Mac Pro Officially Discontinued**: Apple has confirmed the demise of the Mac Pro, removing it from its online store just days ahead of the company's 50th anniversary. The shift signifies that the Mac Studio will serve as Apple's flagship high-end desktop moving forward, as Apple Silicon's efficiency has made the tower's traditional modularity less relevant, though refurbished Mac Pro models currently remain available. ([Source](https://www.macworld.com/article/3100286/the-mac-pro-died-so-apple-silicon-could-live.html))
- **WWDC 2026 Dates Announced**: The 37th Worldwide Developers Conference is scheduled for June 8-12, heavily spotlighting artificial intelligence advancements. A completely revamped, chatbot-style Siri is anticipated in iOS 27, supported by Apple's recent hiring of former Google executive Lilian Rincon to lead AI product marketing. ([Source](https://www.ilounge.com/news/apple/2026-wwdc-date-announced))
- **Critical Security Alerts for Older iOS Versions**: Apple is actively pushing Lock Screen notifications to users running iOS 17 and earlier, warning them of active web-based attacks utilizing "Coruna" and "DarkSword" exploit kits. Concurrently, Apple confirmed on the record that no iPhone using the extreme-security Lockdown Mode has ever been compromised by mercenary spyware. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/27/critical-security-alerts-sent-to-ios-17-iphones/))
- **iPhone 18 to Standardize Smaller Dynamic Island**: Leaks indicate that the entire iPhone 18 series will feature the reduced-size Dynamic Island currently rumored for the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro models. The regular iPhone 18 models are reportedly moving to an early-2027 release cycle, diverging from the fall launch of the Pro and foldable models. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/27/iphone-18-same-bezels-but-smaller-dynamic-island/))
- **iOS 26.4 Introduces Major App Upgrades**: The latest iOS 26.4 release brings an AI-powered Playlist Playground and a new full-screen UI to Apple Music, alongside a significant video overhaul to Apple Podcasts featuring Picture-in-Picture support and a dedicated video hub. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/27/apple-music-in-ios-26-4-five-brand-new-features-available-now/))
- **Apple's 50th Anniversary Global Celebrations**: As Apple approaches its 50th birthday on April 1, celebrations are continuing globally with special Today at Apple sessions in Washington D.C., Mexico City, Shanghai, and Tokyo. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/27/apple-50th-anniversary-washington-dc-tokyo/))
