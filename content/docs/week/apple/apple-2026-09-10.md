# Apple Unveils the Foldable Era: iPhone Duo, iPhone 18 Pro, and Apple Watch Series 12 Dominate Post-Event News — 2026-09-10

## Highlights
Apple’s "Surprise and Shine" keynote aftermath has dominated today's ecosystem news, led by the debut of Apple's first foldable smartphone, the **iPhone Duo**, alongside the 2nm A20 Pro-powered **iPhone 18 Pro** series. The hardware lineup expanded significantly with the **Apple Watch Series 12** and **Ultra 4** featuring an upgraded Health Sensing System, as well as the **AirPods 5** introducing Active Noise Cancellation as a standard feature. On the software front, Apple released **Release Candidate** builds for iOS 27, macOS Golden Gate, watchOS 27, and visionOS 27, while officially opening App Store submissions for developers ahead of next week's public rollouts.

## Top Stories
- **[Apple Unveils Foldable iPhone Duo with 7.6-Inch Display and A20 Pro](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/09/apple-announces-foldable-iphone-duo/)**: Apple has officially introduced its first foldable smartphone, featuring a 7.6-inch nano-texture inner display, a 5.4-inch outer screen, and an IP68 dust-and-water resistance rating. Powered by the 2nm A20 Pro chip, the device introduces custom iOS 27 multitasking with side-dock controls, an under-display FaceTime camera, and a starting price of \$1,999. Pre-orders open October 16 ahead of an October 23 release date. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/09/apple-announces-foldable-iphone-duo/))
- **[iPhone 18 Pro Series Debuts A20 Pro Chip and 4-Stop Variable Aperture](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/09/apple-announces-iphone-18-pro-and-iphone-18-pro-max/)**: Apple unveiled the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max, built on TSMC's 2nm A20 Pro processor and featuring a 48MP Fusion camera with a six-blade variable aperture ranging from f/1.48 to f/4.0. The new flagship devices include a 3x larger vapor chamber cooling system, manual Pro controls in the stock Camera app, a smaller Dynamic Island supporting three Live Activities, and up to 45 hours of battery life on the Pro Max. Pre-orders begin September 12 starting at \$1,199. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/09/apple-announces-iphone-18-pro-and-iphone-18-pro-max/))
- **[Apple Watch Series 12 & Ultra 4 Feature S11 Chip, 4GB RAM, and Readiness Scores](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/09/apple-watch-series-12-announced-all-day-hr-sensor/)**: The Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 introduce a redesigned Health Sensing System that tracks heart rate every five seconds and measures HRV every five minutes. Powered by the new S11 chip paired with a surprise upgrade to 4GB of RAM, the watches enable daytime Readiness scores and ambient Audio Intelligence features like Live Rewind and Siri Recap. Starting prices remain at \$399 for Series 12 and \$799 for Ultra 4 ahead of a September 18 launch. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/09/apple-watch-series-12-announced-all-day-hr-sensor/))
- **[AirPods 5 Bring Active Noise Cancellation as Standard Equipment](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/09/apple-unveils-airpods-5-with-upgraded-noise-cancellation-more/)**: Apple updated its entry-level earbud lineup with AirPods 5, adding Active Noise Cancellation across both the standard \$129 model and the \$149 version with a Wireless Charging Case. The updated acoustic architecture delivers up to 50% better noise reduction compared to previous models alongside enhanced Spatial Audio. Pre-orders are live now with official retail availability set for September 18. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/09/apple-unveils-airpods-5-with-upgraded-noise-cancellation-more/))
- **[iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate Release Candidates Available as App Submissions Open](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=k1mtkt1k)**: Apple seeded Release Candidate builds for iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27 Golden Gate, watchOS 27, tvOS 27, and visionOS 27 following yesterday's event. Apple officially confirmed that all major OS updates will launch publicly on Monday, September 14, and opened the App Store Connect submission pipeline for Xcode 27-built applications. Crucially, macOS Golden Gate marks the final release supporting Intel Mac applications via Rosetta, as Apple shifts to Apple silicon-only targets. ([Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=k1mtkt1k))
