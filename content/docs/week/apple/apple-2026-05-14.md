# Apple Daily Digest: Intel Silicon Production, OpenAI Tensions, and macOS 26.5 Security — 2026-05-14

## Highlights
Today’s news cycle is dominated by significant shifts in Apple's partnerships, most notably reports of Intel taking on chip manufacturing for older devices and rising legal tensions with OpenAI over Siri integration. Meanwhile, macOS 26.5 delivers critical security patches against a new Anthropic AI-discovered exploit, alongside exciting hardware rumors detailing the camera and modem upgrades coming to the iPhone 18 Pro.

## Top Stories
- **OpenAI Contemplates Legal Action Over Apple Partnership**: OpenAI is reportedly considering legal action against Apple, claiming the tech giant has failed to properly promote and integrate ChatGPT within iOS as initially agreed. OpenAI executives feel burned by the "strained" relationship, citing poor subscription conversions and a lack of transparency regarding Apple's Siri implementation. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/14/openai-considering-legal-action-against-apple/))
- **Intel Begins Manufacturing Apple Silicon**: Analyst Ming-Chi Kuo reports that Intel has officially started small-scale production of lower-end, legacy chips for the iPhone, iPad, and Mac. While TSMC will still produce over 90% of Apple's silicon, utilizing Intel's 18A process for older devices helps Apple diversify its supply chain and boost US-based manufacturing. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/14/kuo-intel-iphone-ipad-mac-chips/))
- **macOS 26.5 Patches Exploits Discovered by AI**: Apple released macOS 26.5, which includes crucial fixes for a memory corruption vulnerability uncovered by researchers using Anthropic's new Mythos Preview AI model. The update also adds a convenient new setting to automatically turn on desktop Macs when power is connected and resolves several enterprise-level bugs. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/14/new-macos-vulnerabilities-were-exposed-by-anthropics-mythos-report/))
- **Major Camera and Modem Upgrades for iPhone 18 Pro**: The upcoming iPhone 18 Pro is slated to feature a variable aperture on its main camera, a wider telephoto aperture, and a redesigned Camera Control button. It will also debut Apple's new, more efficient C2 modem, which includes a unique privacy feature to limit precise location data shared with carriers. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/14/iphone-18-pro-three-new-features-could-make-you-want-to-upgrade/))
- **Spotify Adopts Apple's Video Podcast Technology**: Spotify announced it will support Apple's HTTP Live Streaming (HLS) technology for video podcasts later this year. This integration will allow creators to easily distribute and monetize video shows across both platforms without altering their existing publishing workflows. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/14/spotify-to-adopt-apples-tech-for-video-podcasts/))

**Apple Card’s transition to Chase: Here’s what’s not changing (and might be)** ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/14/apple-cards-transition-to-chase-heres-whats-not-changing-and-might-be/))
As Apple Card officially transitions from Goldman Sachs to Chase, Apple has confirmed that core benefits like no fees and the 3% Daily Cash rewards structure will remain completely intact. The card will also continue using the Mastercard network. However, Apple left the door open regarding whether card numbers, physical card designs, or the high-yield Savings Account will undergo changes during the transition. It is also somewhat ambiguous whether the popular 0% interest Apple Card Monthly Installments for purchasing Apple products will survive the move.
