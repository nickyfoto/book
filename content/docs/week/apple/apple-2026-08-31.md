# Executive Changing of the Guard and Fall Event Revelations — 2026-08-31

## Highlights
Today marks a historic turning point in Cupertino as **Tim Cook concludes his 15-year tenure as Apple CEO**, handing the reins to hardware engineering chief **John Ternus** while **Phil Schiller** simultaneously scales back his oversight of the App Store and product keynotes. Ahead of the September 9 "Surprise and Shine" event, Apple has accelerated its software release cycle with **Beta 8 across all major operating system platforms**, even as new leaks crystallize expectations for the **iPhone 18 Pro**, foldable **iPhone Ultra**, and **Apple Watch Series 12**. Meanwhile, intense legal and hardware developments add intrigue to the transition, highlighted by **damaging forensic evidence submitted in Apple's trade secret lawsuit against OpenAI** and a quiet specification revision for the **M5 Ultra Mac Studio**.

## Top Stories
- **[Tim Cook Concludes 15-Year Run as Apple CEO as John Ternus Takes Reins](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/31/tim-cook-steps-down-as-apple-ceo-tomorrow/)**: Today is officially Tim Cook's final day serving as Apple CEO after fifteen years at the helm, with hardware engineering chief John Ternus scheduled to assume the leadership role tomorrow. Cook issued an emotional farewell memo to staff celebrating Apple's unique culture and expressing unwavering confidence in Ternus, while confirming he will remain active as executive chairman of the board of directors. The historic succession arrives with Apple posting all-time revenue records, capping an era that transformed Apple into a multi-trillion-dollar titan. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/31/tim-cook-steps-down-as-apple-ceo-tomorrow/))
- **[Phil Schiller Steps Down from Managing App Store and Product Events](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/31/phil-schiller-stepping-down-app-store/)**: Veteran executive Phil Schiller is relinquishing control of the App Store and product launches in what colleagues describe as another deliberate step toward full retirement. App Store oversight transitions to services chief Eddy Cue—with long-time executive Carson Oliver managing day-to-day operations—while Schiller’s deputy Nola Weinstein takes charge of the events team. Schiller will retain his title as an Apple Fellow and advise on select initiatives after guiding the App Store through years of antitrust disputes and regulatory mandates worldwide. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/31/phil-schiller-stepping-down-app-store/))
- **[Apple Files 'Shocking' Forensic Laptop Evidence in OpenAI Trade Secret Suit](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/31/apple-openai-forensic-macbook-evidence/)**: Apple submitted new forensic filings in federal court after inspecting a MacBook returned by former senior electrical engineer Chang Liu, seeking an expedited discovery process against OpenAI. The forensic inspection revealed that Liu downloaded proprietary Apple circuit schematics after his departure, conducted LTspice simulations for OpenAI power conversion projects, and trained an AI agent to execute those workflows. Apple also uncovered messages instructing an OpenAI colleague to delete evidence once an internal probe began, arguing these findings demonstrate irreversible trade secret misappropriation. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/31/apple-openai-forensic-macbook-evidence/))
- **[Apple Seeds Beta 8 Across All Platforms as Siri AI Reaches Final Polish](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/31/apple-seeds-ios-27-beta-8/)**: Apple released the eighth developer betas and sixth public betas for iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27 Golden Gate, watchOS 27, tvOS 27, and visionOS 27. Software testing is now centered strictly on bug squashing, performance optimizations, and final refinements for the rebuilt chatbot-style Siri AI assistant. These builds represent near-final candidate software, clearing the runway for Release Candidate builds immediately following next week's keynote. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/31/apple-seeds-ios-27-beta-8/))
- **[iPhone 18 Pro Upgrades and Foldable iPhone Ultra Details Solidify Ahead of Keynote](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/31/iphone-18-pro-is-coming-heres-whats-new-with-each-model/)**: Leaks ahead of the September 9 "Surprise and Shine" event indicate the iPhone 18 Pro will debut a 2nm A20 Pro chip, a 35% smaller Dynamic Island, and an enlarged camera module with a variable aperture. Meanwhile, reporting confirms the first foldable iPhone Ultra remains targeted for a September debut, albeit in severely constrained initial supplies. Analysts also reported that Apple tested and ultimately scrapped an Apple Pencil Mini accessory for the Ultra to prevent stylus pressure from scratching the softer folding display. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/31/iphone-18-pro-is-coming-heres-whats-new-with-each-model/))

**[Farewell, Tim Cook: An Era-Defining CEO Who Hung On Too Long](https://www.macworld.com/article/3218782/farewell-tim-cook-an-era-defining-ceo-who-hung-on-too-long.html)** ([Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/3218782/farewell-tim-cook-an-era-defining-ceo-who-hung-on-too-long.html))
This incisive essay delivers a candid appraisal of Tim Cook’s 15-year tenure on the eve of his transfer of power to John Ternus. While acknowledging his unparalleled financial stewardship—scaling Apple from \$350 billion to past \$2 trillion in market valuation—the piece argues that the final six years tarnished his record. The author examines major headwinds including Vision Pro team layoffs following disappointing adoption, an increasingly strained relationship with App Store developers, and reliance on OpenAI and Google after internal AI delays. It provides an essential, balanced balance sheet of the Cook era that contextualizes the enormous challenges facing incoming CEO John Ternus.

**[Apple Finally Hits a VR Home Run](https://www.macworld.com/article/3223204/apple-finally-hits-a-vr-home-run.html)** ([Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/3223204/apple-finally-hits-a-vr-home-run.html))
*Macworld* provides a detailed hands-on impression of Apple’s debut live Major League Baseball broadcast in Apple Immersive 8K 3D video on the Vision Pro. The author explains how field-level views beside the dugout, uncompressed Spatial Audio ambient sound, and dedicated commentary delivered a game-day presence that flat screens simply cannot replicate. While the headset's physical weight and eye strain remain real obstacles during multi-hour games, the broadcast represents the most persuasive consumer use case for spatial computing to date. It is a compelling read for anyone tracking whether immersive live sports can breathe new commercial life into Apple’s visionOS ecosystem.

**[Codex Report: Four Months of Building Joy on My Mac with ChatGPT](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/31/codex-report-four-months-of-building-joy-on-my-mac-with-chatgpt/)** ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/31/codex-report-four-months-of-building-joy-on-my-mac-with-chatgpt/))
Zac Hall outlines an illuminating four-month journey using OpenAI’s Codex desktop agent with background "Computer Use" on macOS. The piece traces the history of Apple automation from Workflow to Apple Shortcuts to OpenAI's acquisition of the Sky assistant team, illustrating how agentic tools bridge the gap for non-programmers. Hall describes creating bespoke utilities tailored to his specific workflows, including Flow for story writing, Better Remote for controlling tvOS from the desktop, and apps styled for macOS 27 Golden Gate. The article is an inspiring read for developers and power users curious about the future of native personal computing on the Mac.
