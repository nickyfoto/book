# End of an Era: Tim Cook Bids Farewell, John Ternus Takes the Reins, and Unprecedented Leaks Reveal Apple’s Upcoming Hardware — 2026-08-24

## Highlights
This week marks a historic turning point for Apple as **Tim Cook celebrates his 15th anniversary as CEO** and prepares to **transition to Executive Chairman on September 1**, handing the keys of Cupertino to **hardware chief John Ternus**. Amidst this momentous leadership transition, Apple is grappling with an **unprecedented series of software branch merging errors** that have accidentally leaked major hardware secrets, including **camera-equipped AirPods** and an upcoming **7-inch "HomePad" smart hub**. Meanwhile, a global memory and chip supply crunch has **inevitable iPhone price hikes** looming over next month's launch, even as developers and public testers navigate the **latest seventh beta releases** of Apple’s next-generation operating systems.

## Top Stories

- **[Tim Cook Steps Down on September 1, John Ternus Prepares to Take the Reins](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/24/tim-cook-replaced-steve-jobs-15-years-ago-today/)**: Marking exactly 15 years since taking over from Steve Jobs, Tim Cook is preparing to step down as Apple CEO on September 1, transitioning to Executive Chairman to handle regulatory policy and global government relations. Senior VP of Hardware Engineering John Ternus, who has overseen AirPods, Mac, iPad, and iPhone development since joining Apple in 2001, will assume the CEO role just ahead of Apple's autumn hardware launches. A private farewell party was held on August 23 on the Apple campus featuring a performance by OneRepublic and tributes from Laurene Powell Jobs, Jeff Williams, Eddy Cue, and Ternus. (**[Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/24/tim-cook-replaced-steve-jobs-15-years-ago-today/)**)

- **[iPhone 18 Pro Price Increases Are "Inevitable" but May Be Modest](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/24/apple-preparing-to-increase-iphone-prices/)**: Driven by a global memory and chip supply-chain crunch that already triggered massive 23% price hikes for Macs and iPads in June, Apple is preparing to raise iPhone prices next month. However, Bloomberg's Mark Gurman expects Apple to follow Samsung's and Google's recent pricing moves by implementing a relatively modest \$100 price increase. This adjustment would see the iPhone 18 Pro start at \$1,199 (up from the iPhone 17 Pro's \$1,099), indicating Apple is choosing to absorb some of the rising production expenses at the expense of its own gross margins. (**[Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/24/apple-preparing-to-increase-iphone-prices/)**)

- **[Upcoming Foldable "iPhone Ultra" Details Leak Along with Key Hardware Limitations](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/24/iphone-ultra-wont-have-these-five-features/)**: While early testers are reportedly amazed by Apple's first foldable phone, the high-end device is rumored to carry a price tag exceeding \$2,000 and come with several significant compromises. Due to its ultra-thin design, the "iPhone Ultra" is expected to feature a dual-camera system without a telephoto lens, replace Face ID with Touch ID on the side power button, omit MagSafe support, and ship as an eSIM-only device worldwide. The left edge of the device is also completely bare, making it the first iPhone to feature neither an Action button nor a physical mute switch. (**[Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/24/iphone-ultra-wont-have-these-five-features/)**)

- **[Apple Backtracks on Controversial iCloud+ Hide My Email Domain Change](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/24/apple-reverses-planned-hide-my-email-domain-change-after-user-pushback/)**: Reversing a plan announced in June, Apple has decided that iCloud+ Hide My Email addresses will remain on the standard `icloud.com` domain rather than shifting to `private.icloud.com`. The reversal follows intense user pushback over concerns that a shared `private.icloud.com` domain would make it too easy for websites to identify and block disposable email aliases. However, starting later this year, Sign in with Apple addresses will still transition to the new `private.icloud.com` domain. (**[Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/24/apple-reverses-planned-hide-my-email-domain-change-after-user-pushback/)**)

- **[Internal Code Merging Error Causes Unprecedented Product Leaks in macOS 26.7 RC](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/24/how-apple-leaked-itself/)**: A major internal gaffe saw Apple include references to several unreleased devices, including an instructional video showing camera-equipped AirPods, in the macOS Tahoe 26.7 Release Candidate. According to Mark Gurman, an internal development build containing support for future hardware was mistakenly merged into the shipping branch destined for developers. While the camera-equipped AirPods are not expected to launch until late 2027, the leak represents an unusual security slip-up for Apple's notoriously secretive PR machine. (**[Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/24/how-apple-leaked-itself/)**)

- **[Apple Releases Seventh Developer Betas for Next-Gen Operating Systems with Siri AI Focus](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/24/ios-27-beta-7/)**: Apple has rolled out the seventh developer betas and fifth public betas of iOS 27, macOS Golden Gate, watchOS 27, iPadOS 27, tvOS 27, and visionOS 27, shifting focus toward bug squashing and stability. Although these builds are highly stable, beta 7 introduces a server-side waitlist infrastructure with dynamic wait times to manage the rollout of Apple's major "Siri AI" upgrade upon public launch. Other newly discovered features include macOS Golden Gate's redesigned Liquid Glass traffic light window controls and new Safari AI-powered custom extensions. (**[Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/24/ios-27-beta-7/)**)

## Articles Worth Reading

**Apple TV Shows Aren't Worth the Wait** (**[Source](https://www.macworld.com/article/3217649/apple-tv-shows-arent-worth-the-wait.html)**)
This sharp opinion piece explores how **extreme multi-year production cycles** are actively damaging the cultural footprint and viewer momentum of Apple TV+’s flagship series. While highly acclaimed shows like *Severance* and *Pluribus* are suffering from **gaps of nearly three years between seasons**, the article argues that these delays cause viewers to lose narrative connection and forget crucial plotlines. By contrast, the espionage thriller *Slow Horses* has built a massive, dedicated following by **consistently maintaining an annual release cadence**. It’s a compelling read for any television enthusiast or streaming analyst examining the trade-offs between creative perfectionism and audience retention in the streaming era.

**What to Do Immediately If You Spill Liquid on Your Mac** (**[Source](https://www.macworld.com/article/672687/what-to-do-if-you-spill-liquids-on-your-mac.html)**)
Spilling a drink on a laptop is a universal nightmare, but this exhaustive guide details the crucial steps to **save your Mac from terminal short-circuiting and corrosion**. The piece debunks the popular myth of putting a wet Mac in a bag of rice—warning that **starch and rice particles create a sticky paste** that ruins ports and internal components—recommending passive upside-down drying and a mandatory **24-to-72-hour power-down period** instead. It also covers how Apple's modern M3-and-later MacBooks utilize **built-in moisture sensors** to display real-time "Liquid Detected" alerts in the USB-C ports. This is an essential, highly practical reference guide that every Mac user should bookmark before disaster strikes.

**Epic Fights Apple’s Bid to Seal Key Data Behind Proposed Fees on Linked-Out Purchases** (**[Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/24/epic-fights-apples-bid-to-seal-key-data-behind-proposed-fees-on-linked-out-purchases/)**)
As Apple submits its final pricing proposal for off-App Store purchases, calling for a **15% commission on standard apps** and **10% on partner programs and subscription renewals**, Epic Games has filed a fierce opposition to block Apple from sealing key commercial data. Epic argues that Apple is attempting to **conceal vital financial metrics, developer survey results, and link-out adoption estimates** from the public and the courts. The legal battle underscores the ongoing friction as Apple works to maintain economic control over the iOS ecosystem, even when forced to permit external purchasing options. This article is a critical read for developers seeking to understand the complicated financial realities of implementing external links and steering users away from Apple's native in-app purchase system.

**Apple Stores Prepare for the Long-Rumored "HomePad" Smart Hub** (**[Source](https://www.macworld.com/article/3219016/apple-stores-are-getting-ready-for-the-release-of-the-long-rumored-home-hub.html)**)
With Apple Store layout teams reportedly being ordered to **reconfigure physical display areas this fall**, Apple is finally preparing for a massive smart home retail push. This store reorganization is intended to demonstrate a **7-inch "HomePad" central display** that runs a widget-based user interface similar to watchOS and handles ambient sensing for face-based profile switching. The article highlights how the **emergence of Siri AI is the primary catalyst** enabling Apple to finally compete with Amazon and Google in the smart home segment. It’s an essential read for hardware enthusiasts interested in how Apple plans to build a cohesive, AI-driven smart home ecosystem in the post-Cook era.
