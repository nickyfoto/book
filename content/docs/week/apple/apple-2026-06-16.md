# Apple Daily Digest — 2026-06-16

## Highlights
Today's news is dominated by fascinating discoveries within the iOS 27 and macOS 27 Golden Gate betas, revealing extensive refinements to Siri AI and Liquid Glass interfaces. Looking ahead, significant leaks shed light on Apple's long-term hardware roadmap, with reports indicating a 20th Anniversary iPhone and a definitive push toward 12GB of RAM as the baseline for all iPhone 18 models to support advanced AI.

## Top Stories
- **[iPhone 18 to Pack 12GB of RAM for Smarter Siri Features, No Price Bump](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/16/iphone-18-to-pack-12gb-of-ram/)**: Apple is reportedly planning to equip the entire iPhone 18 lineup with 12GB of RAM to ensure all models can run the most powerful on-device Siri AI features. This RAM upgrade aims to drive AI adoption without increasing the standard $799 base price, distinguishing it from the iPhone 17 series where the 8GB base model is excluded from the most advanced features.
- **[Apple’s 20th Anniversary iPhones to Come in Two Sizes, Will Launch Alongside Gen 2 Foldable iPhone](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/16/20th-anniversary-iphone-size-options/)**: A new report details Apple's late 2027 roadmap, which includes two 20th-anniversary iPhone models featuring nearly edge-to-edge displays and curved glass. These models will launch alongside a second-generation foldable iPhone and camera-equipped AirPods, all likely powered by a 2-nanometer A21 chip.
- **[Foldable iPhone Delay Rumors Are 'False,' Leaker Claims](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/16/foldable-iphone-delay-rumors-false-leaker/)**: Countering recent reports of a delay to 2027, leaker Fixed Focus Digital claims the inaugural foldable iPhone is still on track to launch alongside the iPhone 18 Pro series this September. Additionally, hidden code in the OS 27 betas—such as `foldState` variables and a freely resizable iPhone Mirroring app—strongly hints the device's release is imminent.
- **[Apple Faces First Italian DMA Probe on iCloud Interoperability](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/16/apple-faces-first-italian-dma-probe-icloud/)**: Italy’s competition regulator has launched an investigation into Apple under the European Digital Markets Act. The watchdog claims Apple is not placing third-party cloud services on an equal footing with iCloud, particularly restricting access to full device backup features in iOS and iPadOS.
- **[Apple releases Beats Studio Buds firmware update with important microphone security fix](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/16/apple-releases-beats-studio-buds-firmware-update-with-important-microphone-security-fix/)**: Apple rolled out firmware version 1B211 for Beats Studio Buds to patch a serious Bluetooth vulnerability. The flaw allowed attackers within Bluetooth range to listen through the device's microphone while the earbuds were seeking pair requests.

## Articles Worth Reading

**[Here’s What’s New With Visual Intelligence in iOS 27](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-27-visual-intelligence/)**
Visual Intelligence receives a massive overhaul in iOS 27, now housed under a dedicated "Siri Mode" within the Camera app. Powered directly by Siri AI rather than passing requests to third-party tools like ChatGPT, it can now assess nutritional value, split restaurant bills, and instantly import calendar events or business card contacts. The system is deeply integrated across the ecosystem, accessible via keyboard shortcuts on macOS Golden Gate and screen grabs on iPadOS.

**[iOS 27 Revamps App Icons With Sharper Liquid Glass Layers](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/16/ios-27-revamps-app-icons/)**
Apple has iterated on its controversial Liquid Glass design language introduced last year, addressing blurriness complaints by integrating distinct Liquid Glass layers directly into the core icon artwork. The new rendering pipeline offers sharper edges, more defined refractions, and completely removes the motion-based gyroscopic shimmer that caused optical illusions in iOS 26. This design update provides a cleaner, more readable look while retaining the signature depth of the Liquid Glass aesthetic.

**[Three ways the OS 27 betas give away Apple’s folding phone secret](https://www.macworld.com/article/3166172/three-ways-the-os-27-betas-give-away-apples-folding-phone-secret.html)**
While Apple hasn't officially announced a folding iPhone, the newly released OS 27 developer betas are packed with clues. The code introduces `foldState` and `angleDegrees` status variables, and the new iPhone Mirroring app on macOS seamlessly resizes to a wider layout, suggesting an incoming device with a passport-like aspect ratio. Furthermore, Apple has quietly reintroduced landscape support for numerous built-in apps, paving the way for a book-style foldable layout.
