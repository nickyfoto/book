# Apple's 50th Anniversary, Foldable iPhone Rumors, and App Store Review Delays — 2026-03-29

## Highlights

Today's news is dominated by massive forward-looking rumors, including an expected foldable iPhone and an all-new Siri app coming in iOS 27. Simultaneously, developers are facing unprecedented App Store review bottlenecks due to the rise of AI "vibe coding," while Apple gears up to celebrate its historic 50th anniversary at Apple Park.

## Top Stories

- **Apple Preparing 'Most Significant Overhaul in the iPhone’s History’**: Apple's first foldable iPhone is heavily rumored to launch this September alongside the iPhone 18 lineup, featuring a 7.7-inch inner display and a starting price of around $1,999. It will boast iPad-like multitasking features, allowing apps to be opened side-by-side, enabled by the upcoming iOS 27. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/29/biggest-iphone-overhaul-ever-rumor/))
- **iOS 27 Rumored to Feature All-New Siri App With 'Extensions' Feature**: Mark Gurman reports that iOS 27 will introduce a dedicated Siri app resembling ChatGPT and Claude, complete with an "Extensions" system to integrate third-party AI chatbots. Siri is also slated for a UI overhaul potentially utilizing the Dynamic Island, alongside a systemwide "Ask Siri" button. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/29/ios-27-siri-app-with-extensions-rumor/))
- **Vibe coding could mark the end of the App Store review process as we know it**: Developers are experiencing agonizing App Store review wait times of up to a week, a stark increase from the traditional one-to-two day turnaround. The surge is attributed to an influx of applications built hands-off via AI "vibe coding," threatening the sustainability of Apple's human review process. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/29/vibe-coding-developers-report-long-app-store-review-queues/))
- **Security Bite: Apple takes aim at cybercriminals’ more desperate tactic to infect Mac users**: The newly released macOS Tahoe 26.4 introduces a vital security prompt warning users before they paste potentially malicious commands into the Terminal app. This defends against a rising social engineering tactic used by cybercriminals to trick users into bypassing Apple's Gatekeeper protections. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/28/security-bite-apple-takes-aim-at-cybercriminals-more-desperate-tactic-to-infect-mac-users/))
- **Apple’s 50th Anniversary Finale Revealed**: Apple is closing its Apple Park Visitor Center early on March 31 to host a corporate finale celebrating its 50th anniversary, which falls on April 1. The event is rumored to feature a headlining performance by Paul McCartney, an artist Steve Jobs deeply admired. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/29/apples-50th-anniversary-finale-revealed/))
