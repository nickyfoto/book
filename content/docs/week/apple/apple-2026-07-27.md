# Apple Daily Digest — 2026-07-27

## Highlights
Today's news is dominated by a massive wave of software updates across Apple's ecosystem, heavily focused on patching unprecedented security vulnerabilities and laying the groundwork for iOS 27's AI features. Beyond software, we're tracking a significant lawsuit over App Store scams, international expansions for AppleCare One, and intriguing long-term hardware roadmaps detailing the future of the Mac and a radical 20th-anniversary iPhone.

## Top Stories
- **[Massive Security and Spotlight Updates Arrive with iOS 26.6 and macOS Tahoe 26.6](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/27/ios-26-6-security-fixes/)**: Apple has rolled out iOS 26.6, macOS Tahoe 26.6, iPadOS 26.6, and other OS updates to patch over 75 vulnerabilities on iPhone and over 130 on the Mac. Crucially, these updates also optimize the Spotlight index to prepare users' devices for the deep Apple Intelligence integration and on-device processing arriving with iOS 27 this fall. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/27/ios-26-6-security-fixes/))

- **[Apple Sued Over Fake App Store Bitcoin Wallet](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/27/apple-app-store-fake-bitcoin-wallet-lawsuit/)**: Three customers filed a lawsuit against Apple in a California federal court after losing $1.8 million to a fraudulent "Sparrow Wallet" app that bypassed App Store review. The plaintiffs argue Apple failed to ensure the safety of its platform, though Apple states it has since taken swift action to remove the offending apps and terminate the associated developer accounts. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/27/apple-app-store-fake-bitcoin-wallet-lawsuit/))

- **['AppleCare One' Expands to Four More Countries](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/27/applecare-one-four-more-countries/)**: AppleCare One, a subscription covering up to three Apple devices for a single monthly fee, is launching in Australia, France, Germany, and the UK on August 4. The all-in-one tier includes Theft and Loss protection, which is also expanding as an individual plan to the iPad and Apple Watch in these regions. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/27/applecare-one-four-more-countries/))

- **[Amazon Files to Build 5,105-Satellite Network for iPhone Features](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/27/amazon-iphone-satellite-network/)**: Amazon has applied to deploy a low Earth orbit satellite constellation that will utilize Globalstar spectrum to power iPhone satellite features. Partnering closely with Apple, this network aims to improve connection speeds and support future connectivity tools like Emergency SOS and Roadside Assistance. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/27/amazon-iphone-satellite-network/))

- **[T-Mobile Outage Forces iPhones into SOS Mode](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/27/psa-t-mobile-is-down-iphones-forced-into-sos-mode/)**: A widespread T-Mobile outage across the United States has left many users unable to make calls or access cellular data, forcing their iPhones to display the "SOS" icon in the status bar. The issue prevents standard network connectivity, though emergency calls remain functional by routing through other available towers. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/27/psa-t-mobile-is-down-iphones-forced-into-sos-mode/))
