# Apple Daily Digest — 2026-07-03

## Highlights
Today's news brings a mix of exciting software discoveries and surprising hardware pivots. While the second iOS 27 developer beta continues to reveal powerful new Apple Intelligence features and hints at future camera-equipped wearables, supply chain reports suggest Apple has abruptly suspended development on its rumored camera-enabled AirPods Pro. Meanwhile, the legal sphere remains active as Apple defends its AI training methods against YouTube creators and battles prominent leakers in court over trade secrets.

## Top Stories
- **[iOS 27 Beta Hints at New Apple Product Such as 'AirPods Ultra'](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/03/ios-27-beta-hints-at-new-apple-product/)**: Code uncovered in the second iOS 27 developer beta references a product codenamed B790 that relies on cameras positioned on either side of the user's head. This firmware instructs the Visual Intelligence feature on how to identify landmarks and objects, pointing toward future smart glasses or advanced "AirPods Ultra".
- **[Camera-Equipped AirPods Pro Development 'Suspended,' Leaker Claims](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/03/camera-airpods-pro-development-suspended-leaker/)**: Despite recent reports that camera-equipped AirPods Pro were entering advanced testing, prototype collector "Kosutami" asserts that the project has been halted. The delay might be tied to industry-wide silicon shortages or the ongoing development of the smarter, Apple Intelligence-powered Siri.
- **[iPhone 18 With 9GB RAM Still Won’t Support Two New iOS 27 Features](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/03/iphone-18-wont-support-two-ios-27-features/)**: Analyst Ming-Chi Kuo reports that the base iPhone 18 and 18e will feature 9GB of RAM, up from 8GB, but will still fall short of the 12GB required for certain advanced Apple Intelligence tasks. As a result, these entry-level devices will miss out on iOS 27's highly accurate speech-to-text dictation and custom Siri voice expressiveness features.
- **[Apple Responds to Lawsuit Filed by Three YouTube Channels](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/02/apple-responds-to-youtuber-ai-lawsuit/)**: Apple has formally requested the dismissal of a class-action lawsuit from YouTube creators who allege the company unlawfully scraped their videos to train AI models. Apple argues that accessing these public, un-paywalled videos does not violate the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) or YouTube's Terms of Service.
- **[Silo season three is streaming today on Apple TV](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/02/apple-tv-silo-season-three/)**: The critically acclaimed dystopian sci-fi series *Silo* returns for its third season, bringing a split timeline that explores both Juliette's present struggles with amnesia and flashbacks detailing the origin of the underground bunkers.

**[12 New Things Your iPhone Can Do in iOS 27](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/03/new-things-iphone-can-do-ios-27/)**
Apple's upcoming iOS 27 update is packed with practical features beyond just fixing bugs. **[Prosser denies conspiring to steal Apple secrets in lawsuit response, blames Ramacciotti](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/02/prosser-denies-conspiring-to-steal-apple-secrets-in-lawsuit-response-blames-ramacciotti/)**
After having a default judgment set aside, YouTuber Jon Prosser has formally responded to Apple's lawsuit regarding the leak of iOS 26's Liquid Glass redesign. Prosser denies conspiring to steal trade secrets, claiming he did not know the development iPhone shown to him on a FaceTime call belonged to a former Apple employee, nor did he induce the disclosure. He maintains that he was simply reporting on exclusives like any news organization, shifting the blame for the actual breach to his co-defendant, Michael Ramacciotti.
