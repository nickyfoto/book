# Foldables and Smart Homes Headline Apple's Next Chapter — 2026-08-23

## Highlights
Today's Apple ecosystem news highlights a major wave of leaks revealing the company's hardware roadmap, led by real-world tester feedback on the highly anticipated foldable **"iPhone Ultra"** and sweeping retail store reorganizations to prepare for an upcoming smart home push. While Apple gears up to launch its first 2nm-based desktop with an M6-powered 24-inch iMac and celebrates strong sales growth that beats rivals, the company is also trimming some of its Vision Pro software and gaming teams. Rounding out today's news are record deals on AirTag 2 and AirPods Max 2, a 5-year AdGuard VPN subscription sale, hands-on reviews of Mophie's pocketable battery banks, a granulated wallpaper release, and a retrospective on 20 years of the indie task-manager *Things*.

## Top Stories
- **[iPhone Ultra: Early Testers Praise Durability and Multitasking Layouts](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/23/apple-foldable-iphone-early-tester-thoughts/)**: Early testers of Apple’s upcoming book-style foldable—reportedly referred to internally as the **"iPhone Ultra"**—have given highly positive feedback on its pocketability, hinge durability, and iPad-like multitasking layouts on the unfolded 7.8-inch inner screen. However, to achieve an ultra-thin 4.5mm profile when unfolded, Apple is making design compromises by implementing Touch ID in the side button rather than Face ID, and omitting a telephoto lens from its 48-megapixel dual-lens rear camera setup. The premium device, which is expected to launch alongside the iPhone 18 Pro series on or around September 9, is rumored to start at \$2,000 and top \$2,500 for higher storage capacities. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/23/apple-foldable-iphone-early-tester-thoughts/))
- **[Apple Store Layout Changes Signal Imminent Smart Home Offensive](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/23/apple-preparing-for-homepod-mini-apple-tv-smart-home-launches/)**: Apple retail teams are preparing significant changes to store perimeter walls ("Avenues") to showcase upcoming smart home devices, indicating that the company's product roadmap is moving forward after Siri-related software delays. While updated Apple TV 4K (A17 Pro chip or better) and HomePod mini (S9 chip, improved sound, new red finish) models are nearly ready, the scale of these retail changes suggests the simultaneous debut of Apple's highly anticipated smart home hub. Rumored to run an all-new OS based on tvOS and feature an A18 chip, this hub will sport a 7-inch square display, support FaceTime, and display customizable lock-screen style Widget Galleries. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/23/apple-preparing-for-homepod-mini-apple-tv-smart-home-launches/))
- **[24-Inch M6 iMac with 2nm Tech Expected to Arrive by Year's End](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/23/m6-imac-this-year-new-colors/)**: Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reports that Apple is close to launching refreshed 24-inch iMac models powered by the next-generation M6 processor, replacing the M4 version released in 2024. The M6 chip will mark a monumental shift as Apple's first Mac processor built on a cutting-edge 2nm process, although Apple will reportedly skip M6 Pro/Max/Ultra tiers to accelerate the subsequent M7 family. Expected to release before the end of 2026 with fresh color palettes but no major external chassis redesign, this update anchors a major fall Mac lineup that could also include touchscreen MacBook Pros and M5-series Mac Studios. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/23/m6-imac-this-year-new-colors/))
- **[Apple Outperforms Competitors Globally with Strong iPhone 17 Demand](https://www.ilounge.com/news/iphone/apple-beats-rivals-as-their-sales-grow-while-others-fall)**: Despite challenging macroeconomic environments that saw overall smartphone sales in India slump by 14% across March and early August, Apple recorded a stellar 15% increase in global sales. Driven by robust, persistent consumer demand for the iPhone 17, Apple has successfully gained market share and outperformed rivals across Europe, India, and Latin America, even as regional shipments for other manufacturers dropped. ([Source](https://www.ilounge.com/news/iphone/apple-beats-rivals-as-their-sales-grow-while-others-fall))
- **[Apple Trims Vision Pro Teams and Refocuses Spatial Computing Content](https://www.ilounge.com/news/apple-vision-pro/vision-pro-teams-laid-off)**: In an apparent reshuffling of resources, Apple has laid off multiple employees who were working on gaming, immersive video content, Siri, and software development for the Vision Pro. This organizational shift highlights the changing developer priorities at Apple as they fine-tune spatial computing platforms, which historically were built around the device's original internal name of "Reality Pro" during most of its developmental cycle. ([Source](https://www.ilounge.com/news/apple-vision-pro/vision-pro-teams-laid-off))
