- **Apple Officially Announces September 9 'Surprise and Shine' Event**: Apple has officially confirmed its highly anticipated fall keynote will take place on Wednesday, September 9, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. PT. Under the tagline "Surprise and shine," the event is expected to showcase the new iPhone 18 Pro lineup, Apple Watch Series 12, Apple Watch Ultra 4, and Apple's highly anticipated first-ever foldable device, rumored to be the "iPhone Ultra". Cook will transition into the role of executive chairman, with John Ternus officially taking over the chief executive mantle at the beginning of the month. This historic shift marks a new era for Apple leadership, making the upcoming September 9 event the first keynote under the Ternus administration. ([Source](https://www.ilounge.com/news/apple/tim-cook-farewell-party-as-ceo-hosted-by-apple))

This surprise gesture effectively gives some buyers a \$100 to \$300 discount while advancing delivery dates to September. However, the forced transition has drawn some criticism from students who found their delivery estimates delayed by several weeks, with no option to decline the upgrade. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/26/apple-free-mac-mini-upgrade-email/))
- **Virginia Launches Apple Wallet Driver's License Support as the 15th U.S. State**: Virginia residents can now add a digital version of their driver's license or state ID directly to Apple Wallet on their iPhone and Apple Watch. This privacy-focused feature can be used to display proof of identity or age at select businesses, venues, and TSA checkpoints across more than 250 U.S. airports. Data transmission is secured using a standards-based, on-device architecture that requires biometric authorization via Face ID or Touch ID before any personal information is shared, meaning users never have to hand over their device to a reader. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/26/apple-wallet-id-feature-launches-in-15th-state/))
- **iPhone 17 Dominates Q2 2026 Global Handset Sales Despite Broader Market Slump**: According to Counterpoint Research, the base iPhone 17 retained its crown as the world's best-selling smartphone in the second quarter of 2026, capturing 6% of global unit sales. Apple dominated the industry by securing five of the top ten spots, with the iPhone 17 Pro Max and iPhone 17 Pro holding the second and third positions, respectively, and the refreshed iPhone 17e ranking seventh. This strong performance enabled Apple to grow its unit sales by 5% year-over-year, bucking a broader 11% contraction in the global smartphone market. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/26/counterpoint-iphone-17-topped-worldwide-smartphone-sales-during-q2/))
- **Survey Highlights Big Consumer-Pricing Gap for Apple's Upcoming Foldable iPhone**: A CNET survey reveals that 75% of U.S. adults are not interested in buying a foldable Apple device, and those who are expressed a willingness to pay an average of only \$781—far below the expected \$2,000+ price tag of the "iPhone Ultra". Despite this pricing mismatch, market analysts remain highly optimistic about the device's first-year prospects. Research firm IDC projects that Apple will ship over 10 million foldable iPhones in its first year of availability and capture 40% of all foldable shipments by 2027. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/26/most-arent-interested-in-a-foldable-iphone/))
