# Apple’s Autumn Surprise: Next-Gen M6 and M5 Ultra Chips Unleashed Alongside Upgraded Mac mini and Mac Studio — 2026-08-25

## Highlights
In a stunning late-August hardware surprise, Apple has unveiled its next-generation silicon family, debuting the industry's **first 2-nanometer M6 chip** and the **powerhouse M5 Ultra**. These ground-breaking chips arrive inside completely refreshed **Mac mini** and **Mac Studio** models, bringing massive performance gains for on-device AI tasks but carrying higher price tags. The announcements coincide with a transitional moment for the company, coming just days before **Tim Cook officially steps down as CEO** after a historic 15-year run.

## Top Stories
- **Apple Announces New M6 and M5 Pro Mac minis, Along with M5 Max and M5 Ultra Mac Studios**: Apple has blindsided the industry with a rare August hardware release, updating both the Mac mini and Mac Studio lineups. The Mac mini is now configurable with either the new M6 or the M5 Pro, while the Mac Studio can be configured with the M5 Max or the behemoth M5 Ultra. Both lineups deliver incredible performance boosts but come with price hikes—**\$100 more for the mini** and **\$200 more for the base M5 Ultra Studio**. ([Source](https://www.macstories.net/news/apple-announces-new-m6-and-m5-pro-mac-minis-along-with-m5-max-and-m5-ultra-mac-studios/))
- **Apple Reveals M6 as First-Ever 2nm Chip**: Apple has officially taken the lead in chip fabrication by unveiling the M6, the industry's first processor built on a cutting-edge **2-nanometer process**. The chip features a redesigned 12-core CPU with two high-powered "super cores" and a 12-core GPU with **Neural Accelerators built into each core**. It also introduces a **Dual 16-core Neural Engine** that delivers up to twice the peak compute performance of prior generations to accelerate on-device AI model execution. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/25/apple-reveals-m6/))
- **Apple Debuts M5 Ultra as Most Powerful Chip Ever**: The M5 Ultra represents a massive leap in desktop computing, fusing two dual-die M5 Max chips with next-generation UltraFusion technology to form Apple's **first quad-die architecture**. The chip scales up to an incredible 36-core CPU, an 80-core GPU with Neural Accelerators, and up to **1.2TB/s of unified memory bandwidth**. When configured with up to **512GB of RAM** in late October, the M5 Ultra allows professional workflows to run massive open-weight AI models completely on-device. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/25/apple-debuts-m5-ultra/))
- **Apple Maps Ads Officially Roll Out in the US and Canada**: Following testing throughout the summer, Apple has officially launched ads inside its native Maps app for users in the US and Canada. The sponsored spots, clearly labeled with a blue **"Ad" badge**, appear in search suggestions under Suggested Places and at the top of relevant search results. While Apple highlights strict, privacy-first user protections that decouple search and location interactions from Apple Accounts, **users have no way to turn them off**. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/25/apple-maps-ads-live/))
- **Dynamic Siri AI Waitlist Estimates Prepped for iOS 27 Launch**: Apple is preparing to manage the extreme cloud server load associated with its upcoming Apple Intelligence features by integrating a waitlist directly into the release versions of iOS 27 and macOS 27. New code discovered in developer betas indicates that Apple will push **personalized wait time estimates** directly from its servers. Users will receive a push notification once access is granted to the advanced Siri AI features, which could take anywhere from one to two weeks based on beta feedback. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/25/prepare-to-wait-for-siri-ai-access-when-ios-27-launches-next-month/))
