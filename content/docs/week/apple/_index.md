# Apple — Week of 2026-08-28 to 2026-09-04

## Week in Review
This week marked a **historic milestone** for Cupertino as Tim Cook concluded his 15-year tenure as CEO, officially handing the reins to new chief **John Ternus on September 1**. In tandem with this leadership transition, Apple scheduled its **"Surprise and Shine" fall hardware event** for Wednesday, September 9 to showcase the premium iPhone 18 Pro series and a highly anticipated foldable iPhone. Meanwhile, the sudden launch of **M6-powered Mac minis** and **powerhouse Mac Studios** has ignited massive, unexpected enterprise demand for on-premise AI model clustering.

## Top Stories

**A New Era in Cupertino: John Ternus Assumes CEO Role** · [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/01/john-ternus-is-now-apple-ceo/)
On September 1, **John Ternus officially took over as Apple’s CEO**, while **Tim Cook transitioned to Executive Chairman** of the board after 15 years leading the company. Ternus’s first memo to employees expressed deep gratitude for Cook’s legacy and teased a **"phenomenal" hardware launch**. In a further organizational shift, long-time executive **Phil Schiller is also relinquishing control** of the App Store and product launches to services chief Eddy Cue as part of a phased retirement.

**"Surprise and Shine" Keynote Scheduled to Introduce iPhone 18 Pro and Foldable "iPhone Ultra"** · [TidBITS](https://tidbits.com/2026/08/28/apples-surprise-and-shine-event-set-for-9-september-2026/)
Apple has officially announced its annual autumn event for **Wednesday, September 9**, which is expected to showcase the 2nm-based **iPhone 18 Pro**, the **Apple Watch Series 12**, and Apple's first foldable device, the **"iPhone Ultra"**. While early reports indicate testers are highly impressed by the foldable's durable, crease-less hinge, initial production at Foxconn is **limited to just a few hundred units a day** due to strict quality controls. To avoid the September 11 anniversary, Apple is breaking tradition by launching **pre-orders on Saturday, September 12 at midnight PT**, while delaying the standard iPhone 18 and iPhone Air 2 to spring 2027.

**Surging Enterprise AI Demand Catches Apple Off Guard as New Desktop Hardware Debuts** · [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/30/apple-unexpected-mac-mini-and-studio-demand/)
Apple unexpectedly accelerated the launch of its new **M6-powered Mac mini** and **M5 Max/Ultra Mac Studio** desktops, which feature advanced Neural Engines and high-speed memory architectures. Corporate clients like Anthropic, Ford, and Disney have rushed to buy these consumer machines, **daisy-chaining them into local clusters** to run large frontier AI models on-premise. However, the surge has caught Cupertino off guard, as Apple currently **lacks a dedicated enterprise sales and engineering team** to support these local AI server deployments.

**Apple Trades Blows with OpenAI in Explosive Forensic Trade Secret Lawsuit** · [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/31/apple-openai-forensic-macbook-evidence/)
Apple submitted shocking forensic evidence in federal court showing that former electrical engineer Chang Liu **downloaded proprietary circuit schematics** and trained an AI agent on those workflows for OpenAI projects. In a sharp counter-filing, OpenAI fired back by calling the lawsuit a **"mess of Apple's own making"** and blaming Apple's own lax IT security practices, such as encouraging personal iCloud accounts. OpenAI characterized Apple's aggressive legal action as **retaliation against employees** leaving a slow-moving legacy company for a fast-paced competitor.

## Also Worth Knowing
- **Apple TV & Apple One Subscription Rates Hike**: Standalone Apple TV subscription prices increased by \$2 to **\$14.99 per month (\$119 annually)** across the U.S., Brazil, Chile, and Mexico, leading to a corresponding bump to **\$21.95 per month** for Apple One Individual bundles. Simultaneously, Apple TV acquired exclusive streaming rights to the BBC comedy *Small Prophets*, marking its **first-ever purchase of an existing, already broadcast hit**.
- **iOS 27 Beta Reveals Stunning Older Device Optimizations**: Real-world beta 8 testing of iOS 27 on legacy hardware like the iPhone 11 Pro Max showed **dramatic optimization gains under the hood**, with Camera app launch times cut by over 30% and Maps app loads slashed from 3.01 to 1.36 seconds. In CarPlay updates, Anthropic's Claude and Meta AI have integrated with the interface, bringing the **total number of third-party dashboard chatbots to five**.
- **Mac App Store Developers Allowed to Drop Intel Support**: Apple announced that universal macOS applications on macOS 13 or later can now **entirely drop Intel support** to target Apple Silicon ("arm64" only). This change coincides with macOS 27 Golden Gate dropping Intel-powered Mac support entirely, with Apple confirming macOS 27 will be the **final release to support the Rosetta 2 translation layer**.
- **watchOS 27 to Deprecate Walkie-Talkie and Multitasking Gestures**: The upcoming watchOS 27 update will retire several features, including the peer-to-peer **Walkie-Talkie app** and the Digital Crown **double-click multitasking shortcut**, replacing them with a new "dynamic app grid" showing Siri and suggested applications.
