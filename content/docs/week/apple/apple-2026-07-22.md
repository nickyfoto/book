# Apple's Mac Roadmap Revealed, iPhone 18 Pro Mass Production Begins, and iOS 27 Betas Expand — 2026-07-22

## Highlights
Today's news is dominated by a sweeping look at Apple's hardware roadmap, detailing an ambitious 11-model Mac overhaul over the next two years as the company leans into its AI-focused future. Simultaneously, the supply chain is moving into high gear with the iPhone 18 Pro entering mass production. On the software front, developers and enthusiasts have plenty to explore as Apple drops the second round of public betas for iOS 27, macOS 27 Golden Gate, and its other operating systems.

## Top Stories
- **Apple to Overhaul Mac Lineup With 11 New Models**: Apple is planning to refresh every Mac it sells over the next two years, starting this fall with an M6 14-inch MacBook Pro and refreshed iMacs. Future updates include a new MacBook Neo powered by an A19 Pro chip with 12GB of RAM, and high-end OLED MacBooks slated for late 2026 or early 2027. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/22/apple-to-overhaul-mac-lineup/))
- **iPhone 18 Pro Reportedly Enters Mass Production**: Apple's primary assembly partner, Foxconn, has entered its peak recruitment period as the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max begin mass production. The upcoming flagship is expected to feature a smaller Dynamic Island, the new A20 Pro chip, and a variable-aperture camera system for improved low-light performance and depth-of-field control. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/22/iphone-18-pro-enters-mass-production/))
- **Second Public Betas Released Across Apple Platforms**: Apple has rolled out the second public betas for iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27 Golden Gate, tvOS 27, and watchOS 27. Testers can access new Siri AI capabilities, an updated Liquid Glass UI, a new clipboard paste shortcut above the iOS keyboard, and a convenient recurring transactions hub in Apple Wallet. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/22/ios-27-public-beta-2-is-here-with-siri-ai-iphone-speed-upgrades-and-more/))
- **Next Mac Mini to Feature Mismatched M6 and M5 Pro Chips**: The highly anticipated Mac mini update will reportedly feature a mix of chip generations, utilizing an M6 chip for the base model and an M5 Pro for the higher-end configuration. Ongoing memory-chip shortages and high AI-driven demand have affected the release timeline, potentially delaying the update until later this year. ([Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/2964754/2026-mac-mini-m5-pro-design-specs-release-date.html))
- **iOS 27 Code Hints at Strict Penalties for Leased Devices**: Code in the latest iOS 27 beta reveals an "App Managed Features" system that could remotely lock down an iPhone if a user misses payments under Apple's rumored new leasing program. A "Restricted Mode" would block most apps except for basic functions like Phone and Settings, essentially turning the device into a brick until payments are resolved. ([Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/3196806/ios-27-code-hints-at-strict-penalties-for-missed-apple-lease-payments.html))

**Claude Code brings live iOS app testing into its Mac app** ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/21/claude-code-brings-live-ios-app-testing-into-its-mac-app/))
Anthropic has launched a significant update to its Claude Code Mac app, integrating live iOS simulator support for users on Pro, Max, and Team plans. If you have Xcode installed, Claude can now automatically build, launch, and test iOS apps directly in a dedicated interactive simulator pane. The AI agent can physically tap through the app, spot UI bugs, and autonomously iterate code fixes without taking over your main screen. This marks a major step forward for AI-assisted development, democratizing app creation for the Apple ecosystem.

**The reported Apple Upgrade program raises a very big concern** ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/22/the-reported-apple-upgrade-program-raises-a-very-big-concern/))
A shift in how Apple sells its hardware is on the horizon, with reports pointing to a new "Apple Upgrade" program that operates more like a car lease rather than a zero-interest financing plan. Unlike the outgoing iPhone Upgrade Program where consumers eventually own their device, this new model would only cover depreciation, leaving users with a balloon payment at the end of the term or forcing them into a perpetual upgrade cycle. Combined with newly discovered iOS 27 code that restricts app access for missed payments, this represents a significant—and potentially worrying—change to Apple product ownership.

**New iPhone screen sizes are launching this fall, here’s what’s coming** ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/22/new-iphone-screen-sizes-are-launching-this-fall-heres-whats-coming/))
Apple is breaking from its traditional smartphone dimensions this fall with the anticipated "iPhone Ultra," a foldable device that will introduce two entirely new screen sizes. Rumors point to a compact 5.3-inch outer display and an expansive 7.6-inch inner display, bringing the unfolded form factor remarkably close to the size of an iPad mini. The device's wider, shorter design—compared to the tall aspect ratio of recent Pro Max models—explains why Apple has been heavily prioritizing landscape mode support across native apps in the iOS 27 betas.
