# Folding iPhone "Ultra" Leaks, Lineup Shakeups, and Siri AI Licensing — 2026-08-12

## Highlights
**Today’s Apple ecosystem landscape is defined by major structural transitions, from leadership changes and supply chain adjustments to aggressive AI content negotiations. As Apple navigates a global memory shortage by restructuring component pricing and delaying its base iPhone 18, details have leaked surrounding its upcoming folding "iPhone Ultra" design and multi-million-dollar AI publisher deals. Meanwhile, regulatory controversies in Europe and a temporary Supreme Court reprieve in the Epic Games battle highlight Apple’s complex legal and public relations landscape.**

## Top Stories
- **Fall Lineup Overhaul: iPhone 18 Pro Set as New Default as Standard Model Postponed to 2027**: **Apple is significantly shaking up its traditional September launch strategy by delaying the release of the base iPhone 18 model until the first quarter of 2027.** According to supplier calls and analyst reports, the standard model—alongside a lower-end **iPhone 18e** and an **iPhone Air 2**—will be held back until March 2027, leaving the upcoming fall event focused solely on the **iPhone 18 Pro**, **iPhone 18 Pro Max**, and the foldable **"iPhone Ultra".** Consequently, the **iPhone 18 Pro** will become **Apple's primary default model** in fall marketing campaigns, representing a departure from previous launch cycles where the standard base model anchored holiday sales. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/12/apple-skipping-iphone-18-launch-this-year/))
- **Leaked "iPhone Ultra" Screen Protectors Confirm Asymmetric Foldable Design**: **Screen protectors for Apple's upcoming foldable device, internally referred to by everyone at Apple as the "iPhone Ultra," have leaked online and confirmed several key design details.** The accessories reveal **asymmetric corners**, with square corners on the side adjacent to the hinge and rounded corners on the opposite outer edge. The leak also corroborates **shorter, wider proportions** for the cover screen and points to an **off-center single punch-hole cutout** for the selfie camera, which sits in the top-right corner of the outer display. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/12/iphone-ultra-screen-protectors-reveal-asymmetry/))
- **Apple Cuts OLED Panel Pricing to Mitigate Skyrocketing Memory Costs**: **Apple has successfully negotiated lower OLED display pricing from suppliers LG and Samsung for its upcoming iPhone 18 Pro line, aiming to offset a massive surge in RAM production costs.** Under the new agreement, Apple will pay around **\$68 per OLED screen for the iPhone 18 Pro Max**, a sharp decrease from the \$110 to \$120 spent on the iPhone 17 Pro Max display. This aggressive price reduction became necessary due to skyrocketing prices for RAM chips, which Apple CEO Tim Cook likened to a **"100-year flood"** caused by chip manufacturers prioritizing AI data centers. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/12/iphone-18-pro-lower-oled-display-cost-report/))
- **Apple Pursues Multi-Million-Dollar Publisher Deals to Enhance Siri AI**: **Apple is reportedly negotiating multiyear agreements worth up to nine figures with major publishers to grant its forthcoming Siri AI better access to current events and news.** The proposed variable compensation plans would pay partner publishers when their content is utilized by the AI. This licensing push occurs as publishers increasingly decide whether to litigate against AI scraping or strike **multi-million-dollar deals** to authorize training usage. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/12/report-apple-seeks-publisher-deals-to-give-siri-ai-better-access-to-current-events/))
- **Supreme Court Grants 24-Hour Stay in App Store Anti-Steering Battle**: **Supreme Court Justice Elena Kagan has granted Apple an emergency 24-hour stay in its ongoing legal battle with Epic Games over alternative payment linking.** The pause extends Apple's deadline to propose App Store commission structures to a California district court after Apple was found in contempt for willfully violating an anti-steering injunction. Apple is seeking to have the Supreme Court overturn the contempt ruling, hoping to avoid setting a **court-established fee rate** that regulators worldwide could use as a reference point. ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/12/app-store-fee-fight-24-hour-delay/))
- **Foxconn's Apple Revenue Share Drops Below 29% Amid Massive AI Server Expansion**: **Foxconn’s revenue share from Apple has permanently fallen below 29%, a significant decline from the era when Apple contract manufacturing accounted for more than half of its business.** This structural shift is driven entirely by Foxconn's rapid expansion in building **AI servers**, with its cloud and networking division capturing **51% of its Q2 2026 revenue**. Despite Apple's concurrent efforts to diversify its assembly locations, Foxconn's primary growth is now firmly anchored in the booming AI infrastructure market. ([Source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/12/foxconn-used-to-make-half-its-revenue-from-apple-now-its-below-29/))
