# Apple Earnings Smash Records as iPhone 17 Soars and Hardware Shortages Loom — 2026-04-30

## Highlights
Today's news is dominated by Apple's historic Q2 2026 earnings report, posting a staggering $111.2 billion in revenue fueled by record-breaking iPhone 17 sales and massive services growth. Meanwhile, enthusiasts are buzzing over incoming CEO John Ternus's debut and Apple's looming AI roadmap, even as severe supply constraints hit the Mac mini and Mac Studio. Beyond the financials, intriguing rumors point toward a new "AirPods Ultra" line and significant Apple Health upgrades coming in iOS 27.

## Top Stories
- **[Apple Reports Record-Breaking Q2 2026 Results](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/04/30/apple-2q-2026-earnings/)**: Apple announced its best March quarter ever with $111.2 billion in revenue and $29.6 billion in net profit, driven by double-digit growth across all geographic segments. The iPhone 17 lineup was crowned the most popular in the company's history, while the Mac install base reached a new all-time record. 
- **[John Ternus Makes First Appearance on Earnings Call](https://9to5mac.com/2026/04/30/john-ternus-joins-apples-q2-2026-earnings-call-touts-incredible-roadmap-ahead/)**: Incoming CEO John Ternus joined Tim Cook on the Q2 earnings call, marking his first public appearance since the leadership transition was announced. Ternus teased an "incredible roadmap ahead" and praised Cook's financial discipline, ensuring investors that Apple's strategic momentum will continue when he takes over in September. 
- **[Apple Says Mac Studio and Mac Mini Will Be in Short Supply for Months](https://9to5mac.com/2026/04/30/apple-mac-mini-studio-supply-constraints/)**: Apple CEO Tim Cook confirmed that ongoing supply shortages for the Mac mini and Mac Studio will persist for several months. The delay is attributed to unexpected demand from users seeking capable platforms for AI and agentic tools, compounded by constraints in advanced node manufacturing.
- **['AirPods Ultra’ Rumored to Feature a Major Upgrade](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/04/30/airpods-ultra-major-upgrade-rumor/)**: Reports indicate Apple is developing a high-end "AirPods Ultra" equipped with infrared cameras to feed computer vision data to Siri. Expected to be positioned above the AirPods Pro, these earbuds would enhance visual intelligence features on the iPhone but are not expected to support hand gestures. 
- **[iOS 27 to Bring Major Apple Health Upgrades](https://9to5mac.com/2026/04/30/ios-26-4-improved-apples-health-app-and-bigger-upgrades-are-coming-soon/)**: Apple is reportedly prepping significant updates for the Health app in iOS 27, including new nutrition logging capabilities integrated with AI. This follows recent additions in iOS 26.4, which brought back Blood Oxygen data to the Vitals screen and added new sleep tracking highlights.

With AI significantly lowering the cost of executing sophisticated, multi-layered social engineering attacks, users must rely on hardware security keys and independently verify all support communications. This piece is a must-read wake-up call for anyone relying on Apple's ecosystem for their daily security.
