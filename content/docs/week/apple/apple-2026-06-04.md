# WWDC 2026 Looms: Siri's Nvidia Infrastructure, iOS 27 Leaks, and MacBook Ultra Details — 2026-06-04

## Highlights
As we inch closer to WWDC 2026, the Apple ecosystem is buzzing with critical intelligence surrounding the company's hybrid approach to AI processing, which will leverage both on-device capabilities and Google Cloud infrastructure powered by Nvidia's advanced Blackwell chips. Hardware rumors are also accelerating, painting a clear picture of an upcoming LTPO+ equipped iPhone 18 Pro lineup and an OLED "MacBook Ultra" that promises to shift industry display standards.

## Top Stories
- **[Apple to use Google servers with Nvidia hardware for the new Siri](https://www.macworld.com/article/3156959/apple-to-use-google-servers-with-nvidia-hardware-for-the-new-siri.html)**: Apple's revamped Siri, debuting at WWDC, will rely on Google's fleet of Nvidia Blackwell B200 data center chips for queries requiring cloud-based processing. To maintain its privacy standards, Apple is enabling Nvidia's confidential compute feature, which encrypts data as it is processed.
- **[Leaker Says iOS 27 Won’t Be Compatible With These iPhones](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/04/ios-27-compatible-iphones-rumor/)**: According to leaker Instant Digital, iOS 27 will drop support for the iPhone 11 series and the second-generation iPhone SE. Furthermore, the advanced Apple Intelligence-powered Siri features are expected to remain exclusive to the iPhone 15 Pro and newer devices.
- **[New MacBook Ultra report covers release timing, display, more](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/04/new-macbook-ultra-report-covers-release-timing-display-more/)**: The upcoming OLED MacBook Pro, rumored to be branded as the "MacBook Ultra," may arrive as soon as the third quarter of 2026. It is expected to feature a hybrid OLED architecture combining oxide TFT and tandem OLED layers, offering significantly improved power efficiency and lifespan.
- **[App Store ecosystem generated $1.4 trillion in 2025, 90% commission-free](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/04/app-store-ecosystem-generated-1-4-trillion-in-2025-90-commission-free/)**: Apple highlighted a new study from Analysis Group revealing the App Store ecosystem facilitated a record $1.4 trillion in billings and sales in 2025. Notably, Apple collected no commission on more than 90 percent of those transactions, while apps utilizing consumer-facing AI saw 4x more growth.
- **[iPhone 18 Pro Max Device Thickness Allegedly Revealed](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/04/iphone-18-pro-max-device-thickness-revealed/)**: Leaker Ice Universe reports that the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro Max will measure 8.75mm thick, which is identical to its predecessor, contradicting earlier rumors of a thicker chassis. Despite the identical thickness, the device is expected to feature a larger 5,100 to 5,200 mAh battery, an upgraded variable aperture camera, and highly efficient LTPO+ OLED display technology.
- **[I still want an Apple Vision Air but would understand it being low priority](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/04/i-still-want-an-apple-vision-air-but-would-understand-it-being-low-priority/)**: Analyst Ming-Chi Kuo reports that Apple has shifted its resources away from a cheaper "Vision Air" headset, focusing exclusively on smart glasses with greater mass-market potential. While Mark Gurman suggests a lighter headset is still in the works, it is not anticipated to launch before late 2028 or 2029.

**[Privacy may still be Apple’s savior when it comes to delayed AI features](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/04/privacy-may-still-be-apples-savior-when-it-comes-to-delayed-ai-features/)**
This editorial explores how Apple's meticulous, privacy-first approach could salvage its reputation following frustrating delays in delivering next-generation AI features. Unlike competitors who utilize user data aggressively to train their models, Apple's three-tiered processing strategy—on-device, Private Cloud Compute, and encrypted Google Cloud servers using Nvidia Blackwell chips—prioritizes user confidentiality. The author argues that as rival AI models inevitably face public privacy scandals, Apple's secure architecture will ultimately validate the long wait for Siri's overhaul.

**[Decoding “Developer” in a Changing App Landscape](https://www.macstories.net/linked/decoding-developer-in-a-changing-app-landscape/)**
MacStories highlights a fascinating surge in indie Mac app development, driven primarily by the integration of AI coding agents into the creative process. While coding by hand was once a strict prerequisite, modern AI tools have lowered the barrier to entry, allowing individuals with innovative ideas and product sense to build robust, native software. The piece notes that despite the influx of new apps, the overall signal-to-noise ratio in software quality has remained stable, proving that human creativity and problem-solving remain essential components of app creation in the AI era.

**[5+ New Features Coming in macOS 27](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/04/macos-27-features/)**
While iOS 27 has dominated the rumor cycle, macOS 27 is shaping up to be a critical "Snow Leopard-style" update focused heavily on bug fixes, stability, and performance. Beyond underlying optimizations, the update will introduce a dedicated Siri app modeled after the iOS counterpart, integrated AI writing tools, and natural language shortcuts. Notably, this release will mark the official end of the line for Intel Macs, requiring an M1 Apple silicon chip or newer, and signaling the imminent phase-out of Rosetta 2 support.
