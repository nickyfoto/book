## Engineering & Dev
**[Critical Vulnerability in BigAnt IM](https://mrxn.net/jswz/bigant-Pan-Upload-upload.html)**
A severe directory traversal and arbitrary file upload vulnerability has been discovered in the enterprise instant messaging system BigAnt (大蚂蚁). The flaw, located in the `Pan/Upload/upload` interface, allows malicious actors to upload crafted PHP files and execute arbitrary code, potentially leading to total server takeover and data theft.

**[The Evolution of AI-Driven Phishing](https://www.infoq.cn/article/U140AaPLg0t5dXxE8x7q)**
AI has drastically altered the cybersecurity landscape by industrializing targeted phishing operations, pushing the click rate of automated AI-generated lures to an alarming 54% compared to just 12% for traditional methods. By utilizing LLMs and deepfakes to automate reconnaissance, victim profiling, content generation, and real-time interaction, attackers can now execute highly sophisticated spear-phishing campaigns at a massive scale and a fraction of the historical cost.

**[Extracting "Vibe" with Multimodal Analytics](https://www.infoq.cn/article/v3lCjatWKZaTu7DhgoLq)**
Snowflake's Cortex AI is enabling organizations to turn previously unstructured media—such as video, audio, and images—into natively queryable assets. By automating the extraction of a content's "vibe," such as customer anger levels in call center audio or the emotional tension and background music in social media clips, engineering teams can build complex, multimodal agent workflows without moving their data outside the platform.
