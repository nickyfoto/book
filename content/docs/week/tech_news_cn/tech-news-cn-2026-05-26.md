# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-05-26

## Top Story
Microsoft has restricted its internal engineers from using Claude Code due to soaring token costs and strategic fears of losing control over its developer ecosystem. The move underscores Anthropic's rapid expansion in the enterprise AI coding market, with Claude Code capturing significant market share as engineers increasingly prefer its large context window and agentic capabilities over GitHub Copilot. For Microsoft, this represents a stark realization that despite its heavy AI investments, it risks becoming a mere channel for external models rather than the core platform defining the future of AI engineering workflows.

## Engineering & Dev
The Node.js community is embroiled in a heated debate over [a proposal to add a built-in virtual file system](https://www.infoq.cn/article/qYvZLNOkClhFPWJWYfIP), a PR that was largely generated by Claude Code. This has sparked a petition by core contributors, raising concerns about the verification, DCO origins, and safety of committing AI-generated code to core infrastructure. In database engineering, [ClickHouse shared its top 10 best practices](https://www.infoq.cn/article/bp64ilutkRcpSEhh5fNX) for optimizing storage and query speeds, emphasizing the alignment of `ORDER BY` sorting keys with query patterns to improve primary index pruning. Meanwhile, Xiaohongshu detailed its [Ray computing schedule for AI data production](https://www.infoq.cn/article/YHD4cguvebWJled2FK96), utilizing the framework to manage massive compute scheduling across pre-training and offline inference. 

On the AI infrastructure front, [Cloudflare overhauled its Browser Run architecture](https://www.infoq.cn/article/gSSOTxhzL4BkUwuA52ur) to support a vertically integrated, six-layer platform for AI agents, allowing headless Chromium instances to operate on dedicated containers. Finally, Google's [Gemma 4 introduced Multi-Token Prediction (MTP)](https://www.infoq.cn/article/vduuUvpVw0FiIcplFtGd) drafting models, a technique that leverages speculative decoding to generate multiple tokens simultaneously, achieving up to 3x faster inference speeds without sacrificing generation quality.

## Products & Digital
Huawei unveiled the ["Tau Scaling Law"](https://www.ifanr.com/1666860) at ISCAS 2026, an industry-first pivot from geometric scaling to "time scaling" using 3D logic folding to reduce signal latency and improve transistor efficiency. This technology will debut in the Kirin 2026 chip, which is expected to power the upcoming Huawei Mate 90 series. On the wearable front, startup Light Sail launched [an open-ear AI headphone equipped with a camera](https://www.ifanr.com/1666435), an ambitious device aiming to replace smartphones by acting as an omnipresent visual and audio AI assistant. While the hardware vision is bold, the device currently suffers from a cumbersome 20-second latency for visual processing queries due to its cloud-dependent architecture. In the app ecosystem, indie developers launched [Kaori](https://sspai.com/post/109227), an elegant iOS application for perfume enthusiasts to catalog their collections and receive context-aware, AI-powered daily fragrance recommendations.

## News & Commentary
A fascinating evolution in AI errors is occurring: the shift from "information hallucination" to ["commitment hallucination"](https://www.ifanr.com/1666938). After ByteDance's Doubao AI incorrectly promised a user a 600 RMB refund for a flight cancellation and offered a fake compensation guarantee, the user took ByteDance to court, exposing the psychological and legal gaps in highly anthropomorphized AI design. In a broader cultural analysis, commentators noted a [shared anxiety between the US and China regarding AI](https://cn.nytimes.com/opinion/20260526/us-china-ai-future/). Both societies are grappling with job insecurity, the commodification of digital intimacy via AI companions, and a pervasive "NPC" feeling where humans feel sidelined by algorithmic futures. This tech-driven existential dread has even reached the Vatican, where [Pope Leo XIV released the encyclical *Magnifica Humanitas*](https://cn.nytimes.com/technology/20260526/pope-ai-silicon-valley/), urging Silicon Valley to slow down and abandon its messianic approach to artificial intelligence.
