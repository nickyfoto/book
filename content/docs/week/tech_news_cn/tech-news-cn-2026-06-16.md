# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-06-16

## Top Story
DeepSeek has successfully completed a massive 50 billion RMB ($7.4 billion) funding round, pushing its valuation past $50 billion with a highly unusual deal structure. Strategic investors like Tencent and CATL have committed to a five-year lockup period and hold no voting rights, allowing CEO Liang Wenfeng to maintain absolute control of the company through a limited partnership. This extraordinary arrangement highlights the immense negotiating leverage held by top-tier AI firms in the current capital market. 

## Engineering & Dev
**[SiliconFlow Series B Funding](https://www.infoq.cn/article/6plxDB37CLvi5xWT0Abb)**: AI infrastructure provider SiliconFlow has raised over 2 billion RMB in its Series B round, reflecting a strategic industry shift from foundational model training to scaling model inference capabilities. 

**[Microsoft Open-Sources pg_durable](https://www.infoq.cn/article/iC6rdCT79aKOaG5mp0Tx)**: Microsoft released a new PostgreSQL extension named `pg_durable`, which allows developers to run fault-tolerant, durable workflows natively within the database. By defining workflows directly in SQL, it removes the need to stitch together external task queues, cron jobs, and orchestrators. 

**[Ant Group's AI R&D Pipeline](https://www.infoq.cn/article/5Ke1HD7F6R7aYr4jERyk)**: At AICon Shanghai, Ant Group shared insights into its transition from individual AI coding assistants to a comprehensive, specification-driven AI research and development pipeline. Their approach uses a "Software Design Document (SDD) driven" framework to help new developers quickly produce high-quality code across repositories. 

**[Pinterest's URL Deduplication](https://www.infoq.cn/article/ptleqt2rgIC96zXp5Tzu)**: Pinterest engineers introduced MIQPS, a data-driven URL normalization system designed to optimize deduplication across millions of domains. The system identifies and retains crucial query parameters while stripping away noise, significantly reducing infrastructure costs during data crawling. 

**[Google Gemma 4 12B](https://www.infoq.cn/article/7djN3gq1MaqGitDAPkhe)**: Google has launched Gemma 4 12B, featuring an innovative encoder-free architecture that pipes multimodal data directly into the large language model. This approach eliminates the latency and memory fragmentation typically caused by separate audio and visual encoders, paving the way for on-device autonomous AI workflows.

**[618 Gaming Mouse Guide](https://sspai.com/post/111065)**: As the 618 shopping festival reaches its peak, the peripheral market shows signs of maturity, with high-performance gaming mice becoming more affordable. ## News & Commentary
**[China's Role in Stalling Global Oil Prices](https://cn.nytimes.com/business/20260616/china-oil-iran/)**: Despite the shock to global energy supplies caused by the Iran war, global oil prices have not surged to extreme highs, largely due to China's strategic reduction in oil imports. By tapping into its massive strategic reserves and leaning on its rapidly expanding renewable energy and EV infrastructure, Beijing successfully buffered the domestic economy from the crisis, inadvertently stabilizing the global market.

**[China's Consumer Spending Slowdown](https://cn.nytimes.com/business/20260617/china-retail-sales/)**: Data from May reveals that China's retail sales unexpectedly dropped by 0.6% year-over-year, marking the first decline since the country abandoned its zero-COVID policies in late 2022. The slump points to a deepening reluctance among households to spend, exacerbated by the ongoing real estate crisis. 

**[Rio 3.5 Model Controversy](https://www.ifanr.com/1669019)**: A Brazilian open-source model named Rio 3.5 Open 397B is facing intense scrutiny within the AI community. The Nex-AGI team released a mathematical analysis demonstrating that Rio 3.5 is essentially a "stitched" wrapper created via model merging, comprising approximately 60% of Nex N2 Pro and 40% of Alibaba's Qwen 3.5.
