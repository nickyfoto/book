# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-06-29

## Top Story
**[Acquired Just a Year Ago, Founder Yangqing Jia Leaves Nvidia Amid AI Infra Clashes](https://www.infoq.cn/article/VceiPBYzkY9lSAOubvtP)**
Less than a year after Nvidia acquired LeptonAI for $700 million, founder and AI framework pioneer Yangqing Jia has reportedly departed. The split highlights a fundamental clash over Nvidia's reluctance to open-source the DGX Lepton platform as promised, and underscores a broader industry shift where the rapid rise of Agentic Coding is threatening the core value proposition of pure AI infrastructure abstraction layers.
