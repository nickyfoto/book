# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-08-06

## Top Story
The global artificial intelligence landscape was shaken by a massive executive restructuring at Google, where Demis Hassabis stepped down from daily operations at Google DeepMind to become Alphabet's Chief Scientist, handing active leadership of Gemini and Core AI research to Koray Kavukcuoglu. Simultaneously, Google's legendary chief scientist Jeff Dean, alongside pioneering co-founders Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le, officially left the search giant to launch **Discovery Loop**, an independent, public-benefit AI venture aimed at automating the scientific method. This historic breakup marks the end of Google’s classic engineering brain trust and reflects growing organizational friction inside Big Tech as the generative AI race shifts from experimental research to raw product integration.

## Engineering & Dev

*   **The Post-Google Era and the Rise of Discovery Loop:** Google’s internal shakeup has centralized productized model development under new DeepMind senior vice president **[Koray Kavukcuoglu](https://www.ifanr.com/1673901)**, an elite NYU machine learning alumnus and Yann LeCun's former PhD student who has earned a reputation inside the company as a tactical "empire builder" capable of resolving friction among brilliant research minds. Meanwhile, Jeff Dean’s newly founded startup, **[Discovery Loop](https://www.infoq.cn/article/x5sIQ2lED2h6G2Uvyu48)**, has secured backing from Khosla Ventures, Radical Ventures, and Google itself, aiming to build a closed-loop "automated scientist" system that runs parallelized simulation-and-verification loops to speed up machine learning, chip design, and biochemistry.
*   **The Radical AI Coding Ideology of Boris Cherny:** In a striking new podcast discussion, **[Claude Code and Boris Cherny's Philosophy](https://www.infoq.cn/article/hUTEb0LSzFUTyib1AJ4W)** reveals a deep shift in software engineering where Cherny encourages developers to "unlearn" (反学习) traditional determinism and treat LLMs like organic, living creatures. He recommends a radical rule of thumb: completely delete your `CLAUDE.md`, Skills, and Hooks files every six months to let the latest model adapt naturally, a strategy Anthropic itself used by erasing 80% of Claude Code's system prompts upon launching the highly agentic Opus 5 model.
*   **Kuaishou's Struggle with the 'Organization Wall':** Chinese short-video titan Kuaishou disclosed how it scaled its AI-Native developer ecosystem to over 10,000 engineers. Their whitepaper on the **[Kuaishou's 'AI Productivity' Framework](https://www.infoq.cn/article/Gj6j8QtZhgeuqedeUEft)** shows that as more developers participate in a single software requirement, the marginal efficiency gains from AI actually diminish due to human-to-human coordination overhead. To break through this bottleneck, Kuaishou shifted its goal from "developer velocity" to "AI Productivity," transforming traditional siloed teams into an AI-Native "triangle" of Product Development Engineers (PDEs), Forward Deployment Engineers (FDEs), and full-stack developers.
*   **Database Architectures for Millions of AI Apps:** Ant Group's database arm shared its design for storing massive amounts of agent-generated data. In **[OceanBase and Ant Group's Lingguang AI](https://www.infoq.cn/article/ix9IT7I9LmH2X0w5xQck)**, engineers detailed how they support the "Lingguang" platform—which has spawned over 30 million user-generated "flash apps" (闪应用)—by utilizing a unique "logical table" framework that shares a single physical storage layer while mapping distinct JSON schemas to relational tables, bypassing the physical overhead of creating millions of database tables.

## News & Commentary

*   **The Generational Battle Over Secondhand Smoke:** In a sharp sociological analysis, **[The Battle Against Secondhand Smoke in China](https://cn.nytimes.com/china/20260806/china-cigarette-smoking/)** examines the rising tension between younger, health-conscious citizens and older, entrenched smokers in public spaces. With over 50% of adult Chinese men smoking compared to less than 2% of women, young people are increasingly using smartphones and social media to record and confront indoor smokers. However, these grass-roots efforts run into weak enforcement and systemic roadblocks, as a national indoor smoking ban remains blocked by the powerful State Tobacco Monopoly Administration, whose tax revenues and profits account for roughly 7% of China's annual government budget.
*   **Africa’s Tech Ecosystem Embracing Open-Weight Chinese AI:** Emerging tech developers across Kenya, Uganda, and Nigeria are increasingly turning away from closed US models to build software on Chinese platforms. In **[African Developers Turn to Chinese AI Models](https://cn.nytimes.com/technology/20260806/ai-china-africa/)**, developers point out that using open-weight systems like Alibaba's Qwen or DeepSeek feels like "owning a home rather than renting one". This shift has been accelerated by US export and access restrictions, as well as the active outreach of Chinese tech giants providing free cloud compute and technical assistance.
*   **The In-Person Keynote Coronation of John Ternus:** Following the announcement that John Ternus will officially take over as Apple's CEO on September 1st, **[The Apple Keynote 'Live Human' Transition](https://www.ifanr.com/1674187)** discusses the significance of Apple's upcoming hardware launch. Breaking with the perfectly edited, pre-recorded video format that Apple has relied on since the 2020 pandemic, the tech giant is inviting retail employees and press to a live, unscripted in-person unveiling. This high-stakes return to live presenting is a crucial test of Ternus's charisma and "live-human feel" (活人感) as he steps up to establish his own authority and vision.
