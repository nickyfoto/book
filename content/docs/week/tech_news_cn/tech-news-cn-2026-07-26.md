# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-07-26

## Top Story
A deeply concerning medical ethics scandal has surfaced involving a fatal gene-editing trial at Shanghai Xinhua Hospital. A 6-year-old girl died shortly after receiving an experimental treatment for a rare genetic disorder, leading the Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine to launch a formal investigation into the research team led by Qiu Zilong. The case, which was not publicly disclosed beforehand, raises major red flags around clinical risk assessment, medical ethics, and the transparency of experimental human trials in China.

## Products & Digital
To help readers survive the brutal summer heat, sspai published a highly practical guide, **[手冲咖啡指北：天太热了，来做一杯不寡淡的冰手冲吧](https://sspai.com/post/112421)**, on brewing iced pour-over coffee. The core premise is that ice must be calculated into the initial brewing ratio rather than added as an afterthought to prevent the coffee from turning into "coffee-flavored ice water". The guide details two beginner-friendly approaches: the "375 iced pour method" using a standard V60 dripper and an immersion method utilizing a Clever Dripper. For the best results, the author recommends choosing light-to-medium roast washed beans with distinct citrus or stone fruit notes, avoiding heavily fermented beans that might mask the desired refreshing summer profile. When it comes to the ice itself, standard square silicone ice cube trays are preferred over fancy spherical molds for easier weight calculation and melting control.
