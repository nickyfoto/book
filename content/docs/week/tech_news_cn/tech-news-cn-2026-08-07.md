# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-08-07

## Top Story
The Shanghai Stock Exchange IPO of **Unitree Robotics (宇树科技)**, priced at 150.80 RMB per share, values the company at approximately 61 billion RMB (\$8.5 billion USD). Backed by high-profile strategic investors including the National Social Security Fund, CNPC, and DeepSeek, Unitree's public listing marks a major milestone as the first humanoid robotics company to debut on China's A-share market. This IPO serves as a crucial barometer for capital market sentiment toward the rapidly evolving but commercially uncertain embodied AI industry.
