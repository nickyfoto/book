# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-05-21

## Top Story
Alibaba released its next-generation flagship model, Qwen3.7-Max, signaling a decisive industry pivot from conversational LLMs toward task-executing autonomous agents. Topping domestic benchmarks in the Arena blind tests, the model boasts significant improvements in coding, tool utilization, and long-context processing. Most notably, Qwen3.7-Max autonomously optimized a production-grade attention kernel over 35 continuous hours, underscoring Alibaba's ambition to position its Model-as-a-Service platform as a critical enterprise infrastructure for the Agentic era.

## Engineering & Dev
The transition from toy API calls to enterprise-grade AI agents is fraught with engineering hurdles. [企业级Agent 落地，绕不开的 4 个工程问题](https://www.infoq.cn/article/qKW5Yu1ORiqMmX6mlLJ6) details the four unavoidable challenges for enterprise deployments: executing sandbox constraints, persisting agent memory, reining in soaring token costs, and governing multi-agent chaos. Part of this governance challenge is highlighted in [AI Agent 最大的问题：它在企业里只是个“无名之辈”](https://www.infoq.cn/article/KMlTCRBIWmlaZLSWks6A), where Snowflake argues that assigning robust identities to agents is mandatory for auditing and regulatory compliance. Meanwhile, the developer community is witnessing a new cult of "Tokenmaxxing," where blowing massive token budgets on AI is seen as a badge of honor, even as critics debate whether it yields true productivity or just bloated code. Ruan Yifeng also observes the broader macroeconomic impact of this AI boom in [科技爱好者周刊（第 397 期）：财富正在向 AI 集中](http://www.ruanyifeng.com/blog/2026/05/weekly-issue-397.html), noting how massive wealth is rapidly centralizing around AI supply chains, particularly memory chip manufacturers.

On the tooling front, autonomous agent infrastructure is maturing rapidly. [OpenAI开源Symphony：面向自主编码智能体编排的SPEC规范文档](https://www.infoq.cn/article/kmcvx8qNTQRYpPHVDq4B) introduces OpenAI's Symphony, an open-source specification using project management boards to coordinate autonomous coding agents rather than relying on human-managed interactive sessions. Taking agent autonomy a step further, [Cloudflare与Stripe推出新协议，让AI智能体创建账号、购买域名和进行生产部署](https://www.infoq.cn/article/TbgvhdcciqULlEEmFBbU) covers a joint protocol allowing AI agents to securely create cloud accounts, buy domains, and deploy apps without human intervention. In the hardware ecosystem, Moore Threads is making aggressive moves to capture developers, highlighted in [从兼容 CUDA 到自我进化，摩尔线程想用 MUSA 解决真正的难题](https://www.infoq.cn/article/Oi49yHUTNwABaK5kWjWG), detailing how its MUSA architecture now achieves 100% CUDA compatibility and native integration with major frameworks like SGLang and vLLM to lower the migration barrier for Chinese GPU adopters.
