# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-07-10

## Top Story
OpenAI has officially launched the GPT-5.6 series, triggering a massive wave of discussion across the Chinese tech ecosystem. By retiring the standalone Codex app and launching the new "ChatGPT Work" agent, OpenAI is shifting the paradigm from AI as a mere coding copilot to an autonomous execution engine capable of handling long-horizon workflows. Chinese developers and analysts are closely examining the new ultra and max reasoning modes, noting that the model dramatically lowers the unit cost of intelligence and redefines the economic threshold for deploying autonomous agents in production.

## Engineering & Dev
A legendary engineering feat is making waves in the Chinese developer community: **[The Most High-Profile AI Rewrite: Claude Ports Bun in 11 Days](https://www.infoq.cn/article/uHkOoJ6Nfm6wNCsUryuO)**. Jarred Sumner utilized 64 concurrent Claude instances across 50 dynamic workflows to rewrite the JavaScript runtime from Zig to Rust, completing a massive 1-million-line migration in under two weeks. While it successfully squashed severe memory leak issues, the community is debating the long-term maintainability of an AI-generated codebase containing 13,000 `unsafe` Rust blocks.

However, not everyone is sold on AI taking the wheel. A sharp critique on sspai, **[In the Vibe Coding Era, Why Cost Reduction Doesn't Equal Efficiency](https://sspai.com/post/111975)**, argues that relying on LLMs without rigorous architectural planning accelerates codebase rot. The author posits that the "Vibe Coding" trend empowers PMs and designers to generate apps instantly, but the resulting "patchwork development" creates fragile structures that collapse under real-world requirements. 

Over in **[Technology Enthusiast Weekly (Issue 403)](http://www.ruanyifeng.com/blog/2026/07/weekly-issue-403.html)**, Ruan Yifeng highlights the ballooning costs of AI coding. Drawing on recent data, he notes that at leading AI firms, the compute cost per engineer is now 2.3 times their base salary, making AI a net-negative investment unless developer output increases symmetrically. He also highlighted the release of TypeScript 7.0, which was rewritten in Go for a 10x speed boost, and offered a post-mortem on why Dropbox stagnated by targeting time-killing consumers instead of efficiency-seeking enterprises.

The sheer volume of AI-generated code is breaking traditional Git infrastructure, as covered in **[GitHub's Old Model Pierced by AI: Cursor, GitLab, and Zed Enter the Fray](https://www.infoq.cn/article/7ZSdewTwDBz1mrx6wmat)**. With GitHub groaning under the weight of 17 million AI-generated pull requests per month, competitors like Cursor's "Origin" project, GitLab's "Switch", and Zed's "DeltaDB" are fundamentally rethinking version control to handle agent-scale workloads rather than human-speed commits.

## Products & Digital
In consumer hardware, **[Laifen Factory Visit: Shedding the 'Dyson Dupe' Label](https://www.ifanr.com/1671565)** explores how the domestic appliance maker is pivoting from a lightweight assembly model to heavy manufacturing. By investing over 500 million RMB in a Zhuhai mega-factory to vertically integrate custom metal motor frames and precision shaver foils, Laifen is attempting to reclaim product definition power and justify its premium pricing without relying on Dyson comparisons.

On the automotive front, Chery is shifting from traditional engineering to aesthetics with the **[Chery Fengyun A9 EV Pre-sale](https://www.ifanr.com/1671578)**. Priced aggressively from 115.9k RMB, the vehicle features a 655km CLTC range standard across all trims, shifting the competitive focus away from battery gating toward advanced autonomous driving packages and cultural design elements. 

Meanwhile, Baidu is redefining enterprise workflows with the launch of its updated Agent ecosystem, detailed in **[Baidu Dazi Redefines the Agent 'Delegation Radius'](https://www.ifanr.com/1671618)**. The platform focuses on expanding the trust boundary from personal task automation to organization-wide asset reuse, integrating closely with Baidu's newly announced partner alliance to ensure robust, enterprise-grade delivery.

For photography enthusiasts, Sony has surprisingly released the **[Sony RX10 V](https://www.ifanr.com/1671523)** for 15,199 RMB. The ultra-telephoto bridge camera boasts a 1-inch stacked sensor, 24-600mm equivalent lens, and a new AI subject recognition autofocus system that handles humans, animals, and vehicles at 60 calculations per second.
