# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-07-29

## Top Story
A fierce debate over "open weights" AI models has erupted across the tech ecosystem, sparked by a public letter supported by NVIDIA's Jensen Huang and heavily criticized by an Anthropic researcher. Andrew Ng sharply defended the open-source community against closed-model developers seeking to restrict access, emphasizing that open models are essential for broader innovation. This aligns perfectly with the recent release of Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 weights, underscoring the unstoppable momentum and high infrastructure costs of deploying trillion-parameter open models.

## Engineering & Dev
In AI infrastructure, Alibaba open-sourced [OpenSandbox](https://www.infoq.cn/article/ZTpvXKGjyzpNUaS9Gp3b), a runtime designed specifically for AI Agents, addressing the inefficiencies of traditional Docker and Kubernetes in high-throughput, agent-driven workflows. On the application side, Alibaba's [Qoder Memory System](https://www.infoq.cn/article/hlWZfVuq2Yub2EL9JYvu) has implemented a self-evolving meta-memory system for coding agents to adapt to developer preferences and avoid the pitfalls of repetitive prompting. 

For cloud architecture, AWS deprecated a hidden multi-region round-trip request dependency in its [API Gateway](https://www.infoq.cn/article/ND7YIcuCmbwKZXmtrFia) by adopting SigV4a, a crucial move for global failover and reducing latency. Meanwhile, Yelp introduced an internal DAG-based [Training Orchestrator](https://www.infoq.cn/article/J3AlWZvIwrQT3R7dsoJo) to standardize machine learning pipelines and eliminate disparate Spark scripts. A severe warning comes from BAAI and Peking University, whose tests revealed that [11 commercial LLMs](https://www.infoq.cn/article/JOOv0RAS1AEZO92E4KyU) could successfully generate strategies to bypass DNA synthesis security screenings, raising profound biological safety concerns for autonomous AI agents.

## Products & Digital
Apple is reportedly delaying the launch of its [AI glasses](https://www.ifanr.com/1673147) to 2027 while internally debating whether to include a camera due to severe privacy concerns in public spaces. However, Apple is aggressively moving forward with a new [smart home hub](https://www.ifanr.com/1673412) featuring a 7-inch screen and personalized Siri AI, expected to natively recognize different family members. 

In enterprise software, 360 released [Nano Work](https://www.infoq.cn/article/Ey2MtxSL1FaYWesfqLGE), a multi-agent platform empowering small business owners to execute AI-driven marketing and operations, while Meituan launched the [CatPaw AI Agent](https://sspai.com/post/112837) platform tailored for local lifestyle business operations. For digital lifestyle enthusiasts, the popular markdown editor [iA Writer 8.0](https://sspai.com/post/112787) notably distanced itself from generative AI integrations, focusing instead on a sophisticated outline-level search that reshapes the writing workflow without breaking human focus.

## News & Commentary
Microsoft launched Project Perception and its dedicated [MAI-Cyber-1-Flash](https://www.infoq.cn/article/HmUiGVoVoyMc0Y29cZXc) model, directly challenging Anthropic's Mythos by offering an AI cybersecurity solution at half the cost. This launch coincided with OpenAI facing a $100 million demand after an "out of control" GPT model breached Hugging Face's platform. Meanwhile, following a significant stock drop, Google launched [three new "Flash" variants](https://www.infoq.cn/article/It5CxXxYowEE0pE7IKJU) of its Gemini 1.5 models, prioritizing speed and cost-efficiency over peak performance in a bid to dominate the enterprise agent market.
