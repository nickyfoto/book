# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-07-01

## Top Story
AI is actively reshaping the organizational structure of major Chinese tech companies, obliterating the traditional boundaries between frontend and backend engineering. As AI coding tools mature, enterprises are pivoting to demand "AI full-stack" developers who can manage end-to-end product delivery, transforming engineering roles from code execution to system-level problem solving.

## Engineering & Dev
**[Frontend and Backend Roles Disappearing](https://www.infoq.cn/article/rHiSH66JZwoQG5Dfvv6x)**: InfoQ reports that Chinese tech giants like Meituan and Alibaba are structurally merging frontend and backend teams. With AI tools handling routine coding, companies now prioritize engineers who can command the entire AI technology stack, marking a shift from specialized coders to end-to-end builders.

**[AI Agents as the New Database Users](https://www.infoq.cn/article/1f1PdxW2458qx5gPfyeg)**: InfoQ highlights OceanBase's new lake-house AI database architecture, designed for a world where AI agents, not just humans, continuously read and execute business decisions. This shift requires databases to provide real-time, consistent multimodal context rather than just static tabular data.

**[Slack's Multi-Cloud AI Architecture](https://www.infoq.cn/article/GMuuiabHp38rbydKJVqW)**: Slack has detailed its transition from self-hosted Amazon SageMaker to a multi-cloud setup using Amazon Bedrock and Google Cloud Vertex AI. By abstracting deployment across vendors, Slack improved inference quality by 10% and cut short-prompt latency by 67%.

**[Cloudflare Fixes QUIC Congestion Bug](https://www.infoq.cn/article/CHON5sllKKAMBg309xiZ)**: Cloudflare engineers uncovered a fatal recovery loop in their Rust-based `quiche` library's CUBIC congestion control implementation. Triggered by high packet loss during initial connections, the bug was elegantly resolved with a nearly one-line fix targeting how idle time is calculated.

**[Argo CD 3.5 Bolsters Supply Chain Security](https://www.infoq.cn/article/MfHAiaeQbq6VWPHLD4pB)**: The new Argo CD v3.5 release candidate introduces mandatory mutual TLS (mTLS) for internal components and native Git commit signature verification. These upgrades directly address longstanding vulnerabilities in enterprise GitOps workflows.
