# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-05-27

## Top Story
Huawei has officially introduced a new semiconductor development principle called the "Tau (τ) Law" to bypass traditional physical process limits. Facing external sanctions and the end of Moore's Law, Huawei shifts the focus from geometric scaling to "time scaling," reducing signal delay through architectural innovations like "LogicFolding". This approach aims to achieve a 1.4nm-equivalent transistor density within five years, with the upcoming Kirin chip being the first to debut this technology in mass production.

## Engineering & Dev
In the realm of AI agents, Poizon (得物) algorithm expert Ren Juwei is set to share their engineering practices for highly controllable customer service agents at the upcoming [AICon Shanghai](https://www.infoq.cn/article/ixyk6VIFhTSF3JL63EgK), detailing the shift from rigid workflows to LLM-driven responses. Researchers from Peking University and StarV have released the EQA-Decision dataset and RoboDecision framework, boasting over 4 million multi-modal Q&A pairs. This open-source training pipeline pushes embodied AI from static perception to dynamic decision-making, notably outperforming GPT-5 on instant decision tasks.

On the data architecture front, [Google Cloud](https://www.infoq.cn/article/kadDStA9JWuOGHujwdoz) has extended BigQuery to natively support Apache Iceberg, eliminating the need for data duplication across different processing engines. For developer workflows, there are detailed technical guides on building self-service report data marts using [Snowflake Semantic View](https://www.infoq.cn/article/a4gbB5g49HBNl2mwXwXY) and utilizing Trace data to monitor the performance and token consumption of [Cortex Agent clusters](https://www.infoq.cn/article/95coakUO0KZrqvL21Ig9). Finally, Transwarp's (星环科技) distributed database [ArgoDB V6](https://www.infoq.cn/article/1w093X0R3Ul2u1RmbM44) has officially passed the national security and reliability assessment, cementing its role as an integrated HTAP and multi-model data platform for critical enterprise infrastructure.

## Products & Digital
For users frustrated by the privacy risks of screen-recording tools like Microsoft's Recall, [ScreenMemo](https://sspai.com/post/110115) (屏忆) offers an open-source, local-first alternative. This app captures screenshots, performs local OCR, and builds an AI-searchable context memory, allowing users to find lost information without sacrificing data sovereignty. Addressing the fatigue of AI-generated content, a sharp piece on [sspai](https://sspai.com/post/110102) critiques how Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) has sterilized LLM writing into repetitive "AI flavors". The author provides practical prompt engineering tips to force models like Claude and Gemini to drop their sycophantic tone and retain the author's distinct voice.

In consumer hardware, AR manufacturer XREAL has launched a youth-focused sub-brand called xbx, debuting the 62g a01 AR glasses starting at a highly competitive 1699 RMB. Reviewers also praised the [RayNeo GT Max](https://www.ifanr.com/1667059) for delivering a 267-inch Dolby Vision virtual screen in a pocketable format. Meanwhile, OPPO's [ColorOS 16](https://www.ifanr.com/1667076) is being lauded for its subtle and practical AI integrations, such as seamless video summaries and dynamic foreign menu translations formatted as WeChat mini-programs. In the mechanical keyboard community, custom builds continue to embrace eccentric themes, from the dog-meme-inspired [GMK Gregory 2](https://sspai.com/post/108618) to the retro Game Boy aesthetic of the KeyBoy40.

## News & Commentary
Samsung successfully averted a massive 40,000-employee strike by reaching a last-minute deal to allocate 10.5% of its semiconductor operating profits to a special performance bonus pool. This labor dispute highlights the exploding demand for High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) in the AI era, where the entire global supply chain relies heavily on a few giants like SK Hynix, Samsung, and Micron.

In sociopolitical analysis, the [New York Times](https://cn.nytimes.com/china/20260527/china-smoking-economy/) unpacks China's stalled anti-smoking initiatives. Despite President Xi Jinping's personal commitment to quitting, the state tobacco monopoly's massive tax contributions—which rival the national defense budget—have continuously hindered the implementation of a national indoor smoking ban. The Times also reported on the harrowing journey of Chinese dissident [Dong Guangping](https://cn.nytimes.com/china/20260527/china-dissident-dong-guangping-south-korea/), who, after multiple failed attempts and imprisonments, successfully escaped to South Korea via a rubber boat.
