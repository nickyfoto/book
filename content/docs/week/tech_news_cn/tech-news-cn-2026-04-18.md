# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-04-18

## Top Story
The Chinese AI landscape is experiencing significant tremors as [DeepSeek reportedly seeks its first external funding at a valuation of over $10 billion](https://www.ifanr.com/1662774). Facing mounting compute costs and a fierce talent war that recently saw core contributors to its V3 model poached by Xiaomi and ByteDance, founder Liang Wenfeng is shifting the previously self-funded strategy to fuel the delayed V4 model. 

## Engineering & Dev
The C++ ecosystem is gearing up for what is being called the most important update in a decade, as the [C++26 standard draft is now complete](https://www.infoq.cn/article/mTR5V1FNI2HdcxOxB8fW). Led by Herb Sutter, the update introduces powerful reflection capabilities without runtime overhead, memory safety enhancements that have already prevented thousands of bugs at Google, and a new `std::execution` framework for concurrency. In the open-source AI sphere, Google has released the [Gemma 4 model family under the Apache 2.0 license](https://www.infoq.cn/article/ry64GrNLAEEf1qdZABb0), featuring edge-friendly sizes and a 31B dense model that punches above its weight class with native multimodal and agentic workflows. Domestic AI developers are also pushing agent capabilities forward, with Zhipu launching a self-evolution mechanism for its [AutoClaw agent](https://www.ifanr.com/1662774) to solve the common "amnesia" problem by learning from user corrections and persisting memory. On the security front, developers using Tiandy's surveillance systems should note a critical [Easy7 SQL injection vulnerability](https://mrxn.net/jswz/easy7-rest-inquestRoom-getInquestIdByRoomId-sqli.html) found in its REST API, which allows attackers to execute arbitrary SQL commands and potentially tamper with databases.

## Products & Digital
In the EV market, Xiaomi's Lei Jun has officially [ruled out releasing a car under 100,000 RMB](https://www.ifanr.com/1662774), arguing that maintaining high-level smart capabilities at that price point will be practically impossible for the next decade. Meanwhile, the Chinese smartphone market saw a major shakeup in Q1 2026; [Huawei reclaimed the top spot with a 20% market share](https://www.ifanr.com/1662774) driven by the Mate 80 series, while Apple matched that growth through heavy subsidies, leaving Xiaomi with a steep 35% decline due to conservative pricing. For productivity and creative professionals, [Canva AI 2.0 has launched](https://www.ifanr.com/1662774) with a unified natural language interface capable of generating complete designs from a single prompt and maintaining persistent brand style memory. For hobbyists looking to step away from modern screens, sspai published a comprehensive [guide to becoming a licensed HAM radio operator](https://sspai.com/post/87807), detailing the exam process, frequency bands, and why the century-old amateur radio culture still thrives alongside the modern internet.

## News & Commentary
Tech blogger William Long shared a deeply personal ["debt repayment diary" documenting his battle with high blood pressure](https://www.williamlong.info/archives/7542.html). After ignoring early warning signs for years, he uploaded his medical reports to an AI model, which provided a stark "death countdown" alongside actionable lifestyle changes that ultimately helped him regain control alongside his prescribed medication. On the business side of the auto industry, a stark contrast in profitability was revealed: despite record production in the auto sector, [overall profit margins sat at a historic low of 4.1%](https://www.ifanr.com/1662774). Seres topped the charts by earning nearly 10,000 RMB per vehicle, whereas new-energy darling Nio is losing roughly 38,000 RMB per car sold. In the consumer protection space, [Chinese regulators fined seven major e-commerce and delivery platforms a staggering 3.597 billion RMB](https://www.ifanr.com/1662774) for failing to vet "ghost takeout" restaurants, resulting in the immediate suspension of new cake shop registrations across these networks.
