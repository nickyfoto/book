# Chinese Tech Daily — 2026-09-09

## Top Story
OpenAI announced that its internal AI system generated a candidate solution proving finite-time singularity formation for the 3D incompressible Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem, one of seven Millennium Prize Problems. The proof was generated by a multi-agent system coordinating approximately 10,000 concurrent AI agents over 88 hours and verified via Lean formalization. This milestone signifies a major shift where frontier AI models advance beyond basic code generation into pure mathematics and fundamental scientific research.

## Engineering & Dev
In developer tooling and AI infrastructure, **[pnpm 12](https://www.infoq.cn/article/h4M6YGm3G7Zph5QEIV5R)** was released after rewriting its TypeScript codebase in native Rust, delivering up to 90% faster package installations in cached environments while retaining full compatibility with existing node_modules layouts. On the cloud-native front, Tencent Cloud released **[Cube Sandbox v0.7.0](https://www.infoq.cn/article/qHiiKwM0Gj0mcqtugMSi)**, adding S3-backed state persistence to enable sub-60ms microVM sandboxes to pause on one Kubernetes node and resume on another without breaking running tasks. Meanwhile, Alibaba highlighted **[Open Code Review](https://www.infoq.cn/article/owxMsObP9h1wFcRqW000)** at QCon Shanghai, detailing how deterministic rule templates and multi-agent coordination processed over a million production tasks to eliminate token inflation and hallucinated code comments.

In enterprise systems architecture, China Merchants Bank published a case study on **[Unified GPU Infrastructure](https://www.infoq.cn/article/UN2Gt8aKT40ed5z52WuL)**, explaining how Kubernetes, Kueue, and KEDA allowed nearly 10,000 heterogenous AI cards to be shared between training and online inference, driving average GPU utilization from 35% to over 60% and cutting per-million-token costs by 60%. On personal engineering blogs, game systems architect Yun Feng shared **[Cloud Bean's Summer English Reading](https://blog.codingnow.com/2026/09/summer.html)**, reflecting on guiding his child through daily intensive reading sessions and advocating for continuous habit formation over rote vocabulary memorization.

## Products & Digital
In consumer hardware, Apple officially unveiled the **[iPhone Duo](https://sspai.com/post/114392)** as its first foldable smartphone, featuring a 7.6-inch inner Super Retina XDR display, an A20 Pro chip built on a 2nm process, and iOS 27 dual-screen split-view features starting at ¥15,999. For mobile power users, a long-term review titled **[A Year with the iPhone 17 Pro Max](https://sspai.com/post/114366)** examined the trade-offs of switching from compact iPhones, praising the flagship's multi-day battery life and optical camera capabilities while critiquing its 233-gram weight and awkward single-handed ergonomics.

In mobile software and photography gear, independent developer Matrix introduced **[Velastar](https://sspai.com/post/113668)**, an iOS star-gazing app that calculates real-time celestial maps, simulates night sky views directly from the surfaces of Mars, the Moon, or Europa, and incorporates offline light pollution maps for astro-photographers. Camera enthusiasts also saw Sony launch the **[FE 8-14mm F3.5 Fisheye G Lens](https://www.ifanr.com/1678959)**, expanding native E-mount wide-angle coverage down to an 8mm fisheye focal length for ¥9,999. In digital gaming culture, a retrospective titled **[Unnoticed Gaming Consoles in the Chinese World](https://sspai.com/post/113842)** documented 40 years of local hardware innovation, tracing a line from 1980s pioneers like VTech and Super A'Can to modern open-source handhelds like GPD Win.
