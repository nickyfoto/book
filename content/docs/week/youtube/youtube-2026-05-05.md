# YouTube — 2026-05-05

## Watch First
[Inside The Old Skydiving Plane Hunting Drones in Ukraine](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d0b6ECKU_Os) from the *New York Times* is a fascinating look at how modern warfare adapts on a budget. It covers how a crew of Ukrainian volunteers transformed a Soviet-era civilian skydiving plane into a drone-killing machine, using mounted machine guns to shoot down Iranian Shahed drones for just $500 in ammo per kill. It is a brilliant, gritty look at human ingenuity solving the asymmetrical cost problem of modern air defense.
