# YouTube — 2026-07-05

## Watch First
**[The Problem with Streaming — and the Case for Physical Media](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yUzvXA5HmtE)** makes a compelling argument for hanging onto your DVDs, using the fascinating history of Soviet bootleggers who cut forbidden jazz and rock records into discarded medical X-rays. It's a quick, thought-provoking TED talk about the dangers of handing unilateral control of culture and art to streaming conglomerates.
