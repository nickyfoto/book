# YouTube — 2026-08-21

## Watch First
If you only have time for a single video today, make it the New York Times’ fascinating and forensic investigation, **[Is the Sports Bubble Going to Pop? | Pablo Torre on The Daily](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hyhVMsakdE)**. ### Learning & Ideas
