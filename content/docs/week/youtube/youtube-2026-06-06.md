# YouTube — 2026-06-06

## Watch First
**[Why Nokia Missed the Smartphone Shift](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=82RoashNMsg)** from CNBC International is a brilliant post-mortem on how the world's once-dominant phone maker completely misread the industry's shift from hardware to software ecosystems. It's a stark reminder that efficiency, hardware design, and global scale mean nothing if you are suddenly playing by the wrong set of rules.
