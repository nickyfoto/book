# YouTube — 2026-03-28

## Watch First
[Why I’m Quitting Physics Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iz7sCKbW8KM) by Looking Glass Universe is a sobering, highly personal look at the exponential growth of AI tools, arguing that the era of AI fully replacing entry-level coding—and soon, intricate video production—is already here. It's a stark, must-watch perspective on tech-induced career anxiety that avoids the usual hype cycle to look at what is actually happening to knowledge work.
