# YouTube — 2026-09-06

## Watch First
If you only have fifteen minutes to spare today, you absolutely must spend them on **[How to Make Learning Impossibly Fun | Mark Rober | TED](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rUhllpnYWR8)**. The former NASA mechanical engineer delivers an incredibly engaging, heartfelt talk detailing his \$60 million mission to launch "Class Crunch Labs," a free, hands-on STEM curriculum for grades 3 through 8. Rober's core philosophy of "hiding the vegetables" focuses on capturing kids' hearts through spectacular, visceral experiences first—like throwing a water-balloon tower or a crash-test dummy—before ever introducing them to the dry physics equations behind the fun.
