# YouTube — 2026-06-14

## Watch First
**[The Man Who Worked At Subway, Then Solved An "Impossible" Problem](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8HBDE-msUjw)** by Veritasium. This is a wonderfully told, inspiring deep dive into the story of Yitang Zhang, a mathematician who spent years working odd jobs before making a breakthrough on the twin prime conjecture. It is a masterclass in making high-level math accessible and a deeply human story of persistence.
