# YouTube — 2026-08-14

## Watch First
**[Apple at 50: Stephen Haber on Quintessential American Innovation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L2i_aQsM_hk)** is an absolute masterclass for anyone interested in why some societies flourish while others stagnate. Rather than just celebrating Apple's \$5 trillion milestone, Stanford Professor Stephen Haber unpacks the deep, invisible "interlocking web" of legal, educational, and financial systems that make American innovation a distributed, contract-driven process. The highlight of the episode is Haber’s beautifully opinionated, analog rebellion: he is banning all screens in his upcoming university classes to force students to actively think and debate rather than passively outsourcing their minds to AI-generated drafts.

## Highlights by Theme

### News & Business
The geopolitical landscape is shifting rapidly, and the *Financial Times* delivers two outstanding, bite-sized reports on this friction. In **[Can Kazakhstan’s oil boom survive Putin’s war?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oLrvbPAsHMY)**, we see how Central Asia is striving to forge an independent future by turning to car manufacturing as "the new oil," as Ukrainian drone strikes on Russian soil threaten the crucial Caspian transit pipelines. Meanwhile, **[Why is Ukraine attacking Russia’s biggest online retailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0uCK9m61cxg)** analyzes Kiev's drone campaign against Wildberries—the Russian equivalent of Amazon—to disrupt military supply lines and bring the reality of war home to ordinary citizens. Back in the U.S., *The Wall Street Journal*’s **[Inflation Cooled in July but Remains Above Fed Target](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oyt5ySevquc)** reports that while core CPI slowed slightly to 2.5%, the Fed faces a delicate balancing act to cool inflation to its 2% target without accidentally bursting the market's fragile, high-interest-rate-sensitive AI bubble. In Chinese-language news, *BBC News 中文* offers a sobering look at how the next generation is navigating job displacement in **[AI浪潮衝擊青年就業 畢業生如何應對？](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mf4Jtx2kd4I)**, alongside a report tracking China's aviation ambitions as its domestic jetliner attempts to disrupt the Boeing-Airbus duopoly in **[中國國產C919首飛國際航線：挑戰波音及空中巴士再進一步？](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9wXjTvkzikM)**.

### Learning & Ideas
The most intellectually stimulating ideas today come from economist Caroline Hoxby’s Hoover Institution lecture, **[What Makes College Opportunity Fair?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t4aT3AkFIIc)**. She turns conventional progressive wisdom on its head by demonstrating with massive data sets that test-optional college admissions actually hurt socioeconomically disadvantaged students—standardized testing remains the single best meritocratic tool to level the playing field against wealthy families who can hire expensive consultants to polish their kids' essays and reference letters. On the science and lifestyle front, Katina Bajaj's TED Talk, **[How Play Boosts Your Creativity and Resilience](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LGb0_ed_euA)**, makes a strong case that we are living through a "play-deprivation crisis", and explains how giving ourselves the "friction" of unstructured, outcome-free daydreaming activates the brain's default mode network to unlock true creativity. For a dose of global environmental reality, *Bloomberg Originals* delivers **[How ‘Super El Niño’ Is Turbocharging Weather Disasters](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OU0PIBP44_4)**, showing how rising baseline sea temperatures act as an energy battery that disrupts West African cocoa yields, triggers erratic Indian monsoons, and physically chokes shipping capacity in the drought-stricken Panama Canal. Finally, *The New York Times* showcases a brilliant ecological solution in **[These Brazilian Mosquitoes Battle Diseases](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BdrB0EIZ4V4)**, showing how a factory in Rio is breeding mosquitoes carrying *Wolbachia* bacteria to successfully drive down local dengue fever cases by a staggering 89%.

### Tech & AI
For product design and logistics enthusiasts, *CNBC* dissects the premium German automaker's strategy to win back dwindling US market share using high-tech smart headlights and a refreshed premium SUV lineup in **[How Audi hopes to win back consumers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fjn8s1-G9Jc)**. In municipal tech, *The Wall Street Journal*’s **[How to Fix New York City’s \$500M Trash Problem](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LDCvtZ4L_GM)** outlines the city's massive, multi-year transition toward containerized streets to starve out its infamous rat populations. More provocatively, it details a future where the city's \$500-million-a-year export pipeline could be entirely transformed by deploying AI-trained sorting robots to autonomously extract valuable, reusable resources directly from NYC's waste stream.
