# YouTube — 2026-05-14

## Watch First
If you only have 15 minutes, [What Really Won the Trillion-Dollar Supreme Court Case | Neal Kumar Katyal | TED](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g3M3WaixeOw) is a masterclass in storytelling and legal strategy. Katyal details how his team used a bespoke AI tool to accurately predict the Supreme Court justices' exact lines of attack, but ultimately argues that human connection and empathy—not algorithms—are what actually win historic arguments.

### Learning & Ideas
For hard geopolitical strategy, [Defending Taiwan: A Strategy To Prevent War With China](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bmNrkHAd0Ic) presents a sobering Hoover Institution talk on "gray zone" deterrence, arguing that economic and political alliances—rather than just military might—are required to counter China's regional coercion. [Why Big Oil is opening new frontiers | FT Film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4XyCWj63NI4) is a beautifully shot documentary looking at Suriname, the world's greenest country, and its paradoxical ambition to fund rainforest conservation using a massive $10.5 billion offshore oil boom. If you want something shorter but equally fascinating, [How giant stones proved money is simply a trust system | FT #shorts](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=igmTYPKw1yQ) explores the anthropological history of money using the island of Yap as a prime example.
