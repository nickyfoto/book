# YouTube — 2026-08-04

## Watch First
Gregg Ward’s deeply personal talk **[How to Live with Your Biggest Regret](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cGZwLEoAx0U)** is an absolute must-watch, exploring the agonizing psychological terrain of moral injury through his own experience of accidentally killing his high school sweetheart. He delivers a profound, counter-intuitive lesson on why self-forgiveness is sometimes the wrong goal, arguing instead for active accountability to transform guilt into world-changing tribute.
