# YouTube — 2026-08-07

## Watch First
If you watch only one thing, make it the BE WATER channel's **[高晓松：聊聊文革 / 我的家族史](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IE2D-vc2HxU)**, which offers a masterpiece of personal oral history detailing how his prominent academic family survived the grueling years of the Cultural Revolution. This highly compelling video weaves together harrowing events—like his parents being sent to build the Daqing oil fields, his uncle digging a cellar to hide from arbitrary arrest, and a tragic 17-year love story spanning from Ningxia's arid farm fields to the shores of West Lake—all told with a deeply moving mix of emotional resilience and pitch-black humor.

## Highlights by Theme

### News & Business
Bloomberg Originals' **[Why Sanctions Aren’t Stopping North Korea](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v30SRprmmLE)** reveals how the Kim regime engineered a massive economic rebound, bringing in an estimated **\$22 billion** between 2022 and 2025 by leveraging state-sponsored crypto hacking to steal billions, exporting artillery shell stockpiles to Russia for \$12.8 billion, and relying on China for 98% of its core trade. For a look at the perils of modern market leverage, WSJ's **[As His Wedding Guests Arrived, His \$45B Hedge Fund Crumbled](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KugpXiaTLKI)** chronicles the spectacular **67% July meltdown** of 24-year-old stockpicker Leopold Ashenbrenner's "Situational Awareness" fund, which imploded during a sudden AI market sell-off after borrowing \$3 to \$4 for every \$1 of capital to make hyper-concentrated tech bets. For macroeconomic watchers, the Financial Times explains why the US Treasury took the extraordinarily rare step of propping up the Japanese yen—marking their first joint intervention in nearly 30 years—in **[Why did the US Treasury undertake its historic yen intervention?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SXelSwYj9Os)**, showing it was a move driven by US self-interest to prevent Japan from dumping US government bonds. Rounding out this section is 罗永浩的十字路口's highly self-reflective Chinese-language short **[永遠都會知道真相……幸好還有個深明大義的老婆](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jni4VMXOLqs)**, which uses a personal dining anecdote to explain why startup founders can never truly hear the unvarnished truth from their employees, alongside GQ Taiwan's brief history lesson **[為什麼政客常常圖利自家人？](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bPPFcPHg1gc)** on why autocrats historically rely on nepotistic corruption networks to guard secrets and maintain control.

### Learning & Ideas
Leading the pack of intellectual content is Alan Resnick's bizarrely brilliant, deadpan TED Talk **[Save It to Your Desktop!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6YNOQTqNfgQ)**, which hilariously parodies technology anxiety with a "simple trick" that involves saving everything directly to your desktop, ignoring toolbars, and performing complex 3D scanning topologies using black water bucket rigs. For science and math enthusiasts, Khan Academy delivers an exceptionally rigorous and clear physics lecture in **[Modeling spring-mass oscillators](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-inpCcFh9qk)**, which uses Newton's second law, differential equations, and sinusoidal cos/sin guesswork to mathematically derive the empirical formula for spring oscillation periods. Additionally, the Hoover Institution presents a fascinating geopolitical strategy lecture in **[Aesop’s Fables and National Security](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKNK-MLW_08)**, showing how national security analysts can use ancient stories of scorpions and lions to distinguish between naturally aggressive adversaries and those who are merely hostile due to changing external circumstances.

### Tech & AI
CNBC's **[Why Crypto Is Betting Big On AI Agents](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lC0EK8RCwxk)** highlights a major industry pivot as crypto giants Coinbase and Circle build stablecoin and smart contract infrastructure to turn autonomous AI agents into the internet's next primary economic actors, allowing them to trade, pay for S-1 filings, and settle machine-to-machine transactions without human oversight. For retail stock pickers, WSJ's **[Passive Index Funds Offer Different Ways To Play the AI Boom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uj8pv1StXzo)** breaks down how equal-weighted S&P 500 ETFs are currently outperforming market-cap-weighted index funds because massive AI hyperscalers like Meta and Microsoft have lagged, offering a crucial diversification choice for investors cautious of tech concentration.
