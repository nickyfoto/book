# YouTube — 2026-07-22

## Watch First
**[AI for Hearing-Impaired Workers: A Good-News Story](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mq0R7oB1PMI)** from the Hoover Institution is the most compelling watch today, offering a rare, data-backed counter-narrative to AI doom-mongering. Economists detail how a simple AI outbound calling tool dramatically boosted the productivity and earnings of hearing-impaired food delivery workers in China, effectively pulling up the bottom of the wage distribution without government mandates.
