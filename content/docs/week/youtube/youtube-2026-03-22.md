# YouTube — 2026-03-22

## Watch First
[Will AI Take Your Job in the Next 10 Years? Wrong Question | Vinciane Beauchene | TED](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lX2V8p-hByE) is a pragmatic, strategic look at how we should adapt to autonomous AI agents in the workplace. Instead of worrying about protecting jobs, Beauchene argues we need to prepare for Artificial Capable Intelligence (ACI) by shifting the value of human work away from tasks and toward relationship building, trust, and connection.

## Highlights by Theme

### News & Business
The *Wall Street Journal* has a fascinating piece on a growing structural shift in [Why Americans Are Leaving the U.S. in Record Numbers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ppMQ0PGcWf8), noting that an unprecedented 1 in 5 Americans now want to leave the US, increasingly moving to European countries like Spain, Ireland, and Portugal for a better, more affordable life. On the geopolitical front, WSJ also covers [Iran’s Shahed Drones: How They’re Wreaking Havoc in the Middle East](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R93nIeNPqBc), explaining how these cheap, low-flying drones easily evade modern air defenses, as well as the human toll of the conflict at the [Iran-Turkey Border: Iranians Fleeing the War Share Their Fears](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jKr14i5svS0). On the finance side, CNBC explores [Why Jewelry Is Becoming A Luxury Investment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3seUHLmqzU) as wealthy buyers shift from soft luxury like handbags to hard assets and colored gemstones. For Chinese-speaking investors, *美投讲美股* offers an excellent, practical breakdown in [这4只ETF，无论何时都可以安心买入！](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fX0Am0Rhnfg), highlighting four specific ETFs (SGOV, JEPI, DGRO, RW) that are designed to be relatively safe, weather-proof buys in any market condition.

### Learning & Ideas
The most intellectually hefty piece today is [Building Civic Unity In A Religiously Diverse Democracy | Hoover Institution](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rha2ZT7ViBo), a deep-dive panel featuring Robert George and Eboo Patel discussing how higher education should handle religious pluralism, free speech, and the necessity of "truth-seeking" over ideological dogmatism. If you want a quick laugh, TED offers a short clip, [Turns out you *should* be taking work less seriously #TEDTalks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QyI7-6F3p04), showing that leaders with a sense of humor are 27% more motivating to their teams. For history buffs, the Chinese-language [【限免】袁Sir翻牌：曹操后人为何草包？#lifeano翻牌 260315](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s7UxAyM-P6A) by *LIFEANO CLUB* is a great Q&A session spanning topics from the persistence of Qing dynasty military armor to the origins of Soviet Russian nationalism.

### Tech & AI
