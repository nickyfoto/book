# YouTube — 2026-08-03

## Watch First
For anyone looking to move past superficial moralizing in global affairs, **[The Political Economy of Palestine (with Hussein Aboubakr Mansour)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R1hEFjoUL3Y)** on the **Hoover Institution** channel is an absolute masterclass. It is a rare, clear-eyed discussion on how "Palestine" has become a globalized currency for moral and political extraction, rather than a solvable physical conflict.
