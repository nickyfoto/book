# YouTube — 2026-04-19

## Watch First
[I Taught Rats to Drive. They Taught Me to Enjoy the Ride | Kelly Lambert | TED](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8ZIeAVrgs4o)
Neuroscientist Kelly Lambert's delightful TED talk reveals that rats trained to drive tiny vehicles experience a measurable joy in the anticipation of a reward, teaching us a profound lesson about "behaviorceuticals" and the mental health benefits of physical effort.
