# YouTube — 2026-06-02

## Watch First
**[How a New U.S. Weapon Killed 21 Civilians in Iran](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HEyfNHBeSSY)** by The New York Times is a chilling, meticulous piece of visual journalism that investigates the lethal blast radius of the new US "precision strike missile" (PrSM). By analyzing satellite imagery and verifying impact points, the team exposes how a weapon touted for its precision still resulted in civilian casualties, offering a stark look at the real-world consequences of next-gen weaponry.

### Learning & Ideas
Over on TED, Hiba Qasas challenges the conventional "Western liberal model" of peacebuilding in **[The Missing Ingredient in Every Peace Deal | Hiba Qasas | TED](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BI3TXc1WtJ8)**. She argues compellingly for "principled pragmatism"—starting peace talks with self-interest and transactionalism rather than forced empathy—drawing from her difficult experiences mediating between Israeli and Palestinian leaders. On a lighter but still educational note, The Wall Street Journal asks a tricky parenting question in **[How Poppi Co-Founder Told Her Kids They Were Rich](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sB2sfCSDVAo)**, exploring how self-made founders try to raise grounded kids amidst sudden generational wealth.
