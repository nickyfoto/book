# YouTube — 2026-06-07

## Watch First
**[Why Everyone Is Wrong About AI's Water Usage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DNkZRt-X4RM)** by *Looking Glass Universe* is a brilliant, physics-based takedown of the media panic surrounding AI emissions. It turns out that a single chatbot prompt uses just a fraction of a water bottle, and data centers—while massively concentrated—are radically more energy-optimized than our spread-out daily habits.

### Tech & AI
AI's insatiable infrastructure demands are quietly reshaping global markets. *CNBC* reports in **[AI Is Rewriting the Global Stock Market Rankings](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y9nXs3eAP5U)** that the sheer volume of capital flowing into hardware has propelled Taiwan into the world's fifth-largest equity market. Complementing this, Mistral's CEO warns in **[AI Demand Is Outpacing Chips, Memory and Electricity](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yP9MfTdMgVg)** that the current rush is severely straining both global chip supplies and regional power grids. If you want a quick hardware refresher, *GQ Taiwan* breaks down the mind-boggling scale of modern microprocessors in **[CPU裡幾十億電晶體到底怎麼作用？](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_rJyoq8o6Qk)**.
