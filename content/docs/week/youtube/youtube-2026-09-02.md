# YouTube — 2026-09-02

## Watch First
**[A Physics Professor Bet Me \$10,000 I'm Wrong...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F5_G0AHfYhI)** by *Veritasium* is an absolute must-watch that chronicles a high-stakes, public wager over a wind-powered car designed to travel faster than the wind itself. It is a thrilling masterclass in scientific integrity, concluding with a physics professor graciously conceding the bet and transferring \$10,000 after being proven wrong by empirical evidence.
