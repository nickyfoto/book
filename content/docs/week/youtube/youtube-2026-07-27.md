# YouTube — 2026-07-27

## Watch First
**[A New Equation: Teaching Math for an AI World (with Po-Shen Loh)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VtrUSXTl1o8)** is an absolute standout discussion on the Carnegie Mellon channel that argues the future of math education isn't about rote memorization, but rather using principles from improv comedy to teach creative problem-solving. In an age where AI can instantly solve equations, Loh makes a compelling case that math's real value lies in teaching humans how to think adaptively and build collaborative communities.
