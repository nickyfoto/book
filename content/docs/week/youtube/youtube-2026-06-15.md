# YouTube — 2026-06-15

## Watch First
**[Steven Spielberg on Watching Movies Alone](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z2OFj-A0ObA)** is a fantastic, brief meditation from the legendary director on why the communal theatrical experience still matters.
