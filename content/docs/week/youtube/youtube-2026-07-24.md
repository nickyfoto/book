# YouTube — 2026-07-24

## Watch First
**[How to Raise Kids Who Question AI | Randi Williams | TED](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X5rOzZq2Ce8)** is an essential watch for anyone navigating the modern tech landscape with children. MIT researcher Randi Williams argues that hiding AI behind friendly, frictionless interfaces teaches kids to blindly trust smart toys, and she offers practical parenting strategies to encourage critical thinking over reliance.
