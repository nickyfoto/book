# YouTube — 2026-08-01

## Watch First
**[The Fascinating World Of Whales | BBC Earth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQT8ZZ-CAtI)** is a breathtaking and surprisingly melancholic look at how whales are adapting to changing marine ecosystems. The underwater footage is stunning, but the story of Eden's whales learning to simply float with open mouths because low-oxygen waters have made traditional hunting too exhausting is a profound reminder of our ecological footprint.
