# YouTube — 2026-06-04

## Watch First
If you only watch one thing today, make it Jonathan Haidt's compelling TED Talk, **[Why You Should Be a Techno-Skeptic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DH9L7vJ03DE)**, which argues persuasively that screens are rewiring adolescent brains and destroying the human capacity for sustained attention. He calls for a major cultural shift back to real-world social bonding and limiting digital exposure until high school.
