# YouTube — 2026-05-15

## Watch First
Bloomberg's "[The $10 Billion Hunt for the Rocks That Power the World](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d2dgJGkw5p0)" is a gripping look at how the renewed US-China trade war has exposed the West's critical vulnerability in rare earth processing.
