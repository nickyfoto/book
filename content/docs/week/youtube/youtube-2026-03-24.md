# YouTube — 2026-03-24

## Watch First
[The Embarrassingly Simple Reason AI Can’t Cure Cancer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFcyWFK1q8I) is a brilliant, counter-intuitive dive into the bottleneck of modern drug discovery. It argues that while AI is incredibly useful at early-stage target discovery, it hits a hard wall at clinical trials because we fundamentally lack the high-quality biological data required to accurately simulate the complexities of the entire human body. 

## Highlights by Theme

### News & Business
The ongoing Iran crisis and the shutdown of the Strait of Hormuz completely dominate today's global news cycle. For an excellent macro view, [伊朗战争，和全球能源格局](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HeVuAKDtWX8) by 小Lin说 masterfully maps out the fragile global energy supply chain, noting that while China and India take the biggest volume hit, Japan and South Korea face the most severe structural risks to their energy grids. [Why President Trump Has a Big Oil Problem](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T19GODD3THM) from the NYT breaks down the administration's 5-day delay on bombing Iranian power plants to give back-channel diplomacy a chance, while [TACO？还没到时候！六十年代大滞胀又来？](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NTMxaUMc1D0) explores the financial market's growing fear of a 1960s-style stagflation driven by this energy shock—though Big Tech stocks strangely remain the ultimate safe haven.

### Learning & Ideas
The Hoover Institution uploaded an incredibly dense but rewarding day-long conference on geoeconomics, with the standout being [Geoeconomics with Matteo Maggiori](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EhIegDfKfe0), which formalizes the math behind how superpowers weaponize economic dependence. You can pair that with [Trade and Financial Policy for National Security](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EctVr5rCJ_w), where Condoleezza Rice and H.R. McMaster debate the messy, often contradictory reality of trying to decouple national security from the global economy. For a total change of pace, [晓松聊北京，北京是个什么样的地方](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3iN4K7QR-EU) offers a highly entertaining cultural history of Beijing, explaining the deep linguistic and psychological divide between the bureaucratic "North City" elites and the deeply grounded, laid-back "South City" commoners.

### Tech & AI
[Arm Releases First Ever AI Chip, With Meta As Initial Customer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zkTrk_ymh4g) highlights a massive industry shift, showing the British chip-design giant moving beyond licensing to manufacture its own physical silicon, specifically optimized for power-efficient AGI inference. Meanwhile, [How Retailers Like Gap Are Betting Big On AI Partnerships](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwSZUARb9vw) looks at the emerging trend of "agentic commerce," where brands format their inventory data to be crawlable by LLMs like Google's Gemini, allowing the AI to handle sizing, checkout, and shipping natively within the chat.

### Everything Else
[How the Iconic $10K Eames Lounge Chair Is Made in America](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5zyhGMMPKs) takes you inside the Herman Miller factory to see the 100,000-cycle stress tests and beautiful veneer pressing that justify the mid-century icon's staggering price tag. Finally, the NYT explores why the ancient Greek tragedy *Antigone* is suddenly everywhere in New York theaters, capturing modern anxieties around state power, feminism, and civil disobedience in [Why Are We Obsessed With Antigone?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ekEJ3lqWrtY).
