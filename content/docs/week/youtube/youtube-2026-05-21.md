### Learning & Ideas
Lead your intellectual deep dives with the fascinating [Wargaming with Jacquelyn Schneider](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XVEpwBsx6rs), where we learn that a 1961 Camp David wargame involving Henry Kissinger directly influenced the real-world diplomatic strategies used during the Cuban Missile Crisis a year later. In Chinese history, Yuan Tengfei's [袁Sir聊一妻多夫：妈妈有俩老公，谁玩“共妻”呢？#lifeano漫聊 260521](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dN5Nk9UEPU4) uses a tone-deaf Mother's Day ad as a jumping-off point to explore the harsh economic and demographic realities—like extreme poverty and gender imbalances—that drove polyandry and "pawning wives" in ancient China and Mongolia. Similarly, Gao Xiaosong's deep dive into the Southern Ming dynasty in [#高晓松|南明悲歌06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vewWKcOQzV0) attributes the empire's ultimate collapse not just to bad emperors, but to being trapped in a Malthusian population cycle and a rigidly uncompromising "no appeasement" foreign policy.
