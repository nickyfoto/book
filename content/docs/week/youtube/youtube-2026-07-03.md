# YouTube — 2026-07-03

## Watch First
**[Nashville: the price of success | FT Film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cVB5Uy_OoZI)** is an essential, sobering look at what happens when a cultural hub becomes a victim of its own economic boom. The Financial Times documents how massive corporate investments, an explosion of tourism, and skyrocketing property taxes are squeezing out the very musicians who gave "Music City" its identity, turning the town into a commercialized playground at the expense of its working-class artists.

## Highlights by Theme

### News & Business
Bloomberg Originals gives a stellar update on the Caribbean in **[Can Cuba’s Economy Survive Trump?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q0PnVLUeFI8)**, detailing how US sanctions, the loss of Venezuelan oil, and a crippling lack of jet fuel have ground the country's grid to a halt and sparked unprecedented economic unrest. Over in consumer finance, CNBC breaks down the fierce competition for high-net-worth millennials and Gen Z in **[Why Amex And Chase Love Lounges](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nadEiZCz8Ns)**, revealing how credit card companies are building exclusive, multi-million-dollar pop-ups at music festivals like Coachella just to drive cardholder loyalty. Meanwhile, the Financial Times reports in **[Donald Trump’s Great American State Fair fails to attract crowds | FT #shorts](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FJcd4QJz6tw)** that the heavily partisan event for the US 250th anniversary is suffering from largely deserted pavilions. For a historical counterpart regarding the anniversary, BBC News 中文 uploaded **[美國建國250週年：五個鮮為人知的中美關係歷史細節 － BBC News 中文](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T55e9Rf289o)** to explore five little-known details of early US-China relations.

### Learning & Ideas
In a highly practical TED Talk, Melissa M. Mikus shares **[A Simple Solution to Fix Workplace Miscommunication](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xx8mwytj4CY)**, arguing that companies could save billions in wasted effort by simply having employees create "communication style tags" that define exactly how and when they prefer to be contacted. For something brilliantly gruesome, GQ Taiwan’s **[蛆竟然可以用於清創手術？](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=94ZLshk9SfY)** explains the fascinating history of field medicine using sterile maggots to eat dead tissue and clean infected wounds. Additionally, the Hoover Institution offers a quick historical primer in **[The Foundation of America's Military Exceptionalism](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xujG9f4nvZQ)**, tracing the nation's enduring military strength directly back to the constitutional principle of a civilian-led volunteer force.

### Tech & AI
GQ Taiwan delivers an incredibly dense one-minute explainer in **[一分鐘帶你了解晶片的分工產業鏈](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ftrFfy_KGdI)**, neatly breaking down the highly specialized semiconductor supply chain from macro-level micro-architects down to the physical design engineers who figure out where every transistor goes. For a lighter take, Bloomberg Originals’ **[Karlie Kloss Critiques Silicon Valley's Most Famous Outfits](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LvrQhEsCDok)** playfully roasts the evolution of tech CEO fashion, honoring Steve Jobs's iconic turtleneck while giving Jensen Huang's signature leather jacket a solid nod of approval.

### Everything Else
