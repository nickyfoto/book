# YouTube — 2026-06-19

## Watch First
**[What Brexit Really Cost](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d6gyCyPwRZo)** from Bloomberg Originals is a staggering look at a decade of economic self-harm, modeling what the UK's GDP would look like today if they had remained in the EU. It is a fascinating autopsy of a political earthquake that ultimately cost the British economy around 2-4% of its total GDP.
