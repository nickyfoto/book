# YouTube — 2026-07-25

## Watch First
The standout today is Veritasium's deep dive into biological scaling laws: **[Why does every mammal get 1 billion heartbeats in their life?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tL9Lw250spc)**. It is a brilliant, perspective-shifting exploration of Kleiber's Law, moving from why a 3,000-kilogram elephant only needs 100 times the calories of a cat, all the way to how human cities scale like biological super-organisms.
