# YouTube — 2026-07-04

## Watch First
If you only have time for one, watch **[How the World Became Obsessed with the American Cowboy | WSJ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KdDFoL5UoQU)**. It's a beautifully shot exploration that peels back the nostalgia and mythology of the American West, contrasting the Hollywood ideal of the rugged individualist with the gritty, day-to-day reality of modern ranching families.

### Learning & Ideas

### Tech & AI

He outlines a roadmap where Alexa learns your patterns to act proactively without prompts, though he acknowledges it's a fine line between helpful and an unwanted intrusion.
