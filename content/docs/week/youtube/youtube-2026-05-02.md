# YouTube — 2026-05-02

## Watch First
If you only have time for one video today, make it [Why Pursuing Happiness Makes You … Less Happy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=76_9x4YyFyQ). Emily Esfahani Smith delivers a profoundly relevant TED Talk arguing that modern culture's obsession with chasing happiness leaves us feeling unmoored, and that true human resilience is actually built on cultivating meaning, purpose, and deep belonging.
