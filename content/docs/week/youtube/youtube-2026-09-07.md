# YouTube — 2026-09-07

## Watch First
If you only have time for a single video this period, make it EconTalk’s **[Meritocracy, Exams, and the Knowledge that Matters](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VvLkXV35hQ)**. This deeply engaging episode explores how rigid, classics-heavy civil service exams in 19th-century China created immense societal frustration and directly fueled the catastrophic Taiping Rebellion, serving as a powerful historical warning about the limits of testing pure mental discipline rather than practical, domain-specific knowledge.
