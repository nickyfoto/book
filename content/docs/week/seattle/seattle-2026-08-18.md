## Local News

**State Probes Volunteer Safety Records for Local Cleanup Nonprofit** · [KOMO News](https://komonews.com/news/local/state-seeks-safety-records-from-we-heart-seattle-in-needle-and-drug-cleanup-investigation-trash-hazardous-toxic-waste-homeless-tent-camping-encampment-neighborhood-community-public-park-playground-safety-lawsuit)
The Washington State Department of Labor and Industries has launched an investigation into We Heart Seattle, requesting safety records after a complaint was filed regarding volunteer safety. The volunteer organization regularly cleans up used needles, trash, and drug paraphernalia from city parks and encampments. While CEO Andrea Suarez maintains that their work is vital for public safety, state regulators are evaluating whether the volunteers are being exposed to hazardous conditions without proper protections.

**King County Establishes New Position to Monitor ICE Flights from Boeing Field** · [The Seattle Times](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/king-county-employee-will-monitor-ice-flights-from-boeing-field/#Echobox=1787059307-2)
King County is creating a first-of-its-kind employee position to monitor Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) charter flights out of Boeing Field. This new role is intended to bring greater transparency to the airport's operations amid a long-running debate over its involvement in federal immigration enforcement. This first-of-its-kind position is one of only a few nationwide and directly addresses the local controversy surrounding municipal airport participation in federal enforcement.

**Proposed Snoqualmie Lithium-Ion Battery Storage Project Put on Hold** · [KOMO News](https://komonews.com/news/local/snoqualmie-area-lithium-battery-storage-project-put-on-hold-concerns-fire-safety-toxic-environment-jupiter-power-unincorporated-king-county)
A proposed 45-acre lithium-ion battery energy storage system near Fisher Creek Park has been put on hold following strong community backlash. The project by Jupiter Power, designed to store surplus renewable energy and release it back to the grid, faced significant fire safety and environmental concerns from local Snoqualmie Ridge residents. Snoqualmie's mayor confirmed the project is on hold after residents expressed concerns about fire safety and potential toxic environmental impacts across from Snoqualmie Ridge.

**Renton Demonstrators Demand Cancellation of Flock Camera Contract** · [FOX 13](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/renton-protesters-flock-camera?taid=6a83ede6c81b2d00015b8ebc&utm_campaign=trueAnthem%3A+Trending+Content&utm_medium=trueAnthem&utm_source=twitter)
Demonstrators gathered outside Renton City Hall to demand that city officials cover existing Flock Safety license plate reader cameras and terminate their contract with the provider. While Renton's 24 cameras have been inactive since a pause in May, the Renton City Council recently approved a motion to physically cover them while their future is decided. Activists cite severe privacy concerns and potential surveillance abuse, with a final city council vote on the contract's cancellation expected soon.

**Boeing White-Collar Union Members Call Latest Contract Offer Insufficient** · [The Seattle Times](https://www.seattletimes.com/business/boeing-aerospace/boeings-contract-offer-just-not-enough-some-speea-workers-say/#Echobox=1787059348-2)

Union members also expressed frustration that the proposal falls short of securing a commitment to keep engineering work within the Puget Sound region. Some white-collar employees are stating that the current offer is simply not enough to satisfy the union's regional goals.
