# Seattle Local — 2026-09-08

## Top Story
**Seattle city leaders and law enforcement are responding to a dramatic surge in gun violence** after three separate shootings in **Belltown** over Labor Day weekend left three people dead and three others injured. **Mayor Katie Wilson** announced that the city will debut a comprehensive **gun violence prevention strategy** next week, while **Interim Police Chief Andre Sayles** confirmed detectives are investigating each incident as separate, unrelated cases with no suspects currently in custody.
