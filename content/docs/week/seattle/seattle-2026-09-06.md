As more than 50,000 Seattle Public Schools students return to class, the Seattle Department of Transportation (SDOT) is wrapping up 70 voter-approved safety projects near local schools. Funded by the \$1.55 billion Seattle Transportation Levy, these projects include new crosswalks, bike lanes, and "intersection daylighting" to restrict parking near corners and increase pedestrian visibility. SDOT has already completed over 30 of these safety improvements across the city to protect children walking and biking to school.
