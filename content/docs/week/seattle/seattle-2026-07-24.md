# Seattle Local — 2026-07-24

## Top Story
All 11 people on board survived after a Kenmore Air seaplane crashed and caught fire in Shallow Bay near Sucia Island in the San Juans on Thursday evening. The flight, which departed from Lake Union en route to Roche Harbor, is now under investigation by the NTSB to determine what caused the accident and whether the pilot's decision to divert around a storm system played a role.

The Tukwila City Council narrowly passed a new ordinance banning camping on city property, which will go into effect on July 28. The measure, requested by Tukwila Police Chief Eric Lund to address growing encampments in local green spaces, carries penalties of up to a $1,000 fine and 90 days in jail for violators.
