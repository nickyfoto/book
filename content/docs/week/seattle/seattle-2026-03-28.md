# Seattle Local — 2026-03-28

## Top Story
An estimated 75,000 to 100,000 demonstrators took to the streets across western Washington on Saturday for the nationwide "No Kings" protests, with the largest gathering marching from Cal Anderson Park to the Seattle Center. Organizers mobilized to make it the largest protest in the city's history, prompting major rolling street closures throughout the downtown core as thousands voiced their concerns.

## Local News

**[Historic Crosslake Light Rail Connection Opens]** · [KOMO News](https://komonews.com/news/local/sound-transit-cross-lake-connection-interstate-90-bridge-bellevue-eastside-redmond-light-rail-netowrk-project-infrastructure-specialized-track-systems)
Sound Transit officially opened the long-awaited Crosslake Connection on Saturday, linking Seattle to the Eastside via the 2 Line nearly two decades after voters initially approved the project. The route marks a historic global transit milestone as the first light rail service to operate across a floating bridge. Leaders from across the state attended the grand opening, and trains began public service shortly before the Mariners hosted the Cleveland Guardians.

**[Woman Sentenced to 20 Years for Murder of Seattle Rideshare Driver]** · [FOX 13 Seattle](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/seattle-woman-sentenced-killing-rideshare-driver?taid=69c736435a6fb6000140d4f8&utm_campaign=trueAnthem%3A+Trending+Content&utm_medium=trueAnthem&utm_source=twitter)
Ne'lani Allen-Bailey, 20, was sentenced to 20 years in prison for the 2023 shooting death of 52-year-old rideshare driver Amare Geda. Allen-Bailey pleaded guilty to second-degree murder after killing Geda during a carjacking and subsequently driving his stolen vehicle to a hair appointment. The victim's family delivered emotional statements in King County Superior Court as the judge weighed the punishment.

**[Gov. Ferguson Signs New Commercial Housing Bill]** · [The Seattle Times](https://ebx.sh/F7abtH)
Governor Bob Ferguson signed a high-profile bill into law on Friday that allows for housing developments in most commercial areas across Washington. The legislation is part of a broader slate of new density and zoning requirements aimed at addressing the state's affordability crunch, though local leaders emphasize that further legislative action is still needed.

**[Record Diesel Prices Hit Washington Pumps]** · [KOMO News](https://komonews.com/news/local/western-washington-seattle-diesel-prices-hit-record-high-twice-in-a-week-strait-of-hormuz-gas-pump-economy)
Diesel fuel prices reached an all-time high in Washington state twice this week, peaking at a statewide average of $6.55 per gallon on Friday. In Seattle, the average is even higher at $6.69 per gallon, with one Georgetown station listing diesel at $7.07, severely impacting local truck drivers and transport businesses. One local analyst warned of a potential 1970s-style oil crisis as prices continue to test new limits.

**[Seattle Mayor Hosts Town Hall on Paused Surveillance Expansion]** · [KOMO News](https://komonews.com/news/local/seattle-mayor-wilson-hosts-town-hall-on-security-and-surveillance-fields-questions-from-public-real-time-crime-center-camera-safety-privacy)
Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson hosted a downtown town hall event at the Wyncote NY Forum to discuss community security and surveillance impacts. The moderated discussion comes just over a week after the mayor announced a pause on the city's planned expansion of its police surveillance camera network.
