Seattle residents are sounding the alarm over the environmental and public health impacts of new large-scale data centers, citing potential pollution, higher utility costs, and heavy power usage. In response, the Seattle City Council has passed a one-year moratorium on building new data facilities. City leaders have also authorized studies to evaluate the potential local impacts of these centers.
