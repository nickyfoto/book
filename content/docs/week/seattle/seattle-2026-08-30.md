# Seattle Local — 2026-08-30

## Top Story
Contract negotiations between Seattle Public Schools and the Seattle Education Association broke down over the weekend, leaving the district on the verge of a strike just days before Wednesday's scheduled start of classes. With no further bargaining sessions scheduled ahead of a Tuesday deadline, the potential walkout threatens to disrupt the start of the school year for roughly 50,000 students and their families across the city.
