# Seattle Local — 2026-03-27

## Top Story
The highly anticipated Sound Transit "Crosslake Connection" debuts this Saturday, marking a historic transit milestone as the first light rail trains in the world will operate across a floating bridge. The 2 Line extension spans 7.4 miles across Lake Washington via the I-90 bridge, finally linking Seattle to Mercer Island, Bellevue, and Redmond after 18 years of planning and construction.
