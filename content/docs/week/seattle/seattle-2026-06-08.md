# Seattle Local — 2026-06-08

## Top Story
With the first FIFA World Cup 2026 match in Seattle just one week away, Mayor Katie Wilson has reactivated CCTV cameras around the Stadium District, citing a "credible threat." The controversial decision is part of an unprecedented $32 million multi-agency security effort to protect the 750,000 fans expected to visit the city for the international tournament.

**[Mayor Opens 50-Unit Interbay Tiny Home Village](https://komonews.com/news/local/first-new-shelter-units-under-seattle-mayor-wilson-open-in-interbay-homeless-houseless-services-mental-health-addiction-camping-resources-fifa-world-cup-summer)** · KOMO News
Seattle opened the Bayside Enhanced Shelter in the Interbay neighborhood on Sunday, providing 50 tiny home units for adults experiencing chronic homelessness. The facility operates as 24/7 transitional housing with wraparound services, including addiction and behavioral health support, though abstinence is not required for entry. While the opening marks progress for the city, it falls short of Mayor Katie Wilson's goal to add 500 shelter units ahead of the World Cup.

**[Teens Arrested After Climbing Tacoma Narrows Bridge](https://komonews.com/news/local/two-arrested-after-climbing-tacoma-narrows-bridge-cables-blocking-westbound-sr-16-weekend-traffic-commute-safety-wind-danger-washington-state-patrol)** · KOMO News
Two teenage boys, ages 16 and 17, were arrested after scaling the 500-foot cable suspensions of the Tacoma Narrows Bridge on Saturday night. The stunt prompted the Washington State Patrol to completely close westbound state Route 16 to get the teens down safely, bringing traffic to a bumper-to-bumper standstill for hours and causing significant weekend travel delays.
