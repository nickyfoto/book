The Washington State Transportation Commission voted 6-0 to create the state's first low-income toll discount program. Eligible drivers will receive a 50% discount on standard Good To Go! rates for two-axle vehicles on the SR 509 and SR 167 Expressways, with state enrollment beginning this fall and lower rates taking effect as early as December.

Rogoff argues that federal law permits him to serve in the position until a Senate-confirmed successor is officially in place.
