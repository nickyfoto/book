**[Two Teens Injured in Mount Si Bear Attack](https://www.kiro7.com/news/local/2-injured-bear-attack-mount-si/LZUNYOPIHJA6RBJE2FN5UV5AYM/)** · KIRO 7
Two teenage hikers are recovering after a black bear charged their group on a popular Mount Si trail on Tuesday afternoon. One teen sustained minor injuries and another was temporarily separated from the group during the encounter, prompting a major response from local rescue agencies. The Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife has officially called off the search for the animal, which remains missing.

**[Business Group Warns Payroll Tax Pushing Jobs to Bellevue](https://komonews.com/news/local/seattle-business-group-says-jumpstart-tax-is-pushing-jobs-investment-toward-bellevue-covid-relief-payroll-jump-start-jobs-in-seattle-tech-companies-amazon-microsoft)** · KOMO News
A new report released by the Downtown Seattle Association claims that Seattle's "JumpStart" payroll tax is making the city less competitive compared to neighboring Bellevue. The tax, originally implemented to fund affordable housing and COVID-19 relief, is now being cited by the business group as a driving factor behind job losses, declining office values, and rising downtown vacancies. 

**[Seattle City Light Proposes Substantial Rate Hikes](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/politics/seattle-city-light-eyes-biggest-bill-increases-in-recent-memory/?utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=owned_echobox_tw_m&utm_source=Twitter#Echobox=1781703177-3)** · The Seattle Times
Seattle City Light is pushing for its largest rate increases in recent memory. The utility company stated that surging electricity demand, aging infrastructure, and the ongoing need to invest in new clean energy sources are the primary reasons for the proposed billing hikes.

**[FBI Investigating Border Patrol Shooting in Blaine](https://komonews.com/news/local/us-border-patrol-agent-involved-in-shooting-in-blaine-washington-whatcom-county-fbi-investigation-gunshots-canadian-border)** · KOMO News
The FBI is investigating an early Tuesday morning shooting involving a U.S. Customs and Border Protection agent just feet from the Canadian border in Blaine. The agent fired on a 39-year-old suspect near 4th Street and A Street; the suspect was injured, hospitalized, and subsequently charged with illegal possession of a firearm. No agents were harmed during the incident, and investigators recovered a gun at the scene.

**[Ballot Measure Could Oust Pierce County Sheriff](https://www.kiro7.com/news/local/ballot-measure-could-oust-controversial-sheriff-in-pierce-county/IWXKLVZQEZCQDNM6EYUSUO2KWE/)** · KIRO 7
The Pierce County Charter Review Commission has voted to place a measure on the November ballot that would allow the county executive to appoint the sheriff, subject to council confirmation. If approved by voters, this structural change could lead to the removal of controversial elected Sheriff Keith Swank by the end of the year. Swank has stated he will actively campaign against the proposal.
