# Seattle Local — 2026-08-27

## Top Story
Over 90% of Seattle Public Schools teachers have voted to authorize a strike after contract negotiations with the district stalled just a week before classes are set to begin. If a tentative agreement is not reached before their current contract expires on August 31, the start of school for roughly 50,000 students could be delayed.
