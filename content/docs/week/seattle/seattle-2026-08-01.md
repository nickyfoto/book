# Seattle Local — 2026-08-01

## Top Story
Governor Bob Ferguson has declared a statewide wildfire emergency and imposed a sweeping ban on most outdoor and agricultural burning through September 30. The dramatic step follows extreme drought, high winds, and a rare "Particularly Dangerous Situation" alert issued by the National Weather Service for critical fire danger east of the Cascades.

**[Mayor Appoints Interim Police Chief After Barnes' Resignation](https://komonews.com/news/local/seattle-mayor-katie-wilson-new-interim-police-chief-andre-sayles-shon-barnes-resigns-mass-shooting-investigation-bite-of-seattle-center-security-policing-community-communications-spd)** · KOMO News
Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson appointed Deputy Chief Andre Sayles to serve as interim police chief after asking former Chief Shon Barnes to step down. Under a resignation agreement, Barnes will receive approximately $700,000 as he leaves the department. The leadership change comes as Wilson emphasizes the need for new priorities and urgently seeks a permanent chief to handle citywide security.

**[Bodycam Footage Released from Bite of Seattle Mass Shooting](https://www.king5.com/article/news/local/police-release-body-camera-footage-seattle-center-shooting/281-d329cbfd-17c7-45b0-8f33-f8ed8c454844)** · KING 5
The Seattle Police Department released body camera video showing officers rushing through crowds to apprehend a suspect and administer first aid during last weekend’s deadly mass shooting at the Seattle Center. A 19-year-old suspect with previously dismissed gun charges is in custody, but interim SPD leadership confirmed the search for additional suspects remains ongoing. The city has implemented heightened security protocols for upcoming weekend events in response to the violence.

**[AG Nick Brown Challenges Puget Sound Energy Rate Hikes](https://www.kiro7.com/news/local/washington-ag-nick-brown-fights-back-against-pses-proposed-29-electric-20-gas-rate-hikes/FNGBZJR4UNAZHKXMBNFBNVCGGI/#Echobox=1785608538)** · KIRO 7
Washington Attorney General Nick Brown is fighting back against major utility rate increases proposed by Puget Sound Energy. The utility company has requested a 29% hike in electric rates and a 20% increase for gas. Brown's pushback is an effort to shield local residents from significant utility cost spikes.

**[Sound Transit Sets 2042 Target for Ballard Light Rail](https://www.kiro7.com/news/local/ballard-light-rail-not-dead-sound-transit-targets-2042-market-street-service/IJ6OXEXHHVBETGMSKVXH4DPMXY/#Echobox=1785606609)** · KIRO 7
Plans for a Ballard light rail extension are still in motion, with Sound Transit now targeting 2042 for passenger service along Market Street. The long-awaited transit project intends to connect the bustling maritime and residential neighborhood to the regional light rail grid.
