# Seattle Local — 2026-09-12

## Top Story

Mayor Katie Wilson's office formally responded to Seattle City Council demands regarding the forced resignation of Police Chief Shon Barnes following the Bite of Seattle mass shooting. The mayor stated she required cabinet leadership aligned with her administration's core values, policy goals, and public safety vision as council members continue to scrutinize the city's gun violence response.

## Local News

**[Big Businesses Call on Seattle Leaders for 100-Day Public Safety Plan](https://komonews.com/news/local/microsoft-starbucks-costco-alaska-airlines-call-for-seattle-public-safety-improvements-tmobile-katie-wilson-city-council-public-safety)** · KOMO News  
Thirty-eight major local business leaders, including executives from Microsoft, Starbucks, Costco, Nordstrom, and Alaska Airlines, sent a joint letter to Mayor Katie Wilson and the City Council demanding urgent public safety reforms. The coalition requested a 100-day action plan, the restoration of surveillance cameras in high-activity areas, and established benchmarks for police response times. They noted that recent polling shows Seattle residents strongly expect immediate, measurable safety actions from city hall.

**[SPD Delays Release of Internal Records in Bite of Seattle Investigation](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/spd-texts-bite-seattle?taid=6aa4f4f6129c9200012a68e4&utm_campaign=trueAnthem%3A+Trending+Content&utm_medium=trueAnthem&utm_source=twitter)** · FOX 13 Seattle  
The Seattle Police Department has delayed releasing internal text messages related to the Bite of Seattle shooting, reversing a prior commitment to disclose the records by Friday. While Mayor Katie Wilson's office previously released text communications from the day of the incident, SPD stated that its own records must remain withheld while the active investigation continues. City council members and community advocates continue to push for transparency surrounding the emergency response.

General Motors announced plans to open a new tech office in Seattle's Denny Triangle neighborhood, expanding its footprint in the city's downtown commercial core. The new facility will support software development and advanced vehicle technology teams as GM scales up its regional operations. Local civic leaders welcomed the investment as a significant positive signal for downtown economic recovery.

**[Boeing Proposes 10% Immediate Wage Hike in New Contract Offer](https://www.seattletimes.com/business/boeing-aerospace/with-its-recovery-at-stake-boeing-sweetens-offer-to-white-collar-union/?utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=owned_echobox_tw_m&utm_source=Twitter#Echobox=1789173137-3)** · The Seattle Times  
Boeing has presented a revised contract proposal to its Washington workforce and white-collar union to head off a potential strike. The offer features an immediate 10% pay increase upon ratification, a 4% raise in 2027, and guaranteed minimum 4% annual increases through 2030. Union members are currently evaluating the sweetened package after rejecting Boeing's initial offer last month.

**[Staffing Shortage Triggers Multi-Year Case Backlog in Snohomish County](https://komonews.com/news/local/backlog-at-snohomish-county-prosecutors-office-leaves-victims-waiting-for-answers-hit-and-run-death-victim-suspect-identity-flock-camera-cctv-public-safety-crime-violence)** · KOMO News  
Snohomish County Prosecuting Attorney Jason Cummings warned that severe attorney shortages have forced nonviolent felony cases into multi-year delays. The prosecutor's office currently has 61 deputy attorneys—matching its staffing levels from 20 years ago despite significant population growth—and faces impending 3% budget cuts. Cummings is calling for prosecutor caseload caps similar to recent public defender standards to address the crisis.

**[Shoreline School District Reaches Deal with Staff, Averting Strike](https://www.seattletimes.com/education-lab/strike-averted-as-shoreline-school-staff-reach-agreement-with-district/?utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=owned_echobox_tw_m&utm_source=Twitter#Echobox=1789178039-3)** · The Seattle Times  
Shoreline Public Schools staff reached a tentative labor agreement with the district, averting a strike planned for Monday. The last-minute deal resolves contract disputes regarding wages and working conditions ahead of the upcoming week. School operations across Shoreline will proceed on schedule without interruption.

**[Questions Raised Over Sound Transit Timeline on I-90 Light Rail Cables](https://komonews.com/news/local/questions-raised-over-sound-transit-timeline-on-i-90-light-rail-cables-killing-fish-link-public-transit-commute-lake-washington-bass-perch-salmonprotected-environmentalism-conservation-seattle)** · KOMO News  
Divers discovered piles of dead fish, including bass, perch, and salmon, near electrified anode cables beneath the I-90 light rail bridge over Lake Washington. Questions have been raised regarding how early Sound Transit was alerted to the environmental risk, with reports indicating notification occurred months before public disclosure. Regional agencies are evaluating the anti-corrosion system to mitigate impacts on aquatic life.

**[King County Holds Anonymous Gun Buyback Event in Shoreline](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/king-county-gun-buyback-shoreline?taid=6aa5a4b86df06e00010d3fc4&utm_campaign=trueAnthem%3A+Trending+Content&utm_medium=trueAnthem&utm_source=twitter)** · FOX 13 Seattle
