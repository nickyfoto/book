**[State Weighs Toll Discounts for Low-Income Drivers](https://komonews.com/news/local/washington-state-department-of-transportation-commission-wsdot-considers-low-income-toll-discount-program-good-to-go-program-cost)** · KOMO News
The Washington State Department of Transportation (WSDOT) and the State Transportation Commission are considering a first-of-its-kind toll discount program for low-income families. The steep discounts would apply to the State Route 509 and State Route 167 expressways, aiming to help lower the cost of driving for residents later this year.

**[Opponents of 'Millionaires' Tax' Push for Repeal](https://komonews.com/news/politics/washington-state-residents-initiative-to-repeal-millionaires-tax-will-take-step-closer-to-november-ballot-governor-bob-ferguson)** · KOMO News
Next week, opponents of Washington State's new capital gains tax will submit signatures in an effort to place a repeal initiative on the November ballot. The controversial measure applies a 9.9% tax rate to individuals or households earning over $1 million annually. Although the tax has been signed by Governor Bob Ferguson, it is not scheduled to take effect until 2028.

**[New $125 Fine for Work Zone Speeding Begins July 1](http://twitter.macworks.dev/komonews/status/2071345395850969380#m)** · KOMO News
Beginning July 1, the penalty for a first citation issued by Washington's work zone speed camera program will increase from a warning to a $125 fine. Fines for second and subsequent violations will remain at $248.
