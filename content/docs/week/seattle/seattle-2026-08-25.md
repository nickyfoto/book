## Local News

**[WA Leaders React After SCOTUS Clears Way for Mail-In Voting Restrictions](https://www.kiro7.com/news/local/washington-leaders-address-scotus-mail-in-ballot-ruling/UGS7TA4KMRBKPHTCAHOK3SQTRI/)** · KIRO 7
The U.S. Supreme Court has sided with the Trump administration, **clearing the way for a nationwide executive order restricting mail-in voting** ahead of the November midterms. In response, Washington Secretary of State Steve Hobbs reassured voters that all local election centers remain prepared to ensure everyone can vote, while state Attorney General Nick Brown expressed deep concern about future federal rulings targeting mail-in ballots.

**[New Denny Way Bus Lane Launches to Fix ‘Always Late 8’ Delays](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/denny-way-bus-lane?taid=6a8d4f101aac09000132419d&utm_campaign=trueAnthem%3A+Trending+Content&utm_medium=trueAnthem&utm_source=twitter)** · FOX 13

The project **fulfills a key 2025 campaign promise by Mayor Katie Wilson** to speed up transit for Route 8, which has seen its on-time performance plummet to 31% during peak summer traffic.

**[Seattle Man Charged with Threatening to Kill Governor Bob Ferguson](https://www.kiro7.com/news/local/seattle-man-charged-with-threatening-kill-governor-bob-ferguson/IQEMWHSWERGPTBVOQQA7NO3C5M/?utm_term=Autofeed&utm_medium=Social&utm_source=Twitter#Echobox=1787696816)** · KIRO 7

**[Bremerton Doubles Vehicle License Fee to Fund Neighborhood Street Repairs](https://bit.ly/4zCOz6e)** · KOMO News
Drivers in Bremerton will soon see an annual vehicle licensing fee increase of \$20 after the City Council voted 5-2 to raise the Transportation Benefit District fee to \$40 per vehicle. According to Council President Jane Rebelowski, **collection could begin as early as this fall**, with the new revenue dedicated to street repairs, potholes, sidewalks, and accessibility upgrades.

**[Bellingham and Blaine Businesses Fear Canada Tariffs Could Drive Away Shoppers](https://www.king5.com/article/money/economy/everything-is-not-fine-bellingham-businesses-fear-new-canada-tariffs-could-drive-away-shoppers/281-1567da6b-c095-46cb-9c80-a0f4f678af43)** · KING 5
