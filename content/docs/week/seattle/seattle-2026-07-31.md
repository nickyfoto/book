# Seattle Local — 2026-07-31

## Top Story
Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson requested the sudden resignation of Police Chief Shon Barnes and appointed Deputy Chief Andre Sayles as interim chief. The major leadership shakeup comes amid intense scrutiny and public outcry over the city's botched communication and delayed response following Sunday's deadly mass shooting at the Bite of Seattle festival.

## Local News

**[Mayor Apologizes for Botched Shooting Response, Ousts Police Chief](https://komonews.com/news/local/mayor-apologizes-for-citys-botched-response-to-the-public-after-bite-of-seattle-shooting-katie-wilson-investigation-fatal-deadly-festival-confirmation-bob-ferguson)** · KOMO News
Mayor Katie Wilson issued a public apology for an hours-long delay in communicating basic safety information following the mass shooting at the Bite of Seattle, which left three dead and four injured. Following the communications breakdown and complaints regarding former Chief Shon Barnes' travel schedule—who was out of town during the tragedy—Wilson asked for his resignation. Several City Council members, including Bob Kettle, have openly questioned the mayor's actions and expressed concern over how the city will recruit a permanent replacement amid the ongoing turmoil. 

**[Sound Transit Unveils Timeline for Ballard Light Rail](https://komonews.com/news/local/sound-transit-announces-timeline-for-building-light-rail-to-ballard-chinatown-international-district-light-rail-stations-in-seattle-public-transit-when-is-the-light-rail-coming-to-ballard-king-county-executive-dow-constantine)** · KOMO News
Sound Transit has officially released its outlined path to bring light rail service to Ballard, estimating completion of the project by the year 2042. The much-anticipated extension aims to significantly improve transit connectivity for Seattle neighborhoods, though residents will have to wait nearly two decades for the long-term infrastructure goal to be realized.

**[First Tiny Home Village Headed to Capitol Hill](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/homeless/capitol-hill-gets-its-first-tiny-home-shelter/#Echobox=1785504733-3)** · The Seattle Times
Capitol Hill is set to receive its first tiny home village to help support the city's unhoused community. The 31-home shelter will be nestled between existing apartment buildings along Belmont Avenue near Olive Way. Additionally, state and local leaders broke ground this week on a separate 67-unit affordable housing development on Capitol Hill, backed by former Seattle Seahawks defensive end Cliff Avril’s real estate firm.

**[State Superintendent Slams Federal Inquiry as a 'Sham'](https://www.seattletimes.com/education-lab/feds-demand-wa-revise-student-policies-on-privacy-transgender-rights/#Echobox=1785464836-3)** · The Seattle Times
Washington State Superintendent Chris Reykdal is pushing back aggressively against a U.S. Department of Education investigation into the state's public school disclosure policies and transgender student rights. Reykdal criticized the federal inquiry, defending the state's protections and lambasting the investigation as a "sham" and a "lengthy fishing expedition on the taxpayer's dime".

**[Fire Crews Contain Brush Fire Near Tacoma Narrows Airport](https://komonews.com/news/local/crews-battle-brush-fire-on-steep-cliff-in-gig-harbor-sheriffs-drone-aids-response-piece-county-brush-fire-dnr-helicopter-drone-water-drops)** · KOMO News
Firefighters successfully contained a brush fire burning on a steep cliffside near the Tacoma Narrows Airport in Gig Harbor on Thursday. A Washington State Department of Natural Resources helicopter was deployed to assist with water drops in hard-to-reach areas. Officials reported that no structures were threatened, and crews remain on the scene to monitor hot spots.

## Community & Lifestyle
Seafair is officially in full swing this weekend at Genesee Park, bringing the beloved tradition of unlimited hydroplane races on Lake Washington and the roaring U.S. Navy Blue Angels. While many residents eagerly anticipate the air shows, local climate coalitions and activists staged protests at Ruby Chow Park, citing environmental and noise concerns. For a quieter weekend activity, the Edmonds Waterfront Center is hosting its third annual Dragon Boat Festival on Friday, honoring a 2,000-year-old tradition. Meanwhile, locals out near the water were treated to a rare and majestic sight Thursday morning as a gray whale was spotted swimming gracefully under the Purdy Bridge in Henderson Bay. Weather-wise, the Cascade Mountains are showcasing a stark divide: Western Washington is enjoying refreshing, cooler temperatures, while the eastern side of the state faces high winds and increased wildfire risks.
