# Seattle Local — 2026-03-31

## Top Story
Washington Governor Bob Ferguson officially signed the state’s first-ever personal income tax into law, establishing a 9.9% "millionaires tax" on high earners. The legislation is designed to fund health care, education, and public safety, but political and legal opponents are immediately preparing constitutional challenges to block its implementation.

The Office of the Washington State Auditor released its annual Single Audit, estimating $37 million in questionable child care subsidy payments for 2025.
