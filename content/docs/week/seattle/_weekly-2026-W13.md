# Seattle Local — Week of 2026-03-25 to 2026-03-26

## Top Story
The NBA Board of Governors has officially voted to explore league expansion in Seattle and Las Vegas, taking a monumental step toward bringing the SuperSonics back to the Emerald City. The announcement has sparked immense excitement among local fans, former Sonics legends, and businesses near Climate Pledge Arena that are anticipating a massive economic boost. 

## Local News
**[King County Limits ICE Access]** · [Seattle Local](#)
The Metropolitan King County Council voted to prohibit U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agents from using non-public county property, such as garages and vacant lots, as processing or staging areas. Concurrently, the Seattle City Council advanced new measures to set limits on surveillance data use and address immigration ahead of the FIFA World Cup.

**[Washington Becomes First State to Regulate AI Chatbots]** · [KING 5](#)
Governor Bob Ferguson signed groundbreaking legislation regulating artificial intelligence chatbots, making Washington the first state to enact such safety measures. The law requires built-in safeguards for minors, including mandatory flags for signs of self-harm, to address growing concerns regarding AI risks.

**[33 Charged Over $1M UW Protest Damage]** · [KOMO News](#)
Thirty-three individuals from the group "SUPER UW" face criminal trespass charges connected to a pro-Palestine demonstration at the University of Washington. The protest caused an estimated $1 million in property damage, though prosecutors noted there is insufficient evidence to pursue felony charges linking specific individuals to the destruction.

**[Seattle Public Schools Mandates Return to Office]** · [KIRO 7](#)
Seattle Public Schools is officially eliminating its remote work options and requiring all employees to return to the office full-time. Superintendent Shuldiner stated that the new mandate was implemented "for the good of the organization".

**[Calls Mount for King County Assessor to Resign]** · [KING 5](#)
King County Assessor John Wilson is facing intense pressure to step down after being charged with violating a no-contact order twice. Prominent local leaders, including King County Executive Girmay Zahilay and Councilmember Claudia Balducci, have publicly urged Wilson to resign from his position.

## Community & Lifestyle
Commuters have a major new transit option launching this Saturday as Sound Transit opens its highly anticipated Crosslake Connection, linking Seattle and Bellevue via light rail across Interstate 90. Meanwhile, the Seattle Mariners are kicking off their 50th milestone season tonight at T-Mobile Park with a brand-new 209-foot video board and high expectations following last year's ALCS run. Travelers heading north should brace for ongoing delays, however, as a massive landslide containing bus-sized boulders continues to block I-5 near Bellingham for a seventh consecutive day.
