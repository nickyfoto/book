## Top Story
A coalition of **38 major Seattle-area business leaders**—including top executives from Microsoft, Starbucks, Costco, Nordstrom, Alaska Airlines, and the Seattle Mariners—sent a joint letter to Mayor Katie Wilson and the City Council demanding immediate public safety reform. The group called on city officials to establish a **100-day public safety action plan** to crack down on open-air drug markets, expand CCTV camera surveillance, and improve police staffing and emergency response times across Seattle.
