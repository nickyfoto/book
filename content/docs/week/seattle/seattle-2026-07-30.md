# Seattle Local — 2026-07-30

## Top Story
Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson accepted the resignation of Seattle Police Chief Shon Barnes following severe communication failures during the city's response to the deadly Bite of Seattle mass shooting. Deputy Chief Andre Sayles has been appointed to lead the department on an interim basis while the city searches for a permanent replacement.
