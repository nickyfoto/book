# Seattle Local — 2026-09-07

## Top Story
A **surge of gun violence** in Seattle's Belltown neighborhood left **three people dead and four others injured** in three separate, unrelated shootings over Labor Day weekend. The fatalities included a man and a woman shot early Sunday morning near an **illegal street takeover**, and a **17-year-old boy killed late Sunday night** near Elliott Avenue and Bell Street.

**[Single-Engine Plane Crashes into Lake Stevens Backyard, Injuring Pilot](https://komonews.com/news/local/small-plane-crashes-in-residential-neighborhood-in-lake-stevens-emergency-crews-on-scene-house-backyard-emergency-aircraft-airplane-cessna-breaking-faa-firefighters-police-investigation)** · KOMO News
A single-engine plane crashed into a **residential backyard near Frontier Air Park** on Sunday evening, trapping and **seriously injuring the pilot**. The aircraft came down just **20 feet from a home** and narrowly missed a **1,000-pound propane tank**, though fortunately, no one on the ground was injured. Snohomish County Fire District #22 and neighbors provided immediate first aid before transporting the pilot to an area trauma center.

**[Wild Waves Water Park Permanently Closes After 50 Seasons in Federal Way](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/wild-waves-water-park-closing?taid=6a9e799261ca2a0001e33dc0&utm_campaign=trueAnthem%3A+Trending+Content&utm_medium=trueAnthem&utm_source=twitter)** · FOX 13
The water park portion of the iconic Federal Way attraction **Wild Waves has permanently closed** after a historic **50-season run**. Park owners cited **years of financial losses since the pandemic and rising operational costs** as the primary drivers behind the decision. While the theme park’s dry rides will remain open through November 1st for seasonal events, a proposal has already been submitted to build a massive, **1-million-square-foot warehouse** on the 66-acre site.

**[Malfunctioning Light Rail Strands Apple Cup Fans in U District Tunnel](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/transportation/light-rail-train-breaks-down-near-husky-stadium-after-apple-cup/?utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=owned_echobox_tw_m&utm_source=Twitter#Echobox=1788749684-1)** · The Seattle Times
Sound Transit is investigating a serious train malfunction that left a packed **Link light rail car stranded in a tunnel** near the U District on Sunday. Thousands of college football fans were heading home from the **Apple Cup** when the train stalled, leaving passengers trapped for **nearly two hours without power or air conditioning**. The incident has sparked frustration among riders and prompted an official investigation into transit reliability during major regional events.

Local leaders utilized the protest to highlight labor rights and press hotel management to negotiate a fair resolution.
