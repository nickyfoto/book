**[Records Show Prior Leaks at Longview Mill Before Deadly Implosion](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/environment/longview-pulp-mill-had-reported-other-leaks-at-tank-that-killed-11-workers-records-show/)** · The Seattle Times
Newly uncovered documents reveal that the massive pulp mill tank at the Nippon Dynawave plant in Longview had sprung multiple leaks in late 2023 and early 2024 before its catastrophic failure. The tank ruptured last month, killing 11 workers and injuring eight in the deadliest industrial incident in recent Washington State history.

**[WSTC Considers Low-Income Toll Discounts for SR 509 and SR 167](https://komonews.com/news/local/washington-state-department-of-transportation-commission-wsdot-considers-low-income-toll-discount-program-good-to-go-program-cost)** · KOMO News
