## Local News

**Missing Arizona Child Rescued in Olympia** · [KOMO News](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AJ-MDoVNj4o)
U.S. Marshals safely recovered an endangered missing girl from Arizona at "The Jungle," an encampment along Interstate 5 in Olympia. Authorities located the child after acting on tips from the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children indicating she may have been a victim of sex trafficking. The massive 20-acre encampment is known to law enforcement for having high rates of violence and ongoing community safety concerns.

**Wildfire Prompts Evacuations in Winthrop** · [KOMO News](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pzJSsmuq29U)
A Level 3 "GO NOW" evacuation order was issued Sunday for areas in and around Winthrop due to the fast-moving Hamilton Fire. The blaze burned approximately 100 acres in Okanogan County before firefighting teams successfully stopped its forward progress.

**Work Zone Speeding Fines Take Effect** · [KING 5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mO80Rub72v8)
Starting July 1, drivers caught by Washington's expanding work zone speed cameras will face a $125 ticket for a first-time offense. The pilot program's grace period is ending as the state cracks down on speeding to boost construction zone safety, with repeat offenders facing fines of up to $248.

**Seniors Stranded by Everett Elevator Outage** · [KING 5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAgxZQhqhqM)
Disabled tenants and seniors living at the Artspace Everett Lofts have been left struggling without elevator access for 40 days. The building's only elevator remains entirely out of service, essentially stranding vulnerable residents who have no other safe way out of the complex.

**'Millionaires Tax' Repeal Heads for Ballot** · [The Seattle Times](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/politics/could-initiative-to-kill-wa-millionaires-tax-also-kill-capital-gains-tax/)
The political group "Let's Go Washington" is turning in signatures to put a repeal of the state's new millionaires' tax on the upcoming November ballot. Opponents argue the initiative could jeopardize other state taxes, a claim that financial backers dismiss as a false scare tactic.

Bellevue police arrested a 30-year-old man in connection with the 2025 death of his wife. Detectives took the suspect into custody following a recent interview, though the homicide investigation remains ongoing.

**New Ballard Coffee Shop Burglarized** · [KIRO 7](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EoLCTuJceCg)
