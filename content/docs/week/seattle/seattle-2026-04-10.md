## Local News

**[Lawsuit Challenges New Millionaires Tax](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LA7IXb78hXA)** · KOMO News
A lawsuit aiming to block Washington's recently approved "millionaires tax" was filed Thursday by the Citizen Action Defense Fund. Supporters argue the tax could fix an inequitable system, while opponents claim it is an unconstitutional income tax. The case's filing in rural Klickitat County positions it for a potentially lengthy legal battle.

**[Seattle Sweeps Homeless Encampments in Beacon Hill](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h4QcTEDyuI8)** · KOMO News
City and state crews removed a network of homeless encampments from Daejon, Lewis, and Sturgus parks in the Beacon Hill neighborhood. Removal notices were posted earlier this week at the sites, which outreach workers described as drug-fueled environments situated close to dealers. 

**[Violent Crime Spike Highlighted by Belltown Apartment Shooting](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BypwmFtuW7k)** · KOMO News
Seattle police are investigating an early morning shooting and robbery at an apartment on 4th Avenue in Belltown that left one man in critical condition with a gunshot wound to the head and another pistol-whipped. The incident highlights a recent spike in violent crime in the neighborhood, and police are currently searching for the suspect.

**[Remains Found at JBLM Identified as Missing Tacoma Woman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mhsQvnc4lec)** · KIRO 7
Human remains discovered in January in a remote area of Joint Base Lewis-McChord have been identified as Aidan Spear, a 21-year-old Native American woman who went missing in Tacoma in January 2022. Pierce County and U.S. Army authorities are actively investigating the case.

**[Alaska Airlines Increases Checked Baggage Fees](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NXgkn_v84y4)** · KOMO News
Citing soaring jet fuel prices and an uncertain global environment, Sea-Tac-based Alaska Airlines is raising its checked baggage fees. Starting Friday, customers will be charged $45 for a first checked bag, $55 for a second, and $200 for each additional bag.
