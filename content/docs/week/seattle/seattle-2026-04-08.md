# Seattle Local — 2026-04-08

## Top Story
The Seattle Police Department has established a permanent Neighborhood Resource Center at Magnuson Park, dedicating three officers to the area to address loud parties, occasional gunfire, and summer chaos. Officials report that this return to localized community policing has already reduced crime by double digits during a recent 90-day pilot program.

## Local News

**[City Advances Funding for 500 Tiny Homes Ahead of World Cup](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/seattle-add-homeless-shelters)** · FOX 13 / KOMO News
A Seattle City Council finance committee advanced Mayor Katie Wilson’s request for $5 million to help stand up 500 new shelter units and tiny homes by June. The push comes as the city prepares for an influx of 750,000 visitors for the 2026 FIFA World Cup. While the measure moves to the full council for a vote next week, some members voiced concerns about recent surges in neighborhood encampments.

**[First Avenue South Bridge Stuck Open Amid Ongoing Repairs](https://www.kiro7.com/news/local/sr-99-bridge-stuck-open-traffic-blocked/53DBBYSYRBGNTLOMPNNQL6QXKE/)** · KIRO 7 / The Seattle Times
The northbound SR 99 First Avenue South Bridge became stuck in the open position during the morning commute, prompting WSDOT maintenance crews to troubleshoot the disruption with no immediate timeline for reopening. The bridge has been undergoing weeks of scheduled repairs ahead of the World Cup after inspectors discovered cracks in its steel deck gates last month.

**[State Raids Kirkland Pharmacy Over Illegal Knockoff Diet Drugs](https://www.king5.com/article/news/local/patients-at-serious-risk-state-officials-find-several-violations-at-mochi-run-pharmacy)** · KING 5
Washington state health investigators raided Aequita, a Kirkland-based pharmacy, finding numerous violations including a refrigerated storage area used for illegal operations. The company illegally imported products to manufacture knockoff GLP-1 diet drugs that were shipped to thousands of people, placing patients at "serious risk".

**[Kent Police Investigate Separate Deadly Overnight Shootings and Stabbing](https://www.komonews.com/news/local/kent-police-investigate-deadly-shooting)** · KOMO News / KIRO 7
Kent police are investigating two separate fatal incidents, including the shooting deaths of two men in the East Hill neighborhood and a fatal stabbing outside a bar on West Meeker Street. Authorities are currently searching for suspects in both cases, with no arrests made yet in the double shooting.

**[Amazon Flex Driver Arrested for Stealing $12K in Packages](https://www.kiro7.com/news/local/delivery-driver-steals-hundreds-amazon-packages-pierce-county/PEL2OJZ3WVDHTFYQ3BALJWFOPE/)** · KIRO 7 / KOMO News
