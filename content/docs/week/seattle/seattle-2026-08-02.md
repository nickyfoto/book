# Seattle Local — 2026-08-02

## Top Story
Governor Bob Ferguson has declared a statewide wildfire emergency and imposed a burn ban through September 30 as multiple fast-moving fires, including the devastating Old Trails Fire, sweep through the Spokane area. Driven by extreme weather and high winds, the fires have already destroyed over 600 structures, prompted Level 3 evacuations for thousands of residents, and become the most destructive wildfire incident in Washington state history.

## Local News

**[Sound Transit 1 and 2 Lines Suspended Following Overnight Derailment](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/seattle-light-rail-train-derailment-leads-to-suspended-service-alternate-routes/#Echobox=1785682787-3)** · The Seattle Times
A Sound Transit light rail train derailed just before midnight while approaching the International District/Chinatown station, causing service suspensions on the 1 Line from Capitol Hill to SODO and the 2 Line from Capitol Hill to Judkins Park. No passengers were on board at the time, and crews are working to clear the incident while providing shuttle buses for impacted commuters.

**[King County Council Approves Sweeping Ethics Reforms Following Grant Scandal](https://komonews.com/news/local/king-county-passes-ethics-reforms-after-program-manager-rewarded-813k-in-grants-to-family-relatives-relationship-corruption-investigation-financial-taxes-taxpayer-economy-paycheck-public-government)** · KOMO News
The King County Council unanimously passed a series of ethics reforms to prevent county employees from using their positions to benefit friends or family. The legislation follows an investigation revealing a county program manager had overseen approximately $813,000 in grant payments to businesses connected to her relatives.

**[South Seattle Counseling Agency Says City Failed to Pay for Victim Services](https://komonews.com/news/local/redeemed-wellness-center-gun-violence-victims-counseling-help-support-services-claims-city-seattle-mayor-katie-wilson-is-not-paying-them-out-tens-of-thousands-of-dollars-money-organization-king-county)** · KOMO News
A South Seattle counseling agency that provides services to gun violence victims has accused the city of failing to pay for two months of their work. The organization claims Mayor Katie Wilson's administration is withholding tens of thousands of dollars owed to them for community support services.

**[Former SPD Chief Receives Six-Figure Departure Payout](https://www.kiro7.com/news/local/former-spd-chief-receives-payout-departure-agreement/QSNPFZ6XQRAHLFNOWHZG6TTWH4/#Echobox=1785640181)** · KIRO 7
Former Seattle Police Chief Shon Barnes is receiving a six-figure payout as part of his departure agreement from the department.
