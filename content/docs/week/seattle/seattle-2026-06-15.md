# Seattle Local — 2026-06-15

## Top Story
Seattle hosted its first-ever FIFA World Cup match at Seattle Stadium on Monday, where Belgium and Egypt played to a 1-1 draw amidst a record-breaking early summer heat wave. The intense weather pushed temperatures to an observed high of 88 degrees at SeaTac on Sunday, prompting widespread heat advisories as massive crowds flooded dedicated pedestrian zones in Pioneer Square to celebrate.

**[Teenager Drowns at Lake Sammamish State Park](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/teenage-girl-drowns-in-lake-sammamish/)** · The Seattle Times
A 15-year-old girl tragically drowned at Lake Sammamish State Park on Sunday afternoon after reportedly falling off a walking trail and into the water. Following the incident, local agencies are urgently reminding the public to practice water safety as residents flock to waterways to beat the heat.

Police are actively investigating the circumstances surrounding both collisions.
