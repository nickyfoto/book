# Seattle Local — 2026-08-24

## Top Story
**The City of Seattle has officially launched a new eastbound bus-only lane on Denny Way between 5th Avenue North and Stewart Street to speed up transit times for the heavily delayed Route 8. Funded by the voter-approved Seattle Transit Measure, the project introduces turning and lane restrictions, removes some parking zones along Boren Avenue, and aims to better serve Route 8's 7,000+ daily riders in downtown Seattle.**
