**[Seattle Mayor wants to ban rental junk fees](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/homeless/seattle-mayor-wants-to-ban-rental-junk-fees/)** · The Seattle Times
The Seattle City Council may soon agree to end hidden rental "junk fees" to ease the burden on local renters. The proposed ban would eliminate charges for pet rent, mail service, in-unit appliances, using common areas, and fees associated with paying rent via checks or money orders.

A three-car collision during a private event at Pacific Raceways in Kent on Sunday afternoon left one driver dead and another injured. The King County Sheriff's Office and local authorities are currently investigating the circumstances of the fatal crash.
