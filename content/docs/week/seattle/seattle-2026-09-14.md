# Seattle Local — 2026-09-14

## Top Story
Thirty-eight Seattle-area business leaders—including top executives from **Microsoft, Starbucks, Costco, Nordstrom, Alaska Air, and the Seattle Mariners**—sent an urgent joint letter to Mayor Katie Wilson and the City Council demanding immediate action against **open-air drug markets and street violence**. The major corporate push comes as regional leaders launch a new partnership to address economic competitiveness following a report showing the central Puget Sound region **lost nearly 7,000 jobs** in 2025.

## Local News

**[Man Critically Injured in Pioneer Square Stabbing Before Boarding Light Rail](https://komonews.com/news/local/man-stabbed-on-light-rail-train-in-downtown-seattle-suspect-at-large-link-public-safety-stabbing-knife-attack-police-investigation-danger-safety-weekend)** · KOMO News
A 30-year-old man was critically injured after being stabbed Sunday afternoon at **Occidental Square in Seattle's Pioneer Square neighborhood**. Following the attack, the victim walked into the nearby Pioneer Square station and boarded a northbound Link light rail train before being treated by first responders at Symphony Station and transported to **Harborview Medical Center**. The **Seattle Police Department** has taken over the investigation, and the suspect remains at large.

**[State Patrol Investigates Car Driving 70 MPH on I-90 Bike and Pedestrian Trail](https://www.king5.com/article/news/local/state-patrol-investigates-after-video-shows-car-on-i-90-bike-trail/281-3c1a1244-845c-4d11-8334-aa74b7f25b8c)** · KING 5
The **Washington State Patrol** is investigating viral dashcam footage showing a white sedan driving along the enclosed **I-90 bicycle and pedestrian trail** connecting Seattle and Bellevue. Witnesses reported the car was speeding at approximately **70 mph** along the paved ten-mile trail reserved strictly for non-motorized traffic. State troopers are actively trying to identify the driver and determine how the vehicle managed to access the pedestrian bridge path.

Both union negotiators and district leadership praised the 11th-hour resolution as a vital win for students and staff across the city.

**[Renton City Council Prepares to Vote on Public Homeless Camping Ban](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/renton-public-homeless-camping-ban?taid=6aa766bea004de0001eccb6e&utm_campaign=trueAnthem%3A+Trending+Content&utm_medium=trueAnthem&utm_source=twitter)** · FOX 13 Seattle
The **Renton City Council** scheduled a final vote Monday evening on a proposed municipal ordinance that would **prohibit homeless encampments on public property**. The measure includes misdemeanor penalties for violations, following similar bans enacted by neighboring Puget Sound cities including **Tukwila, Burien, and Lakewood**. City officials view the ordinance as a necessary measure for public space management, while local advocates monitor its potential impact on unhoused residents.

**[WinCo Foods Resumes Permit Process for First Seattle Location on Aurora Avenue](https://www.seattletimes.com/business/winco-resumes-planning-for-first-seattle-store-after-legal-challenge/?utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=owned_echobox_tw_m&utm_source=Twitter#Echobox=1789396293-3)** · The Seattle Times
Discount grocery chain **WinCo Foods** has submitted new permit filings for a proposed store location at **13550 Aurora Avenue North in North Seattle**. Development plans at the site had been temporarily stalled due to ongoing legal challenges regarding land use and zoning compliance. If approved, the store will mark WinCo's **first location within Seattle city limits**, providing expanded budget grocery access for North End neighborhoods.

**[Independent Team Investigates Death of Man Handcuffed by Seattle Police in Georgetown](https://www.fox13seattle.com/news/man-dies-seattle-georgetown?taid=6aa797f66a20e5000103464c&utm_campaign=trueAnthem%3A+Trending+Content&utm_medium=trueAnthem&utm_source=twitter)** · FOX 13 Seattle
An independent oversight team is investigating an officer-involved death after an incapacitated man died in police custody in **Seattle's Georgetown neighborhood**. Officers initially sat the unhandcuffed man on the hood of a patrol vehicle while waiting for medical aid, but he fell to the ground, rolled into the street, was handcuffed, and subsequently lost consciousness. Investigators are reviewing body-worn camera footage and emergency response protocols to establish the precise cause of death.
