### League News & Culture
Off the field, the geographic landscape of the league might be shifting. According to NFL on ESPN's [NEW INFO following up the Myles Garrett to the Los Angeles Rams trade 👀 | The Rich Eisen Show](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QbuJZKNpjDE), the Chicago Bears have concluded their stadium due diligence and their board has voted to push forward with a potential new world-class stadium project in Hammond, Indiana. If the deal goes through, the franchise hopes to connect northwest Indiana to the south side of Chicago, giving the region a massive economic boost while keeping their midwestern roots intact. 

## League Pulse
The defining storyline right now is the sheer aggressiveness of NFL front offices, with teams like the Rams and Patriots treating first-round picks as expendable currency to acquire proven superstars. Whether it is orchestrating blockbuster trades for elite pass rushers, loading up on receiver talent, or engineering pressure-cooker quarterback battles in training camp, franchises are prioritizing immediate championship windows over slow, methodical development.
