### Draft & Prospects
The Rams selecting Ty Simpson at 13 was universally panned as the head-scratcher of the weekend, with analysts arguing the team stranded Matthew Stafford and wasted a premium pick on a physically limited developmental prospect. Ben Solak tears the pick apart in [Recapping the BIGGEST moments of the 2026 NFL Draft | NFL on ESPN](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hiAB49u8Qs), emphasizing that Los Angeles failed to manipulate the board and reached for a career backup. As discussed in [Are Malik Nabers' draft comments something or nothing? 🤔 + NFL Draft Winners & Losers | Get Up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8sozQrZvQ3U), John Harbaugh brilliantly defused the drama, maintaining team unity and publicly showing Nabers the defensive vision. ### Free Agency & Trades
Howie Roseman strikes again, quietly executing a massive trade during the draft by acquiring edge rusher Jonathan Greenard from the Vikings for two third-round picks. As highlighted in [What Jonathan Greenard trade means for both Eagles and Vikings | Pro Football Talk | NFL on NBC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VlKCWZlSvMY), Greenard instantly inked a four-year, $100 million extension, exposing Minnesota's dire cap situation and inability to pay their top pass rusher. Meanwhile, the wide receiver trade market is entirely frozen. In [Dan O calls Brandon Aiyuk’s situation ODD 😬 What team needs him the most? | NFL Live](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QavRJ7a5Z_Q), Adam Schefter notes the 49ers are refusing to release Brandon Aiyuk despite his contract standoff, while the Cowboys sit in a stalemate with George Pickens, who is refusing to sign his franchise tag while demanding long-term money.
