## League Pulse
The overarching theme across the league today is the ruthless business of roster management and high-stakes investments. Teams are showing zero hesitation in cutting highly-drafted talent at the first sign of severe off-field legal trouble (Terrion Arnold), while simultaneously shelling out massive, top-of-the-market extensions based purely on physical upside (Kyle Pitts). As training camp approaches, the focus is narrowing onto accountability, massive billion-dollar ownership shifts, and which franchises actually did enough in the spring to survive the winter.
