## League Pulse
The pre-draft quarterback debate has reached a boiling point, with analysts fiercely split between the safety and production of Indiana's Fernando Mendoza and the raw physical traits of Alabama's Ty Simpson. Off the field, Jaxon Smith-Njigba's historic $168.6 million extension with the Seahawks has sent shockwaves through front offices, putting massive pressure on the Cowboys and Rams to pay George Pickens and Puka Nacua before the market inflates further. Meanwhile, teams like the Dolphins are undergoing brutal cap-clearing rebuilds, opting to absorb historic dead money hits rather than continually kicking the can down the road.
