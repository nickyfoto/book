## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
While we are well past the draft, the battle of young signal-callers and rising prospects is dominating offseason reps. On ESPN's **[Andrew Berry updates the Browns QB competiton](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VjcX-_0eYfs)**, the Browns GM noted the "incredible strides" from Shedeur Sanders as he battles a healthy Deshaun Watson for the starting job. Over on NBC's **[Do Minnesota Vikings have the QB of the future on roster?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tFN4cgwbrD4)**, the focus is on J.J. McCarthy trying to fend off veteran Kyler Murray in Kevin O'Connell's system, a battle that is keeping the Vikings' QB room incredibly crowded with former top-10 picks. Additionally, ESPN's **[Patrick Mahomes will make over $500M in reworked Chiefs deal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vafl1jSqIB8)** slipped in an update from Seattle that rookie running back Jadarian Price is already turning heads with his receiving ability as he steps up to replace Kenneth Walker. 

### Free Agency & Trades
Blockbuster trades are entirely reshaping the league's landscape. NBC dissected the massive fallout in **[Will Eagles or Patriots win the A.J. Brown trade?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=awS99x35teo)**, analyzing how the Eagles shipped Brown to New England for a 2028 first-rounder and a 2027 fifth-rounder. Over on ESPN's **[Why Jared Verse was the KEY to the Myles Garrett trade](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMAZvXKoFxs)**, we learned that the Rams sending young standout Jared Verse was the absolute linchpin to prying Myles Garrett away from the Browns. In the AFC, NBC's **[Cincinnati Bengals restructure Joe Burrow’s contract](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mYm6fniHnZ0)** broke down how Burrow's deal was reworked to create $10M in cap space to absorb their monstrous acquisition of Dexter Lawrence, while ESPN covered the Chiefs locking up their franchise face with a historic deal valued at over half a billion dollars through 2033. On the drama front, **[Brandon Aiyuk must force San Francisco 49ers’ hand](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLDL-bpgtQ8)** highlights the receiver torching the 49ers on IG as he pushes for a release to sign with the Commanders, and **[Will George Pickens attend Dallas Cowboys’ mandatory minicamp?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Se671khkbTY)** notes that Pickens is keeping the Cowboys sweating on his attendance despite accepting his $27.2M franchise tag.

### Game Analysis & Strategy
CBS offered a phenomenal look into the evolution of the deep passing game in **[Is throwing the deep ball in the NFL like shooting a 3?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xm3e83Fw9Qg)**, with Super Bowl champ Sam Darnold likening the launch angle and mechanics of throwing deep to Steph Curry shooting a three-pointer. Darnold emphasized the importance of using the lower body to generate torque and keeping eyes downfield on the target instead of prematurely watching the ball's flight. It pairs perfectly with the NFL's official compilation video, **[1 Hour of Deep Passes from the 2025 Season](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wGo-_vA8OZQ)**, which serves up the league's best high-flying connections.

## League Pulse
The overriding narrative across the league right now is the staggering movement of elite talent and the financial gymnastics required to make it happen. With superstars like Myles Garrett, A.J. Brown, and Dexter Lawrence swapping jerseys in blockbuster deals, front offices are proving they are willing to prioritize immediate impact over future draft capital as the arms race for the Lombardi Trophy intensifies.
