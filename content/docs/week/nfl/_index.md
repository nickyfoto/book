## Week in Review
The league is an absolute arms race right now, dominated by teams mortgaging their futures for proven, veteran game-wreckers. Whether it’s the Rams landing Myles Garrett, the Patriots paying up for A.J. Brown, or the Bengals reshaping their cap to absorb Dexter Lawrence, front offices are operating with terrifying urgency. Meanwhile, patience for rookie quarterbacks has evaporated, turning mandatory minicamps into high-stakes battlegrounds.

Teams have completely abandoned the traditional, patient rebuild. We are witnessing a ruthless resetting of organizational timelines where front offices are desperate to win now, aggressively mortgaging future assets for veteran superstars.
