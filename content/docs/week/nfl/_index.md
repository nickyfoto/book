## League Pulse
The overriding theme across the league is immense quarterback pressure coupled with front-office desperation, as teams take massive, high-stakes gambles on veteran restructures and contract standoffs to salvage fragile rosters. Top signal-callers like Joe Burrow are declaring a "now or never" mindset, while coaching staffs frantically adjust their schemes to protect quarterbacks behind heavily injured lines. Ultimately, the razor-thin margin between championship aspirations and season-altering injury is keeping the entire league on an absolute knife's edge.
