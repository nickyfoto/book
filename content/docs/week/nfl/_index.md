## Week in Review
The week's news cycle was entirely hijacked by the quarterback carousel, headlined by Aaron Rodgers officially returning to Pittsburgh on a one-year deal for a highly publicized final season under Mike McCarthy. Meanwhile, chaotic rookie battles are already boiling over, with Kyler Murray threatening J.J. McCarthy's job in Minnesota, and the Shedeur Sanders circus making life miserable for Deshaun Watson in Cleveland.

The Shedeur Sanders evaluation is polarizing the entire league, with his undeniable raw talent clashing against sluggish processing times and relentless media interference from his father, Deion. In Cleveland, the rookie is already splitting first-team reps, forcing a do-or-die camp for a rusty Deshaun Watson, as fiercely debated in **[Would the Browns start Deshaun OVER Shedeur to JUSTIFY his draft position? 👀 | First Take](https://www.youtube.com)** · NFL on ESPN. Finally, the Rams' decision to spend the 13th overall pick on Ty Simpson looks every bit like a panicked succession plan for Matthew Stafford.

### Free Agency & Trades
Aaron Rodgers swallowed his pride, officially signing a pragmatic one-year, $25 million band-aid deal with the Steelers to avoid ending his legendary career on a disastrous injury. The move crowds out Mason Rudolph and sets up an unprecedented farewell tour under his old coach, Mike McCarthy, broken down in **[AARON RODGERS' LAST DANCE 🏈 Finishing career with Mike McCarthy & the Steelers | NFL Live](https://www.youtube.com)** · NFL on ESPN. Out west, the Rams locked down reigning MVP Matthew Stafford with a $55 million extension, a move that purposefully maintains their year-to-year leverage over the veteran. In the trenches, the Bengals made a massive splash by trading for game-wrecker Dexter Lawrence to plug their porous run defense, while Jacoby Brissett is staging a completely justified contract holdout away from Cardinals OTAs.

### Game Analysis & Strategy
The tape is forcing difficult conversations about undersized quarterbacks, with analysts highlighting how Bryce Young and Kyler Murray's height limitations cause conservative, underneath throws and poor pocket presence, evaluated in **[Chris Simms’ 2026 QB Countdown: Kyler Murray, Vikings | Chris Simms Unbuttoned](https://www.youtube.com)** · NFL on NBC. Defensively, the Ravens are undergoing a massive schematic shift under new coordinator Jesse Minter, importing a light-box, split-safety system that raises serious questions about Trey Hendrickson holding up against the run. Up in New York, the Giants' offensive braintrust is desperately trying to scheme protection for sophomore Jaxson Dart to keep him upright without neutralizing his dynamic rushing ability, focusing heavily on his under-center mechanics.

### League News & Culture
The league's most glaring self-inflicted disaster belongs to Rashee Rice, who squandered his knee rehab window by violating probation with a failed marijuana test and is spending 30 days in a Dallas jail. Meanwhile, Bill Belichick is attempting a desperate PR "podwash" tour after a miserable, zero-draft-pick first season at UNC, fiercely criticized in **[Bill Belichick, UNC football looking to ‘rebrand’ with media run | Pro Football Talk](https://www.youtube.com)** · NFL on NBC. On the business side, owners officially approved expanding the international slate to up to 10 games, stripping protections that previously kept teams from losing premium home matchups.

## League Pulse
We are watching a brutal collision of aging egos and high-stakes rookie arrivals. The dominant undercurrent across the league is leverage: veterans like Rodgers and Stafford are squeezing out one last run, while desperate front offices in Cleveland, Minnesota, and Vegas bank their jobs on unproven youth to rebuild their foundations.
