### League News & Culture
Off the field, the Chicago Bears are playing a dangerous game of stadium chicken with local politicians. As broken down in **[Bears and Indiana stadium news a 'leverage play’ - Mike Florio | Pro Football Talk | NFL on NBC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nw9hw62JRM8)**, their sudden board vote to advance construction in Hammond, Indiana is a transparent leverage play to squeeze public funding out of Illinois.
