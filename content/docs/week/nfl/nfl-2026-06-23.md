## Highlights by Theme

### Draft & Prospects
The NFL threw a massive roadblock in front of Brendan Sorsby by outright canceling the 2026 supplemental draft. The league issued a brutal letter to the prospect, stating his petition threatened their "core integrity interests" following his college sports betting ban. While the *Rotoworld Football Show* panel eviscerated Sorsby for treating the process like a joke and predicted he will never play an NFL down, *NFL on NBC*’s Mike Florio took the opposite stance, arguing the league's ruling **[oozes with hypocrisy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rO-dy6bLO5c)**. The fallout is dominating the news cycle, with debates raging across **[The Pat McAfee Show](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=avFWklDe6hY)** and **[The Adam Schefter Podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KTdbgy-GYJ8)** about what comes next and whether Sorsby will attempt to **[sue the league](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OO7wy0SX92c)** to force his entry.

### Free Agency & Trades
The tight end market just got completely reset. As covered by *NFL on ESPN*, **[Kyle Pitts is signing a 3-yr/$54M contract with Atlanta](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8UZAutdkuRo)**, making it the most lucrative three-year deal for a tight end in league history. *SportsCenter* unpacked the ripple effects of the Pitts deal while also sharing updates on **[George Kittle's recovery timeline](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6nQr64NNbfE)**. Over on *Pro Football Talk*, the mailbag episode dove into the looming financial decisions surrounding **[Puka Nacua's contract expectations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ecw97Y69zAE)** and the ongoing Vikings quarterback competition.
