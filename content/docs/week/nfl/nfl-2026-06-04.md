# NFL — 2026-06-04

## Watch First
The bombshell of the day is Russell Wilson calling it quits and immediately pivoting to the desk at CBS. If you only watch one clip today, hit up the **[Dan Patrick Show](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1o4gyFPkfZA)** for their fierce debate on whether Russ's early Seattle magic and two Super Bowl appearances are enough to secure a bronze bust, or if his disastrous final chapters in Denver and New York permanently derailed his Hall of Fame legacy. 

## Highlights by Theme

### Free Agency & Trades
The trade market is absolutely unhinged right now, led by the shocking blockbuster sending reigning DPOY Myles Garrett to the Rams and Jared Verse to the Browns. Over on **[The Rich Eisen Show](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_AKUXB_ZfaE)**, Terron Armstead didn't mince words, boldly declaring the Rams could challenge the '72 Dolphins' undefeated record with Garrett now hunting quarterbacks in LA. Cleveland's side of the deal looks much bleaker, as **[Pro Football Talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1OF02S2lxp8)** broke down the subtle warning signs that the Browns were ready to move Garrett, and later evaluated **[Jared Verse's reluctant adjustment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FE0pTm0dySE)** to his struggling new franchise. Meanwhile, the wide receiver market exploded: Drake London secured a jaw-dropping **[four-year, $141 million extension](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ng2paN8TNFo)** with the Falcons, and Christian Watson cashed in with a **[massive $110.5 million deal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FWUsBBesw1A)** from the Packers. Keep a close eye on Vegas, too; Pro Football Talk continues to dissect why a **[Maxx Crosby trade is still very much in play](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2U2C-9nu5zQ)** after a scuttled deal with the Ravens fell apart due to long-term knee concerns.

### Game Analysis & Strategy
Chris Simms dropped his **[highly anticipated 2026 quarterback rankings](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zkzg-x8FI3g)** on Pro Football Talk, crowning Josh Allen at No. 1 and Joe Burrow at No. 2. Simms aggressively dropped Lamar Jackson to No. 3 due to inconsistent play and missed throws, while Patrick Mahomes slid to No. 4—a ranking Simms attributes to an expected adjustment period as Mahomes returns from a torn ACL. Elsewhere on the injury front, the Packers are staring down a catastrophic start to their defensive season, as edge rusher Micah Parsons will **[miss the first chunk of the year rehabbing his own ACL injury](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DY3G9Z_zTU8)** until at least October.

Russell Wilson’s retirement dominated the airwaves, with **[First Take](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iwAaqnR2dhk)** and **[Chris Simms Unbuttoned](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6zDaxOkavzw)** honoring his 14-season career while he officially brings his "Mr. Unlimited" persona to the *NFL Today* desk at **[CBS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fLzpEDQleos)**. Finally, Pro Football Talk tackled the broader gridiron landscape, shredding the NCAA's hypocritical stance on collective bargaining during the **[latest Senate hearings on college sports](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zfd6mmMPnrg)**, while acknowledging the 49ers for rewarding their squad by **[canceling mandatory minicamp](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rAj5-obaszo)** after perfect offseason attendance.

Blockbuster trades and massive contract extensions have completely rewired the league's hierarchy heading into the summer.
