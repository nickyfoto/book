# Seahawks — Week of 2026-08-28 to 2026-09-04

## Top Story
The Seahawks finalized their initial 53-man roster and 17-man practice squad as the franchise transitioned from its preseason finale into regular-season game preparation. While the offense suffered an early blow with running back Zach Charbonnet starting the year on the Reserve/PUP list, General Manager John Schneider fortified the defense by trading for rookie nickel corner Avery Smith and signing veteran cornerback Trevon Diggs to the practice squad. These roster moves, combined with massive contract extensions for defensive stalwarts Leonard Williams and Derick Hall, have Seattle primed to defend their championship title in Week 1.
