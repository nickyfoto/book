# Seahawks — Week of 2026-08-22 to 2026-08-28

## Top Story
In a historic franchise milestone, NFL team owners voted unanimously to approve the sale of the Seattle Seahawks to billionaire venture capitalist Vinod Khosla and his family, officially closing the book on the Jody Allen ownership era. The incoming ownership group immediately signaled its commitment to championship contention, with Neal Khosla rallying the 12s by asserting that winning is "the only thing that matters". Capping off the monumental week, general manager John Schneider secured the core of the defense for the new era by signing All-Pro defensive tackle Leonard "Big Cat" Williams to a massive three-year, \$90 million extension with \$56 million guaranteed.

## 12s Pulse
The 12s navigated an emotional rollercoaster this week, balancing the heartbreak of losing key contributors like Jake Bobo and Bud Clark with immense excitement over Mike Macdonald's aggressive culture, the Khosla ownership transition, and Leonard Williams' monster extension. Off-field banter flourished across Reddit following reports of division rival 49ers owner Jed York's arrest, while former rival Cooper Kupp continued to rapidly win over the Seattle faithful with his veteran leadership. Between spirited debates over backfield health and high anticipation for Jalen Milroe's preseason audition, the fanbase is locked in and ready for the regular season.
