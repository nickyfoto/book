# The 'Pacing' Schism, Regulatory Capture Audits, and On-Device Agents — 2026-09-14

## Highlights
The AI community today is consumed by a high-stakes battle over frontier lab "pacing," regulatory capture, and existential risk narratives. Prominent researchers and investors accuse top labs of using safety hysteria to entrench market dominance and distract from slowing capability gains ahead of IPOs. Simultaneously, practical engineering continues to advance, highlighted by Perplexity and NVIDIA launching local agent harnesses on Windows RTX PCs.
