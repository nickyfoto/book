# The Orchestration Era Arrives Amidst IP Lawsuits and Rogue Agents — 2026-07-10

## Highlights
Today’s discourse is defined by the rapid shift from standalone models to complex orchestration harnesses, as evidenced by Perplexity's sweeping updates and OpenAI's massive rollout of GPT-5.6 Sol. However, this blistering pace of deployment is colliding with stark realities: OpenAI is facing a devastating lawsuit from Apple over alleged hardware IP theft, and power users are discovering the dangerous edge of highly agentic models accidentally wiping local systems.

## Top Stories
- **[Apple Sues OpenAI Over Alleged Systematic Hardware IP Theft](http://twitter.macworks.dev/HedgieMarkets/status/2075688831961931818#m)**: Just weeks before a potential IPO, Apple has filed a lawsuit alleging OpenAI's hardware chief, Tang Tan, exploited system bugs and coached recruits to steal confidential Apple components and bypass exit security. This litigation threatens to severely handicap OpenAI's nascent hardware ambitions, casting a long shadow over their supply chain and raising serious questions regarding their corporate culture.
- **[GPT-5.6 Sol Launches to Great Fanfare—and Disastrous Local Errors](http://twitter.macworks.dev/mattshumer_/status/2075657271401390161#m)**: OpenAI launched GPT-5.6 Sol, achieving a new state-of-the-art on the ARC-AGI-3 benchmark (7.8%) and heavily pushing updates to their ChatGPT Work and Codex modes. However, the release suffered a chaotic start with OpenAI forcing multiple usage limit resets to fix confusing, overly expensive compute defaults, while researcher Matt Shumer reported that Sol running in the new "Ultra mode" accidentally deleted almost all the files on his Mac.
- **[Perplexity Pivots to Orchestration, Crowns Grok 4.5 as Top Model](http://twitter.macworks.dev/perplexity_ai/status/2075660058625790159#m)**: Highlighting that the "model alone is no longer the product," Perplexity CEO Aravind Srinivas leaned heavily into their new Computer harness, which now orchestrates subagents across Opus, Grok, Sol, and others. Surprisingly, SpaceXAI's Grok 4.5 scored highest on Perplexity's internal WANDR research benchmark, beating all other frontier models at roughly half the cost of Claude Opus 4.8.
- **[Neuroscience Upends AI "Scale" Theory with Dendritic Computing](http://twitter.macworks.dev/OwenGregorian/status/2075182946646978859#m)**: A groundbreaking new study revealed that single human cortical neurons are not simple switches, but possess raw processing capabilities equivalent to entire multi-layered deep artificial neural networks. This fundamentally challenges the prevailing scientific theory that intelligence emerges simply from scaling cell counts, offering a potential new blueprint for neuromorphic AI design based on dendritic complexity rather than uniform mathematical points.
- **[Open-Source Advocates Warn of Coming "Civilizational Suicide" via Regulation](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Dan_Jeffries1/status/2075253735563886595#m)**: Prominent industry voices, including Yann LeCun and Adam Thierer, sounded the alarm over an opaque, de facto licensing regime taking shape in the U.S. that threatens to suffocate open-source foundation models. Thierer warned that if national security officials explicitly restrict open-source capabilities through a "restrict-until-permitted" presumption, it will trigger the most critical battle over computational freedom since the Clipper Chip wars of the 1990s.

## Articles Worth Reading

**[Unlocking the Enterprise: Box's Vision for Unstructured Data](http://twitter.macworks.dev/levie/status/2075416313481290077#m)**
Aaron Levie provides a compelling thesis on how the next decade of competitive advantage will be defined by applying frontier models to unstructured data. For years, enterprise knowledge work was rate-limited by human processing, but the deep domain expertise encoded in models like GPT-5.6 and Grok 4.5 allows automated agents to synthesize hundreds of pages of contracts, financials, and diligence materials at scale. Levie argues that the compounding returns of seamlessly connecting an AI to proprietary workflows will prevent the commoditization of enterprise intelligence, even as base models become ubiquitous.

**[The UI Chaos of OpenAI's ChatGPT Work Launch](http://twitter.macworks.dev/thsottiaux/status/2075641131002700120#m)**
OpenAI's rollout of the new ChatGPT Work and Codex modes served as a stark lesson in product design for powerful, multi-agent systems. By burying classic chats, introducing regressions in multi-agent workflows, and failing to clearly communicate the massive limit-burn of GPT-5.6 Sol's higher reasoning levels, OpenAI caused significant user friction. Tibo from OpenAI acknowledged these missteps, leading to immediate UI rollbacks and multiple limit resets while promising better visibility into compute spend in future updates. 

**[MireloAI and Kyutai Introduce Breakthrough Audio-to-MIDI Model](http://twitter.macworks.dev/MireloAI/status/2075536492177354771#m)**
In a fascinating technical update for the generative audio space, MireloAI partnered with Kyutai to launch an advanced Audio-to-MIDI model. Unlike existing tools that require isolated audio stems, this system processes a fully mixed recording and successfully separates it into distinct MIDI tracks for voice, drums, bass, and keys. By also detecting broader musical contexts like chords, key, and tempo, the tool promises to radically accelerate music production and complex sampling workflows.
