# Fable 5's Breakthrough and the Rise of Enterprise "Deploycos" — 2026-07-03

## Highlights
Today's discussions highlight a profound shift in the AI landscape on two main fronts: the jaw-dropping capabilities of Claude Fable 5 rewriting the rules of developer empowerment, and a massive structural pivot toward enterprise deployment over foundational wrappers. As foundational models commoditize, the ecosystem is rapidly realizing that the true value lies in the applied layer and leveraging test-time compute for complex problem framing.

## Top Stories
- **[Claude Fable 5 Redefines Developer Expectations](http://twitter.macworks.dev/mattshumer_/status/2072910608727425499#m)**: Developers are reporting unprecedented empowerment using Fable 5, suggesting that last-generation software needs to be completely rebuilt from the ground up. Users note the model thrives when given the autonomy to use its own judgment, such as delegating tasks to lower-power subagents to save tokens.
- **[The AI Battle Shifts to Enterprise Context and Deploycos](http://twitter.macworks.dev/levie/status/2072875685811716182#m)**: Merely wrapping LLMs is no longer viable; deploying AI requires cleaning fragmented data and utilizing Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) to align systems with business workflows. Big tech is aggressively entering this Palantir-like space, with Microsoft dedicating $2.5B and 6,000 engineers to a new "Frontier Co".
- **[Test-Time Compute Alters Software Economics](http://twitter.macworks.dev/fchollet/status/2072877930469110058#m)**: François Chollet notes that for the first time in software history, test-time compute can be converted directly into competence, making marginal costs critical. Consequently, future jobs will pivot heavily toward complex problem framing and creativity rather than repetitive execution.
- **[Imagination and Dreaming as the Next Training Paradigm](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ziv_ravid/status/2072402889092616309#m)**: The next frontier in model training involves models learning inside their own environments by imagining outcomes, rather than strictly trying them out in reality. 

## Articles Worth Reading

**[The Danger of Concentrated AI Power](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ylecun/status/2073037974153896312#m)** 
Yann LeCun warns that the greatest danger in AI is the concentration of power among a few private entities, comparing closed models to the Ottoman Empire's ban on the printing press. He predicts that foundational models will inevitably commoditize like early internet infrastructure, proving that the real long-term value resides strictly within the application layer.

**[Getting the Most Out of Claude Fable 5](http://twitter.macworks.dev/mattshumer_/status/2073150750411088190#m)** 
Matt Shumer published an in-depth guide on maximizing Claude Fable 5's capabilities, demonstrating its capacity with a self-generating 3D educational game built in just two days. He emphasizes that Fable is capable of "one-shotting" massively complex tasks—like a Pixar-quality educational video—drastically raising the bar for autonomous generation.

**[Bridgewater Replicates Expert Judgment](http://twitter.macworks.dev/miramurati/status/2072050039317578236#m)** 
Mira Murati highlighted a new partnership where Bridgewater used their unique financial knowledge to fine-tune a specialized model. This model specifically helps financial analysts focus on high-leverage tasks, serving as a prime example of domain experts directly improving AI to empower their own workflows.
