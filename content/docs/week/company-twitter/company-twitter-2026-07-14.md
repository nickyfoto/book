## Key Announcements

**Google** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2077092587492573228#m)
Google is bringing its latest Nano Banana model directly into Search via AI Overviews, allowing users to generate custom images on the results page. The company is also rolling out a dynamic, real-time gallery revamp for Google Images on desktop. This marks a significant integration of generative visual capabilities into core search workflows rather than siloed applications.

**Chai Discovery** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/jackdent/status/2077019873147052357#m)
Chai Discovery secured a $400M Series C at a $3.8B valuation, backed by Index Ventures, Sequoia, and Kleiner Perkins. The massive funding round signals high capital conviction that AI-driven drug discovery is transitioning from theoretical promise to scalable, engineered deployment.

**Tencent / Hy3** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ciruai/status/2076809483230007676#m)
A new 298B-parameter Hy3 model release features aggressive 1-bit and 2-bit FPX quants that allow the massive architecture to run on a single 128GB GPU or AMD Strix Halo hardware. This pushes the boundary of local inference, enabling flagship-scale intelligence on radically constrained hardware.

**Singularity** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ycombinator/status/2077078710348640461#m)
Hard-tech defense startup Singularity unstealthed with an $80M Series A at a $400M valuation. Founded by former SpaceX and Anduril engineers, the company is building low-cost air defense interceptors engineered explicitly for automotive-scale mass production rather than bespoke defense manufacturing.

**PrismML** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/PrismML/status/2077084891284721827#m)
PrismML open-sourced Bonsai 27B, positioning it as the first 27B-class model optimized to run locally on smartphones. By utilizing extreme quantization down to 1.125 effective bits per weight, the 3.9GB footprint brings multi-step reasoning and agentic workflows directly to mobile edge devices.

**Anthropic** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/claudeai/status/2077047278078931243#m)
Anthropic launched Claude for Teachers, offering verified K-12 educators in the US free access to premium model capabilities alongside evidence-based curricula mapped to academic standards. Separately, the company committed $10 million CAD to fund new AI research in partnership with Canadian institutions.
