# Company@X — 2026-08-19

## Signal of the Day
**Stripe** has agreed to acquire **OpenRouter**, the leading AI model gateway and routing platform processing 10 trillion tokens daily, cementing its position at the intersection of dollar and token flows.

## Key Announcements

**Stripe** · [Source](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-agrees-to-acquire-openrouter)
Stripe has signed a definitive agreement to acquire OpenRouter, the world's largest AI model gateway and router supporting over 400 models. The acquisition cements Stripe's position as a core infrastructure layer for the multi-model AI economy, bridging both revenue and token flows. Stripe’s Will Gaybrick signaled that the firm's strategic focus is to make moving between tokens and dollars as seamless, safe, and native as traditional currency exchange.

**Anthropic** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AnthropicAI/status/2089842387845804246#m)
In a major milestone for autonomous scientific research, Anthropic demonstrated that Claude could autonomously design novel protein binders from scratch against 14 out of 15 targeted proteins. Bypassing weeks of traditional expert labor, Claude utilized open-source protein design tools like ESMFold2 guided by human-expert prompts. The generated binders were subsequently synthesized and validated in-lab by partners Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience, proving that LLMs can now successfully execute complex, physical-world biological engineering tasks.

**Google** · [Source](https://goo.gle/4wC9HHq)
Google launched Gemini 3.7 Flash globally to AI Pro and Ultra users, securing the #1 rank on the Artificial Analysis AnalystAgent leaderboard for complex real-world data analysis. In addition to speed improvements, the model is integrated into Google Search's AI Mode, enabling real-time generation of custom interactive 3D simulations (like a zoomable Mandelbulb) and customized exam practice quizzes partnered with top education companies. This rollout demonstrates Google's aggressive push to embed agentic capabilities and on-the-fly code generation directly into consumer web browsing and educational tools.

**Rillet** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/nicckopp/status/2089863231821205593#m)
Finance infrastructure startup Rillet announced a \$100 million Series C round at a \$1 billion valuation led by ICONIQ Capital, completing its third funding round in just 14 months. The company's AI-agent-driven platform is seeing 70% month-over-month growth as CFOs utilize Rillet to replace traditional ERP platforms like Oracle Fusion, SAP, and Workday. Alongside the capital, Rillet announced a strategic alliance with EY and formal partnerships with over half of the top 20 CPA firms as it scales its continuous, agent-collaborative ERP.

**Cursor** · [Source](https://cursor.com/changelog/08-19-26)
Cursor shipped a major architectural update introducing cloud agents and subagents capable of running on independent virtual machines. Cloud agents can now autonomously monitor pull requests, track Slack threads, run scheduled tasks, and work towards long-lived objectives via the new `/goal` command. The VM-isolated subagents allow teams to swarm independent fixes and run tests in fresh environments without risking the parent environment's state.

**OpenAI** · [Source](https://openai.com/index/offering-zero-data-retention-for-frontier-models/)
OpenAI committed to continuing its Zero Data Retention policies for eligible frontier model API customers while previewing a new security framework called Private Safety Processing. Designed to address risks across long-lived, autonomous workflows, Private Safety Processing improves safety filtering without giving OpenAI personnel access to underlying enterprise data. This move signals OpenAI’s focus on enterprise privacy as AI transitions from single-turn prompts to autonomous, multi-turn business agents.

**Pocket** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AkshayNarisetti/status/2090123297518391318#m)
Hardware startup Pocket has crossed \$100 million in Annualized Run Rate (ARR) just seven months after graduating from Y Combinator. This achievement makes Pocket the fastest hardware company in YC history to reach the \$100M ARR milestone, with over 270,000 devices currently deployed globally. The rapid scaling demonstrates an unprecedented velocity of adoption for consumer hardware in the modern tech ecosystem.

**Oak Lab** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/sonyatweetybird/status/2090117264712642858#m)
Reinforcement learning pioneer Rich Sutton and Kory Javed left academia to co-found Oak Lab, a startup backed by Sequoia Capital. Oak Lab aims to build a 1-trillion parameter agent running on just 20 watts—matching the efficiency of the human brain—by focusing on continual learning instead of static, human-curated training data. Sutton argues that current LLMs are stuck in local minima because their weights stop changing once they ship, whereas Oak Lab plans to solve catastrophic forgetting to build a self-maintaining mind.
