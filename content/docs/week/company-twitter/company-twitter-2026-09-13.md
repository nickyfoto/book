## Key Announcements

**Boom Supersonic** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/bscholl/status/2098910276360048809#m)
Boom Supersonic released footage demonstrating its engineering team successfully **building and testing jet turbines in-house**. The development directly counters the aerospace industry belief that turbine manufacturing is restricted to legacy prime contractors. By vertically integrating engine turbomachinery, Boom aims to reduce reliance on external suppliers and accelerate propulsion iteration cycles for supersonic aircraft.

**AWS** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/awscloud/status/2099034664161718384#m)

The system leverages AWS cloud infrastructure and real-time edge processing to track player telemetry and on-field metrics during international broadcasts . This deployment highlights AWS's ongoing strategic push to lock in major sports broadcasting data pipelines and enterprise telemetry contracts globally .

**Muse** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/alexandr_wang/status/2099164106490347745#m)

Wang noted that every detail of the model architecture and product interface was purposefully designed and refined through relentless problem-solving. The release underscores a broader industry move toward vertically integrated AI products where custom model development is tightly coupled with specialized software design.
