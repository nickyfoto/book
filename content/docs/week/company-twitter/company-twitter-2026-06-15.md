# Company@X — 2026-06-15

## Signal of the Day
Following its absorption of xAI in February, SpaceX has formally updated its mission statement to focus on "scaling to make a sentient sun to understand the Universe". This signals the complete integration of a major space launch provider and a frontier AI lab into a single technology stack aimed at a post-scarcity transition.

## Key Announcements

**[AWS]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/awscloud/status/2066627201487761678#m)
AWS made its WAF AI traffic monetization service generally available, allowing publishers to seamlessly protect and monetize content being scraped by AI bots. With AI bot traffic growing 300% year-over-year, this enables automated payment collection at the edge without requiring any code changes, creating immediate new revenue streams for content owners.

**[Google]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2066627463350718705#m)
Google has globally launched Information agents in Search for its AI Ultra tier subscribers. These agents persistently track specific topics, tasks, or projects on behalf of the user, delivering comprehensive intelligent updates and actionable links across the web.

**[xAI]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/xai/status/2066625790704521699#m)
xAI announced a developer integration allowing users to run their SuperGrok or X Premium subscriptions directly inside the Warp terminal. Simultaneously, the company rolled out an Agent Dashboard (`/dashboard`) in Grok Build, enabling users to orchestrate multiple autonomous agents, monitor their ongoing tasks, and dispatch new commands from a single interface.

**[Waymo]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Waymo/status/2066574020682940618#m)
Waymo established a fleet partnership with Element Fleet Management to scale its commercial autonomous operations. The collaboration provides end-to-end operational services for Waymo's vehicles, beginning with a deployment in San Diego and slated to expand to further markets.

**[Chainguard]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/chainguard_dev/status/2066555327470764098#m)
Chainguard launched Athena, an operational industry coalition built to defend open-source software against AI-accelerated cyber attacks. The coalition, operating with over two dozen members, has already processed over 20,000 findings and generated more than 2,000 patches across 500 open-source projects.
