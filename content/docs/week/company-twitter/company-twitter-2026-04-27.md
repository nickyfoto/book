# Company@X — 2026-04-27

## Signal of the Day
**Google Cloud debuts 8th-Gen TPUs as enterprise AI hits massive scale.** Sundar Pichai announced the launch of TPU 8t (optimized for training) and TPU 8i (optimized for inference), while Google Cloud revealed that 330 of its customers have processed over 1 trillion tokens, and 35 have crossed the 10-trillion token milestone. 

## Key Announcements

**Microsoft** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/satyanadella/status/2048829906478682304#m)
Microsoft announced its largest M365 Copilot deployment to date, with Accenture and Avanade rolling out the AI assistant to over 740,000 employees globally. Based on data from the first 200,000 users, Accenture reports a 53% improvement in productivity, with routine tasks being completed up to 15x faster.

**AWS & OpenAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/awscloud/status/2048853693357810099#m)
AWS is actively teasing a joint live announcement with OpenAI focused on "what's next in agentic AI". This signals either new product integrations or a deepening strategic partnership between the leading cloud infrastructure provider and the top AI lab.

**Y Combinator** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ycombinator/status/2048834285197812146#m)
YC launched its Summer 2026 Request for Startups (RFS), declaring that AI has transitioned from a feature to the foundational layer. The accelerator is heavily signaling a push into hard tech and physical AI, explicitly calling for startups building counter-swarm defense for drones, electronics and industrial capabilities in space, hardware supply chain infrastructure, and purpose-built inference chips for agent workflows.

**DeepSeek / Hugging Face** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/novita_labs/status/2047913896120578337#m)
DeepSeek V4 is officially live on Hugging Face, bringing a 1M-token context window and a massive-scale Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture. The release is already shifting the local AI ecosystem, with developers successfully running the V4 Flash variant on consumer hardware via 2-bit quantization—noting it feels like the first true frontier model available for local inference.

**Google Workspace** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleCloudTech/status/2048865645995905312#m)
Google introduced Workspace Intelligence, an enterprise layer powered by Gemini that eliminates context silos across Google's application suite. The system functions as a unified reasoning engine, allowing AI to pull deep context and intent seamlessly across a company's docs, emails, and drives.
