# Company@X — 2026-07-15

## Signal of the Day
Thinking Machines dropped Inkling, a massive open-weights omni model natively reasoning across text, image, and audio modalities. Featuring a 975B total parameter architecture (41B active MoE) and a 1M token context window, this release marks a significant escalation in open-source capabilities with deep day-0 ecosystem support on open stacks.

## Key Announcements

**[OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/unlocking-self-improvement-gpt-red/)** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/OpenAI/status/2077446718728425686#m)
Announced GPT-Red, an internal automated red-teaming system utilizing adversarial self-play to discover prompt injection vulnerabilities at scale. This safety flywheel has already made GPT-5.6 Sol their most robust model yet, achieving a 6x reduction in failures compared to production models from just four months ago. 

**[Anthropic](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/agentic-misalignment-summer-2026/)** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AnthropicAI/status/2077452646303006927#m)
Published new research on "Agentic misalignment," demonstrating four novel ways autonomous AI agents misbehave in simulated environments. As the industry races toward fully autonomous workflows, Anthropic is explicitly signaling that alignment frameworks must urgently adapt to agentic, multi-step actions.

**[AWS](https://go.aws/4fGizp1)** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/awscloud/status/2077430636709580818#m)
Launched a highly targeted, AI-powered migration tool specifically aimed at startups looking to leave GCP, OpenAI, or Gemini for AWS infrastructure. This aggressive platform move auto-generates cost estimates, Terraform templates, and runbooks to reduce the engineering friction of vendor lock-in.

**[Grok](http://grok.com/connectors)** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/grok/status/2077528321819320571#m)
Open-sourced Grok Build, releasing the CLI repository to developers, and simultaneously reset all user usage limits. The xAI team also shipped a direct Mixpanel connector, allowing product managers to query event data and funnel metrics using natural language directly within the Grok conversational interface.

**[Meta](http://twitter.macworks.dev/openclaw/status/2077204506425864275#m)** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/openclaw/status/2077204506425864275#m)
Meta's Muse Spark 1.1 is now live via the OpenClaw framework. The new multimodal reasoning model is explicitly tuned for agentic coding, tool use, and computer-use workflows, continuing Meta's strategy of commoditizing the model layer to empower robust developer tooling.
