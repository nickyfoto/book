# Company@X — 2026-07-23

## Signal of the Day
Etched has raised $300M in Series C funding at a massive $10.3B valuation to build custom hardware clusters dedicated strictly to AI inference. Backed by Sequoia, a16z, Jane Street, and others, this round underscores a major hardware industry shift to optimize system-level deployment as inference rapidly becomes the world's largest compute workload.

## Key Announcements

**OpenAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/OpenAI/status/2080339982288568709#m)
OpenAI is rolling out a "Health in ChatGPT" feature for US users, allowing them to securely connect Apple Health and medical records to contextualize health queries. Built alongside physicians, the system tracks sleep, activity changes, and prior test results, while explicitly preventing this connected medical data from being used to train foundation models or target ads.

**Grok** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/grok/status/2080321013565579709#m)
Grok 4.5, described as xAI's most capable model yet, has officially launched across the web, X, and mobile apps. Alongside the model update, Grok introduced "Workflows" to its Build platform, enabling developers to plan and orchestrate hundreds of parallel agents to tackle massive tasks like repository-wide code reviews or bulk issue triaging.

**Google DeepMind** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleDeepMind/status/2080321516814647630#m)
DeepMind announced Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber, a specialized, lightweight model built for security teams to rapidly identify and patch vulnerabilities before exploitation. Demonstrated to catch complex codebase flaws that standard models miss, the model is being deployed through a limited-access pilot strictly for governments and trusted partners.

**Black Forest Labs** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/bfl_ai/status/2080308988961554582#m)
Black Forest Labs released early access to FLUX 3, a unified multi-modal architecture covering image, video, audio, and action-prediction. The model represents a strategic leap beyond media generation, actively extending its single-architecture training into action prediction for robotics.

**Google** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GeminiApp/status/2080401074083799229#m)
Gemini Spark is beginning its rollout to Google AI Pro subscribers in the US. Operating as a 24/7 background personal AI agent, Spark allows users to delegate tasks for autonomous, continuous execution under their direction.

**OpenAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/OpenAI/status/2080378182469857576#m)
ChatGPT Voice is deploying globally on macOS and Windows desktop apps for paid and enterprise tiers, powered by the GPT-Live architecture. The update allows users to direct multiple agents simultaneously using only their voice, seamlessly speaking, listening, and coordinating work across applications like Codex.
