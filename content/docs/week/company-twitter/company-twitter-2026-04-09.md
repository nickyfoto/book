# Company@X — 2026-04-09

## Signal of the Day
OpenAI fundamentally restructured its pricing tiers around AI coding, introducing a new $100/month ChatGPT Pro subscription specifically targeting "longer, high-effort Codex sessions". This highlights that intensive, multi-hour AI development has matured into a distinct, highly monetizable enterprise user behavior that requires more dedicated compute capacity than standard consumer chat.

## Key Announcements

**OpenAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/OpenAI/status/2042295688323875316#m)
OpenAI launched a $100/month Pro tier providing 5x more Codex usage than the Plus plan, aimed at heavy developers. Plus users will see their Codex usage rebalanced to favor steady, week-long sessions over long single-day bursts, while existing $200 Pro users received an extension on their 2x usage multiplier. This tiered approach cements OpenAI's strategy of capturing higher margins from developers relying on Codex for complex workflows.

**AWS** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/awscloud/status/2042298042204700891#m)
AWS introduced a preview of the AWS Agent Registry, natively available through Amazon Bedrock AgentCore. By providing a searchable discovery and governance layer to manage agents regardless of where they are built or hosted, AWS is positioning itself as the critical operational orchestrator for enterprise multi-agent systems. 

**Waymo** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Waymo/status/2042227277723763076#m)
Waymo partnered with Waze to integrate its autonomous fleet data with community reports to help cities detect and repair potholes faster. This is a sharp strategic move that transforms Waymo's continuous AV sensor scanning into valuable civic infrastructure mapping, offering cities secondary benefits from robotaxi deployments.

**Cursor** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/cursor_ai/status/2042287192895267212#m)
Cursor shipped an update allowing its cloud agents to automatically attach functional visual demos and screenshots to the pull requests they open. This bridges a critical gap in autonomous coding by giving human reviewers immediate visual context directly within GitHub, turning agents into first-class contributors in standard DevOps environments.

**Google Workspace & DeepMind** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleWorkspace/status/2039735565696786919#m)
Google integrated Veo 3.1 directly into Google Vids, rolling out high-quality AI video generation to all users at no cost. Concurrently, DeepMind reported that its Gemma 4 model—which outperforms models 10x its size without massive compute—surpassed 10 million downloads in its first week, indicating fierce adoption in the open research community.

**Meta** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AIatMeta/status/2042360012576866686#m)
Meta highlighted impressive community momentum around Muse Spark (available via the Meta AI app), which is demonstrating advanced capabilities in inferring product logic. Developers are using the model to seamlessly convert screenshots into code with properly extracted assets, build speed tests, and one-shot 3D table tennis games with audio.

**Skild AI / Sequoia** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/stephzhan/status/2042119298106466631#m)
Backed by Sequoia, Skild AI showcased a single, end-to-end learned robotics policy capable of handling complex, long-horizon tasks. Without stage-wise programming or hand-coded transitions, the identical base model operates on a "pixels in, motor control out" architecture to reliably cook scrambled eggs or assemble Blackwell GPUs on a server rack.

**Hugging Face** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/tomaarsen/status/2042239810631241992#m)
Hugging Face released Sentence Transformers v5.4, pushing the architecture to be fully multimodal for both embeddings and reranking. Featuring a modular CrossEncoder and automatic Flash Attention 2 input flattening, this update provides a massive performance and capability upgrade for engineers building state-of-the-art retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines.
