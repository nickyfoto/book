# Company@X — 2026-06-05

## Signal of the Day
For the first time in internet history, agentic traffic has surpassed human traffic online, according to Cloudflare Radar. This systemic shift is fundamentally altering internet infrastructure and data systems, as APIs originally built for human interactions are now being overwhelmed by massive swarms of autonomous agents performing delegated tasks.

## Key Announcements

**[Y Combinator](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ycombinator/status/2062649272911438132#m)**
Y Combinator announced two portfolio decacorns in a single day: Helion Energy and Supabase. Helion secured a $465M Series G at a $15.5B valuation to build a commercial fusion power plant, backed by a Microsoft power purchase agreement, while database provider Supabase raised a $500M Series F at a $10B valuation, driven by explosive AI tool adoption.

**[Hugging Face](http://twitter.macworks.dev/victormustar/status/2063017894221591008#m)**
The open-source AI ecosystem experienced an unprecedented week with over 25 major open-weight drops across every modality. Standouts include NVIDIA’s Nemotron 3 Ultra (a 550B hybrid Mamba-MoE), Google’s Gemma 4 12B, and Ideogram 4, which released its first-ever open-weight image generation model. 

**[Google](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2062977921384341884#m)**
Google launched the Fitbit Air, a lightweight, entirely screenless wearable fitness tracker. On the software side, the company showcased advanced text-rendering capabilities for its Gemini Omni model, which can now actively sync generated typography with physical onscreen movements and textures without breaking visual consistency.

**[Anthropic](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AnthropicAI/status/2062979607448682731#m)**
Anthropic released research detailing Claude's new capabilities as an autonomous tool for chemists. The company revealed that its Opus 4.7 model now matches—and in some tasks, beats—dedicated software for interpreting NMR (nuclear magnetic resonance) spectroscopy, a crucial step for manipulating molecular structures.

**[Miso Labs](http://twitter.macworks.dev/SurKopu/status/2062435710301528560#m)**
Miso Labs open-sourced an 8-billion parameter voice model capable of generating speech with genuine emotional prosody at an ultra-low 110-millisecond latency. This release drastically narrows the moat of closed-source leaders like ElevenLabs by making highly emotive, low-latency voice cloning freely available under an Apache 2.0 license.

**[Cursor](http://twitter.macworks.dev/cursor_ai/status/2062950344687272144#m)**
The AI code editor introduced "Design Mode," a feature allowing developers to shrink the communication gap between visual intent and agent comprehension. Users can now simply point, draw, or talk to prompt the system to dynamically update UI elements.
