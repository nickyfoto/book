# Company@X — 2026-09-10

## Signal of the Day
OpenAI expanded its enterprise and developer footprint by releasing GPT-Live-1 in the API for real-time streaming voice agents alongside ChatGPT for Financial Services, a specialized workspace powered by GPT-6 Astra reasoning.

## Key Announcements

**OpenAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/OpenAI/status/2098118191029624911#m)
OpenAI announced the API availability of GPT-Live-1, enabling developers to build voice agents that listen while speaking with low-latency, natural back-and-forth interaction. Simultaneously, the company launched ChatGPT for Financial Services—a tailored tier combining GPT-6 Astra reasoning with integrated financial datasets from Daloopa, PitchBook, and LSEG News to build auditable financial models and pitchbooks. OpenAI also introduced a new Data agent in ChatGPT Work that connects directly to enterprise data sources to generate interactive dashboards and automated analysis.

**Skild AI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/SkildAI/status/2098093862548099111#m)
Skild AI crossed \$100 million in ARR just 10 months after its first commercial deployment. The physical AI startup has deployed general-purpose robotics models across factory lines, construction sites, and commercial kitchens, including high-precision assembly of Nvidia Blackwell systems with Foxconn. The milestone demonstrates unprecedented commercial velocity and operational scaling for embodied AI in real-world industrial environments.

**Cursor** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/cursor_ai/status/2098162488013455784#m)
Cursor introduced "Projects" in beta, transitioning its developer environment from task-specific chat threads to a persistent coordinator agent. The coordinator agent operates continuously in a single thread, delegating work to subagents, maintaining shared project memory, and handling background tasks like monitoring GitHub PRs, resolving CI issues, and tracking Slack bug reports. This launch signals a broader paradigm shift from interactive coding assistants toward fully autonomous software engineering agents.

**Google** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2098084519320383940#m)
Google launched its native Gemini desktop application globally for Windows 10 and 11. Triggered via an `Alt + Space` system-wide shortcut, the application integrates Gemini's text drafting, document summarization, and multimodal generation tools alongside native desktop software. The release highlights a strategic push to embed AI assistant capabilities directly at the desktop operating system layer.

**Anthropic** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AnthropicAI/status/2098097512544444447#m)
Anthropic published a comprehensive threat intelligence report documenting sophisticated misuse attempts targeting Claude across cyberattacks, influence operations, surveillance, and biological research. The company confirmed it disrupted every identified operation between December 2025 and August 2026 and shared threat indicators with law enforcement and peer AI labs. Publicizing these real-world attack vectors aims to establish empirical benchmarks for AI safeguards and infrastructure monitoring.

**The Boring Company** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/boringcompany/status/2097916729246810158#m)
The Boring Company closed a \$3 billion Series D financing round led by the United Arab Emirates, valuing the tunneling infrastructure venture at \$23 billion. Key investors in the round included Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, Valor Equity Partners, Human Capital, Vy Capital, and Temasek. The massive capital injection underscores sustained sovereign and institutional backing for underground transit and infrastructure scale-outs.

**Microsoft** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/BradSmi/status/2097711623180099702#m)
Microsoft partnered with the American Federation of Teachers (AFT) to establish the National AI Safety & Privacy Standard for Schools. The framework creates legally enforceable protections that prohibit student data collection for AI model training or commercial targeting while defining strict boundaries for classroom technology. The agreement sets a formal labor and governance benchmark for institutional AI adoption in public education.
