# Company@X — 2026-07-21

## Signal of the Day
**Google** dramatically expanded its Gemini model family with three new variants—Gemini 3.6 Flash, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, and Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber—specifically engineered to scale autonomous agentic workflows. This rollout signals a critical industry shift from general-purpose foundation models to highly optimized, cost-efficient AI engines built specifically for low-latency production agents and automated security patching.

## Key Announcements

**Google** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2079589747366724030#m)
Google released three new models optimized for speed and token efficiency in agent architectures. Gemini 3.6 Flash provides up to 65% token-usage reduction on output while improving coding and reasoning, and Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber is being deployed via Google's CodeMender agent to autonomously locate and patch critical software vulnerabilities. 

**Microsoft & Mistral AI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/BradSmi/status/2079544838257881527#m)
Microsoft and Mistral AI expanded their strategic partnership, bringing Mistral's frontier European models to Azure, Microsoft Foundry, Copilot Studio, and Azure Local. This is a targeted strategic play for the European enterprise market, giving regulated industries access to frontier capabilities without surrendering digital sovereignty or operational control over their data.

**Applied Intuition** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AppliedInt/status/2079582667465736690#m)
Applied Intuition launched Dana, an agentic integrated development environment (IDE) aimed at designing and deploying physical AI applications. By connecting simulators, world models, and data pipelines under a single agentic layer, Dana attempts to remove the tooling bottleneck for autonomous robots and intelligent physical machines.

**NVIDIA** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/HuggingApps/status/2079255503914332371#m)
NVIDIA introduced Nemotron, a new audio-native, open-weight model available in 2B and 30B parameters. The model handles transcription, translation, and full speech-to-speech interactions natively by processing sound rather than relying on text intermediaries. 

**Unsloth** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/UnslothAI/status/2079207457788952944#m)
Unsloth launched hardware support for AMD, enabling developers to natively train and run over 500 LLMs on AMD GPUs. Using custom Triton kernels and optimized math algorithms, it enables models like Qwen and Gemma to run up to 2x faster with 70% less VRAM on Radeon, Instinct, and Ryzen architectures.

**HandoffAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/dmitry_alexin/status/2079572374043705477#m)
HandoffAI unveiled H1, an autonomous model that can perform construction takeoffs at the fidelity of a human estimator. The model converts standard blueprints into machine-readable data, automatically calculating detailed material budgets down to individual studs and nails.
