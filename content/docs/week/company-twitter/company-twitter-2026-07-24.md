# Company@X — 2026-07-24

## Signal of the Day
A massive coalition of tech heavyweights—including Microsoft, NVIDIA, Meta, a16z, and Hugging Face—published a joint letter defending open-weight AI models. This coordinated policy push frames open models as an essential requirement for global competitiveness, cybersecurity, and sovereign innovation, aiming to steer the regulatory narrative away from locked-down, closed-ecosystem mandates.

## Key Announcements

**Anthropic** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/claudeai/status/2080699495453528290#m)
Anthropic introduced Claude Opus 5, describing it as a thoughtful and proactive model. It reportedly approaches the frontier intelligence of competitor Fable 5 but operates at half the price, signaling a fierce pricing war at the most advanced model tier.

**xAI (Grok)** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/grok/status/2080716512009343207#m)
Grok has officially integrated into Google Workspace via a unified add-on for Docs, Sheets, and Slides to automate drafting and reporting. Concurrently, xAI highlighted that Grok 4.5 outperformed Gemini Flash 3.6, GPT 5.6 Terra, and Sonnet 5 in a 150,000-invoice zero-click processing benchmark run by Ramp.

**AMD** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/LysandreJik/status/2080661807010902127#m)
AMD's new MI455X GPU ships with 432GB of HBM4, packing more memory than five NVIDIA H100s combined. With early access, Hugging Face reports that a standard 4-GPU server (1.7TB memory) can now comfortably hold the FP8 weights of a trillion-parameter model in a single machine.

**Induction Labs** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/induction_labs/status/2080322704973160760#m)
The YC-backed startup emerged from stealth to launch "imagination models," a new foundation architecture that learns from internet-scale, unlabeled video. Their first model, Photon-1, learned to use a computer entirely by watching 18 years' worth of screen recordings.

**OpenCode** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ycombinator/status/2080655833810231505#m)
The open-source AI coding agent revealed explosive scale, hitting 4.6 million weekly active users, processing 7 trillion tokens daily, and reaching roughly $40M in annualized revenue. The platform's enterprise growth was reportedly accelerated by an Anthropic API clampdown that drove massive developer demand for model-agnostic tooling.

**Google** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/HuggingApps/status/2080663048365150291#m)
Google published `gmn`, a parametric differentiable 3D head model that operates efficiently on a CPU. The model allows for every facial expression and movement to be differentiable and malleable, with an interactive demo currently hosted on Hugging Face Spaces.
