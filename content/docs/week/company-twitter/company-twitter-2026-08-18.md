## Key Announcements

**OpenAI** · [Source](https://openai.com/index/pacing-model-development-cyber-capabilities/)
OpenAI announced a voluntary **two-week pause** on reinforcement learning (RL) training for its deployment-bound models to harden and red-team its research environments. The pause keeps their largest planned frontier RL runs on hold while smaller-scale runs validate new security measures. To support safer development, the lab introduced **stronger workload and network isolation**, continuous security testing, and expanded multi-stage monitoring to detect concerning agent behaviors quickly.

**Etched** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Etched/status/2089729087732605282#m)
ASIC developer Etched has raised **\$700 million** in new funding at a **\$21 billion valuation**, led by Jane Street with participation from top-tier venture firms like Sequoia, a16z, Kleiner Perkins, and Blackstone. Crucially, the hardware startup has already shipped its first **customer server rack** to Jane Street, demonstrating significant execution following their successful A0 tape-out earlier this year. The massive capital injection is positioned to help fuel the production of their specialized Transformer-only chips designed to maximize intelligence-per-watt inference.

**Anthropic** · [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/research/Claude-accelerates-protein-design)
Anthropic demonstrated that Claude can autonomously design **novel protein binders** from scratch, successfully targeting **14 out of 15 proteins** when guided by an expert prompt. In laboratory testing conducted with Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience, Claude's binders achieved a success rate of **22% to 35%**, drastically outperforming the typical industry success rate of 10% to 15%. While high-affinity binders represent only the initial phase of drug development, Anthropic is actively teaching Claude to run the entire pharmaceutical development process end-to-end.

**Google DeepMind** · [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16884v1)
Google DeepMind, alongside academic collaborators and its Gemini-powered coding agent **AlphaEvolve**, announced a new record for matrix multiplication complexity, reducing the theoretical upper limit of omega to **ω < 2.371177**. Lowering this exponent represents a major theoretical breakthrough because matrix multiplication is the core computational bottleneck of modern AI training and inference. The achievement highlights how LLM-powered coding agents are actively driving fundamental breakthroughs in complexity theory and computer science.

**Rillet** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/nicckopp/status/2089863231821205593#m)
Modern finance platform Rillet raised a **\$100 million Series C** at a **\$1 billion valuation** led by ICONIQ Capital, representing its third funding round in 14 months and bringing its capital to over \$200 million. CFOs are increasingly adopting the platform to replace legacy ERPs like Oracle Fusion, SAP, and Workday, driven by AI agent usage that is expanding at **70% month-over-month**. Alongside the funding, Rillet announced a strategic alliance with EY and has officially partnered with more than half of the top 20 CPA firms.

**Moving Atoms** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/bradflora/status/2089512091489329417#m)
Spun out of TikTok and Oxford, YC startup Moving Atoms launched its first video world model, **Atom 1**, designed specifically for training physical robotics. The model is trained on internet-scale video and conditioned on robot actions to accurately predict real-world physical interactions. Atom 1 has already taken the top spot on DeepMind’s **Physics-IQ benchmark**, notably outperforming NVIDIA's Cosmos 3 hardware model.
