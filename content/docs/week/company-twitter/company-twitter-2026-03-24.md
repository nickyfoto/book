# Company@X — 2026-03-24

## Signal of the Day
Figma and Cursor have fundamentally bridged the design-to-code gap via new agentic workflows. Figma launched a `use_figma` Model Context Protocol (MCP) tool in open beta, allowing AI agents to design directly on the canvas, while Cursor simultaneously integrated the capability to generate code components and frontends directly from a team's Figma design system.

## Key Announcements

**Figma & Cursor** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/figma/status/2036434766661296602#m)
Figma has opened beta access for AI agents to interact directly with its canvas via the new `use_figma` MCP tool. In tandem, Cursor announced it can now create new frontend components in Figma that strictly adhere to a team’s existing variables, tokens, and naming conventions. This signals a major maturation from generic UI mockups to production-grade design system implementation driven by agents.

**Meta & Arm** · [Source](https://x.com/AIatMeta)
Meta announced a strategic partnership with Arm to co-develop multiple generations of purpose-built CPUs. This collaboration is specifically targeted at supporting Meta's massive internal compute and artificial intelligence infrastructure needs, signaling a continued push by hyperscalers to vertically integrate their silicon.

**xAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Designarena/status/2036533116605702176#m)
Grok Imagine by xAI debuted its reference image-to-video model, immediately taking first place overall on the Multi Image to Video Arena with an Elo score of 1342. The release establishes a new Pareto frontier for preference versus speed, achieving an average generation time of just 58.9 seconds.

**Anthropic** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AnthropicAI/status/2036499691571953848#m)
Anthropic published its new "Economic Index," revealing how user behavior shifts as they gain experience with Claude. Data shows that longer-term users hand over less full autonomy to the AI, choosing instead to iterate carefully on higher-value tasks and achieving more successful outcomes. Broader consumer use is also decentralizing; the top 10 tasks now make up 19% of conversations, down from 24% in November 2025.

**Google** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2036535458809700822#m)
Google is aggressively pushing multimodal AI into the living room with the rollout of Gemini for Google TV. New features include live visual sports scorecards, video tutorials, and narrated "deep dives" breaking down educational topics like economics and technology directly on the television.

**Y Combinator** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/nemild/status/2036518663642292426#m)
YC’s Winter 2026 Demo Day revealed record-breaking startup velocity, with the batch of nearly 200 companies averaging 14% week-over-week revenue growth—the fastest in YC history. The cohort heavily features AI agents, robotics, and defense tech, including autonomous warehouse manipulation, defense drone detection, and Spade Data, which recently raised a $40M Series B to process up to 1.9 billion bank transactions daily.
