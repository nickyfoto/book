# Company@X — 2026-07-01

## Signal of the Day
Anthropic is redeploying Claude Fable 5 globally following negotiations with the US government, signaling a major industry shift toward standardized vulnerability frameworks as they co-draft new jailbreak assessment protocols alongside Amazon, Microsoft, and Google.

## Key Announcements

**Anthropic** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AnthropicAI/status/2072163884430229756#m)
After addressing cybersecurity concerns, Anthropic is restoring global access to Claude Fable 5 with updated classifiers designed to block misuse. The company is routing some routine coding tasks to Opus 4.8 in the near term to minimize false positives, while scaling pre-release model testing collaborations with the US government.

**AWS** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/awscloud/status/2072390337117536654#m)
Amazon Bedrock in AWS GovCloud (US) now hosts OpenAI GPT OSS and NVIDIA Nemotron 3 open-weight models. Achieving FedRAMP High and DoD IL-4/5 approvals, this deployment allows defense and government agencies to run frontier models within strict zero-trust compliance boundaries.

**xAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/xai/status/2072342803787702422#m)
The company launched Voice Agent Builder in beta, offering a tightly coupled, no-code platform for creating human-like voice agents at $0.05 per minute. By building directly on Grok Voice rather than stitching together separate speech and language APIs, xAI aims to reduce the latency, cost, and failure modes typical of legacy voice stacks.

**Google** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2072409250312425824#m)
Google is bringing its Gemini Spark agent to macOS in a beta for US-based Google AI Ultra subscribers. The release adds significant workflow utility through custom Model Context Protocol (MCP) support and direct integrations with third-party apps like Canva and Dropbox, as well as native Google Tasks syncing.

**Google Cloud** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleCloudTech/status/2072326264405287064#m)
Google Cloud launched a preview of Gemini Omni Flash, a model enabling conversational video generation and editing directly within applications. The multimodal API allows developers to build dynamic video workflows with text and action synchronization.
