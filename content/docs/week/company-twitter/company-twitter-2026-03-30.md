# Company@X — 2026-03-30

## Signal of the Day
Space-based infrastructure startup Starcloud raised a $170M Series A at a $1.1B valuation to build orbital data centers for AI inference. Having already deployed an Nvidia H100 and trained an LLM in space, the company aims to make its next satellite, Starcloud-3, cost-competitive with Earth-based data centers.

## Key Announcements

**AWS** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/awscloud/status/2038659548714594787#m)
Toyota used Amazon Q Developer to reverse-engineer 45 years of mainframe COBOL code in a single day. This highlights a major enterprise use case for agentic AI, turning multi-month documentation efforts into a rapid AWS Transform migration pipeline.

**Hugging Face** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/xenovacom/status/2038610331417608691#m)
Launched Transformers.js v4, introducing a new WebGPU backend that supports browsers, Node.js, Bun, and Deno. The release features a complete codebase refactor and supports over 200 model architectures, significantly advancing client-side and edge ML capabilities.

**Google** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GeminiApp/status/2038647896300826853#m)
Gemini Live is now officially powered by a newly designated Gemini 3.1 Flash Live model. Simultaneously, Google Cloud is pushing its agentic workflow capabilities, demonstrating the creation of a summarizer and translator multi-agent system in under two minutes using Gemini and ADK.

**Open-Source AI (Community)** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/UnslothAI/status/2038625148354679270#m)
A locally runnable model—Qwen3.5-27B fine-tuned on distilled reasoning data from Claude 4.6 Opus—has dominated Hugging Face trending charts for weeks. The model beats Sonnet 4.5 on SWE-bench verified tasks and runs locally on 16GB in 4-bit, signaling a rapid maturation of localized agentic reasoning.

**Waymo** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Waymo/status/2038617268687388879#m)
The autonomous driving company expanded the localized user experience of its Waymo Driver. The robotaxis now welcome riders in seven new languages, including Japanese, French, and German, suggesting broader market readiness and a focus on international tourism.
