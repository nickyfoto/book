# Company@X — 2026-07-27

## Signal of the Day
**NVIDIA** has officially introduced the Open Secure AI Alliance, partnering with open-source entities like **OpenClaw** to standardize security and develop new defense techniques for AI software and agents. This move signals a strategic industry consolidation to build defensive tooling in the open, broadening the community of defenders as agentic workflows move into production environments.

## Key Announcements

**[NVIDIA & OpenClaw]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/nvidia/status/2081666629264449730#m)
NVIDIA launched the Open Secure AI Alliance, aiming to share models, tooling, and research to collaboratively safeguard AI ecosystems. OpenClaw, a founding member, noted that NVIDIA has been an instrumental partner in securing their agentic framework, highlighting an industry shift toward open, collaborative AI defense mechanisms.

**[Google Cloud]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/boredabdel/status/2079923455319847134#m)
Google Cloud highlighted the immediate availability of Gemini 3.6 Flash on AI Studio. The model refresh claims over a 40% efficiency gain on input tokens, a 45% gain on output tokens, and an overall 10% reduction in total time compared to the previous 3.5-flash iteration.

**[Anthropic]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AnthropicAI/status/2081864750296658008#m)
Addressing ongoing industry speculation, Anthropic published a formal position piece detailing their official stance on open-weights models. This release clarifies the company's strategic positioning and policy views in the ongoing debate surrounding open versus closed model ecosystems.

**[Google Cloud]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/shirmeir86/status/2074532660920332383#m)
Google Cloud's developer relations highlighted a maturing stack for production-grade AI agents, showcasing the Antigravity SDK for creating automated workflows. The company is actively pushing developers to orchestrate long-running, scalable agents using Google's ADK, Gemma 4, and BigQuery MCP via Cloud Run.

**[Physical Intelligence]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/karolina_dubiel/status/2081534295823589877#m)
During YC Startup School, co-founder Chelsea Finn revealed details on how their generalist robot model, π, addresses the critical memory bottleneck in robotics. By combining short-term video memory with long-term text summaries of the past 10–15 minutes, π can autonomously execute multi-step tasks and demonstrate compositional transfer to new hardware platforms.
