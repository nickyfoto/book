## Key Announcements

**OpenClaw** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/openclaw/status/2068567818862485740#m)
OpenClaw released v2026.6.9, delivering deeper integration with Codex that includes automatic plugin approvals and remote-node execution as a dynamic tool. The framework update slims down its core distribution by moving official provider plugins to standalone npm releases, while substantially improving agent recovery mechanics to ensure interrupted AI tasks reach terminal outcomes.

**Google Cloud** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleCloudTech/status/2068770931447570457#m)
Google Cloud revealed that it successfully migrated some of Google’s largest-scale production machine learning models from TensorFlow to JAX. Notably, this massive infrastructure translation was executed by deploying specialized, multi-agent AI systems, proving the viability of using agentic workflows for complex, production-grade syntax and framework updates.

**Google Cloud** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleCloudTech/status/2068670772520931556#m)
Accelerating its agent development tooling, Google demonstrated how to feed an OpenAPI spec directly into its Agent Development Kit (ADK). This integration allows developers to instantly connect backend APIs to their language models, transforming them from standard chatbots into capable system agents.

**Boom** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/bscholl/status/2068781524657336754#m)
The supersonic aviation manufacturer successfully assembled the Symphony high-pressure turbine rotor and its first two compressor stages. Facing supply chain bottlenecks with legacy vendors on the Stage 3 blisk, Boom announced a strategic pivot toward vertical integration and intends to manufacture future engine blisks in-house to maintain operational velocity.

**Tesla** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Tesla/status/2068519225623466074#m)
Tesla showcased the practical maturation of FSD v14.3.4, amplifying real-world validations of the software managing zero-intervention, door-to-door commutes from home garages to office parking. The company also highlighted the system's collision avoidance capabilities, noting specific instances where FSD Supervised successfully prevented high-speed sideswipes from erratic drivers.
