# Company@X — 2026-05-13

## Signal of the Day
Microsoft launched a new multi-model agentic security system utilizing over 100 specialized agents to proactively uncover exploitable software bugs. The system, which achieved top performance on the CyberGym benchmark and successfully discovered 16 vulnerabilities ahead of Patch Tuesday, is now available in private preview.

## Key Announcements

**Microsoft** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/satyanadella/status/2054351354156794163#m)
Microsoft is moving offensive security to an agentic model, bringing together more than 100 specialized agents across frontier and custom models. By successfully using the system internally to patch 16 vulnerabilities before release, Microsoft is proving that multi-agent frameworks are now viable for highly complex, autonomous cybersecurity tasks at an enterprise scale.

**Waymo** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Waymo/status/2054563561653846026#m)
Waymo is aggressively expanding its autonomous ride-hailing footprint to Miami, Austin, Atlanta, Houston, and the SF Bay Area. This rapid rollout will bring Waymo's coverage to over 1,400 square miles across 11 cities, cementing its significant operational lead in the commercial robotaxi market.

**Cursor** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/cursor_ai/status/2054651526715502998#m)
Cursor launched cloud agents that operate inside fully configured, multi-repo development environments. These environments act like persistent engineer laptops with independent version history, rollbacks, and scoped egress and secrets, allowing enterprise teams at companies like Amplitude and Snyk to let agents handle end-to-end tasks safely.

**Salesforce** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/a16z/status/2054588904041537982#m)
Salesforce announced the launch of open APIs and a headless product, signaling a major architectural shift for legacy systems. As highlighted by a16z, this represents an implicit bet that as software moves toward an agentic paradigm, the defensive moat and core value for incumbents lies in the data layer rather than the user interface.

**Hugging Face** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ClementDelangue/status/2054517565179695420#m)
Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue issued a strategic call to the American AI community to defend international open-source AI—specifically highlighting Chinese models like Deepseek and Qwen—amidst the Trump-Xi summit. Delangue pushed back on narratives framing open-source AI as a cybersecurity threat, arguing instead that restricting access creates dangerous capability gaps, whereas openness empowers defenders.

**Resemble AI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/zohaibahmed/status/2054605722462437802#m)
Resemble AI's research team released Dramabox, an open-source Voice AI model built to deliver high-quality audio performances alongside verifiable cryptographic signatures. This dual approach aims to solve the growing enterprise need for audio provenance and deepfake defense natively within generation models.
