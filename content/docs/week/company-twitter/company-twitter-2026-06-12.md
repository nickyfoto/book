# Company@X — 2026-06-12

## Signal of the Day
SpaceX officially began trading publicly on the Nasdaq today under the ticker $SPCX. This highly anticipated public market debut marks a historic milestone for the aerospace industry, prompting widespread congratulations across the tech ecosystem from key investors like Andreessen Horowitz and Sequoia Capital.

## Key Announcements

**[MiniMax]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/MiniMax_AI/status/2065436935188058208#m)
MiniMax launched M3, an open-weight model engineered for agentic workflows and coding that features ~428B total parameters with ~23B activated during inference. The release natively scales context up to 1 million tokens using a custom Sparse Attention kernel, which the company has also published on Hugging Face to deliver massive speedups for long-sequence tasks.

**[OpenAI]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/OpenAI/status/2065225362544726371#m)
OpenAI is rolling out the ability for Codex users to save and bank their rate limit resets for later use, giving Go, Plus, Pro, and Business tier users one free banked reset to start. To drive adoption, they are pairing this flexibility with a referral program that grants Plus and Pro users additional banked resets when invited friends send their first Codex message.

**[Google Labs]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleLabs/status/2064801929339752527#m)
Google expanded global access to its interactive world-building AI, Project Genie, making it available to subscribers of a new "Google AI Ultra 5X" tier. This indicates an aggressive strategy to lock high-compute generative capabilities behind premium, top-tier subscription walls.

**[OpenClaw]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/openclaw/status/2065415939240145314#m)
The open-source agent framework OpenClaw shipped version 2026.6.6, adding critical integration for Claude Fable 5's adaptive thinking and Gemma 4 reasoning replays. The update focuses heavily on security infrastructure for autonomous agents, implementing stricter sandbox bindings, host environment inheritance, and tighter execution approvals.

**[Google DeepMind]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleDeepMind/status/2065388989146628563#m)
DeepMind launched a European Robotics Accelerator, selecting 15 startups focused on the future of physical AI. The three-month program provides these early-stage companies with direct access to Google's specialized Gemini Robotics models and hands-on infrastructure support.

**[Browser Use]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/browser_use/status/2065515287018082488#m)
Browser Use unveiled version 0.13.0 beta, featuring a complete Rust rewrite designed to move away from legacy GPT-4 architecture toward state-of-the-art models. The framework introduces custom LLM and browser harnesses specifically optimized for deploying and managing thousands of long-running, autonomous web agents.
