# Company@X — 2026-05-20

## Signal of the Day
OpenAI's general-purpose reasoning model autonomously solved the planar unit distance problem, a famous open mathematical question posed by Paul Erdős in 1946. This marks a historic milestone as the first time an AI system has autonomously solved a prominent open problem central to a field of mathematics, signaling a shift toward AI capable of executing long, difficult chains of reasoning.

## Key Announcements

**Google** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2057154490143588388#m)
Google released a major wave of product updates at I/O, headlined by Gemini Omni—a natively multimodal model capable of generating and editing video via conversational prompts—and Gemini 3.5 Flash. The company is aggressively targeting advanced developers and knowledge workers with a new $100/month AI Ultra subscription that unlocks Gemini Spark, a 24/7 AI agent, and Project Genie.

**Hugging Face** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ClementDelangue/status/2057071550352781771#m)
Hugging Face open-sourced Carbon, a frontier foundational model for DNA that utilizes a unique base-level tokenizer to process an entire human genome on a single GPU in under two days. Running 275x faster than the previous state-of-the-art model Evo2, Carbon is designed for local execution and represents a major push to bring open-source AI infrastructure to biology and personal health.

**OpenAI / Y Combinator** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/bosmeny/status/2056914385814401238#m)
In a strategic move to capture the next generation of startups, Sam Altman offered $2M in OpenAI API tokens to every Y Combinator startup in the current batch in exchange for equity. This compute-for-equity exchange highlights the rising capitalization costs of AI-native companies and signals OpenAI's aggressive platform lock-in strategy at the earliest stages of company creation.

**Cursor** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/cursor_ai/status/2057167359593603471#m)
Cursor launched automations within its Agents Window in the new Cursor 3.5 release, allowing developers to attach multiple repositories to a single automation. This enables AI agents to natively reason, execute, test, and verify tasks across complex, multi-repo codebases, pushing the IDE closer to autonomous engineering workflows.

**AWS** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/awscloud/status/2057102278725664920#m)
AWS unveiled "AWS Transform," positioning it as the world's first agentic AI service for enterprise modernization. Showcased through a partnership with Mercedes-Benz, the tool uses AI-assisted code generation to refactor legacy mainframe code at high speeds, successfully migrating massive ERP systems in just 9 months.

**Exa** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ExaAILabs/status/2057132080317042697#m)
Exa secured a $250M Series C led by a16z at a $2.2B valuation to continue building a search engine optimized specifically for AI agents. Serving over 5,000 companies including Cursor and Cognition, Exa's model reduces text return by 90% to make RAG cheaper and faster, underscoring the industry shift from human-readable web search to agent-consumable data.

**Tesla** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Tesla/status/2057133720994529601#m)
Tesla natively integrated xAI's Grok into its vehicles, allowing drivers to initiate a voice interface via a "Hey Grok" command. The integration allows users to request navigation routing seamlessly tied into the car's Self-Driving system, bridging xAI's conversational models directly with Tesla's autonomous physical hardware.
