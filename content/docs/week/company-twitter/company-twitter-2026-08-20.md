# Company@X — 2026-08-20

## Signal of the Day
**Meta** has released Muse Spark 1.2, a major multimodal upgrade that translates visual observations directly into executable code and physical robotics coordination, alongside WildArtifactBench to establish a preference-based, real-world utility standard for AI agents.

## Key Announcements

**Meta** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AIatMeta/status/2090485743034716420#m)
Meta previewed **Muse Spark 1.2**, introducing advanced **audio-visual understanding** for video-heavy enterprise workflows and **spatial intelligence** to orchestrate physical, bimanual robotics tasks like desk tidying. Alongside the model, Meta debuted **WildArtifactBench**, an evaluation framework of 10 open tasks that assesses agents on complex deliverables using win rates and **Elo scores** from human and agentic judges. This dual release signals a strategic push toward deploying and measuring **long-horizon agents** capable of executing multi-turn real-world workflows.

**Apollo Atomics** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AssilHalimi/status/2090426148454846813#m)
Apollo Atomics secured **\$31M in seed financing**—including \$26M in equity led by FCVC and \$5M in equipment financing from Oak Ridge Financial—to build high-power output nuclear reactors (**>80 MW**) that fit on a truck. Currently running a low-power reactor demonstrator at MIT, the startup is developing a full-scale 1 MW prototype and collaborating with the U.S. NRC for **commercial deployment in 2028**. This design prioritizes mass manufacturing and extreme cost-effectiveness to solve the energy grid's power density challenges.

**Waymo** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Waymo/status/2090441658043175219#m)
Waymo disclosed its engineering architecture for **autonomous driving compute**, detailing key partnerships with semiconductor giants AMD, NVIDIA, TSMC, Samsung, and Micron. Simultaneously, the Alphabet-backed company officially **opened its ride-hailing service to everyone in Space City** (Houston), marking another massive metropolitan expansion. The announcements underscore Waymo's dual-track strategy of solidifying its physical supply chain while aggressively scaling its commercial market footprint.

**Y Combinator** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ycombinator/status/2090114065477865533#m)
Y Combinator launched **"Send a SAFE,"** a free, automated tool enabling founders to generate, sign, and distribute SAFE agreements in under two minutes. Crucially, the platform is optimized to run with **AI agents**, allowing automated workflows to handle early-stage transaction mechanics. By updating the fundraising standard that has facilitated over \$15B in funding since 2013, YC is actively building the legal rail system for **agentic commerce**.

**Google** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2090497445826322464#m)
Google’s Gemma family of open models has officially surpassed **one billion downloads**, with the developer ecosystem producing over 100,000 fine-tuned variants. To capitalize on this, Google launched the **"Awesome Gemma"** GitHub repository and highlighted key domain-specific breakthroughs, including **C2S-Scale** (a 27B parameter model that discovered a novel cancer therapy pathway) and **DolphinGemma** for interspecies vocalization research. The massive distribution footprint solidifies Google’s strategy of leveraging open-weights ecosystems to maintain developer mindshare.

**Pocket** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/harjtaggar/status/2090136048823394650#m)
Hardware startup Pocket reached **\$100M in Annualized Run Rate (ARR)** just five months after graduating from Y Combinator’s Winter 2026 batch. Scaling from \$1M to \$100M ARR in just seven months with over **270,000 active devices** deployed globally, the company marks the fastest growth trajectory of any hardware company in YC history. The milestone signals a substantial acceleration in the commercial adoption rate and venture-scale viability of consumer hardware.
