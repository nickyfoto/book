# Company@X — 2026-06-03

## Signal of the Day
Google released Gemma 4 12B, an open Apache 2.0-licensed multimodal model with a novel, encoder-free architecture that runs locally on 16GB VRAM laptops. By entirely removing separate vision and audio encoders and projecting multimodal inputs directly into the LLM backbone, Google has drastically reduced latency and memory footprint to bring frontier agentic reasoning to edge devices.

## Key Announcements

**[Google]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2062225896400994451#m)
Google introduced Gemini Omni, a video generation model that incorporates real-world physics such as gravity, kinetic energy, and fluid dynamics. Instead of merely predicting the next pixel, Omni relies on a fundamental understanding of physical logic to ensure realistic movements and prevent floating objects or broken physics in generated scenes.

**[OpenAI]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/OpenAI/status/2062281977122996256#m)
OpenAI unveiled GPT-Rosalind, a new model series specifically engineered for enterprise-scale life sciences research. The system brings GPT-5.5's agentic coding and tool-use capabilities into specialized biological domains, offering stronger intelligence for drug discovery, analysis, design, and experimental workflows.

**[Microsoft & Mayo Clinic]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/MayoClinic/status/2061878675390550163#m)
Microsoft is collaborating with Mayo Clinic to develop a frontier AI model specifically designed for healthcare and clinical decision-making. Notably, Mayo Clinic will own the resulting model, signaling an emerging strategic playbook where highly regulated enterprise clients maintain direct ownership over their localized frontier architectures.

**[xAI]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/xai/status/2062294202625696081#m)
xAI is rapidly accelerating Grok's distribution, integrating its LLM, audio, and vision models directly into Cloudflare's AI Gateway for frictionless developer access. Concurrently, xAI launched Grok STT and TTS APIs on enterprise voice platform Vapi, and partnered with Gopuff to deploy "Go," an AI shopping assistant powered by Grok's multimodal stack.

**[Ideogram]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ideogram_ai/status/2062202208700313872#m)
Ideogram open-sourced the weights for Ideogram 4.0, issuing a direct challenge to closed-source image generation ecosystems. Developers can now download the state-of-the-art text-to-image model, fine-tune it on proprietary data, and run it locally on their own hardware setups.

**[a16z]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/a16z/status/2062186811414475005#m)
a16z heavily validated a strategic pivot toward "AI-native services," leading a $47M Series A in Lassie and a $55M Series A in Town. The underlying investment thesis abandons the "copilot" model in favor of fully autonomous systems that execute back-office operations and messy personal pipelines natively, fundamentally replacing traditional software workflows.
