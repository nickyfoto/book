# Company@X — 2026-09-02

## Signal of the Day
**Google** and **Meta** have dramatically escalated the rapid-fire model release cycle, with Google dropping **Gemini 3.8 Flash** just three weeks after its predecessor, and Meta firing back hours later with **Muse Spark 1.3**. This dual-release event shifts the frontier of AI competition toward highly optimized, low-cost agentic execution and autonomous software engineering.

## Key Announcements

**Google** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2095175518068904380#m)
Google released two new variants of its model, **Gemini 3.8 Flash** and **Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber**, targeting agentic reasoning and cybersecurity. Gemini 3.8 Flash achieves a **73.7% score on the DeepSWE v1.1 benchmark** for autonomous software engineering, outperforming larger frontier models at a fraction of their cost. Meanwhile, Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber delivers specialized vulnerability detection and automated patching, demonstrating a **2.6x improvement in generating correct security patches** in Google Chrome codebases compared to much larger commercial models.

**Meta** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AIatMeta/status/2095234385129963666#m)
Meta launched **Muse Spark 1.3**, which immediately seized the top spot on the **DeepSWE v1.1 software engineering benchmark with a record 75.4% score**. The model is highly optimized for long-horizon multi-workflow execution, requiring **20% fewer tool calls and 25% fewer tokens** than version 1.2. It enters the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with elite-level scores of **61 for the xhigh variant and 62 for the max preview**, while offering a highly competitive pricing structure of **\$1.25 per million input tokens and \$4.25 per million output tokens**.

**World Labs** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/theworldlabs/status/2094839756329041984#m)
World Labs introduced **Atlas**, a multimodal world model that generates image and video frames with camera control and reconstructs them in 3D. The technology enables a rapid **"real-to-sim-to-real" workflow**, allowing users to take casual phone photos of a room, reconstruct the 3D space in minutes, and stage physics-simulated robotics interactions. This spatial simulation capability allows general-purpose robotics models to be **reinforced and fine-tuned** for physical environments they have never seen.

**Cursor** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/cursor_ai/status/2095257412781396114#m)
Cursor announced that developers can now run cloud agents on their own **self-hosted infrastructure**, enabling automated machine scaling while keeping the agent loop in Cursor. This security-conscious feature allows agents to securely access sensitive internal services or specialized hardware through managed pools or sandbox providers like **AWS Lambda and Modal**. Additionally, Cursor has integrated Google’s newly launched **Gemini 3.8 Flash model**, expanding its suite of developer-focused coding tools.

**Regent** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/billythalheimer/status/2095141091934626242#m)
Regent successfully completed the maiden test flight of its human-crewed **Viceroy Seaglider prototype**, a 15,000 lb maritime aerospace vehicle. The smooth flight marks the transition of the company’s technology from **early YC startup renderings** to fully functional, full-scale aviation hardware. This successful flight represents a significant developmental milestone for the **wing-in-ground-effect transit** sector.

**Moderna** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/a16z/status/2095179949296750851#m)
Moderna reported positive Phase 3 clinical trial results for its **individualized mRNA cancer vaccine**, demonstrating superior recurrence-free and metastasis-free survival in melanoma patients compared to standard care. The highly complex personalized treatment works by sequencing a patient's tumor and healthy DNA, then encoding up to **34 unique cancer mutations** into a patient-specific mRNA strand. Moderna has engineered its automated manufacturing pipeline to shrink the production time to just **42 days from the initial biopsy** to final patient delivery.

**ConveoAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ycombinator/status/2095179231865258141#m)
ConveoAI secured a **\$50M Series A funding round** to scale its automated AI-driven consumer interview platform. The software engages in deep, multi-respondent video conversations, enabling brand managers to acquire qualitative insights in days that traditionally required months of focus groups. Over **400 enterprises, including Fortune 500 giants Unilever and Google**, are already utilizing Conveo to eliminate the trade-off between market research scale and conversational depth.
