# Company@X — 2026-05-14

## Signal of the Day
OpenAI launched a preview of Codex within the ChatGPT mobile app, allowing developers to start, review, and steer code execution from their iOS or Android devices while the code itself runs locally on their laptop, Mac mini, or devbox. This fundamentally shifts mobile developer productivity by turning smartphones into remote control interfaces for complex, local dev environments, with Windows phone connectivity coming soon.

## Key Announcements

**Anthropic** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AnthropicAI/status/2054941901900611787#m)
Anthropic partnered with the Gates Foundation, committing $200 million in grants, Claude credits, and technical support. The initiative targets global health, life sciences, education, agriculture, and economic mobility, embedding Claude heavily into the operations of high-impact NGOs and scientific sectors. Separately, the company published a strategic paper outlining requirements for the US and its democratic allies to maintain their lead in frontier AI against China.

**xAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/xai/status/2054993285152989373#m)
Rolled out an early beta of "Grok Build," an agentic CLI for coding, building apps, and automating workflows. The beta is exclusively available for SuperGrok Heavy subscribers, acting as a real-world testing ground to gather feedback and refine both the underlying model and the product surface.

**a16z** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/takaichi_sanae/status/2054881813143924791#m)
The venture firm is aggressively expanding its international footprint, announcing it will open its first-ever overseas office in Japan this summer. Co-founder Ben Horowitz met with Japanese Prime Minister Takaichi, aligning the firm's expansion with Japan's "new technology nation" economic and national security strategies. Additionally, a16z made its first investment in Saudi Arabia by leading a $25M Series A in Middle Eastern fintech operating system Stitch. 

**Datadog & Hugging Face** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ClementDelangue/status/2054991352295731619#m)
Datadog released Toto 2.0 weights (4M to 2.5B parameters) on Hugging Face under an Apache 2.0 license. This open-weights family of Time Series Foundation Models (TSFM) is highly significant because it proves scaling laws apply to time series data; every model size outperforms the previous one from a single hyperparameter config, removing a major hurdle in scaling data and compute predictably for time series applications.

**Google Cloud** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleCloudTech/status/2054909596095897654#m)
Highlighted Stitch, an AI-powered UI generation tool out of Google Labs. The tool is designed to allow developers to build modern, visually stunning interfaces from scratch without writing initial CSS, addressing a core pain point for backend-heavy development teams.

**Turing** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/turingcom/status/2053949350951501987#m)
Released the Open MM-RL Dataset, a PhD-level multimodal STEM benchmark built specifically for verifiable reasoning across physics, chemistry, biology, and math. Designed to stress-test frontier models and RL systems, the dataset eliminates "vibes-based" grading with deterministic answers that are automatically verifiable, and scales complexity from single to multi-image tasks to isolate where a model's reasoning chain breaks.
