## Key Announcements

**World Labs** · **[Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/theworldlabs/status/2094839756329041984#m)**
World Labs has officially unveiled Atlas, a multimodal spatial intelligence world model that unifies pixel-level generation and 3D reconstruction. By operating on "new view prediction," Atlas can reconstruct highly detailed 3D scenes from as few as three standard images, achieving a 50x to 100x reduction in traditional dense capture data requirements. This release signals a critical shift in AI development away from purely language-based architectures toward spatial representation, aiming directly at the data bottleneck currently constraining robotics.

**Anthropic** · **[Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AnthropicAI/status/2095947707605266436#m)**
Anthropic’s Claude has completed the first formalized, machine-verified proof of Fermat’s Last Theorem in Lean, completing a massive verification project that experts estimated would take years. The final proof totals over 13 million lines of code and required the formalization and machine verification of over 29,000 intermediate mathematical theorems. This achievement highlights Claude's capability in managing massive codebases and high-level abstract reasoning, opening up new possibilities for AI-assisted mathematical peer review.

**Microsoft** · **[Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/satyanadella/status/2095912050535059918#m)**
CEO Satya Nadella announced the integration of HydraFusion into GitHub Copilot, pushing developer tools from simple model selection to sophisticated model orchestration. By leveraging a heterogeneous ecosystem of multiple models to plan, execute, critique, and finalize coding tasks, HydraFusion is able to deliver completed code at up to a 67% reduction in cost. The launch reflects Microsoft's strategic focus on the "cost-to-outcome" frontier for enterprise developer tooling.

**Meta** · **[Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/MetaforDevs/status/2095939388559720720#m)**
Meta has launched Muse Spark 1.3 Max, an optimized model tier featuring advanced coding and agentic reasoning capabilities, now accessible on Muse Code and the Meta Model API. The model delivers frontier-level performance at lower pricing, debuting at third globally on the Artificial Analysis benchmark. The launch signals Meta's ongoing aggression in open-weight developer ecosystems to compete directly with commercial closed-source APIs.

**Google** · **[Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2095905262229995736#m)**
Google announced Lyria 3.5, its most advanced music generation model boasting expressive vocals and rich arrangements, now available across the Gemini App and artist platforms. Google also rolled out Gemini 3.8 Flash and Agentic Video Understanding, which lets developers build systems that can autonomously navigate a 2-hour video timeline, zoom in on key gestures, and isolate exact timestamps. Together, these releases solidify Google's dual strategy of shipping creative consumer features alongside raw agentic video tools for developers.

**a16z** · **[Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/a16z/status/2095905665516736760#m)**
Andreessen Horowitz led a \$300M Series B investment in Gimlet Labs, valuing the startup at \$3B to scale its multi-silicon inference cloud. Gimlet's cloud infrastructure is engineered from scratch for high-efficiency inference workloads, promising up to a 10x gain in throughput and interactivity on frontier models under identical power budgets. The massive bet underscores investor focus on inference efficiency as physical inputs—like power grids and fabs—struggle to scale alongside software demand.

**Y Combinator** · **[Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ycombinator/status/2095883627200659800#m)**
Ollama CEO Jeffrey Morgan revealed that Ollama Cloud token usage has jumped 150-fold since the start of the year, driven by a structural shift toward open models among developers and the Fortune 500. Morgan noted that open models are currently less than three months behind closed frontier models, capturing roughly 80% of enterprise workloads and offering a fast, cheap alternative. This momentum indicates that startups are increasingly organizing around local execution and cheap, orchestrated "flash" models over massive monolithic engines.
