# Company@X — 2026-07-02

## Key Announcements

**Weave Robotics** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/evan_wineland/status/2072366059215302841#m)
Weave Robotics officially launched Isaac 1, an autonomous robot built entirely from the ground up for home use. The hardware marks a significant step in consumer robotics, with initial customer deliveries scheduled to begin this fall.

**64x Bio** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/64xbio/status/2071576996430426505#m)
64x Bio announced the LV Apex Suite, expanding its computational CellMap-driven platform from AAV into lentiviral vector production. By treating cell lines, reagents, and processes as interdependent parts of a single system, the company is leveraging computational biology to engineer highly scalable biomanufacturing foundations.

**Reka AI Labs** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/RekaAILabs/status/2071970771233038475#m)
Reka AI Labs released the CS2-10k dataset on Hugging Face, containing over 600,000 egocentric gameplay videos spanning 10,000 hours. Crucially, every frame is paired with exact keyboard, mouse, and 3D position data, providing an immediate foundational asset for teams training world models and action-conditioned video generation.

**Amazon Web Services** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/awscloud/status/2072686489276649676#m)
AWS is pushing its enterprise AI assistant, Amazon Quick, positioning it as a tool that can aggregate data from up to five different work tools into a single prompt. The platform aims to automate the generation of competitive performance reports, complete with charts and written comparisons.

**Google Cloud Tech** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/peterfriese/status/2069173424103080392#m)
Xcode 27 is officially embracing agentic workflows, featuring native integration paths that allow developers to set up Google's Gemini as their primary coding agent. This highlights the ongoing ecosystem race to embed frontier models directly into incumbent IDEs.
