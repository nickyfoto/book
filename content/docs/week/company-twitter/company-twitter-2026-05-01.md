# Company@X — 2026-05-01

## Signal of the Day
The enterprise shift toward managing AI as a digital workforce has accelerated with Microsoft's general availability of Agent 365. By extending corporate frameworks for identity, security, management, and governance to AI agents, Microsoft is pulling agentic systems out of shadow IT experiments and securing them as fully integrated, auditable enterprise assets. 

## Key Announcements

**OpenAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/OpenAI/status/2050250926888468929#m)
One week post-launch, GPT-5.5 has proven to be OpenAI's strongest model debut, with API revenue scaling more than twice as fast as any previous release. The company's developer focus is paying off, as Codex doubled its revenue in under seven days driven by massive enterprise demand for agentic coding tools. To capture momentum, OpenAI is heavily promoting one-click migration to Codex by allowing developers to instantly import settings, plugins, and agents.

**Microsoft** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/satyanadella/status/2050251014691840015#m)
Satya Nadella announced that Agent 365 is now generally available, marking a critical step in the enterprise maturation of autonomous agents. The platform ensures that AI agents and their interactions operate strictly within an organization's established systems for identity, security, and governance. This enables enterprises to safely deploy and manage AI systems at scale alongside their human workforces.

**Google** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2050293156067881013#m)
Google introduced agentic booking for restaurants through AI Mode in Search across select countries and via Ask Maps in the U.S. and India. Users can prompt the system with complex constraints—such as group size, dietary preferences, and vibe—and the AI will scan across reservation platforms to find real-time matches. The system then curates a list of available tables and provides direct links to finalize the booking through partners like OpenTable and Resy.

**xAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/xai/status/2050355373052223585#m)
xAI released Voice Cloning via its API, giving developers access to over 80 existing voices across 28 languages or the ability to generate a custom voice in under two minutes. This infrastructure is designed to personalize voice agents, audiobooks, and video game characters. Simultaneously, the company rolled out Imagine Agent Mode for Grok, an infinite canvas tool allowing users to brainstorm, write, generate images, and output video in a single unified workflow.

**Waymo** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Konstantine/status/2050264225940152749#m)
Waymo's hardware and software stack has officially achieved a 13x lower rate of serious accidents compared to human drivers, powered by their 6th-generation hardware. As the autonomous vehicle market transitions from R&D to scale, Waymo has now logged 170 million fully autonomous miles and 20 million rides. Notably, the adoption curve is going vertical, with half of those 20 million rides happening within just the last two months.

**Hugging Face & DeepInfra** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/hanouticelina/status/2049536917897617495#m)
DeepInfra is now an official Inference Provider directly on the Hugging Face Hub, allowing developers to run over 100 open models—including DeepSeek V4 Pro, GLM-5.1, and Kimi-4.6. With no markup on per-token pricing and an OpenAI-compatible API, this partnership also integrates out-of-the-box with major agent harnesses like Hermes Agent, OpenClaw, and Pi. 

**Y Combinator** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ycombinator/status/2050224443461718118#m)
In a detailed technical breakdown, YC highlighted breakthroughs in recursive reasoning models (HRMs and TRMs) that bypass the fundamental reasoning ceilings of standard LLMs. By using recursion at inference time to generate deeper compute paths, an architecture with just 7 million parameters is currently outperforming models 1,000 times larger on complex reasoning tasks like the ARC Prize.
