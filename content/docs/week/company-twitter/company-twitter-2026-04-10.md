# Company@X — 2026-04-10

## Signal of the Day
Replit and Accenture announced a major partnership to bring "secure vibecoding" to enterprises globally, with Accenture investing in and adopting Replit internally for its 700,000+ employees. This signals a massive push for AI-native software development within the world's largest legacy enterprises, aiming to break down barriers and turn non-engineers into builders.

## Key Announcements

**Google Cloud** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleCloudTech/status/2042679007154500067#m)
Google Cloud released the Java SDK for the MCP Toolbox for Databases, providing enterprise developers a native, scalable way to give AI agents read-and-write access to mission-critical systems. This is a critical building block for agentic AI, allowing models to take direct action on enterprise systems of record rather than just retrieving information.

**Tesla** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Tesla/status/2042710059818586562#m)
Tesla's FSD Supervised has officially received regulatory approval in the Netherlands and will begin rolling out to the country shortly. This marks a major geographic and regulatory milestone for Tesla's autonomous driving ambitions, setting the stage for wider European deployment on residential roads and city streets.

**Google** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2042626811083853857#m)
Google Search is expanding its agentic capabilities in "AI Mode" to eight new locations outside the U.S., including the UK, India, Australia, and Canada. The feature allows users to describe their restaurant preferences, after which the AI searches across multiple platforms for real-time availability and allows the user to tap to complete the booking.

**Hugging Face** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ClementDelangue/status/2042624622395302127#m)
Hugging Face is doubling down on AI infrastructure by launching "Kernels" on its Hub. Designed for hardcore AI engineers, these are collections of optimized binary operations with first-class hardware support for CUDA, ROCm, Apple Silicon, and Intel XPU.

**Google Cloud** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleCloudTech/status/2042633709711441945#m)
BigQuery introduced autonomous embedding generation in preview, which automatically syncs data with Vertex AI models. This eliminates the need for manual vector management, streamlining operations for clustering, classification, and recommendation models measuring semantic similarity.

**OpenAI / SweetspotGov** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/sachsubra/status/2042596378614935686#m)
SweetspotGov has partnered with OpenAI to release its federal contract data search directly within ChatGPT for free. This bypasses expensive public sector procurement tools, allowing users to search SAM.gov data and initiate qualification workflows natively in the chat interface.
