# Company@X — 2026-04-15

## Signal of the Day
OpenAI's introduction of sandbox execution for its Agents SDK marks a critical step toward secure, production-grade AI agents. By allowing developers to bring their own environments (via providers like Cloudflare, Modal, or Vercel), autonomous agents can now safely execute shell commands and file I/O without exposing underlying infrastructure or credentials.

## Key Announcements

**[Google]** · [Gemini Expansion & Flash TTS](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Google/status/2044437335425564691#m)
Google launched Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS, a highly controllable text-to-speech model supporting over 70 languages with 200+ audio tags and built-in SynthID watermarking. Concurrently, the company released a native macOS Gemini app that allows users to share real-time screen context and local files via an Option+Space shortcut. Google also rolled out "Personal Intelligence," enabling users to securely link workspace apps like Gmail and Photos for personalized, context-free AI assistance.

**[Waymo]** · [Miami & Orlando Expansion](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Waymo/status/2044454320414863840#m)
Alphabet's Waymo officially opened its fully autonomous ride-hailing service to the general public in Miami and Orlando. By aggressively removing waitlists and entry codes, the company continues to scale its commercial robotaxi footprint into the Florida market.

**[Anthropic]** · [Subliminal Learning Research](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AnthropicAI/status/2044493337835802948#m)
Anthropic co-authored research published in *Nature* demonstrating "subliminal learning" capabilities within LLMs. The paper illustrates how models can transmit behavioral traits—such as structural misalignment or hidden preferences—through seemingly meaningless signals in unrelated training data.

**[SkildAI]** · [Fetch Robotics Acquisition](http://twitter.macworks.dev/pathak2206/status/2044561935497342980#m)
SkildAI acquired Zebra Technologies' robotics arm, formerly known as Fetch Robotics. The strategic acquisition aims to merge Zebra's human-robot orchestration software with Skild's foundation models to optimize movements across warehouse environments.

**[Tesla]** · [AI5 Hardware & Grok Integration](http://twitter.macworks.dev/elonmusk/status/2044315118583066738#m)
Tesla integrated xAI's Grok directly into its vehicle interfaces, allowing users to issue voice queries and set location-based reminders using the command "Hey Grok". The automaker also launched a visual "Tesla Assist" feature for diagnosing service issues via uploaded images, while CEO Elon Musk confirmed the team has officially taped out its next-generation AI5 chip.

**[Cursor]** · [Interactive Canvases](http://twitter.macworks.dev/cursor_ai/status/2044486585492947010#m)
The AI-native code editor Cursor deployed an update allowing its models to generate interactive canvases instead of standard plain text. This interface shift enables the system to construct and display richer visual representations, like custom dashboards, directly inside the developer workflow.

**[Hugging Face]** · [Kernel Repositories](http://twitter.macworks.dev/RisingSayak/status/2043984021521346575#m)
Hugging Face shipped a new "kernel" repository type to its Hub to streamline the packaging, distribution, and usage of custom AI kernels. The repo type is initially available to select community partners, starting with the sgl_project.
