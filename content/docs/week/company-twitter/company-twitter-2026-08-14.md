# Company@X — 2026-08-14

## Signal of the Day
**SpaceX** has officially closed its acquisition of **Cursor (Anysphere)**, the developer of the breakout AI-powered code editor, merging the team with SpaceXAI to improve the Grok AI developer ecosystem. This represents a massive strategic consolidation in the AI developer tools space, combining the fastest-growing software startup in history with a leading frontier engineering lab.

## Key Announcements

**SpaceX** · [X Post](http://twitter.macworks.dev/cursor_ai/status/2088249881718919393#m)
SpaceX has completed its acquisition of Cursor, the developer of the breakout AI code editor that recently became the fastest software company to reach \$100 million in annual recurring revenue. The Cursor team will join the SpaceXAI division to improve Grok Build, Grok Bot, Grok API, and Cursor itself, moving the project from a compute-starved IDE into a deeply integrated frontier developer environment. As a result of the deal, Sequoia Capital's Martin Casado has stepped off the board, cementing SpaceX's control over one of the most beloved engineering platforms in the industry.

**Google** · [X Post](http://twitter.macworks.dev/NewsFromGoogle/status/2088334689744417062#m)
Google is expanding the rollout of its newly released Gemini 3.7 Flash "workhorse" model, bringing it globally to all Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers. The model is now active in Gemini chat, AI Mode in Google Search, and Google Workspace, starting with a native rollout in Google Sheets canvas. Gemini 3.7 Flash drops token costs by 50% and reduces latency while boosting software engineering benchmarks (DeepSWE v1.1 performance surging from 37.0% to 65.3%) and enterprise automation.

**Waymo** · [X Post](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Waymo/status/2088332349734068491#m)
Waymo has secured approval from the California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC) to scale its autonomous ride-hailing services across the state. The regulatory greenlight allows Waymo to expand its driverless taxi coverage throughout the SF Bay Area and Los Angeles, while paving the way for brand-new service launches in Sacramento and San Diego. Waymo plans to execute this massive footprint expansion gradually under its safety framework while keeping local officials and residents closely updated.

**Atoms** · [X Post](http://twitter.macworks.dev/a16z/status/2088287593570128079#m)
Uber co-founder Travis Kalanick has broken eight years of public silence to discuss his new venture, Atoms, which is backed by the largest check ever written by Andreessen Horowitz GP Ben Horowitz. Atoms is an industrial AI company that conceptualizes manufacturing, real estate, and logistics as the CPU, storage, and network primitives of the physical world. The startup aims to bring software-speed optimization to heavy industry under a culture focused on extreme iteration and letting the best ideas win.

**Anthropic** · [X Post](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AnthropicAI/status/2088343978873966687#m)
Anthropic has published its second official Risk Report under its Responsible Scaling Policy and issued a detailed FAQ regarding its text watermarking strategy. To comply with the EU AI Act, Anthropic is implementing watermarking for Claude's outputs in coordination with other major model developers under the same Code of Practice. The company clarified that its watermarking technique has zero impact on Claude's output quality, costs, or token count, is completely indistinguishable to human readers, and cannot be traced to specific users.

**xAI** · [X Post](http://twitter.macworks.dev/grok/status/2088319896597975501#m)
xAI has officially integrated its Grok 4.6 model into GitHub Copilot, making it directly accessible across the Copilot CLI, IDE, and cloud platforms. Following its release, Grok 4.6 has demonstrated explosive developer adoption, recording the fastest adoption curve of any model added to Augment Code's Cosmos platform in its first three days. This strategic distribution deal signals a major escalation in the developer-tooling wars, placing Grok in direct competition with OpenAI and Google within native developer environments.
