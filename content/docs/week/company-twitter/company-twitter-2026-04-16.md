# Company@X — 2026-04-16

## Signal of the Day
Microsoft has brought its massive Fairwater datacenter online ahead of schedule, linking hundreds of thousands of NVIDIA GB200 GPUs into a single, liquid-cooled, closed-loop cluster. This deployment marks a severe escalation in the compute scaling wars, delivering a stated 10x performance improvement over current top supercomputers and demonstrating the reality of multi-gigawatt AI infrastructure investments.

## Key Announcements

**[Microsoft]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/satyanadella/status/2044767391293509761#m)
CEO Satya Nadella confirmed the launch of the Fairwater datacenter in Wisconsin, built to handle single-job AI training at exponential scale. Operating with matched renewable energy and zero post-construction water consumption, it represents Microsoft's shift toward highly integrated compute, network, and storage environments specifically tailored for frontier model training.

**[Anthropic]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/claudeai/status/2044785261393977612#m)
Anthropic shipped Claude Opus 4.7, its most capable model to date, focused heavily on autonomy and self-verification. The model's immediate integration into ecosystem tools—including day-one availability on Google Cloud's Vertex AI and deep integration within the Cursor IDE—signals strong enterprise demand for agentic systems capable of handling long-running, unsupervised tasks. 

**[OpenAI]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/OpenAI/status/2044861690911850863#m)
OpenAI introduced GPT-Rosalind, a frontier reasoning model specifically trained for the life sciences. Optimized for protein and chemical reasoning, genomics, and biochemistry, it is currently in research preview for major biomedical players including Amgen, Moderna, and Thermo Fisher Scientific. Separately, OpenAI updated its Codex desktop app on macOS to feature direct "computer use" via cursor control and integrated image generation with gpt-image-1.5.

**[Google DeepMind & Boston Dynamics]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleDeepMind/status/2044763625680765408#m)
DeepMind has successfully integrated Gemini Robotics embodied reasoning models into Boston Dynamics' Spot robot. By mapping a basic set of movement and sensory tools to the model, engineering teams can now command the quadruped in plain English to execute complex, multi-step actions like tidying up environments without writing custom logic.

**[Alibaba Cloud]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Alibaba_Qwen/status/2044768734234243427#m)
The Qwen team open-sourced Qwen3.6-35B-A3B under the Apache 2.0 license. This highly optimized sparse MoE model features 35B total parameters with only 3B active during inference, yet reportedly achieves agentic coding performance comparable to models 10x its active size.

**[PrismML]** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/PrismML/status/2044833023682896134#m)
PrismML released Ternary Bonsai, an open-source family of highly compressed models operating at 1.58 bits. By utilizing ternary weights, the 8B model fits into roughly 1.75 GB of memory while competing with standard 16-bit models in its class, unlocking extreme edge capabilities like running at 60 tokens per second locally inside a browser via WebGPU.
