# Company@X — 2026-08-27

## Signal of the Day
**Anthropic** has unveiled a research preview of the **Model Hardware Standard (MHS)**, a new shared specification designed to allow AI agents to safely operate physical laboratory and advanced manufacturing equipment. By slashing integration times from days to minutes, this initiative signals a critical industry shift from digital-only agents to standardized, real-world physical automation.

## Key Announcements

**Anthropic** · [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview)
Anthropic has initiated the first phase of its research preview for the **Model Hardware Standard (MHS)**, a shared specification aiming to unify how AI agents control physical hardware. The standard reduces bespoke integration timelines from days or weeks to mere hours or minutes, while providing a discoverable interface and rigorous safety boundaries to prevent equipment damage. Early testing with partners like Genentech, HHMI Janelia, and QuEra demonstrated dramatic improvements, such as stabilizing quantum computer lasers from 58% to 99.3% and compressing biological imaging workflows.

**Hugging Face** · [Source](https://pollen-robotics.com/microduck/)
Hugging Face, in partnership with Pollen Robotics, launched **Microduck**, a \$399 open-source bipedal robot tailored for reinforcement learning and physical AI development. The 25-cm robot features 15 actuators, a LiDAR sensor, a camera, and an articulated beak, and is backed by an open-source SDK, simulator, and RL training pipeline on GitHub. Demanding immediate developer interest, the platform surpassed \$1,000,000 in sales on its launch day.

**Google** · [Source](https://goo.gle/4xq4W4U)
Google announced **Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash**, its newest multimodal model designed for developer video generation and editing control. The release introduces advanced scene extension (up to 10 seconds of context and character-consistent generation), starting/ending frame interpolation, video reference inputs, and upscaling to 4K. Rollouts are live in Google AI Studio, Flow, and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform.

**Google DeepMind** · [Source](https://goo.gle/3St2xan)
Google DeepMind is piloting an industry-first **double-blind evaluation framework** for frontier AI safety and performance benchmarking. By establishing a secure, sandboxed environment where evaluator test prompts are hidden from the model providers and the proprietary model weights are hidden from the evaluators, DeepMind solves a major industry bottleneck. This architecture ensures unbiased, high-integrity benchmarking without compromising safety-critical weights or proprietary IP.

**Cursor** · [Source](https://cursor.com/changelog/start-from-scratch)
Cursor released **"Start from scratch,"** enabling its AI Cloud Agents to construct and preview new web applications from scratch without requiring a connected Git repository. The updated environment supports live-preview capabilities and direct, single-click publishing to Vercel, accelerating the prototyping loop for developers.

**REGENT** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/billythalheimer/status/2092954312301302161#m)
Electric aerospace manufacturer REGENT secured **\$240 million in Series B funding** (a mix of equity co-led by Mare Liberum and AE Ventures, alongside \$120 million in debt from Erebor Bank). The capital will bring its Rhode Island manufacturing facility online to scale production of the Viceroy, a 12-passenger electric wing-in-ground seaglider capable of speeds up to 180 mph. First human flights of the Viceroy prototype are expected imminently as the company prepares for commercial deliveries.
