# Company@X — 2026-09-08

## Signal of the Day
OpenAI announced that a swarm of 10,000 coordinating AI agents running on a next-generation model beyond GPT-6 Astra produced a formal analytical proof and Lean formalization solving the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize Problem in 88 hours. Unresolved for roughly 90 years, the milestone represents the first time an AI system has resolved one of mathematics' seven open Millennium Prize problems.

## Key Announcements

**OpenAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/OpenAI/status/2097374640582668336#m)
OpenAI revealed an AI-generated analytical proof and Lean formalization solving the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize Problem, achieved in 88 hours using 10,000 coordinating agents on a model significantly beyond GPT-6 Astra. Alongside the scientific result, OpenAI launched ChatGPT Images 2.5 with comment-based editing tools and two new API models, while fully rolling out its Codex Astra agent to paid enterprise users. The simultaneous releases demonstrate OpenAI's dual focus on fundamental frontier science research and rapid commercial deployment across consumer and developer workflows.

**Meta** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/AIatMeta/status/2097401493770956808#m)
Meta officially rolled out Muse, a 24/7 personal AI assistant powered by its new Muse Spark 1.3 model with native browser execution, app integration, and dedicated secrets management. The assistant leverages Meta's distribution channels—including Instagram's social graph and Facebook Marketplace—to position AI agents as daily consumer utility tools. In addition to the standalone product, Meta made Muse Spark 1.3 immediately available inside the Cursor developer environment.

**Google DeepMind** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleDeepMind/status/2097325048109384166#m)
Google DeepMind introduced AlphaGenome Atlas, a searchable 1-petabyte database mapping the predicted biological impact of all 9 billion possible single-letter human DNA changes. The database combines AlphaGenome and AlphaMissense into an AI variant impact score available for free to academic researchers via a web portal, API, and Google Cloud. Concurrently, Google launched Gemini 3.5 Transcribe in its macOS application for local file summarization and screen context processing.

**Cognition** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/cognition/status/2097369798518681891#m)
AI software development company Cognition closed a \$2 billion Series E funding round at a \$48 billion valuation, led by a16z, Accel, Founders Fund, General Catalyst, and Avenir. The company disclosed that its annual run-rate revenue grew from \$492 million in May to nearly \$900 million. The massive capital injection highlights sustained investor demand for autonomous coding systems operating at enterprise scale.

**Stoke Space** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/stoke_space/status/2097308985149808988#m)
Spacecraft developer Stoke Space raised a \$1 billion Series E round, bringing its total funding to \$2.3 billion to advance fully reusable rocket infrastructure. The funding will finance flight testing for Nova Pathfinder and accelerate production of the 15-ton-class Nova Block 2 vehicle featuring a reusable upper stage. Stoke's design aims to eliminate disposable upper stages in orbital space transportation through a specialized heat shield and thruster array.

**Tesla** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Tesla/status/2097382902602609141#m)
Tesla released software updates to Full Self-Driving (FSD) that dynamically add lateral offsets to maintain safety margins around pedestrians, cyclists, and roadside obstacles. On the hardware front, the automaker transitioned Cybertruck underbody shields to lighter polypropylene and updated side-repeater camera housings with passive airflow geometry to prevent water buildup on lenses. The updates reflect Tesla's strategy of deploying immediate hardware and software manufacturing revisions rather than holding changes for traditional model years.

**Centralize** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ycombinator/status/2097407336088469828#m)
Enterprise sales platform Centralize secured \$15 million in Series A funding led by NEA to expand its automated relationship-mapping system. The platform parses emails, call logs, and CRM data to generate dynamic customer org charts, helping sales teams identify key decision-makers. Already adopted by companies including Cognition and Intercom, the tool aims to shorten complex enterprise deal cycles from months to weeks.
