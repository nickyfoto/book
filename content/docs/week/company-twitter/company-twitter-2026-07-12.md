# Company@X — 2026-07-12

## Signal of the Day
AfterQuery revealed its revenue has grown to multiples of $100 million, signaling explosive demand for specialized AI training data and high-fidelity reinforcement learning environments outside of the established "giant" incumbents. This underscores the massive scale and profitability of the backend data market quietly powering the latest models from major AI labs.

## Key Announcements

**AfterQuery** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/spencermateega/status/2076325301026721986#m)
AI data provider AfterQuery announced it is rapidly closing the gap with major market players, citing a revenue run rate that is multiples past $100 million. The startup is aggressively targeting post-training researchers and major AI labs by supplying specialized long-horizon tasks, professional knowledge work data, and code generation data. This highlights a highly lucrative, multi-billion dollar secondary market of companies supplying the fundamental training data and internal evaluation tools needed to drive AI progress.

**Google Cloud** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleCloudTech/status/2076305579371372644#m)
Google Cloud launched an intermediate course on engineering AI agents using its Agent Development Kit (ADK). This certification course is the third step in the Gemini Enterprise Agent Ready learning path, directly supporting Google's strategy to incentivize and train developers to build enterprise applications on its Gemini ecosystem.

**Tesla** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/Tesla/status/2076426214416498949#m)
Tesla highlighted a real-world edge case where its FSD Supervised software successfully avoided a collision with a deer despite the cameras facing direct, blinding sunlight. The company is using this to signal the robustness of its vision-only autonomous driving architecture, particularly its ability to maintain perception in harsh lighting conditions that typically compromise human vision.
