# Company@X — 2026-08-25

## Signal of the Day
**OpenAI** has unveiled the first performance results for **Jalapeño**, its custom AI inference chip, claiming a major power-efficiency leap alongside higher throughput and lower latency. The company plans to begin deploying the silicon in its production compute infrastructure by the end of 2026, marking a critical step in a multi-generational custom hardware roadmap to scale its services.

## Key Announcements

**Skild AI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/SkildAI/status/2092300842900865389#m)
The robotics startup has launched **S1**, a new foundation model capable of executing long-horizon tasks (spanning over 10 minutes) from a single video prompt without any task-specific fine-tuning. S1 demonstrates general-purpose robot intelligence by executing complex physical manipulations far outside its training distribution, such as preparing pancakes after watching a single demonstration. This breakthrough represents a major milestone for in-context learning directly on physical robotic hardware.

**Adaptyv Bio** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/adaptyvbio/status/2092324587342303697#m)
Adaptyv Bio has raised a \$40 million Series A round to construct high-throughput automated wet lab infrastructure—a "biological gigafactory"—for agentic biology. In a joint effort with Anthropic, the company verified that Claude can autonomously design functional, novel protein binders from scratch, which were then physically synthesized and tested in Adaptyv’s automated labs. This partnership establishes a closed-loop engineering system where AI agents design, execute, and iterate on biological experiments.

**Waymo** · [Source](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/08/waymo-in-munich/)
Waymo is expanding its fully autonomous ride-hailing services internationally, announcing plans to bring the **Waymo Driver** to Munich, Germany. Mapping and testing vehicles will arrive in Munich over the coming weeks to begin laying the groundwork. The deployment targets a commercial, fully autonomous ride-hailing service launch in Munich in late 2027.

**AWS** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/i/article/2092285928823717994)
Amazon Web Services has launched **AWS Agent Registry** under Amazon Bedrock AgentCore to manage and govern enterprise AI agent deployments. The registry allows organizations to discover, register, share, and reuse AI agents, tools, and agent skills across internal teams. This product directly addresses the operational challenge of "agent sprawl" and technical debt as enterprise autonomous workflows scale.

**OpenAI** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/ChatGPT/status/2092366554965107164#m)
OpenAI has upgraded **ChatGPT Work** with the capability to autonomously log in and interact with third-party web and mobile sites. The agent operates securely within a browser environment, shielding user passwords from the model while carrying out end-to-end tasks like booking appointments or preparing utility registrations. This capability transitions ChatGPT from a conversational assistant to an active browser automation agent.

**Apodex AI** · [Source](https://www.apodex.ai/)
Apodex AI has launched its **Apodex 1.1** model family, introducing frontier-level intelligence designed to coordinate asynchronous agent teams. Alongside the models, the startup open-sourced **FrontierAgent**, a locally deployable research workbench that lets users break down complex tasks and manage multiple agents in parallel. The release includes a high-capacity frontier model, a local open-weight version called "Apodex 1.1 mini," and API access.

**Floot** · [Source](http://floot.com)
The development platform launched an integration enabling users to build, debug, and publish live applications directly inside Claude and ChatGPT on web, desktop, and mobile. Floot automates the underlying infrastructure, bypassing Git, Vercel, and database configurations to publish directly to a custom domain. The service is free to start and does not charge separate AI credits, instead running entirely on the user's existing Claude or ChatGPT subscription.
