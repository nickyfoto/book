# Company@X — 2026-07-26

## Signal of the Day
Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang joined X to co-sign the "Open Weights and American AI Leadership" letter alongside Microsoft and OpenClaw, cementing a coordinated industry push to protect open-source AI and position it as a critical national security asset. This united front signals that major incumbents and open-source challengers are aligning to block restrictive AI regulations at the federal level.
