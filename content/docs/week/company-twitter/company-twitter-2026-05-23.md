# Company@X — 2026-05-23

## Signal of the Day
Google DeepMind announced an expanded partnership with the government of Singapore to safely deploy AI technologies at a national scale. The initiative will focus on high-impact, sovereign-level applications including accelerating scientific discovery, advancing pandemic preparedness, and improving healthcare.

## Key Announcements

**Google DeepMind** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleDeepMind/status/2057985225100235022#m)
Google DeepMind is expanding its collaboration with Singapore to safely deploy AI technologies at scale. This new program connects the company with country experts to target critical applications like healthcare, pandemic preparedness, and scientific discovery. This signals an accelerating trend of top AI labs striking direct strategic partnerships with nation-states.

**Y Combinator** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/snowmaker/status/2058254975781031938#m)
Y Combinator extended the application deadline for its summer batch to May 25, leveraging a major new perk where OpenAI is offering $2M in tokens to every YC startup in the spring and summer cohorts. This massive compute subsidy is being aggressively positioned as a replacement for human capital, equated to the raw line-of-code output of a 450-person pre-AI engineering team working for two years.

**Google / Gemini** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/demishassabis/status/2058137765284557092#m)
DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis highlighted the integration of Gemini Live into GBrain v0.40.0, an open-source voice agent. The implementation showcases Gemini Live's capabilities in managing large context windows and executing advanced tool use.

**Hugging Face / zeroentropy.dev** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/garrytan/status/2058187750705418448#m)
A six-person team behind zeroentropy.dev has hit 500K downloads on Hugging Face by building task-specific AI models that run 4-8x faster than models from OpenAI or Anthropic. This highlights a growing strategic shift where highly optimized, task-specific models compete effectively on performance and speed against massive proprietary LLMs.

**a16z** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/a16z/status/2057978671449948488#m)
Marc Andreessen is actively highlighting the regulatory and structural roadblocks to scaling AI physical infrastructure in the US. He emphasizes that the inability to rapidly build data centers, chip plants, and energy plants is a massive problem at the center of current tech discourse.

**OpenCode** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/harjtaggar/status/2058233768121667821#m)
OpenCode is gaining traction as a fast-growing alternative to established coding assistants like Codex. Developers report routing around 5% of production traffic to OpenCode, indicating a diversifying ecosystem of AI coding tools.
