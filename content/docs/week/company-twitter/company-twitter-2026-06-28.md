# Company@X — 2026-06-28

## Signal of the Day
Coinbase demonstrated a blueprint for decoupling exponential AI usage from ballooning costs, cutting its AI spend by nearly 50% through aggressive caching and intelligent model routing. Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue highlighted this architectural shift as proof that enterprise AI infrastructure is moving definitively toward a multi-model future.

## Key Announcements

**Google Cloud** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/GoogleCloudTech/status/2071262347545485684#m)
Google launched GEAR (Gemini Enterprise Agent Ready), showcasing its ADK open-source framework. The release signals an industry push away from brittle prompt engineering, advocating for a structured, code-first software development approach to building enterprise multi-agent systems.

**Coinbase** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/brian_armstrong/status/2070670644577280109#m)
Rather than capping engineer token usage, Coinbase implemented an AI gateway that automatically routes tasks, reserving frontier models for complex planning and defaulting to open-weight models like GLM 5.2 and Kimi 2.7 for execution. Combined with cache-aware request handling that boosted LibreChat cache hits from 5% to 60%, the company slashed its overall AI expenditure while maintaining growth.

**Mastra** · [Source](http://twitter.macworks.dev/calcsam/status/2070572382516129910#m)
Mastra shipped "Durable Agents," a new infrastructure feature that persists agent data streams in real-time via a server cache. This ensures multi-turn autonomous workflows can survive client disconnects, browser refreshes, and network blips without losing state, removing a major friction point for production deployments.
