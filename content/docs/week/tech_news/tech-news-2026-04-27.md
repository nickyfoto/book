# Tech News — 2026-04-27

China has abruptly blocked Meta's $2 billion acquisition of agentic AI startup Manus, citing national security regulations. The unprecedented move forces Meta to unwind the completed deal and marks a dramatic escalation in the US-China AI rivalry as Beijing aggressively tightens its grip on domestic tech talent.

## Top Stories

**Microsoft and OpenAI End Exclusive AI Pact** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/918981/openai-microsoft-renegotiate-contract)
Microsoft and OpenAI have fundamentally restructured their foundational partnership, dropping the exclusivity clause that kept OpenAI tied strictly to Azure. OpenAI is now free to serve its products across rival cloud providers, while Microsoft will no longer pay a revenue share to the AI startup and instead holds a non-exclusive license through 2032. It is a massive shift that decouples the two behemoths and allows OpenAI to aggressively pursue enterprise customers on its own terms.

**Musk and Altman Face Off in High-Stakes Trial** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/917225/sam-altman-elon-musk-openai-lawsuit)
The bitter feud between Elon Musk and Sam Altman has officially landed in an Oakland federal courtroom, with jury selection kicking off today. Musk is seeking up to $150 billion in damages, alleging Altman and Greg Brockman deceived him into funding OpenAI under the guise of a nonprofit before pivoting to a closed-source, profit-driven model. The outcome of this trial could force OpenAI to alter its corporate structure right as it eyes a highly anticipated IPO, potentially reshaping the entire AI industry.

**DeepSeek V4 Triggers an AI Price War** · [Slashdot](https://news.slashdot.org/story/26/04/27/0328257/deepseek-v4-arrives-with-near-state-of-the-art-intelligence-at-16th-the-cost)
Chinese AI lab DeepSeek has released V4, a massive 1.6-trillion-parameter open-source model that rivals the performance of leading proprietary systems like GPT-5.5 and Claude Opus 4.7. More importantly, it achieves this near state-of-the-art intelligence at roughly one-sixth of the API cost, aggressively undercutting Silicon Valley's best. The release is poised to force a major rethink of advanced AI economics and demonstrates China's rapidly accelerating capabilities.

**Valve Resurrects the Steam Controller** · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/gaming/valves-steam-controller-costs-99-and-arrives-may-4-170058529.html?src=rss)
After years of R&D, Valve is officially launching a $99 sequel to the Steam Controller on May 4. The heavily revised gamepad features dual trackpads, drift-proof TMR joysticks, and deep integration with the Steam ecosystem, though it explicitly functions only for games booted through Steam. It serves as Valve's opening salvo for its renewed push into the living room ahead of the long-delayed Steam Machine.

**EU Orders Google to Open Android to Rival AIs** · [Slashdot](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/26/04/27/2113232/eu-tells-google-to-open-up-ai-on-android-google-says-thats-unwarranted-intervention)
The European Commission is leveraging its Digital Markets Act to force Google to give third-party AI assistants the same deep, system-level Android integration currently enjoyed by its own Gemini model. The proposed rules would grant rival AIs access to local device data and screen context, prompting Google to slam the move as an unwarranted intervention that compromises privacy and security.

**Trump Administration Purges the National Science Board** · [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/science/2026/04/national-science-board-eviscerated-trump-admin-fires-all-22-members/)
In an unprecedented move, the Trump administration has abruptly fired all 22 members of the National Science Board via a terse two-sentence email. The mass termination eviscerates the independent advisory body that steers the National Science Foundation, delivering a severe blow to the American scientific enterprise and heightening fears of political interference.
