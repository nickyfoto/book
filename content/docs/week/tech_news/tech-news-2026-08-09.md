# Tech News — 2026-08-09

The decision highlights a growing industry-wide panic as advanced agentic models cross capability thresholds that existing safety infrastructure is ill-equipped to contain.

**Google Divestiture Battle: Public Knowledge Pushes for Chrome Sale, Mozilla Warns of Collapse** · [Slashdot](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/26/08/09/0351205/google-should-still-be-forced-to-shed-chrome-advocacy-group-argues)

This highlights the high-stakes balancing act of antitrust enforcers as they attempt to dismantle Google's search monopoly without collateral damage to the open web's fragile ecosystem.

**Stripe Abandons Static Runbooks for Automated Database Remediation** · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/database-remediation-graph/)
**Stripe** has overhauled its global database operations by modeling MongoDB shards as dynamic graphs and using Dijkstra's algorithm to plan automated incident recoveries. This shift from rigid, hard-coded runbooks has eliminated manual intervention for complex failure states, cutting database-related pager alerts by 30% and eliminating 12 days of unhealthy database states annually. The engineering achievement demonstrates how large-scale enterprise platforms are turning to autonomous state machines to prevent on-call engineer burnout.

**Gem Browser Hallucinates a Customized Internet on the Fly** · [Slashdot](https://idle.slashdot.org/story/26/08/09/2048241/ai-powered-browser-just-generates-every-website-from-scratch)
A DeepMind engineer has released a test-flight mobile browser named **Gem** that treats every entered URL as a prompt, generating custom-themed web layouts dynamically using Google's Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite. While the browser allows users to instantly skin sites into various styles—such as "punk zine" or "Windows 95"—it routinely hallucinates content, inventing realistic-looking fake links and inaccurate biography details. The prototype is a fascinating look at a world where fast, cheap inference could soon replace fetching actual web pages with generated approximations.

**California City Declares State of Emergency After Cyberattack Knocks Out 911 Operations** · [Slashdot](https://yro.slashdot.org/story/26/08/09/2144257/california-city-declares-state-of-emergency-after-cyberattack)
**Suisun City, California** has declared a state of emergency after a cyberattack compromised police and fire dispatch, 911 routing, and municipal records. The city took its entire network offline to isolate the threat, relying on neighboring Solano County to handle emergency routing while federal agencies investigate the breach. The attack is the latest in a wave of local government security failures across the region, putting a spotlight on the vulnerability of municipal infrastructure to cyber extortion.

**Anthropic Minimizes Developer Friction by Defaulting Claude Code to Auto Mode** · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/09/anthropic-is-turning-claude-codes-auto-mode-on-by-default/)
**Anthropic** announced that its command-line tool **Claude Code** will soon have its autonomous "auto mode" turned on by default, allowing the AI to execute multi-step programming tasks with less developer oversight. This aggressive step toward developer autonomy comes as rival platforms also expand agentic capabilities, despite growing concerns about AI agents causing unintended system disruptions. The change signals a major bet on the reliability of Claude’s software development models under hands-free execution.
