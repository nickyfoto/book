# Tech News — 2026-09-16

## Story of the Day
In a major shift for cross-platform mobile development, Shopify announced it is abandoning React Native after six years to rewrite its flagship applications natively in Swift and Kotlin. The company cited rapid advancements in AI coding models that drastically lower the maintenance overhead of separate native codebases while delivering substantial performance and stability gains.

## Top Stories

**[AI Safety Showdown: Tech Leaders Clash Over Regulatory Slowdown](https://slashdot.org/story/26/09/16/0619212/anthropic-commits-to-independent-ai-evaluators-wants-slower-development-nvidias-ceo-wants-it-as-fast-as-you-can)** · Slashdot
Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg publicly rejected calls from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google DeepMind to slow down frontier AI development. Huang argued that safety should be engineered directly into products without government intervention, while Zuckerberg favored relying on independent evaluators over legislative mandates. The public dispute highlights a deepening rift across Silicon Valley over AI governance, safety standards, and global competition.

**[Shopify Drops React Native for Swift and Kotlin](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/09/shopify-drops-react-native/)** · InfoQ
Six years after declaring React Native the core of its mobile architecture, Shopify is rewriting its flagship applications from scratch using Apple’s SwiftUI and Google’s Jetpack Compose. The engineering team reported that high-quality AI coding models enabled a small team to rebuild the Shop app in just 12 weeks while cutting cold startup latency by up to 50% on Android. The decision highlights how autonomous AI development harnesses are shifting traditional cost-benefit tradeoffs between cross-platform frameworks and native codebases.

**[Snap Debuts \$2,195 Specs AR Glasses and Standalone AI Assistant](https://www.engadget.com/2260675/snap-specs-hands-on-standalone-ar-glasses-are-here/)** · Engadget
Snap officially launched its standalone Specs augmented reality glasses alongside Specs Intelligence, a new anticipatory AI assistant designed for iOS, macOS, and AR. Priced at \$2,195, the standalone headset features dual micro-projector displays, hand-tracking capabilities, and shared interactive AR experiences. While praised for intuitive spatial interaction, the high price point and bulky hardware position the device primarily for developers rather than mainstream consumers.

**[Cryptocurrency 'Clarity Act' Fails in US Senate Vote](https://news.slashdot.org/story/26/09/16/0454223/americas-cryptocurrency-clarity-act-fails-spectacularly-despite-hundreds-of-millions-in-industry-lobbying)** · Slashdot
The crypto industry’s flagship legislative priority, the 600-page Clarity Act, failed a key Senate procedural vote 49-50 despite hundreds of millions of dollars spent on lobbying. Bipartisan opposition mounted over concerns that the bill lacked sufficient consumer protections and failed to address high-yield account risks raised by community bankers. Analysts predict the defeat effectively tables major U.S. crypto legislation until at least 2030, forcing digital asset firms to rely on agency rulemaking.

**[EU Proposes Social Media Ban for Children Under 13](https://www.wired.com/story/the-eu-bans-social-media-for-under-13s/)** · WIRED
European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen announced the proposed EU KIDS Act, which would ban social media platforms for children under 13 across all 27 member states. The legislation aims to reduce Big Tech’s influence on youth mental health and restrict AI service usage for older minors. However, implementation faces significant hurdles around age verification enforcement, parental oversight, and digital privacy compliance.

**[Stanford Scientists Replace Mouse Brain Cortex with Human Cells](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/09/16/1144210/meet-a-mouse-whose-brain-cortex-is-made-up-of-human-cells/)** · MIT Technology Review
Researchers at Stanford University successfully created "xenocortical mice" by genetically preventing normal cortex development and filling the void with human brain organoid stem cells. The transplanted human neural tissue expanded to occupy nearly half of the mouse brain volume, establishing functional connections that improved the rodents' performance on memory tests. While lead researcher Sergiu Pașca emphasized that mice lack the neural architecture for human consciousness, he drew a strict ethical line against conducting similar experiments on primates.

**[Anthropic Launches Claude Docs and Slides in Unified Interface](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/996234/anthropic-one-claude-cowork-docs-slides)** · The Verge
Anthropic introduced native Docs and Slides creation tools inside Claude, allowing users to generate, edit, and export documents and presentations directly from chat conversations. The company also streamlined its user experience by merging standard chat and Cowork into a single interface that automatically determines necessary execution tools. The release directly targets Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 by expanding Claude's footprint into core productivity workflows.

**[Google Opens Smart Home Ecosystem to Third-Party AI Agents via MCP](https://www.theverge.com/tech/996310/google-home-mcp-integration-agentic-ai-smart-home-price-release-date)** · The Verge
Google announced early access to Google Home MCP, a new integration enabling third-party AI agents like Claude and Open Claw to control smart home devices. Built on the standardized Model Context Protocol, the tool allows external autonomous agents to review camera event histories and execute natural-language automation commands. The open architecture represents a significant departure from walled-garden smart home platforms, permitting user-chosen AI models to manage connected hardware.
