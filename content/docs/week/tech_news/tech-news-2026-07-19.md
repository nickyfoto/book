# Tech News — 2026-07-19

## Story of the Day
OpenAI's latest flagship model, GPT-5.6-Sol, has been accidentally deleting users' files—including an entire production database—in what the company defensively brushed off as an "honest mistake". The catastrophic bug occurs when the model runs in full access mode without sandboxing, leading the system to mistakenly override and erase the host's home directory.

**[Netflix Drops $587M on Ben Affleck's AI Startup](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/19/netflix-paid-587m-for-ben-afflecks-ai-filmmaking-startup/)** · TechCrunch
Netflix has acquired InterPositive, an AI filmmaking startup co-founded by Ben Affleck, for $587 million in cash. The massive acquisition underscores the streaming giant's aggressive push to integrate generative AI directly into its creative and production pipelines.

**[SpaceX Open Sources Grok Build After Privacy Backlash](https://news.slashdot.org/story/26/07/19/034258/grok-build-coding-tool-open-sourced-this-week-promises-to-respect-zero-data-retention)** · Slashdot
Days after researchers caught Grok Build silently uploading entire user repositories to Google Cloud, SpaceX has open-sourced the coding tool's CLI to regain developer trust. Elon Musk and SpaceX promised to delete all previously scooped coding data, acknowledging that data retention had been quietly enabled by default during the tool's beta phase.

**[Google AlphaEvolve Hits General Availability](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/07/alphaevolve-generally-available/)** · InfoQ
Google has released AlphaEvolve, an evolutionary code optimization agent powered by Gemini, to all Google Cloud enterprise customers. By testing mutated candidate programs against user-defined metrics, the tool has already shown impressive results for early adopters, though it requires highly structured environments and automatable evaluation functions to actually work in production.

**[Robotaxi 'Sleepers' Are Tying Up Emergency Services](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/26/07/19/236218/dozens-of-robotaxi-riders-are-falling-asleep-sparking-frantic-calls-for-emergency-services)** · Slashdot
Riders falling asleep in autonomous vehicles are creating an absurd new headache for municipal first responders. Waymo and other robotaxi operators are frequently forced to dial 911 when remote assistants can't rouse unresponsive passengers through in-car speakers, treating the intoxicated or exhausted sleepers as potential medical emergencies.

**[India’s First Private Commercial Rocket Reaches Orbit](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/07/indias-first-privately-developed-rocket-reaches-orbit-on-dramatic-debut-launch/)** · Ars Technica
Skyroot Aerospace successfully launched its Vikram-1 rocket, marking India's first fully commercial satellite launcher to reach orbit. The solid-fueled rocket can carry up to 350 kilograms to low-Earth orbit, positioning Skyroot as a serious competitor in the dedicated small satellite launch market.

**[France Orders ISPs to Block Polymarket](https://yro.slashdot.org/story/26/07/18/2224236/france-orders-isps-to-block-access-to-polymarket)** · Slashdot
France's regulatory authority for gambling has ordered internet service providers to block access to the popular prediction market platform Polymarket. The strict geoblock follows a previous regulatory move targeting French financial transactions on the site, mirroring similar recent crackdowns in Spain.
