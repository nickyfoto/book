## Top Stories

**[Apple Sends Legal Letters To Dozens of OpenAI Employees](https://apple.slashdot.org/story/26/07/17/1552258/apple-sends-legal-letters-to-dozens-of-openai-employees)** · Slashdot
Apple is escalating its trade secrets lawsuit against OpenAI by issuing preservation letters to around 40 former employees now working at the AI firm. The litigation alleges a coordinated effort by OpenAI to poach key hardware engineers and misappropriate proprietary designs. With OpenAI reportedly considering an IPO, this aggressively timed legal maneuver could significantly disrupt the company's public offering plans.

**[Meta In Talks To Lease Computing Power To Anthropic In Potential $10 Billion Deal](https://hardware.slashdot.org/story/26/07/17/1732212/meta-in-talks-to-lease-computing-power-to-anthropic-in-potential-10-billion-deal)** · Slashdot
Meta is in early discussions to lease computing power from its vast data centers to Anthropic in an arrangement potentially worth $10 billion over two years. The deal would create a massive new cloud computing revenue stream for Meta. It also underscores the severe infrastructure constraints facing frontier AI developers as they scramble to secure enough chips to train and run their models.

**[Billing Software Error Sends Billion-Dollar AWS Estimates](https://news.slashdot.org/story/26/07/17/1835215/billing-software-error-sends-billion-dollar-aws-estimates)** · Slashdot
A bug in Amazon Web Services' Cost Explorer system caused customers to receive heart-stopping billing estimates, with some accounts showing inaccurate fees in the billions or trillions of dollars. Amazon identified a unit pricing computation issue and halted updates, reassuring panicked users that the staggering figures did not reflect their actual usage or charges.

**[Astronomers Find an Atmosphere On a Nearby Earth-Like Planet](https://science.slashdot.org/story/26/07/17/0029203/astronomers-find-an-atmosphere-on-a-nearby-earth-like-planet)** · Slashdot
Researchers have directly detected helium escaping from LHS 1140 b, a rocky exoplanet located 48 light-years away in its star's habitable zone. This marks the first confirmed atmosphere on a rocky, Earth-like planet outside our solar system. The discovery significantly boosts the odds that the world could harbor liquid water, making it a prime candidate in the search for extraterrestrial life.

**[Truth Social To Sell Wall Street Firms the 'Fastest' Access To Trump’s Post](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/26/07/17/0022208/truth-social-to-sell-wall-street-firms-the-fastest-access-to-trumps-post)** · Slashdot
Trump Media is launching a licensed data feed dubbed "Truth API" to give algorithmic trading firms the fastest possible access to Donald Trump's market-moving posts. Launching August 1st, the API directly monetizes the president's statements for high-speed traders. The move raises immediate concerns over profound conflicts of interest, given the Trump family's massive shareholder stake in the platform.

**[San Francisco orders Apple, Google to remove nudify apps from app stores](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/07/apple-google-must-stop-profiting-off-ai-nudify-apps-san-francisco-ag-says/)** · Ars Technica

The city claims both tech giants are profiting off tools explicitly used to non-consensually target women and girls, which violates California state laws.
