**[Anthropic Eclipses OpenAI With $65B Raise and Opus 4.8 Launch]** · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-raises-65-billion-nears-1t-valuation-ahead-of-ipo/)
Anthropic has secured $65 billion in Series H funding, pushing its post-money valuation to $965 billion and formally overtaking its primary rival, OpenAI. Alongside the massive capital raise, the company launched Claude Opus 4.8, which introduces enhanced computer programming skills and a lower likelihood of hallucinating unsupported claims. **[Illinois Lawmakers Just Passed America’s Strongest AI Safety Bill]** · [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/illinois-pass-major-ai-safety-law-pritzker/)
In the face of stalled federal regulation, Illinois has passed SB 315, a landmark piece of legislation requiring major AI developers to submit annual third-party safety testing reports. Assuming the governor signs the bill, the law will compel tech giants to report critical safety incidents within 72 hours and mandate robust protections for corporate whistleblowers. This decisive move positions Illinois as a major testing ground for state-level AI governance.

**[Oura’s New Ring 5 Is Smaller and Lighter—and Adds an AI Health Coach]** · [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/oura-ring-5/)
Oura has debuted the Ring 5, shedding 40 percent of the physical bulk from its predecessor while managing to extend battery life up to nine days. Beyond a slimmer profile, the $399 wearable expands its platform ecosystem with a live activity mode, new GLP-1 response tracking tools, and a "Health Radar" feature to monitor nighttime breathing and blood pressure. The health tracking platform is also partnering with Counsel Health to provide AI-assisted medical guidance directly within its companion app.

**[How a new extraction process could unlock the world’s lithium]** · [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/05/28/1138096/lithium-extraction-rock-zero/)
A team of MIT researchers has commercialized a highly efficient method for extracting lithium from rocks using ammonium fluoride, a weak acid commonly found in glass etching cream. Unlike traditional hard-rock mining that requires roasting ore at extreme temperatures, this process occurs in simple plastic tanks below the boiling point of water, dramatically reducing energy costs and emissions. At scale, the startup behind the technology, Rock Zero, estimates it will be able to extract battery-grade lithium for less than $6,000 per metric ton.

**[Waymo to begin passenger rides in its new Ojai robotaxi]** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/transportation/938156/waymo-zeekr-ojai-robotaxi-passenger-trips)
Alphabet's Waymo is officially deploying its newest custom-built robotaxi, an electric minivan manufactured by Zeekr that has been rebranded as "Ojai". The purpose-built, driverless vehicles will begin offering free rides to select passengers in San Francisco, Los Angeles, and Phoenix to collect operational data before transitioning to paid trips.
