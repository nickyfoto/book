**[Google AI Reshuffles Leadership as Pioneer Jeff Dean Departs](https://www.theverge.com/tech/976108/google-ai-leadership-shakeup-jeff-dean-demis-hassabis-deepmind)** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/976108/google-ai-leadership-shakeup-jeff-dean-demis-hassabis-deepmind)
Seismic shifts are shaking up Google's AI leadership as DeepMind co-founder Demis Hassabis steps down from day-to-day operations to become the unit's chairman and Alphabet’s chief scientist. Meanwhile, legendary AI pioneer Jeff Dean is leaving the company after 27 years to launch a scientific research automation startup called Discovery Loop, which Google is backing. To combat pressure from rivals like OpenAI and Anthropic, Google is consolidating its AI leadership at its Mountain View headquarters, a transition coming right after the tech giant reported its first-ever negative cash flow quarter.

**[FCC Scraps Historic Broadcast TV Ownership Limits](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/08/trump-fcc-kills-tv-ownership-cap-claiming-authority-over-limit-set-by-congress/)** · [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/08/trump-fcc-kills-tv-ownership-cap-claiming-authority-over-limit-set-by-congress/)
In a split 2-1 vote, Republican Commissioners Brendan Carr and Olivia Trusty eliminated the historic 39 percent audience reach cap, replacing it with a "case-by-case" review process. Carr argued the move would allow local stations to invest in local news and better compete with unregulated streaming platforms. However, dissenting Democratic Commissioner Anna Gomez warned that the decision will simply allow national media conglomerates to squeeze local broadcasters and dictate regional programming.

**[SoftBank's \$50 Million Trump Library Donation Preceded Federal Data Center Lease](https://www.theverge.com/policy/976138/softbank-trump-library-data-center-ohio)** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/policy/976138/softbank-trump-library-data-center-ohio)
SoftBank Group has admitted to donating \$50 million to the Trump Presidential Library in January, a disclosure that is raising eyebrows across Washington. Just months after the donation, the federal government announced a lease deal allowing the Japanese tech giant to build a sprawling AI data center on federal land in Portsmouth, Ohio. Democratic lawmakers have expressed deep concerns about potential bribery and quid pro quo, while SoftBank continues to face soaring debt levels from its heavy investments in the AI infrastructure gold rush.

**[CosmosEscape: The Six-Month Race to Fix Azure's Global Database Vulnerability](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/cosmosescape-master-key/)** · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/cosmosescape-master-key/)
Wiz Research has disclosed a severe security failure in Azure Cosmos DB, dubbed "CosmosEscape," which gave attackers complete read and write access to every database on the service. By executing a sandbox escape using reflective .NET code, researchers compromised the database gateway and exposed Microsoft's "Cosmos Master Key," a master credential that pinpoints primary keys for all accounts. While Microsoft hotfixed the primary vulnerability immediately, fully removing the platform-wide key and rearchitecting the database credential model took the cloud giant over six months.

**[Ford Targets the Budget Market with \$28,350 'Fathom' Electric Truck](https://www.theverge.com/transportation/975977/ford-fathom-universal-ev-platform-logo-price)** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/transportation/975977/ford-fathom-universal-ev-platform-logo-price)
Ford is aiming to trigger its own "Model T moment" in the EV market by announcing the Fathom, a midsize electric pickup truck starting at a highly competitive \$28,350. Developed by a clean-sheet California skunkworks team, the truck is built on a universal platform that uses a zonal electronics architecture to dramatically slash manufacturing costs and wiring. Despite the bold pricing strategy, the real vehicle remains under wraps, as Ford does not plan to reveal what the Fathom actually looks like until early next year.

**[FDA Approves Moderna’s mRNA Seasonal Flu Vaccine](https://arstechnica.com/health/2026/08/modernas-mrna-flu-shot-earns-fda-approval-after-rollercoaster-review/)** · [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/health/2026/08/modernas-mrna-flu-shot-earns-fda-approval-after-rollercoaster-review/)
Despite intense political friction, the FDA has approved Moderna's mFLUSIVA, marking the first time an mRNA-based seasonal influenza vaccine has been authorized for use. The vaccine, which proved 27 percent more effective than traditional flu shots in clinical trials, can be manufactured and matched to circulating strains in just two to three months. The approval represents a major victory for mRNA technology, especially in the face of ongoing efforts by anti-vaccine Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. to cancel federal immunization campaigns.
