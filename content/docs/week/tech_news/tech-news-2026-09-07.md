## Top Stories

**CERN Renounces RHEL for Debian to Save Legacy Hardware** · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/09/cern-debian-infra/)
**CERN** is transitioning its entire accelerator-control fleet away from Red Hat-derived distributions to **Debian 13 ("Trixie")**, aiming to complete the migration by the end of 2026. The primary driver for this major platform pivot is Red Hat's progressive tightening of compiler microarchitecture baselines (RHEL 9 requiring x86-64-v2, and RHEL 10 targeting v3), which would prematurely obsolete thousands of CERN's legacy embedded control devices engineered for 10- to 15-year lifecycles. While CERN’s massive compute farms and Tier-0 grid infrastructure will remain standardized on AlmaLinux and RHEL, engineers decided that throwing away reliable, functional hardware simply to comply with Red Hat's baselines would be an irresponsible waste of research funds.

**Liquid Network Suffers \$320 Million Hack on Federation Wallet** · [Slashdot](https://yro.slashdot.org/story/26/09/07/0727220/bitcoin-based-liquid-network-says-320-million-withdrawn-in-hack)
The Bitcoin-based payments and settlement platform, **Liquid Network**, announced that roughly **\$320 million** has been drained from its federation wallet in a major security breach. Perpetrated by self-described "white-hat hackers," the attackers withdrew approximately **4,000 of the 4,200 Bitcoin** held in the network's central repository. Liquid Network publicly apologized and warned its users that Liquid wallets would be directly impacted, highlighting the persistent vulnerabilities plaguing core scaling layers in the cryptocurrency ecosystem.

**vlt 1.0 Ships as a Secure, Malware-Blocking Drop-In npm Replacement** · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/09/vlt-npm-replacement/)
Created by the original founders of npm, **vlt 1.0** has launched alongside its hosted package registries and ecosystem mirrors. The new JavaScript package manager mitigates supply chain risks by separating the installation process into a download phase and an execution phase, ensuring that dependency lifecycle scripts **do not run automatically** on developer machines. Furthermore, vlt's hosted registries actively reject known-malicious packages at the gateway, boasting a catalog where more than **275,000 bad package versions** have already been flagged and blocked.

**Early Clinical Data Indicates AI-Generated Drug Candidate Reverses Biological Aging** · [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/07/science/ai-generated-drug-longevity.html)
An experimental drug candidate, **rentosertib**, which was originally developed using artificial intelligence to treat a rare lung condition, has shown early signs of reducing biological age. In a recent clinical trial, the drug candidate successfully reduced the biological age markers of participants by **up to six years**. While the therapeutic agent's developer, Insilico Medicine, focuses on the broader applications of this AI-discovered molecule, the breakthrough represents a major milestone in both machine-learning-driven pharmaceutical research and longevity science.

**US Military Disables Ad Tracking on Official Devices to Shield Troop Locations** · [Slashdot](https://yro.slashdot.org/story/26/09/06/0553230/us-military-disables-ad-trackers-on-devices-to-protect-troops)
The **United States Military** is disabling advertising identifiers and ad trackers on official computers and mobile devices to block foreign adversaries from tracking military movements. The defense initiative is a direct response to reports that commercially available geolocation data was used by adversaries to target American troops deployed in the Middle East. While ad tracking has been blocked on Windows PCs since before 2021, mobile operating systems like iOS and Android have only had tracking disabled by default **since early February 2026**, prompting concerns from lawmakers that current measures are still not robust enough to neutralize the threat.

**Switzerland Launches 3,000-User Pilot to Replace Microsoft 365 with openDesk** · [Slashdot](https://yro.slashdot.org/story/26/09/07/0128205/switzerlands-federal-government-tests-open-source-alternatives-to-microsoft-on-3000-computers)
Switzerland's federal government has initiated a pilot program deploying **openDesk**—a German open-source collaboration suite—across 3,000 workstations to evaluate it as an alternative to Microsoft 365. The strategic migration is driven by cybersecurity and sovereignty concerns, specifically the risk of U.S. cloud legislation exposing sensitive Swiss data to foreign authorities and rising proprietary licensing costs. Demonstrating the urgency of the threat, Switzerland's military cybersecurity unit, **Cyber Command**, is bypassing the civilian trial and is poised to fully transition to openDesk by October 2026.

**Lake Mariner Data Center Fire Exposes Oversight Gaps in AI Buildout** · [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/the-ai-data-center-boom-is-causing-new-accountability-problems/)
A fire at the unfinished **\$3.2 billion Lake Mariner data center** in Somerset, New York has revealed alarming accountability issues in the rapid expansion of AI infrastructure. Responding firefighters reported going in **"blind"** to face chemical smoke with dead hydrants, no working alarms, and safety documents that allegedly burned up in the blaze. The facility operates under a complex web of corporate entities, including operator TeraWulf leasing land from its own CEO, Fluidstack running the compute, and Anthropic and Google backing the site's massive power demand.

**Netflix Slashes Flink Compute Expenditures by 58% via Operator-Level Autoscaling** · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/09/netflix-flink-autoscaler/)
Netflix is transitioning its massive stream processing fleet of more than 30,000 jobs to the open-source **Apache Flink Autoscaler** to optimize complex, stateful pipelines. Unlike their legacy cluster-level approach, the new autoscaler estimates the **true processing rate** of individual operators within the job graph and adjusts their parallelism dynamically. This fine-grained optimization has yielded substantial efficiency gains, with one internal team already reducing its annualized Flink compute expenditure by **58%—saving roughly \$1.1 million**.
