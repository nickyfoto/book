**[DeepSeek permanently guts pricing on its flagship AI model](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-23/deepseek-to-make-permanent-75-discount-on-flagship-ai-model)** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-23/deepseek-to-make-permanent-75-discount-on-flagship-ai-model)

**[Decade-old Linux kernel flaw grants instant root access](https://linux.slashdot.org/story/26/05/23/0727241/linux-kernel-flaw-lets-unprivileged-users-access-root-only-files-execute-arbitrary-commands-as-root)** · [Slashdot](https://linux.slashdot.org/story/26/05/23/0727241/linux-kernel-flaw-lets-unprivileged-users-access-root-only-files-execute-arbitrary-commands-as-root)
A critical logic vulnerability (CVE-2026-46333) discovered by Qualys has been hiding in mainline Linux kernels since November 2016, allowing unprivileged local users to effortlessly snatch root access and exfiltrate sensitive credential material, including host private keys. With working exploits already circulating in the wild, the threat completely erodes the privilege boundaries on shared multi-tenant hosts, turning any low-privilege service account into a vector for total system compromise.

Anthropic's Project Glasswing is proving its mettle in automated cybersecurity, with the company reporting that its Mythos model has already identified over 10,000 high and critical vulnerabilities for partners. It's a stark reminder that while the industry worries about AI writing malware, generative models are already having an immediate, massive impact by relentlessly auditing deeply flawed legacy codebases.

**[NASA may sever Caltech’s century-long grip on JPL](https://science.slashdot.org/story/26/05/23/038238/caltech-could-lose-control-of-jpl-for-first-time-in-decades)** · [Slashdot](https://science.slashdot.org/story/26/05/23/038238/caltech-could-lose-control-of-jpl-for-first-time-in-decades)
For the first time since the deep-space lab's inception in the 1930s, NASA is opening up the management contract for the Jet Propulsion Laboratory to outside competition when the current agreement expires in 2028. Opening this FFRDC (federally funded research and development center) to new institutions outside of Caltech could radically reshape the culture, oversight, and execution of America's robotic planetary missions.

**[Nvidia pressures Super Micro over Taiwan compliance](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-23/nvidia-ceo-urges-super-micro-to-tighten-up-amid-taiwan-crackdown)** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-23/nvidia-ceo-urges-super-micro-to-tighten-up-amid-taiwan-crackdown)

**[Google's AI Search goes blank over the word "disregard"](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/26/05/23/1647242/friday-googles-ai-powered-search-results-glitched-on-the-word-disregard)** · [Slashdot](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/26/05/23/1647242/friday-googles-ai-powered-search-results-glitched-on-the-word-disregard)

Though Google appears to have patched the broken tool, it's a glaring indicator of the unpredictable fragility introduced when a deterministic search index is forcefully replaced by a generative model.

**[FBI pushes for real-time license plate surveillance](https://www.wired.com/story/security-news-this-week-fbi-license-plate-reader-real-time-access/)** · [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/security-news-this-week-fbi-license-plate-reader-real-time-access/)
