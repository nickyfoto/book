# Tech News — 2026-06-14

## Top Stories

**[State Attorneys General Launch Sweeping Investigation Into OpenAI](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/13/technology/states-investigating-openai.html)** · The New York Times
A coalition of state attorneys general has officially opened an investigation into OpenAI's core business practices. Regulators are heavily scrutinizing the AI giant's handling of consumer data, its advertising activities, and how its products impact the safety of minors.

**[Meta Forced to Unwind $2B Manus Deal After Beijing Intervention](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/13/meta-reportedly-moves-to-unwind-2b-manus-deal-after-beijings-demand/)** · TechCrunch
Meta is backing out of its $2 billion acquisition of Manus after the Chinese government explicitly demanded the deal be reversed. The forced dismantling of the acquisition signals a severe, direct escalation of Beijing's interference in global technology M&A.

**[US and Japan Pour $1B into 'Genesis' AI Platform for Science](https://yro.slashdot.org/story/26/06/14/0354205/how-americas-energy-department-is-building-a-national-platform-for-doing-science-with-ai)** · Slashdot
The US Department of Energy is attempting to build a "national operating system for science" by integrating 17 national labs, supercomputers, and AI agents into a unified research platform called the Genesis Mission. Japan has signed on as the first international partner, bringing the initial joint investment to $1 billion in an effort to maintain a strategic lead over China in quantum tech, fusion, and biotechnology.

**[UK Prepares Under-16 Social Media and Chatbot Ban](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-14/uk-to-ban-under-16s-from-social-media-including-ai-chatbots-tiktok)** · Bloomberg
The United Kingdom is poised to ban children under 16 from using social media platforms and AI chatbots, mirroring similarly aggressive legislation recently proposed in Australia. Culture Secretary Lisa Nandy acknowledged the ban isn't a "silver bullet," but described it as a necessary structural change to protect child safety online.

**[Meta’s $14B Pivot to Proprietary AI is Failing to Impress Developers](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/26/06/14/1842208/will-metas-14-billion-bet-on-ai-ever-pay-off)** · Slashdot
Despite spending $14 billion and hiring Alexandr Wang to build its "Muse Spark" model, Meta's sudden shift away from its open-source Llama approach is facing heavy skepticism from the developer community. With its virtual reality division bleeding over $80 billion, Wall Street is growing impatient, demanding that Mark Zuckerberg deliver tangible AI monetization rather than just closed-garden ad features.
