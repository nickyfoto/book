**[Stripe Clinches Blockbuster \$7 Billion Deal to Acquire AI Gateway OpenRouter](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/stripe-nears-deal-to-buy-ai-firm-openrouter-for-over-7-billion)** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/stripe-nears-deal-to-buy-ai-firm-openrouter-for-over-7-billion)

This acquisition is one of the largest AI transactions of the year, signaling Stripe’s aggressive play to dominate the payment and routing plumbing of the emerging cognitive economy.

The reorganization highlights the tension between academic safety research and market realities as OpenAI restructures its research-led models ahead of an anticipated, massive commercial initial public offering (IPO).

**[Meta Faces Landmark Trial Over Addictive Design and Child Data Violations](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-16/meta-faces-landmark-trial-over-youth-harm-claims-video)** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-16/meta-faces-landmark-trial-over-youth-harm-claims-video)

The states are seeking significant financial penalties alongside court-ordered structural changes to how Meta’s products operate, while Meta continues to deny the allegations.

**[X Open-Sources Recommendation Algorithm on GitHub and Adds Transparency Tools](https://slashdot.org/story/26/08/16/0223258/x-open-sources-its-ranking-and-filtering-algorithms)** · [Slashdot](https://slashdot.org/story/26/08/16/0223258/x-open-sources-its-ranking-and-filtering-algorithms)
**X has dramatically expanded its open-source footprint on GitHub by releasing the core ranking engine and spam filters that power its default "For You" timeline.** The new release makes X's public repository 10 to 15 times larger than before, exposing the specific configurations and parameters used to score, weight, and filter user posts. To back up the release, X is launching an "Under the Hood" setting allowing users to download their aggregate account metrics as a JSON file to see if any filtering labels have impacted their visibility.

**[Researchers Use Ordinary WiFi Beamforming to Identify and Image People](https://mobile.slashdot.org/story/26/08/16/057254/researchers-use-ordinary-wifi-connections-to-create-images-of-nearby-people-and-their-surroundings)** · [Slashdot](https://mobile.slashdot.org/story/26/08/16/057254/researchers-use-ordinary-wifi-connections-to-create-images-of-nearby-people-and-their-surroundings)
**Cybersecurity experts have demonstrated that unencrypted beamforming feedback information (BFI) exchanged between standard routers and devices can be analyzed to construct radio-based images of people.** In trials involving 197 participants, the machine-learning-assisted system identified individuals with nearly 100% accuracy within seconds, regardless of angle or gait. Because the system reads the ambient radio waves reflecting off physical bodies, people can be tracked even if they are not carrying a phone, smartwatch, or any active WiFi-enabled device themselves.

**[AWS Open-Sources Dogwood to Govern Sequences of AI Agent Tool Calls](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/aws-dogwood-agent-policy/)** · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/aws-dogwood-agent-policy/)
**AWS has open-sourced Dogwood, an Apache-licensed policy language designed to prevent autonomous AI agents from committing multi-step security or financial violations.** Where previous policy engines like Cedar evaluated individual requests in isolation, Dogwood statefully tracks temporal event histories, allowing engineers to write rules that limit running totals or require sequential human approvals. The release addresses the critical distributed-system challenge of parallel, multi-agent coordination, though AWS warns that stateful tracking makes policies ineligible for automated reasoning.

**[China’s Rapid Progress in South Pole Lunar Exploration Sparks Space Race Alarm](https://science.slashdot.org/story/26/08/16/231206/chinas-moon-landing-plans-and-why-the-us-is-so-worried)** · [Slashdot](https://science.slashdot.org/story/26/08/16/231206/chinas-moon-landing-plans-and-why-the-us-is-so-worried)
**China is preparing to launch its Chang'e-7 robotic mission as early as this month to drill for and analyze water ice at the moon's south pole.** The mission will utilize a highly synchronized squad of four vehicles—an orbiter, lander, rover, and mobile "hopper"—putting China ahead of U.S. robotic drilling efforts which are slated for next year at the earliest. This progress, coupled with China's top-down funding and plans for a permanent settlement by 2040, has raised alarm in Washington as NASA struggles to secure a functional lunar lander and remains reliant on a fragmented commercial ecosystem.

**[AWS Rolls Out Serverless Native Vector Search inside Amazon DynamoDB](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/aws-dynamodb-vector-search/)** · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/aws-dynamodb-vector-search/)
**AWS has introduced a native vector search feature for its managed NoSQL database Amazon DynamoDB, simplifying semantic search and RAG workloads.** By storing embeddings and querying vectors directly within the same database table, developers can bypass the architectural complexity and latency overhead of synchronizing external pipelines. The horizontal scaling handles up to 4,096 dimensions with single-digit millisecond latency at a trillion-vector scale, though early adopters note that DynamoDB's storage metering makes it significantly more expensive than S3-based vector alternatives.
