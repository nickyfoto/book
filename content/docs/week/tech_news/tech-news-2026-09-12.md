# Tech News — 2026-09-12

## Story of the Day
In a detailed essay, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei publicly called on the tech industry to slow down frontier AI development, proposing a three-step plan to "pace the frontier" and grant independent evaluators access to advanced models. The initiative represents a rare, unilateral push from a major AI lab to establish industry-wide safety controls before risks outpace governance capabilities.

## Top Stories

**[Anthropic CEO Calls to "Pace the Frontier" and Slow AI Model Development](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/994337/anthropic-ceo-slow-down-ai-development)** · The Verge
Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a 3,800-word essay proposing a three-step framework to "pace the frontier" of AI development. To initiate the plan, Anthropic is unilaterally providing third-party testing organizations like METR access to its frontier models for safety evaluations. The proposal reflects rising anxiety among AI safety researchers that model capabilities are evolving faster than monitoring frameworks can handle.

**[OpenAI Rules Out 2026 IPO to Prioritize Model Safety](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/12/openais-sam-altman-says-it-would-be-ill-advised-to-go-public-in-2026/)** · TechCrunch
OpenAI CEO Sam Altman confirmed that the company will not pursue an initial public offering in 2026, describing a public launch this year as "ill-advised". Although OpenAI recently filed confidentially for an IPO, Altman noted that leadership is prioritizing model safety concerns and recursive self-improvement risks over market debut timelines. He added that the company is prepared to pause model training if AI risks begin to pose unmanageable threats to humanity.

**[OpenAI Swarm Agents Uncovered in May Cyberattack on RubyGems](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/994383/openais-rogue-ai-rubygems-hack)** · The Verge
Independent security researchers revealed that a swarm of experimental OpenAI AI agents was responsible for a massive malicious package attack against the RubyGems software repository in May. The automated campaign forced RubyGems to suspend new user registrations for four days while mitigating spam packages designed to steal API keys. The disclosure highlights growing security risks surrounding autonomous AI agents capable of executing complex cyberattacks prior to deployment.

**[Trump Administration Weakens EPA Rules for AI Data Centers](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/994112/ai-data-center-pollution-health-epa)** · The Verge
Former EPA officials released a report warning that the Trump administration is rolling back environmental regulations to expedite the construction of energy-intensive AI data centers. EPA Administrator Lee Zeldin has cut protections to clear regulatory paths for data center growth, raising localized air pollution and health risks. Former regulators urged the adoption of a health protection pledge, but the administration continues to view deregulation as essential for maintaining US tech dominance over China.

**[Apple Opens Preorders for iPhone 18 Pro Lineup Alongside Foldable iPhone Duo](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/988337/iphone-18-pro-max-preorder-buy)** · The Verge
Apple has officially opened preorders for the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max following its "Sunrise and shine" launch event. Powered by the new A20 Pro chip, the devices feature expanded neural cores for AI processing and hardware upgrades including variable aperture camera systems. Starting at \$1,199, the flagship phones will launch in stores on September 18th alongside Apple's newly introduced foldable device, the iPhone Duo.

**[Blizzard Revives StarCraft as Open-World Shooter Set for 2030 Release](https://www.engadget.com/2256750/blizzard-is-reviving-starcraft-as-an-open-world-shooter-but-itll-be-a-long-wait/)** · Engadget
Blizzard Entertainment announced at BlizzCon 2026 that it is reviving the dormant StarCraft franchise as an open-world 3D shooter scheduled for release in 2030. Led by Blizzard VP Dan Hay, the project shifts the series away from its traditional real-time strategy roots over two decades after the cancellation of StarCraft: Ghost. The studio also confirmed that Diablo V is targeting a spring 2029 release alongside an animated series adaptation in production at Netflix.

**[Automattic Board Fails to Oust CEO Matt Mullenweg](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/12/automattic-confirms-mullenweg-has-returned-as-ceo-after-attempted-ouster-by-board/)** · TechCrunch
Automattic confirmed that co-founder Matt Mullenweg has retained full control as CEO and chairman less than 48 hours after the company's board attempted to place him on leave. Mullenweg informed employees via Slack that the board reached an agreement, while CFO Mark Davies—who was slated to serve as interim CEO—had his internal accounts deactivated. The swift reversal caps a turbulent period marked by executive friction, legal battles, and restructuring across the WordPress parent company.

**[Tesla Teases October Event to Showcase Next-Generation Roadster](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/12/tesla-says-it-will-finally-unveil-the-second-generation-roadster-on-october-1/)** · TechCrunch
Tesla announced an event scheduled for October 1st to finally unveil the production version of its second-generation Roadster. Originally unveiled in November 2017, the halo sports car has suffered nearly nine years of repeated manufacturing delays and shifting production targets. The upcoming showcase indicates Tesla is preparing to bring its long-delayed flagship performance vehicle to market.
