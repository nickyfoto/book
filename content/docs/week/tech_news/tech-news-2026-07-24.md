# Tech News — 2026-07-24

The potential megadeal underscores the exploding value of the infrastructure layer that provides developers with seamless, unified access to both proprietary and open-weight AI models.

## Top Stories

**[Anthropic launches Opus 5](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/anthropic-launches-opus-5/)** · TechCrunch
Anthropic released Claude Opus 5, a model that nears the capabilities of its flagship Fable 5 but at half the price. The release features improved coding and scientific research performance, while intentionally loosening its cybersecurity guardrail interventions by 85 percent to allow for legitimate research use.

**[Qualcomm Plans to Increase Its Prices by Double Digits](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/qualcomm-tells-customers-that-prices-are-going-up-by-double-digits)** · Bloomberg
Qualcomm warned customers it will raise processor prices by "double digits" starting September 1 due to ongoing component shortages. The aggressive hike is expected to send a ripple effect across the consumer electronics sector, inevitably driving up the cost of next-generation smartphones.

**[Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta Warn Against 'Premature Restrictions' of Open-Weight Models](https://meta.slashdot.org/story/26/07/24/1911233/nvidia-microsoft-meta-warn-against-premature-restrictions-of-open-weight-models)** · Slashdot
A broad coalition of tech heavyweights signed an open letter urging US policymakers not to impose sweeping bans on open-weight AI models. The industry pushback comes as Washington debates restricting access to foreign and domestic models to combat the rise of Chinese AI competitors like Kimi K3.

**[Paramount/WBD merger delayed for months as states’ lawsuit moves toward trial](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/07/after-court-loss-paramount-agrees-to-delay-warner-bros-merger-until-trial/)** · Ars Technica
Paramount Skydance agreed to delay its $111 billion merger with Warner Bros. Discovery until June 2027, or until after a ruling on a multi-state antitrust lawsuit. The concession freezes the mega-consolidation while state attorneys general argue the deal would severely damage competition in theatrical and cable markets.

**[SpaceX launches new V3 Starlink satellites but suffers another booster failure](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/spacex-launches-new-v3-starlink-satellites-but-suffers-another-booster-failure/)** · TechCrunch
SpaceX completed its 13th Starship test flight, successfully deploying a payload of 20 next-generation V3 Starlink satellites into orbit. Despite suffering another booster relight anomaly, the flight advances Elon Musk's aggressive timeline for scaling the company's satellite communications empire.

**[Roku raises streaming stick prices by up to 60 percent](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/07/roku-raises-streaming-stick-prices-by-up-to-60-percent/)** · Ars Technica
Roku is drastically hiking prices across its streaming hardware lineup, blaming memory and component shortages driven by the AI data-center boom. The entry-level Streaming Stick jumps from $30 to $40, while the flagship Roku Ultra increases from $100 to $150.

**[Astronomers May Have Discovered First Moon Outside Our Solar System](https://science.slashdot.org/story/26/07/24/0712238/astronomers-may-have-discovered-first-moon-outside-our-solar-system)** · Slashdot
Scientists studying the CD-35 2722 system 73 light-years away believe they have detected an "exosatellite." The discovery is forcing a rethink of planetary classifications, as the massive, gaseous object orbits a brown dwarf—a failed star—rather than a traditional planet.
