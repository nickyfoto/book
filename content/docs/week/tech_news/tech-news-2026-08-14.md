# Tech News — 2026-08-14

## Top Stories

**SpaceX Finalizes \$60 Billion Acquisition of AI Coding Sensation Cursor** · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/spacex-completes-its-60-billion-cursor-acquisition)
**SpaceX has completed its landmark \$60 billion acquisition of Cursor, the red-hot artificial intelligence developer tool**. The buyout represents one of the largest technology acquisitions in history, serving as a core pillar of Elon Musk's aggressive push to rival OpenAI and Anthropic in generative AI. While the colossal premium highlights the immense valuation currently placed on automated software workflows, integrating Cursor's localized AI capabilities directly into SpaceX's operations underscores how mission-critical software automation has become to the aerospace giant's grander ambitions.

**Trump Slaps 100% Tariffs on Sensitive Heavy Drones and Aircraft Components** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/policy/979977/trump-declares-100-percent-tariffs-on-many-drones-and-all-aircraft-parts)
**President Donald Trump has declared a whopping 100 percent tariff on imports of high-capacity and national-security-sensitive drones**. The new trade rules specifically target uncrewed systems weighing over 25 kilograms, drones equipped with thermal cameras, and any corresponding parts, while smaller consumer models face a newly imposed 25 percent tariff. Although the White House frames the protectionist measure as a vital move to secure US airspace and revitalize domestic production, it deals a severe financial blow to commercial operators relying on DJI hardware, even as the news sent US-based drone stocks soaring.

**Judge Orders Google to Eliminate Play Store Friction Within One Week** · [Slashdot](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/26/08/14/1733213/judge-orders-google-to-make-rival-app-store-installs-easier)
**US District Judge James Donato has ordered Google to immediately strip away "anticompetitive friction" from Google Play store downloads**. While Google recently introduced its first non-Google store, Aptoide Games, Epic Games successfully demonstrated to the court that the multi-step, warning-laden installation process was designed to discourage alternative choices. The judge's rapid seven-day deadline signals deep judicial impatience with Google's stalling tactics following Epic's landmark 2023 antitrust victory.

**Apple Partners with Alibaba to Train Custom, China-Approved AI Architecture** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/980160/apple-intelligence-china-custom-ai-model-alibaba)
**Apple has reportedly trained a custom large language model for the Chinese market in partnership with domestic cloud giant Alibaba**. This rare cross-border collaboration represents a major pivot from Apple's previous strategy in China and is aimed at giving the company direct control over its AI ecosystem in a highly competitive smartphone landscape. If approved, the custom model would make Apple the first foreign hardware firm to deploy a Beijing-approved AI suite, helping it defend premium market share amid intense US-China geopolitical tensions.

**NPM 12 Disables Lifecycle Install Scripts by Default in Massive Security Overhaul** · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/npm-12-released)
**In a fundamental shift toward explicit trust, GitHub's newly released npm 12 package manager has disabled automatic dependency preinstall, install, and postinstall scripts**. These automatic lifecycle scripts have historically been a primary vector for supply-chain compromises, involved in roughly 53 percent of all malicious npm attacks over the past year. While security researchers are applauding the change, developer communities are bracing for widespread broken builds and "approval fatigue" as teams are forced to manually verify and explicitly whitelist scripts across heavily nested dependencies.

**Connecticut Court Catches and Sanctions Plaintiff Over Hidden AI Prompt Injections** · [Slashdot](https://yro.slashdot.org/story/26/08/14/0941240/person-hides-prompt-injection-in-legal-filing-telling-ai-to-side-with-them)
**A Connecticut judge has sanctioned a self-represented plaintiff after discovering hidden, software-readable instructions designed to manipulate AI systems in official court filings**. The plaintiff, Matthew Elliott, hid instructions written in a tiny, 3-point white font telling any hypothetical AI model reviewing the document to ignore previous court denials and ensure a ruling in his favor. Although the court does not use AI to process filings, Judge Walter Spader Jr. excoriated the manipulation attempt as a dangerous precedent, permanently banning the plaintiff from electronic filings and forcing him to submit hard, physical paper copies.

**US AI Heavyweights Slit API Pricing to Defend Against Cut-Price Chinese Competitors** · [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/openai-and-anthropic-in-price-war-as-chinese-ai-rivals-gain-ground/)
**Faced with rising enterprise AI bills and stiffening competition, OpenAI and Anthropic have launched aggressive price cuts for their flagship models**. OpenAI has slashed API pricing for its GPT-5.6 Luna by a staggering 80 percent, while Anthropic introduced Claude Opus 5 at half the price of its predecessor, Fable 5. The fierce price war comes as cost-conscious Silicon Valley and European companies look toward highly capable, cheaper alternatives from Chinese open-weight rivals like Moonshot, Z.ai, and DeepSeek.
