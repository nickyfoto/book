# Tech News — 2026-08-29

## Story of the Day
**Meta** agreed to a landmark **\$17.1 billion settlement** with state attorneys general to resolve escalating lawsuits over child safety and youth mental health harms. The historic deal marks one of the largest regulatory resolutions in modern tech history, shifting the burden of young user safety from parental oversight to mandatory platform architecture and strict age verification.

**[Meta Settles State Youth Safety Lawsuits for Record \$17.1 Billion](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/29/technology/meta-states-settlement.html)** · [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/29/technology/meta-states-settlement.html)
After months of intense negotiations and mounting courtroom setbacks, Meta’s legal leadership delivered a massive \$17.1 billion pact with state attorneys general. **[Sony Music and Warner Chappell Sue Anthropic in Multi-Billion Dollar Copyright Action](https://yro.slashdot.org/story/26/08/29/2149242/anthropic-sued-by-sony-warner-for-allegedly-stealing-tens-of-thousands-of-songs-torrenting-copyrighted-works)** · [Slashdot](https://yro.slashdot.org/story/26/08/29/2149242/anthropic-sued-by-sony-warner-for-allegedly-stealing-tens-of-thousands-of-songs-torrenting-copyrighted-works)
Publishing giants Sony Music and Warner Chappell filed a sweeping federal lawsuit in California against Anthropic, demanding statutory damages of up to \$150,000 per infringed work across tens of thousands of copyrighted musical compositions. The complaint alleges Anthropic executives orchestrated the mass torrenting and scraping of pirated libraries and licensed lyrics databases to train the Claude model family. Coming on the heels of Anthropic’s \$1.5 billion settlement with book authors, the action threatens billions in liabilities and represents the music industry’s broadest offensive yet against AI training methods.

**[UC Berkeley and MIT Researchers Unveil FreeToken for Consumer MoE Serving](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/freetoken-local-inference/)** · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/freetoken-local-inference/)
A team of computer scientists from UC Berkeley and MIT—alongside Databricks co-founders Matei Zaharia and Ion Stoica—released FreeToken, an open-source inference engine designed to execute frontier Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models on consumer workstations. By abandoning static memory offloading in favor of a dynamic CPU-GPU co-scheduling policy and semantic prompt caching, the system eliminates traditional PCIe transfer stalls. Benchmark tests demonstrated FreeToken serving DeepSeek-V4-Flash (284B) on a single desktop RTX 5090 and running Qwen3.6-35B at roughly 39 tokens per second on an 8GB laptop.

**[One-Time CRISPR Treatment Cuts High Cholesterol by 50% for Over a Year](https://science.slashdot.org/story/26/08/29/1942224/with-the-snip-of-a-gene-scientists-hope-to-erase-high-cholesterol-for-life)** · [Slashdot](https://science.slashdot.org/story/26/08/29/1942224/with-the-snip-of-a-gene-scientists-hope-to-erase-high-cholesterol-for-life)
Updated pilot clinical data published in *The New England Journal of Medicine* showed that a single in-vivo CRISPR-Cas9 infusion reduced heart-clogging LDL cholesterol and triglycerides by 50% with sustained durability over a full year. Cleveland Clinic researchers reported zero adverse reactions in patients with severe, medication-resistant disease. Cardiologists emphasize that if phase trials validate the results, a single intervention could permanently replace lifetime regimens of daily statin pills or bi-weekly injections.

**[Cloudflare Workers Opens Inbound TCP and gRPC to Power Edge AI Agents](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/workers-inbound-tcp-grpc/)** · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/workers-inbound-tcp-grpc/)
Cloudflare introduced a new raw socket handler that allows Workers to accept inbound TCP traffic for the first time since the platform’s 2017 launch, rolling out gRPC support as its initial implementation. Framed around low-latency, persistent bidirectional links for voice AI agents, the feature routes sockets through Spectrum into Durable Objects and unmodified containers. While native stream backpressure remains under scrutiny in private beta, the update breaks an eight-year HTTP barrier and positions the edge as an ingress gateway for custom binary protocols.

**[AI Chatbot Conversations Are Increasingly Subpoenaed as Court Evidence](https://yro.slashdot.org/story/26/08/29/0539211/chatbot-conversations-can-become-evidence-in-court)** · [Slashdot](https://yro.slashdot.org/story/26/08/29/0539211/chatbot-conversations-can-become-evidence-in-court)
A review of court dockets found that transcripts of user interactions with commercial AI models have surfaced as critical evidence across at least a dozen civil and criminal trials. Federal judges have dismissed arguments that chatbot queries carry attorney-client privilege, establishing that conversations logged on hosted servers can be subpoenaed or flagged directly to law enforcement. With government disclosure requests surging more than fourfold year-over-year, the trend creates severe legal hazards for users treating commercial chatbots as confidential sounding boards.
