# Tech News — 2026-06-13

## Story of the Day
The US government forced Anthropic to completely pull the plug on its brand-new Fable 5 and Mythos 5 foundation models globally over cybersecurity "jailbreak" fears. The unprecedented intervention—reportedly sparked by Amazon CEO Andy Jassy directly alerting the White House—is a massive escalation in federal AI oversight that has essentially paralyzed one of Silicon Valley's leading AI labs.

## Top Stories

**[The US Government Just Killed Anthropic's Latest Models](https://news.slashdot.org/story/26/06/13/2227234/amazon-ceos-talks-with-us-officials-triggered-crackdown-on-anthropic-models)** · Slashdot
A US export control directive forced Anthropic to abruptly disable its Fable 5 and Mythos 5 AI models for all customers worldwide to ensure foreign nationals could not access them. The shutdown was reportedly triggered by Amazon researchers and CEO Andy Jassy, who convinced the White House that the models could serve up intelligence to aid cyberattacks. Anthropic publicly pushed back, arguing the vulnerabilities were minor and widely available in competitor models like GPT-5.5, exposing a massive rift between frontier AI builders and the national security apparatus.

**[Local Backlash Derails $130 Billion in Data Center Projects](https://news.slashdot.org/story/26/06/12/2242249/data-center-opponents-have-blocked-or-delayed-projects-worth-nearly-130-billion-in-2026)** · Slashdot
In a staggering reflection of the physical toll of the AI infrastructure boom, community opposition blocked or delayed at least 75 data center projects worth $130 billion in Q1 2026 alone. The number of active grassroots opposition groups more than doubled to 833 across 49 states, signaling a structural shift where local communities and lawmakers are actively resisting the massive energy footprint required by hyperscalers.

**[OpenAI Faces Broad Investigation by Coalition of State AGs](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-13/openai-probed-by-coalition-of-state-attorneys-general)** · Bloomberg
A coalition of state attorneys general hit OpenAI with a sweeping subpoena targeting the company's handling of user data, health information, and interactions with minors. This probe follows a recent lawsuit by Florida over product safety and marks a sharp escalation in coordinated, state-level legal scrutiny of the generative AI giant's business practices.

**[GM Wants Its EVs to Bail Out the AI-Strained Power Grid](https://hardware.slashdot.org/story/26/06/13/0224235/gm-updates-250000-evs-with-vehicle-to-grid-firmware-announces-grid-scale-sodium-ion-batteries)** · Slashdot
General Motors is rolling out a firmware update to 250,000 electric vehicles to enable bidirectional vehicle-to-grid charging, effectively turning consumer driveways into a massive virtual power plant. Concurrently, GM announced a major bet on sodium-ion batteries—which bypass China's lithium-iron phosphate dominance and don't require active cooling—to supply grid-scale energy storage and stabilize a power grid currently buckling under the demands of AI.

**[Microsoft Weighs Spinning Off Xbox](https://www.theverge.com/games/949584/microsoft-spinning-off-xbox)** · The Verge
Amid sweeping layoffs and a reevaluation of its next-generation "Project Helix" console, Microsoft is reportedly considering drastic structural changes to its gaming division. Internal options reportedly on the table include turning Xbox into a wholly owned subsidiary, a joint venture, or completely spinning it off for a potential sale as the parent company seeks to make the unit more financially sustainable.

**[Arch Linux User Repository Hit with Massive Malware Attack](https://linux.slashdot.org/story/26/06/13/1817206/arch-linux-malware-incident-malicious-commits-found-in-1579-packages)** · Slashdot
Over 1,500 user-contributed packages in the Arch Linux User Repository (AUR) were compromised with malicious commits. Maintainers scrambled to delete the 1,579 malware-infected packages they could identify, dealing a significant blow to trust in the decentralized community repository system.
