# Tech News — 2026-08-28

## Story of the Day
In a stinging rebuke to executive overreach, a federal judge struck down the Trump administration's national security blacklist against Anthropic, ruling that the Pentagon unlawfully retaliated against the AI lab for establishing ethical "red lines" against autonomous lethal weapons and domestic mass surveillance. 

## Top Stories

**[Judge Overturns Pentagon's Supply-Chain Blacklist Against Anthropic](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/985947/anthropic-supply-chain-risk-lawsuit-judge-ruling)** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/985947/anthropic-supply-chain-risk-lawsuit-judge-ruling)
US District Judge Rita F. Lin permanently vacated the Defense Department's designation of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk, ruling that the government violated the First Amendment by retaliating against the company's protected speech. The Pentagon had barred federal agencies from deploying Claude after the lab refused military demands to drop terms prohibiting lethal autonomous weapons and mass domestic surveillance. While the administration may appeal and a parallel suit proceeds in Washington, the decision establishes an essential precedent limiting the government's ability to weaponize defense procurement to force commercial AI labs into compliance.

**[Stripe and Advent Ditch \$53 Billion Pursuit of PayPal](https://news.slashdot.org/story/26/08/28/1633219/stripe-and-advent-ditch-50-billion-paypal-takeover-bid)** · [Slashdot](https://news.slashdot.org/story/26/08/28/1633219/stripe-and-advent-ditch-50-billion-paypal-takeover-bid)
Stripe and private equity firm Advent International have walked away from their joint attempt to acquire PayPal for roughly \$53 billion. The collapse terminates what would have stood as the largest fintech takeover in corporate history, following weeks of speculation and Stripe's separate \$8 billion deal for AI routing hub OpenRouter. PayPal is now left to execute its turnaround as an independent public entity amid intense competitive pressure and higher interest rates.

**[Ninth Circuit Rules States Can Regulate Prediction Markets as Gambling](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/08/kalshi-cant-evade-nevada-gambling-laws-by-calling-bets-swaps-court-rules/)** · [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/08/kalshi-cant-evade-nevada-gambling-laws-by-calling-bets-swaps-court-rules/)
A three-judge panel on the US Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit unanimously affirmed that Nevada state regulators can enforce gambling laws against Kalshi's sports event contracts. The court dismissed Kalshi's argument that its contracts qualify as federally preempted financial swaps under the Commodity Exchange Act, labeling its effort to separate sports betting from gambling "disingenuous" and "unpersuasive". The decision delivers state regulators a major legal victory and creates an irreconcilable federal split that almost certainly sends prediction markets to the US Supreme Court.

**[Over 100 Tech Companies Warn of Imminent Wave of AI Cyber Threats](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/26/08/28/216235/openai-anthropic-google-and-100-other-companies-call-for-action-to-defend-against-rogue-ai)** · [Slashdot](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/26/08/28/216235/openai-anthropic-google-and-100-other-companies-call-for-action-to-defend-against-rogue-ai)
More than 100 tech leaders—including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, and cybersecurity vendors CrowdStrike and Okta—signed an open letter warning that AI-enabled cyber attacks against critical civilian infrastructure will rapidly escalate in frequency and sophistication. The coalition is urging immediate public-private defense partnerships to protect essential utilities, hospitals, and networking backbones. The joint warning highlights an unresolved tension for frontier AI developers, who are simultaneously releasing increasingly autonomous agentic systems while selling specialized defense tools to contain them.

**[Meta Details In-House MTIA 300 Accelerator with Integrated Networking](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/meta-hccl/)** · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/meta-hccl/)
Meta unveiled technical specifications for its MTIA 300 silicon, embedding twelve custom 800 Gbps RDMA network interface chiplets and 16 autonomous message engines directly into the accelerator package to handle recommendation model workloads. Because recommendation models store over 99% of their parameters in sprawling embedding tables, interconnect bandwidth and collective communication are far more critical than raw matrix math. Meta claims the hardware and its co-designed HCCL library slashed communication latency by 3.9x over standard GPU clusters while keeping compute throughput degradation under 0.5%.

**[White House Considers Expanding Tariffs to Semiconductors and AI Hardware](https://yro.slashdot.org/story/26/08/28/009205/us-considers-fresh-round-of-tariffs-on-semiconductors)** · [Slashdot](https://yro.slashdot.org/story/26/08/28/009205/us-considers-fresh-round-of-tariffs-on-semiconductors)
The Trump administration is drafting a staggered rollout of fresh import tariffs targeting foreign semiconductors, data center servers, and laptops to encourage domestic manufacturing. Tech trade organizations pushed back aggressively, noting that domestic fabrication cannot meet current demand and warning that inflating hardware costs will kneecap the American data center buildout. Industry representatives emphasized that factory lead times span several years and that duty-free allowances would fail to cover even initial hyperscaler orders.

**[Apple TV and Apple One Prices Jump by Up to 20 Percent](https://www.theverge.com/tech/986130/apple-tv-plus-price-hike)** · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/986130/apple-tv-plus-price-hike)
Apple rolled out its fourth price increase in four years, bumping Apple TV's monthly rate from \$12.99 to \$14.99 and its annual plan from \$99 to \$119, while lifting individual Apple One bundles to \$21.95. The service's monthly price has now tripled since its \$4.99 debut in 2019. The recurring increases demonstrate how Big Tech streaming offerings are shifting focus away from subscriber subsidies and customer acquisition toward margin preservation and subscription services revenue.
