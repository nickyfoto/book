# Tech News — 2026-06-26

## Story of the Day
Less than 24 hours after reports surfaced that the Trump administration requested a rollout delay, OpenAI boldly pushed live a limited preview of its GPT-5.6 model suite. The defiant launch underscores an escalating tug-of-war between Washington regulators and Silicon Valley over who controls the deployment timeline of frontier AI models.

## Top Stories

**[OpenAI unveils GPT-5.6 amid US AI regulatory drama](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/957845/openai-gpt-5-6-trump-administration-ai-preview)** · The Verge
OpenAI ignored government pressure to stagger its latest release, rolling out the GPT-5.6 suite—featuring the flagship Sol, medium-tier Terra, and fast Luna models—to select partners. The company publicly pushed back against the Trump administration's intervention, stating that restrictive government access processes "shouldn't be the norm" because they withhold essential tools from developers and cyber defenders.

**[Apple Price Hikes Spark Asia Tech Selloff on Memory Cost Concern](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-26/apple-price-hikes-spark-asia-tech-selloff-on-memory-cost-concern)** · Bloomberg
Apple and Microsoft have initiated unprecedented, sweeping price hikes across their hardware lines—including MacBooks, iPads, and Xbox consoles—as the tech sector grapples with an AI-driven memory chip shortage. This component squeeze, dubbed "RAMaggedon," has spooked investors, sparking a sharp selloff in Asian tech stocks as markets digest the collateral damage of the AI data center boom.

**[VW may close four factories to adapt to the future, report says](https://arstechnica.com/cars/2026/06/vw-may-close-four-factories-to-adapt-to-the-future-report-says/)** · Ars Technica
Volkswagen is reportedly preparing to shut down up to four of its German factories and slash as many as 100,000 jobs to adapt to its bleeding margins. This massive 15 percent workforce reduction comes as the automaker faces flat sales in Europe, plummeting profits, and a steep decline in crucial North American and Chinese markets.

**[Anthropic Moves Toward Deal With US to Lift Curbs on AI Models](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-26/anthropic-moves-toward-deal-with-us-to-lift-curbs-on-ai-models)** · Bloomberg
After a tense 14-day standoff that forced Anthropic to pull its Mythos-class AI models offline, the company is nearing an agreement with the Trump administration. The Commerce Department has just granted partial clearance to allow "trusted partners" to access the Mythos 5 model, though full, unrestricted availability remains in limbo while security talks continue.

**[OpenAI Weighs IPO in 2027 After Expected Anthropic Public Debut](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-26/openai-weighs-ipo-in-2027-after-expected-anthropic-public-debut)** · Bloomberg
OpenAI is reconsidering its timeline to go public, reportedly pushing its initial public offering to 2027, which could allow rival Anthropic to beat it to the market. The delay, attributed to choppy global markets, sent shockwaves through its investor base, causing shares of key backer SoftBank to plummet by as much as 13 percent.

**[Samsung will soon start charging to access its smart home API](https://www.theverge.com/tech/957597/samsung-smartthings-api-charges)** · The Verge
Starting in October, Samsung will end free, unrestricted access to its SmartThings API, introducing paid tiers that include a $4.99 monthly plan for individual developers. The policy shift will heavily impact advanced smart home users and third-party integrations like Home Assistant, forcing them onto paid personal plans to maintain their flexible controls.
