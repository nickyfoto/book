**[IETF Publishes RFC 10008 Adding the QUERY Method to HTTP](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/09/http-query-method/)** · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/09/http-query-method/)
The Internet Engineering Task Force published RFC 10008, officially introducing QUERY as the first new standard HTTP verb since PATCH landed in 2010. The new method allows client requests to carry complex payload bodies while maintaining safe, idempotent, and cacheable semantics. The specification aims to replace cumbersome API workarounds like cramming long parameters into GET URLs or tunneling safe reads through POST requests.

**[US Executive Order Bans Foreign Batteries in Grid Energy Storage](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/09/10/1143791/us-china-batteries/)** · [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/09/10/1143791/us-china-batteries/)

Industry analysts warn the ban could slow near-term battery deployments and increase installation costs while domestic manufacturing ramps up toward 2030 targets. The decision underscores the tension between accelerating renewable energy deployment and securing critical infrastructure supply chains.
