## Top Stories

**[AliExpress hit with record $625M fine after failing to make EU-ordered fixes](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/07/aliexpress-fined-625m-for-failing-to-remove-unsafe-toys-dangerous-cosmetics/)** · Ars Technica
The European Commission hit AliExpress with a €550 million ($625 million) fine under the Digital Services Act for failing to moderate illegal and dangerous goods on its platform. Regulators found the company understaffed its moderation teams and actively recommended unsafe toys and dangerous cosmetics to buyers. AliExpress called the fine "disproportionate" and plans to appeal.

**[Judge halts Paramount’s $111B purchase of Warner Bros. in win for US states](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/07/judge-halts-paramounts-111b-purchase-of-warner-bros-in-win-for-us-states/)** · Ars Technica
A federal judge granted a temporary restraining order to pause the $111 billion merger between Paramount Skydance and Warner Bros. Discovery. The 14-day hold comes after 12 state attorneys general sued to block the deal, arguing it violates antitrust laws by combining major Hollywood studios and basic cable owners. The court noted that irreparable harm could occur if the companies were allowed to consolidate operations before the lawsuit is resolved.

**[LG’s monitors come with an unwanted addition for Windows: McAfee pop-up ads](https://www.theverge.com/tech/967983/lg-monitors-mcafee-adware-gamers-nexus)** · The Verge
Connecting certain LG monitors to a Windows 11 PC triggers Windows Update to silently install an LG Monitor App Installer. Without prompting for user permission, the software runs at startup and repeatedly pushes pop-up ads for McAfee antivirus trials. The adware-like behavior appears to affect multiple monitor models, with user complaints dating back to 2024.

**[Hugging Face confirms breach affected internal datasets and credentials, urges users to take action](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/hugging-face-confirms-breach-affected-internal-datasets-and-credentials-urges-users-to-take-action/)** · TechCrunch
Hugging Face disclosed that its production infrastructure was breached by an autonomous AI agent, prompting the company to urge users to rotate access tokens. In a striking twist, the company's incident responders were blocked by the guardrails of US frontier AI models, forcing them to use a Chinese open-source model, GLM 5.2, to analyze the attacker's logs. The incident highlights growing cybersecurity concerns around agentic AI and the practical limitations of heavily restricted domestic models.

**[The FCC is planning to retroactively ban disguised DJI gadgets](https://www.theverge.com/news/968310/fcc-dji-drone-camera-ban-skyrover-xtra)** · The Verge
The FCC is preparing to use its newly granted power to retroactively ban electronics previously approved for US sale, targeting "front companies" used by Chinese drone maker DJI. The agency proposed fines and outright import bans on eight companies distributing disguised DJI tech like Skyrover drones and Xtra cameras. This marks the commission's first major move to aggressively enforce its foreign drone ban against attempts to evade sanctions.
