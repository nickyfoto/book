### AI & Machine Learning
The most technically substantive update is [From 46% to 90%: Fine-Tuning Tiny LLMs for On-Device Agents — Cormac Brick, Google](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-TiET_K-E_g) on the *AI Engineer* channel, which details how fine-tuning a 270-million parameter "Function Gemma" model on synthetic data increased function calling success rates from 46% to over 90%.
