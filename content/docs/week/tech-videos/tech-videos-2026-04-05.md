### Hardware & Infrastructure
In [David Friedberg: Mining the Moon is Humanity’s Next Industrial Revolution](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OxDNaae3zsw) on the All-In Podcast, Friedberg argues that the Moon's 1/6th gravity and lack of atmosphere make it a highly credible environment for frictionless, low-energy manufacturing. He notes that mined materials could be returned to Earth or sent to Mars at virtually zero transport cost using a "mass driver"—an electric rail that accelerates packages to 100 G-forces.
