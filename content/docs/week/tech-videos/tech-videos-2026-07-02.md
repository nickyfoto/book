### AI & Machine Learning
**[The Prompt Is Still a Punch Card - Ted Johnson, JoinIn AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hVJOnuhFmTA)** delivers a standout conceptual talk on the AI Engineer channel, arguing that the modern LLM text box is essentially a legacy batch-processing punch card that forces humans to front-load context. True AI interfaces, the speaker argues, must evolve to support real-time, turn-taking conversational protocols that relieve the user of the interface burden. Addressing current AI limitations, Lenny's Podcast explores **[Why is AI so bad at design?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TuXk2bNwT8o)**, pointing out that models fail at generating true novelty and struggle with deep semantic abstractions, making them incapable of systematically managing a large component library rebrand.
