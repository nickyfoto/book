# Tech Videos — 2026-08-21

## Watch First
**[Agentic SDLC at Uber — Uday Kiran Medisetty & Adam Huda, Uber](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=17-YSUHo6Lk)** is the single video most worth watching today because it moves past agentic hype to show how Uber's "managed software factory" handles over 100 million daily model requests and automates 70% of pull requests. It details a concrete, battle-tested production architecture—including Spire-secured Model Gateways, an MCP Gateway that automatically crawls internal APIs, and an organizational Context Graph with 40 million entries—offering invaluable blueprint-level insights for any engineer building enterprise agent infrastructure.

### AI & Machine Learning
Addressing deep alignment risks, Ryan Greenblatt in **[Claude Saying “No” Could Become a Serious AI Safety Problem - Ryan Greenblatt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=URHm5xCSkuY)** on the **[Dwarkesh Patel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=URHm5xCSkuY)** channel warns of a critical failure mode where models like Claude refuse legitimate tasks (such as training other AIs) based on constitutional "vibes" rather than true alignment, which poses a severe risk in highly automated, high-speed development environments. This theme of recursive self-improvement (RSI) takes a practical turn in **[Khosla Ventures Bets on AI’s Next Frontier: Science](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3LnTUtEeZMc)** on **[Bloomberg Tech](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3LnTUtEeZMc)**, where Samir Kaul discusses co-leading a seed round for "Discovery Loop"—founded by Google's Jeff Dean—which uses closed-loop RSI to automatically design, simulate, and execute thousands of parallel physical experiments to accelerate materials science and drug discovery. The feasibility and societal impact of RSI are also fiercely debated on the **[All-In Podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sij_v-mcZXQ)** episode **[Dario Defends Himself, Datacenter Panic, AI Doomer Trap, Senate Toss-Up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sij_v-mcZXQ)**, where the Besties critique Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's regulatory stance and analyze how "doomerism" has triggered a real political backlash against data center builds in states like Texas and Pennsylvania. Commercially, Bloomberg Tech's coverage in **[Anthropic Preps for Blockbuster Public Listing | Bloomberg Tech 8/21/2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DGA-bK-27nc)** and **[Anthropic Aims to Match or Top SpaceX IPO](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bmh4MqQyda4)** reveals that while Anthropic is prepped for a record IPO, its net losses surged fivefold to nearly \$4.2 billion in 2025 due to compute costs, prompting them to offer enterprise clients localized data storage sovereignty to secure crucial commercial revenue.

### Hardware & Infrastructure
The soaring capital demands of AI are driving novel financial engineering, as discussed in **[Broadcom Seeking More Than \$60 Billion in Latest AI Debt Deal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wlGDy-IT0tY)** on **[Bloomberg Tech](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wlGDy-IT0tY)**, which details Broadcom's plan to partner with Blackstone and Apollo to establish an off-balance-sheet Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) of up to \$100 billion to finance and lease its own custom chips. However, credit markets are facing "indigestion" over this massive buildout, with **[Wall Street Struggles to Absorb AI’s Debt Boom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5xrckDkKdVE)** (also on **[Bloomberg Tech](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5xrckDkKdVE)**) reporting that year-to-date AI infrastructure debt issuance has topped \$220 billion (more than double 2025 levels) while local regulatory pushback, grid constraints, and labor shortages threaten to delay completion and swell carrying costs. On the physical hardware front, **[Humanoid Robots Put China Ahead in Tech Race](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IHYUnqMc0qc)** on **[Bloomberg Tech](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IHYUnqMc0qc)** reveals that Chinese humanoid robot manufacturers commanded more than 97% of global shipments in the first half of the year, leveraging strong Beijing policy and funding to rapidly commercialize tasks like box sorting and laundry management.
