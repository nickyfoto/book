# Tech Videos — 2026-09-17

## Watch First
Stanford Professor Emeritus John Ousterhout makes a rigorous case for replacing legacy TCP and RDMA with Homa, a clean-slate receiver-driven transport protocol engineered to eliminate tail latency in sub-millisecond agentic synchronization in **[Homa: The End of TCP for AI Clusters — John Ousterhout, Stanford](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eZ8WWZzoaR0)** on AI Engineer. It is the single most actionable talk for systems engineers, demonstrating how shifting congestion control from sender to receiver reduces packet queuing delays by an order of magnitude without requiring network hardware overhauls.
