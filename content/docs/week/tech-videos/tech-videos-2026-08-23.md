### AI & Machine Learning
On Dwarkesh Patel’s channel, Redwood Research's Ryan Greenblatt takes a blunt, conceptual swipe at LLM theory in **[Machine Learning Is Shallow Compared to Math - Ryan Greenblatt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gfi0bOuHiDU)**, arguing that ML is a conceptually shallow field. He posits that the field's most celebrated formulas, like scaling laws, are ultimately simple heuristics that can be explained in minutes, contrasting them with math's genuinely deep, hard-to-understand abstract frameworks. This suggests that despite the hype around complex "AI science," ML remains a fundamentally empirical, brute-force engineering discipline rather than a conceptually deep theoretical science.

Sacks argues that this hypocritical regulatory doomerism effectively blocks the massive infrastructure development required to actually win the AI scaling race.
