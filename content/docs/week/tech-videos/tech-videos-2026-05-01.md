# Tech Videos — 2026-05-01

## Watch First
[Agents on the Canvas in tldraw — Steve Ruiz, tldraw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sPUjIBH5Cwg) is the must-watch of the day. Ruiz ditches the tired chat UI by wrapping the canvas in an Electron app that gives Claude direct script-injection access to the DOM, effectively demonstrating what it looks like when AI models break out of the sidebar to collaborate natively on a visual canvas.

## Highlights by Theme

### Developer Tools & Platforms
[The Download: Linux 486 retirement, DeepSeek v4, TanStack AI & more](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PpL7vQupWqM) by GitHub highlights the alpha release of TanStack AI, an open-source, framework-agnostic toolkit with isomorphic typing that ensures type safety across server and client boundaries. Meanwhile, [Strands Agents just shipped TypeScript SDK 1.0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g5gCMTchjKs) from AWS Developers brings Zod-validated tools and built-in Model Context Protocol (MCP) to the browser, unlocking truly client-side agent execution. On the noisier side, OpenAI's [Bring your work into Codex in a few clicks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=flvZ6jEj3VU) is a polished 60-second marketing reel showing seamless Slack and Google Drive integrations, but offers zero technical depth on how the underlying context routing is managed.

### AI & Machine Learning
In a high-signal interview on [Baseten CEO Tuhin Srivastava on Custom Models, and Building the Inference Cloud](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XAbKflCncDo), the CEO notes that over 95% of their served tokens now come from custom, post-trained models rather than vanilla open-source weights. To help operationalize these complex multi-stage flows, [Shipping complex AI applications — Braintrust & Trainline](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZdheJTfLu-s) provides a gritty, hands-on workshop about replacing vibe-based prompts with nested tracing and deterministic scoring functions to prevent autonomous agents from breaking production systems. Additionally, GitHub's open-source news notes the arrival of DeepSeek v4, a model heavily optimized for agentic tool use that pushes open-weights inference costs down to an order of magnitude cheaper than closed-source frontier models.

### Hardware & Infrastructure
The All-In Podcast's [OpenAI Misses Targets, Codex vs Claude, Elon vs Sam Trial, Big Hyperscaler Beats, Peptide Craze](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fpC4sbawSzQ) notes that hyperscalers like Amazon, Microsoft, Google, and Meta are projecting over $700 billion in capital expenditures to build out AI data centers, sacrificing their once-legendary free cash flow. However, the actual bottleneck is shifting from GPU supply chains to the raw power grid capacity required to run these massive clusters. For everyday cloud practitioners, [You Deleted Everything and AWS Is Still Charging You](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NJ6F0atWKpM) offers a highly practical reminder to clean up unattached EBS volumes, elastic IPs, and NAT gateways that silently accumulate monthly charges long after instances are terminated. 

### Everything Else
[Mastering AI Pricing: Flexible & Agile Monetization — Mayank Pant, Stripe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CrqPcIZOOXA) points out that pure subscription SaaS pricing is dangerous for AI products due to power users destroying hardware margins, leading 56% of top AI companies to adopt hybrid billing models. On the startup strategy front, [The One Thing Nobody Can Copy About Corgi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sdN7zw_XbuU) explains how enduring massive regulatory friction to become a licensed insurance carrier—rather than just another API broker—created a nearly unassailable two-year infrastructure moat.
